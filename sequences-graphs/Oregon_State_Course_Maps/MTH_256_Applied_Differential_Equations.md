# MTH 256 — Applied Differential Equations

## Course Description

Applied Differential Equations introduces methods for describing and solving problems in which an unknown function is related to its derivatives. The course focuses on ordinary differential equations and applications appropriate to science and engineering, particularly electrical circuits and mechanical oscillators. Coverage includes first-order linear and selected nonlinear equations, second-order linear equations, and introductory work with higher-order equations and Laplace transforms.

## Course Overview

The archived syllabus begins with mathematical modeling and the classification of differential equations, then develops methods for first-order and second-order equations. This organization connects the structure of an equation with an appropriate solution approach. Applications give meaning to the unknown functions and their derivatives rather than treating equations as purely formal expressions.

Laplace transforms provide an additional approach to solving differential equations, while introductory higher-order work extends the range of equations considered. Electrical circuits and mechanical oscillators serve as central application settings. The published description also identifies familiarity with complex numbers and Euler’s identities as relevant background.

## Course Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will have mastered the following:

### Equation Classification and Mathematical Modeling

- Classify ordinary differential equations by order and distinguish linear equations from nonlinear equations.
- Translate a basic scientific or engineering model into a differential equation relating an unknown function to its rates of change.
- Interpret the variables, derivatives, and solutions of a differential-equation model in the context of the problem.

### Analytical Solution Methods

- Solve first-order linear equations and selected first-order nonlinear equations using methods appropriate to their form.
- Obtain solutions of second-order linear differential equations.
- Apply introductory solution methods to higher-order linear differential equations.

### Laplace Transform Methods

- Explain how a Laplace transform can convert a linear differential-equation problem into an algebraic problem.
- Use elementary Laplace transforms and inverse transforms to solve basic linear differential-equation problems.

### Electrical and Mechanical Applications

- Formulate and solve differential-equation problems involving electrical circuits and mechanical oscillators, interpreting the resulting functions in terms of the modeled system.
