# 3. Activity Capture

Capture this content while doing the activity and reviewing its history page.

## Questions

Save question text, Math Academy IDs, difficulty, topic/knowledge-point/example references, and original HTML.

## Answer Fields and Answers

Save field types, positions, choices, and Math Academy's correct answers when revealed.

## Worked Solutions

Save each explanation and its formatting, linked to the question it explains.

## Tutorials and Examples

Save tutorial content and canonical examples with their Math Academy IDs and lesson placement.

## Activities, Steps, and Multisteps

Save instructions, shared context, content references, knowledge-point associations, step IDs, and order.

Use the question count and time limit from Queue Processing; check the live timer for time remaining.

## Images

Preserve original images and record where each one appears in the content.

## Submitted Responses and Results

Save answers, skips, retries, grades, and timing. Match history-page results to the captured questions. Keep our responses and reasoning separate from Math Academy's content.

Never defer or abandon the activity. Save our place so capture can resume after an interruption.
