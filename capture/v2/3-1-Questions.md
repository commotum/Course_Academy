# 3.1. Questions

1. Save the question text, answer fields, choices, original images, and HTML.
2. Record its Math Academy ID, difficulty, and topic/knowledge-point/example references when available. Keep it linked to its activity and any shared context.
3. Follow the activity's rules for answering or skipping. Enter the response and verify what was entered.
4. Record every submitted response, skip, and retry, together with any feedback shown.
5. Save feedback HTML and images. Add Math Academy's grades, revealed correct answers, and worked solutions to the same question when they appear during the activity or on its history page.
6. Keep our responses and reasoning separate from Math Academy's original content. Save our place after each interaction so capture can resume.

Each activity controls when to submit answers and when to read feedback or history.
