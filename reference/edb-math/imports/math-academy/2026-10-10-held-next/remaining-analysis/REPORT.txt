Why they remain held

1. Multi-part and paraphrased choices need all clauses checked together.

   The 519 prose/mixed radio questions include 273 choices containing two or more
   math fragments. Examples include pairs of intercepts, multiple axes, several
   eigenvalues, or a result together with its classification. A source may state
   these in separate paragraphs; the old matcher largely expects one terminal
   expression or a narrowly formatted concluding sentence.

   q-15692 asks for both y-intercepts. The solution explicitly gives
   (0,4-sqrt(5)) and (0,4+sqrt(5)); the original option joins them with 'and'.
   q-16455 asks for major and minor axis lengths. The source names 4 sqrt(2)
   and 2 separately, and their requested order matters.

   A separate overlapping flag finds 83 radio candidates naming Roman-numbered
   statements. Each statement and its original order must be retained. q-29163
   says only equation III has no solutions, while its choice says 'III only'.
   This is a wording/statement-label match, not a new mathematical solution.

2. The requested answer is often not the final mathematical expression.

   Of the 235 literal-match cases, 154 match only earlier source formulas;
   81 include a match in the final formula. Later formulas can be verification
   steps, examples, comparison values, or incidental variables. Literal presence
   alone cannot determine the requested answer or its scope.

   q-31862 asks for row operations. The source explicitly lists both operations,
   then finishes with the inverse matrix. Selecting the final matrix would be
   wrong for this prompt.
   q-31634 ends by identifying t=5 as the only simultaneous stationary time,
   followed by incidental mentions of t in the prose. The whole conclusion must
   be bound to the question instead of extracting the last inline variable.

   Twenty-three remaining radio solutions contain an explicit 'correct answer'
   phrase. Even these can miss narrow extraction rules: q-22428 says
   'Therefore, the correct answer is, "I and III only."' and then includes an
   explanatory image. The comma after 'is' and the trailing image defeat the
   earlier strict patterns. These are promising review candidates, not yet keys.

3. Equivalent source presentations need structure-preserving interpretation.

   The remaining 332 radio math cases include notation differences, equation
   chains, combined results, and nested mathematical layouts. Across all radio
   categories, 163 candidate answers contain a matrix/aligned expression, four
   solutions have nested outer aligned environments, 15 candidates contain
   measurement units, and 12 answers are Markdown tables. These flags overlap;
   they are not 332 additional exclusive subcategories.

   q-1393: source concludes 10i-2j+30k; the option is <10,-2,30>.
   q-1913: source and option put the two derivative terms in opposite order.
   q-2194: the integral conclusion is wrapped in two aligned environments.
   q-6836: source gives dy/dx = intermediate fraction = final expression;
           the option combines the original left side with the final right side.
   q-3435: one option combines p+q=80 with both roots, stated in different places.
   q-2939: the source uses km/h across separate text nodes; the option uses one.

   Recovery should copy/match source-supported expressions, preserving complete
   factors, matrix shape, vectors, constants, signs, domains and units. Broad
   algebraic equivalence or substring matching would mask genuinely different
   choices. None of these diagnostic examples authorizes a transaction yet.

4. Select grading often confirms every field except the intentionally wrong one.

   The 75 select questions have 239 fields. The prior source audit confirms 153
   fields and leaves 86 unconfirmed:

      69 questions need one field (62 need only field-1).
       5 questions need two fields.
       1 question, q-335673, needs all seven fields.

   All 75 prompts contain their expected field placeholders. The seven known
   damaged select layouts were repaired and committed, so missing placement is
   no longer a blocker in this remainder. Matrix/table geometry still needs
   review even when every placeholder is present.

   Capture intent is W for 70 selects, skip for three, and C for two. In a common
   case, q-335460, the captured first field says Incorrect with no source_correct
   key; the other two fields have explicit Correct/source_correct values. Its
   worked solution spells out the completed sentence, but the old multi-field
   matcher handles equations and tables rather than general prose templates.

   The 75 selects group as follows:
      24 classifications or logical qualifiers
      17 numeric/symbolic results needing field context
      14 Fourier formulas or convergence conditions
      11 proof-line justifications
       7 variation-of-parameters intermediate forms
       2 matrix/vector-entry questions

   q-335422 requires the justification for a particular L1-to-L2 proof step.
   q-337376 asks for the VOP assumed form in field-1, not the final particular
   solution. The source explicitly presents y_p(x)=u(x)e^(3x) before simplifying.
   q-343395 asks for an early cosine evaluation, a continuity classification,
   f(0), and an infinite sum. Its early chain says cos(...*0)=cos(0)=1; a matcher
   comparing the complete template directly to the chain can miss field-1.
   q-335673 was skipped, so no directly graded keys were captured; the solution
   supplies a 2x2 Jacobian, two eigenvalues, and an unstable-saddle classification
   that must be bound to seven separate fields.

   Multiple-unconfirmed questions:
   q-335673, q-343341, q-343395, q-343438, q-343445, q-343446.

5. Wrong submissions and genuine discrepancies require separate handling.

   Across the selected captures for all remaining questions, 1,149 were intended
   wrong, six skipped, four intended correct, and two lack an intent label.
   Their recorded grades are 1,159 Incorrect, one No Credit, and one Correct.
   This explains why submissions cannot supply the missing answer authority.

   Two remaining select fields have competing saved agent candidates:
   - q-339169: 'has a unique solution' versus 'has at most one solution'. The
     first was submitted and graded Incorrect. The saved source conclusion says
     'has at most one solution'. Retain the source's exact logical qualifier.
   - q-342410: 'a regular singular' versus 'an irregular singular' at x=-1.
     The submitted regular-singular choice was graded Incorrect; the source
     explicitly calls it irregular singular. Each point needs its own binding.

   q-4772 is a genuine unit disagreement: prompt/options use centimeters, while
   the worked solution states meters. Do not convert or silently correct it.

   q-64676 has a recorded Correct grade but no submitted-option/value binding in
   the saved before fields. The grade label alone cannot identify its key.
