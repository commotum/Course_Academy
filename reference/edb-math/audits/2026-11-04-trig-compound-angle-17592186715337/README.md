The generated hard family changes three things at once: it starts from an expanded product expression (reverse identity recognition), omits the supplied decomposition, and requires a second identity application to evaluate the resulting non-special angle. That combines the neighboring KPs Simplifying Expressions in Radians/Degrees Using the Sum and Difference Formulas with this KP's exact-evaluation move. It can be an intentional synthesis task, but it is not a close variant of the imported practice, and the absence of imported hard anchors means its hard-band calibration is authored rather than source-verified.

The generator deliberately creates this extra work. In generate(), every hard item gets an outer pair (u,v,t), with u not among recalled special angles and u constrained to at most 345 degrees in magnitude. In make_record(), the outer branch prints the expanded trig products, adds a reverse-identity prefix to the solution, and tells the learner to choose a special-angle decomposition only in the worked solution. Thus arithmetic, sign variation, missing scaffolding and an additional recognition direction are bundled into a single hard label.

Its use as a hard source anchor reproduces an earlier authored pattern rather than independently establishing that Math Academy uses or labels that pattern hard. This does not imply those files claim all published cases are imported; the problem is treating that authored hard case as pedagogical calibration evidence.

Recommended correction: keep the same exact-value objective and supplied angle decomposition across difficulty bands; vary manageable signed special-angle substitutions and resulting radical arithmetic. Treat unsupplied decomposition and reverse-plus-forward synthesis as separately justified objectives with explicit coverage, rather than silently inventing a hard band from a generated predecessor. Do not use coefficient/angle magnitude or additional hidden moves as automatic difficulty proxies.
