# Backbone and allowed variation

Use real quadratic equations A x² + B xy + C y² + D x + E y = K. Coefficients remain small integers. Diagonal, translated diagonal, parabola and product families appear at moderate; normal versions, signed diagonal hyperbolas and mixed-product quadratics appear at hard. Easy uses positive diagonal quadratics and requires the learner to differentiate them. A nonzero finite tangent slope and a unique finite requested intercept are required for fresh practice, because horizontal/vertical exceptions would add a separate rule. The given point cannot already be on the requested axis: source cb9cd239 permits skipping the target operation, so it is retained only in historical replay. Product families may include small linear terms: this conservative extension combines the product rule from 5b7bca4e with linear-term differentiation from 019eae01, preventing every product tangent intercept from being merely twice a given coordinate. Unshifted products remain eligible. Zero intercepts through the origin remain legitimate and are not rejected. Fresh points have small integer coordinates; a zero coordinate on the other axis remains eligible as in e-192 and q-14614. A point already on the requested axis is excluded because it supplies the requested intersection without the target work. Radical source parameters belong to incomplete evidence and are not necessary to preserve the supported complete families.

Choose the axis and family before compatible numbers. Point quadrants, gradient signs, derivative collection, line role and requested coordinate are answer-bearing roles. Both axes and both slope signs must occur within each served family; normal is a justified fixed role at hard, tangent at easy/moderate, reflecting source labels and target-work progression. No correct position, numerical rank, or unique opposite-sign pair is fixed. Coefficients, signed point coordinates and translations change decisive evidence; proportional rescaling of the entire equation must preserve the response. Fresh equation constants are derived by substituting the chosen point. A scalar-rescaling counterfactual checks that arbitrary equation presentation is irrelevant. Axis changes with the curve and contact point held fixed must update prompt, line intersection and key together.

All fresh prompts are algebra-only. Source questions both with and without diagrams establish that a schematic is optional when the equation, point, line role and axis are explicit; it gives no extra numerical data. Source diagrams remain canonical in replay. No assets are generated by runtime. The omission reduces incidental visual cues without altering the mathematical task.

# Difficulty and response contract

Easy: five complete coordinate choices; prompt supplies the point-slope form and axis reminder. Learner differentiates implicitly, isolates the derivative, evaluates its slope, constructs a tangent and calculates the intercept. Prerequisite work is small rational arithmetic. Moderate: same target chain with no derivative or line scaffold; both radio and two-coordinate exact blank contracts are supported by imports, but fresh practice uses coordinate blanks to avoid preserving arbitrary source-bank patterns. Translated and product terms add derivative algebra; exact entry also adds response demand, recorded explicitly. Hard: coordinates are blanks, same small numbers; normal conversion adds a target decision. Mixed-product families add the already-taught product rule and collection of derivative terms, as in complete hard examples. Hard need not use mixed products in every case: normal conversion alone distinguishes matched moderate/hard questions. Large coefficients do not define difficulty.

Matched comparisons retain x²+2y²=17 at (3,2), x-axis: easy requires deriving 2x+4y y′=0 with radio responses; moderate removes the point-slope guidance and asks exact coordinates (17/3,0); hard changes tangent to normal with no further arithmetic burdens, giving (3/2,0). A separate hard matched product instance xy+y²=6 at (1,2), y-axis follows its published exemplar. Source hard parabola 4ca93831 and translated ellipse 96578920 calibrate normal workload; moderate 20636dab and 019eae01 calibrate chain-rule workload.

# Banks, derivations, validation

One instance yields the equation, derivative stages, tangent slope, requested line slope, intercept, answer and solution. Easy error profiles each contain four distinct mistakes chosen together: wrong slope sign, negative reciprocal, reciprocal without sign, doubled/halved slope, missing point translation, or a final sign error when solving for the intercept. Rejection must preserve distinctness and one correct choice; no arbitrary offset distractors. Numerical rank, position, sign counterpart and visible fraction complexity are checked conditionally by family and axis, before and after actual delivery preparation. Exact blanks avoid choice-only shortcuts in the less scaffolded bands.

The final answer is a rational coordinate, derived from the shown point-slope substitution; only rational arithmetic conversions are needed. CAS checks are independent of the operational derivative arithmetic.
