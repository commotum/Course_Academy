# Why the generators acquired scaffolding and artificial difficulty

This is a read-only causal audit of the generator pipeline. No prompts, generator
registrations, approvals or database content were changed. `evidence.json` retains
the exact historical paths, line numbers, excerpts, hashes, published exemplars
and accepted review explanations from four traced jobs.

## Confirmed historical cause

The actual archived author prompts for KPs 17592186059665, 17592186059666,
17592186059672 and 17592186059820 instructed the author to:

> Retain a matched-instance comparison showing the intended increase in target
> work or reduction in support. Control incidental work while adding one
> relevant challenge

That made additional reasoning and reduced assistance the expected progression.
It did not clearly permit the same independent task throughout the bands with
modest numerical changes. Alongside mandatory three-band construction, this
encouraged inventing assistance for an absent easy band and an extra operation
for an absent hard band.

The investigation accounts explicitly describe that interpretation:

| KP | Recorded decision | Published outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17592186059665, cosine radian shifts | “Easy adds explicit support because no complete easy import exists. Moderate removes that support” | 17592186714557 supplies the difference identity and both special-angle values. |
| 17592186059820, sine radian shifts | Added support “establishes an entry level absent from imports.” | 17592186701792 supplies the identity and values. |
| 17592186059672, repeated sine double-angle | “Easy support is deliberately added in the prompt” | 17592186696011 supplies the identity and both substitutions in order. |
| 17592186059666, exact cosine values | Hard adds structural recognition and method selection inferred from another KP's progression. | 17592186714820 requires reverse identity recognition and then a new forward evaluation, although there are no imported hard anchors. |

This was deliberate instructional design under the previous rubric, rather than
accidental wording introduced by a renderer. All four downstream reviews accepted
the resulting generators. The repeated-angle review explicitly praised “Easy
retains both applications and exact evaluation with task-specific guidance” and
called easy “an authored scaffold.” The exact-cosine review cited the generated
investigation exemplar as support for its authored hard progression.

## Remaining structural causes

1. **Missing evidence must become a complete three-band answer.**
   `generate/pipeline/investigator.md:18` requires exactly three complete questions
   when usable coverage is incomplete. `scripts/generator_investigation.py`
   triggers construction whenever any band has no complete imported question.
   `generate/pipeline/author.md:65` still requires all three bands and says an
   unobserved imported level is not unsupported. This makes uncertainty a
   requirement to author a progression. It can be defensible, but should not
   imply that each band needs a different method or support level.

2. **The proposed examples become live authority too early.**
   `scripts/generator_pipeline.py:478` publishes the investigator's examples
   before generator authorship and independent generator review. The published
   examples are explicitly passed forward as `exemplars_file`. The author is
   told to use them, and observed reviews relied on them to justify the disputed
   progression. Import provenance is not falsely assigned, but an unreviewed
   agent's pedagogical choice still becomes the next agent's reference.

3. **The exemplar route bypasses the new pedagogy guard.**
   `Publisher.prepare_exemplars()` calls generic `validate_question()` directly;
   it does not call the delivery policies in `prepare_records()`. An offline
   reproduction with actual question 17592186714557 passes generic question
   validation and fails the ordinary delivery guard because it supplies the
   formula. Thus the current guard protects ordinary generated publication,
   while investigation exemplars can still bypass that same gate.

4. **A reviewed intended solution does not prove the skill is necessary.**
   The repeated-angle final check found mathematically correct cases in which a
   familiar intermediate angle permits stopping after one application. An
   intended two-application worked solution and successful grading did not expose
   that bypass. Review should also seek the cheapest reasonable solution using
   mastered prerequisites, then check what target work remains. Source-backed
   intentional cancellation must be distinguished from accidental bypasses.

5. **Prompt changes do not automatically change approval eligibility.**
   `scripts/generator_review.py` still declares policy version 6, also used by
   the four historical acceptances. Review bindings contain package, source,
   inventory, batch, asset and policy identities, but no prompt or pedagogical
   contract identity. Cached structured agent results can be reused. Changing
   Markdown instructions alone therefore does not require another review of an
   unchanged approved generator. Existing stored questions also remain unless a
   separate retirement/repair operation addresses them.

## Current instructions and skills

The current top-level independent-practice rule explicitly prohibits formula,
method and intermediate-result giveaways. `author.md:48` now permits the same
reasoning throughout the bands, and `principles.md:33` says extra reasoning or
fewer hints are not required. Those are appropriate corrections.

There is still a wording tension: `docs/6-template-generator.md:40` says a harder
band “should add one justified target condition,” while the higher-level rule
allows the same reasoning. The investigator also retains broad scaffolding
language. These should be reconciled under one explicit assessment contract,
rather than interpreted as separate objectives.

The author prompt alone contains 4,563 words; the reviewer contains 5,643 before
their required additional reading. Both carry many topic-specific repair rules.
Instruction overload and uneven priority are plausible contributors, not a
demonstrated substitute for the causal chain above.

No dedicated generator-design SKILL.md is directly injected by the pipeline.
`CodexSession` loads the investigator/author/reviewer Markdown prompts and agents
read the referenced principles and template documents. Across the eight archived
investigator/author traces examined, the only actual SKILL.md read was
`just-say-no-to-process-porn-and-ceremony`; it contains no instruction to scaffold
math questions. This audit does not rule out every possible skill in other jobs.

## Recommended repair order

1. Make one short assessment contract authoritative: the learner must still
   retrieve, choose and execute the target move before feedback; simplify
   incidental demands while preserving that work. Calibrated bands may overlap.
2. Keep invented exemplars provisional until independently reviewed. Identify
   authored assumptions explicitly in the handoff, and compare them to original
   target practice rather than treating their publication as justification.
3. Apply the same admission policies to exemplars and ordinary batches. Add the
   actual historical formula-giveaway examples as negative regressions for both.
4. Require an independent bypass attempt alongside the intended solution.
5. Bind approvals to an explicit pedagogical-contract revision, with targeted
   review and old-stock retirement for affected families. Avoid invalidating all
   packages for cosmetic prompt edits.
6. Consolidate duplicated/topic-specific instructions into the short contract
   plus relevant reference material. Retain the finite-capacity mechanism added
   in the preceding repair so stock quotas do not demand filler questions.

The preceding thirteen-generator repair remains live. This audit identifies why
those designs were produced and which pipeline weaknesses remain.
