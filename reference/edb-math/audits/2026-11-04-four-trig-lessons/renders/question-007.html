Find the exact value of the expression below.

cos2(7π8)−sin2(7π8)=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

22

First, let's recall the double-angle formula for cosine:

cos2x−sin2x=cos2x

Substituting x=7π8 into the above, we get

cos2(7π8)−sin2(7π8)=cos(2⋅7π8)=cos(7π4)=22.
