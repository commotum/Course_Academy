Set $x=\frac{225}{2}^{\circ}$.  $$ \begin{aligned}(\sin x-\cos x)^2 &= \sin^2 x-2\sin x\cos x+\cos^2 x \\ &=1-2\sin x\cos x \\ &=1-\sin(2x)\end{aligned} $$  $$ \begin{aligned}2\sin x\cos x &=\sin(2x) \\ &=\sin\left(225^{\circ}\right) \\ &=- \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\end{aligned} $$  Substitute this exact value:  $$ \begin{aligned}\left(\sin\left(\frac{225}{2}^{\circ}\right)-\cos\left(\frac{225}{2}^{\circ}\right)\right)^2&=1-\left(- \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) \\ &=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + 1\end{aligned} $$
