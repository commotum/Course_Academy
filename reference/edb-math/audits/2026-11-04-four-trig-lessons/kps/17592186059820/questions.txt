SOLUTION Use the difference formula for sine:  $$ \sin(u-v)=\sin u\cos v-\cos u\sin v. $$  The fixed angle has exact values  $$ \cos\left(\pi\right)=-1,\qquad \sin\left(\pi\right)=0. $$  Substitute $u=x$ and $v=\pi$, then simplify the coefficients:  $$ \begin{aligned} \sin\left(x-\pi\right)&=\sin x\cos\left(\pi\right)-\cos x\sin\left(\pi\right)\\ &=\sin x\left(-1\right)-\cos x\left(0\right)\\ &=-\sin x. \end{aligned} $$
