- [ ] $-\frac{4x}{y}$

- [ ] $\frac{x}{4y}$

- [ ] $-\frac{x}{2y}$

- [x] $-\frac{x}{4y}$

- [ ] $-\frac{4y}{x}$

Since our ellipse is centered at the origin and has semiaxes $4$ and $2,$ its equation is given by

$$
\frac{{x}^{2}}{{4}^{2}}+\frac{{y}^{2}}{{2}^{2}}=1\,⟹\,\frac{{x}^{2}}{16}+\frac{{y}^{2}}{4}=1.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(\frac{{x}^{2}}{16}+\frac{{y}^{2}}{4}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(1) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(\frac{{x}^{2}}{16})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(\frac{{y}^{2}}{4}) & =0 \\ \frac{2x}{16}+\frac{2y}{4}\cdot \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ \frac{y}{2}\cdot \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{8} \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{4y}\end{aligned}
$$
