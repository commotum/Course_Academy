Evaluate the surface integral

$$
\iint_S 1\,\mathrm{d}S
$$

where $S$ is defined by $z=2 x + 2 y$ for $1\le x\le 2$ and $0\le y\le 2$.

For a surface $S$ defined by $z=g(x,y)$ with $(x,y)\in D,$ the surface integral of a scalar function $f(x,y,z)$ over $S$ is given by

$$
{∬}_{S}f(x,y,z)\,\text{d}S={∬}_{D}f(x,y,g(x,y))\sqrt{1+{(\frac{∂z}{∂x})}^{2}+{(\frac{∂z}{∂y})}^{2}}\,\text{d}A.
$$

For the partial derivatives, we have

$$
\frac{∂z}{∂x}=\frac{∂}{∂x}(2 x + 2 y)=2,
$$

and

$$
\frac{∂z}{∂y}=\frac{∂}{∂y}(2 x + 2 y)=2.
$$

Therefore, the surface integral can be evaluated as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}{∬}_{S}1\,\text{d}S & ={∬}_{D}f(x,y,g(x,y))\sqrt{1+{(\frac{∂z}{∂x})}^{2}+{(\frac{∂z}{∂y})}^{2}}\,\text{d}A\\ & ={∬}_{D}1\sqrt{1+\left(2\right)^{2}+\left(2\right)^{2}}\,\text{d}A\\ & ={∬}_{D}3\,\text{d}A\\ & =3{∬}_{D}\,\text{d}A\\ & =3\cdot\text{Area}(D)\\ & =3\cdot\left(2-\left(1\right)\right)\cdot\left(2-\left(0\right)\right)\\ & =3\cdot\left(1\right)\cdot\left(2\right)\\ & =6.\end{aligned}
$$
