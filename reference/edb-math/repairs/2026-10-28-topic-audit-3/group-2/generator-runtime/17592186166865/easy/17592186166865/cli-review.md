Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $\frac{x}{y}$

B. $-\frac{2x}{y}$

C. $-\frac{y}{x}$

D. $-\frac{x}{y}$ **correct**

E. $-\frac{x}{2y}$

Since our circle is centered at the origin and has a radius of $1,$ its equation is given by

$$
{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}={1}^{2}\,⟹\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}=1.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(1) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({y}^{2}) & =0 \\ 2x+2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2x \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{y}\end{aligned}
$$

D. $\frac{x+4}{y-1}$

E. $-\frac{x+4}{2(y-1)}$

Since our circle is centered at $(-4,1)$ and has a radius of $1,$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-(-4))^{2}+(y-1)^{2}={1}^{2}\,⟹\,(x+4)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=1.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+4)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(1) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+4)^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y-1)^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x+4)+2(y-1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y-1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x+4) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x+4}{y-1}\end{aligned}
$$

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{5}{2}}$ **correct**

B. $-\frac{y+\frac{5}{2}}{x+\frac{7}{2}}$

C. $-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{2(y+\frac{5}{2})}$

D. $-\frac{2(x+\frac{7}{2})}{y+\frac{5}{2}}$

E. $\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{5}{2}}$

Since our circle is centered at $(- \frac{7}{2},- \frac{5}{2})$ and has a radius of $3,$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-(- \frac{7}{2}))^{2}+(y-(- \frac{5}{2}))^{2}={3}^{2}\,⟹\,(x+\frac{7}{2})^{2}+(y+\frac{5}{2})^{2}=9.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{7}{2})^{2}+(y+\frac{5}{2})^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(9) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{7}{2})^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y+\frac{5}{2})^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x+\frac{7}{2})+2(y+\frac{5}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y+\frac{5}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x+\frac{7}{2}) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{5}{2}}\end{aligned}
$$

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $\frac{x+\frac{3}{2}}{y-\frac{1}{2}}$

B. $-\frac{x+\frac{3}{2}}{2(y-\frac{1}{2})}$

C. $-\frac{2(x+\frac{3}{2})}{y-\frac{1}{2}}$

D. $-\frac{y-\frac{1}{2}}{x+\frac{3}{2}}$

E. $-\frac{x+\frac{3}{2}}{y-\frac{1}{2}}$ **correct**

Since our circle is centered at $(- \frac{3}{2},\frac{1}{2})$ and has a radius of $\frac{7}{2},$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-(- \frac{3}{2}))^{2}+(y-\frac{1}{2})^{2}={\frac{7}{2}}^{2}\,⟹\,(x+\frac{3}{2})^{2}+(y-\frac{1}{2})^{2}=\frac{49}{4}.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{3}{2})^{2}+(y-\frac{1}{2})^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(\frac{49}{4}) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{3}{2})^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y-\frac{1}{2})^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x+\frac{3}{2})+2(y-\frac{1}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y-\frac{1}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x+\frac{3}{2}) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x+\frac{3}{2}}{y-\frac{1}{2}}\end{aligned}
$$
