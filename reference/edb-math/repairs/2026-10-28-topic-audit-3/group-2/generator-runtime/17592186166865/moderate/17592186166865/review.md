Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $\frac{y}{x}$

B. $-\frac{4x}{y}$

C. $\frac{2x}{y}$

D. $-\frac{{x}^{2}}{y}$

E. $-\frac{x}{y}$ **correct**

Since our circle is centered at the origin and has a radius of $4,$ its equation is given by

$$
{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}={4}^{2}\,⟹\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}=16.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(16) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({y}^{2}) & =0 \\ 2x+2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2x \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{y}\end{aligned}
$$

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $-\frac{y+1}{x-1}$

B. $-\frac{x-1}{y+1}$ **correct**

C. $-\frac{x-1}{2(y+1)}$

D. $\frac{x-1}{y+1}$

E. $-\frac{2(x-1)}{y+1}$

Since our circle is centered at $(1,-1)$ and has a radius of $4,$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-1)^{2}+(y-(-1))^{2}={4}^{2}\,⟹\,(x-1)^{2}+(y+1)^{2}=16.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x-1)^{2}+(y+1)^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(16) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x-1)^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y+1)^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x-1)+2(y+1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y+1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x-1) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x-1}{y+1}\end{aligned}
$$

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $\frac{y}{x}$

B. $-\frac{\sqrt{2}x}{y}$

C. $\frac{2x}{y}$

D. $-\frac{{x}^{2}}{y}$

E. $-\frac{x}{y}$ **correct**

Since our circle is centered at the origin and has a radius of $\sqrt{2},$ its equation is given by

$$
{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}={\sqrt{2}}^{2}\,⟹\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}=2.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(2) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({y}^{2}) & =0 \\ 2x+2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2x \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{y}\end{aligned}
$$
