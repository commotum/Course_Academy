Review topic

Find an antiderivative.

∫2|2x|4x2−1dx

Work on a connected interval where |2x|>1.

Choose the antiderivative. In each choice, C is an arbitrary constant.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

arcsec(2x)+C

Let u=2x. Then du=(2)dx.

Work on a connected real interval where |2x|>1.

The differential is dx=12du. The outside factor is 2⋅12=1.

Including the differential factor gives

∫2|2x|4x2−1dx=∫1u2−1|u|du.

Use the integration rule:

∫1u2−1|u|du=arcsec(u)+C.

∫1u2−1|u|du=arcsec(u)+C.

Substitute back:

arcsec(2x)+C.
