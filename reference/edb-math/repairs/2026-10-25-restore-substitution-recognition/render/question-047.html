Answer · correct value:

Transform both bounds:

x=0⟹u=2x=1⟹u=4

∫014⋅32x2+2xdx=∫243udu.

Use the integration rule:

∫3udu=3uln⁡(3)+C.

Evaluate the primitive and subtract lower from upper:

[3uln⁡(3)]24=(81ln⁡(3))−(9ln⁡(3)).

The exact answer is

72ln⁡(3).
