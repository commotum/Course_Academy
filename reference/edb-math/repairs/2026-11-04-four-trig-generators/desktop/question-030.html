Review topic

Express cos⁡(x−5π3) in terms of sin⁡x and cos⁡x.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

−32sin⁡x+12cos⁡x

Use the difference formula:

cos⁡(u−v)=cos⁡ucos⁡v+sin⁡usin⁡v

With u=x and v=5π3, the exact values are cos⁡(5π3)=12 and sin⁡(5π3)=−32. Substitute and collect the terms in the requested order:

cos⁡(x−5π3)=cos⁡xcos⁡(5π3)+sin⁡xsin⁡(5π3)=cos⁡x⋅(12)+sin⁡x⋅(−32)=−32sin⁡x+12cos⁡x.
