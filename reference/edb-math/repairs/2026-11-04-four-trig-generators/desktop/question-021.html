Simplify.

sin⁡(48∘)tan⁡(24∘)(1−cos⁡(24∘))

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2(1+cos⁡(24∘))

Write s=sin⁡(24∘) and c=cos⁡(24∘). The double-angle formula gives sin⁡(48∘)=2sc.

Also, tan⁡(24∘)=sc.

Rewrite the quotient ratio and cancel. Use s2+c2=1, factor the difference of squares, then cancel the matching binomial.

2scsc(1−c)=2s2(1−c)=21−c2(1−c)=2(1−c)(1+c)(1−c)=2(1+c)=2(1+cos⁡(24∘))

All cancellations use nonzero factors in the original expression.
