Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2sin⁡(π7)

Write s=sin⁡(π7) and c=cos⁡(π7). The double-angle formula gives sin⁡(2π7)=2sc.

Expand the doubled sine and cancel the common factor.

2scc=2s=2sin⁡(π7)

All cancellations use nonzero factors in the original expression.
