Review topic

Without using a calculator, find the exact value of sec⁡(67.5∘)sin⁡(33.75∘)cos⁡(33.75∘).

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

42

Let E denote the given expression. Apply sin⁡xcos⁡x=12sin⁡(2x) at x=33.75∘ in the denominator. Use sec⁡x=1/cos⁡x, then apply the double-angle identity again at x=67.5∘. E=2sec⁡(67.5∘)sin⁡(67.5∘)=2sin⁡(67.5∘)cos⁡(67.5∘)=4sin⁡(135∘) The terminal sine is sin⁡(135∘)=22. Rationalize the denominator by multiplying numerator and denominator by 2: E=4(22)=82=42.
