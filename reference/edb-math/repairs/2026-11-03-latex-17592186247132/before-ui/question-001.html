Review topic

Find the slope of the tangent line to the curve f(x)=−7arccscx at x=5.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

725

The formula for the derivative of arccsc is

ddx(arccscx)=−1|x|x2−1.

Using the formula above, we obtain

f′(x)=ddx(−7arccscx)=−7⋅(−1|x|x2−1)=7|x|x2−1.

Therefore, the slope of the tangent line to the curve y=f(x) at x=5 is

f′(5)=7|5|52−1=755−1=725.
