Calculate f′(x) for f(x)=arctan(arcsec(x2)).

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2xx4−1(1+arcsec2(x2))

The formulae for the derivatives of arctan and arcsec are

ddx(arctanx)=11+x2,ddx(arcsecx)=1|x|x2−1.

Using the chain rule and the formulae above, we obtain

f′(x)=ddx(arctan(arcsec(x2)))=11+(arcsec(x2))2⋅ddx(arcsec(x2))=11+(arcsec(x2))2⋅1|x2|(x2)2−1⋅ddx(x2)=11+(arcsec(x2))2⋅1|x2|(x2)2−1⋅2x=2xx4−1(1+arcsec2(x2)).
