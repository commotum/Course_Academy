Source question: 4.

Use the Fundamental Theorem of Line Integrals to evaluate ∫CF⋅dr. The field F=⟨5x5x2+3y2,3y5x2+3y2⟩ is conservative with the supplied potential f=ln⁡5x2+3y2. The piecewise smooth curve joins (3,4) to (5,12).

Integral: .

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer: integral · correct value:

12ln⁡55793

5(5)2+3(12)2=557 and 5(3)2+3(4)2=93. Thus f(B)−f(A)=ln⁡557−ln⁡93. The curve is understood to lie in the field domain, excluding the origin.

Since 557 and 93 are positive, the power and quotient rules for logarithms give

ln⁡557−ln⁡93=12ln⁡557−12ln⁡93=12ln⁡(55793).
