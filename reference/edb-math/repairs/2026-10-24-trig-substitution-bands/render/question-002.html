∫−π15−π204cot⁡(−5x)csc⁡(−5x)dx

Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−8315+425

Let u=−5x. Then

dudx=−5.

The negative derivative is included in the factor outside the new integral.

Change both bounds:

x=−π15⟹u=π3x=−π20⟹u=π4

∫−π15−π204cot⁡(−5x)csc⁡(−5x)dx=−45(∫π3π4cot⁡(u)csc⁡(u)du).
