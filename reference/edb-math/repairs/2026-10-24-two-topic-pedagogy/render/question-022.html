Use the substitution u=2x2+1. Which integral is obtained?

∫0ln⁡(2)2xe2x2+1dx

Answer: answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer: answer · correct value:

12(∫11+ln⁡(4)eudu)

Let u=2x2+1. Then du=(4x)dx.

Transform both bounds:

x=0⟹u=1x=ln⁡(2)⟹u=1+ln⁡(4)

Including the differential factor gives

∫0ln⁡(2)2xe2x2+1dx=12(∫11+ln⁡(4)eudu).

The transformed integral is

12(∫11+ln⁡(4)eudu).
