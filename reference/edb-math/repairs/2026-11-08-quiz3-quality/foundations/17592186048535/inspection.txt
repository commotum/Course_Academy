
KP 17592186087734 Writing a Complex Number Shown on an Argand Diagram in Exponential Form 

QUESTION 17592186087540 e-5280
Write the complex number $z$, shown in the following Argand diagram, in exponential form.

![](images/33/335311049419928acc946721242c6e8e4096313dca1290b89b1cc7a2c30748fd.png)
SOLUTION: The complex number shown in the Argand diagram can be written as

$$
\begin{array}{rl}\displaystyle z & \displaystyle  = x + \text{i} y \\ \displaystyle  & \displaystyle  = - 3 + 2 \text{i} .\end{array}
$$

To write it in the form $z = r\textrm{e}^{\textrm{i}\theta},$ where $r=|z|$ and $\theta = \arg(z),$ we first calculate $r\mathbin{:}$
$$
\begin{array}{rl}\displaystyle r = | z | & \displaystyle  = \sqrt{ {x}^{2} + {y}^{2}} \\ \displaystyle  & \displaystyle  = \sqrt{( - 3 {)}^{2} + {2}^{2}} \\ \displaystyle  & \displaystyle  = \sqrt{13}\end{array}
$$

Now, since $z$ lies in the second quadrant, we can find the angle $\theta$ as
$$
\begin{array}{cc}\arg   ( z ) &  = \pi  - ∣ \arctan   \left( \frac{y}{x} \right) ∣ \\  &  = \pi  - ∣ \arctan   \left( \frac{2}{- 3} \right) ∣ \\  &  = \pi  - ∣ - \arctan   \left( \frac{2}{3} \right) ∣ \\  &  = \pi  - 0.588 \\  &  = 2.554\end{array}
$$

to three decimal places.

Finally, substituting $r=\sqrt{13}$ and $\theta=2.554$ into $z=re^{\textrm{i}\theta},$ we get 
$$
z = \sqrt{13}\,\textrm{e}^{2.554\,\textrm{i}}\,.
$$
