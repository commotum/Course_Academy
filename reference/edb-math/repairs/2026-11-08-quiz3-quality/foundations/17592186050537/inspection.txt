QUESTION 17592186228952 e-6572
Show that the differential operator

$$
D(y) = y\dfrac{\textrm d^2 y}{\textrm d x^2} + \dfrac{\textrm d y}{\textrm d x}
$$

is nonlinear.
SOLUTION: For any linear differential operator $L,$ we have the linearity condition

$$
L(ay_1 + by_2) = aL(y_1) + bL(y_2)
$$

for functions $y_1(x), y_2(x),$ and where $a$ and $b$ are constants. 

For the given differential operator, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}D(a{y}_{1}+b{y}_{2}) & =(a{y}_{1}+b{y}_{2})\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}(a{y}_{1}+b{y}_{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(a{y}_{1}+b{y}_{2}) \\  & =(a{y}_{1}+b{y}_{2})\left(a\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+b\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}\right)+a\frac{\text{d}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}x}+b\frac{\text{d}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}x} \\  & =+{a}^{2}{y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+ab{y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+ab{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+{b}^{2}{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}} \\  & +a\frac{\text{d}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}x}+b\frac{\text{d}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}x} \\  & ={a}^{2}{y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+{b}^{2}{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+a\frac{\text{d}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}x}+b\frac{\text{d}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}x} \\  & +ab\left({y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}\right).\end{aligned}
$$

This is *not* the same as the right-hand side, because

$$
\begin{aligned}aD({y}_{1})+bD({y}_{2}) & =a\left[{y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+\frac{\text{d}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}x}\right]+b\left[{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+\frac{\text{d}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}x}\right] \\  & =a{y}_{1}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+b{y}_{2}\frac{{\text{d}}^{2}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}{x}^{2}}+a\frac{\text{d}{y}_{1}}{\text{d}x}+b\frac{\text{d}{y}_{2}}{\text{d}x}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, since $D(ay_1+by_2)  \neq aD(y_1) + bD(y_2),$ the operator $D$ is nonlinear.
