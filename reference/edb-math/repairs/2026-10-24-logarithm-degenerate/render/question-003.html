Use a calculator to evaluate log21⁡(11) rounded to 3 decimal places.

Choose an answer

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

0.788

Let's calculate log21⁡(11) by changing the base 21 to the common logarithm (base 10).

We use the change of base formula:

loga⁡X=logb⁡Xlogb⁡a

In our case, a=21, X=11, b=10. So, we have

log21⁡(11)=log10⁡(11)log10⁡(21)=log⁡(11)log⁡(21)≈1.0413931.322219≈0.788
