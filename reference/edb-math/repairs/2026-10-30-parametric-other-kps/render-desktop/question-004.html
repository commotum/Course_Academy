Given that a curve is defined parametrically as

x=2t−3,y=t2+1,t≤1,

determine the range of x.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

x≤−1

Consider each coordinate separately.

For x:

x=2t−3

The affine coefficient is 2. The boundary t=1 gives x=−1 and is included. The coordinate is continuous on the domain and unbounded below, so every value on the stated side of the bound occurs. Thus x≤−1.

Therefore, x≤−1.
