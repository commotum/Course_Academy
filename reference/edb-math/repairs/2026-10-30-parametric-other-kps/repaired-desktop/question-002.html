Consider the curve with parametric equations

x=t+5,y=t2+t+1,−2≤t≤2.

Fill in the three blanks in the table. Give exact values.

t −2 −1 0
x 3 2 5
y 1
t 1 2
x 6
y 3 7
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

7

Answer 2 · correct value:

3

Answer 3 · correct value:

1

The missing entry is in the x-row under t=2. Substitute into x=t+5:

x(2)=(2)+5=7

The missing entry is in the y-row under t=−2. Substitute into y=t2+t+1:

y(−2)=(−2)2+(−2)+1=3

The missing entry is in the y-row under t=0. Substitute into y=t2+t+1:

y(0)=(0)2+(0)+1=1

The completed table is

t −2 −1 0
x 3 2 5
y 3 1 1
t 1 2
x 6 7
y 3 7
