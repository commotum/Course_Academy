Let u=4x. Then du=(4)dx.

The differential is dx=14du. The outside factor is 4⋅14=1.

Transform both bounds:

x=0⟹u=0x=18⟹u=12

Including the differential factor gives

∫01841−16x2dx=∫01211−u2du.

Endpoint values: arcsin⁡(0)=0,arcsin⁡(12)=π6.

Use the integration rule:

∫11−u2du=arcsin⁡(u)+C.

Evaluate the primitive and subtract lower from upper:

[arcsin⁡(u)]012=(π6)−(0).

The exact answer is

π6.
