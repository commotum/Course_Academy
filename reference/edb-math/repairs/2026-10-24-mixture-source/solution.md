We will use the following formula:

$$
\textrm{part} = \textrm{percentage} \times \textrm{total}
$$

Let $x$ be the number of tons of $55\%$ carbon steel. Since the mixture must total $8$ tons, there will be $8-x$ tons of $30\%$ carbon steel.

We organize this information in a percentage–total–part table:

| % Carbon | Total (tons) | Part Carbon (tons) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $55$ | $x$ | $0.55x$ |
| $30$ | $8-x$ | $0.30(8-x)$ |
| $40$ | $8$ | $0.40\cdot 8$ |

The parts carbon in the two starting amounts add up to the part carbon in the final mixture. Therefore,

$$
0.55x + 0.30(8-x) = 0.40\cdot 8
$$

Simplifying this equation, we get the following:

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.55x + 2.4 - 0.30x &= 3.2 \\
0.25x + 2.4 &= 3.2 \\
0.25x &= 0.8
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying both sides by $100$ allows us to solve for $x$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
25x &= 80 \\
x &= \frac{80}{25} \\
x &= 3.2
\end{aligned}
$$

The amount of $30\%$ carbon steel is $8-3.2=4.8$ tons.

Therefore, mix **$3.2$ tons of $55\%$ carbon steel and $4.8$ tons of $30\%$ carbon steel**, in the order asked.
