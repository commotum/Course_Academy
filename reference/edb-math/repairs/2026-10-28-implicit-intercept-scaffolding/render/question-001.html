The curve has equation 3x2+y2=49. The tangent line at (4,−1) meets the x-axis at P. Find the coordinates of P.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

(4912,0)

Treat y as a function of x. Differentiate implicitly and collect the derivative terms:

6x+(2y)dydx=0(2y)dydx=−(6x)dydx=−6x2y

Evaluate the tangent slope at the given point:

mt=−24−2=12

The tangent line uses m=12.

Use the given point and this slope:

y−(−1)=12(x−(4))y=12(x−(4))+(−1)

At the x-axis, y=0. Substitute and simplify:

0=12(x−(4))+(−1)x−(4)=−−112x=4912

Therefore P=(4912,0).
