Find eAt for A=[061−1].

Hint: The eigenvalues of A are −3 and 2.

eAt=15
6e2t−6e−3t
2e2t+3e−3t
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

3e2t+2e−3t

Answer 2 · correct value:

e2t−e−3t

We have a square matrix of dimension n=2. Thus,

eAt=α1At+α0I=α1[061−1]t+α0[1001]=[α06α1tα1tα0−α1t]

where α1 and α0 are functions to be determined.

Consider the polynomial r(λ)=α1λ+α0. The eigenvalues of At are −3t and 2t, both of multiplicity one. Thus, we have the following:

r(−3t)=e−3tr(2t)=e2tα0−3α1t=e−3tα0+2α1t=e2t

Now, we solve the system for α1 and α0:

  • Subtracting the first equation from the second one, we get

5α1t=e2t−e−3t⇒α1=e2t−e−3t5t.

  • Multiplying the first equation by 2 and the second by 3, then adding, we get

5α0=3e2t+2e−3t⇒α0=3e2t5+2e−3t5.

Substituting α1 and α0 into the expressions for the required entries of eAt, we get the following:

α0=3e2t5+2e−3t5=15(3e2t+2e−3t)α1t=e2t5−e−3t5=15(e2t−e−3t)

Therefore,

eAt=15[3e2t+2e−3t6e2t−6e−3te2t−e−3t2e2t+3e−3t].

The coefficient α1 at t=0 is understood by continuity; the final matrix formula holds for every real t.
