Let's denote f1(x,y)=x−0.5xy and f2(x,y)=−0.2y+2xy.

Now, we compute the partial derivatives:

∂f1∂x=1−0.5y,∂f1∂y=−0.5x,∂f2∂x=2y,∂f2∂y=−0.2+2x.

Thus, the Jacobian matrix is

J(x,y)=[1−0.5y−0.5x2y−0.2+2x].

J(x∗)=[1−0.5(2)−0.5(0.1)2(2)−0.2+2(0.1)]=[0−0.0540].

det(J(x∗)−λI)=λ2+0.2=0.
