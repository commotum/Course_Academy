Recall that the general solution of an inhomogeneous system can be written as

x(t)=xc(t)+xp(t)

where xc(t) is the complementary solution (solution of the corresponding homogeneous system) and xp(t) is a particular solution.

Notice that we are given that

xc(t)=c1[11]e−3t+c2[51]et.

We'll look for a particular solution in the form

xp(t)=Φu(t)

where Φ is a fundamental matrix for the corresponding homogeneous system, f(t) is the forcing column vector and

u(t)=∫Φ−1f(t)dt.

In our case,

Φ=[e−3t5ete−3tet],f(t)=[02t].

Since Φ is a fundamental matrix, it must be invertible. Using the formula for the inverse of a 2×2 matrix, we get the following:

Φ−1=1e−3t⋅et−5et⋅e−3t[et−5et−e−3te−3t]=−14e−2t[et−5et−e−3te−3t]=−14[e3t−5e3t−e−te−t].

Φ−1f(t)=−14[e3t−5e3t−e−te−t][02t]=[52te3t−12te−t].

Now, using our formula for u(t), we obtain

u(t)=∫Φ−1f(t)dt=∫[52te3t−12te−t]dt=[∫52te3tdt∫−12te−tdt].

Integrating the components by parts, we get the following:

  • For the first component, let u=52t and dv=e3tdt. Then, du=52dt and v=13e3t. So,

∫52te3tdt=(52t)⋅13e3t−∫13e3t⋅(52dt)=56te3t−56∫e3tdt=56te3t−518e3t=518e3t(3t−1).

∫−12te−tdt=(−12t)⋅(−e−t)−∫−e−t⋅(−12dt)=12te−t−12∫e−tdt=12te−t+12e−t=12e−t(t+1).
