Recall that the general solution of an inhomogeneous system can be written as

x(t)=xc(t)+xp(t)

where xc(t) is the complementary solution (solution of the corresponding homogeneous system) and xp(t) is a particular solution.

The forcing term

[−6t2+413t−83t2−133t+5]

of the inhomogeneous system of ODEs is polynomial of degree 2. Therefore, we assume that the particular solution is also polynomial of degree 2, i.e.

xp(t)=[a1t2+b1t+c1a2t2+b2t+c2]=[a1a2]t2+[b1b2]t+[c1c2],

where a1,a2,b1,b2,c1,c2 are constants that are to be determined.

Calculating the derivative of xp with respect to t gives

xp′(t)=[2a1t+b12a2t+b2].
