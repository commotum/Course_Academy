Recall that the general solution of an inhomogeneous system can be written as

x(t)=xc(t)+xp(t)

where xc(t) is the complementary solution (solution of the corresponding homogeneous system) and xp(t) is a particular solution.

The forcing term

[−2t+18t]

of the inhomogeneous system of ODEs is a polynomial of degree 1. Therefore, we assume that the particular solution is also a polynomial of degree 1, i.e.

xp(t)=[a1t+b1a2t+b2]=[a1a2]t+[b1b2],

where a1,a2,b1,b2 are constants that are to be determined.

Calculating the derivative of xp with respect to t gives

xp′(t)=[a1a2].

To find the unknown constants, we substitute xp and xp′ into our system:

[a1a2]=[01−2−3]([a1a2]t+[b1b2])+[−2t+18t][a1a2]=[a2−2a1−3a2]t+[b2−2b1−3b2]+[−2t+18t][0t+a10t+a2]=[(a2−2)t+(b2+1)(−2a1−3a2+8)t+(−2b1−3b2)]

Solving the first two equations, we get

a2=2,a1=1.

Substituting these into the third and fourth equations, and solving for b1 and b2, we have

b2=0,b1=−1.

Therefore, the particular solution is

xp(t)=[12]t+[−10].
