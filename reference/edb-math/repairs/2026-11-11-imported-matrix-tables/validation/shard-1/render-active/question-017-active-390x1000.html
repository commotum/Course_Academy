Recall that the general solution of an inhomogeneous system can be written as

x(t)=xc(t)+xp(t)

where xc(t) is the complementary solution (solution of the corresponding homogeneous system) and xp(t) is a particular solution.

Notice that we are given that xc(t)=c1[10]e−2t+c2[01]e−4t.

We'll look for a particular solution in the form

xp(t)=Φu(t)

where Φ is a fundamental matrix for the corresponding homogeneous system, f(t) is the forcing column vector and

u(t)=∫Φ−1f(t)dt.
