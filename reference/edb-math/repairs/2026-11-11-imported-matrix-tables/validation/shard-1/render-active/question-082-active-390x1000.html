A=[−30−44],B=[1011],y=[−13].

Given the matrices above, write the following expression as a Hadamard product. Here (⋅) denotes matrix multiplication.

(AB)⋅(By)=
4
⊙
−1
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−3

Answer 2 · correct value:

2

The Hadamard product of two compatible vectors u and v can be expressed as the matrix product

v⊙u=diag(v)u.

In our case, we have

AB=[−30−44][1011]=[−3004]

and

By=[1011][−13]=[−12].

Hence,

(AB)⋅(By)=[−3004][−12]=diag([−34])[−12]=[−34]⊙[−12]
