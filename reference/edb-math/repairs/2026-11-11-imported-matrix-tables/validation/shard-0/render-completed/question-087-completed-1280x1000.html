Given the matrices above, write AxT⊙(y+xT) as a matrix product.

AxT⊙(y+xT)=

The Hadamard product of two compatible vectors u and v can be expressed as the matrix product

v⊙u=diag(v)u.

In our case, we have

AxT=[−1−4−1−5][21]=[−6−7]

and

y+xT=[34]+[21]=[55].

Notice that they are of the same size; hence, their Hadamard product is well-defined. We conclude that

AxT⊙(y+xT)=diag(AxT)(y+xT)=[−600−7][55].
