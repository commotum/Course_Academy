The system can be rewritten in matrix form as

x′=f(x)

where x=[x(t)y(t)] and f(x)=[x2−6y5x+y2].

We need to compute the Jacobian matrix (or the total derivative) of f(x).

In our case, we have the following:

f′(x)=[∂f1∂x∂f1∂y∂f2∂x∂f2∂y]

First, we compute the partial derivatives:

∂f1∂x=∂∂x(x2−6y)=2x,∂f1∂y=∂∂y(x2−6y)=−6∂f2∂x=∂∂x(5x+y2)=5,∂f2∂y=∂∂y(5x+y2)=2y

Thus, we have

f′(x)=[2x−652y].

Next, we evaluate the Jacobian at the equilibrium x∗=[00] to get

f′(x∗)=[2(0)−652(0)]=[0−650].

Finally, near the equilibrium, the nonlinear system is approximated by

u′=[0−650]u,

where the deviation vector is

u=x−x∗=[xy]−[00]=[xy].
