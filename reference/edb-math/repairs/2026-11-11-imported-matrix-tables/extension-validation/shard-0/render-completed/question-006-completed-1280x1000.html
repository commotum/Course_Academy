The Newton-Raphson method of optimizing a multivariable function f:Rn→R is

xn+1=xn−[Hf(xn)]−1∇f(xn),

where H(xn) and ∇f(xn) are the Hessian and gradient of f, respectively, evaluated at xn.

In our case, the gradient is

∇f(x)=[∂∂x(x2y−2x2−2xy+3y2+x)∂∂y(x2y−2x2−2xy+3y2+x)]=[2xy−4x−2y+1x2−2x+6y],

and the Hessian is

Hf(x)=[∂∂x(2xy−4x−2y+1)∂∂x(x2−2x+6y)∂∂y(2xy−4x−2y+1)∂∂y(x2−2x+6y)]=[2y−42x−22x−26].

Therefore, our Newton-Raphson formula is

xn+1=xn−[2y−42x−22x−26]xn−1[2xy−4x−2y+1x2−2x+6y]xn.

Starting with x0=[10], we have

x1=x0−[2y−42x−22x−26]x0−1[2xy−4x−2y+1x2−2x+6y]x0=[10]−[2(0)−42(1)−22(1)−26]−1[2(1)(0)−4(1)−2(0)+1(1)2−2(1)+6(0)]=[10]−[−4006]−1[−3−1]=[10]−1(−4)⋅6−(0)⋅(0)[600−4][−3−1]=[10]+124[−184]≈[0.250.167].
