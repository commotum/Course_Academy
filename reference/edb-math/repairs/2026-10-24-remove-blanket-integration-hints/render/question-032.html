Review topic

Find an antiderivative.

∫4⋅3x3x+4dx

Work where 3x+4>0.

+C

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

4ln⁡(3)(2(3x+4)323)

Let u=3x+4. Then du=(3xln⁡(3))dx.

Work where 3x+4>0.

Including the differential factor gives

∫4⋅3x3x+4dx=4ln⁡(3)(∫udu).

Use the integration rule:

∫udu=2u323+C.

4ln⁡(3)(∫udu)=4ln⁡(3)(2u323)+C.

Substitute back:

4ln⁡(3)(2(3x+4)323)+C.
