Review topic

Use the substitution u=2x. Which integral is obtained?

∫π23π42cos⁡(2x)dx

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

∫π3π2cos⁡(u)du

Let u=2x. Then

dudx=2.

Transform both bounds:

x=π2⟹u=πx=3π4⟹u=3π2

Including the differential factor gives

∫π23π42cos⁡(2x)dx=∫π3π2cos⁡(u)du.

The transformed integral is

∫π3π2cos⁡(u)du.
