The graph of y=f(x) is plotted above. What are the x-coordinates of the critical points?

Solution:

false

Answer: x=2 · correct value:

true

Answer: x=5 · correct value:

false

true

The critical points of f(x) are the values of x where f′(x)=0 or f′(x) doesn't exist. Graphically, those are the values where the tangent to the graph is horizontal or where the tangent does not exist, respectively.

In the given graph, we can see that

  • at x=2 there is a sharp corner, so f′(2) doesn't exist, and

  • at x=8 there is a discontinuity, so f′(8) doesn't exist either.

Therefore, there are two critical points, and their x-coordinates are x=2 and x=8.
