Review topic

In medieval times, there were 9660 people living in a city struck by a plague. If the disease resulted in a daily decrease in the population at a continuously compounded rate of 5.2%, what was the city's population after 3 weeks? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

A scientific calculator is required to answer this question.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

3241

The continuously compounded growth equation is P=P0ert. The initial amount is P0=9660. Since the amount decreases, the signed decimal rate is r=−5.2/100=−0.052. The rate is daily, so convert the duration to days: t=3×7=21 days.

Substitute these values and round only the final amount:

P=P0ert=9660e(−0.052)(21)=9660e−1.092=9660(0.3355447…)≈3241

After 3 weeks, the population was approximately 3241 people.
