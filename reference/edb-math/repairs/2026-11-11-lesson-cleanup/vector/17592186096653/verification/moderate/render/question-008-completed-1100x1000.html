In the diagram, M lies on the segment KQ. The given vectors are OK→=e and OQ→=r. The lengths satisfy KM:MQ=5:11.

Express OM→ in terms of e and r.

Choose an answer

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OM→=1116e+516r

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OK→=eOQ→=r.

Trace the path from K through O to Q:

KQ→=KO→+OQ→=−e+r.

The ratio KM:MQ=5:11 gives 16 parts in the whole segment. Thus

KM→=516KQ→=516(−e+r).

Add along the path O→K→M and collect coefficients:

OM→=OK→+KM→=e+516(−e+r)=1116e+516r.
