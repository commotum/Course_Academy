In the diagram, F lies on the segment RZ. The given vectors are OR→=u and OZ→=e. The lengths satisfy RF:FZ=11:12.

Express OF→ in terms of u and e. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OF→= u+ e

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

1223

Answer 2 · correct value:

1123

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OR→=uOZ→=e.

Trace the path from R through O to Z:

RZ→=RO→+OZ→=−u+e.

The ratio RF:FZ=11:12 gives 23 parts in the whole segment. Thus

RF→=1123RZ→=1123(−u+e).

Add along the path O→R→F and collect coefficients:

OF→=OR→+RF→=u+1123(−u+e)=1223u+1123e.
