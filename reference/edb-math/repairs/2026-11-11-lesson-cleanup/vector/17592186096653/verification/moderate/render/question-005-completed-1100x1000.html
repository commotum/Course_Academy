In the diagram, Y lies on the segment KH. The given vectors are OK→=e and OH→=a. The lengths satisfy KY:YH=7:11.

Express OY→ in terms of e and a. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OY→= e+ a

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

1118

Answer 2 · correct value:

718

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OK→=eOH→=a.

Trace the path from K through O to H:

KH→=KO→+OH→=−e+a.

The ratio KY:YH=7:11 gives 18 parts in the whole segment. Thus

KY→=718KH→=718(−e+a).

Add along the path O→K→Y and collect coefficients:

OY→=OK→+KY→=e+718(−e+a)=1118e+718a.
