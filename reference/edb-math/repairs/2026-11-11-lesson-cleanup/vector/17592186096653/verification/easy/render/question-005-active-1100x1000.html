In the diagram, E lies on the segment YW. The given vectors are OY→=a and OW→=d. The lengths satisfy YE:EW=1:2.

Express OE→ in terms of a and d.

Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OE→=23a+13d

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OY→=aOW→=d.

Trace the path from Y through O to W:

YW→=YO→+OW→=−a+d.

The ratio YE:EW=1:2 gives 3 parts in the whole segment. Thus

YE→=13YW→=13(−a+d).

Add along the path O→Y→E and collect coefficients:

OE→=OY→+YE→=a+13(−a+d)=23a+13d.
