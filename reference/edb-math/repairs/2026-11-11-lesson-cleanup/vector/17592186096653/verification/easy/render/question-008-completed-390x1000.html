In the diagram, K lies on the segment HL. The given vectors are OH→=s and OL→=p. The lengths satisfy HK:KL=1:4.

Express OK→ in terms of s and p.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OK→=45s+15p

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OH→=sOL→=p.

Trace the path from H through O to L:

HL→=HO→+OL→=−s+p.

The ratio HK:KL=1:4 gives 5 parts in the whole segment. Thus

HK→=15HL→=15(−s+p).

Add along the path O→H→K and collect coefficients:

OK→=OH→+HK→=s+15(−s+p)=45s+15p.
