In the diagram, C lies on the segment FP. The given vectors are OF→=g and OP→=v. The lengths satisfy FC:CP=2:1.

Express OC→ in terms of g and v. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OC→= g+ v

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

13

Answer 2 · correct value:

23

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OF→=gOP→=v.

Trace the path from F through O to P:

FP→=FO→+OP→=−g+v.

The ratio FC:CP=2:1 gives 3 parts in the whole segment. Thus

FC→=23FP→=23(−g+v).

Add along the path O→F→C and collect coefficients:

OC→=OF→+FC→=g+23(−g+v)=13g+23v.
