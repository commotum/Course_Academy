In the diagram, E lies on the segment PY. The given vectors are OP→=k and OY→=d. The lengths satisfy PE:EY=5:4.

Express OE→ in terms of k and d.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OE→=49k+59d

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OP→=kOY→=d.

Trace the path from P through O to Y:

PY→=PO→+OY→=−k+d.

The ratio PE:EY=5:4 gives 9 parts in the whole segment. Thus

PE→=59PY→=59(−k+d).

Add along the path O→P→E and collect coefficients:

OE→=OP→+PE→=k+59(−k+d)=49k+59d.
