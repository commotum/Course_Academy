In the diagram, Z lies on the segment XR. The given vectors are OX→=f and OR→=u. The lengths satisfy XZ:ZR=3:4.

Express OZ→ in terms of f and u. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OZ→= f+ u

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

47

Answer 2 · correct value:

37

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OX→=fOR→=u.

Trace the path from X through O to R:

XR→=XO→+OR→=−f+u.

The ratio XZ:ZR=3:4 gives 7 parts in the whole segment. Thus

XZ→=37XR→=37(−f+u).

Add along the path O→X→Z and collect coefficients:

OZ→=OX→+XZ→=f+37(−f+u)=47f+37u.
