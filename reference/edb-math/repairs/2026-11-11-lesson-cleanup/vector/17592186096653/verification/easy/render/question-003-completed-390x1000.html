In the diagram, L lies on the segment DV. The given vectors are OD→=g and OV→=r. The lengths satisfy DL:LV=1:5.

Express OL→ in terms of g and r.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OL→=56g+16r

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OD→=gOV→=r.

Trace the path from D through O to V:

DV→=DO→+OV→=−g+r.

The ratio DL:LV=1:5 gives 6 parts in the whole segment. Thus

DL→=16DV→=16(−g+r).

Add along the path O→D→L and collect coefficients:

OL→=OD→+DL→=g+16(−g+r)=56g+16r.
