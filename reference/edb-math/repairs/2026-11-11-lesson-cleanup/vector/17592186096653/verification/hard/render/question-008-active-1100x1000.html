In the diagram, S lies on the segment ZF. The given vectors are ZO→=e and FO→=d. The lengths satisfy ZS:SF=5:1.

Express SO→ in terms of e and d. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

SO→= e+ d

Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

16

Answer 2 · correct value:

56

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OZ→=−eOF→=−d.

Trace the path from Z through O to F:

ZF→=ZO→+OF→=e−d.

The ratio ZS:SF=5:1 gives 6 parts in the whole segment. Thus

ZS→=56ZF→=56(e−d).

Add along the path O→Z→S and collect coefficients:

OS→=OZ→+ZS→=−e+56(e−d)=−16e−56d.

The requested vector has the reverse direction, so negate both coefficients:

SO→=−OS→=16e+56d.
