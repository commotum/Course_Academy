In the diagram, Z lies on the segment BP. The given vectors are BO→=k and OP→=f. The lengths satisfy BZ:ZP=1:3.

Express OZ→ in terms of k and f. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OZ→= k+ f

Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−34

Answer 2 · correct value:

14

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OB→=−kOP→=f.

Trace the path from B through O to P:

BP→=BO→+OP→=k+f.

The ratio BZ:ZP=1:3 gives 4 parts in the whole segment. Thus

BZ→=14BP→=14(k+f).

Add along the path O→B→Z and collect coefficients:

OZ→=OB→+BZ→=−k+14(k+f)=−34k+14f.
