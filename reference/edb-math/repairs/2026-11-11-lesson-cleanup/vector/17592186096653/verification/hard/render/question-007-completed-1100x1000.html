In the diagram, T lies on the segment WD. The given vectors are WO→=c and DO→=r. The lengths satisfy WT:TD=3:5.

Express OT→ in terms of c and r. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OT→= c+ r

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−58

Answer 2 · correct value:

−38

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OW→=−cOD→=−r.

Trace the path from W through O to D:

WD→=WO→+OD→=c−r.

The ratio WT:TD=3:5 gives 8 parts in the whole segment. Thus

WT→=38WD→=38(c−r).

Add along the path O→W→T and collect coefficients:

OT→=OW→+WT→=−c+38(c−r)=−58c−38r.
