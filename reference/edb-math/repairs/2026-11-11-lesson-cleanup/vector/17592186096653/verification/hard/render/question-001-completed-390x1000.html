In the diagram, Z lies on the segment GN. The given vectors are OG→=h and ON→=d. The lengths satisfy GZ:ZN=4:5.

Express ZO→ in terms of h and d. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

ZO→= h+ d

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−59

Answer 2 · correct value:

−49

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OG→=hON→=d.

Trace the path from G through O to N:

GN→=GO→+ON→=−h+d.

The ratio GZ:ZN=4:5 gives 9 parts in the whole segment. Thus

GZ→=49GN→=49(−h+d).

Add along the path O→G→Z and collect coefficients:

OZ→=OG→+GZ→=h+49(−h+d)=59h+49d.

The requested vector has the reverse direction, so negate both coefficients:

ZO→=−OZ→=−59h−49d.
