In the diagram, P lies on the segment LV. The given vectors are LO→=k and OV→=h. The lengths satisfy LP:PV=4:3.

Express PO→ in terms of k and h. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

PO→= k+ h

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

37

Answer 2 · correct value:

−47

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OL→=−kOV→=h.

Trace the path from L through O to V:

LV→=LO→+OV→=k+h.

The ratio LP:PV=4:3 gives 7 parts in the whole segment. Thus

LP→=47LV→=47(k+h).

Add along the path O→L→P and collect coefficients:

OP→=OL→+LP→=−k+47(k+h)=−37k+47h.

The requested vector has the reverse direction, so negate both coefficients:

PO→=−OP→=37k−47h.
