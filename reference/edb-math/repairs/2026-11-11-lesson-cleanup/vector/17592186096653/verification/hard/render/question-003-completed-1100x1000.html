In the diagram, T lies on the segment LZ. The given vectors are OL→=a and ZO→=d. The lengths satisfy LT:TZ=1:4.

Express TO→ in terms of a and d. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

TO→= a+ d

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−45

Answer 2 · correct value:

15

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OL→=aOZ→=−d.

Trace the path from L through O to Z:

LZ→=LO→+OZ→=−a−d.

The ratio LT:TZ=1:4 gives 5 parts in the whole segment. Thus

LT→=15LZ→=15(−a−d).

Add along the path O→L→T and collect coefficients:

OT→=OL→+LT→=a+15(−a−d)=45a−15d.

The requested vector has the reverse direction, so negate both coefficients:

TO→=−OT→=−45a+15d.
