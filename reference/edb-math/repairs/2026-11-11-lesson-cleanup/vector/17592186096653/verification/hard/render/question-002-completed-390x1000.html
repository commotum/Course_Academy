In the diagram, P lies on the segment HR. The given vectors are OH→=r and RO→=a. The lengths satisfy HP:PR=1:1.

Express PO→ in terms of r and a. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

PO→= r+ a

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−12

Answer 2 · correct value:

12

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OH→=rOR→=−a.

Trace the path from H through O to R:

HR→=HO→+OR→=−r−a.

The ratio HP:PR=1:1 gives 2 parts in the whole segment. Thus

HP→=12HR→=12(−r−a).

Add along the path O→H→P and collect coefficients:

OP→=OH→+HP→=r+12(−r−a)=12r−12a.

The requested vector has the reverse direction, so negate both coefficients:

PO→=−OP→=−12r+12a.
