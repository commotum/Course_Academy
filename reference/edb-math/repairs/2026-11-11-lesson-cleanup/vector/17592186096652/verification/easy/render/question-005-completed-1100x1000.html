The vectors are drawn on a square grid: OA→=a, OB→=b, and OC→=c.

Given that c=λa+μb, find λ and μ.

λ=

μ=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

2

Answer 2 · correct value:

4

Construct the route O→X→C, first parallel to a and then parallel to b.

Along OX, move 2 squares left and 4 squares up. A copy of a moves 1 square left and 2 squares up. Thus OX=2|a| and the segment points in the same direction to a. Hence OX→=2a.

Along XC, move 4 squares up. A copy of b moves 1 square up. Thus XC=4|b| and the segment points in the same direction to b. Hence XC→=4b.

c=OX→+XC→=2a+4b

Since a and b are not parallel, their coefficients are unique. Matching coefficients gives λ=2 and μ=4.
