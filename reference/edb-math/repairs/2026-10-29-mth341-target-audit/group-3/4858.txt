SOLUTION: First, we recall the following properties of the cross product:

- The cross product distributes over addition/subtraction:

$$
\begin{aligned}(a\pm b)\times c & =a\times c\pm b\times c \\ c\times (a\pm b) & =c\times a\pm c\times b\end{aligned}
$$

- The cross product is associative with respect to scalar multiplication:

$$
\begin{aligned}(\lambda a)\times b & =\lambda (a\times b) \\ a\times (\lambda b) & =\lambda (a\times b)\end{aligned}
$$

With that in mind, let's evaluate our expression:

- First, we use distributive property:

$$
\begin{aligned}(-2a+5b)\times c & =(-2a)\times c+(5b)\times c\end{aligned}
$$

- Next, we factor out the scalars:

$$
\begin{aligned}(-2a)\times c+(5b)\times c & =-2(a\times c)+5(b\times c)\end{aligned}
$$

- Finally, we substitute the given values for $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{c}$ and $\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c},$ and simplify:

$$
\begin{aligned}-2(a\times c)+5(b\times c) & =-2\cdot (i)+5\cdot (-3i+j) \\  & =-2i-15i+5j \\  & =-17i+5j\end{aligned}
$$
