  • For x(t)=3ln(t+1), we have

x′(t)=3t+1x″(t)=−3(t+1)2.

  • For y(t)=t, we have

y′(t)=12ty″(t)=−14t3.

So, we conclude that

r″(t)=⟨x″(t),y″(t)⟩=⟨−3(t+1)2,−14t3⟩.
