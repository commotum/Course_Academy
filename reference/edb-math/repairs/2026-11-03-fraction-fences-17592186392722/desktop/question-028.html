Find the derivative of f(t)=⟨cos2t,t2+1,(t+2)2⟩ at t=π.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

⟨0,ππ2+1,2(π+2)⟩

First, we find f′(t) by differentiating each component:

f′(t)=⟨ddt(cos2t),ddt(t2+1),ddt((t+2)2)⟩=⟨−2sin2t,tt2+1,2(t+2)⟩

Now, to compute f′(π) we substitute t=π into the expression for f′(t):

f′(π)=⟨−2sin2π,ππ2+1,2(π+2)⟩=⟨0,ππ2+1,2(π+2)⟩
