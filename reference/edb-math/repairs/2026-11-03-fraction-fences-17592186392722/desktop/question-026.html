Calculate the derivative of f(t)=⟨1t,ln(t+1),t3⟩ with respect to t.

Answer · correct value:

f′(t)=⟨ddt(1t),ddt(ln(t+1)),ddt(t3)⟩=⟨−1t2,1t+1,3t2⟩
