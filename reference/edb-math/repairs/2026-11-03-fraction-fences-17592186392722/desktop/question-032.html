Review topic

Find the derivative of f(t)=⟨sin⁡t,cos⁡t,t⟩ at t=π4.

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

First, we find f′(t) by differentiating each component:

f′(t)=⟨ddt(sint),ddt(cost),ddt(t)⟩=⟨cost,−sint,1⟩

Now, to compute f′(π4), we substitute t=π4 into the expression for f′(t):

f′(π4)=⟨cos(π4),−sin(π4),1⟩=⟨22,−22,1⟩
