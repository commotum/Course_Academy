Given that r(t)=⟨t6,e2te2t+2⟩, what is r′(t)?

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

⟨6t5,4e2t(e2t+2)2⟩

For the x-component, use the power rule.

x(t)=t6x′(t)=6t5

For the y-component, use the quotient and chain rules.

y(t)=e2te2t+2y′(t)=(2e2t)(e2t+2)−(e2t)(2e2t)(e2t+2)2=4e2t(e2t+2)2

Retain the coordinate order:

r′(t)=⟨6t5,4e2t(e2t+2)2⟩
