Review topic

The area of the quadrilateral BCDA is 5cm2. The sides BC=9cm and BA=4cm meet at 42∘. What is the area of △CDA, rounded to the nearest integer?

You may use a scientific calculator.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

7cm2

First calculate the area of the enclosing triangle BCA. Use the two given sides and their included angle, with the calculator in degree mode.

ABCA=12⋅BC⋅BA⋅sin⁡∠CBA=12⋅9⋅4⋅sin⁡42∘≈12.044351cm2.

The enclosing triangle consists of the quadrilateral BCDA and the missing triangle CDA. Subtract the quadrilateral area:

ACDA=ABCA−ABCDA=18sin⁡42∘−5≈7.044351cm2.

Keep full precision until this subtraction is complete. Rounded to the nearest integer, the area is 7cm2.
