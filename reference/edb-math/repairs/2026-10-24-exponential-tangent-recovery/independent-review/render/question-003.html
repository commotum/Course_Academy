Find the equation of the tangent to the curve y=3x+1 at the point where x=0.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

y=3xln⁡(3)+3

First, we express y as the composite function

y=3u,u=x+1.

Differentiating gives

dydu=3uln⁡(3),dudx=1.

We now apply the chain rule and get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=3uln⁡(3)⋅(1)=(1)⋅3uln⁡(3)=1⋅3x+1ln⁡(3).

Finally, to find the slope m of the tangent to the curve at x=0, we have to evaluate dydx at that point as follows:

m=dydx|x=0=(1)⋅3(0)+1ln⁡(3)=1⋅31ln⁡(3)=3ln⁡(3).

Therefore, the slope of the tangent to the curve at the point where x=0 is 3ln⁡(3).

Also, the y-value of the point on the curve where x=0 is

y1=31=3.

Finally, we find the equation of the tangent at that point:

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−(3)=(3ln⁡(3))(x−(0))y=3xln⁡(3)+3.
