It accepts only banks with two slopes of one sign and three of the other. All five resulting tangent lines pass through the same point and have different slopes. Before bank rejection, the normal sampler chooses uniformly among valid coefficients for a fixed base, point and evaluated exponent. Every five-member bank containing a given coefficient is sampled with probability `1 / choose(N-1, 4)`. Rejection depends only on the complete bank's sign split, and ordinary generation resamples both the target and the bank. Thus conditional on an accepted bank, each of its five coefficients is equally eligible to be the target. Accepted target marginals can differ in asymmetric sign pools; they are not forced to be uniform. The method does not designate a median, singleton, majority-sign option or repeated coefficient as correct.
