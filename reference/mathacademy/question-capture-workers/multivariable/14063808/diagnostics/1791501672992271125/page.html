The second-degree Taylor polynomial of f(x)=tan⁡(x) about x=π4 is

a
1+(x−π4)−(x−π4)2
b
1+4(x−π4)+4(x−π4)2
c
1+2(x−π4)+2(x−π4)2
d
1+4(x−π4)+(x−π4)2
e
1+(x−π4)+4(x−π4)2
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find the second-degree Taylor polynomial about x=π4, we plug a=π4 into the general formula to give

P2(x)=f(π4)+f′(π4)(x−π4)+12f″(π4)(x−π4)2.

So, we need to work out f(π4), f′(π4), and f″(π4):

f(x)=tan⁡(x)⟹f(π4)=1f′(x)=1cos2⁡(x)⟹f′(π4)=2f″(x)=2sin⁡(x)cos3⁡(x)⟹f″(π4)=4

We now plug the results for f(π4), f′(π4), and f″(π4) into our second-degree Taylor polynomial. This gives

P2(x)=1+2(x−π4)+4⋅12(x−π4)2=1+2(x−π4)+2(x−π4)2.
