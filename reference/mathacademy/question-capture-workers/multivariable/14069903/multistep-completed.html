Question 1 ?
The divergence divV of the velocity field represents the scalar rate of expansion of the fluid per unit of area at a given point. Find the corresponding expansion rate at the point (1,1).
The required rate of expansion is divV|(1,1)=
4
Submit
Correct
Explanation

If V=Pi+Qj is a vector field on R2 and the partial derivatives of the components of V all exist, then the divergence of V is given by

∇⋅V=divV=∂P∂x+∂Q∂y.

Therefore, we have

divV=∂P∂x+∂Q∂y=∂∂x(x2y)+∂∂y(2xy)=2xy+2x.

Finally, we evaluate divV at the point (1,1), and obtain divV|(1,1)=2(1)(1)+2(1)=4.

Explanation

The linear approximation of the function f at the point (a,b) is given by f(x,y)≈f(a,b)+∇f(a,b)⋅⟨x−a,y−b⟩.

So, the linear approximation of f at the point (1,1) is f(x,y)≈f(1,1)+∇f(1,1)⋅⟨x−1,y−1⟩.

∇f(1,1)=∇(divV)|(1,1)=⟨4,2⟩.
