Identify the surface 4x2+y2−25z2+16x+50z+91=0.

a
A hyperboloid of one sheet centered at (2,0,−1)
b
A hyperboloid of two sheets centered at (−2,0,1)
c
A hyperboloid of one sheet centered at (−2,0,1)
d
A hyperboloid of two sheets centered at (1,0,5)
e
An elliptic cone centered at (−2,0,1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we complete the square in the x and z terms:

4x2+y2−25z2+16x+50z+91=04(x2+4x)+y2−25(z2−2z)+91=04[(x+2)2−4]+y2−25[(z−1)2−1]+91=04(x+2)2−16+y2−25(z−1)2+25+91=04(x+2)2+y2−25(z−1)2+100=0

Now, by rearranging, we get the equation

4(x+2)2+y2−25(z−1)2+100=04(x+2)2+y2−25(z−1)2=−100(x+2)225+y2100−(z−1)24=−1.

This is the equation of a hyperboloid of two sheets centered at (−2,0,1).

Identify the surface z29+y2−x2=−1.

a
A hyperboloid of one sheet whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis
b
A hyperboloid of two sheets whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis
c
A hyperbolic paraboloid whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis
d
A hyperboloid of one sheet whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis
e
A hyperboloid of two sheets whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the equation of a hyperboloid of two sheets centered at O whose axis of symmetry is the z-axis is given by

x2a2+y2b2−z2c2=−1.

Thus, a hyperboloid of two sheets centered at O whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis is given by

−x2a2+y2b2+z2c2=−1.

Therefore, our equation is a hyperboloid of two sheets whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis and a=b=1,c=3.
