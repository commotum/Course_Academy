Newton’s method can be extremely fast, but only under the following conditions:

  • The function is twice continuously differentiable (i.e., smooth).

  • The starting point is close to a critical point.

  • The Hessian is invertible and preferably positive definite near the solution.

When these conditions are met, Newton’s method often converges in just a few iterations.
