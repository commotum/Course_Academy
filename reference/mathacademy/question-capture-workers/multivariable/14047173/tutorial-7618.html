This gives us a fresh perspective of the meaning behind the usual formulas:

  • Firstly, they describe a transformation that maps a region in the plane (often a rectangle) to another region in the -plane.

  • Similarly, the inverse transformation (which always exists) maps a region in the -plane to a region in the plane.

This idea becomes important later on. But, broadly speaking, many problems become easier after transforming them to another coordinate system where the domain under consideration is rectangular.
