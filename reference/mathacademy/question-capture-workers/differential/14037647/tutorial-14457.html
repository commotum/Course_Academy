Finding a Particular Solution
?

We've discussed how to find the complementary solution of a first-order inhomogeneous ODE

We will now turn our attention to finding a particular solution.

The particular solution of a linear ODE captures the part of the solution that arises directly from the forcing term
In other words, if we substitute into the left-hand side of the equation, it should produce the forcing function

For equations with constant coefficients, we can often find by looking at the type of function appearing on the right-hand side and then guessing a form that matches it. This is sometimes called the method of undetermined coefficients.

For example, if the forcing function is a polynomial of degree we guess a particular solution that is also a polynomial of degree After making this guess, we substitute it into the differential equation and solve for the unknown constants.
