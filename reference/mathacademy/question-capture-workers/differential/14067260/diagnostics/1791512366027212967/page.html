A particle moves along a straight line relative to a fixed origin O with acceleration a(t)=2e2t, where t≥0 is the time. If the particle has position x=e2 and velocity v=e2 when t=1, calculate the position x of the particle at time t>0.

a
x(t)=2e2t+e2
b
x(t)=2e2t+te2+12e2
c
x(t)=e2t+te2
d
x(t)=12e2t+12e2
e
x(t)=12e2t+te2+e2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We start by integrating the acceleration to get the velocity v(t):

v(t)=∫a(t)dt=∫2e2tdt=e2t+C

To determine C, we use the fact that v(1)=e2. Substituting this into the above gives

e2=e2(1)+Ce2=e2+CC=0.

So the velocity is given by v(t)=e2t.

Now, we integrate the velocity to get x(t):

x(t)=∫v(t)dt=∫e2tdt=12e2t+K

To determine K, we use the fact that x(1)=e2. Substituting this into the above gives

e2=12e2(1)+Ke2=12e2+KK=12e2.

Therefore, the position x of the particle at time t is x(t)=12e2t+12e2.
