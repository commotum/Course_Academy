Another technique for solving homogeneous differential equations is to write the solution as a general power series and find a recurrence relation between the coefficients of the series. This technique involves the following steps:

  • Step 1: Write the solution as a general power series

  • Step 2: Compute any derivatives ( etc.) so that we can substitute the general series solution into the differential equation.

  • Step 3: Rewrite all the series in the equation so that they use the same index of summation, the powers of are the same, and the indices start at the same value.

  • Step 4: Consolidate all the series into a single series that is equal to

  • Step 5: Set the coefficients of the series equal to and solve for the recurrence relation.
