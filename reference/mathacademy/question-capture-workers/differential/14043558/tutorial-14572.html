Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RL circuit, there are two voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality

  • The voltage drop across the inductor is proportional to the rate of change of current, with a constant of proportionality
