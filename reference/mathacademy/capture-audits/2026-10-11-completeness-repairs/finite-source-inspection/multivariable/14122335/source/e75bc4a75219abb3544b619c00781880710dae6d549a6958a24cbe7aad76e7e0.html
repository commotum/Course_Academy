EXPLANATION

If X1,X2,…,Xn is a random sample of size n from a normal population, where Xi∼N(μ,σ2),1≤i≤n, then the sample mean X― has the distribution X―∼N(μ,σ2n).

Therefore, the distribution of the sample mean X― is X―∼N(4,18236)∼N(4,9).

We're required to find P(|X―|<2.07).

First, we transform X into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring: P(|X―|<2.07)=P(−2.07<X―<2.07)=P(−2.07−49<Z<2.07−49)=P(−2.02<Z<−0.64)=Φ(−0.64)−Φ(−2.02)

From the tables, we have Φ(−0.64)=0.2611,Φ(−2.02)=0.0217.

Therefore, P(|X―|<2.07)=Φ(−0.64)−Φ(−2.02)=0.2611−0.0217=0.2394.
