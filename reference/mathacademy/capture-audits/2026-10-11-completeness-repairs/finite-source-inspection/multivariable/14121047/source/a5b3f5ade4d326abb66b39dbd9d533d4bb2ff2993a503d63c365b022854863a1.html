EXPLANATION

To check whether f is even or odd, we compare f(−x) and f(x):

f(−x)=−5(−x)4+2(−x)2=−5x4+2x2=f(x). Since f(−x)=f(x), we have that f is even.

For the Fourier series on [−π,π], the sine coefficients are given by bn=1π∫−ππf(x)sin⁡(nx)dx.

Here, f(x) is even and sin⁡(nx) is odd, so f(x)sin⁡(nx) is odd.

Now, the integral of an odd function over [−π,π] is 0. Thus, bn=0 for all n≥1.

Therefore, the function f(x) is even and the sine terms in its Fourier series must be zero.
