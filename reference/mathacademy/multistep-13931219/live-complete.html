Explanation

The factor theorem states that (x−r) is a factor of a polynomial p(x) if and only if the root x=r is also a root of p(x).

Here, we consider the numbers 5, 10, and 15 as possible roots. Let's check each of these values in turn.

  • x=5 is not a root of p(x):

f(5)=(5)3+(5)2−3600=125+25−3600=−3450≠0

  • x=10 is not a root of p(x):

f(10)=(10)3+(10)2−3600=1000+100−3600=−2500≠0

  • x=15 is a root of p(x):

f(15)=(15)3+(15)2−3600=3375+225−3600=3600−3600=0

Therefore, by the factor theorem, (x−15) is a factor of p(x).
