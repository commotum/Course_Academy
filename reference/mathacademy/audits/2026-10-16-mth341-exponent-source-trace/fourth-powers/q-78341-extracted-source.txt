Problem:
If $A=[\begin{aligned}1 & 1 \\ -1 & -1\end{aligned}]$, then ${A}^{4}=$

Source worked solution:
Computing ${A}^{2},$ we have

$$
\begin{aligned}{A}^{2} & =A\cdot A \\  & =[\begin{aligned}1 & 1 \\ -1 & -1\end{aligned}]\cdot [\begin{aligned}1 & 1 \\ -1 & -1\end{aligned}] \\  & =[\begin{aligned}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{aligned}] \\  & =O.\end{aligned}
$$

Notice that, since ${A}^{2}=O,$ the matrix $A$ is a nilpotent matrix of index $2.$ 

We can use this fact to compute ${A}^{4}$ quickly, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}{A}^{2} & ={A}^{2}\cdot {A}^{2} \\  & =O\cdot O \\  & =O\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, 

$$
{A}^{4}=[\begin{aligned}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{aligned}].
$$
