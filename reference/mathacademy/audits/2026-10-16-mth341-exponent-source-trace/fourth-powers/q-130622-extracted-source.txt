Problem:
If $R=[\begin{aligned}-15 & 25 \\ -9 & 15\end{aligned}]$, then ${R}^{4}=$

Source worked solution:
Computing ${R}^{2},$ we have

$$
\begin{aligned}{R}^{2} & =R\cdot R \\  & =[\begin{aligned}-15 & 25 \\ -9 & 15\end{aligned}]\cdot [\begin{aligned}-15 & 25 \\ -9 & 15\end{aligned}] \\  & =[\begin{aligned}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{aligned}] \\  & =O.\end{aligned}
$$

Notice that, since ${R}^{2}=O,$ the matrix $R$ is a nilpotent matrix of index $2.$ 

We can use this fact to compute ${R}^{4}$ quickly, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}{R}^{2} & ={R}^{2}\cdot {R}^{2} \\  & =O\cdot O \\  & =O\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, 

$$
{R}^{4}=[\begin{aligned}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{aligned}].
$$
