An integer a has the multiplicative inverse modulo n if and only if a and n are coprime, i.e., gcd(a,n)=1.

In our case, a=11 and n=35. So, we have

gcd(11,35)=1.

Therefore, we conclude the following:

The integer 11 has the multiplicative inverse modulo 35 since gcd(11,35) =1.
