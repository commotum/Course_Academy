Which of the following integers has a multiplicative inverse modulo 30?

  1. 7
  2. 15
  3. 21
a
I only
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
I and III only
e
I, II, and III
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

An integer a has a multiplicative inverse modulo n if and only if a and n are coprime, i.e., gcd(a,n)=1.

With this in mind, let's examine the given options.

  • 7 has a multiplicative inverse modulo 30 since gcd(7,30)=1.✓

  • 15 does not have a multiplicative inverse modulo 30 since gcd(15,30)=15≠1.×

  • 21 does not have a multiplicative inverse modulo 30 since gcd(21,30)=3≠1.×

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
