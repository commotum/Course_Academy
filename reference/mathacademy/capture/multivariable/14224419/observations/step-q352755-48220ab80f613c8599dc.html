Suppose that S=∑n=1∞(−2)n(n+1)! and that s5 is the fifth partial sum of S. According to the alternating series error bound, |S−s5|<k for some real number k. Find the value of k. Do not round the answer.
k=
4315​
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let an=2n(n+1)! and bn=(−2)n(n+1)!. Then an is positive and decreasing for n≥1, and an→0 as n→∞. So, the series is convergent by the alternating series test.

Because our series is a convergent alternating series, the alternating series error bound applies. In particular, for the 5th partial sum, it states that

|S−s5|<|b6|=|(−2)6(6+1)!|=645040=4315.

Therefore, k=4315.
