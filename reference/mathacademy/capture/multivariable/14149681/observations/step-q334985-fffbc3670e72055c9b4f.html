The Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model is given by {x′=ax−bxyy′=−cy+dxy where a,b,c,d are positive real constants.

First, recall that a derivative represents a rate of change.

For the x′ equation, we have x′=2.5x−0.5xy.

Therefore, x must represent the prey. Indeed,

  • it grows exponentially in the absence of predators (the 2.5x term), and

  • decreases when interacting with predators (the −0.5xy term).
