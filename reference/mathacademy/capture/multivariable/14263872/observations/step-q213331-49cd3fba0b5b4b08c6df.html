EXPLANATION

Let b=325a+101. Note that b is positive. Now, let d4, d3, d2, and d1 be the last four digits of the number a:

a=…d4d3d2d1

Then, a can be written in expanded form as

a=⋯+1000d4+100d3+10d2+d1=100(⋯+10d4+d3)+10d2+d1.

Now, we compute the residue of a modulo 100:

a≡100(⋯+10d4+d3)+10d2+d1(mod100)≡0⋅(⋯+10d4+d3)+10d2+d1(mod100)≡0+10d2+d1(mod100)≡10d2+d1(mod100)

Notice that 10d2+d1 is the two-digit number consisting of the last two digits of a.

Finally, since b=325a+101, and 325≡25,101≡1(mod100) we have the following: b≡10d2+d1(mod100)325a+101≡10d2+d1(mod100)25a+1≡10d2+d1(mod100)

Therefore, the expression that gives the last digit of 325a+101 is the following:
