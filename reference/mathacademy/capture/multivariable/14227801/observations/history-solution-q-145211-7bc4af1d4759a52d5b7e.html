y′(θ0)≠0andx′(θ0)=0.

However, if the curve is continuous at θ0 yet the derivative takes the indeterminate form 00 at this point, we say that the curve has a vertical tangent at this point if

limθ→θ0−dydx=±∞andlimθ→θ0+dydx=±∞.
