EXPLANATION

The formula for the derivative of the inverse hyperbolic tangent function is ddx(artanh⁡x)=11−x2.

Using the formula above with the chain rule, we get

dydx=ddx(2artanh⁡(6x))=2⋅ddx(artanh⁡(6x))=2⋅11−(6x)2⋅(6x)′=2⋅11−6x2⋅6=231−6x2.
