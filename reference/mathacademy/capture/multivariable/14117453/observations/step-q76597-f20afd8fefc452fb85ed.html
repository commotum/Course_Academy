If u(t)=[et0] and v(t)=[0e2t] are solutions to x′(t)=Ax(t), find Aw(t) where w(t)=[9et2e2t].

Aw=[9et4e2t]

Aw=[9et2e2t]

Aw=[9e2t4et]

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since u(t) and v(t) are solutions to x′(t)=Ax(t), the superposition principle guarantees that cu(t)+dv(t) is also a solution to x′(t)=Ax(t) for all c,d∈R.

Notice that w(t)=9u(t)+2v(t).

Therefore, w is a solution to x′(t)=Ax(t). So, in order to find Aw(t) we just need to find w′(t) as follows: Aw=w′(t)=[(9et)′(2e2t)′]=[9et4e2t].
