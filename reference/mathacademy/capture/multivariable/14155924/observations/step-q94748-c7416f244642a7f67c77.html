Let f(x,y)=yx2, where x=ts and y=s−t. Calculate ∂f∂t.

a
st2(s−2t)

ts2(2s−3t)

Correct
EXPLANATION

The chain rule for a multivariable function f(x,y) with two independent variables s and t, where x=x(s,t) and y=y(s,t), can be expressed as follows: ∂f∂t=∂f∂x⋅∂x∂t+∂f∂y⋅∂y∂t

First, we find the partial derivatives of f with respect to x and y: ∂f∂x=2xy,∂f∂y=x2

Writing the above results in terms of s and t, we get ∂f∂x=2(ts)(s−t)=2ts2−2st2,∂f∂y=(ts)2=t2s2.

Next, we take the derivatives of x and y with respect to t, and we get ∂x∂t=s,∂y∂t=−1.

Finally, substituting all of our derivatives into the multivariable chain rule, we get ∂f∂t=∂f∂x⋅∂x∂t+∂f∂y⋅∂y∂t=(2ts2−2st2)(s)+(t2s2)(−1)=2ts3−2s2t2−t2s2=2ts3−3t2s2=ts2(2s−3t).
