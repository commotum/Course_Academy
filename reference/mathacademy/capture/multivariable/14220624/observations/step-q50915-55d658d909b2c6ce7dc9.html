EXPLANATION

The first thing we notice is that we can rewrite the terms of the series as powers of 17:

1+17+149+⋯+17n+⋯=1+17+172+⋯+17n+⋯=1+17+(17)2+⋯+(17)n+⋯

Then, we recognize that the series looks similar to the Maclaurin expansion for f(x)=11−x.

11−x=1+x+x2+x3+⋯+xn+⋯

Substituting x=17 into our Maclaurin expansion gives

1(1−17)=1+17+(17)2+(17)3+⋯+(17)n+⋯76=1+17+149+1343+⋯+17n+⋯.

Therefore, the sum of the series is 76.
