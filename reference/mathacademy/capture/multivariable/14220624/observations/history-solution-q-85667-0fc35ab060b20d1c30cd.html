The first thing we notice is that we can rewrite the terms of the series as powers of e4: 1−e4+e216−⋯+(−1)nen4n+⋯=1−e4+e242+⋯+(−1)nen4n+⋯=1−e4+(e4)2+⋯+(−1)n(e4)n+⋯

Then, we recognize that the series looks similar to the Maclaurin expansion for f(x)=11+x.

11+x=1−x+x2−x3+⋯+(−1)nxn+⋯

Substituting x=e4 into our Maclaurin expansion gives

1(1+e4)=1−e4+(e4)2−(e4)3+⋯+(−1)n(e4)n+⋯44+e=1−e4+e216−e364+⋯+(−1)nen4n+⋯.

Therefore, the sum of the series is 44+e.
