Find the sum S of the series

S=7−2−(7−2)22+(7−2)33−⋯+(−1)n+1(7−2)nn+⋯

Do not round the answer.

S=
ln(√7−1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that we can rewrite the given sum as follows: 7−2−(7−2)22+(7−2)33−⋯+(−1)n+1(7−2)nn+⋯=(7−2)−(7−2)22+(7−2)33−⋯+(−1)n+1(7−2)nn+⋯

Then, we recognize that the series looks similar to the Maclaurin expansion for f(x)=ln⁡(1+x). ln⁡(1+x)=x−x22+x33−⋯+(−1)n+1xnn+⋯

Substituting x=7−2 into our Maclaurin expansion gives ln⁡(1+7−2)=(7−2)−(7−2)22+(7−2)33−⋯+(−1)n+1(7−2)nn+⋯ln⁡(7−1)=7−2−(7−2)22+(7−2)33−⋯+(−1)n+1(7−2)nn+⋯.

Therefore, the sum of the series is ln⁡(7−1).
