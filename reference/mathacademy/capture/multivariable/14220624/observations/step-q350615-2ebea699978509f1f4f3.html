Find the sum of the following series. Do not round the answer.
1−π3+π218−π3162+⋯+(−π)n3nn!+⋯=
e−π3​

EXPLANATION

We recognize that the series looks similar to the Maclaurin expansion for f(x)=ex.

ex=1+x+x22!+x33!+⋯+xnn!+⋯

Substituting x=−π3 into our Maclaurin expansion gives

e−π/3=1+(−π3)+(−π3)22!+(−π3)33!+⋯+(−π3)nn!+⋯e−π/3=1−π3+π218−π3162+⋯+(−π)n3nn!+⋯.

Therefore, the sum of the series is e−π/3.
