EXPLANATION

Two negations in a row cancel each other out. So, we can simplify ¬(¬A) to A.

As a result of this, the statement simplifes to

¬[¬(¬B∨A)]

Then, we can simplify ¬(¬(¬B∨A)) to ¬B∨A.

Therefore, the given statement can be simplified to

¬B∨A.
