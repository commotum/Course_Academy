3.Identifying Subspaces of Function Vector Spaces?

Consider the vector space C[0,1] of all continuous functions f:[0,1]→R and its subset L containing all polynomials of degree exactly 1.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. L is closed under function addition
  2. L is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. L is a subspace of C[0,1]
  4. L is not a subspace of C[0,1]
