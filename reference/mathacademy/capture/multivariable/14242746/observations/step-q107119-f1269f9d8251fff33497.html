Consider the vector space M2(R) of all 2×2 matrices over R, and its subset T containing all 2×2 matrices of the form [ab0c].

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. T is closed under matrix addition
  2. T is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. T is a subspace of M2(R)
  4. T is not a subspace of M2(R)
a
I, II, and III only
b
I and IV only
c
II and IV only
d
I only
e
IV only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. If A1=[a1b10c1] and A2=[a1b10c1] are two matrices from T, then A1+A2=[a1+a2b1+b20c1+c2] is also an element of T. So, T is closed under addition.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, if A=[ab0c]∈T and α∈R, then αA=[αaαb0αc] also belongs to T. So, T is closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is true, while statement IV is false. T is a subspace because it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III only."
