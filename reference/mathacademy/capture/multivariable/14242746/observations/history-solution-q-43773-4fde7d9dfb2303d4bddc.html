Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The sum of two constant functions is a constant function. So, K is closed under addition.

  • Statement II is true. The product of a constant function by a scalar is again a constant function. So, K is closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is true, while statement IV is false. K is a subspace because it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III only."
