5.Identifying Subspaces of Matrix Vector Spaces?

Consider the vector space M2(R) of all 2×2 matrices over R, and its subset T containing all 2×2 matrices of the form [ab0c].

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. T is closed under matrix addition
  2. T is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. T is a subspace of M2(R)
  4. T is not a subspace of M2(R)
