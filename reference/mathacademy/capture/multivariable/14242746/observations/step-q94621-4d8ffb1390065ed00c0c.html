Consider the vector space M2(R) of all 2×2 matrices over R, and its subset H containing all 2×2 matrices such that the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. H is closed under matrix addition
  2. H is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. H is a subspace of M2(R)
  4. H is not a subspace of M2(R)
a
I and IV only
b
II and IV only
c
I, II, and III only
d
IV only
e
II only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. If A1 and A2 are two matrices from H such that the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0, then A1+A2 is a matrix where the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0+0=0. This matrix belongs to H. So, H is closed under addition.

  • Statement II is true. If A is an element of H such that the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0, and k∈R, then kA is a matrix such that the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0⋅k=0. This matrix belongs to H. So, H is closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is true, while statement IV is false. H is a subspace because it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III only."
