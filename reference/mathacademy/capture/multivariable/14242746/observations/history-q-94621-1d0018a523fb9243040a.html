2.Identifying Subspaces of Matrix Vector Spaces?

Consider the vector space M2(R) of all 2×2 matrices over R, and its subset H containing all 2×2 matrices such that the sum of the elements on the main diagonal is equal to 0.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. H is closed under matrix addition
  2. H is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. H is a subspace of M2(R)
  4. H is not a subspace of M2(R)
