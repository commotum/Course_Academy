∬SF⋅dS=∬SF⋅ndS.

F⋅n=⟨z2,x,z−y⟩⋅15⟨4,0,−3⟩=15⟨z2,x,z−y⟩⋅⟨4,0,−3⟩=15(2z+0−3(z−y))=3y−z5.

∬SF⋅dS=∬SF⋅ndS=∬S3y−z5dS=15∬S3y−zdS.
