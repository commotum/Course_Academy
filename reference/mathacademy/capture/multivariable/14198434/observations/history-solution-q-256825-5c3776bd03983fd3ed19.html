The Newton-Raphson process for optimization is

xn+1=xn−f′(xn)f″(xn),

where f′(xn) and f″(xn) are the first and second derivatives of f(x) evaluated at xn, respectively.

In our case, we have

f′(x)=(x5−4x3+x−2)′=5x4−12x2+1,f″(x)=(5x4−12x2+1)′=20x3−24x.

Therefore, our Newton-Raphson formula is

xn+1=xn−5xn4−12xn2+120xn3−24xn.
