The Newton-Raphson process for optimization is

xn+1=xn−f′(xn)f″(xn),

where f′(xn) and f″(xn) are the first and second derivatives of f(x) evaluated at xn, respectively.

f′(x)=((1−x)e−x2)′=(−1−2x(1−x))e−x2=(2x2−2x−1)e−x2,f″(x)=((2x2−2x−1)e−x2)′=(4x−2−2x(2x2−2x−1))e−x2=(−4x3+4x2+6x−2)e−x2.

xn+1=xn−(2xn2−2xn−1)e−xn2(−4xn3+4xn2+6xn−2)e−xn2.
