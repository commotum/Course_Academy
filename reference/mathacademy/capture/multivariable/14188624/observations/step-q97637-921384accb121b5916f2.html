A particle moves along a clockwise circular path with center O in the xy-plane with a constant angular velocity of π2 rad/s. Given that the object is at the position (1,0) when t=0, find the position function of the particle.

a
r(t)=cos⁡(πt2)i−sin⁡(πt2)j
b
r(t)=π2(cos⁡ti−sin⁡tj)
c
r(t)=cos⁡(πt2)i+sin⁡(πt2)j
d
r(t)=π2(cos⁡ti+sin⁡tj)
e
r(t)=−(cos⁡(πt2)i+sin⁡(πt2)j)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The position function of a particle moving in a circular path of radius R in the xy-plane with a constant angular velocity ω is given by r(t)=R(cos⁡ωti+sin⁡ωtj), where it is assumed that the circle is centered at O, and particle is at the position (R,0) when t=0.

Since the object's initial position is (1,0), we have that r(0)=1i, and we can conclude that the radius of the circle R=1.

Therefore, since we have R=1 and ω=−π2 rad/s (negative because the motion is clockwise), we get that the position function is given by

r(t)=cos⁡(−πt2)i+sin⁡(−πt2)j=cos⁡(πt2)i−sin⁡(πt2)j.
