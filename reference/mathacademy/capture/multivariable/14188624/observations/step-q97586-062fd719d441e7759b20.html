A particle moves along a clockwise circular path with center O in the xy-plane with a constant angular velocity of 2π rad/s. Given that the object is at the position (3,0) when t=0, find the position function of the particle.

a
r(t)=3(cos⁡2πti−sin⁡2πtj)
b
 r(t)=−2(cos⁡3πti+sin⁡3πtj)
c
 r(t)=−3(cos⁡2πti−sin⁡2πtj)
d
r(t)=2(cos⁡3πti−sin⁡3πtj)
e
 r(t)=3(cos⁡2πti+sin⁡2πtj)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The position function of a particle moving in a circular path of radius R in the xy-plane with a constant angular velocity ω is given by r(t)=R(cos⁡ωti+sin⁡ωtj), where it is assumed that the circle is centered at O, and particle is at the position (R,0) when t=0.

Since the object's initial position is (3,0), we have that r(0)=3i, and we can conclude that the radius of the circle R=3.

Therefore, since we have R=3 and ω=−2π rad/s (negative because the motion is clockwise), we get that the position function is given by

r(t)=3(cos⁡(−2πt)i+sin⁡(−2πt)j)=3(cos⁡2πti−sin⁡2πtj).
