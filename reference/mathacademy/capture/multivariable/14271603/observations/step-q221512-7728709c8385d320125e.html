If p, q, and w are primes with p≠q, and p|qw4, then
p=w
q=w
q=w4
p=w
w=p4
p=w4
.

Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that for any integers a, b, and c,

c|ab and gcd(a,c)=1 ⟹ c|b.

Also, given that p and r are primes, we get

pm|rn ⟹ p=r,

where m and n are positive integers.

With that in mind, we proceed as follows:

  • Since p and q are different primes, gcd(p,q)=1. Since p|qw4, we must obtain that p|w4.

  • Since p and w are primes and p|w4, we get p=w.
