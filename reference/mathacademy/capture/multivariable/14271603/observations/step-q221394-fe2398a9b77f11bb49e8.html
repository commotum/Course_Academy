For any integers m and n and any prime numbers p and q, we have that

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. As 5 and q are prime numbers, 3 and 6 are integers, and 53| q6, we must have q=5.

  • Statement II is false. As a counterexample, let q=2. Then, 23∣42 but 2≠4.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
