Recall that for any integers a, b, and c,

c|ab and gcd(a,c)=1 ⟹ c|b.

Also, given that p and r are primes, we get

pm|rn ⟹ p=r,

where m and n are positive integers.

With that in mind, we proceed as follows:

  • Since p and q are different primes, gcd(p,q)=1. Since p|qs9, we must obtain that p|s9.

  • Since p and s are primes and p|s9, we get p=s.
