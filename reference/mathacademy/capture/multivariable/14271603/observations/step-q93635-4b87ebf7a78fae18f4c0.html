EXPLANATION

For any integers a and b, and any prime p,

p|ab ⟹ p|a or p|b.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is false. As a counterexample, let a=2. Then, 3∣6⋅2 but 3∤2.

  • Statement II is true. As 7 is prime and 7|5a, we must have 7|5 or 7|a. Since 5 is not divisible by 7, we obtain that a must be divisible by 7.

  • Statement III is false. As a counterexample, let a=2. Then, 4∣2⋅2 but 4∤2.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
