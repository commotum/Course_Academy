Recall that for any integers a, b, and c,

c|ab and gcd(a,c)=1 ⟹ c|b.

Also, given that p and q are primes, we get

pm|qn ⟹ p=q,

where m and n are positive integers.

With that in mind, we proceed as follows:

  • Since gcd(a,p)=1 and p|aq3, we must obtain that p|q3.

  • Since p and q are primes and p|q3, we get q=p.
