Which of the following statements are true?

  1. If 23|12a then 23|a
  2. If 3|12a then 3|a
  3. If 12|3a then 12|a

Correct
EXPLANATION

For any integers a and b, and any prime p,

p|ab ⟹ p|a or p|b.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. As 23 is prime and 23|12a, we must have 23|12 or 23|a. Since 12 is not divisible by 23, we obtain that a must be divisible by 23.

  • Statement II is false. As a counterexample, let a=2. Then, 3∣12⋅2 but 3∤2.

  • Statement III is false. As a counterexample, let a=4. Then, 12∣3⋅4 but 12∤4.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
