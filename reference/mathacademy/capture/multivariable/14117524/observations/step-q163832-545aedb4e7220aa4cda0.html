Two point masses A and B of mass 3lb and 5lb, respectively, are connected by a rigid, massless rod of length 8yd that is perfectly balanced on a fulcrum, as shown above. Find the distance between the fulcrum and A.

4.8yd

5yd

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let d1 and d2 be the distances between the fulcrum and A and B, respectively, as shown below.

Let's write down the moment of each mass about the fulcrum:

  • The moment of mass A equals −3⋅d1=−3d1lb⋅yd (negative since the motion induced by this mass would be counterclockwise).

  • The moment of mass B equals 5⋅d2=5d2lb⋅yd (positive since the motion induced by this mass would be clockwise).

The principle of moments tells us that for the two masses to balance perfectly, the sum of the moments must equal zero.

So, in this case, we have

5d2−3d1=0.

We need to find d1, so we eliminate d2. Using the fact that the rod has length 8yd, we have

d1+d2=8⟹d2=8−d1.

Substituting this into the first equation, we get

5(8−d1)−3d1=040−5d1−3d1=08d1=40d1=5yd.
