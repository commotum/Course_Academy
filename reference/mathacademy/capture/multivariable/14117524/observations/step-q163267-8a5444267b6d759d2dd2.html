EXPLANATION

The center of mass of a collection of point masses m1,m2,…,mn located at (x1y1),(x2,y2),…,(xn,yn) on the plane is given by (x―,y―)=(Mym,Mxm), where

  • m=∑i=1nmi is the total mass of the system,

  • Mx=∑i=1nmiyi is the total moment of the system about the x-axis, and

  • My=∑i=1nmixi is the total moment of the system about the y-axis.

Since we require the x-coordinate of the center of mass, we need to compute the moment about the y-axis.

Let's denote the masses as m1=3,m2=6,m3=4,m4=2.

Then, the corresponding x-coordinates of the masses are x1=−4,x2=−1,x3=3,x4=4.

So, the total mass of the system is

m=∑i=1nmi=m1+m2+m3+m4=3+6+4+2=15kg

and the moment of the system about the y-axis is

My=∑i=1nmixi=m1x1+m2x2+m3x3+m4x4=3⋅(−4)+6⋅(−1)+4⋅3+2⋅4=−12−6+12+8=2.

Therefore, x―=Mym=215.
