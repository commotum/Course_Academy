Two point masses A and B of mass 12lb and 15lb, respectively, are connected by a rigid, massless rod that is perfectly balanced on a fulcrum, as shown above. If the distance between the fulcrum and B is 4yd, find the distance between the fulcrum and A.

EXPLANATION

Let d be the distance between the fulcrum and A, as shown below.

Let's write down the moment of each mass about the fulcrum:

  • The moment of mass A equals −12⋅d=−12dlb⋅yd (negative since the motion induced by this mass would be counterclockwise).

  • The moment of mass B equals 15⋅4=60lb⋅yd (positive since the motion induced by this mass would be clockwise).

The principle of moments tells us that for the two masses to perfectly balance, the sum of the moments must equal zero.

Therefore, in this case, we have

60−12d=012d=60d=5yd.
