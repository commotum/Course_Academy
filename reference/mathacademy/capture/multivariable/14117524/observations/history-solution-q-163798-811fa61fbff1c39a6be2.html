Let d1 and d2 be the distances between the fulcrum and A and B, respectively, as shown below.

Let's write down the moment of each mass about the fulcrum:

  • The moment of mass A equals −9⋅d1=−9d1kg⋅m (negative since the motion induced by this mass would be counterclockwise).

  • The moment of mass B equals 3⋅d2=3d2kg⋅m (positive since the motion induced by this mass would be clockwise).

The principle of moments tells us that for the two masses to balance perfectly, the sum of the moments must equal zero.

So, in this case, we have

3d2−9d1=0.

We need to find d2, so we eliminate d1. Using the fact that the rod has length 4m, we have

d1+d2=4⟹d1=4−d2.

Substituting this into the first equation, we get

3d2−9(4−d2)=03d2−36+9d2=012d2=36d2=3m.
