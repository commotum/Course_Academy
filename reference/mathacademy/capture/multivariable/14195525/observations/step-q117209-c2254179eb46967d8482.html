Consider a box with width x, height y, and depth z, as shown above. The width of the box is four times its height. Given that the box's volume is 800 cubic units, what is the smallest possible surface area of the box?

a
600 square units
b
1200 square units
c
480 square units

300 square units
e
125 square units
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Step 1: We need to find the value of y such that the surface area S=2xz+2xy+2yz is the smallest possible value.

Step 2: The volume is exactly 800 cubic units. So, we have V=x⋅y⋅z800=xyz

Now, using the fact that x=4y, we get the constraint equation

800=4zy2.

Step 3: We make z the subject of the constraint equation: 4zy2=800z=200y2

Then, we substitute the above into our expression for S, and get

S=2xz+2xy+2yz=2(4y)(200y2)+2(4y)y+2y(200y2)=1600y+8y2+400y=8y2+2000y.

Step 4: We solve the equation S′(y)=0. Taking the derivative, we have S′(y)=16y−2000y2, and solving for the stationary points, we get S′(y)=016y−2000y2=016y3−2000=016y3=2000y3=125y=5

Step 5: We confirm that y=5 is a minimum using the second derivative test. The second derivative is S″(y)=16+4000y3⟹S″(5)=16+32=48>0.

Since S″(5) is positive, we conclude that y=5 is indeed a minimum.

Now, the smallest surface area that the box can have is S(5)=8(5)2+20005=600.
