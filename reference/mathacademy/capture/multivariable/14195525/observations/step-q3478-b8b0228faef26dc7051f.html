A box whose width x is 3 times its depth z has a manufacturing cost that depends on two materials. The material for the top and bottom costs $5 per square foot, and the material for the lateral faces costs $2 per square foot. The box must have a volume of 50ft3.

What is the value of z for the box that has the smallest production cost?

a
1693ft
b
863ft
c
493ft
d
8063ft
e
4093ft

EXPLANATION

Step 1: We need to find the value of z such that the manufacturing cost C=5⋅2xz+2⋅2xy+2⋅2yz=10xz+4xy+4yz is the smallest possible value.

Step 2: The volume must be 50ft3. So, we have the constraint equation V=x⋅z⋅y50=3z2y.

Step 3: We make y the subject of the constraint equation: 3z2y=50y=503z2

Then, we substitute the above into our expression for C, and get

C=10xz+4xy+4yzC=10(3z)z+4(3z)(503z2)+4(503z2)zC=30z2+200z+2003zC=30z2+8003z.

Step 4: We solve the equation C′(z)=0. Taking the derivative, we have C′(z)=60z−8003z2, and solving for the stationary points, we get C′(z)=060z−8003z2=0180z3=800z3=800180z=4093.

Step 5: We confirm that z=4093 is a minimum using the second derivative test. The second derivative is

C″(z)=60+1600z3⟹C″(4093)>0.

Since C″(4093) is positive, we conclude that z=4093 is indeed a minimum of C(z).
