EXPLANATION

Step 1: We need to find the value of y such that the volume V=x⋅y⋅z is the greatest possible value.

Since y=32x, we have x=23y, so V=(23y)⋅y⋅z=23y2z.

Step 2: The surface area is 196 square units. So, we have S=2xy+2xz+2yz196=2xy+2xz+2yz.

Now, using the fact that x=23y, we get the constraint equation 196=2(23y)y+2(23y)z+2yz196=43y2+43yz+2yz196=43y2+103yz.

Step 3: We make one of the variables the subject in the constraint equation: z=2945y−25y Then, we substitute the above into the expression V=23y2z, and we get V(y)=23y2(2945y−25y)=1965y−415y3.

Step 4: We solve the equation V′(y)=0. Taking the derivative, we have V′(y)=1965−45y2, and solving for the stationary points, we get V′(y)=01965−45y2=0196−4y2=0y2=49y=7.

Note that in the last step, we only considered the positive root because the side length y must be positive.

Step 5: We confirm that y=7 is a maximum using the second derivative test. The second derivative is V″(y)=−85y⟹V″(7)=−565<0,

Since V″(7) is negative, we conclude that y=7 is indeed a maximum of V(y).

Therefore, the box with the largest possible volume has height y=7 units.
