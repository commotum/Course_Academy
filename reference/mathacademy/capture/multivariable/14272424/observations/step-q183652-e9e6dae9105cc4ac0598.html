Negate the following statement:

Some composite numbers are not divisible by 3.

a
All composite numbers are divisible by 3.
b
None of the listed
c
Some composite numbers are not divisible by 3.
d
Some composite numbers are divisible by 3.
e
All composite numbers are not divisible by 3.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let C be the set of all composite numbers. The given statement can be re-phrased as follows:

There exists x∈C such that x is not divisible by 3.

Introducing the existential quantifier, we get the following:

∃x∈C,x is not divisible by 3.

Negating this, we obtain ¬(∃x∈C,x is not divisible by 3)≡∀x∈C,¬(x is not divisible by 3)≡∀x∈C,x is divisible by 3

which reads

For all x∈C, x is divisible by 3.

or simply

All composite numbers are divisible by 3.
