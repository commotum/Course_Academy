The area of one loop of the polar curve r=3sin⁡(5θ) is given by the integral A=32∫0π/5sin2⁡(5θ)dθ. Which of the following represents the total area bounded by the polar curve?

a
12∫4ππsin2⁡(5θ)dθ

52∫02π/5sin2⁡(5θ)dθ
c
3∫2π/53π/5sin2⁡(5θ)dθ
d
72∫3π/5πsin2⁡(5θ)dθ
e
32∫0πsin2⁡(5θ)dθ

Correct
EXPLANATION

The given polar curve is the polar rose with 5 petals, and we know that the area of the first petal is A=32∫0π/5sin2⁡(5θ)dθ.

The 1st petal is traversed from θ1=0 to θ2=π5, and the length of this interval is π5.

To find the interval for the second petal, we have to add π5 to both ends of the interval of the first petal:

θ3=θ1+π5=0+π5=π5θ4=θ2+π5=π5+π5=2π5

Next, to obtain the interval for the third petal, we add π5 to both ends of the interval of the second petal:

θ5=θ3+π5=π5+π5=2π5θ6=θ4+π5=2π5+π5=3π5

Now, to obtain the interval for the fourth petal, we add π5 to both ends of the interval of the third petal:

θ7=θ5+π5=2π5+π5=3π5θ8=θ6+π5=3π5+π5=4π5

Finally, to obtain the interval for the fifth petal, we add π5 to both ends of the interval of the fourth petal:

θ9=θ7+π5=3π2+π5=4π5θ10=θ8+π5=4π5+π5=π

Consequently, we obtain the following intervals for all 5 petals of our rose: [0,π5]⏟1st,[π5,2π5]⏟2nd,[2π5,3π5]⏟3rd,[3π5,4π5]⏟4th,[4π5,π]⏟5th

Therefore, the total area of the given polar curve can be obtained if the curve is traversed from θ=0 to θ=π:

Atotal=32∫0πsin2⁡(5θ)dθ
