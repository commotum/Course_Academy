The equivalence classes of ∼ partition A, meaning that any element a∈A belongs to exactly one distinct equivalence class.

So, we compute equivalence classes until all the elements of A have been placed into an equivalence class.

[0]={a∈A:a∼0}={a∈A:2 ∣(a−0)}={a∈A:2∣a}={0,2,4,6,8}[1]={a∈A:a∼1}={a∈A:2∣(a−1)}={1,3,5,7,9}
