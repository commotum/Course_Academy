The change in kinetic energy of a particle with mass m, initial velocity v0, and final velocity v1 is given by ΔKE=12mv12−12mv02.

When the mass and velocity are measured in kilograms and meters per second, respectively, the particle's kinetic energy is measured in Joules (J).

First, we find v(t) by integrating the acceleration with respect to time: v(t)=∫a(t)dtv(t)=∫1t+1dtv(t)=ln⁡|t+1|+C

To determine C, we use the fact that v(0)=0. Substituting this into the above gives 0=ln⁡|0+1|+C⟹C=0.

Hence, the velocity of the particle at time t is v(t)=ln⁡|t+1|. In particular, its inital velocity at time t=1s and final velocity at time t=3s are v0=v(1)=ln⁡|1+1|=ln⁡2m/s,v1=v(3)=ln⁡|3+1|=2ln⁡2m/s.

Therefore, the change in kinetic energy of the particle between these times is ΔKE=12mv12−12mv02=12⋅4⋅(2ln⁡2)2−12⋅4⋅(ln⁡2)2=8ln2⁡2−2ln2⁡2=6ln2⁡(2)J.
