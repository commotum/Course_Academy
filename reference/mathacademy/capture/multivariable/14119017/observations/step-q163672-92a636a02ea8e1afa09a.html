A particle P of mass 3kg moves along the x-axis under the action of a resultant force. The particle's velocity is (3t−1)m/s, where t≥0 is the time in seconds. Calculate the work done by the force between the times t=1s and t=3s.

a
96J
b
90J
c
85J
d
118J
e
103J
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The work-energy principle states that the work done by a resultant force on a particle equals the change in the particle's kinetic energy. That is,

W=ΔKE

where the change in kinetic energy of a particle is given by

ΔKE=12mv12−12mv02

and m is the particle's mass, v0 is its initial velocity, and v1 is its final velocity.

The particle's velocity is given by v(t)=3t−1, where t is time in seconds. So, the particle's initial velocity, at t=1, is v0=v(1)=3(1)−1=2m/s, and final velocity, at t=3, is v1=v(3)=3(3)−1=8m/s.

Therefore, by the work-energy principle, the work done by the force between these times is

W=ΔKE=12⋅3⋅(8)2−12⋅3⋅(2)2=96−6=90J.
