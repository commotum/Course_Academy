The kinetic energy of a particle with mass m and velocity v is given by KE=12mv2.

When the mass and velocity are measured in kilograms and meters per second, respectively, the particle's kinetic energy is measured in Joules (J).

First, we find v(t) by integrating the acceleration with respect to time: v(t)=∫a(t)dtv(t)=∫(1+2t)dtv(t)=t+t2+C

To determine C, we use the fact that v(0)=0. Substituting this into the above gives 0=0+02+C⟹C=0.

Hence, the velocity of the particle at time t is v(t)=t+t2. In particular, its velocity at time t=2s is v=v(2)=2+22=6m/s.

Therefore, the kinetic energy of the particle at t=2 is KE=12mv2=12⋅32⋅62=12⋅32⋅36=27J.
