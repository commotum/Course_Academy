Consider the vector space M2(R) of all 2×2 matrices over R, and its subset E containing all diagonal 2×2 matrices.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. E is closed under matrix addition
  2. E is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. E is a subspace of M2(R)
  4. E is not a subspace of M2(R)
a
I and IV only
b
IV only
c
I, II, and III only
d
II and IV only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. If A1 and A2 are two matrices from E, then A1+A2 is a diagonal matrix. This matrix belongs to E. So, E is closed under addition.

  • Statement II is true. If A is an element of E, and k∈R, then kA is a diagonal matrix. This matrix belongs to E. So, E is closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is true, while statement IV is false. E is a subspace because it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III only."
