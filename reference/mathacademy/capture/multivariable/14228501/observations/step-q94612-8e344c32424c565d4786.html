Consider the vector space R2[t] of all polynomials in the variable t of degree at most 2, and its subset B={p(t)∈R2[t]|p(1)=2}.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. B is closed under polynomial addition
  2. B is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. B is a subspace of R2[t]
  4. B is not a subspace of R2[t]

I, II, and III only
b

Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. For example, if f(1)=g(1)=2, then f(1)+g(1)=2+2=4, which is not a polynomial from B. So, B is not closed under addition.

  • Statement II is false. For example, if f(1)=2 and k=2, then kf(1)=2⋅2=4, which is not a polynomial from B. So, B is not closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is false, while statement IV is true. B is not a subspace because it's not closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "IV only."
