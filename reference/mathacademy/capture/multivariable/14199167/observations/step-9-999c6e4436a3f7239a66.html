Explanation

A stationary point of g(x) is a value of x where g′(x)=0. Graphically, those are the values where the tangent to the graph is horizontal.

In the given graph, we can see that at x=−2 and x=2, the tangent to the graph is horizontal, so we have g′(x)=0 at these points.

Therefore, there are two stationary points, and their x-coordinates (in ascending order) are x=−2 and x=2.
