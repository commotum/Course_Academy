The time that a particular laptop battery lasts is normally distributed with a mean of 32 hours and a standard deviation of 4 hours. What is the probability that a full charge lasts for 36 hours or more? Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

EXPLANATION

Let X represent the time the full charge lasts. Then we have X∼N(32,42).

We want to find P(X≥36). Thus, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X≥36)=P(Z≥36−324)=P(Z≥1)=1−P(Z≤1)=1−Φ(1)

From the z-table, we see that

1−Φ(1)=1−0.8413≈0.1587.

Therefore, the probability that a full charge lasts 36 hours or more is approximately 0.16.
