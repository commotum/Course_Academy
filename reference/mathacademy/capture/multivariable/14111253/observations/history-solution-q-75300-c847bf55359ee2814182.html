We will use the formula

d2ydx2=ddθ(dydx)(dxdθ).

First, we need to find dydx and dxdθ.

To convert from polar coordinates to Cartesian coordinates, we use

x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ

as parametric equations of a curve in terms of θ.

Using the fact that r=csc⁡θ, we get

x=cot⁡θ,y=1.

Then, to find dydx we use the rule

dydx=y′(θ)x′(θ).

Calculating the necessary derivatives, we have

x′(θ)=−csc2⁡θ,y′(θ)=0.

Therefore,

dydx=0−csc2⁡θ=0.

Finally, substituting into our formula for the second derivative, we get

d2ydx2=ddθ(0)−csc2⁡θ=0−csc2⁡θ=0.
