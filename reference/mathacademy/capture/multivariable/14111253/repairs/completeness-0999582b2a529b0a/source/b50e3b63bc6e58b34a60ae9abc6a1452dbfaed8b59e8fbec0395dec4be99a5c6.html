The extrema of the velocity occur at the critical points of v. Since the acceleration of the particle P is (4−x)m/s2, we have dvdt=4−x.

To find the moments where v is a maximum, we need to solve dvdt=0 and test each solution using the first or second derivative test.

Solving dvdt=0 gives 4−x=0x=4.

Hence, the extremum of the velocity function occurs when x=4m.
