Let X represent the drone flight time (in minutes). Then we have X∼N(35,42).

We want to find P(X>40.08). Thus, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X>40.08)=P(Z>40.08−354)=P(Z>1.27)=1−P(Z≤1.27)=1−Φ(1.27)

From the z-table, we see that

1−Φ(1.27)=1−0.8980≈0.10.

Therefore, the probability that a randomly selected drone flies more than 40.08 minutes on a single charge is approximately 0.10.
