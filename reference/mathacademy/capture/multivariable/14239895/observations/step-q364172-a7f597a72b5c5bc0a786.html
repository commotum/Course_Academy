EXPLANATION

First, let's round the number of laptops and tablets in store A:

  • Laptops: 72⟶70

  • Tablets: 38⟶40

Therefore, the ratio of the number of laptops to the number of tablets (expressed as a fraction) is approximately:

7040

So the numerator represents the number of laptops and the denominator represents the number of tablets.

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 10 gives an equivalent fraction:

70÷1040÷10=74

We can make a numerator of 35 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 5:

7×54×5=3520

Therefore, there are approximately 20 tablets in store B.
