Given a rectangular solid, find the differential dS for the surface area S of the rectangular solid if the lengths of the sides are a=4, b=5, and c=7.

a
4da+5db+7dc
b
24da+22db+18dc
c
35da+28db+20dc
d
8da+10db+14dc
e
12da+11db+9dc
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The surface area of the rectangular solid is S=2ab+2bc+2ac. Let's find the differential: dS(a,b,c)=(∂S∂a)da+(∂S∂b)db+(∂S∂c)dc=2(b+c)da+2(a+c)db+2(a+b)dc

For a=4, b=5, and c=7, the differential is dS(4,5,7)=2(5+7)da+2(4+7)db+2(4+5)dc=24da+22db+18dc.
