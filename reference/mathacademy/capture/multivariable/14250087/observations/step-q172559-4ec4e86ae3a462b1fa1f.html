EXPLANATION

Notice that the sets are disjoint since

{a,b,c}∩{1,2}=∅.

Therefore, the disjoint union is simply the union:

{a,b,c}⊔{1,2}={a,b,c}∪{1,2}={a,b,c,1,2}
