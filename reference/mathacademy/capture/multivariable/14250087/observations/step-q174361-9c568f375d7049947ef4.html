EXPLANATION

Two sets A and B are disjoint if they have no element in common, i.e., their intersection is empty: A∩B=∅

First, notice that the elements of B are positive integers that satisfy the inequality 4n+6≥304n≥24n≥6.

With that in mind, let's examine our sets.

  • Statement I is true. B={n∈N∣4n+6≥30}={n∈N∣n≥6}={6,7,8,…}

  • Statement II is true. Notice that A={n∈N∣n≤5}={1,2,3,4,5}. Then, the intersection is A∩B={1,2,3,4,5}∩{6,7,8,…}=∅.

  • Statement III is true. Since the intersection is empty, the two sets are disjoint.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II and III."
