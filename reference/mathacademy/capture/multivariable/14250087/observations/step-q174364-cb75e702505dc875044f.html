Consider the following sets: A={n2∣n∈N},B={2n∣n∈N}

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. A={1,4,9,16,…}
  2. A∩B=∅
  3. A and B are disjoint

Correct
EXPLANATION

Two sets A and B are disjoint if they have no element in common, i.e., their intersection is empty: A∩B=∅

With that in mind, let's examine our sets.

  • Statement I is true. A={n2∣n∈N}={1,4,9,16,…}

  • Statement II is false. Notice that B={2n∣n∈N}={2,1,23,12,…}. Then, the intersection is A∩B={1,4,9,16,…} ∩{2,1,23,12,…}={1}.≠∅.

  • Statement III is false. Since the intersection is not empty, the two sets are not disjoint.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
