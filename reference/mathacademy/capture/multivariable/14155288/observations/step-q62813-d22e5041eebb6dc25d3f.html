The price of crude oil, measured in dollars per barrel, increases at a rate r(t) given by the function

r(t)=21+3t,

where r(t) is measured in dollars per month, and t>0 is the time in months since the start of a particular year.

If the price was $25 per barrel at the start of the year, which expression gives the price of crude oil t months since the start of the year?

a
∫0t21+3xdx
b
∫025(25+21+3x)dx
c
25+∫0t21+3xdx
d
∫02521+3xdx
e
∫0t(25+21+3x)dx

Correct
EXPLANATION

We're given the rate of change of the oil price r(t). To find the total price after t months (call it P(t)), we apply the second fundamental theorem of calculus:

P(t)=P(0)+∫0t21+3xdx=25+∫0t21+3xdx

To ensure that our final function is a function of t only, we use a different variable (x) for the integration.
