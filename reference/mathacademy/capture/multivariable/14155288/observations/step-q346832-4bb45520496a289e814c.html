r(t)=40t2+6t,

where r(t) is measured in fish per month, and t>0 is the time in months since the study began. At the same time, 10 fish are lost each month due to predation.

Correct
EXPLANATION

The population of the lake is increasing at a rate of r(t) fish per month and is decreasing at a rate of 10 fish per month. Therefore, the rate of change R(t) of the number of fish is R(t)=r(t)−10=40t2+6t−10.

To find the total number P(t) of fish after t months, we apply the second fundamental theorem of calculus:

P(t)=P(0)+∫0tR(x)dx=500+∫0t(40x2+6x−10)dx

To ensure that our final function is a function of t only, we use a different variable (x) for the integration.
