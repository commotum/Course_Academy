Consider the finite region bounded by the curves x=3y−y2 and x=3−y, as shown above. Find an integral expression for the volume generated when this region is rotated 2π radians about the x-axis.

 2π∫24y(−3+4y−y2)dy

 2π∫13y(−3+4y−y2)dy

 2π∫13y(3−4y+y2)dy

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let f(y)=3y−y2 and g(y)=3−y.

We start by determining the points where the curves intersect by solving the following equation for y:

f(y)=g(y)3y−y2=3−yy2−4y+3=0(y−1)(y−3)=0

The solutions in our region are y=1 and y=3. So, we need to integrate from y=1 to y=3.

Now, let's draw our usual horizontal arrow through the region to determine the left and right functions.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the left function x=g(y)=3−y, and

  • leaves through the right function x=f(y)=3y−y2.

Therefore, using the shell method, the volume of the solid can be expressed as follows:

V=2π∫cdy([right]−[left])dy=2π∫13y([3y−y2]−[3−y])dy=2π∫13y(−3+4y−y2)dy
