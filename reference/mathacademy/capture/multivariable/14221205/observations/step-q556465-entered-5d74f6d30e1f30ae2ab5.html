5.4 and 5.5

e

Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the two consecutive tenths between which 30 lies, we need to find two numbers with one decimal place whose squares bracket 30.

First, let's establish between which two consecutive integers 30 lies. We know that 52=25 and 62=36. Since 25<30<36, we know that 5<30<6.

Next, we can test decimals between 5 and 6 to narrow down the interval: 5.32=28.095.42=29.165.52=30.25

Since 29.16<30<30.25, we can write: 5.42<30<5.52

Taking the square root of each part of the inequality gives: 5.4<30<5.5

Therefore, 30 lies between 5.4 and 5.5.
