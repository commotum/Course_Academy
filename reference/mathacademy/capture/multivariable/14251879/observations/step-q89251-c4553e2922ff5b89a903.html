Simplify the sentence "The dress is red and elegant, and it is elegant."

a
The dress is elegant
b
The dress is red
c
The dress is red and it is not elegant
d
The dress is red or elegant
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's rewrite the phrase "The dress is red and elegant" as the equivalent phrase "The dress is red and the dress is elegant." The sentence now reads

"The dress is red and the dress is elegant, and it is elegant."

Then, using the associative law, we can move the comma:

"The dress is red, and the dress is elegant and it is elegant."

Now, we can simplify the phrase "the dress is elegant and it is elegant" to just "the dress is elegant." The sentence now reads

"The dress is red, and the dress is elegant."

Finally, we can write the sentence more concisely as

"The dress is red and elegant."
