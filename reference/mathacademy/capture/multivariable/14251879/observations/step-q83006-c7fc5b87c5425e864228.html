Simplify the following sentence: "The day is rainy and it is cold, and the day is rainy."

a
The day is cold or rainy
b
The day is cold
c
The day is rainy
d
The day is cold and rainy
e
The day is rainy but not cold
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Using the commutative law, we can replace the phrase "The day is rainy and it is cold" with the phrase "The day is cold and it is rainy." The sentence now reads

"The day is cold and it is rainy, and the day is rainy."

Then, using the associative law, we can move the comma:

"The day is cold, and it is rainy and the day is rainy."

Now, we can simplify the phrase "it is rainy and the day is rainy" to just "it is rainy." The sentence now reads

"The day is cold, and it is rainy."

Finally, we can write the sentence more concisely:

"The day is cold and rainy."
