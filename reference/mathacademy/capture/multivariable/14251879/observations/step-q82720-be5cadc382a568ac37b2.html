Simplify the following sentence: "The pen is blue and it is a blue pen, or the pen is blue or it is a red pen."

a
 The pen is blue and red.
b
 The pen is red.
c
The pen is blue.
d
 The pen is blue or red.
e
 The pen is blue or red but not both.

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we simplify the phrase "the pen is blue and it is a blue pen" to just "the pen is blue." The sentence now reads

"The pen is blue, or the pen is blue or it is a red pen."

Then, using the associative law, we can move the comma in the sentence:

"The pen is blue or the pen is blue, or it is a red pen."

Now, we simplify the phrase "the pen is blue or the pen is blue" to just "the pen is blue." The sentence now reads

"The pen is blue, or it is a red pen."

Finally, we simplify the sentence to

"The pen is blue or red."
