EXPLANATION

To calculate the total circulation of a vector field over the boundary of a region containing holes, we add together the circulations over each positively oriented boundary.

Remember that:

  • the positive direction is counterclockwise for the outer curve C1 (because the region D is always on the left as we traverse C1 counterclockwise), and

  • the positive direction is clockwise for the inner curves C2 and C3 (because the region D is always on the left as we traverse C2 and C3 clockwise).

The diagram below shows the positive orientations of all three curves.

Notice that the positive orientation of the outer curve C1 is opposite that of C1 as stated in the question.

Therefore, we have

∮∂DF⋅dr=−∮C1F⋅dr+∮C2F⋅dr+∮C3F⋅dr=−4+(−6)+(−8)=−18.
