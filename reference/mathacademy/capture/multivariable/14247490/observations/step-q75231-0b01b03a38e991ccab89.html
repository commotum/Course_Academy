Let R=[0,1]×[1,3] be a region in the xy-plane, and let f(x,y)=3x+y. Further, let P be a partition of R, where

P1={0,1},P2={1,2,3},P=P1×P2.

Calculate the lower Riemann sum L(f,P).

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's draw the region R and the partition P.

The lower sum L(f,P) is given by

L(f,P)=m11⋅Area(R11)+m12⋅Area(R12)

where mij is the minimum value of f in each subregion Rij. We pick the minimum value of f because we're finding the lower sum.

Taking into account the fact that Area(Rij)=1 for all 2 subregions, the formula for L(f,P) reduces to

L(f,P)=m11+m12.

Now, notice that

  • f(x,y)=3x+y increases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=3x+y increases as y increases.

As a result, the minimum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the smallest possible x and the smallest possible y. This is the value at the bottom left corner of each subregion.

Hence:

m11=f(0,1)=1m12=f(0,2)=2

Finally,

L(f,P)=1+2=3.
