First, let's draw the region R and the partition P.

The lower sum L(f,P) is given by

L(f,P)=m11⋅Area(R11)+m21⋅Area(R21)+m12⋅Area(R12)+m22⋅Area(R22)

where mij is the minimum value of f in each subregion Rij. We pick the minimum value of f because we're finding the lower sum.

Taking into account the fact that Area(Rij)=1 for all 4 subregions, the formula for L(f,P) reduces to

L(f,P)=m11+m21+m12+m22.

Now, notice that

  • f(x,y)=x2+y2 increases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=x2+y2 increases as y increases.

As a result, the minimum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the smallest possible x and the smallest possible y. This is the value at the bottom left corner of each subregion.

Hence:

m11=f(2,2)=8m21=f(3,2)=13m12=f(2,3)=13m22=f(3,3)=18

Finally,

L(f,P)=8+13+13+18=52.
