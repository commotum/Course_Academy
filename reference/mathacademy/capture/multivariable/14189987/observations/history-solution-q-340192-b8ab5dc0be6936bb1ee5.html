The function has a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=3. Note that

limx→3+f(x)=−∞,

which is enough to conclude that there is a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=3.

There are also discontinuities at x=−2 and x=0 but they are not discontinuities due to vertical asymptotes.
