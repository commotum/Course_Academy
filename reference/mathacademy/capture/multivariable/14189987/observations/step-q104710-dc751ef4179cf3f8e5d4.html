EXPLANATION

The function has a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=3. Note that limx→3+f(x)=−∞, which is enough to conclude that there is a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=3.

There is also a discontinuity at x=−2, but this is not a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote.
