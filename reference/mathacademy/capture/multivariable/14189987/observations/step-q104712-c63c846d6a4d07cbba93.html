a
x=3

x=0 and x=3

Correct
EXPLANATION

The function has a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=0. Note that limx→0−f(x)=−∞, which is enough to conclude that there is a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=0.

There are also discontinuities at x=1 and x=3, but they are not discontinuities due to vertical asymptotes.
