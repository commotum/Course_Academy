Infinite discontinuities coincide with the vertical asymptotes of y=f(x). The only possible way that y=f(x) can have vertical asymptotes is if −1x+8 grows without bound within its respective subdomain.

We compute the asymptotes of −1x+8. Setting the denominator equal to zero, we get x+8=0⟹x=−8. Although both branches of the function are defined for x≠−8, we have that limx→(−8)−(−1x+8)=∞,limx→(−8)+(−1x+8)=−∞, and therefore we conclude that x=−8 is indeed an infinite discontinuity.
