For the function y=f(x) above, where does the function have a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote?

a
x=−2
b
x=2
c
x=−2 and x=2
d
x=−2 and x=0
e
x=0 and x=2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The function has discontinuities due to vertical asymptotes at x=±2. Note that limx→(−2)+f(x)=−∞, which is enough to conclude that there is a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=−2. Similarly limx→2−f(x)=∞, which is enough to conclude that there is a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote at x=2.

There is also a discontinuity at x=0, but this is not a discontinuity due to a vertical asymptote.
