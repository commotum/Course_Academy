Consider a linear transformation T that maps R2 onto R2. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. ⟨1,1⟩∈Im(T)
  2. Ker(T)≠{0}
  3. nullity(T)=0, where T is the standard matrix of T
a
I only
b
II only
c
I and II only
d
I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a linear transformation T maps R2 onto R2 if and only if rank(T)=rank(T)=2

and, according to the rank-nullity theorem,

nullity(T)=nullity(T)=2−rank(T)=2−2=0.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, The condition rank(T)=2 in R2 is equivalent to Im(T)=R2. Hence, T is surjective and so ⟨1,1⟩∈Im(T).

  • Statement II is false. The condition nullity(T)=0 in R2 is equivalent to Ker(T)={0}.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, we have that nullity(T)=0.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
