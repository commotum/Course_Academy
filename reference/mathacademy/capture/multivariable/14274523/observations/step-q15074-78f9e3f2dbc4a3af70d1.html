Consider all possible circular sectors MON with perimeter P=1 units. What is the largest possible area A of the circular sectors?

a
4 square units
b
12 square units
c
116 square units
d
14 square units
e
2 square units
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let r be the radius of the circular sector and let θ be the angle (in radians) subtended by it. We want to maximize the area A=12r2θ. We know that the perimeter is fixed at 1 unit, so we also have the constraint condition 1=2r+rθ.

Now, by making θ the subject of the constraint equation, we find that θ=1−2rr=1r−2.

Plugging the above into our expression for A, we obtain A(r)=12r2(1r−2)=12r−r2.

We want to maximize A, so we need to differentiate A and set the derivative equal to zero. The derivative is A′(r)=12−2r.

Setting A′(r)=0 and solving for r gives A′(r)=012−2r=0r=14.

Now, we confirm that r=14 is a maximum using the second derivative test. The second derivative gives A″(r)=−2⟹A″(14)=−2<0.

Since A″(14) is negative, we conclude that r=14 is indeed a maximum of A(r).

Therefore, the circular sector of the largest area will have a radius of 14, and the area is given by

A=12(14)−(14)2=18−116=116.
