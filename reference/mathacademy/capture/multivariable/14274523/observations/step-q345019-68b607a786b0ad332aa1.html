EXPLANATION

We let r be the radius of the semicircle and let x be the height of the rectangle. Then, we want to minimize the perimeter P=2x+2r+πr.

We know that the area is fixed at 100 cm, so we also have the constraint condition 100=2rx+πr22.

Now, by making 2x the subject of the constraint equation and taking into account that r>0, we find that 100=2rx+πr222rx=100−πr222x=100r−πr2.

Plugging this into our expression for P, we obtain P=100r−πr2+2r+πr=100r+πr2+2r.

We want to minimize P, so we need to differentiate P and set the derivative equal to zero. Computing the derivative gives P′(r)=−100r2+π2+2.

Setting the derivative equal to zero and solving for r gives −100r2+π2+2=0100r2=π+42r2=200π+4r=200π+4.

Finally, we confirm that r=200π+4 is a minimum using the second derivative test.

Since the second derivative P″(r)=200r3>0, the stationary point r=200π+4 is a minimum.
