The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land$", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "$\lor$".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: $7$ is a composite number and odd. This corresponds to \[ \underbrace{7 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is a composite number}}_A \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{7 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is odd} }_B, \] that is, $A \land B.$

  • Part 2: $2+6 \geq 10.$ This corresponds to $C.$

So, we can express the sentence as \[ \underbrace{7 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is a composite number} \textbf{ and } \textrm{odd} }_{A \, \land \, B}, \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{or}}_{\lor} \quad \underbrace{2+6 \geq 10 }_C \] that is, $(A \land B) \lor C.$
