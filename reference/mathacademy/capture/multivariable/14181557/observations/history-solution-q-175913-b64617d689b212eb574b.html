First, let's recall the following facts about the "and" $(\land)$ and "or" $(\lor)$ logical connectives:

  • The statement $A \land B$ is true if both $A$ and $B$ are true.

  • The statement $A \lor B$ is true if either $A$ is true, or $B$ is true, or both $A$ and $B$ are true.

In our case, $A$ is true, and $B$ is true. Therefore, the result is true: \begin{align*} \underbrace{\sqrt{5}\in\mathbb{R}}_A \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{10 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is even}}_B &\:\:\equiv\: \text{true} \land \textrm{true} \equiv \textrm{true} \end{align*}
