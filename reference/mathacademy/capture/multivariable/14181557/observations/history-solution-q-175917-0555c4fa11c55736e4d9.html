First, let's recall the following facts about the "and" $(\land)$ and "or" $(\lor)$ logical connectives:

  • The statement $A \land B$ is true if both $A$ and $B$ are true.

  • The statement $A \lor B$ is true if either $A$ is true, or $B$ is true, or both $A$ and $B$ are true.

In our case, $A$ is false, and $B$ is false. Therefore, the result is false: \begin{align*} \underbrace{\{1, 2\}\cap \{3\}=\{1,2,3\}}_A \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{9 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is prime}}_B &\:\:\equiv\: \text{false} \land \textrm{false} \equiv \textrm{false} \end{align*}
