The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land.$"

Note that the given sentence is a shortened version of the following full sentence:

$8$ is a natural number and $8$ is divisible by $4.$

So, the sentence reads

\[ \underbrace{8 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is a natural number} }_P \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textrm{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{8 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is divisible by } 4}_Q, \]

that is, $P \land Q.$

$P \land P$
