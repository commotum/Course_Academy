The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land$", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "$\lor$".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: $6|10$ and $6|18.$ This corresponds to \[ \underbrace{6|10}_M \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{6|18}_P \] that is, $M \land P.$

  • Part 2: $11$ is prime and odd. This corresponds to \[ \underbrace{11 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is prime}}_N \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{11 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is odd }}_Q, \] that is, $N \land Q.$

So, we can express the sentence as \[ \underbrace{6|10 \textbf{ and } 6|18}_{M \, \land \, P} \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{or}}_{\lor} \quad \underbrace{11 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is prime } \textbf{and} \textrm{ odd }}_{N \, \land \, Q}, \] that is, $(M \land P) \lor (N \land Q).$
