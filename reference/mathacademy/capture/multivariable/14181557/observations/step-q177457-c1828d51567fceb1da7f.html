EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: 4 is even or 42=12. This corresponds to 4p is even ⏟Mpor⏟∨42=12⏟N, that is, M∨N.

  • Part 2: −2 is an integer number. This corresponds to P.

So, we can express the sentence as 4p is even or p42=12⏟M∨N,pand⏟∧−2p is an integer number⏟P
