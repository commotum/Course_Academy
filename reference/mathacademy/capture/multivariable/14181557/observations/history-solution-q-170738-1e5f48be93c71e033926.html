The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land.$"

Note that the given sentence is a shortened version of the following full sentence:

$4$ is even and $4$ is positive.

So, the sentence reads

\[ \underbrace{4 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is even} }_X \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textrm{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{4 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is positive}}_Y, \]

that is, $X \land Y.$
