EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: 6≤32 and 6 is even. This corresponds to 6≤32⏟Ppand⏟∧6p is even⏟Q, that is, P∧Q.

  • Part 2: 13 is a prime number. This corresponds to R.
