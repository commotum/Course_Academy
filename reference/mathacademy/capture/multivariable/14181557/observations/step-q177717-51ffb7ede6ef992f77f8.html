EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: 6|10 and 6|18. This corresponds to 6|10⏟Mpand⏟∧6|18⏟P that is, M∧P.

  • Part 2: 11 is prime and odd. This corresponds to 11p is prime⏟Npand⏟∧11p is odd ⏟Q, that is, N∧Q.

So, we can express the sentence as 6|10 and 6|18⏟M∧Ppor⏟∨11p is prime and odd ⏟N∧Q, that is, (M∧P)∨(N∧Q).
