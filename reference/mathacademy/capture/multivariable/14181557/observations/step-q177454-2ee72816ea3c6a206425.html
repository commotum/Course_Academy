EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: 7 is a composite number and odd. This corresponds to 7p is a composite number⏟Apand⏟∧7p is odd⏟B, that is, A∧B.

  • Part 2: 2+6≥10. This corresponds to C.

So, we can express the sentence as 7p is a composite number and odd⏟A∧B,por⏟∨2+6≥10⏟C that is, (A∧B)∨C.
