The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land$", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "$\lor$".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: $6 \leq 3^2$ and $6$ is even. This corresponds to \[ \underbrace{6 \leq 3^2}_P \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{6 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is even} }_Q, \] that is, $P \land Q.$

  • Part 2: $13$ is a prime number. This corresponds to $R.$
