First, let's recall the following facts about the "and" $(\land)$ and "or" $(\lor)$ logical connectives:

  • The statement $A \land B$ is true if both $A$ and $B$ are true.

  • The statement $A \lor B$ is true if either $A$ is true, or $B$ is true, or both $A$ and $B$ are true.

In our case, $A$ is true, and $B$ is true. Therefore, the result is true:

\begin{align*} \underbrace{5 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is odd} }_A \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{or}}_{\lor} \quad \underbrace{-5 \in \mathbb{Z}}_B &\:\:\equiv\: \text{true} \lor \textrm{true} \equiv \textrm{true} \end{align*}
