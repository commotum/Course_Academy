The word "and" is represented by the symbol "$\land$", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "$\lor$".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: $4$ is even or $4^2=12.$ This corresponds to \[ \underbrace{4 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is even }}_M \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{or}}_{\lor} \quad \underbrace{4^2=12 }_N, \] that is, $M \lor N.$

  • Part 2: $-2$ is an integer number. This corresponds to $P.$

So, we can express the sentence as \[ \underbrace{4 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is even} \textbf{ or } \vphantom{p} 4^2=12}_{M \, \lor \, N}, \quad \underbrace{\vphantom{p}\textbf{and}}_{\land} \quad \underbrace{-2 \vphantom{p}\textrm{ is an integer number} }_P \]

that is, $(M \lor N) \land P.$
