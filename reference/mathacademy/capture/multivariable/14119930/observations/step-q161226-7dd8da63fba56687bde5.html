EXPLANATION

For a surface S defined by x=f(y,z) with (y,z)∈D, the surface area of S is given by

∬SdS=∬D1+(∂x∂y)2+(∂x∂z)2dA.

In this case, our surface is given by

x=y−z3,(y,z)∈D,

where

D={(y,z):1≤y≤4,1≤z≤3},

as shown in the diagram below.

Now, since

∂x∂y=∂∂y(y−z3)=13,∂x∂z=∂∂z(y−z3)=−13,

the surface area is given by

∬SdS=∬D1+(∂x∂y)2+(∂x∂z)2dA=∬D1+(13)2+(−13)2dA=∬D1+19+19dA=∬D119dA=113∬DdA=113⋅Area(D)=113⋅(4−1)⋅(3−1)=211.
