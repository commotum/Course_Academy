EXPLANATION

Step 1: We need to find the value of z such that the manufacturing cost C=2xz+1⋅2xy+1⋅2yz=2xz+2xy+2yz is the smallest possible value.

Step 2: The volume must be 972m3. So, we have the constraint equation V=x⋅z⋅y972=x⋅z⋅(z3)972=13xz2.

Step 3: We make x the subject of the constraint equation: 13xz2=972x=2916z2

Then, we substitute the above into our expression for C, and get C=2xz+2xy+2yz=2(2916z2)z+2(2916z2)(z3)+2(z3)z=5832z+1944z+23z2=7776z+23z2.

Step 4: We solve the equation C′(z)=0. Taking the derivative, we have C′(z)=43z−7776z2, and solving for the stationary points, we get C′(z)=043z−7776z2=043z3=7776z3=5832z=18.

Step 5: We confirm that z=18 is a minimum using the second derivative test. The second derivative is C″(z)=43+15552z3⟹C″(18)=43+83=4>0. Since C″(18) is positive, we conclude that z=18 is indeed a minimum of C(z).

Therefore, the production cost is the smallest when z=18 units.
