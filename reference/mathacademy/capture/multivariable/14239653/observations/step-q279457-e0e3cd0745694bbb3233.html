EXPLANATION

The rotation matrix Rθ represents a rotation of angle θ counterclockwise about the origin and is given by

Rθ=[cos⁡θ−sin⁡θsin⁡θcos⁡θ].

Comparing A with the rotation matrix, we have

{sin⁡θ=−12cos⁡θ=−32.

Since cos⁡θ and sin⁡θ both are negative we must have π≤θ≤3π2.

Dividing the first equation by the second gives tan⁡θ=33. The principal value θ1 is θ1=arctan⁡(33)=π6, but this is outside the range π≤x≤3π2. To find a value in the required range, we add π: θ=θ1+π=7π6. Therefore, our matrix represents a rotation of 7π6 radians anticlockwise about the origin.

Finally, this is equivalent to a clockwise rotation with an angle of rotation given by

2π−7π6=5π6.
