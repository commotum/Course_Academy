Consider the polynomials p1(t) and p2(t), given by p1(t)=1+t+t2,p2(t)=1+t−t2. Find p(t) given that B={p1(t),p2(t)} is a basis of Span{p1,p2}, and the coordinates of p(t) relative to B are [p(t)]B=[−11].

a
0
b
−2t2
c
−2−2t
d
2+2t
e
t2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Any vector in Span{p1,p2} can be written as

x1p1(t)+x2p2(t).

In our case, we have

[p(t)]B=[x1x2]=[−11].

Therefore,

p(t)=(−1)⋅(1+t+t2)+1⋅(1+t−t2)=−1−t−t2+1+t−t2=−2t2.
