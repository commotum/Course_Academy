A sample X1,X2,…,X16 is drawn from a population with population mean μ and population variance σ2. If μ is unknown and σ is known, which of the following are statistics?

  1. μ⋅σ−min(X1,X2,…,X16)
  2. μ−median(X2,X4,…,X16)
  3. σ−max(X1,X3,…,X15)
a
III only
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
None of those listed
e
I, II, and III
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Suppose X1,X2,…,Xn is a sequence of random variables representing a random sample of size n drawn from a population. A statistic is a random variable expressed as a function of Xi.

Statistics cannot involve unknown population parameters.

With that in mind, let's examine each expression.

  • Expressions I and II are not statistics. They are functions that contain the unknown population mean μ.

  • Expression III is a statistic. It is a function of the sample X1,X2,…,X16 and the known population standard deviation σ only. Note that a statistic doesn't need to include every sample element.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
