Question 6
?

P is not a rhombus or Q is a rectangle.

What is the negation of the compound statement above?

a
P is a rhombus or Q is not a rectangle
b
P is a rhombus and Q is not a rectangle
c
P is a rhombus or Q is a rectangle
d
P is not a rhombus or Q is not a rectangle
e
P is a rhombus and Q is a rectangle
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

De Morgan's law for disjunctions states that the negation of a disjunction of two statements is equivalent to the conjunction of the negations of the individual statements. It can be stated symbolically as follows:

¬(A∨B)=¬A∧¬B.

So, the negation of our statement is

¬(P is not a rhombus or Q is a rectangle)≡¬((P is not a rhombus)∨(Q is a rectangle))≡¬(P is not a rhombus)∧¬(Q is a rectangle)≡(P is a rhombus)∧(Q is not a rectangle)≡P is a rhombus and Q is not a rectangle.
