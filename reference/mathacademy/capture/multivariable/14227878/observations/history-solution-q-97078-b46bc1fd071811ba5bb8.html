Recall that a linear transformation T maps R2 onto R2 if and only if rank(T)=rank(T)=2

and, according to the rank-nullity theorem,

nullity(T)=nullity(T)=2−rank(T)=2−2=0.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. The condition rank(T)=2 in R2 is equivalent to
    Im(T)=R2.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, the condition nullity(T)=0 in R2 is equivalent to Ker(T)={0}.

  • Statement III is false. We have that nullity(T)=0.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
