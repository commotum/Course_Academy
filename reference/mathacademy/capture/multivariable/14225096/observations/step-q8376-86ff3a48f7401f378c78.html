We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

The general solution to our equation is

2x+30∘=−30∘+n⋅360∘,2x+30∘=210∘+n⋅360∘.

Solving the two equations above for x gives:

2x=−60∘+n⋅360∘2x=180∘+n⋅360∘x=−30∘+n⋅180∘x=90∘+n⋅180∘

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0∘≤x<360∘. So, the solutions x1,x2,x3,x4 (written in ascending order) are

x=90∘,150∘,270∘,330∘.

Finally, since x2=150∘ and x3=270∘, we have
