We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

The general solution to our equation is

2x+π3=π3+2nπ,2x+π3=2π3+2nπ.

Solving the two equations above for x gives:

2x=2nπ2x=π3+2nπx=nπx=π6+nπ

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0≤x<π.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=0 and x=π6

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0≤x<π.
