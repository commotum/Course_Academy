EXPLANATION

We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

The general solution to our equation is given by

4x=π2+2nπ.

Solving the equation above for x gives:

x=π8+nπ2

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0≤x<2π.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=π8

  • Setting n=1 gives x=5π8

  • Setting n=2 gives x=9π8

  • Setting n=3 gives x=13π8

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0≤x<2π. So, the solutions x1,x2,x3 (written in ascending order) are

x=π8,5π8,9π8,13π8.

Therefore, we conclude that:

x1=π8,x2=5π8,x3=9π8,x4=13π8
