EXPLANATION

We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

First, we rearrange the equation, as follows:

23sin⁡2x−3=023sin⁡2x=3sin⁡2x=323sin⁡2x=32

The general solution to our equation is

2x=60∘+n⋅360∘,2x=120∘+n⋅360∘.

x=60∘2+n⋅360∘2x=120∘2+n⋅360∘2x=30∘+n⋅180∘x=60∘+n⋅180∘

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0∘≤x<360∘.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=30∘ and x=60∘

  • Setting n=1 gives x=210∘ and x=240∘

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0∘≤x<360∘. So, the solutions x1,x2,x3,x4 (written in ascending order) are

x=30∘,60∘,210∘,240∘.

Finally, since x2=60∘ and x4=240∘, we have

x2+x4=60+240=300.
