EXPLANATION

We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

First, we rearrange the equation, as follows:

2sin⁡(2x−7π3)+1=02sin⁡(2x−7π3)=−1sin⁡(2x−7π3)=−12sin⁡(2x−7π3)=−22

The general solution to our equation is

2x−7π3=−π4+2nπ,2x−7π3=5π4+2nπ.

Solving the two equations above for x gives:

2x=25π12+2nπ2x=43π12+2nπx=25π24+nπx=43π24+nπx=25π24+24nπ24x=43π24+24nπ24x=π(25+24n)24x=π(43+24n)24

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0≤x<2π.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=25π24 and x=43π24

  • Setting n=−1 gives x=π24 and x=19π24

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0≤x<2π. So, the solutions x1,x2,x3,x4 (written in ascending order) are

x=π24,19π24,25π24,43π24.

Finally, since x1=π24 and x3=25π24, we have

x1+x3=π24+25π24=26π24=13π12.
