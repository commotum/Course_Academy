The general solution to our equation is

4x−π4=7π6+2nπ,4x−π4=11π6+2nπ.

Solving the two equations above for x gives:

4x=7π6+π4+2nπ4x=11π6+π4+2nπ4x=17π12+2nπ4x=25π12+2nπx=17π48+nπ2x=25π48+nπ2x=17π48+24nπ48x=25π48+24nπ48x=(24n+17)π48x=(24n+25)π48

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0≤x<π.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=17π48 and x=25π48

  • Setting n=−1 gives x=−7π48 (too small) and x=π48

  • Setting n=1 gives x=41π48 and x=49π48 (too big)

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0≤x<π. So, the solutions x1,x2,x3,x4 (written in ascending order) are

x=π48,17π48,25π48,41π48.

Therefore, we conclude that: x1=π48,x2=17π48,x3=25π48,x4=41π48
