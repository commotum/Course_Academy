EXPLANATION

The number of bikes is increasing at a rate of r(t) bikes per hour and is decreasing at a rate of 4 bikes per hour. Therefore, the rate of change R(t) of the number of bikes is R(t)=r(t)−4=5t3+2t−4.

To find the total number B(t) of bikes after t hours, we apply the second fundamental theorem of calculus:

B(t)=B(0)+∫0tR(x)dx=100+∫0t(5x3+2x−4)dx

To ensure that our final function is a function of t only, we use a different variable (x) for the integration.
