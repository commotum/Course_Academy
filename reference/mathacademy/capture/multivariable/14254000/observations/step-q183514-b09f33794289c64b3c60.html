P(x): |x|≥2

Given the predicate above, which of the following expresses the statement ∃x∈(0,2],P(x)?

a
For all x∈(0,2], |x|≥2.
b
There exists x≥2 such that x∈(0,2].
c
For all x≤2, x∈(0,2].
d
There exists x∈(0,2] such that |x|≥2.
e
There exists x∈(−2,2) such that |x|≥2.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The existential quantifier "∃" means "there exists." So, the statement ∃x∈(0,2],P(x) reads as follows:

There exists x∈(0,2] such that |x|≥2.

Note: This statement is true.
