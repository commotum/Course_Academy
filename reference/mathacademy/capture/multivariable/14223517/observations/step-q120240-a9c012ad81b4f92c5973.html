The rate of growth in the number of people P(t) with food poisoning in an small town follows the logistic growth model

dPdt=0.15P−0.00003P2, where t is the time, in weeks, since the first reported cases. If there were 100 cases initially, how many people with food poisoning there are after 4 weeks?

a
149
b
300
c
116
d
179
e
150
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A logistic growth equation has the form

dPdt=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

The general solution to the logistic growth equation is P(t)=a1+Ce−akt,

where C is an arbitrary constant.

We start by factoring the given differential equation:

dPdt=0.15P−0.00003P2=0.00003P(5000−P)

From here, we see that the constant of proportionality is k=0.00003, and the carrying capacity is a=5000. Therefore, the general solution is

P(t)=50001+Ce−(5000⋅0.00003)t=50001+Ce−0.15t.

We now substitute the initial condition P(0)=100 and solve for C. We get

P(0)=10050001+Ce−0.15(0)=1001+C=50C=49.

So, we have

P(t)=50001+49e−0.15t.

Finally, we substitute t=4 into the expression for P(t), and we get

P(4)=50001+49e−0.15(4)≈179.

Therefore, after 4 weeks, there will be approximately 179 people with food poisoning.
