The growth rate of the number of households P(t) participating in a neighborhood composting program is jointly proportional to the number of participating households at time t, measured in years, and the difference between the program’s carrying capacity and the number of participating households. The constant of proportionality is 0.0005 and the carrying capacity is 800.

How many households will be participating after 7 years given that there were 100 households participating initially? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

After 7 years, there will be approximately
561
households participating in the program.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the number of households participating grows at a rate that is jointly proportional to the number of households participating at time t and the difference between the carrying capacity and the number of households participating, the number of households participating follows a logistic growth model.

A logistic growth equation has the form dPdt=kP(a−P), where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

The general solution to the logistic growth equation is P(t)=a1+Ce−akt.

In this case, the constant of proportionality is k=0.0005, and the carrying capacity is a=800. Therefore, the growth is given by the equation dPdt=0.0005P(800−P).

The general solution to this equation is P(t)=8001+Ce−(800⋅0.0005)t=8001+Ce−0.4t.

We now substitute the initial condition P(0)=100 and solve for C. We get 8001+Ce−0.4(0)=1001+C=8C=7.

So, we have P(t)=8001+7e−0.4t.

Finally, we substitute t=7 into the expression for P(t), and we get P(7)=8001+7e−0.4(7)≈561.

Therefore, after 7 years, there will be approximately 561 households participating in the program.
