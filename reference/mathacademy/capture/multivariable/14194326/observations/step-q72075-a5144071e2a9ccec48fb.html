An oil tank is leaking. The volume of oil in the tank, in liters, after t hours is given by the function q(t). The values of q(t) for different values of t are given in the table below:

t 0 1 2 3 4
q(t) 200 192 186 182 180

Approximate the rate at which the volume of oil in the tank is changing when t=2 using a backward difference approximation.

a
5 liters per hour
b
−4 liters per hour
c
6 liters per hour
d
4 liters per hour
e
−6 liters per hour
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the backward difference approximation formula q′(t)≈q(t)−q(t−h)h, at the time t=2. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible, in this case h=1. This gives

q′(2)≈q(2)−q(2−1)1=q(2)−q(1)1=186−192=−6.

So, the rate of change of the volume is −6 liters per hour.
