EXPLANATION

First, we rearrange the equation as follows: 4cos⁡(3x−π6)+2=04cos⁡(3x−π6)=−2cos⁡(3x−π6)=−12

We will find the general solution to the equation, and then generate all of the solutions in the required domain.

The general solution to our equation is given by

3x−π6=2π3+n⋅2π,3x−π6=4π3+n⋅2π.

Solving the two equations above for x gives: 3x=2π3+π6+n⋅2π3x=4π3+π6+n⋅2π3x=5π6+n⋅2π3x=3π2+n⋅2πx=5π18+n⋅2π3x=π2+n⋅2π3

We set n=0,±1,±2,…, until we have generated all solutions in the domain 0<x<π.

  • Setting n=0 gives x=5π18 and x=π2.

  • Setting n=1 gives x=17π18 and x=7π6. We reject the solution 7π6 since it lies outside the domain of interest.

All other values of n generate solutions outside the domain 0<x<π.

So, in ascending order, the solutions are x1=5π18,x2=π2,x3=17π18.
