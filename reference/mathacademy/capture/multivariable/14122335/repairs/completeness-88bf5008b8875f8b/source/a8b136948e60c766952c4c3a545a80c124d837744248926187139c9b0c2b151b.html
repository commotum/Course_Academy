EXPLANATION

If X1,X2,…,Xn is a random sample of size n from a normal population, where

Xi∼N(μ,σ2),1≤i≤n, then the sample mean X― has the distribution X―∼N(μ,σ2n).

We're told that the population mean μ=10, the population standard deviation σ=0.1, and that the sample size n=5. Therefore, the distribution of the sample mean X― is

X―∼N(10,0.125)∼N(10,0.002).

We're required to find P(X―<9.9).

First, we transform X into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring:

P(X―<9.9)=P(Z<9.9−100.002)≈P(Z<−2.24)=Φ(−2.24)

From the table, we have

Φ(−2.24)=0.0125.

Therefore,

P(X―<9.9)≈Φ(−2.24)=0.0125.
