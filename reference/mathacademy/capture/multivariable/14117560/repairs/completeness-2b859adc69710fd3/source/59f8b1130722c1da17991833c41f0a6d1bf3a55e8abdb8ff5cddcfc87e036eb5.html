Let G(x)=x+1 for x∈R. Suppose g(x) is the 4-periodic extension of G(x) from x∈[0,4) to x∈R. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. g(x+8)=g(x) for all x∈R
  2. g(x)=G(x) for all x∈R
  3. g(x+2)=g(x) for all x∈R

EXPLANATION

Since g is the 4-periodic extension of G, we have

  • g(x+4)=g(x) for all x∈R, and

  • g(x)=G(x) for all x∈[0,4).

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. We have g(x+8)=g((x+4)+4)=g(x+4)=g(x) for all x∈R.

  • Statement II is false. For example, take x=5. Then, g(5)=g(1+4)=g(1)=G(1)=2, while G(5)=5+1=6. Hence, g(x)≠G(x) for x=5.

  • Statement III is false. For example, take x=1. Then, g(1+2)=g(3)=G(3)=4 while g(1)=G(1)=2. Hence, g(x+2)≠g(x) for x=1.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only".
