EXPLANATION

The directional derivative fu′(a,b) of a function f(x,y) at the point (a,b) in the direction of the vector v is given by

fu′(a,b)=∇f(a,b)⋅u

where u is a unit vector in the direction of v.

First, we find the direction vector and the corresponding unit vector:

AB→=⟨−2,2⟩−⟨−1,1⟩=⟨−1,1⟩u=AB→‖AB→‖=1(−1)2+12⟨−1,1⟩=12⟨−1,1⟩

Now, we calculate the gradient ∇f(x,y) and evaluate it at the point A(−1,1):

∇f(x,y)=⟨∂∂x(2x2−y)2,∂∂y(2x2−y)2⟩=⟨16x3−8xy,2y−4x2⟩∇f(−1,1)=⟨16(−1)3−8(−1)(1),2(1)−4(−1)2⟩=⟨−8,−2⟩

Therefore, the directional derivative is

fu′(−1,1)=∇f(−1,1)⋅u=⟨−8,−2⟩⋅⟨−12,12⟩=(−8)⋅(−12)−2⋅(12)=62=32.
