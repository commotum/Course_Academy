EXPLANATION

The directional derivative fu′(a,b) of a function f(x,y) at the point (a,b) in the direction of the vector v is given by fu′(a,b)=∇f(a,b)⋅u where u is a unit vector in the direction of v.

First, we find the unit vector u in the direction of v: ‖v‖=12+12=2u=12(i+j)

Now, we compute the gradient ∇f(x,y) and evaluate it at the point (−1,3): ∇f(x,y)=∂∂x(x2−6xy+y2) i+∂∂y(x2−6xy+y2) j=(2x−6y)i+(2y−6x)j∇f(−1,3)=(2(−1)−6(3))i+(2(3)−6(−1))j=−20i+12j

Therefore, the directional derivative is fu′(−1,3)=∇f(−1,3)⋅u=(−20i+12j)⋅12(i+j)=−20(1)+12(1)2=−82=−42.
