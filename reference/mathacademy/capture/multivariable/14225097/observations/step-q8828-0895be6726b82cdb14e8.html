Given that x1 and x2 are the solutions to the equation 5sin⁡x−2cos⁡x=0 for 0≤x<2π, what is the value of x1⋅x2, rounded to 1 decimal place?

a
1.1
b
1.7
c
1.5
d
1.9
e
1.3
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We divide both sides of the equation by cos⁡x and use the identity tan⁡x=sin⁡xcos⁡x.

5sin⁡x−2cos⁡x=05sin⁡xcos⁡x−2cos⁡xcos⁡x=05sin⁡xcos⁡x−2cos⁡xcos⁡x=05⋅sin⁡xcos⁡x−2=05tan⁡x−2=0

Now, we rearrange the equation and isolate tan⁡x:

5tan⁡x=2tan⁡x=25

Next, we find the principal value:

x=arctan⁡(25)=0.380506…≈0.3805 rounded to 4 decimal places.

Since 0.3805 lies inside the given domain, our first solution is x1=0.3805.

To find the other solution in the given interval, we add π to the principal value. Hence,

x2=0.380506…+π=3.522099…≈3.5221 rounded to 4 decimal places.

Finally, x1⋅x2≈0.3805⋅3.5221≈1.3 rounded to one decimal place.
