In ascending order, the exact solutions in radians to the equation sin⁡x=3cos⁡x, for −π≤x<π, are
x1=
−2π3​

EXPLANATION

We divide both sides of the equation by cos⁡x and use the identity tan⁡x=sin⁡xcos⁡x. sin⁡x=3cos⁡xsin⁡xcos⁡x=3cos⁡xcos⁡xtan⁡x=3

Next, we find the principal value: x=arctan⁡(3)=π3

Since π3 is inside the given domain, one solution is x2=π3.

To find another solution in the domain, we subtract π: x1=π3−π=−2π3.

So, in ascending order, the solutions are x1=−2π3,x2=π3.
