5.Finding Confidence Intervals: Applications?

A primary school teacher records how many words his second-year students can read in 5 minutes. The number of words read is distributed with a population standard deviation of σ=36 words. In a school of 180 students, he finds that the mean number of read words is 255 words. Find a 95% confidence interval for the mean number of words μ for the entire population of second-year children.
