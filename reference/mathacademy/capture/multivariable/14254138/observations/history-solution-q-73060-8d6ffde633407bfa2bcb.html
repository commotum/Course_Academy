Comparing these limits with the formula for the instantaneous rate of change

f′(a)=limh→0f(a+h)−f(a)h, we see that

limh→0f(h)−f(0)h=f′(0),limh→0f(2+h)−f(2)h=f′(2),limh→0f(3+h)−f(3)h=f′(3).

The function consists of two line segments. Inspecting the graph, we see that

  • for x<2, we have f′(x)=0.5,

  • for x>2, we have f′(x)=1.5.

The derivative does not exist at x=2. So, we have

limh→0f(h)−f(0)h=0.5,limh→0f(2+h)−f(2)h=DNE,limh→0f(3+h)−f(3)h=1.5.

Therefore, only statement III is true.
