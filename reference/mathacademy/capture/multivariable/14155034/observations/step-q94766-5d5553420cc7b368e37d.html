Given that A is a 5×5 real symmetric matrix, which of the following statements are true?

  1. A must have 5 distinct eigenvalues
  2. A is orthogonally diagonalizable
  3. If u and v are two eigenvectors that correspond to the same eigenvalue, then u⊥v
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
II only
d
I and II only
e
I and III only

Correct
EXPLANATION

Given that A is an n×n symmetric matrix over R, the spectral theorem states the following:

  • The eigenvalues of A are all real.

  • Eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues are orthogonal.

  • The dimension of the eigenspace for each eigenvalue equals the multiplicity of that eigenvalue as a root of the characteristic polynomial.

  • A is orthogonally diagonalizable.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. According to the spectral theorem, the eigenvalues are real but not necessarily distinct. This occurs when the characteristic polynomial has roots whose (algebraic) multiplicity is greater than 1.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, any symmetric matrix with real entries is orthogonally diagonalizable.

  • Statement III is false. According to the theorem, eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues must be orthogonal, but two eigenvectors that correspond to the same eigenvalue may not be orthogonal.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
