Given that A is a real symmetric matrix, which of the following statements are true?

  1. A must have all distinct eigenvalues
  2. If u and v are two eigenvectors that correspond to different eigenvalues, then u is parallel to v.
  3. A is diagonalizable.
a
I and III only
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
I only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Given that A is an n×n symmetric matrix over R, the spectral theorem states the following:

  • The eigenvalues of A are all real.

  • Eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues are orthogonal.

  • The dimension of the eigenspace for each eigenvalue equals the multiplicity of that eigenvalue as a root of the characteristic polynomial.

  • A is orthogonally diagonalizable.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. According to the spectral theorem, the eigenvalues are real but not necessarily distinct. This occurs when the characteristic polynomial has roots whose (algebraic) multiplicity is greater than 1.

  • Statement II is false. According to the spectral theorem, eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues must be orthogonal. Since the eigenvectors are nonzero, we can't have u parallel to v.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, any symmetric matrix with real entries is orthogonally diagonalizable.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
