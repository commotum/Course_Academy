Given that f(x,y,z)=xy+y2z+xz2, find ∂f∂z, where (x,y,z) are Cartesian coordinates and (r,θ,z) are the corresponding cylindrical polar coordinates.

a
r2sin⁡(2θ)+rz2cos⁡θ
b
r2sin⁡θ+2rcos⁡θ
c
r2sin2⁡θ+2rzcos⁡θ
d
rcos⁡θ+2rzsin⁡θ
e
rsin⁡θ+z2
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Applying the multivariable chain rule for the function f(x,y,z) with three independent variables r,θ, and z, where x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ, and z=z, we have

∂f∂z=∂f∂x⋅∂x∂z+∂f∂y⋅∂y∂z+∂f∂z⋅∂z∂z=∂f∂z.

First, we find the partial derivatives of f with respect to z:

∂f∂z=y2+2xz

Writing the above results in terms of r,θ, and z, we get

∂f∂z=r2sin2⁡θ+2rzcos⁡θ.
