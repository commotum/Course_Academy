EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we apply the candidates test.

First, note that for selling x units in total, the total revenue R(x) is given by R(x)=x⋅p(x)=x(x2−9x+15+45x)=x3−9x2+15x+45.

The permitted domain is 1≤x≤6. Therefore, x=1 and x=6 are both candidates for the global maxima of R(x).

We need to find the critical points of R(x) for 1<x<6. Taking the derivative, we get R′(x)=3x2−18x+15, and solving R′(x)=0 gives the following critical points: R′(x)=03x2−18x+15=0x2−6x+5=0(x−1)(x−5)=0

So, the critical points are x=1 and x=5. However, x=1 is already a candidate, so the new candidate is x=5.

Our candidates are x=1,5, and 6. Computing R(x) at each of these values, we get the following: R(1)=(1)3−9(1)2+15(1)+45=1−9+15+45=52✓R(5)=(5)3−9(5)2+15(5)+45=125−225+75+45=20R(6)=(6)3−9(6)2+15(6)+45=216−324+90+45=27

Therefore, to maximize revenue, the studio should book 1 photo session per day.
