A summer camp has capacity for 200 kids. The price for each kid attending the camp is $300. The cost of setting up the camp is made up of a fixed cost of $2000 plus a cost of $100 per kid. How many kids should attend the summer camp to maximize its profit?

a
200
b
100
c
30
d
38
e
20
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we apply the candidates test.

First, note that for x kids attending, the summer camp has a revenue (in dollars) of

R(x)=300⋅x=300x

and a cost (in dollars) of

C(x)=2000⏟fixed cost+100x.⏟variable cost

Therefore, the profit function is given by

P(x)=R(x)−C(x)=300x−(2000+100x)=200x−2000.

We note that the permitted domain is 0≤x≤200. Therefore, x=0 and x=200 are candidates for the global maxima of P(x).

We need to find the critical points of P(x) for 0<x<200. Taking the derivative, we get

P′(x)=200.

We can see that the equation P′(x)=0 has no solutions.

Thus, our candidates are x=0 and x=200. Computing P(x) at each of these values, we get the following:

P(0)=200(0)−2000=−2000P(200)=200(200)−2000=38000

Therefore, we conclude that the summer camp will have a maximum profit level when 200 kids attend.
