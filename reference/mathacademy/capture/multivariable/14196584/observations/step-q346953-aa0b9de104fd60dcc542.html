We need to find the critical points of C(x) for 0<x<10. Taking the derivative, we get C′(x)=3x2−30x+48, and solving C′(x)=0 gives the following critical points: C′(x)=03x2−30x+48=03(x2−10x+16)=03(x−2)(x−8)=0x=2,8
