EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we apply the candidates test.

First, note that for selling x pants, the total revenue R(x) is given by R(x)=x⋅p(x)=x(200+40x−103x2)=200x+40x2−103x3.

The permitted domain is 5≤x≤10, since x=5 corresponds to 5⋅100=500 and x=10 corresponds to 10⋅100=1000. Therefore, x=5 and x=10 are both candidates for the global maxima of R(x).

We need to find the critical points of R(x) for 5<x<10. Taking the derivative, we get R′(x)=200+80x−10x2,

and solving R′(x)=0 gives the following critical points:

R′(x)=010x2−80x−200=0x2−8x−20=0(x+2)(x−10)=0x=−2,10

So, the critical points are x=−2 and x=10. However, the value x=−2 is not in the permitted domain. Moreover, x=10 is already a candidate, so our candidates are x=5 and x=10. Computing R(x) at each of these values, we get the following:

R(5)=200(5)+40(5)2−10353≈1583R(10)=200(10)+40(10)2−103103≈2667✓

Therefore, to maximize its revenue, the clothes store should sell 1000 pants monthly.
