EXPLANATION

If S is an oriented surface with unit normal vector n, and F is continuous vector field, then the flux of F across S measured with respect to n is given by

∬SF⋅dS=∬SF⋅ndS.

For a sphere or radius r centered at O, the unit normal to the sphere is given by

n=⟨x,y,z⟩r.

In our case, we have r=3. Therefore,

n=13⟨x,y,z⟩.

So, we have

F⋅n=⟨xz2,0,−x2z⟩⋅13⟨x,y,z⟩=13⟨xz2,0,−x2z⟩⋅⟨x,y,z⟩=13(x2z2+0−x2z2)=13⋅0=0.

Therefore, we can calculate the flux of F across S as follows:

∬SF⋅dS=∬SF⋅ndS=∬S0dS=0
