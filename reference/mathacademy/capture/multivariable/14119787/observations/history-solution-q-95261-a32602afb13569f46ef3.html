The singular values of the matrix A are the square roots of the eigenvalues of the matrix ATA.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since A is a 4×3 matrix and AT is a 3×4 matrix, ATA must be a 3×3 matrix: AT3×4×A4×3=ATA3×3 Hence, A has at most 3 distinct singular values.

  • Statement II is true. All singular values of any matrix are non-negative.

  • Statement III is false. Since A is a 3×4 matrix and AT is a 4×3 matrix, ATA must be a 4×4 matrix: AT4×3×A3×4=ATA4×4 Hence, A has at most 4 distinct singular values.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
