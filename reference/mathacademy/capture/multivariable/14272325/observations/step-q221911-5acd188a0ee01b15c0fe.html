The notation a∤b means a does not divides b. So, 4∤5 means 4 doesn't divide 5.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a does not divide b means that for any integer k, we have that b≠ak.

Therefore, 4∤5 is equivalent to the statement that for any integer k, we have 5≠4k.
