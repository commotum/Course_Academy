EXPLANATION

Since 3 and 4 are coprime, then according to the Chinese remainder theorem, the system has a unique solution modulo 3⋅4=12.

From the first congruence, we get

x≡1(mod3)⟹x=1+3t,t∈Z.

Substituting this into the second congruence, we obtain the following:

x≡3(mod4)1+3t≡3(mod4)3t≡2(mod4)

Since 4 is a relatively small modulus, by trial and error, we have that 3⋅3≡1(mod4). So, we multiply both sides by 3:

3t≡2(mod4)3⋅3t≡6(mod4)t≡2(mod4)

As a result, t=2+4s, where s∈Z. Substituting this into the expression for x, we get

x=1+3t=1+3(2+4s)=7+12s.

Therefore, x≡7(mod12) is the unique solution modulo 12, and x=7 is the smallest natural number solution to the system.
