EXPLANATION

Since 2, 3, and 7 are coprime, then according to the Chinese remainder theorem, the system has a unique solution modulo 2⋅3⋅7=42.

We will solve the system in two steps.

Step 1. Find the common solution for the first and second congruences of the system.

From the first congruence, we get

x≡1(mod2)⟹x=1+2t,t∈Z.

Substituting this into the second congruence, we obtain the following:

x≡2(mod3)1+2t≡2(mod3)2t≡1(mod3)

Since 3 is a relatively small modulus, we can solve it by trial and error. We have that 2⋅2≡1(mod3). So, we multiply both sides by 2:

2t≡1(mod3)2⋅2t≡2(mod3)t≡2(mod3)

As a result, t=2+3s, where s∈Z. Substituting this into the expression for x, we get the common solution for the first and second congruences:

x=1+2t=1+2(2+3s)=5+6s

Step 2. Find the common solution of all three congruences.

Now, substituting the common solution of the first two congruences into the third congruence, we obtain the following:

x≡5(mod7)5+6s≡5(mod7)6s≡0(mod7)

Since 7 is a relatively small modulus, by trial and error, we have that 6⋅6≡1(mod7). Hence,

6s≡0(mod7)6⋅6s≡0(mod7)s≡0(mod7)

As a result, s=7u, where u∈Z. Substituting this into the expression for the common solution of the first two congruences, we get

x=5+6s=5+6(7u)=5+42u.

Therefore, x≡5(mod42) is the unique solution modulo 42, and x=5 is the smallest natural number solution to the system.
