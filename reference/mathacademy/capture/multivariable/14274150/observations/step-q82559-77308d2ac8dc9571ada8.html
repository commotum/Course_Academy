According to the Chinese remainder theorem, which of the following systems of congruences has a unique solution modulo 60?

I.{x≡1(mod3)x≡2(mod4)x≡3(mod5)II.{x≡3(mod6)x≡2(mod10)III.{x≡2(mod5)x≡3(mod12)

a
None
b
II only
c
I and II only
d
I only
e
I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The Chinese remainder theorem states that if the natural numbers n1,n2,…,nk are pairwise coprime, meaning that gcd(ni,nj)=1 whenever i≠j, then for any integers a1,a2,…,ak the system

{x≡a1(modn1)x≡a2(modn2)⋮x≡ak(modnk)

has a unique solution modulo n1n2⋯nk.

Let's now inspect each of the given systems in turn.

  • The theorem applies to system I. The numbers 3, 4, and 5 are pairwise coprime natural numbers. Therefore, according to the Chinese remainder theorem, the system has a unique solution modulo 3⋅4⋅5=60.

  • The theorem does not apply to system II since 6 and 10 are not coprime: gcd(6,10)=2>1

  • The theorem applies to system III. The numbers 5 and 12 are pairwise coprime natural numbers. Therefore, according to the Chinese remainder theorem, the system has a unique solution modulo 5⋅12=60.

So, the correct answer is "I and III only."
