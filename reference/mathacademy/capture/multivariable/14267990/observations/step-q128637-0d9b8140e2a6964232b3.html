Which statement is equivalent to A∧(¬Y∨C)?

a
(¬Y∧A)∧(C∧A)
b
A∨(¬Y∧C)
c
(¬Y∧A)∨(C∧A)
d
(C∧¬Y)∨(C∧A)
e
C∧(A∨¬Y)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We distribute the conjunction over the disjunction, as follows:

A∧(¬Y∨C)≡(A∧¬Y)∨(A∧C)

Now, since A∧¬Y≡¬Y∧A and A∧C≡C∧A, we can write

(A∧¬Y)∨(A∧C)≡(¬Y∧A)∨(C∧A).
