Let R=[0,2]×[0,2] be a region in the xy-plane, and let f(x,y)=5−x−y. Further, let P be a partition of R, where

P1={0,1,2},P2={0,1,2},P=P1×P2.

Calculate the lower Riemann sum L(f,P).

EXPLANATION

First, let's draw the region R and the partition P.

The lower Riemann sum L(f,P) is given by

L(f,P)=m11⋅Area(R11)+m21⋅Area(R21)+m12⋅Area(R12)+m22⋅Area(R22)

where mij is the minimum value of f in each subregion Rij. We pick the minimum value of f because we're finding the lower sum.

Taking into account the fact that Area(Rij)=1 for all 4 subregions, the formula for L(f,P) reduces to

L(f,P)=m11+m21+m12+m22.

Now, notice that

  • f(x,y)=5−x−y decreases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=5−x−y decreases as y increases.

As a result, the minimum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the largest possible x and the largest possible y. This is the value at the top right corner of each subregion.

Hence:

m11=f(1,1)=3m21=f(2,1)=2m12=f(1,2)=2m22=f(2,2)=1

Finally,

L(f,P)=3+2+2+1=8.
