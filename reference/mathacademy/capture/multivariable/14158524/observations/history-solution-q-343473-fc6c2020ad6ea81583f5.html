The equation of motion for a particle of mass m moving with forced harmonic motion without damping under the action of a spring with spring constant k and external force F(t) is given by

my″=−ky+F(t).

Substituting our values for m and F(t) gives

0.5y″=−ky+(3cos2⁡(2t)−32).

Simplifying and rearranging the above equation, we get

y″+2ky=3(2cos2⁡(2t)−1).

The complementary solution yc here represents simple harmonic motion, and it can be written as

yc=Acos⁡ωt+Bsin⁡ωt,

where A and B are arbitrary constants of integration, and ω is the system's internal frequency.

In our case,

ω2=2k⟹k=12ω2.

The oscillator experiences resonance when the frequency of the external periodic force f(t)=3(2cos2⁡(2t)−1) is equal to the frequency of the complementary solution ω. Since

f(t)=3(2cos2⁡(2t)−1)=3cos⁡(4t),

the frequency of the external periodic force is 4.

Therefore, k=12ω2=12⋅42=8kg/s2.
