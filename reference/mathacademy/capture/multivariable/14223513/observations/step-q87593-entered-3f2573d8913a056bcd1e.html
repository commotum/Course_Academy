A cinema hall can hold 120 people. The price of each ticket is $10. The cost of maintaining the cinema is made up of a fixed cost of $400 plus a cost of $0.25 per person. How many tickets should the cinema sell to maximize its profit?

a
80
b
120
c
90
d
100
e
110
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we apply the candidates test.

First, note that for selling x tickets, the cinema has a revenue (in dollars) of

R(x)=10⋅x=10x

and a cost (in dollars) of

C(x)=400⏟fixed cost+0.25x.⏟variable cost

Therefore, the profit function is given by

P(x)=R(x)−C(x)=10x−(400+0.25x)=9.75x−400.

We note that the permitted domain is 0≤x≤120. Therefore, x=0 and x=120 are candidates for the global maxima of P(x).

We need to find the critical points of P(x) for 0<x<120. Taking the derivative, we get

P′(x)=9.75.

We can see that the equation P′(x)=0 has no solutions.

Our candidates are x=0 and x=120. Computing P(x) at each of these values, we get the following:

P(0)=9.75(0)−400=−400P(120)=9.75(120)−400=770 

Therefore, we conclude that the cinema will have a maximum profit level when it sells 120 tickets.

A daycare center can take care of between 10 and 20 children. The fee that parents must pay each month depends on the number of children enrolled in the center. If there are 10 children enrolled, parents must pay $40. On the other hand, if there are more than 10 children enrolled, the price drops by $2 for each child. All the parents pay the same monthly fee.

How many children must enroll for the center to maximize its revenue?

a
10
b
12
c
20
d
15
e
17
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, note that if there are 10+x children enrolled, then the monthly fee is 40−2x. So, the total revenue is given by R(x)=(10+x)⏟number of children enrolled×(40−2x)⏟monthly fee=(10+x)(40−2x)=400−20x+40x−2x2=400+20x−2x2.

Since the center can only take care between 10 and 20 children, the permitted domain for x is given by 10≤x+10≤200≤x≤10.

Therefore, x=0 and x=10 are both candidates for the global maxima of R(x).

We need to find the critical points of R(x) for 0<x<10. Taking the derivative, we get R′(x)=20−4x,

and solving R′(x)=0 gives the following critical point:

R′(x)=020−4x=04x=20x=5

Notice that x=5 lies in the interval 0<x<10. All that's left now is to confirm that x=5 does indeed give a maximum value of R(x). To do this, we compute the second derivative:

R″(x)=−4<0

Since the second derivative is negative, the point x=5 is indeed a local maximum of R(x). And since R(x) is quadratic, we conclude that x=5 is a global maximum of R(x).

Therefore, the center must have 10+5=15 children enrolled to maximize its revenue.
