To solve this problem, we apply the candidates test.

First, note that for selling x tickets, the cinema has a revenue (in dollars) of

R(x)=10⋅x=10x

and a cost (in dollars) of

C(x)=400⏟fixed cost+0.25x.⏟variable cost

Therefore, the profit function is given by

P(x)=R(x)−C(x)=10x−(400+0.25x)=9.75x−400.

We note that the permitted domain is 0≤x≤120. Therefore, x=0 and x=120 are candidates for the global maxima of P(x).

We need to find the critical points of P(x) for 0<x<120. Taking the derivative, we get

P′(x)=9.75.

We can see that the equation P′(x)=0 has no solutions.

Our candidates are x=0 and x=120. Computing P(x) at each of these values, we get the following:

P(0)=9.75(0)−400=−400P(120)=9.75(120)−400=770 

Therefore, we conclude that the cinema will have a maximum profit level when it sells 120 tickets.
