The least-squares solution of Ax=b satisfies the normal equation

ATAx=ATb.

Substituting A=QR, we obtain

ATAx=ATb(QR)TQRx=(QR)TbRT(QTQ)Rx=RTQTb.

Since Q has orthonormal columns, QTQ=I, and we get

RTRx=RTQTb.

Finally, since RT is invertible, pre-multiplying by (RT)−1 gives

(RT)−1⋅RTRx=(RT)−1⋅RTQTbRx=QTb.

Therefore, the missing part of the equation is R.
