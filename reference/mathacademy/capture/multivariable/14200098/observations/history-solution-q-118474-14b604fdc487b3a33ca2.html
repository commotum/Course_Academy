A logistic growth equation has the form

y′=ky(a−y),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>y is the carrying capacity.

A diagram showing a typical logistic growth curve is shown below.

First, let's factor the right-hand side of the given equation. This gives

y′=10y(100−y),y(0)=40.

From here, we see that a=100. Hence, the carrying capacity is 100.

For a given logistic growth model, y→a as t→∞. Therefore,

limt→∞y(t)=100.

40
