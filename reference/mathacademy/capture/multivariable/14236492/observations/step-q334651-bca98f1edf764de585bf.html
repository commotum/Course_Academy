Let's denote f1(x,y)=2x−3xy and f2(x,y)=−5y+4xy.

Now, we compute the partial derivatives:

∂f1∂x=∂∂x(2x−3xy)=2−3y,∂f1∂y=∂∂y(2x−3xy)=−3x∂f2∂x=∂∂x(−5y+4xy)=4y,∂f2∂y=∂∂y(−5y+4xy)=−5+4x

Thus, the Jacobian matrix is J=[2−3y−3x4y−5+4x].

J(x∗)=[2−3(0)−3(0)4(0)−5+4(0)]=[200−5].
