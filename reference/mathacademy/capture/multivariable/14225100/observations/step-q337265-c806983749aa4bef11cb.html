EXPLANATION

First, we factor the equation:

sin⁡xcos⁡x=7sin⁡xsin⁡xcos⁡x−7sin⁡x=0sin⁡x(cos⁡x−7)=0

Therefore, by the zero-product property, we have to solve two equations: sin⁡x=0,cos⁡x−7=0

Let's solve each equation separately.

  • First, we solve sin⁡x=0. The solutions to this equation for 0≤x<2π are x=0,π.

  • Then, we solve cos⁡x−7=0, or equivalently cos⁡x=7. But since |cos⁡x|≤1, then cos⁡x=7 has no solution.

Arranging all solutions from both equations in ascending order, we get x1=0,x2=π.
