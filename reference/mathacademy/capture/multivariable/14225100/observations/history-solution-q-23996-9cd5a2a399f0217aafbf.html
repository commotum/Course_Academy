By the zero-product property, we have to solve two equations:

sin⁡x=0,cos⁡x−2=0

Let's solve each equation separately.

  • First, we solve sin⁡x=0. The solutions to this equation for 0∘≤x≤180∘ are x=0∘,180∘.

  • Then, we solve cos⁡x−2=0, or equivalently cos⁡x=2. But since |cos⁡x|≤1, then cos⁡x=2 has no solution.

Combining all solutions from both equations, we get

x1=0∘,x2=180∘.

Finally, x1⋅x2=0⋅180=0.
