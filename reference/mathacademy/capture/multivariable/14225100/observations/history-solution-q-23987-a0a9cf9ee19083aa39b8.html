By the zero-product property, we have to solve two equations: cos⁡x=0,2sin⁡x−1=0

Let's solve each equation separately.

  • Then, we solve 2sin⁡x−1=0, or equivalently sin⁡x=12. The solutions to this equation for 0∘≤x≤180∘ are x=30∘,150∘.

Combining all solutions from both equations, we get

x1=30∘,x2=90∘,x3=150∘.
