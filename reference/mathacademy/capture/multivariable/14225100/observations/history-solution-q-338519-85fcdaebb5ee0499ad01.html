Note that the functions cot⁡x and sec⁡x are not defined at the points where sin⁡x=0 or cos⁡x=0. On the interval −π≤x<π, the excluded values are x=0,±π2,±π.

Therefore, by the zero-product property, we have to solve two equations:

2sec⁡x+1=0,cot⁡x−1=0

Let's solve each equation separately.

  • First, we solve 2sec⁡x+1=0, or equivalently cos⁡x=−2. But since |cos⁡x|≤1, then cos⁡x=−2 has no solution.

  • Then, we solve cot⁡x−1=0, or equivalently tan⁡x=1. The solutions to this equation for −π≤x<π, are x=−3π4,π4.

Arranging all solutions from both equations in ascending order, we get x1=−3π4,x2=π4.
