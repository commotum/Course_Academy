Which of the following global truncation error expressions represent a second-order numerical method?

  1. e(Δx)=0.4Δx+5(Δx)2
  2. e(Δx)=7(Δx)2+(Δx)4
  3. e(Δx)=3(Δx)2+0.2(Δx)3
a
I only
b
I, II, and III
c
II only
d
I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A numerical method has order p if p is the largest positive integer such that the global truncation error (GTE) satisfies e(Δx)=O((Δx)p)asΔx→0. The order describes how fast errors shrink as Δx decreases.

With that in mind, let's check each option in turn:

  • For e(Δx)=0.4Δx+5(Δx)2, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 0.4Δx. So, statement I corresponds to an order 1 method.

  • For e(Δx)=7(Δx)2+(Δx)4, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 7(Δx)2. So, statement II corresponds to an order 2 method.

  • For e(Δx)=3(Δx)2+0.2(Δx)3, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 3(Δx)2. So, statement III corresponds to an order 2 method.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only".
