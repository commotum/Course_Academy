Let m∈N and n∈N. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. ∀m∈N,m≤10.
  2. The truth set of the predicate (∀m∈N,m≤n) is ∅.
  3. ∃n∈N,∀m∈N,m≤n.
a
 II only
b
 III only
c
 I only
d
 None of the statements
e
 I and III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, notice that the statement

∃n∈N,∀m∈N,m≤n

from option III reads as follows:

There exists a natural n such that, for every natural m, we have m≤n.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false since m≤10 is not true for some natural m. Say, for m=11.

  • Statement II is true. The predicate P(n):∀m∈N,m≤n is false for any n∈N. For example, P(1):∀m∈N,m≤1 is false since m=2 is not less than or equal to 1. Similarly, P(2):∀m∈N,m≤2 is false since m=3 is not less than or equal to 2. And so on. In general, it's sufficient to take m=n+1.

  • Statement III is false. The sentence (∃n∈N,∀m∈N,m≤n) can be written as ∃n∈N(∀m∈N,m≤n⏟P(n)) which is true whenever the inner predicate P(n) has a nonempty truth set. And according to statement II, this is not he case.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
