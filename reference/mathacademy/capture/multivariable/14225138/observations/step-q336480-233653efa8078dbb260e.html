EXPLANATION

Let's justify each of the selected steps.

  • We first consider lines L1 and L2. Since Φ0(t)=eAt is a fundamental matrix for the system, any solution of the system can be written as x(t)=Φ0(t)c. Thus, L2 follows from L1 by the properties of a fundamental matrix.

  • Next, we consider lines L2 and L3. From L2 we have x(t)=Φ0(t)c. Evaluating this identity at t=0 gives x(0)=Φ0(0)c, which is precisely line L3. Thus, L3 follows from L2 by evaluating both sides at t=0.

  • Finally, we consider lines L3, L4, and L5. From L3 we know x(0)=Φ0(0)c, and from L4 we know Φ0(0)=In. Replacing Φ0(0) with In in the equation x(0)=Φ0(0)c gives x(0)=Inc=c, which is line L5. Therefore, L5 follows from lines L3 and L4 by replacing Φ0(0) with In in x(0)=Φ0(0)c.
