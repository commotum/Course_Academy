Given that the equivalence P⇔Q is true, we have that P is a necessary and sufficient condition for Q.

In our case, we need to find the conditions that are necessary and sufficient for the given one. In other words,

(14∣x)⇒Condition and (14∣x)⇐Condition.

With that in mind, let's inspect each of the given conditions.

  • Condition I is both necessary and sufficient: A number is divisible by 14 if and only if it's divisible by both 2 and 7. (14∣x)⇒(2∣x and 7∣x) and (14∣x)⇐(2∣x and 7∣x). Therefore, we can write (14∣x)⇔(2∣x and 7∣x)✓

  • Condition II is not a sufficient condition: (14∣x)⇍(7∣x)× For example, the number x=7 is divisible by 7 but not divisible by 14.

  • Condition III is not a necessary condition: (14∣x)⇏(70∣x)× For example, the number x=14 is divisible by 14 but not divisible by 70.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
