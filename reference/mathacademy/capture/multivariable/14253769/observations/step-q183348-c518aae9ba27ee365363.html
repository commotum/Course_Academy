If x has more than three divisors, then x is composite.

Which of the following is logically equivalent to the conditional predicate above?

a
x has more than three divisors, or x is composite.
b
x doesn't have more than three divisors, or x is composite.
c
x doesn't have more than three divisors, and x is composite.
d
x has more than three divisors, and x is composite.
e
x doesn't have more than three divisors, or x is not composite.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's denote by P(x) and Q(x) the following predicates:

  • P(x): x has more than three divisors.

  • Q(x): x is composite.

Then, our initial sentence can be written as P(x)⇒Q(x), which is logically equivalent to

¬P(x)∨Q(x).

The negation of P(x) can be written as

  • ¬P(x): x doesn't have more than three divisors.

Therefore, the given sentence is logically equivalent to the following:

x doesn't have more than three divisors, or x is composite.
