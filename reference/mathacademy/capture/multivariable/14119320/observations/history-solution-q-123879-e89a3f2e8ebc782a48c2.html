Let C be a piecewise-smooth curve given by the vector function r(t) for t∈[a,b]. Let f be a differentiable function of two or three variables such that ∇f is continuous on C. The fundamental theorem for line integrals states that

∫C∇f⋅dr=f(r(b))−f(r(a)).

We start by parametrizing the curve C. A suitable parametrization is r(t)=⟨t24,t⟩,t∈[0,22].

Then, the position vectors of the endpoints of the curve are given by r(0)=⟨0,0⟩,r(22)=⟨2,22⟩.

Applying the fundamental theorem for line integrals, we have ∫C∇f⋅dr=f(r(22))−f(r(0))=f(2,22)−f(0,0)=((22)2−22)−(02−02)=(8−4)−(0)=4.
