EXPLANATION

To find which two consecutive whole numbers 110 lies between, we first identify the perfect squares that are just below and just above 110.

The perfect squares closest to 110 are 100 and 121. We have 100<110<121.

Taking the square root of each number, we get 100<110<12110<110<11.

This means that 110 lies between the consecutive whole numbers 10 and 11.

Therefore, since a<b, we have a=10 and b=11.
