EXPLANATION

To estimate 203, we first find the two perfect cubes closest to 20.

The closest perfect cubes are 8=23 and 27=33.

Since 8<20<27, we know that the cube root of 20 lies between 2 and 3.

Because 20 is closer to 27 than to 8, we expect 203 to be close to 3.

Let's test a decimal near 3, such as 2.7, by cubing it: 2.73=19.683

Since 19.683 is slightly less than 20, we know that 2.7<203<2.8.

Next, we determine whether 20 is closer to 2.73 or 2.83:

  • The distance from 20 to 2.73 is 20−19.683=0.317.

  • The distance from 20 to 2.83 is 21.952−20=1.952.

Since 20 is much closer to 19.683, 203 is closer to 2.7.

Therefore, rounded to 1 decimal place, 203≈2.7.
