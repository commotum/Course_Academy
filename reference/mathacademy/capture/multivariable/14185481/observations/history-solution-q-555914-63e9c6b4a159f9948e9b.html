First, we find two consecutive whole numbers that bracket each root. We do this by finding the perfect squares just below and just above the number under the radical.

  • For 3: Since 1<3<4, we have 1<3<4. This means 1<3<2.

  • For 7: Since 4<7<9, we have 4<7<9. This means 2<7<3.

  • For 15: Since 9<15<16, we have 9<15<16. This means 3<15<4.

From these bounds, we can see that all three numbers fall into different unit intervals: 1<3<2<7<3<15<4

Comparing these intervals, we have 3<7<15.

Therefore, the numbers in order from least to greatest are 3, 7, and 15.
