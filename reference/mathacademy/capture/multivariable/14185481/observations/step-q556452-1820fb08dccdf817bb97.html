Consider the numbers 20, 5, and 11. Write these three numbers in order from least to greatest.
The numbers, in order from least to greatest, are:

EXPLANATION

First, we find two consecutive whole numbers that bracket each root. We do this by finding the perfect squares just below and just above the number under the radical.

  • For 5: Since 4<5<9, we have 4<5<9. This means 2<5<3.

  • For 11: Since 9<11<16, we have 9<11<16. This means 3<11<4.

  • For 20: Since 16<20<25, we have 16<20<25. This means 4<20<5.

From these bounds, we can see that all three numbers fall into different unit intervals: 2<5<3<11<4<20<5

Comparing these intervals, we have 5<11<20.

Therefore, the numbers in order from least to greatest are 5, 11, and 20.
