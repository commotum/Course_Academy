Between which two consecutive whole numbers does 203 lie?

a
2 and 3
b
19 and 21
c
1 and 2
d
3 and 4
e
4 and 5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find which two consecutive whole numbers 203 lies between, we can compare 20 to nearby perfect cubes.

  • The closest perfect cube less than 20 is 8, since 23=8.

  • The closest perfect cube greater than 20 is 27, since 33=27.

This means 20 is between 8 and 27: 8<20<27

Taking the cube root of each part of the inequality gives 83<203<2732<203<3

Therefore, 203 lies between 2 and 3.
