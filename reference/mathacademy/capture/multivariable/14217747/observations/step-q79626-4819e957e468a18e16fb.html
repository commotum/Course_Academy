Which of the following gives the area bounded by graph of the polar curve r=3sin⁡(2θ) for 0≤θ≤π2?

a
29∫0π/2sin2⁡(2θ)dθ
b
92∫0π/2sin2⁡(4θ)dθ
c
3∫0π/2sin2⁡(4θ)dθ
d
9∫0π/2sin2⁡(2θ)dθ
e
92∫0π/2sin2⁡(2θ)dθ
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The area bounded by a polar curve is given by A=12∫θ1θ2r2(θ)dθ.

When r(θ)=3sin⁡(2θ), θ1=0 and θ2=π2, this gives A=12∫0π/2(3sin⁡(2θ))2dθ=12⋅9∫0π/2sin2⁡(2θ)dθ=92∫0π/2sin2⁡(2θ)dθ.
