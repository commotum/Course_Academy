4.Identifying Solutions to Forced Harmonic Motion Equations?

A particle is moving with forced harmonic motion without damping. The particle is acted on by an external periodic force f(t)=−7sin⁡(2t). Assuming the system experiences resonance, which of the following could be used to represent the general solution for the position y(t)?

  1. y=Rcos⁡(2t+ϕ)+74tcos⁡(2t)
  2. y=Rsin⁡(2t+ϕ)+74tsin⁡(2t)
  3. y=Acos⁡(2t)+Bsin⁡(2t)+74tcos⁡(2t)
Note that A, B, R, and ϕ are constants.
