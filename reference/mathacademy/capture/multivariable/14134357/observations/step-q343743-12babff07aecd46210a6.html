A particle is moving with forced harmonic motion without damping. The particle is acted on by an external periodic force f(t)=4sin⁡(3t). Assuming the system experiences resonance, which of the following could be used to represent the general solution for the position y(t)?

  1. y=Rsin⁡(3t+ϕ)+23tsin⁡(3t)
  2. y=Rcos⁡(3t+ϕ)−23tcos⁡(3t)
  3. y=Acos⁡(3t)+Bsin⁡(3t)−tsin⁡(3t)
Note that A, B, R, and ϕ are constants.

a
I only
b
I and II only
c
I and III only
d
II only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Because the motion is undamped and the system experiences resonance, the forcing frequency equals the natural frequency ω.

The external force is f(t)=4sin⁡(3t), which is sinusoidal with frequency 3. Therefore, ω=3.

The complementary solution for the associated simple harmonic motion equation can be written in three different forms:

  1. yc=Rcos⁡(ωt+ϕ)=Rcos⁡(3t+ϕ)

  2. yc=Rsin⁡(ωt+ϕ)=Rsin⁡(3t+ϕ)

  3. yc=Acos⁡ωt+Bsin⁡ωt=Acos⁡(3t)+Bsin⁡(3t)

At resonance, a sinusoidal trial at frequency ω duplicates the complementary solution, so we multiply by t and take a resonant particular solution of the form yp(t)=t(Ccos⁡(3t)+Dsin⁡(3t)).

By substituting the particular solution and ω=3 into the differential equation, y″+ω2y=f(t), we get [9(D−Ct)cos⁡(3t)−9(C+Dt)sin⁡(3t)]+9t(Ccos⁡(3t)+Dsin⁡(3t))=4sin⁡(3t)9Dcos⁡(3t)−9Csin⁡(3t)=4sin⁡(3t)

Equating coefficients, C=−23 and D=0.

So, the particular solution is yp(t)=−23tcos⁡(3t).

Finally, then, the general solution is given by the sum of the complementary and particular solutions. So, it can be written in any of the following forms:

  1. y=Rcos⁡(3t+ϕ)−23tcos⁡(3t)

  2. y=Rsin⁡(3t+ϕ)−23tcos⁡(3t)

  3. y=Acos⁡(3t)+Bsin⁡(3t)−23tcos⁡(3t)

Only solution II matches the forms above. Therefore, the correct answer is "II only".
