Recall that critical points of y=f(x) occur when f′(x)=0 or f′(x) does not exist. In our case, there are three critical points:

x=−3, x=−2, and x=−1.

Also, we have the following:

  • If f′(x)>0 then f(x) is increasing.

  • If f′(x)<0 then f(x) is decreasing.
