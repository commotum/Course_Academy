The tangent line approximation of f(x) at the point x=a is

L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a).

In our case, we have a=36, which gives L(x)=f(36)+f′(36)(x−36).

Let's compute f(36) and f′(36) in the expression above. Evaluating the function, we get f(36)=36=6, and computing the derivative, we get f′(x)=ddx(x)=12x, which in turn gives f′(36)=1236=112.

So, the tangent line approximation is L(x)=6+112(x−36).

We can approximate 37 by evaluating the tangent line approximation at x=37. We get 37≈L(37)=6+112(37−36)=6+112=7312.
