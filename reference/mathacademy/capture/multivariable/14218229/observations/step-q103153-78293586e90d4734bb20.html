Given that the power series ∑n=0∞an(x−4)n converges at x=−1, which of the following must be true?

  1. The series diverges at x=3
  2. The series converges at x=−2
  3. The series converges at x=1
a
I and III only
b
I and II only
c
II and III only
d
III only
e
I only

Correct
EXPLANATION

The center of the expansion is x=4, and since the series is convergent for x=−1, this means that the radius of convergence R is at least 5, i.e., R≥5. So, the series is convergent for every point in the interval [−1,9). However, we know nothing about the convergence of the series at any point outside the interval [−1,9).

With this in mind, let's check each of our statements.

  • Statement I is false. Since 3∈[−1,9), the series is convergent at x=3.

  • Statement II is false. Since −2∉[−1,9), we cannot guarantee that the series is convergent at x=−2.

  • Statement III is true. Since 1∈[−1,9), the series is convergent at x=1.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
