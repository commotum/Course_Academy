The power series ∑n=0∞an(x−2)n converges at x=0. Which of the following must be true?

  1. The series converges at x=1
  2. The series diverges at x=4
  3. The series diverges at x=−2
a
II and III only
b
I, II, and III
c
II only
d
III only
e
I only

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The center of the expansion is x=2, and since the series is convergent for x=0, this means that the radius of convergence R is at least 2, i.e., R≥2. So, the series is convergent for every point in the interval [0,4). However, we know nothing about the convergence of the series at any point outside the interval [0,4).

With this in mind, let's check each of our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since 1∈[0,4), the series is convergent at x=1.

  • Statement II is false. Since 4∉[0,4), we cannot guarantee that the series is divergent at x=4.

  • Statement III is false. Since −2∉[0,4), we cannot guarantee that the series is divergent at x=−2.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
