EXPLANATION

Let us consider the squared distance S between (2,2) and a general point P(x,y): S=(x−2)2+(y−2)2

We require that the point P lies on the curve y=|x−1|+2. Substituting this into the above gives

S=(x−2)2+(y−2)2=(x−2)2+(|x−1|+2−2)2=(x−2)2+|x−1|.

For x≥1, we have |x−1|=x−1, so S=(x−2)2+x−1=x2−4x+4+x−1=x2−3x+3.

To find the minimum distance, we take the first derivative of S(x) and equate it to zero. This gives the following: S′(x)=2x−3=02x=3x=32

We can see that S″(x)=2>0. Therefore, x=32 is a minimum of S.

Since S gives the squared distance between the two points, the actual distance d between the two points when x=32 is given by d=S(32).

Computing this quantity, we get d=S(32)=(32)2−3(32)+3=94−92+3=34=32.
