Since the growth rate of infected trees is jointly proportional to the number of trees that are infected at time t, and the number of uninfected trees, the number of infected trees follows a logistic growth model.

A logistic growth equation has the form

dPdt=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

In this case, the constant of proportionality is k=0.2, and the carrying capacity is a=300. Therefore, the number of trees that are infected is given by the equation

dPdt=0.2P(300−P).
