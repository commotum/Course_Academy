Emily bought 1 foot of gold wire and paid $5.60 for it. What's the unit price per yard?

Assume that there are 3 feet in one yard.

a
$18.20 per yard
b
$16.80 per yard
c
$12.25 per yard
d
$14.75 per yard
e
$15.60 per yard
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that there are 3 feet in each yard.

Since the price is $5.60 per foot, to find the price per yard, we multiply by 3:

5.60×3=16.80

Therefore, the gold wire costs $16.80 per yard.
