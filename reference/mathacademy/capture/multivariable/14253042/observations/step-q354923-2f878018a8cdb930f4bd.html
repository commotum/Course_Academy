A scientist bought 1 fluid ounce of a chemical solution and paid $1.60 for it. What's the unit price per cup?

Assume that there are 8 fluid ounces in one cup.

a
$14.40 per cup
b
$8.00 per cup
c
$10.20 per cup
d
$9.60 per cup
e
$12.80 per cup
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, notice that there are 8 fluid ounces in each cup.

Since the price is $1.60 per fluid ounce, to find the price per cup, we multiply by 8:

1.60×8=12.80

Therefore, the chemical solution costs $12.80 per cup.
