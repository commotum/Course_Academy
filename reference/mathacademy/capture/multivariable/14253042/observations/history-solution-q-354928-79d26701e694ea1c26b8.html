First, we convert from fluid ounces to cups. There are 8 fluid ounces in each cup, so we divide the number of fluid ounces by 8:

48÷8=6

So, 48 fluid ounces is the same as 6 cups. Now, to find the unit price per cup, we divide the total price by 6:

11.52÷6=1.92
