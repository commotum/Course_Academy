Emma bought 2 pounds of raisins for $4.80. What's the unit price per ounce?

Assume that there are 16 ounces in one pound.

a
$0.10 per ounce
b
$0.15 per ounce
c
$0.30 per ounce
d
$0.25 per ounce
e
$0.20 per ounce
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we convert from pounds to ounces. There are 16 ounces in each pound, so we multiply the number of pounds by 16:

2×16=32

So, 2 pounds is the same as 32 ounces. Now, to find the unit price per ounce, we divide the total price by 32:

4.80÷32=0.15

Therefore, the raisins cost $0.15 per ounce.
