Let's recall the following grammatical constructions for the biconditional P⇔Q:

  • P if and only if Q

  • P iff Q

  • P is necessary and sufficient for Q

  • P is equivalent to Q

  • If P then Q, and conversely

From the given options, the only one that is NOT a grammatical construction for P⇔Q is "Q whenever P" (this is the grammatical construction of P⇒Q).
