EXPLANATION

Let's recall the following grammatical constructions for the implication P⇒Q:

  • Whenever P, then Q

  • Q whenever P

  • Q, provided that P

Let's now compare these against the entries in our table:

  • "Whenever A, then B" means A⇒B

  • "B whenever A" means A⇒B

  • "A, provided that B" means B⇒A
