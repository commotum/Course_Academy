The area of one loop of the polar curve r=2cos⁡(2θ) (shown above) is given by the integral A=2∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ. Which of the following represents the total area bounded by the polar curve?

a
8∫π/4π/2cos2⁡(2θ)dθ
b
8∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ
c
4∫0π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ
d
12∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ
e
4∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The given polar curve is the polar rose with 2⋅2=4 petals, and we know that the area of the first petal is A=2∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ.

Therefore, the total area bounded by the polar curve is Atotal=4⋅A=8∫−π/4π/4cos2⁡(2θ)dθ.
