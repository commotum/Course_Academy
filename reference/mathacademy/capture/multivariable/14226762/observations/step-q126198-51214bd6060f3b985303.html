EXPLANATION

We are given that our curves intersect when θ1=π12 and θ2=5π12.

To determine the inner function and the outer, let's draw an arrow from the origin that passes through the shaded region.

The arrow

  • enters the region through the inner function r=12+sin⁡(2θ), and

  • leaves through the outer function r=2sin⁡(2θ).

To find the area, we use the formula

A=12∫θ1θ2([outer]2−[inner]2)dθ,

where

θ1=π12, and θ2=5π12.

Therefore, we obtain

A=12∫π/125π/12[(2sin⁡(2θ))2−(12+sin⁡(2θ))2]dθ=12∫π/125π/12[4sin2⁡(2θ)−(14+sin⁡(2θ)+sin2⁡(2θ))]dθ=12∫π/125π/12(3sin2⁡(2θ)−sin⁡(2θ)−14)dθ.
