EXPLANATION

We are given that our curves intersect when θ1=π12 and θ2=π4.

To determine the inner function and the outer, let's draw an arrow from the origin that passes through the shaded region.

The arrow

  • enters the region through the inner function r=cos⁡(2θ), and

  • leaves through the outer function r=sin⁡(4θ).

To find the area, we use the formula

A=12∫θ1θ2([outer]2−[inner]2)dθ,

where

θ1=π12, and θ2=π4.

Therefore, we obtain

A=12∫π/12π/4(sin2⁡(4θ)−cos2⁡(2θ))dθ.
