The definitions of cosh⁡x and sinh⁡x are

cosh⁡x=12(ex+e−x),sinh⁡x=12(ex−e−x).

Substituting the above definitions into the equation 5cosh⁡x−sinh⁡x=5, we get

5⋅(12(ex+e−x))−12(ex−e−x)=552ex+52e−x−12ex+12e−x=52ex+3e−x=5.

Next, we multiply both sides of the equation by ex, and simplify:

ex⋅(2ex+3e−x)=ex⋅52e2x+3=5ex2e2x−5ex+3=0

This is a quadratic equation in ex that we can factor as follows:

(2ex−3)(ex−1)=0

By the zero-product property, we have to solve two equations:

2ex−3=0,ex−1=0

Solving the two equations above for x, we get:

2ex−3=0ex−1=0ex=32ex=1ex=ln⁡(32)x=ln⁡1=0

Therefore, x=0 and x=ln⁡(32) are the real solutions to the equation.
