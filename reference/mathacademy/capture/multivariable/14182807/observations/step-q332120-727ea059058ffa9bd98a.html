EXPLANATION

The first shifting theorem states that if L{f(t)}=F(s), then L{eatf(t)}=F(s−a).

This implies that multiplying a function f(t) by eat shifts the corresponding Laplace transform horizontally along the s-axis.

  • If a>0, the graph of F(s) shifts to the right.

  • If a<0, the graph of F(s) shifts to the left.

In our case, to get the graph of L{etf(t)}, we must shift the graph of F(s) by 1 unit to the right.
