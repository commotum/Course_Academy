Correct
EXPLANATION

Writing the terms of the series explicitly, we have

f(x)=2x−34x2+⋯+(−1)n+16n(n+2)xn⏟the nth term+⋯.

Differentiating the above series term-by-term gives

f′(x)=2−32x+⋯+(−1)n+16n+2xn−1⏟the nth term+⋯.

f′(x)=∑n=1∞6n+2(−1)n+1xn−1
