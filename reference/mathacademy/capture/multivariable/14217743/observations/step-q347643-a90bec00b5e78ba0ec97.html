f(x)=3(x+2)−92(x+2)2+⋯+(−1)n+13nn(x+2)n⏟the nth term+⋯.

f′(x)=3−9(x+2)+⋯+(−1)n+13n(x+2)n−1⏟the nth term+⋯.
