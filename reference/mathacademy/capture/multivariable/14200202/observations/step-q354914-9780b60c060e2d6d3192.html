Sarah bought 1 ounce of almonds and paid $0.75 for it. What's the unit price per pound?

Assume that there are 16 ounces in one pound.

a
$20 per pound
b
$10 per pound
c
$12 per pound
d
$16 per pound
e
$14 per pound
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that there are 16 ounces in each pound.

Since the price is $0.75 per ounce, to find the price per pound, we multiply by 16:

0.75×16=12

Therefore, the almonds cost $12 per pound.
