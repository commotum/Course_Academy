First, we convert from pounds to ounces. There are 16 ounces in each pound, so we multiply the number of pounds by 16:

5×16=80

So, 5 pounds is the same as 80 ounces. Now, to find the unit price per ounce, we divide the total price by 80:

11.20÷80=0.14
