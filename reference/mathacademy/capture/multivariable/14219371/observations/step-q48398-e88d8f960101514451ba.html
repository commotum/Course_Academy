  • The function f(x) is continuous on [−1,2] but has a corner point at x=1. Hence, is not differentiable at x=1, which means it is not differentiable on (−1,2). Therefore, f(x) does not satisfy the conditions of the mean value theorem.

  • The function h(x) has a jump discontinuity at x=0. Hence, it is not continuous on [−1,2]. Therefore, h(x) does not satisfy the conditions of the mean value theorem.
