?

x≠5

The predicate P(x) is given above. Which of following represents ¬P(x)?

a
x≠−5
b
x<5
c
x>5
d
x=5
e
x≠15
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

In plain English, our predicate P(x) means the following:

x is not equal to 5.

The symbol "¬" represents negation. To negate our plain English expression, we place the words "It is not true that" in front of it. This gives the following:

It is not true that x is not equal to 5.

In other words, x is equal to 5. Therefore, ¬P(x) means the following:

x=5
