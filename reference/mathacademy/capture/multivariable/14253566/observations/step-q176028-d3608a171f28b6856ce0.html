The predicate P(x,y) is given above. Which of following represents ¬P(x,y)?

a
x≥y
b
x<y
c
x≤y
d
x≥−y
e
x>1y

Correct
EXPLANATION

In plain English, our predicate P(x,y) means the following:

x is greater than or equal to y.

The symbol "¬" represents negation. To negate our plain English expression, we place the words "It is not true that" in front of it. This gives the following:

It is not true that x is greater than or equal to y.

In other words, x is smaller than y. Therefore, ¬P(x,y) means the following:

x<y
