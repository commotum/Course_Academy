EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧", while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨".

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: 2 is prime. This corresponds to R.

  • Part 2: 14 is a multiple of 7 and 5. This corresponds to 14p is a multiple of 7⏟Spand⏟∧14p is a multiple of 5⏟T, that is, S∧T.

So, we can express the sentence as 2p is prime⏟Rpor⏟∨14p is a multiple of 7 and 5⏟S∧T,

that is, R∨(S∧T).
