Question 2

Let $\mathbf{v}_1=\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{v}_2=\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ be eigenvectors of a matrix $A$ that correspond to eigenvalues $\lambda_1=-3$ and $\lambda_2=3,$ respectively. What is the general solution of the matrix differential equation $\mathbf{x}'(t)=A\mathbf{x}(t)?$

a
 $\mathbf{x}(t) =c_1\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} e^{3t} + c_2 \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}e^{-3t}, \qquad c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{R}$
b
 $\mathbf{x}(t) =-3\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} e^{c_1t} + 3\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}e^{c_2 t}, \qquad c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{R}$
c
 $\mathbf{x}(t) =c\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} e^{-3t}, \qquad c\in\mathbb{R}$
d
 $\mathbf{x}(t) =c_1\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} e^{-3t} + c_2 \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}e^{3t}, \qquad c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{R}$
e
 $\mathbf{x}(t) =c \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}e^{3t}, \qquad c\in\mathbb{R}$

Question 4

Consider the vector field $\mathbf F$ and the surface $S$ with parametrization $\mathbf r(u,v),$ where

\[ \mathbf{F}(x,y,z) =\left\langle -2x,\: -2y,\: -z \right\rangle, \quad \mathbf{r}(u,v) = \left\langle u^3,\: v^3 ,\: uv \right\rangle, \quad (u,v)\in D\subset \mathbb{R}^2. \]

If the flux of $\mathbf F$ across $S$ with respect to $\mathbf{n}$ is given by

\[ \iint\limits_S \mathbf{F} \cdot \textrm{d}\mathbf{S} = \iint\limits_D \fbox{$\phantom{AAAAAAA}$} \:\textrm{d}A \]

what is the missing expression?

Hint: You can use the fact that $\mathbf{r}'_u \times \mathbf{r}'_v = \left\langle -3 v^3,\: -3 u^3,\: 9 u^2v^2 \right\rangle.$

a
 $2u^2v^2(uv+1)$
b
 $3u^3v^3$
c
 $u^2v^2(3u+2v)$
d
 $3u^2v^2(u+v)$
e
 $6u^3v^3$
