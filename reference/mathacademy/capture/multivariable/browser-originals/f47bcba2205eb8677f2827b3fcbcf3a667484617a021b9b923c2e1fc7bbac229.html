Question 1

A $2 \times 3$ matrix $A$ maps the unit sphere $\big\{ \mathbf{x} \,|\, \| \mathbf{x} \| =1 \big\}$ from $\mathbb{R}^3$ onto an ellipse in $\mathbb{R}^2.$ Given that the eigenvalues of $A^T A$ are $11,$ $9,$ and $0,$ find the length of the ellipse's semi-major axis.

a
 $\sqrt{11}$
b
 $0$
c
 $\sqrt{10}$
d
 $9$
e
 $11$
Question 2

\[ R \, \bbox[5px, border: 1px solid black]{\phantom{A}} = Q^T \, \bbox[5px, border: 1px solid black]{\phantom{A}} \]

Let $A=QR$ be a QR decomposition of an $m \times n$ matrix $A$ with linearly independent columns, and the matrix equation above gives the least-squares solution of $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}.$ From left to right, what are the missing parts of the equation?

a
 $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{b}^T$
b
 $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{x}$
c
 $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{b}$
d
 $\dfrac{1}{\Vert \mathbf b \Vert} \cdot \mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{x}$
e
 $\mathbf{x}$ and $\dfrac{1}{\Vert \mathbf b \Vert} \cdot \mathbf{b}$
Question 3

a
 $ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $
b
 $ \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} $
c
 $ \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $
d
 $ \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} $

Question 4

Consider the following set of polynomials: \[ p_1(t)=t-1, \qquad p_2(t)=2t+2, \qquad p_3(t)=2-2t. \] Given that the inner product in the vector space $\mathbb{R}_1[t]$ of polynomials of degree at most $1$ in the variable $t$ is defined as \[ \langle p(t),q(t) \rangle =p(-1)q(-1)+p(1)q(1), \] which of the following statements are true?

  1. $p_1(t) \not\perp p_2(t)$ with respect to the given inner product
  2. $p_2(t) \perp p_3(t)$ with respect to the given inner product
  3. $S=\{ p_1(t), p_2(t), p_3(t) \}$ is an orthogonal set with respect to the given inner product
a
 I only
b
 I and II only
c
 II only
d
 II and III only
e
 None of the statements are true
Question 5

The linear transformation $\mathbf T$ has the standard matrix \[ T = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & -2 \end{bmatrix}. \] What are the respective images of the standard basis vectors $\{ \mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j} \}$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ under the action of $\mathbf T?$

a
 $\begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 3\\ -2 \end{bmatrix}$
b
 $\begin{bmatrix} 3\\ -2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$
c
 $\begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 3 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 1\\ -2 \end{bmatrix}$
d
 $\begin{bmatrix} 2\\ -2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} 3\\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$
e
 $\begin{bmatrix} 3\\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\begin{bmatrix} -2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$
Question 6

Let $V = \mathcal{C}[-1,1],$ the vector space of continuous functions over $[-1,1]$, and let the inner product on $V$ be defined as \[ \langle x(t),y(t) \rangle =\int_{-1}^{1} x(t) y(t) \, \text{d}t. \]

Now consider the following elements of $V\mathbin{:}$

\[ f_1(t)=3 t,\quad f_2(t)=t^2+1, \quad g(t)=10t^3 \]

Find the orthogonal projection of $g(t)$ onto $S=\text{Span}\{f_1(t), f_2(t)\}$ given that $f_1(t) \perp f_2(t)$ with respect to the inner product, and that the orthogonal projection of $g(t)$ onto $\textrm{Span}\{f_1(t)\}$ equals $6t.$

a
 $t^2+1$
b
 $6t$
c
 $t^2+6t+1$
d
 $t^2-1$
e
 $10t^3+3t$

b

 No intersection
