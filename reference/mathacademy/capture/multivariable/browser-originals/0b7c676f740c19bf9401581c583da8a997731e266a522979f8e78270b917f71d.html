Question 1

Find the average value $g_{\textrm{avg}}$ of the function $g(x) = 6\cos 3x - 4\cos x$ on the interval $\left[-\dfrac \pi 4, \dfrac \pi 4\right].$ Do not round the answer.

Question 2
A calculator is required to answer the following question.

The number $y(t)$ of people who have adopted electric vehicles in a town follows the logistic growth model \[ y' = 4200y - 7y^2, \qquad y(0) = 55, \] where $t$ is the time, in years, since the observations began. According to this model, what is the maximum possible number of people who will adopt electric vehicles?

Question 3

a
 $\displaystyle2\pi \int_{0}^{4} e^{t}\,\textrm d t$
b
 $\displaystyle 2\pi \int_{0}^{4} e^{t/2}\sqrt{2e^t+4}\,\textrm d t$
c
 $\displaystyle4\pi \int_{0}^{4} e^{t}\sqrt{e^t+1}\,\textrm d t$
d
 $\displaystyle4\pi \int_{0}^{4} e^{t/2}\,\textrm d t$
e

Question 5

Consider the arc of the curve $x = -y^2 - 5y - 4$ that lies in the fourth quadrant between its $y$-intercepts. What integral gives the surface area generated when this arc is rotated $2\pi$ radians about the $y$-axis?

a
 $\displaystyle-2\pi\sqrt{2} \int_{-5}^{-2} y\sqrt{y^2 + 5y + 1} \: \textrm{d}y$
b
 $\displaystyle -2\pi\sqrt{2} \int_{-4}^{-1} \left(2y + 5\right) \sqrt{2y^2 + 10y + 13} \: \textrm{d}y$
c
 $\displaystyle -2\pi\sqrt{2} \int_{-4}^{-1} \left(y^2 + 5y + 4\right) \sqrt{y^2 + 5y + 1} \: \textrm{d}y$
d
 $\displaystyle -2\pi\sqrt{2} \int_{-5}^{-2}y \sqrt{2y^2 + 10y + 13} \: \textrm{d}y$
e
 $\displaystyle -2\pi\sqrt{2} \int_{-4}^{-1} \left(y^2 + 5y + 4\right) \sqrt{2y^2 + 10y + 13} \: \textrm{d}y$
