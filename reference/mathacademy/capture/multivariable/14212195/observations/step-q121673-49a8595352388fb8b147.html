A beverage is pumped into a conical dispenser at a constant rate of 19.6πcm3/min. The mouth of the tank is 70cm in diameter and the height of the tank is 40cm. How fast is the beverage level increasing when the volume of the beverage in the dispenser is 12.25πcm3? Round your answer to two decimal places.

a
1.45cm/min
b
1.94cm/min
c
1.14cm/min

1.87cm/min

1.31cm/min

EXPLANATION

First, let's consider the dimensions of the tank. Let's call H=40cm the height of the tank, and R=702=35cm its radius at the top. These quantities are fixed and do not change with time.

Now, let's consider the solution level. We call h the solution level's height and r the radius of the cone formed by the solution. Both of these quantities, and the solution volume v, change with time. In particular, we know that

dvdt=19.6πcm3/min,

and we want to find dhdt when v=12.25πcm3.

We need to find a relation between h and the variable for which we know the rate of change, which is v. This is given by the formula for the volume of a cone:

v=13πr2h

Before we differentiate the equation with respect to time, note that r also appears in the formula. So, we need to simplify the formula by writing r in terms of h.

Note that the cones formed by the tank and the solution must be similar. Therefore, we must have

rh=RHrh=3540r=7h8.

Therefore, we can write the volume formula as

v=13πr2h=13π(7h8)2h=49192πh3.

Now that we have an equation relating v and h, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule:

ddtv=ddt(49192πh3)dvdt=4964πh2dhdt.

We want to find the rate at which the level of the beverage in the dispenser is changing when v=12.25πcm3. We can compute the value of h at this moment as follows:

12.25π=49192πh3h3=48h=263cm.

Finally, we plug in the known values into the formula for dvdt, and we get

19.6π=4964π(263)2dhdt19.6π=4936316πdhdtdhdt≈1.94cm/min rounded to two decimal places.

Therefore, the solution level is increasing at a rate of approximately 1.94cm/min.
