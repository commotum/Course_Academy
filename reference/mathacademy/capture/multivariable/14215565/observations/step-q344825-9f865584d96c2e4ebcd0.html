Find the y-coordinate of the point on the curve y=x2−2x that is closest to the point (22,0). Do not round the answer.

EXPLANATION

Let us consider the squared distance S between (22,0) and a general point P(x,y):

S=(x−22)2+(y−0)2=x2−2x+12+y2

We require that the point P lies on the curve y=x2−2x. Substituting this into the above gives

S(y)=y+12+y2=y2+y+12.

To find the minimum distance, we take the first derivative of S(y) and equate it to zero. This gives the following:

S′(y)=2y+1=02y=−1y=−12

We can see that S″(y)=2>0. Therefore, y=−12 is a minimum of S.

So, the squared distance (and therefore the actual distance) is minimized when y=−12.
