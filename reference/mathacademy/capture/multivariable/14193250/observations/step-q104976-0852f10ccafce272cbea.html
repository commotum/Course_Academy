EXPLANATION

Comparing these limits with the formula for the instantaneous rate of change

f′(a)=limh→0f(a+h)−f(a)h, we see that

limh→0f(h)−f(0)h=f′(0),limh→0f(1+h)−f(1)h=f′(1),limh→0f(3+h)−f(3)h=f′(3).

The function consists of three line segments. Inspecting the graph, we see that

  • for −4<x<−1, we have f′(x)=−1,

  • for −1<x<1, we have f′(x)=0,

  • for 1<x<4, we have f′(x)=−1,

The derivative does not exist at x=±1. So, we have

limh→0f(h)−f(0)h=0,limh→0f(1+h)−f(1)h=DNE,limh→0f(3+h)−f(3)h=−1.

Therefore, only statements I and III are true.
