∂f1∂x=∂∂x(5x(1−2x)−xy)=5−20x−y,∂f1∂y=∂∂y(5x(1−2x)−xy)=−x∂f2∂x=∂∂x(−2y+3xy)=3y,∂f2∂y=∂∂y(−2y+3xy)=−2+3x
