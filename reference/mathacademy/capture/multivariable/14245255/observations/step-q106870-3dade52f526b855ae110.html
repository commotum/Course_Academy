What is the dimension of R13[t], the vector space of all polynomials in the variable t of degree at most 13?

a
26
b
12
c
15
d
13
e
14
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The standard basis of Rn[t] is

{1,t,t2,…,tn}.

So, the standard basis of R13[t] is

{1,t,t2,…,t13}.

Therefore, we have that

dim(R13[t])=13+1=14.

In general,

dim(Rn[t])=n+1.
