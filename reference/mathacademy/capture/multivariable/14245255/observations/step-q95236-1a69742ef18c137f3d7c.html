Which of the following vector spaces are finite-dimensional?

  1. ℓ2, the vector space of infinite real sequences where the sum of the squares of the terms are finite
  2. R2[t], the vector space of all polynomials in the variable t of degree at most 2
  3. M100×1000(R), the vector space of all 100×1000 matrices over R
a
II and III only
b
I and III only
c
III only
d
I, II, and III
e
I and II only

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's examine our vector spaces.

  • ℓ2 is not finite-dimensional. To understand why, we first note that the following subset of ℓ2 is linearly independent: {e1,e2,…,en},n∈N where, for each j∈{1,2,…,n}, ej=(0,…,0,1,↑jth position0,…). Indeed, we have that ∑i=0nxiei=0 if and only if xi=0 for all i=0,1,2,…,n. Therefore, for any n∈N, there exists a linearly independent subset of ℓ2 of size n, which imples that ℓ2 cannot have finite dimension.

  • R2[t] and M100×1000(R) are finite-dimensional. Indeed, dim(R2[t])=2+1=3, and dim(M100×1000(R))=100⋅1000=100000 are both finite numbers.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
