Writing our system in matrix form, we have x′(t)=[1002]x(t) where x(t)=[x(t)y(t)].

Since our system is decoupled, its eigenvectors are the following:

  • Eigenvalue λ1=1 with the corresponding eigenvector [10].

  • Eigenvalue λ2=2 with the corresponding eigenvector [01].

The general solution of the system is x(t)=c1et[10]+c2e2t[01].

Now, notice that:

  • The straight-line solutions of the form c1et[10] lie on the x-axis. Since et→∞ as t→∞, these solutions approach infinity as t→∞.

  • The straight-line solutions of the form c2e2t[01] lie on the y-axis. Since e2t→∞ as t→∞, these solutions approach infinity as t→∞.
