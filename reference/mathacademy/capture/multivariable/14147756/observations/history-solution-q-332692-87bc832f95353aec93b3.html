Since our system is decoupled, its eigenvectors are parallel to the axes. These correspond to so-called straight-line solutions of the form c1eλ1t[10]andc2eλ2t[01].

Now, notice that:

  • The tangents along the y-axis are pointing toward the origin, meaning that the solutions of the form c2eλ2t[01] must approach the origin as t→∞. So, we must have λ2<0.
