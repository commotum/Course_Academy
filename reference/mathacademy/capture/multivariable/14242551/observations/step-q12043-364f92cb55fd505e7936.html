What linear transformation T is represented by the matrix T=[120012]?

a
Reflection in the line x=12
b
Reflection in the line y=12
c
Dilation with scale factor 112
d
Dilation with scale factor 12
e
Reflection across the y-axis
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that a dilation of scale factor k is represented by the matrix [k00k].

Here, we have k=12. Therefore, the matrix represents a dilation with a scale factor of 12.
