The region enclosed by the curves x+2y2=1 and x=2sin2⁡(πy3) for y∈[−12,12] is rotated 2π radians about the line x=1. Which of the following gives the volume of the resulting solid?

a
π∫−1/21/2(cos2⁡(2πy3)−4y4)dy
b
π∫−1/21/2(sin2⁡(πy3)−4y4)dy
c
π∫−1/21/2(sin2⁡(2πy3)−4y4)dy
d
π∫−1/21/2(cos2⁡(πy3)−(1−2y2)2)dy
e
π∫−1/21/2(cos2⁡(2πy3)−(1−2y2)2)dy
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, since we are rotating about a vertical line, we need to solve the equation of our first curve for x:

x+2y2=1⟹x=1−2y2

Now, let's draw a horizontal arrow through the given region. The arrow should be pointing away from the axis of rotation.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the inner curve x=1−2y2, and

  • leaves through the outer curve x=2sin2⁡(πy3).

Next, we can find the volume of our solid as a difference between the volumes of the solids generated by the outer and inner curves.

  • Step I. Figure out the volume generated by the outer curve x=2sin2⁡(πy3).

The corresponding volume is given by Vouter=π∫−1/21/2[(right)−(left)]2dy=π∫−1/21/2[(1)−(2sin2⁡(πy3))]2dy=π∫−1/21/2[1−2sin2⁡(πy3)]2dy=π∫−1/21/2[cos⁡(2πy3)]2dy.

  • Step II. Figure out the volume generated by the inner curve x=1−2y2.

The corresponding volume is given by Vinner=π∫−1/21/2[(right)−(left)]2dy=π∫−1/21/2[(1)−(1−2y2)]2dy=π∫−1/21/2[2y2]2dy.

  • Step III. Find the difference.

Finally, we can write the volume of the original solid as V=Vouter−Vinner=π∫−1/21/2((cos⁡(2πy3))2−(2y2)2)dy=π∫−1/21/2(cos2⁡(2πy3)−4y4)dy.
