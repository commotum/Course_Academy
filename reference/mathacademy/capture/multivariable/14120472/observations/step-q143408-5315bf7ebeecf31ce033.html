The time it takes John to get to school, in minutes, is normally distributed with a mean of 20min and a standard deviation of 6min. What is the probability that the sum of the times John takes to get to school on 5 randomly selected school days is more than 90min? You may assume that the times of each day are mutually independent.

EXPLANATION

Let Xi denote the time it took John to get to school on the ith day. Then, we have

Xi∼N(20,62),1≤i≤5.

Let the random variable W denote the time John takes to get to school on 5 randomly selected days. Then, since

W=X1+X2+⋯+X5,

we have that W is normally distributed, where

E[W]=5⋅20=100,Var[W]=5⋅62=180.

Therefore,

W∼N(100,180).

In order to find P(W>90), we convert W to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(W>90)=P(Z>90−100180)≈P(Z>−0.75)=P(Z<0.75)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(0.75)≈0.7734.

Therefore,

P(W>90)≈0.7734.
