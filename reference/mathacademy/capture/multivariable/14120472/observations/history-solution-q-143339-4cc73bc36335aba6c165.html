Recall that if X1,X2,…,Xn are independent and identically distributed (I.I.D) normal random variables, where

Xi∼N(μ,σ2),1≤i≤n,

then

Y=X1+X2+⋯+Xn∼N(nμ,nσ2).

Therefore, since X1,X2,…,X15 are independent and identically distributed, we have

Y=X1+X2+⋯+X15∼N(15⋅0,15⋅(25)2)∼N(15⋅0,15⋅425)=N(0,125).

We wish to compute the probability P(Y≥1). We first convert Y into a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(Y≥1)=P(Z≥1−0125)=P(Z≥512)=P(Z≥0.65)=1−P(Z≤0.65)=1−Φ(0.65)

From the z-table, we have

Φ(0.65)=0.7422.

Therefore,

P(Y≥1)=1−Φ(0.65)=0.2578.
