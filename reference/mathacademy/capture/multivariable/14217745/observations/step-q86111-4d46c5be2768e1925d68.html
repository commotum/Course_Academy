Consider the finite region bounded by y=8x and y=x4. Which of the following gives the volume of the solid generated when that region is rotated 2π radians about the x-axis?

a
π∫02(6x2−x6)dx
b
π∫02(4x2−x8)dx
c
π∫02(64x3−x9)dx
d
π∫02(64x2−x8)dx
e
π∫04(64x2−x8)dx
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let f(x)=8x and g(x)=x4.

First, we determine the limits of integration. To do that, we find the intersections of the curves by solving the following equation for x: f(x)=g(x)8x=x4x4−8x=0x(x3−8)=0 Hence, the limits of integration are from x=0 to x=2.

Let's draw our vertical arrow through the region to determine the inner function and the outer function. The arrow should be pointing away from the axis of rotation.

The vertical arrow

  • enters the region through the inner function y=g(x)=x4, and

  • leaves through the outer function y=f(x)=8x.

Therefore, the integral that gives our volume is

V=π∫ab([outer]2−[inner]2)dx=π∫02([8x]2−[x4]2)dx=π∫02(64x2−x8)dx.
