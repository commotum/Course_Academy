Some viral content on the internet starts spreading through an audience of 60000 people. The rate of growth in the number of people P(t) who have seen the content is jointly proportional to the number of people who have seen the content at time t, measured in hours, and the number who have not. If the constant of proportionality is 0.75, which differential equation could be used to model P(t)?

a
dPdt=−0.75P(60000−P)
b
dPdt=60000P(0.75+P)
c
dPdt=60000P(0.75−P)
d
dPdt=0.75P(60000−P)
e
dPdt=0.75P(60000+P)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the growth rate of people who have seen the content is jointly proportional to the number of people who have seen the content at time t, and the number who have not, the number of people who have seen the content follows a logistic growth model.

A logistic growth equation has the form

dPdt=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

In this case, the constant of proportionality is k=0.75, and the carrying capacity is a=60000. Therefore, the number of people who have seen the content is given by the equation

dPdt=0.75P(60000−P).
