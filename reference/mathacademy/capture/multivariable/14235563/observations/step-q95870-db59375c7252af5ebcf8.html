Which of the following equations defines a parabola that opens in the direction of the positive x1-axis, in the x1x2-plane?

a
C4:x12=4
b
C1:x12+x22=0
c
C2:x124−x229=1
d
C3:3x22=x1
e
C5:x12+x224=−1
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The correct answer is

C3:3x22=x1.

This equation defines a parabola that opens in the direction of the positive x1-axis.

Notice that the other equations do not define a parabola:

  • C1:x12+x22=0 defines a single point (0,0) in the x1x2-plane.

  • C2:x124−x229=1 is the equation of a hyperbola with semiaxes 2 and 3.

  • C4:x12=4 represents two parallel lines in the x1x2-plane: x1=2 and x1=−2.

  • C5:x12+x224=−1 has no points in the x1x2-plane that satisfy the equation (imaginary ellipse).
