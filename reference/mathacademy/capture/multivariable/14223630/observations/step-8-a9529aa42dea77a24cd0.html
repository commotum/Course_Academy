Let a denote the approximation of g(1) found in part 5. According to the alternating series error bound, we have that |g(1)−a|<k for some real number k. Find the value of k.
k=
140​

Correct
Explanation

First, notice that g(1) represents the sum of the convergent alternating series, while the approximation a represents the fourth partial sum of this series:

g(1)=S=∑n=1∞an,a=s4

As a result,

|g(1)−a|=|S−s4|.

We also know that a5=140. Therefore, according to the alternating series error bound, we obtain that

|S−s4|<|a5|=|140|=140.
