Find the value of a5, the fifth nonzero term of the alternating series S=∑n=1∞an.
a5=
140​

Correct
Explanation

First, notice that the terms of our series S=∑n=1∞an are the coefficients of the Maclaurin series for g(x).

Previously, we found the fourth-degree Taylor polynomial of f(x):

f(x)≈P4(x)=2−12x+14x2−16x3+18x4

To find the x5 term of the Maclaurin series of g(x), we simply need to integrate the x4 term of P4(x):

∫0x18t4dx=18∫0xt4dx=140t5|0x=140x5

Finally, a5 is the coefficient of 140x5, that is, a5=140.
