Recall that

f″(x)=(f′(x))′.

Notice that 4∈[3,6], where the graph of y=f′(x) is represented by a straight line segment. So, f″(4) is just the slope of this segment.

Since this segment passes through the points (x1,y1)=(3,0) and (x2,y2)=(6,−4), we can compute its slope as follows:

y2−y1x2−x1=−4−06−3=−43=−43

Therefore, f″(4)=−43.
