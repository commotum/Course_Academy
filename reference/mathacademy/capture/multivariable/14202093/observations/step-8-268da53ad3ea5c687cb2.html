Explanation

Recall that

f″(x)=ddx(f′(x))

is the slope of the tangent line to the graph of y=f′(x).

Notice that the graph of y=f′(x) on x∈[−3,3] is a semicircle of a radius 3 centered at (0,0). Its equation (for y>0) is given by

(x−0)2+(y−0)2=32⟹x2+y2=9.

Now, to calculate dydx, we use implicit differentiation:

ddx(x2+y2)=ddx(9)ddx(x2)+ddx(y2)=02x+2ydydx=02ydydx=−2xdydx=−xy

Therefore, we obtain

f″(2)=dydx|(2,5)=−25.
