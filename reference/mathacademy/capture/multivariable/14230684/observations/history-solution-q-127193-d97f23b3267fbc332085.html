If F is a vector field and C is a piecewise-smooth, simple, closed curve, then the flux of F across C is given by ∮CF⋅nds, where n denotes the outward-pointing unit normal vector to C. In this case, we have

∮CF⋅nds=∫C1F⋅n1ds+∫C2F⋅n2ds+∫C3F⋅n3ds+∫C4F⋅n4ds,

where the sides Ci and unit vectors ni are shown below.

Now, since the vector field F=−3j is parallel to C1 and C3, there is zero net flow through these two sides. Therefore,

∫C1F⋅n1ds=∫C3F⋅n3ds=0.

So, our expression for the flux reduces to ∮CF⋅nds=∫C2F⋅n2ds+∫C4F⋅n4ds.

Now, since the vector field is uniform, the net inflow through C2 will cancel with the net outflow through C4. That is

∫C2F⋅n2ds=−∫C4F⋅n4ds⟹∫C2F⋅n2ds+∫C4F⋅n4ds=0.

Therefore, we conclude that

∮CF⋅nds=0.
