There are three supermarket chains in a particular city: chain A, chain B, and chain C. A study has shown that, each month

  • from the customers that prefer chain A, 5% change to chain B and 15% change to chain C,

  • from the customers that prefer chain B, 25% change to chain A and 25% change to chain C,

  • from the customers that prefer chain C, 15% change to chain A and 35% change to chain B.

The stochastic matrix that describes this situation is given below. What is the value of a+b+c?

P=[0.8a0.15b0.50.350.150.25c]

a
0.8

1.1

EXPLANATION

We are given the following situation for each month:

  • From the customers that prefer chain A, 5% change to chain B and 15% change to chain C. This implies that 80% of them continue to prefer chain A.

  • From the customers that prefer chain B, 25% change to chain A and 25% change to chain C. This implies that 50% of them continue to prefer chain B.

  • From the customers that prefer chain C, 15% change to chain A and 35% change to chain B. This implies that 50% of them continue to prefer chain C.

Writing this information in tabular form, we obtain the following:

From:
Chain A Chain B Chain C
To: Chain A 0.8 0.25 0.15
Chain B 0.05 0.5 0.35
Chain C 0.15 0.25 0.5

We can write the corresponding stochastic matrix as follows:

P=[0.80.250.150.050.50.350.150.250.5].

Finally, we have

a+b+c=0.25+0.05+0.5=0.8.
