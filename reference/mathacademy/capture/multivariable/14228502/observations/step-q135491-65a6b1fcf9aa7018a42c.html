In a particular town, the housekeepers use 2 types of olive oil for preparing food: oil A and oil B. A study has shown that, each month

  • 40% of housekeepers who used oil A swap to oil B, and

  • 30% of housekeepers who used oil B swap to oil A.

Which of the following tables describes how these housekeepers change their preferences each month?

a
From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.7 0.6
Oil B 0.3 0.4
b
From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.6 0.3
Oil B 0.4 0.7
c
From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.3 0.6
Oil B 0.7 0.4
d
From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.6 0.7
Oil B 0.4 0.3
e
From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.4 0.3
Oil B 0.6 0.7
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are given the following situation for each month:

  • 40% of housekeepers who used oil A swap to oil B. This implies that 60% of them remain using oil A.

  • 30% of housekeepers who used oil B swap to oil A. This implies that 70% of them remain using oil B.

Writing this information in tabular form, we obtain the following:

From:
Oil A Oil B
To: Oil A 0.6 0.3
Oil B 0.4 0.7

Note that we can write a corresponding stochastic matrix as follows:

[0.60.30.40.7].
