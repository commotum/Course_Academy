There are three groups of fans in a certain town: those who prefer basketball, those who prefer baseball, and those who prefer football. A study has shown that, each year

  • from the people that prefer basketball, 32% change to baseball and 28% change to football,

  • from the people that prefer baseball, 45% change to football and 15% change to basketball,

  • from the people that prefer football, 25% change to basketball and 65% change to baseball.

The stochastic matrix that describes this situation is given below. What is the value of a+b+c?

P=[0.40.15a0.32b0.65c0.450.1]

a
0.99
b
1
c
0.87

1.07

0.93

Correct
EXPLANATION

We are given the following situation for each year:

  • From the people who prefer basketball, 32% change to baseball and 28% change to football. This implies that 40% of them continue to prefer basketball.

  • From people who prefer baseball, 45% change to football and 15% change to basketball. This implies that 40% of them continue to prefer baseball.

  • From people who prefer football, 25% change to basketball and 65% change to baseball. This implies that 10% of them continue to prefer football.

Writing this information in tabular form, we obtain the following:

From:
Basketball Baseball Football
To: Basketball 0.4 0.15 0.25
Baseball 0.32 0.4 0.65
Football 0.28 0.45 0.1

We can write the corresponding stochastic matrix as follows:

P=[0.40.150.250.320.40.650.280.450.1].

Finally, we have

a+b+c=0.25+0.4+0.28=0.93.
