Recall that a stochastic vector is a vector with nonnegative entries that add up to 1.

With that in mind, let's examine our vectors in turn.

  • Vector u is not a stochastic vector since the entries do not add up to one: 0+0.5=0.5≠1
  • Vector v is not a stochastic vector since it contains negative entries.

  • Vector w is a stochastic vector. Indeed,

    • its entries are all nonnegative, and

    • the entries add up to one: 0+0.1+0.9=1

Therefore, the correct answer is "w only."
