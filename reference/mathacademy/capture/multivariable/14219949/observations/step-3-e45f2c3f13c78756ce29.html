Use the tangent line approximation of f(x) found in part 1 to estimate the value of f(4.1).
f(4.1)≈
−5.7
Submit
Correct
Explanation

Our tangent line approximation is

L(x)=−7x+23.

We can approximate f(4.1) by evaluating the tangent line approximation at x=4.1. We get

f(4.1)≈L(4.1)=−7(4.1)+23=−5.7.
