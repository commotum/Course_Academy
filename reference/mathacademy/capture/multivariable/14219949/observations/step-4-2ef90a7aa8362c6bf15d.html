Write the second-degree Taylor polynomial of f(x) about x=3.
P2(x)=
6−5(x−3)−3(x−3)2
Submit
Correct
Explanation

To find the second-degree Taylor polynomial about x=3, we plug a=3 into the general formula to give

P2(x)=f(3)+f′(3)(x−3)+12f″(3)(x−3)2.

Plugging in f(3)=6, f′(3)=−5, and f″(3)=−6 into our second-degree Taylor polynomial gives

P2(x)=6−5(x−3)+12(−6)(x−3)2=6−5(x−3)−3(x−3)2.
