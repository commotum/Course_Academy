EXPLANATION

We want to determine the limits of integration. We find the intersection of the curve and the line x=4 by solving the following equation: y+1=4y=3

So, the limits of integration are from y=0 to y=3 along the y-axis.

To determine which is the left and which is the right function, let's draw our horizontal arrow.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the left function x=y+1, and

  • leaves through the right function x=4.
