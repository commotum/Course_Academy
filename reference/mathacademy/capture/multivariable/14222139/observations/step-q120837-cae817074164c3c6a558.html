The region enclosed by the curve y=−1−x, the vertical line x=−3, and the horizontal line y=0 is rotated 2π radians about the line x=−3, as shown in the picture. Which of the following gives the volume of the resulting solid?

a
∫02(y2+2y+2)dy
b
π∫01(2−y)dy
c
∫03(y2−y+2)dy
d
π∫02(y2−4y+4)dy
e
π∫03/2(y2+4y+4)dy
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since our region rotates about a vertical line, we need to write the equation of the given curve in terms of y:

y=−1−x⟹x=−1−y

Now, we want to determine the limits of integration. We find the intersections of the curve and the line x=−3 by solving the following equation:

−1−y=−3y=2

So, the limits of integration are from y=0 to y=2 along the y-axis.

To determine which is the left and which is the right function, let's draw our horizontal arrow.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the left function x=−3, and

  • leaves through the right function x=−1−y.

Therefore, the integral that gives our volume is

V=π∫02[(right function)−(left function)]2dy=π∫02[(−1−y)−(−3)]2dy=π∫02(2−y)2dy=π∫02(y2−4y+4)dy.
