EXPLANATION

We want to determine the limits of integration. We find the intersection of the curve and the line x=5 by solving the following equation: y2+1=5y2=4y=2

Since y≥0, the limits of integration are from y=0 to y=2 along the y-axis.

To determine which is the left and which is the right function, let's draw our horizontal arrow.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the left function x=y2+1, and

  • leaves through the right function x=5.

Therefore, the integral that gives our volume is V=π∫02[(right function)−(left function)]2dy=π∫02[5−(y2+1)]2dy=π∫02(4−y2)2dy=π∫02(16−8y2+y4)dy=π(16y−83y3+15y5)|02=π(32−643+325)=256π15.
