The area of a square increases at a rate of 24 cm2/min. Given that x and A denote the side length and area of the square, respectively, find an expression for dxdt, where t is the time in minutes, as a function of x.
dxdt=
−12x​

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Here, the area is changing at a constant rate, and we want to know the rate at which the side lengths are changing.

First, let's draw a diagram:

We want to find dxdt. The area A is related to side length x by the formula

A=x2.

Differentiating this relationship with respect to t using implicit differentiation, we get

ddt(A)=ddt(x2)dAdt=dxdt⋅ddx(x2)dAdt=dxdt⋅2x.

We know from the given information that

dAdt=24 cm2/min.

Substituting this information, we reach

24=dxdt⋅2xdxdt=12x.
