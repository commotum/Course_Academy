Suppose X1,X2,…,Xn is a sequence of random variables representing a random sample of size n drawn from a population with population mean μ. Then, the sample mean X― is given by

X―=1n∑i=1nXi.

Since X― is an unbiased estimator of μ, we have

E[X―]=μ.

We're told that the nails in the population have lengths 6cm and 12cm, and these are distributed in the ratio 3:1. Therefore, to compute the population mean, we can consider a population consisting of 3+1=4 nails only with the following lengths:

6cm,6cm,6cm,12cm.

Therefore, the population mean is

μ=14∑ixi=14(3⋅6+12)=14(18+12)=304=7.5cm.

Finally, E[X―]=μ=7.5cm.
