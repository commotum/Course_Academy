EXPLANATION

Recall that the correlation coefficient of random variables X and Y is defined as

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[Y],

where

  • Cov[X,Y] is the covariance, and

  • SD[X] and SD[Y] are the standard deviations of X and Y.

First, by applying the rule of the lazy statistician, we have

E[XY]=∑x∈SX∑y∈SYxyf(x,y)=0⋅0⋅0.1+0⋅1⋅0.1+0⋅2⋅0.2=+1⋅0⋅0.3+1⋅1⋅0.1+1⋅2⋅0.2=0.5.

Then, we compute the covariance and get

Cov[X,Y]=E[XY]−E[X]⋅E[Y]=0.5−(0.6)(1)=−0.1.

Therefore, the correlation coefficient is

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[Y]≈−0.10.4899⋅0.8944≈0.23,

rounded to 2 decimal places.
