EXPLANATION

If a relation R is defined over A={a1,a2,…,am} and B={b1,b2,…bn} of cardinalities m and n, respectively, the adjacency matrix of R is a m×n-matrix (or table), where the element rij at the intersection of the ith row and the jth column is given by rij={1,if aiRbj,0,otherwise.

With that in mind, let's examine the given ordered pairs.

  • The pair (1,4) does not belong to the relation since the adjacency matrix has 0 on the intersection of the 1-row and the 4-column.

  • The pair (4,9) belongs to the relation. Indeed, the adjacency matrix has 1 on the intersection of the 4-row and the 9-column.

  • The pair (9,4) belongs to the relation. Indeed, the adjacency matrix has 1 on the intersection of the 9-row and the 4-column.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
