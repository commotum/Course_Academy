The coefficients of the line that best fits the data can be found as the least-squares solution β^ to the system

[1−21−11011]⏞X[β0β1]⏞β=[15810]⏞y,

where X is the design matrix, β is the parameter vector, and y is the observation vector.

Pre-multiplying this equation by XT, we obtain the corresponding normal equation

XTXβ^=XTy.

Since the columns of X are linearly independent, the matrix XTX is invertible and

β^=(XTX)−1XTy.

β^=(XTX)−1XTy=120[6224][243]=[7.53].
