EXPLANATION

The line integral of unity with respect to arc length over C is equal to the arc length L of C, i.e., ∫Cds=L.

In polar coordinates, the arc length element ds is given by

ds=r2+(drdθ)2dθ.

So, for a polar curve defined as r=r(θ) for θ1≤θ≤θ2, we have

L=∫Cds=∫θ1θ2r2+(drdθ)2dθ.

Since r=1−sin⁡2θ we have
drdθ=−cos⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θ.

Therefore, we evaluate our line integral as follows:

∫Cds=∫θ1θ2r2+(drdθ)2dθ=∫π/2π(1−sin⁡2θ)2+(−cos⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θ)2dθ=∫π/2π(1−sin⁡2θ)+cos2⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θdθ=∫π/2π(1−sin⁡2θ)2+cos2⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θdθ=∫π/2π1−2sin⁡2θ+sin2⁡2θ+cos2⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θdθ=∫π/2π2−2sin⁡2θ1−sin⁡2θdθ=2θ|π/2π=2 (π−π2)=π2
