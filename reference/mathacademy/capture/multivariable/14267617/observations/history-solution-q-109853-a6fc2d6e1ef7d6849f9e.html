Recall that Null(BT) is the left null space of B. This consists of vectors that, when pre-multiplied by BT, result in the zero-vector 0.

When pre-multiplying a vector by BT, we actually multiply each row of BT, that is, each column of B, by that vector. So, Null(BT) must be orthogonal to the column space of B, which is Col(B).

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
