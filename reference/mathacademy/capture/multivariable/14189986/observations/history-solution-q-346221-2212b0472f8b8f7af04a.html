The definite integral can be defined in terms of the limit of a right Riemann sum as

∫abf(x)dx=limn→∞∑i=1nf(xi)⋅Δx,

where xi=a+iΔx and Δx=b−an.

In the definite integral

∫0π/6cos⁡(3x)dx,

the function is f(x)=cos⁡(3x), the lower bound is a=0 and the upper bound is b=π/6. So, we have

Δx=π/6−0n=π6n.

Therefore, ∫0π/6cos⁡(3x)dx=limn→∞∑i=1nf(xi)⋅Δx=limn→∞∑i=1nf(0+i⋅π6n)⋅π6n=limn→∞∑i=1nf(πi6n)⋅π6n=limn→∞∑i=1ncos⁡(3⋅πi6n)⋅π6n=limn→∞∑i=1ncos⁡(πi2n)⋅π6n.
