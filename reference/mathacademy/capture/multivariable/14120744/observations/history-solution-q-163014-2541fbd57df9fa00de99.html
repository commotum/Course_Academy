Given a thin rod oriented along the x-axis over the interval a≤x≤b, if λ(x) denotes the mass density function of the rod, then the total mass of the rod is given by

m=∫abλ(x)dx.

In our case, we have

λ(x)=4x3−3,a=1,b=2.

We calculate the mass of the rod as follows:

m=∫124x3−3dx=[x4−3x]12=(16−6)−(1−3)=12

Therefore, the mass of the rod is 12kg.
