Step 1: Calculate the derivative dydx:

In this case, we're given that

y′(θ)=e−θ(−sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ),x′(θ)=−e−θ(cos⁡θ+sin⁡θ).

Therefore, we can find dydx as follows:

dydx=y′(θ)x′(θ)=−e−θ(−sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ)e−θ(cos⁡θ+sin⁡θ)=−e−θ(−sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ)e−θ(cos⁡θ+sin⁡θ)=sin⁡θ−cos⁡θcos⁡θ+sin⁡θ
