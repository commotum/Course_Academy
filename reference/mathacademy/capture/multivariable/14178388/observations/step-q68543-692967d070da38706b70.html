Thomas filled a 20-quart container with gasoline and paid $16.80 for it. What's the unit price per gallon?

Assume that there are 4 quarts in one gallon.

a
$3.50 per gallon
b
$4.02 per gallon
c
$3.36 per gallon
d
$4.10 per gallon
e
$3.25 per gallon
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we convert from quarts to gallons. There are 4 quarts in each gallon, so we divide the number of quarts by 4:

20÷4=5

So, 20 quarts is the same as 5 gallons. Now, to find the unit price per gallon, we divide the total price by 5:

16.80÷5=3.36

Therefore, the gasoline costs $3.36 per gallon.
