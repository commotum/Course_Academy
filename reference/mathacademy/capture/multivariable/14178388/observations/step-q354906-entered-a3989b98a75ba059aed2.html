Question 6
?

At a gourmet shop, a 4-cup container of artisanal soup costs $11.20. What's the unit price per fluid ounce?

Assume that there are 8 fluid ounces in one cup.

a
$0.35 per fluid ounce
b
$0.40 per fluid ounce
c
$0.25 per fluid ounce
d
$0.30 per fluid ounce
e
$0.45 per fluid ounce
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we convert from cups to fluid ounces. There are 8 fluid ounces in each cup, so we multiply the number of cups by 8:

4×8=32

So, 4 cups is the same as 32 fluid ounces. Now, to find the unit price per fluid ounce, we divide the total price by 32:

11.20÷32=0.35

Therefore, the artisanal soup costs $0.35 per fluid ounce.
