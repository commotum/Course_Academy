David bought 1 foot of decorative ribbon and paid $1.20 for it. What's the unit price per yard?

Assume that there are 3 feet in one yard.

a
$3.60 per yard
b
$4.80 per yard
c
$2.40 per yard
d
$3.00 per yard
e
$4.20 per yard
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that there are 3 feet in each yard.

Since the price is $1.20 per foot, to find the price per yard, we multiply by 3:

1.20×3=3.60

Therefore, the decorative ribbon costs $3.60 per yard.
