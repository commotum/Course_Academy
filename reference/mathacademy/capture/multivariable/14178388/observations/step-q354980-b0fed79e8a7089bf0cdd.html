A container holding 1.5 liters of organic apple juice costs $6.75. What is the unit price per liter?

a
$4.20 per liter
b
$4.40 per liter
c
$4.60 per liter
d
$4.50 per liter
e
$4.80 per liter
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the unit price, we divide the total cost by the number of liters:
6.75÷1.5=4.5

Therefore, the unit price is $4.50 per liter.
