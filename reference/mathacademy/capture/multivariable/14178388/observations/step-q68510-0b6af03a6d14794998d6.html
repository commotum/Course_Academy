At a supermarket, a box containing 80 tea bags costs $2.40. What is the unit price per bag?

a
 $0.10 per bag
b
 $0.025 per bag
c
 $0.30 per bag
d
 $0.05 per bag
e
 $0.03 per bag
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the unit price, we divide the total cost by the number of bags:

2.40÷80=0.03

Therefore, the unit price is $0.03 per bag.
