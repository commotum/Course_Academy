First, we recall the following:

  • If h′(x)=0 or h′(x) is undefined, then we have a critical point.

  • If h′(x)>0, then h(x) is increasing.

  • If h′(x)<0, then h(x) is decreasing.

The function h(x) has a relative maximum at x=a, if the following two conditions are satisfied:

  • h′(x)=0 or it's undefined, and

  • h′(x) changes its sign from positive to negative (going from left to right) at that point.
