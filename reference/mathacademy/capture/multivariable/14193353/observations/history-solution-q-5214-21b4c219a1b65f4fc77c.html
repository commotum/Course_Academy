First, we recall the following:

  • If f′(x)=0 or f′(x) is undefined, then we have a critical point.

  • If f′(x)>0, then f(x) is increasing.

  • If f′(x)<0, then f(x) is decreasing.

The function f(x) has a relative maximum at x=a, if the following two conditions are satisfied:

  • f′(x)=0 or it's undefined, and

  • f′(x) changes its sign from positive to negative (going from left to right) at that point.
