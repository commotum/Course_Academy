  • If f′(t)=0 or f′(t) is undefined, then we have a critical point.

  • If f′(t)>0, then f(t) is increasing.

  • If f′(t)<0, then f(t) is decreasing.

The function f(t) has a relative minimum at t=a, if the following two conditions are satisfied:

  • f′(a)=0 or it's undefined, and

  • f′(t) changes its sign from negative to positive (going from left to right) at that point.
