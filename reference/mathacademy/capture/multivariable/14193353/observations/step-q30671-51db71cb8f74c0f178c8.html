First, we recall the following:

  • If f′(x)=0 for all x∈(a,b), then the slope of f(x) is equal to zero on (a,b).

  • If f′(x)>0 for all x∈(a,b), then the slope of f(x) is positive on (a,b).

  • If f′(x)<0 for all x∈(a,b), then the slope of f(x) is negative on (a,b).
