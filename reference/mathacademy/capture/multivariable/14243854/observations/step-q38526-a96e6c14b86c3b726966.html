Which of the following are rotation-scaling matrices?

  1. A=[1001]
  2. B=[−433−4]
  3. C=[−6−556]
a
I and III only
b
None
c
I, II, and III
d
I only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A rotation-scaling matrix has the form [a−bba], where a and b are real numbers that are not equal to zero simultaneously. Notice that the entries on the main diagonal are the same, while the antidiagonal entries have opposite signs.

With that in mind let's check each of the given matrices:

  • A=[1001] is a rotation-scaling matrix.

  • B=[−433−4] is not a rotation-scaling matrix since the entries on the antidiagonal do not have opposite signs.

  • C=[−6−556] is not a rotation-scaling matrix since the entries on the main diagonal are not the same.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "I only."
