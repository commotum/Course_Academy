Which of the following are rotation-scaling matrices?

  1. A=[59−95]
  2. B=[−1−33−1]
  3. C=[9779]
a
I and II only
b
II only
c
I only
d
II and III only
e
I and III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A rotation-scaling matrix has the form [a−bba], where a and b are real numbers that are not equal to zero simultaneously. Notice that the entries on the main diagonal are the same, while the antidiagonal entries have opposite signs.

With that in mind let's check each of the given matrices:

  • A=[59−95] is a rotation-scaling matrix.

  • B=[−1−33−1] is a rotation-scaling matrix.

  • C=[9779] is not a rotation-scaling matrix since the entries on the antidiagonal do not have opposite signs.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "I and II only."
