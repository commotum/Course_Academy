The tangent line approximation of f(x) at the point x=a is

L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a).

In our case, we have a=81, which gives L(x)=f(81)+f′(81)(x−81).

Let's compute f(81) and f′(81) in the expression above. Evaluating the function, we get f(81)=681=54, and computing the derivative, we get f′(x)=ddx(6x)=3x, which in turn gives f′(81)=381=39=13.

So, the tangent line approximation is L(x)=54+13(x−81).

We can approximate 687.2 by evaluating the tangent line approximation at x=87.2. We get 687.2≈L(87.2)=54+13(87.2−81)=54+13(6.2)=54+13(315)=54+3115=84115.
