At each point P on a particular curve, the derivative y′(x) is proportional to the distance from P to the origin. What differential equation describes this curve?

a
y′(x)=kx2
b
y′(x)=kx2y2
c
y′(x)=k(x2+y2)
d
y′(x)=kx2+y2
e
y′(x)=kxy
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

At each point P on a particular curve, the derivative y′(x) is proportional to the distance from P to the origin.

We note the following:

  • The distance from P(x,y) to the origin is given by x2+y2.

  • Since the derivative is proportional to the distance from P to the origin, we have y′=k⋅x2+y2, where k is a constant.

So, our differential equation is y′=kx2+y2.
