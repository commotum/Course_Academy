EXPLANATION

Let us consider the squared distance S between (3,−172) and a general point P(x,y):

S=(x−3)2+(y+172)2=x2−6x+9+y2+17y+2894=x2−6x+y2+17y+3254

We require that the point P lies on the curve y=x2−6x. Substituting this into the above gives

S(y)=y+y2+17y+3254=y2+18y+3254.

To find the minimum distance, we take the first derivative of S(y) and equate it to zero. This gives the following:

S′(y)=2y+18=02y=−18y=−9

We can see that S″(y)=2>0. Therefore, y=−9 is a minimum of S.

So, the squared distance (and therefore the actual distance) is minimized when y=−9.
