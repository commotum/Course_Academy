Identify the sentence that is equivalent to the following: "The cup is red and it is new."

a
The cup is red but not new
b
The cup is new and red
c
The cup is new or red
d
The cup is red
e
The cup is new but not red
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let's write the sentence more concisely:

"The cup is red and new."

Using the commutative law, we can swap the order of the words "red" and "new." The sentence now reads

"The cup is new and red."
