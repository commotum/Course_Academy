Between which two consecutive whole numbers does 55 lie?

a
8 and 9
b
27 and 28
c
26 and 28
d
6 and 7
e
7 and 8
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find which two consecutive whole numbers 55 lies between, we first identify the perfect squares that are just below and just above 55.

The perfect squares closest to 55 are 49 and 64. We have 49<55<64.

Taking the square root of each number, we get 49<55<647<55<8.

Therefore, 55 lies between 7 and 8.
