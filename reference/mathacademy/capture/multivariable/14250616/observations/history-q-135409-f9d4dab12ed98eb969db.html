5.Finding the Steady-State Vector of a Stochastic 2x2 Matrix?

A factory produces two types of sugar: brown and extra-refined. Every year, the factory's sales executives study how customers' preferences change between the two types of sugar. The table below shows the results of this study.

From:
Brown Extra-Refined
To: Brown 0.8 1
Extra-Refined 0.2 0

It's found that 20% of customers who currently buy brown sugar will switch to extra-refined sugar next year, while 100% of customers who currently purchase extra-refined sugar will switch to brown sugar next year.

Assuming that there are 450000 customers in total and that the given probabilities remain constant over many years, how many customers will eventually consume extra-refined sugar?
