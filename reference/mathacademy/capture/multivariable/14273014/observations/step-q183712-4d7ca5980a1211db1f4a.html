There exists an integer number such that any natural number is greater than it.

Express the statement above in formal language.

∃n∈N,∀k∈Z,k<n

EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statement:

  • The words "there exists" indicate that we use an existential quantifier, ∃.

  • The word "any" indicates that we use a universal quantifier, ∀.

  • The sentence refers to integers and natural numbers. So, by denoting our numbers as k and n, respectively, we have ∃k∈Z,∀n∈N.

  • Finally, the condition in the statement can be written as follows: k<n

Therefore, we write the statement as follows:

∃k∈Z,∀n∈N,k<n
