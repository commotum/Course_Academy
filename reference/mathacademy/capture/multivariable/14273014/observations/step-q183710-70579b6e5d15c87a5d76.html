EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statement:

  • The word "every" indicates that we use a universal quantifier, ∀.

  • The words "there exists" indicate that we use an existential quantifier, ∃.

  • The sentence refers to rational numbers. So, by denoting our numbers as a and b, we have ∀a∈Q,∃b∈Q.

  • Finally, the condition in the statement can be written as follows: a+b=0

Therefore, we write the statement as follows:

∀a∈Q,∃b∈Q,a+b=0
