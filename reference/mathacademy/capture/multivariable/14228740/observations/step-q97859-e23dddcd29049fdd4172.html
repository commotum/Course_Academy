If B is a 3×3 non-invertible matrix, then which of the following could be the characteristic equation of B?

  1. λ3−4λ=0
  2. λ3−3λ2−λ+4=0
  3. λ(λ+4)(λ−1)=0
a
III only
b
I and III only
c
I only
d
I and II only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since B is non-invertible, according to the invertible matrix theorem, 0 must be an eigenvalue of B. As a result, λ=0 should be a solution to the corresponding characteristic equation.

With that in mind, let's examine the given equations in turn.

  • Equation I could be the characteristic equation of B. Indeed, λ=0 is a root of the equation.

  • Equation II cannot be the characteristic equation of B since λ=0 is not a root of the equation.

  • Equation III could be the characteristic equation of B. Indeed, λ=0 is a root of the equation.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
