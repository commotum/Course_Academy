A matrix B has the characteristic equation λ3+2λ2−λ=0. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. λ=2 is a root of the characteristic equation
  2. λ=0 is an eigenvalue of B
  3. B is non-invertible

EXPLANATION

The invertible matrix theorem in terms of eigenvectors states the following:

The square matrix A is invertible if and only if 0 is not an eigenvalue of A.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is false. Indeed, substituting λ=2 into the equation, we obtain λ3+2λ2−λ=0(2)3+2(2)2−2=08+8−2=014≠0×

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, λ=0 is a root of the characteristic equation. As a result, λ=0 is an eigenvalue of B.

  • Statement III is true. Since λ=0 is an eigenvalue of B, according to the invertible matrix theorem, B is non-invertible.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
