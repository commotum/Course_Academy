If T maps R2 onto the line y=−3x then, which of the following statements are true?

  1. ⟨1,3⟩ belongs to the range of T
  2. Ker(T)≠{0}
  3. Im(T)={0}
a
None of the listed
b
II and III only
c
I only
d
All of the listed
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that when the image of a linear transformation T in R2 with the standard matrix T is a one-dimensional line, then

rank(T)=rank(T)=1

and, according to the rank-nullity theorem,

nullity(T)=nullity(T)=2−rank(T)=2−1=1.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. Notice that the point (1,3) does not satisfy the equation of the line y=−3x since 3≠−3⋅1. Therefore, the vector ⟨1,3⟩∉Im(T).

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, since T maps R2 onto a line passing through the origin, we have that the standard matrix T is singular. Hence, Ker(T)≠{0}.

  • Statement III is false. For example, the point (1,−3) satisfies the equation y=−3x since −3=−3⋅1. Therefore, the vector ⟨1,−3⟩∈Im(T). Thus, Im(T)≠{0}.
