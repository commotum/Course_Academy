For a plane lamina D with mass density function λ(x,y), we have the following definitions:

  • The moment of inertia about the x-axis is given by Ix=∬Dy2λ(x,y)dA.

  • The moment of inertia about the y-axis is given by Iy=∬Dx2λ(x,y)dA.

  • The moment of inertia about the origin is given by I0=∬D(x2+y2)λ(x,y)dA=Ix+Iy.
