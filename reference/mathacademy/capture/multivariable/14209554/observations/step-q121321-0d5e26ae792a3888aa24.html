A man on a boat is sailing towards a 247ft high lighthouse. If the height of the man above the sea is 10ft, how fast is the man approaching the lighthouse if his shadow is shrinking at a rate of 0.25mi/h? Round your answer to one decimal place.

EXPLANATION

Let's draw a figure according to the given data.

In the figure, x is the distance from the man to the lighthouse, while y is the length of his shadow. Both x and y change with time. In particular, we know that the man's shadow's velocity is dydt=−0.25mi/h.

Notice that the man's shadow's velocity is negative because he is walking toward the lighthouse, and therefore the distance between him and the lighthouse decreases with time.

Since we want to calculate dxdt, we need to find a relation between x and y. Since the two triangles are similar, the ratios between the corresponding sides are equal, and we have x+y247=y1010x+10y=247y10x=237yx=23.7y.

We differentiate both sides of the above equation with respect to t using the chain rule, and we get ddt(x)=ddt(23.7y)dxdt=23.7⋅dydt.

Finally, substituting dydt=−0.25mi/h into the above gives dxdt=23.7(−0.25)≈−5.9mi/h to one decimal place.

Therefore, the man's speed is approximately 5.9mi/h.
