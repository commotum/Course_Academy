A bus is traveling at night at 50km/h. After running past a 6.25m tall lamppost, the bus driver notices that the shadow of the bus is growing. If the height of the bus is 3.75m, how fast is the length of the shadow growing while it travels away from the lamp?

a
65km/h
b
25km/h
c
75km/h
d
33km/h
e
72km/h
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's draw a figure according to the given data.

In the figure, x is the bus' distance from the lamp, while y is the length of the shadow. Both x and y change with time. In particular, we know that the runner's velocity is

dxdt=50km/h.

Notice that the bus's velocity is positive because it is moving away from the lamppost, and therefore, the distance between the bus and the lamp increases with time.

Since we want to calculate dydt, we need to find a relation between x and y. Since the two triangles are similar, the ratios between the corresponding sides are equal, and we have

x+y6.25=y3.753.75x+3.75y=6.25y3.75x=2.5yy=1.5x.

We differentiate both sides of the above equation with respect to t using the chain rule, and we get

ddt(y)=1.5dxdt.

Finally, substituting dxdt=50km/h into the above gives

ddt(y)=75km/h.

Therefore, the length of the bus' shadow increases at 75km/h.
