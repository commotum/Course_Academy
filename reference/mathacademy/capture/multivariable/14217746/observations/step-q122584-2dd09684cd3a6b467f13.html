A curve is defined by the parametric equations x=t+1,y=t3+4,−1<t<∞.

Calculate the equation of the tangent to the curve at the point where t=1.

a
y−5=62(x−2)
b
y+2=324(x+5)
c
y+5=23(x+2)
d
y−5=2(x−2)
e
y−5=6(x−2)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we find the coordinates of the point by substituting t=1 into the parametric equations: x=1+1=2,y=(1)3+4=5.

So we're looking to find the equation of the tangent at the point (2,5).

To find the equation of the tangent line, we need to find dydx. To compute it, we first need to find the derivatives of x and y, as follows:

x′(t)=12t+1,y′(t)=3t2.

So, we have

dydx=y′(t)x′(t)=3t2(12t+1)=6t2(t+1).

At the point where t=1, the slope of the tangent line is given by m=dydx|t=1=6⋅121+1=62.

Finally, to calculate the equation of the tangent line, we use point-slope form with the point (x1,y1)=(2,5) and the slope m=62. We get y−5=62(x−2).
