Let X represent the volume of soda in a randomly selected can. Then we have X∼N(12,σ2), and we want to find P(X<12.2). But first, we need to find the standard deviation σ.

We know that P(X<11.7)=0.03. First, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring, we get

P(X<11.7)=0.03P(Z≤11.7−12σ)=0.03Φ(−0.3σ)=0.03−0.3σ=Φ−1(0.03)

From the table,

Φ−1(0.03)≈−1.8808.

Therefore, we have

−0.3σ=−1.88081σ=6.26933σ=16.26933≈0.1595.

Finally, we can solve for P(X<12.2) as follows:

P(X<12.2)=P(X≤12.2)=P(Z≤12.2−120.1595)≈P(Z≤1.25)=Φ(1.25)≈89.4%

Therefore, approximately 89.4% of soda cans contain less than 12.2 ounces of soda.
