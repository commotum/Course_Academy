Given that A is a 4×4 real symmetric matrix, which of the following statements are true?

  1. A is not diagonalizable
  2. A must have 4 integer eigenvalues
  3. If α is an eigenvalue of A with the multiplicity 2, then the dimension of the eigenspace for the eigenvalue α is 2.

II only

I only

EXPLANATION

Given that A is an n×n symmetric matrix over R, the spectral theorem states the following:

  • The eigenvalues of A are all real.

  • Eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues are orthogonal.

  • The dimension of the eigenspace for each eigenvalue equals the multiplicity of that eigenvalue as a root of the characteristic polynomial.

  • A is orthogonally diagonalizable.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. According to the spectral theorem, any symmetric matrix with real entries is orthogonally diagonalizable, so, A is diagonalizable.

  • Statement II is false. According to the spectral theorem, the eigenvalues are real but not necessarily integers.

  • Statement III is true. According to the theorem, the dimension of the eigenspace for each eigenvalue equals the multiplicity of that eigenvalue as a root of the characteristic polynomial.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
