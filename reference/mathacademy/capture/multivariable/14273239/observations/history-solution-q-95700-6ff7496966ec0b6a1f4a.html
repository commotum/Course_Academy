Given that A is an n×n symmetric matrix over R, the spectral theorem states the following:

  • The eigenvalues of A are all real.

  • Eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues are orthogonal.

  • The dimension of the eigenspace for each eigenvalue equals the multiplicity of that eigenvalue as a root of the characteristic polynomial.

  • A is orthogonally diagonalizable.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, any symmetric matrix with real entries is orthogonally diagonalizable.

  • Statement II is false. According to the spectral theorem, the eigenvalues are real but there are not 36 of them. An n×n matrix has at most n distinct eigenvalues.

  • Statement III is false. According to the theorem, eigenvectors corresponding to different eigenvalues must be orthogonal. So if the two eigenvalues correspond to different eigenvalues, they cannot be parallel.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
