The electrical energy level of a battery powering a house was recorded over time for 10 hours, starting from time t=0 hours. The following table shows the energy level E(t), in kilowatt-hours, recorded at time t.

t 0 2 4 6 8 10
E(t) 40 34 29 24 18 12

Approximate the rate of change of energy level at time t=2 using a forward difference approximation. Do not round the answer.

Hint: Assume that the rate of change is negative if the electrical energy level decreases.

At t=2, the estimated rate of change is
−2.5
kilowatt-hours per hour.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the forward difference approximation E′(t)≈E(t+h)−E(t)h at the point t=2. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible, in this case h=2. This gives E′(2)≈E(2+2)−E(2)2=E(4)−E(2)2=29−342=−52=−2.5. So, at t=2 the rate of change was approximately −2.5 kilowatt-hours per hour.
