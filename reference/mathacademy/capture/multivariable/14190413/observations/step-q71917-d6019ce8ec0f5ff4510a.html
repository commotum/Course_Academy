A function F(x) has values according to the table below:

x 0 2 4 6 8 10
F(x) 3.5 8.7 11.2 12.9 6.7 1.2

Approximate F′(x) at the point x=2 using a forward difference approximation.

a
0.85
b
3.75
c
1.25
d
1.6
e
2.5
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We use the forward difference approximation formula F′(x)≈F(x+h)−F(x)h at the point x=2. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible, in this case h=2. This gives

F′(2)≈F(2+2)−F(2)2=F(4)−F(2)2=11.2−8.72=2.52=1.25.
