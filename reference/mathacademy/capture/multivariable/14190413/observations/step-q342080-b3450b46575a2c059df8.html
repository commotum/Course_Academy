A slice of jackfruit is placed in an air dryer. The graph of the moisture content M(t), in percent, after t hours is shown above. Approximate the rate of change of the moisture content at t=3 using a forward difference with step size h=2. Do not round the answer.

Hint: Assume that the rate of change is negative if the content decreases.

After 3 hour, the rate of change of moisture content is
−10
percent per hour.

Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the forward difference approximation M′(t)≈M(t+h)−M(t)h, at the time t=3 with step size h=2. This gives M′(3)≈M(3+2)−M(3)2=M(5)−M(3)2=20−402=−10. So, after 3 hour, the rate of change of moisture content is −10 percent per hour.
