The population of a city is given by the function n(t), where t is the time, in years, from when records first began. The values of n(t), in thousands, for different values of t are given in the table below.

t 0 1 2 3 4
n(t) 851 856 861 865 870

Approximate the rate of growth in population in the second year (t=2) using a forward difference approximation.

a
5000 people per year
b
4000 people per year
c
2000 people per year
d
6000 people per year
e
3000 people per year
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the forward difference approximation n′(t)≈n(t+h)−n(t)h at the point t=2. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible, in this case h=1. This gives

n′(2)≈n(2+1)−n(2)1=n(3)−n(2)1=865−8611=4.

So at t=2 the rate of growth is approximately 4000 people per year.
