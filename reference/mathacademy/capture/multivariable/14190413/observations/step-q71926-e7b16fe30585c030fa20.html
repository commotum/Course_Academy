A shepherd keeps track of the rate of growth of his herd. He takes note of the total number of sheep every 6 months. The results are shown in the table below, where S(m) is the number of sheep after m months.

m 6 12 18 24 30
S(m) 90 98 116 130 139

Approximate the rate of growth of the herd at the point where m=12 using a forward difference approximation.

a
2sheep/month
b
4sheep/month
c
1sheep/month
d
5sheep/month
e
3sheep/month
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We use the forward difference approximation S′(m)≈S(m+h)−S(m)h, at the point m=6. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible, in this case h=6. This gives

S′(12)≈S(12+6)−S(12)6=S(18)−S(12)6=116−986=186=3.

So, at m=12 the rate of growth is approximately 3sheep/month.
