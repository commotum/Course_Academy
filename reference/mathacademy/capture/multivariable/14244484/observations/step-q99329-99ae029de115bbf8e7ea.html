EXPLANATION

Recall that for any 2×2 real matrix A with complex eigenvalues, we have A=PCP−1, where

P=[||Re(v)Im(v)||],C=[Re(λ)Im(λ)−Im(λ)Re(λ)],

and v is an eigenvector of A corresponding to the complex eigenvalue λ.

  • From the matrix C=[0−2−(−2)0], we deduce that Re(λ)=0 and Im(λ)=−2. So, our eigenvalue is λ=0+(−2)i=−2i.

  • From the matrix P=[41−3−1], we deduce that our corresponding eigenvector is v=[4+1i−3+(−1)i]=[4+i−3−i].

Finally, the second eigenvalue of A must be the complex conjugate of λ, and the second eigenvector must be the complex conjugate of v.

Therefore, our eigenvalues and eigenvectors are

λ=∓2i,v=[4±i−3∓i].
