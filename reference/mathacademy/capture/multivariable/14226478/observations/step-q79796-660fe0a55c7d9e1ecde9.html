a
14∫π2π(1+sin⁡(θ)6)2dθ
b
12∫π/2π(sin⁡(θ)3)2dθ

12∫π/2π(1+sin⁡(θ)3)2dθ
d
12∫π/2π(1+sin2⁡(θ)9)dθ
e
14∫π/2π(1+sin⁡(θ)9)2dθ
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The area bounded by a polar curve is given by A=12∫θ1θ2r2(θ)dθ.

When r(θ)=1+sin⁡(θ)3, θ1=π2 and θ2=π, this gives

A=12∫π/2π(1+sin⁡(θ)3)2dθ.
