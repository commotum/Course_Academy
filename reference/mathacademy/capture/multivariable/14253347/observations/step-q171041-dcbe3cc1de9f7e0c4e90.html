a
x is odd.
b
 x is odd and prime.
c
 x is odd or prime but not both.

 x is odd or prime.

EXPLANATION

First, we simplify the phrase "x is odd and it is an odd number" to just "x is odd." The sentence now reads

"x is odd, or x is odd or it is a prime number."

Then, using the associative law, we can move the comma in the sentence:

"x is odd or x is odd, or it is a prime number."

Now, we simplify the phrase "x is odd or x is odd" to just "x is odd." The sentence now reads

"x is odd, or it is a prime number."

Finally, we simplify the sentence to

"x is odd or prime."
