Identify the sentence that is equivalent to the following: "x is prime and it is less than 15."

a
x is prime but less than or equal to 15
b
x is less than 15
c
x is less than 15 and prime
d
x is less than 15 or prime
e
x is less than 15 but not prime
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's write the sentence more concisely:

"x is prime and less than 15."

Using the commutative law, we can swap the order of the words "prime" and "less than 15." The sentence now reads

"x is less than 15 and prime."
