EXPLANATION

The word "and" is represented by the symbol "∧," while the word "or" is represented by the symbol "∨."

Note that this sentence consists of two parts:

  • Part 1: x is negative and irrational. This corresponds to xis negative⏟Npand⏟∧xis irrational⏟W, that is, N∧W.

  • Part 2: x is positive and less than 5. This corresponds to xp is positive ⏟Ppand⏟∧xp is less than 5⏟U, that is, P∧U.

So, we can express the sentence as xis negative and irrational⏟N∧Wpor⏟∨xp is positive and less than 5⏟P∧U,
