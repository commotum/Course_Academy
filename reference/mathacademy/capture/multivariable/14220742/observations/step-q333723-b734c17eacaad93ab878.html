EXPLANATION

Let's justify each of the selected steps.

  • We first consider lines L1 and L2. Since Φ(t) is a fundamental matrix, its columns x1(t) and x2(t) form a basis of the solution space for our system. Thus, L2 follows from L1 by the definition of a fundamental matrix.

  • Next, we consider lines L2 and L3. Once x1(t) and x2(t) form a basis, every solution x(t) of the system can be expressed uniquely as a linear combination

  • Finally, we consider lines L1 and L4. Since x1(t) and x2(t) are the columns of Φ(t), we can write Φ(t)=[||x1(t)x2(t)||]. Then by the definition of matrix multiplication, Φ(t)c=[||x1(t)x2(t)||][c1c2]=c1x1(t)+c2x2(t). Thus, L4 follows from L1 by the definition of matrix multiplication.
