A rectangular plate of mass 12kg and uniform mass density occupies the region D={(x,y):0≤x≤2,0≤y≤2}, where x and y are measured in meters. Find the moment of inertia of the plate about a line perpendicular to D at a distance of 2m from a parallel line passing through its center of mass.

a
64kg⋅m2
b
48kg⋅m2
c
52kg⋅m2
d
58kg⋅m2
e
56kg⋅m2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

For a plane lamina of mass M and a line lM perpendicular to the lamina passing through its center of mass, the parallel axis theorem states that the moment of inertia of the lamina about a line l parallel to lM is given by Il=IM+d2M, where d is the distance between the lines, and IM and Il are the moments of inertia about the lines lM and l, respectively.

The area of the rectangular plate D is A=2⋅2=4m2. Since the plate has a uniform density, the mass density function is given by

λ(x,y)=MA=124=3kg/m2,

and its center of mass is its centroid:

(x¯,y¯)=(0+22,0+22)=(1,1)

Then, the moment of inertia about the perpendicular line through the center of mass is

IM=∬D((x−x¯)2+(y−y¯)2)λ(x,y)dA=∫02∫023(x−1)2+3(y−1)2dydx=∫02[3(x−1)2y+(y−1)3]y=0y=2dx=∫026(x−1)2+2dx=[2(x−1)3+2x]02=2+4−(−2)=8kg⋅m2.

Finally, applying the parallel axis theorem, the moment of inertia about a line 2m from the center of mass is

I=IM+d2M=8+22⋅12=8+48=56kg⋅m2.
