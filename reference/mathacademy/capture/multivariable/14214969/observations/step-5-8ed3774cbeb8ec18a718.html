Explanation

Notice that g(x) is continuous on the interval [−4,−1] and differentiable on (−4,−1).

Given that g(x) is continuous on [a,b] and differentiable on (a,b), the mean value theorem states the following:

There exists a point c∈(a,b) such that g′(c)=g(b)−g(a)b−a.

In our case, we have a=−4 and b=−1. Thus,

g′(c)=g(−1)−g(−4)−1−(−4)=1−(−1)−1+4=1+13=23.

Therefore, the complete sentence is as follows:

The mean value theorem states that there exists a point c∈(−4,−1) such that g′(c)=23.
