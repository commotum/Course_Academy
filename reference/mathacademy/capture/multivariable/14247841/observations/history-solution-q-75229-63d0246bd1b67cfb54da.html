First, let's draw the region R and the partition P.

The upper sum U(f,P) is given by

U(f,P)=M11⋅Area(R11)+M21⋅Area(R21)+M12⋅Area(R12)+M22⋅Area(R22)

where Mij is the maximum value of f in each subregion Rij. We pick the maximum value of f because we're finding the upper sum.

Taking into account the fact that Area(Rij)=4 for all 4 subregions, the formula for U(f,P) reduces to U(f,P)=4⋅M11+4⋅M21+4⋅M12+4⋅M22=4(M11+M21+M12+M22).

Now, notice that

  • f(x,y)=6−2x−y decreases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=6−2x−y decreases as y increases.

As a result, the maximum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the smallest possible x and the smallest possible y. This is the value at the bottom left corner of each subregion.

Hence:

M11=f(−2,−2)=12M21=f(0,−2)=8M12=f(−2,0)=10M22=f(0,0)=6

Finally, U(f,P)=4(12+8+10+6)=144.
