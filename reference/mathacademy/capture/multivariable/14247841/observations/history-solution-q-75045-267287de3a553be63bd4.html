First, let's draw the region R and the partition P.

The upper sum U(f,P) is given by

U(f,P)=M11⋅Area(R11)+M21⋅Area(R21)+M12⋅Area(R12)+M22⋅Area(R22),

where Mij is the maximum value of f in each subregion Rij. We pick the maximum value of f because we're finding the upper sum.

Taking into account the fact that Area(Rij)=1 for all 4 subregions, the formula for U(f,P) reduces to U(f,P)=M11+M21+M12+M22.

Now, notice that

  • f(x,y)=x+y+2 increases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=x+y+2 increases as y increases.

As a result, the maximum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the largest possible x and the largest possible y. This is the value at the top right corner of each subregion.

Hence: M11=f(0,0)=2M21=f(1,0)=3M12=f(0,1)=3M22=f(1,1)=4

Finally, U(f,P)=2+3+3+4=12.
