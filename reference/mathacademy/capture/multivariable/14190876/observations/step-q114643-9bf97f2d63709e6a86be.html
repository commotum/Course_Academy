EXPLANATION

Since the function is piecewise-linear, we get a constant derivative over each linear part. Notice the following:

  • When f(x) is decreasing, we get f′(x)<0.

  • When f(x) is constant, we get f′(x)=0.

We can now summarize the information from the graph in the table below.

x (−∞,3) 3 (3,∞)
f ↗ max const.
f′ + undef. 0

From the graph of y=f(x), we can conclude that there are no inflection points.

Additionally, we have a change of the slope of the tangent line (from positive to 0) when we pass through x=3 from left to right. This means that the derivative is not defined at x=3 and we obtain a jump discontinuity at this point for the graph of y=f′(x).
