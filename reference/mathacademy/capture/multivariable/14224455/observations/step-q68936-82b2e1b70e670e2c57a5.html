Nathan, a freelance journalist, charges $0.04 per word for his financial articles. How many words would it take for him to earn 1 dollar?

a
16 words
b
25 words
c
40 word
d
4 words
e
20 words
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are given the unit rate of $0.04 per word (dollars per word). To find how many words it would take for him to earn 1 dollar we need to find the unit rate in words per dollar. So, we take the reciprocal:

0.04dollars per word→1÷0.04=25words per dollar

So, the unit rate is 25 words per dollar. Therefore, Nathan writes 25 words for 1 dollar.
