Negate the following statement:

At least one existential statement is not true.

a
Every existential statement is not true.
b
None of the listed.
c
At least one existential statement is true.
d
At least one existential statement is not true.
e
Every existential statement is true.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let E be the set of all existential statements. The given statement can be re-phrased as follows:

There exists S∈E such that S is not true.

Introducing the existential quantifier, we get the following:

∃S∈E,S is not true.

Negating this, we obtain ¬(∃S∈E,S is not true)≡∀S∈E,¬(S is not true)≡∀S∈E,S is true

which reads

For all S∈E, S is true.

or simply

Every existential statement is true.
