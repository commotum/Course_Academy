EXPLANATION

First, let's round the length and width of the building model:

  • Length: 84⟶80

  • Width: 41⟶40

Therefore, the ratio of the length to the width (expressed as a fraction) is approximately

8040.

So the numerator represents the length of the building model, and the denominator represents the width.

We can simplify this ratio by dividing both numbers by 10:

80÷1040÷10=84

Then, we divide by 4:

8÷44÷4=21

We can make a denominator of 20 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 20:

2×201×20=4020

Therefore, the length of the building is approximately 40 meters.
