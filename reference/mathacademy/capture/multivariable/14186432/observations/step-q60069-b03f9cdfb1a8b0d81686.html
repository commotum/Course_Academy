EXPLANATION

First, let's round the number of dogs and cats in shelter A:

  • Dogs: 51⟶50

  • Cats: 32⟶30

Therefore, the ratio of the number of dogs to the number of cats (expressed as a fraction) is approximately:

5030

So the numerator represents the number of dogs and the denominator represents the number of cats.

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 10 gives an equivalent fraction:

50÷1030÷10=53

We can make a numerator of 20 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 4:

5×43×4=2012

Therefore, there are approximately 12 cats in shelter B.
