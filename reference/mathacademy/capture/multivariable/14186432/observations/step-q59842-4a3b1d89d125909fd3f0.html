An election took place in a small town between two political parties, The Green Party and The Labour Party. The table below shows the total number of votes that each party received.

The Green Party The Labour Party
1020 3022

By rounding to the nearest hundred, find the approximate ratio of Labour Party votes to total votes.

a
1
b
34
c
23
d
13
e
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's approximate the total number of votes that each party received by rounding to the nearest hundred.

  • The Green Party: 1020⟶1000

  • The Labour Party: 3022⟶3000

So the total number of votes received was approximately 1000+3000=4000.

Therefore, the ratio of Labour Party votes to total votes (expressed as a fraction) is approximately

30004000.

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 1000 gives

3000÷10004000÷1000=34.

Therefore, the ratio of Labour Party votes to total votes is approximately 34.
