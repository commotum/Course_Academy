Boys Girls
102 81

EXPLANATION

First, let's round the number of babies born in the New York Hospital to the nearest 10:

  • Boys: 102⟶100

  • Girls: 81⟶80

Therefore, the ratio of the number of boys to the number of girls (expressed as a fraction) is approximately:

10080

So the numerator represents the number of boys and the denominator represents the number of girls.

We can simplify this ratio by dividing both numbers by 10:

100÷1080÷10=108

Then, we divide by 2:

10÷28÷2=54

We can make a denominator of 120 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 30:

5×304×30=150120

Therefore, approximately 150 boys were born at Boston Hospital in that particular month.
