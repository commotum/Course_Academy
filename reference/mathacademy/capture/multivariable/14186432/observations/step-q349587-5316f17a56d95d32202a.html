The following table shows the width and length, in meters, of a rectangular storage warehouse.

Width Length
843 1617

Rounding to the nearest hundred, determine the approximate ratio of width to length for this warehouse.

a
58
b
34
c
13
d
23
e
12
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's approximate the width and length by rounding to the nearest 100:

  • width: 843⟶800

  • length: 1617⟶1600

Therefore, the ratio of width to length (expressed as a fraction) is approximately

8001600.

We can simplify this ratio by dividing both numbers by 100:

800÷1001600÷100=816

Then, we divide by 8:

8÷816÷8=12

Therefore, the ratio of width to length is approximately 12.
