Notice the following properties of the given slope field:

  • First, the slope lines on the same vertical line are all the same. This means that the slope does not vary with y. In other words, y′ is a function of x only.

    Of the given equations, the only equations that satisfy this condition are the following: y′=1xy′=exy′=x2

  • Second, the slopes become vertical as x→0.

    Of the remaining options, the only one that gives this behavior is y′=1x. Notice that |y′|=|1x|→∞asx→0. Therefore, the slopes of the slope field for the differential equation y′=1x become vertical as x→0. By contrast, y′=ex→1asx→0, and y′=x2→0asx→0.

Therefore, the required equation is y′=1x.
