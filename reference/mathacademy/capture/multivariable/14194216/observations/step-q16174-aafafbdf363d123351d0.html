EXPLANATION

Notice that if we try to substitute x=10 directly into the limit, we reach the indeterminate form 00.

limx→10f(x)+52x+g(x)=f(x)+52x+g(x)|x=10=f(10)+520+g(10)=−5+520+(−20)=00

So, we use L'Hopital's rule: limx→10f(x)+52x+g(x)=limx→10(f(x)+5)′(2x+g(x))′=limx→10f′(x)2+g′(x)

We substitute x=10 again, but once more, we reach the indeterminate form 00.

limx→10f′(x)2+g′(x)=f′(x)2+g′(x)|x=10=f′(10)2+g′(10)=02+(−2)=00

We apply L'Hopital's rule again. We get limx→10f′(x)2+g′(x)=limx→10(f′(x))′(2+g′(x))′=limx→10f″(x)g″(x),

and this time, when we substitute x=10, we are actually able to determine the result: limx→10f″(x)g″(x)=f″(x)g″(x)|x=10=f″(10)g″(10)=3−6=−12

Therefore, limx→10f(x)+52x+g(x)=−12.
