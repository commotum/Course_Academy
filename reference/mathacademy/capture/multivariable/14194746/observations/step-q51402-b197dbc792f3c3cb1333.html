Sam wanted to estimate the rate of weight growth of his new puppy. He took note of its weight every week, for 7 weeks. The results are shown in the table below, where W(t) is the puppy's weight (measured in pounds) after t weeks from birth.

t 8 9 10 11 12 13 14
W(t) 9 10.1 11.3 12.4 13.5 14.6 15.8
Approximate the puppy's rate of weight growth 12 weeks after birth using a central difference approximation with step size h=1.

a
2.1lb/week
b
1.05lb/week
c
1.1lb/week
d
2.2lb/week
e
1lb/week
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the central difference approximation W′(t)≈W(t+h)−W(t−h)2h, at the time t=12 and step size h=1. This gives

W′(0.2)≈W(12+1)−W(12−1)2(1)=W(13)−W(11)2=14.6−12.42=2.22=1.1.

So, the rate of weight growth at the instant of time t=12 is 1.1lb/week.
