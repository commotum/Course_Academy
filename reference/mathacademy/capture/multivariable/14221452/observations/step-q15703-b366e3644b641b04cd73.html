Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The unsigned area bounded by the parametric curve x=x(t),y=y(t),t1≤t≤t2

and the x-axis is given by the absolute value of the integral

∫t1t2ydxdtdt.

First, we compute

dxdt=ddt(−tan⁡t)=−sec2⁡t.

So, we obtain the integral expression ∫t1t2ydxdtdt=∫0π/4ln2⁡(t+1)(−sec2⁡t)dt=−∫0π/4ln2⁡(t+1)sec2⁡tdt.

Notice that this integral will give us a negative number since ydxdt≤0 for all t∈[0,π4].

Therefore, the area is given by A=−(−∫0π/4ln2⁡(t+1)sec2⁡tdt)=∫0π/4ln2⁡(t+1)sec2⁡tdt.
