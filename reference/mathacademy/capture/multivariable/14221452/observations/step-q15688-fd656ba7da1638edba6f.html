Which of the following gives the area bounded between the curve defined parametrically as x=t4−2t2,y=t3−1 and the x-axis for t∈[1,2]?

a
4∫12(t3−1)(t2−t)dt
b
2∫12t(t3−t2)(t2−1)dt
c
4∫12(t3−t)(t2−1)dt
d
4∫12t(t3−1)(t2−1)dt
e
2∫12(t3−t)(t2−t)dt

Correct
EXPLANATION

The unsigned area bounded by the parametric curve x=x(t),y=y(t),t1≤t≤t2

and the x-axis is given by the absolute value of the integral

∫t1t2ydxdtdt.

First, we compute

dxdt=ddt(t4−2t2)=4t3−4t=4t(t2−1).

So, we obtain the integral expression

∫t1t2ydxdtdt=∫12(t3−1)4t(t2−1)dt=4∫12t(t3−1)(t2−1)dt.

Notice that this integral will give us a positive number since ydxdt≥0 for all t∈[1,2].

Therefore, the area is given by

A=4∫12t(t3−1)(t2−1)dt.
