EXPLANATION

The unsigned area bounded by the parametric curve x=x(t),y=y(t),t1≤t≤t2 and the x-axis is given by the absolute value of the integral ∫t1t2ydxdtdt.

First, we compute dxdt=ddt(t)=1.

So, we obtain the integral expression ∫t1t2ydxdtdt=∫π2πtsin⁡t⋅1dt=∫π2πtsin⁡tdt.

Notice that this integral will give us a negative number since sin⁡t≤0 for all t∈[π,2π] and t≥0.

To solve the above integral, we use integration by parts. We set u=t⇒u′=1v′=sin⁡t⇒v=−cos⁡t.

Therefore, we have ∫π2πtsin⁡tdt=−tcos⁡t|π2π+∫π2πcos⁡t⋅1dt=−tcos⁡t|π2π+∫π2πcos⁡tdt.

Finally, the area is A=|∫π2πtsin⁡tdt|=|−tcos⁡t|π2π+∫π2πcos⁡tdt|=|−tcos⁡t|π2π+sin⁡t|π2π|=|−(2πcos⁡2π−πcos⁡π)+(sin⁡2π−sin⁡π)|=|−(2π(1)−π(−1))|=3π.
