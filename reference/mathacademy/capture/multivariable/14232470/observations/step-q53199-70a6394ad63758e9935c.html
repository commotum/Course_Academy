EXPLANATION

In the figure, y is the kite's height, and z is the distance between the boy and the kite. The quantity z=20 meters does not change with time because the string's length doesn't change.

We wish to calculate dθdt. To do this, let's start by setting up a relation between y and z. Relative to the angle θ, the sides y and z are the opposite and hypotenuse, respectively. So, we have

sin⁡θ=yzsin⁡θ=y20y=20sin⁡θ.

Now, we differentiate both sides of the equation with respect to t using implicit differentiation, and we get

ddt(y)=ddt(20sin⁡θ)dydt=(20cos⁡θ)dθdt.

Converting the given information from degrees to radians, we have dydt=1 and θ=45∘⋅π180∘=π4. Substituting this into the above, we reach

1=(20cos⁡(π4))dθdt120=(22)dθdtdθdt=220.

Therefore, the angle of elevation is increasing at a rate of 220 radians per second.
