For a function f(x,y), the double integral of f over a rectangular region R is the unique number I (if it exists) such that

L(f,P)≤I≤U(f,P),

over all partitions P of R, where the lower and upper sums respectively are given by

L(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1nmijΔxiΔyjU(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1nMijΔxiΔyj

and mij and Mij are the minimum and maximum values of f respectively in each subregion Rij.

We need to compute L(f,P).

Notice that for all subregions Rij, we have that

  • f(x,y)=x−y+2 increases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=x−y+2 decreases as y increases.

As a result, the minimum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the smallest possible x and the largest possible y. This is the value at the top left corner of each subregion. Therefore,

mij=f(xi−1,yj)=xi−1−yj+2

and the lower Riemann sum is given by

L(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1n(xi−1−yj+2)ΔxiΔyj.

So, the missing expression is xi−1−yj+2.

Your Answer:
xi−yj−1+2
