Recall that for any integers a, b, and c,

a|c, b|c, and gcd(a,b)=1 ⟹ ab|c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. First, note that gcd(3,8)=1 and 3⋅8=24. Therefore, we have (3|a),(8|a), and gcd(3,8)=1, which implies (24|a).

  • Statement II is true. First, note that gcd(10,21)=1 and 10⋅21=210. Therefore, we have (10|b),(21|b), and gcd(10,21)=1, which implies (210|b).

  • Statement III is true. First, note that gcd(32,42)=1 and 32⋅42=144. Therefore, we have (32|c),(42|c), and gcd(32,42)=1, which implies (144|c).

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
