For any integers a, b, and c, we have transitivity of divisibility:

a∣b and b∣c ⟹ a∣c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. First, note that 7∣14. Therefore, we have 7∣14 and 14∣p, which implies that 7∣p.

  • Statement II is false. As a counterexample, let p=14. Then, 14∣14 but 4∤14.

  • Statement III is true. First, note that 3∣21. Therefore, we have 3∣21 and 21∣p, which implies that 3∣p.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
