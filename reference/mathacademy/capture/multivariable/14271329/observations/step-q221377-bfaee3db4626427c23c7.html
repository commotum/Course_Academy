EXPLANATION

For any integers a, b, and c, we have transitivity of divisibility:

a∣b and b∣c ⟹ a∣c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. First, note that 8∣16. Therefore, we have 8∣16 and 16∣n, which implies that 8∣n.

  • Statement II is false. As a counterexample, let n=16. Then, 16∣16 but 12∤16.

  • Statement III is false. As a counterexample, let n=4. Then, 4∣4 but 16∤4.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
