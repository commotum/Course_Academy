EXPLANATION

For any integers a, b, and c, we have transitivity of divisibility:

a∣b and b∣c ⟹ a∣c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is false. As a counterexample, let c=10. Then, 10∣10 but 4∤10.

  • Statement II is true. First, note that 5∣10. Therefore, we have 5∣10 and 10∣c, which implies that 5∣c.

  • Statement III is false. As a counterexample, let c=5. Then, 5∣5 but 10∤5.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
