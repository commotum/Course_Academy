EXPLANATION

Recall that for any integers a, b, and c,

a|c, b|c, and gcd(a,b)=1 ⟹ ab|c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. First, note that gcd(2,3)=1 and 2⋅3=6. Therefore, we have (2|a),(3|a), and gcd(2,3)=1, which implies (6|a).

  • Statement II is false. As a counterexample, let b=14. Then, (7∣14) and (14∣14), but (98∤14).

  • Statement III is true. First, note that gcd(23,3)=1 and 23⋅3=24. Therefore, we have (23|c),(3|c), and gcd(23,3)=1, which implies (24|c).

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
