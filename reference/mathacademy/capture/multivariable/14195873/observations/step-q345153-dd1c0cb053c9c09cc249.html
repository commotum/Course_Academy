EXPLANATION

First, let's draw a picture of a cylinder. Let r be the radius, and h the height.

We wish to minimize the surface area S=2πrh+πr2.

We know that the volume is fixed at 8πin3, so we also have the constraint condition 8π=πr2h.

Now, by making h the subject of the constraint equation, we have that h=8r2.

Plugging the above into our expression for S, we obtain

S(r)=2πr(8r2)+πr2=16πr+πr2.

We want to minimize S, so we need to differentiate S and set the derivative equal to zero. The derivative is S′(r)=−16πr2+2πr.

Setting the derivative equal to zero and solving for r gives a stationary point, as follows: S′(r)=0−16πr2+2πr=02πr=16πr22πr3=16πr3=8r=83r=2in

Using a calculator, we can verify that if r<2 then S′(r)<0, and if r>2 then S′(r)>0, which means that the stationary point r=2 is indeed a minimum of S(r).

Finally, to find the smallest surface area Smin, we substitute r=2in into the equation for the surface area S:

Smin=S(2)=16π2+π(2)2=8π+4π=12πin2
