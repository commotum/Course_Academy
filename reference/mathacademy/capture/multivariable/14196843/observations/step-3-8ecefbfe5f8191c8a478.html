59

Explanation

Again, we use the fact that June 1st corresponds to t=1.

We use the backward difference approximation formula

q′(t)≈q(t)−q(t−h)h

at the point t=1. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible. In this case, we choose h=1. This gives

q′(1)≈q(1)−q(1−1)1=q(1)−q(0)1=59−01=59.

So, using a backward difference approximation, the rate of change in the number of customers on June 1st is approximately 59 customers per month.
