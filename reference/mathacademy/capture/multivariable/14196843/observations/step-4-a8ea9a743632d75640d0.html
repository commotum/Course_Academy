Explanation

The general formula for the trapezoidal rule states that

∫abq(t)dt≈12[q(a)+2(q(t1)+q(t2)+…+q(tn−1))+q(b)]Δt,

where n is the number of trapezoids and Δt=b−an is the step size.

Here, we have the following values:

  • The number of trapezoids is n=2.

  • The corresponding interval is [0,4], so we have a=0 and b=4.

  • The step size is given by Δt=b−an=4−02=2.

Using the above values, the general formula becomes

∫04q(t)dt≈12[q(0)+2q(2)+q(4)]⋅2.

Substituting the appropriate numbers into the above expression gives

∫04q(t)dt≈12[0+2(120)+108]⋅2=240+108=348.
