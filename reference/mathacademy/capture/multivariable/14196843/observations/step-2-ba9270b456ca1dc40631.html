From the given data, the best forward difference approximation for the rate at which the number of customers was changing on June 1st is
61
customers per month.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

First, we note that June 1st corresponds to t=1.

We use the forward difference approximation formula

q′(t)≈q(t+h)−q(t)h

at the point t=1. We choose the step size h to be as small as possible. In this case, we choose h=1. This gives

q′(1)≈q(1+1)−q(1)1=q(2)−q(1)1=120−591=61.

So, using a forward difference approximation, the rate of change in the number of customers on June 1st is approximately 61 customers per month.
