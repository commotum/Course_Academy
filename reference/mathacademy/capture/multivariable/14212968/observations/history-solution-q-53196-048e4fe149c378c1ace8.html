Let x be the horizontal distance from the potato to the cannon and z be the actual distance between them, as shown above.

We want to find dzdt. To do this, let's start by setting up a relation between x and z. Relative to the angle θ=π6, the sides x and z are the adjacent and hypotenuse, respectively. So, we have

cos⁡(π6)=xz32=xzz=233x.

Now, we differentiate both sides of the equation with respect to t using implicit differentiation, and we get

ddt(z)=ddt(233x)dzdt=233dxdt.

We know that dzdt=18m/s. Substituting this into the above gives

dzdt=233⋅18=123.

Therefore, the speed of the potato is 123m/s.
