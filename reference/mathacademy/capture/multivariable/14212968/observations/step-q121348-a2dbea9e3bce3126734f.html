A bullet B is fired from a cannon C at an angle of 60∘ from the horizontal. Given that the bullet's horizontal speed is 300ft/s, calculate the actual speed of the bullet in the direction it is traveling.

a
750ft/s
b
150ft/s
c
300ft/s
d
2003ft/s
e
600ft/s
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x denote the horizontal distance from the bullet's projection to the cannon, and z be the distance between the bullet and the cannon, as shown above.

We want to find dzdt. To do this, let's start by setting up a relation between x and z. Relative to the angle θ=60∘, the sides x and z are the adjacent and hypotenuse, respectively. So, we have

cos⁡(60∘)=xz12=xzz=2x.

Now, we differentiate both sides of the equation with respect to t using implicit differentiation, and we get

ddt(z)=ddt(2x)dzdt=2dxdt.

We know that dxdt=300ft/s. Substituting this into the above gives

dzdt=2⋅300=600.

Therefore, the speed of the plane is 600ft/s.
