A drone D is approaching a landing point L on the ground at an angle of π4 radians to the horizontal. The drone's shadow S is projected onto the ground vertically below the drone. If the drone is approaching the ground with a speed of 6ft/s, what is the speed of the drone's shadow?

a
32ft/s
b
33ft/s
c
3ft/s
d
3ft/s
e
2ft/s
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let x be the distance between the landing point and the shadow, and let z be the distance between the landing point and the drone, as shown above.

We want to find dxdt. To do this, let's start by setting up a relation between x and z. Relative to the angle θ=π4, the sides x and z are the adjacent and hypotenuse, respectively. So, we have

cos⁡(π4)=xz22=xzx=22z.

Now, we differentiate both sides of the equation with respect to t using implicit differentiation, and we get

ddt(x)=ddt(22z)dxdt=22dzdt.

We know that dzdt=−6ft/s (negative because the distance between the landing point and the drone is decreasing). Substituting this into the above gives

dxdt=22⋅(−6)=−32.

Therefore, the speed of the shadow is 32ft/s.
