Angela observes that a drone is flying horizontally at a constant height of 5m above the ground. If the drone is flying in the opposite direction from Angela's location at a speed of 1m/s, determine the rate at which the angle of elevation θ decreases at the moment when θ=π6.

0.1rad/s

0.05rad/s

Correct
EXPLANATION

In the figure, y is the drone's altitude, and x is the horizontal distance between the drone and Angela. The quantity y=5m does not change with time because the altitude of the drone is constant.

We wish to calculate dθdt. To do this, let's start by setting up a relation between x and y. Relative to the angle θ, the sides x and y are the adjacent and opposite, respectively. So, we have

tan⁡θ=yxtan⁡θ=5xx=5cot⁡θ.

Now, we differentiate both sides of the equation with respect to t using implicit differentiation, and we get

ddt(x)=ddt(5cot⁡θ)dxdt=−5csc2⁡θ⋅dθdt.

From the given information, we have dxdt=1 and θ=π6. Substituting this into the above, we reach

1=−5csc2⁡(π6)⋅dθdt1=−5(2)2⋅dθdt1=−20⋅dθdtdθdt=−0.05.

Therefore, the angle of elevation is decreasing at a rate of 0.05 radians per second.
