Consider a plane lamina of mass 3kg and center of mass (1,1), where x and y are measured in meters. If the moment of inertia of the lamina about an axis perpendicular to the lamina passing through its center of mass is 1.5kg⋅m2, what is the moment of inertia of the lamina about an axis parallel to the first passing through the point (−3,4)?

a
68.5kg⋅m2
b
88.5kg⋅m2
c
76.5kg⋅m2
d
72.5kg⋅m2
e
82.5kg⋅m2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

For a plane lamina of mass M and a line lM perpendicular to the lamina passing through its center of mass, the parallel axis theorem states that the moment of inertia of the lamina about a line l parallel to lM is given by Il=IM+d2M, where d is the distance between the lines, and IM and Il are the moments of inertia about the lines lM and l, respectively.

The distance between the line through the center of mass (1,1) and the line through the point (−3,4) is

d=(−3−1)2+(4−1)2=16+9=25=5.

Then, applying the parallel axis theorem, we get

Il=1.5+52⋅3=1.5+75=76.5.

Therefore, the moment of inertia is 76.5kg⋅m2.
