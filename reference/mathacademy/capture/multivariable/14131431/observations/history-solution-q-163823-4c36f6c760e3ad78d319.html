For a plane lamina of mass M and a line lM perpendicular to the lamina passing through its center of mass, the parallel axis theorem states that the moment of inertia of the lamina about a line l parallel to lM is given by Il=IM+d2M, where d is the distance between the lines, and IM and Il are the moments of inertia about the lines lM and l, respectively.

The distance between the lines is d=20cm=0.2m. Then, applying the parallel axis theorem, we get

Il=1.5+(0.2)2⋅5=1.5+0.2=1.7.

Therefore, the moment of inertia is 1.7kg⋅m2.
