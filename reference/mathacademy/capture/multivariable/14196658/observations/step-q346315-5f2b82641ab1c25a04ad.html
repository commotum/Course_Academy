EXPLANATION

Using the hint, the volume of the solid generated when the region bounded by a curve y=f(x), the x-axis, and the vertical lines x=5 and x=20 is rotated 2π radians about the x-axis is given by

V=π∫520[f(x)]2dx.

Let g(x)=(f(x))2. The (regular) step size Δx is given by Δx=20−55=3.

We can approximate the integral above using a left Riemann sum with 5 equal subintervals and step size Δx as follows:

π∫520g(x)dx≈π⋅(g(5)+g(8)+g(11)+g(14)+g(17))⋅Δx

We set up a table with values of g(x) at each point:

x 5 8 11 14 17 20
f(x) 1 2 1 3 2 3
g(x)=(f(x))2 1 4 1 3 4 9

Finally, we obtain the following approximation for our volume:

V=π∫520g(x)dx≈π⋅(g(5)+g(8)+g(11)+g(14)+g(17))⋅Δx=π⋅(1+4+1+3+4)⋅3=π⋅13⋅3=39π
