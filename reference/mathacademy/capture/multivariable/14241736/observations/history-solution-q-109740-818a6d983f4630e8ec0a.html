The number of generalized eigenvectors of rank greater than 1 that correspond to an eigenvalue λ is equal to the difference between the algebraic multiplicity and the geometric multiplicity of λ.

In the characteristic polynomial p(λ)=λ3(4−λ)2(7−λ)6, we see that the root λ=0 has multiplicity 3. So the algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalue λ=0 is 3.

However, there are only 2 eigenvectors corresponding to the eigenvalue λ=0, so the geometric multiplicity of the eigenvalue λ=0 is 2.

Therefore, the number of generalized eigenvectors of rank greater than 1 corresponding to λ=0 is 3−2=1.
