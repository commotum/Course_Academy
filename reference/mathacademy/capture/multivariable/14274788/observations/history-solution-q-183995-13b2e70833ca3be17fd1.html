The principle of strong induction states the following: Suppose we have a sequence of mathematical statements P(1), P(2), P(3),…, where

  • P(1) is true (as well as P(2),P(3),P(4),…, if required), and

  • P(1),P(2),…,P(k)⇒P(k+1) for every k∈N.

Then, P(n) is true for every n∈N.

P(n):an=6n−1
