Find the inverse of the following statement:

If you are not sick, then you don't go to the hospital.

a
If you are not sick, then you go to the hospital.
b
If you go to the hospital, then you are not sick.
c
If you are sick, then you don't go to the hospital.
d
If you are sick, then you go to the hospital.
e
If you don't go to the hospital, then you are not sick.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

In our conditional statement:

  • The antecedent is A: You are not sick.

  • The consequent is B: You don't go to the hospital.

The inverse of a conditional statement A⇒B is given by

¬A⇒¬B.

First, we write down the negations of A and B:

  • ¬A: You are sick.

  • ¬B: You go to the hospital.

Therefore, the inverse is the following:

If you are sick, then you go to the hospital.
