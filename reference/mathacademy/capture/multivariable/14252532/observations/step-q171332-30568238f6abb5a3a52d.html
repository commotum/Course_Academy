Find the contrapositive of the following statement:

If {1,2}∩{2,3}=∅, then 6 is prime.

a
If 6 is prime, then {1,2}∩{2,3}=∅.
b
If {1,2}∩{2,3}≠∅, then 6 is not prime.
c
If {1,2}∩{2,3}=∅, then 6 is prime.
d
None of the listed.
e
If 6 is not prime, then {1,2}∩{2,3}≠∅.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

In our conditional statement:

  • The antecedent is A: {1,2}∩{2,3}=∅.

  • The consequent is B: 6 is prime.

The contrapositive of a conditional statement A⇒B is given by

¬B⇒¬A.

First, we write down the negations of A and B:

  • ¬A: {1,2}∩{2,3}≠∅.

  • ¬B: 6 is not prime.

Therefore, the contrapositive is the following:

If 6 is not prime, then {1,2}∩{2,3}≠∅.
