If we do not go to the cinema, then we watch a movie at home.

Which of the following statements are equivalent to the conditional statement above?

  1. If we do not watch a movie at home, then we go to the cinema.
  2. If we watch a movie at home, then we do not go to the cinema.
  3. If we go to the cinema, then we do not watch a movie at home.

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's recall the following facts:

  • The contrapositive is logically equivalent to the original statement.

  • The inverse and the converse are logically equivalent to each other, but not to the original statement.

So, we are looking for the contrapositive.

  • Statement I is the contrapositive. ✓

  • Statement I is the converse, not the contrapositive. ×

  • Statement III is the inverse, not the contrapositive. ×

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
