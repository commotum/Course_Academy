Find the contrapositive of the following statement:

If you do not go to work, then you do not get your salary.

a
If you get your salary, then you go to work.
b
If you do not get your salary, then you do not go to work.
c
If you go to work, then you get your salary.
d
If you get your salary, then you do not go to work.
e
If you do not get your salary, then you go to work.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In our conditional statement:

  • The antecedent is A: You do not go to work.

  • The consequent is B: You do not get your salary.

The contrapositive of a conditional statement A⇒B is given by

¬B⇒¬A.

First, we write down the negations of A and B:

  • ¬A: You go to work.

  • ¬B: You get your salary.

Therefore, the contrapositive is the following:

If you get your salary, then you go to work.
