Find the inverse of the following statement:

If my pet is not a fish, then it needs a food bowl.

a
If my pet is not a fish, then it does not need a food bowl.
b
If my pet is a fish, then it needs a food bowl.
c
If my pet does not need a food bowl, then it is a fish.
d
If my pet is a fish, then it does not need a food bowl.
e
If my pet needs a food bowl, then it is not a fish.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In our conditional statement:

  • The antecedent is A: My pet is not a fish.

  • The consequent is B: It needs a food bowl.

The inverse of a conditional statement A⇒B is given by

¬A⇒¬B.

First, we write down the negations of A and B:

  • ¬A: My pet is a fish.

  • ¬B: It does not need a food bowl.

Therefore, the inverse is the following:

If my pet is a fish, then it does not need a food bowl.
