We need to express the summation S(k+1) in terms of S(k).

Let ai for i≥0 be a sequence, and let S(k) denote the sum of the first k+1 terms. S(k)=∑i=0kai

We can always express S(k+1) in terms of S(k) by writing the last term on its own, as follows:

∑i=0k+1ai=(∑i=0kai)+ak+1.

In our case, we have ai=5i+3. Therefore,

ak+1=5(k+1)+3=5k+8.

Hence,

∑i=0k+1(5i+3)=(∑i=0k(5i+3))+(5k+8),

which we can write as

S(k+1)=S(k)+5k+8.
