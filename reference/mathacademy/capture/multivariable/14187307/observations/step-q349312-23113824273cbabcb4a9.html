A box contains 25 colored pencils. The ratio of red pencils to blue pencils is shown in the diagram above. How many blue pencils are in the box?

a
5
b
15
c
10
d
25
e
20
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The tape diagram shows 3 boxes corresponding to red pencils, and 2 boxes corresponding to blue pencils. Therefore, the ratio of red to blue pencils is 3:2.

We are told that there are 25 pencils in total. The tape diagram has 3+2=5 boxes in total. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 25÷5=5 pencils.

Now, we fill the number 5 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 2 boxes corresponding to blue pencils, and each box represents 5 pencils, the number of blue pencils must be:

2×5=10

So, there are 10 blue pencils in the box.
