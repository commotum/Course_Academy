EXPLANATION

We are told that he buys 16 liters of milk. The tape diagram for liters of milk shows 4 boxes. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 16÷4=4 liters.

Now, we fill the number 4 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 6 boxes corresponding to the liters of juice and milk in total, and each box represents 4 liters, the total number of liters is:

4×6=24

So, he buys 24 liters of milk and juice altogether.
