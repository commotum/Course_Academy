EXPLANATION

The tape diagram shows 3 boxes corresponding to green, and 5 boxes corresponding to yellow. Therefore, the ratio of green balls to yellow balls in David's bag is 3:5.

We are told that David's bag has 40 balls in total. The tape diagram has 3+5=8 boxes in total. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 40÷8=5 balls in the bag.

Now, we fill the number 5 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 3 boxes corresponding to green balls, and each box represents 5 balls, the number of green balls must be:

3×5=15.

So, there are 15 green balls in David's bag.
