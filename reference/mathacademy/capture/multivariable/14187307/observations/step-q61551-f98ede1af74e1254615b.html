Correct
EXPLANATION

We are told that there are 45 stairs in the warehouse. The tape diagram for the stairs shows 5 boxes. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 45÷5=9.

Now, we fill the number 9 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 9 boxes corresponding to the stairs and carts in total, and each box represents 9 of these items, the total number of stairs and carts are:

9×9=81

So, there are 81 stairs and carts altogether.
