EXPLANATION

We are told that there are 18 spherical beads in total. The tape diagram for those shows 3 boxes. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 18÷3=6 beads.

Now, we fill the number 6 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 5 boxes corresponding to cubic beads, and each box represents 6 beads, the number of the cubic beads is:

5×6=30

So, there are 30 cubic beads in the necklace.
