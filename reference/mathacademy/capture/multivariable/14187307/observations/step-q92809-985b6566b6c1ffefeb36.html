The tape diagram shows 3 boxes corresponding to boys, and 5 boxes corresponding to girls. Therefore, the ratio of boys to girls born is 3:5.

We are told that Ms. Robinson's class has 32 students in total. The tape diagram has 3+5=8 boxes in total. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 32÷8=4 students in Ms. Robinson's class.

Now, we fill the number 4 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 5 boxes corresponding to girls, and each box represents 4 students, the number of girls in Mr. Robinson's class must be:

5×4=20.

So, there are 20 girls in Mr. Robinson's class.
