Timmy set up a one-day lemonade sale in front of his house. The ratio of disposable cups to lemonade servings is given by the tape diagram above. If he had a total of 40 disposable cups to begin with, how many lemonade servings did he have then?

a
42
b
54
c
56
d
48
e
36
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are told that there were 40 disposable cups in total. The tape diagram for those shows 5 boxes. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 40÷5=8 disposable cups.

Now, we fill the number 8 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 6 boxes corresponding to lemonade servings, and each box represents 8 servings, the number of servings was:

6×8=48

So, Timmy had 48 lemonade servings in total.
