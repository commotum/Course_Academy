Barbie made a cake that had candied fruit and raisins. The ratio of candied fruit to raisins is given by the tape diagram above. If she used 8 ounces of candied fruit to make the cake, how many ounces of raisins did she use?

a
12
b
24
c
10
d
15
e
28
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are told that Barbie used 8 ounces of candied fruit in total. The tape diagram shows 4 boxes for candied fruit. Therefore, each box in the diagram above represents 8÷4=2 ounces.

Now, we fill the number 2 in each box of both tapes:

Since there are 5 boxes corresponding to raisins, and each box represents 2 ounces, the number of ounces of raisins used was :

5×2=10

So, Barbie used 10 ounces of raisins.
