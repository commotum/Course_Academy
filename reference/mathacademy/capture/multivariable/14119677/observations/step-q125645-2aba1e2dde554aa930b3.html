Consider the ellipse E and the vector field F, shown above. Let C be any path where E is traversed once in the clockwise direction. Which of the following statements are true?

  2. ∮−CF⋅dr<0
  3. ∮CF⋅dr<0

Correct
EXPLANATION

The circulation Γ measures the total work done by a force F in moving a particle along a closed curve C. It is given by the formula Γ=∮CF⋅dr=∫abF(r(t))⋅r′(t)dt.

We now recall the following:

  • If the force works with the motion of the particle (i.e., the force helps to push the particle along the path), then ∮CF⋅dr>0.

  • If the force works against the motion of the particle (i.e., the force impedes the motion of the particle as it moves along the path), then ∮CF⋅dr<0.

With that in mind, let's check each statement.

  • Statement I is true, while statement III is false. It's clear from the picture below that F assists the motion of the particle as it moves clockwise along C.
    So, Γ=∮CF⋅dr>0.
  • Statement II is true. It's clear from the picture below that F works against the motion of the particle as it moves counterclockwise along C.
    So, Γ=∮−CF⋅dr<0.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
