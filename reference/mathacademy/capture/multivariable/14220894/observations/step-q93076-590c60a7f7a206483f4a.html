Apple trees Cherry trees
271 425

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's approximate the number of apple and cherry trees by rounding to the nearest hundred.

  • Apple trees: 271⟶300

  • Cherry trees: 425⟶400

Thus, the total number of trees is approximately 300+400=700.

Therefore, the ratio of the number of apple trees to the total number of trees (expressed as a fraction) is approximately

300700.

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 100 gives

300÷100700÷100=37.

Therefore, the ratio of the number of apple trees to the total number of trees is approximately 37.
