EXPLANATION

First, let's approximate the number of orange and peach trees by rounding to the nearest 100:

  • Orange trees: 352⟶400

  • Peach trees: 618⟶600

Thus, the total number of trees is approximately 400+600=1000.

Therefore, the ratio of the number of orange trees to the total number of trees is approximately:

4001000

We can simplify this ratio by dividing both numbers by 100:

400÷1001000÷100=410

Then, we divide by 2:

4÷210÷2=25

Therefore, the ratio of the number of orange trees to the total number of trees is approximately 25.
