The vertical velocity of the eagle increases at a rate proportional to the square of the time t. In addition, the eagle loses 2m/s in the positive y-direction due to wind resistance. It is known that the eagle's vertical velocity equals 2m/s when t=2s.
The differential equation that describes the eagle's vertical velocity is y′(t)=
t2−2

Explanation

The vertical velocity increases proportionally with t2 and decreases by 2. The differential equation that describes this is

y′(t)=kt2−2,

where k is a (positive) constant of proportionality.

Since y′(2)=2, we can solve for k:

y′(2)=2k(2)2−2=24k=4k=1

Therefore, the differential equation is

y′(t)=t2−2.
