The vertical velocity increases proportionally with t2 and decreases by 2. The differential equation that describes this is y′(t)=kt2−2, where k is a (positive) constant of proportionality. Since y′(2)=2, we can solve for k: y′(2)=2k(2)2−2=24k=4k=1 Therefore, the differential equation is y′(t)=t2−2.
