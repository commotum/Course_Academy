The tangent line approximation to y(t) at the point a=5 is L(t)=y(a)+y′(a)(t−a)=y(5)+y′(5)(t−5).

We're told that y(5)=953, and since y′(t)=t2−2, then y′(5)=25−2=23.

So, the tangent line approximation is given by L(t)=953+23(t−5).

Now, we can approximate y(11023) by evaluating the tangent line approximation at x=11023. We get y(11023)≈L(11023)=953+23(11023−5)=953+23(−523)=953−5=803m.
