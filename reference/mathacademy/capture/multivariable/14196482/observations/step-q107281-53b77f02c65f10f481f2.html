Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's factor the numerator and denominator: f(x)=x2+4xx2+3x−4=x(x+4)(x+4)(x−1)

Therefore, the domain is x≠−4,1, and we notice that the function can be simplified: f(x)=xx−1.

Now we see that the function has the only vertical asymptote x=1. So statement I is false.

To find limx→1−f(x), and limx→1+f(x), we evaluate f(x) at x-values very close to x=1 from the left and right.

x 0.99 0.999 1 1.001 1.01
f(x) −99 −999 undefined 1001 101

All values are negative and get smaller and smaller as x→1 from the left and all values are positive and get larger and larger as x→1 from the right. Therefore, limx→1−f(x)=−∞,limx→1+f(x)=∞ Consequently, statement II is false and statement III is true.
