There is a rational number whose natural powers are all less than or equal to that number.

Express the above in formal language.

a
∃q∈Q,∀n∈N,q≤qn
b
∃q∈Q,∀n∈N,nq≤q
c
∀q∈Q,∃n∈N,q≤qn
d
∃q∈Q,∀n∈N,qn≤q
e
∀q∈Q,∃n∈N,qn≤q
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that our sentence can be re-phrased as

There exists a rational number q whose natural powers are all less than or equal to q.

Now, "the natural powers of q are all less than or equal to q" means that

For any natural number n, we have that qn≤q.

Therefore, we get the following statement:

There exists a rational number q such that for any natural number n we have that qn≤q.

More formally, this reads as

∃q∈Q,∀n∈N,qn≤q.
