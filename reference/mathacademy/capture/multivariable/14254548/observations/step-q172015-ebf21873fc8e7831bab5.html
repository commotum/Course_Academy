There is some pair of real numbers such that the difference of their squares is equal to double their sum.

Express the statement above in formal language.

a
∃x,y∈Z,x2−y2=2(x+y)
b
∃x,y∈R,x2+y2=2xy
c
∃x,y∈R,x2−y2=2(x+y)
d
∀x,y∈R,x2−y2=2(x+y)
e
∀x,y∈Z,x2+y2=2xy

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statement:

  • The words "there is" indicate that we use an existential quantifier, ∃.

  • The sentence refers to two real numbers. So, by denoting our numbers as x and y, we have ∃x,y∈R.

  • Finally, the condition in the statement can be written as follows: x2−y2=2(x+y)

Therefore, we write the statement as follows:

∃x,y∈R,x2−y2=2(x+y)
