Let R=[0,1]×[−21,−3] be a region in the xy-plane, and let f(x,y)=−23−13x−14y. Further, let P be a partition of R, where P1={x0,x1,…,xm},P2={y0,y1,…,yn},P=P1×P2.

Given that the upper Riemann sum of f with respect to P is given by U(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1nAAAAAΔxiΔyj where Δxi=xi−xi−1 and Δyj=yj−yj−1, what is the missing expression in the double summation above?

a
−21xi−3yj
b
−23−13xi−14yj
c
−23−13xi−1−14yj−1
d
−21xi−1−3yj−1
e
−23−13xi−1−14yj
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The upper Riemann sum is given by

U(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1nMijΔxiΔyj

where Mij is the maximum value of f in each subregion Rij.

We consider an arbitrary subregion Rij of the partition. This subregion lies between xi−1 and xi over the x-axis and yi−1 and yi over the y-axis, as shown below.

Notice that:

  • f(x,y)=−23−13x−14y decreases as x increases, and

  • f(x,y)=−23−13x−14y decreases as y increases.

As a result, the maximum value of f in each subregion is attained when we have the smallest possible x and the smallest possible y. This is the value at the bottom left corner of each subregion.

Therefore,

Mij=f(xi−1,yj−1)=−23−13xi−1−14yj−1 for all 1≤i≤m and 1≤j≤n.

Finally, the upper Riemann sum is U(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1n(−23−13xi−1−14yj−1)ΔxiΔyj.

So, the missing expression is −23−13xi−1−14yj−1.
