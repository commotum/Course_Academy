Consider all possible circular sectors MON with perimeter P=180 units. What is the radius r of the circular sector with the largest possible area?

a
58 units
b
54 units
c
45 units
d
35 units
e
44.5 units
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let r be the radius of the circular sector and let θ be the angle (in radians) subtended by it. We want to maximize the area A=12r2θ. We know that the perimeter is fixed at 180 units, so we also have the constraint condition 180=2r+rθ.

Now, by making θ the subject of the constraint equation, we find that θ=180−2rr=180r−2.

Plugging the above into our expression for A, we obtain A(r)=12r2(180r−2)=90r−r2.

We want to maximize A, so we need to differentiate A and set the derivative equal to zero. The derivative is A′(r)=90−2r.

Setting A′(r)=0 and solving for r gives A′(r)=090−2r=0r=45.

Finally, we confirm that r=45 is a maximum using the second derivative test. The second derivative gives A″(r)=−2⟹A″(45)=−2<0.

Since A″(45) is negative, we conclude that r=45 is indeed a maximum of A(r).
