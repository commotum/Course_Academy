We let r be the radius of the circular sector and let θ be the angle (in radians) subtended by it. Then, we want to minimize the perimeter

P=2r+rθ.

We know that the area is fixed at 144 square units, so we also have the constraint condition 144=r2θ2.

Now, by making θ the subject of the constraint equation and taking into account that r>0, we find that 144=12r2θθ=288r2.

Plugging this into our expression for P, we obtain P=2r+rθ=2r+r⋅288r2=2r+288r.

We want to minimize P, so we need to differentiate P and set the derivative equal to zero. Computing the derivative gives P′(r)=2−288r2.

Setting the derivative equal to zero and solving for r gives 2−288r2=02r2=288r2=144r=12.

Finally, we confirm that r=12 is a minimum using the second derivative test.

Since the second derivative P″(r)=576r3>0, the stationary point r=12 is a minimum.

Finally, when r=12, we have θ=288r2=288144=2.

The dimensions of the circular sector with the smallest perimeter are r=12 and θ=2. Therefore, the smallest perimeter is P=2r+rθ=2⋅12+12⋅2=24+24=48.
