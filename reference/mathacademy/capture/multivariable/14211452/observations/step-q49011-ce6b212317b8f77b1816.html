In a laboratory, a researcher is increasing the edges of an iron cube at a rate of 0.5 feet/min. How fast is the surface area changing when each edge is 3 feet?

a
9 feet2/min
b
18 feet2/min
c
15.5 feet2/min
d
10.5 feet2/min
e
12 feet2/min
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let x be the side of the cube, let S be its surface area, and let V be its volume. All of the variables change with time. In particular, we are given that dxdt=0.5feet/min.

We would like to find dSdt when x=3feet.

We want to find the rate of change of S knowing the rate of change of x. So, we start with the formula for the surface area of the cube:

S=6x2

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get

ddt(S)=ddt(6x2)dSdt=12xdxdt.

We can now plug in the known values into the above expression. This gives dSdt=12xdxdtdSdt=12(3)(0.5)dSdt=18.

Therefore, the cube's surface area is changing at a rate of 18feet2/min.
