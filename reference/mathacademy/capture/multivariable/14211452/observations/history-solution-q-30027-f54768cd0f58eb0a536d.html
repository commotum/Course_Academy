Let x,y,z be the dimensions of the solid, and let V be its volume. We are given that x=2 feet,y=1 feet,z=3 feet.

Also, we know that

dxdt=−0.5 feet/min,dydt=1 feet/min,dVdt=−10 feet3/min.

We would like to find dzdt when x=2feet, y=1feet, and z=3feet.

We start with the formula for the volume of the rectangular prism: V=xyz

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get

dVdt=ddt(x⋅y⋅z)dVdt=xy(dzdt)+xz(dydt)+yz(dxdt)

We can now plug in the known values into the above expression. This gives −10=(2)(1)(dzdt)+(2)(3)(1)+(1)(3)(−0.5)dzdt=−7.25 feet/min.

Therefore, the width of the solid is decreasing at a rate of 7.25feet/min at the moment when x=2feet, y=1feet, and z=3feet.
