We define the following variables:

  • let H and W be the height and width of the rectangular mold, respectively, and

  • let L and V be the length and volume of the resin, respectively.

Note that L and V change with time, while H and W are constants.

We are given that H=10in,W=18in,dLdt=3in/min.

We would like to find dVdt.

The relation between the two time-dependent variables, V and L, is given by the formula for the volume of resin: V=LWH

We differentiate both sides of V=LWH with respect to t, treating both H and W as constants, and get ddtV=ddt(LWH)dVdt=WHdLdt.

Finally, we can plug in the known values and get dVdt=(18)(10)dLdtdVdt=180⋅3dVdt=540.

Therefore, the resin's volume is increasing at a rate of 540in3/min.
