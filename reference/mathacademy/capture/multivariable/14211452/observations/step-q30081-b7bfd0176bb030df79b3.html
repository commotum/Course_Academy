The edges of a cube decrease at a rate of 9 cm/s. At what rate is the volume decreasing when the length of each edge is 40 cm?

a
 41200 cm3/s
b
 40150 cm3/s
c
43200 cm3/s
d
 44100 cm3/s
e
 42200 cm3/s
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x be the side length of the cube and let V be its volume. We are given that dxdt=−9 cm/s.

We would like to find dVdt when x=40 cm.

We start with the formula for the volume of the cube: V=x3

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get

ddt(V)=ddt(x3)dVdt=3x2dxdt.

We can now plug x=40 and dxdt=−9 cm/s into the expression for dVdt. This gives

dVdt=3x2dxdtdVdt=3(40)2⋅(−9)dVdt=−43200.

Therefore, the volume of the cube is decreasing at a rate of 43200 cm3/s at the moment when x=40cm.
