An elastic rectangular prism whose height is 15 in and width is 24 in is being stretched. If its length is increasing at a rate of 8 in/min, what is the rate of change of the surface area of the rectangular prism?
The surface area is increasing at a rate of
624
in2/min.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x,y,z be the dimensions of the rectangular prism, let S be its surface area, and let V be its volume. The variables x,S, and V change with time. In particular, we are given that y=15 in,z=24 in,dxdt=8 in/min.

We would like to find dSdt.

We want to find the rate of change of S knowing the rate of change of x. So, we start with the formula for the surface area of the rectangular prism:

S=2xz+2xy+2yz

Substituting the known values, we get S=2(x)(24)+2x(15)+2(15)(24)=78x+720.

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get

dSdt=78(dxdt).

Finally, we evaluate the above expression using dxdt=8, and get dSdt=78(8)=624.

Therefore, the rectangular prism's surface area is increasing at a rate of 624in2/min.
