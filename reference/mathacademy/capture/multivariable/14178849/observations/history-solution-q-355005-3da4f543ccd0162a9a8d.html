The best deal is the one with the lowest unit price. So, we need to compute the unit prices for all three bags. To compute the unit price, we divide the cost by the total number of kilograms.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of kilograms in bag A, we get 7.50÷5=1.50. So, the unit price of bag A is $1.50 per kilogram.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of kilograms in bag B, we get 3.20÷2=1.60. So, the unit price of bag B is $1.60 per kilogram.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of kilograms in bag C, we get 1.70÷1=1.70. So, the unit price of bag C is $1.70 per kilogram.
