Lisa wants to buy some bulk candy. The price of candy in a nearby store is $2.15/lb. How much would Lisa pay for 3 pounds of candy?

a
$6.45
b
$6.30
c
$6.65
d
$6.75
e
$6.15
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The unit price of candy is $2.15/lb. To find the cost of 3 pounds of candy, we multiply:

2.15×3=6.45

Therefore, Lisa would pay $6.45 for 3 pounds of candy.
