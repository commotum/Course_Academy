Sarah wonders what bag of coffee to buy from three different types available at the cafe:

  • Bag A weighs 12 ounces, and costs $9.60.

  • Bag B weighs 16 ounces, and costs $12.00.

  • Bag C weighs 8 ounces, and costs $6.80.

Which bag of coffee is the best deal?

a
A only
b
A and B only
c
B only
d
C only
e
A and C only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The best deal is the one with the lowest unit price. So, we need to compute the unit prices for all three bags. To compute the unit price, we divide the cost by the total number of ounces.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of ounces in bag A, we get 9.60÷12=0.80. So, the unit price of bag A is $0.80 per ounce.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of ounces in bag B, we get 12.00÷16=0.75. So, the unit price of bag B is $0.75 per ounce.

  • Dividing the total cost by the number of ounces in bag C, we get 6.80÷8=0.85. So, the unit price of bag C is $0.85 per ounce.

From the above, we see that bag B has the lowest unit price. Therefore, bag B is the best deal.
