Sarah, a registered nurse, charges $1.25 per minute for her work in a rehabilitation center. How long should she work for 1 dollar?

a
0.8 minutes
b
0.7 minutes
c
0.9 minutes
d
0.4 minutes
e
0.6 minutes
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are given the unit rate of $1.25 per minute (dollars per minute). To find how many minutes Sarah should work for $1, we need to find the unit rate in minutes per dollar. So, we take the reciprocal:

1.25dollars per minute→1÷1.25=0.8minutes per dollar

So, the unit rate is 0.8 minutes per dollar. Therefore, Sarah should work 0.8 minutes for 1 dollar.
