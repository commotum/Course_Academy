Mia charges $0.20 per minute for babysitting. For how long did she babysit if she earns 1 dollar?

a
5 minutes
b
2 minutes
c
4 minutes
d
6 minutes
e
10 minutes
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are given the unit rate of $0.2 per minute (dollars per minute). To find how many minutes she babysits for $1, we need to find the unit rate in minutes per dollar. So, we take the reciprocal:

0.2dollars per minute→1÷0.2=5minutes per dollar

So, the unit rate is 5 minutes per dollar. Therefore, Mia babysits 5 minutes for 1 dollar.
