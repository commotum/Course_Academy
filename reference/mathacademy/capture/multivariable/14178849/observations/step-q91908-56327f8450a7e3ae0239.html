EXPLANATION

The unit price of strawberries is $2.00/lb. To find the cost of 18 of a pound of strawberries, we multiply:

2×18=21×18=2×11×8=28=2÷8=0.25

Therefore, it would cost Lucy $0.25 to buy 18 of a pound of strawberries.
