Find the projection of the intersection between the sphere x2+y2+z2=10 and the plane z−y=2 onto the xy-plane.

a
(x+1)28+(y+1)24=1
b
x22+(y+1)24=1
c
x28+(y+1)24=1
d
(x+1)22+y28=1
e
(x+1)24+y24=1
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Since we want to find the projection onto the xy-plane, we need to eliminate the variable z. First, we solve for z in the equation of the plane, as follows:

z−y=2⟹z=2+y

Substituting the expression for z into the equation of the sphere, we obtain:

x2+y2+z2=10x2+y2+(2+y)2=10x2+y2+(4+4y+y2)=10x2+2y2+4y+4=10

Finally, by completing the square for the variable y, we have:

x2+2y2+4y+4=10x2+2y2+4y2+2=8x2+2(y2+2y2+1)=8x2+2(y+1)2=8x28+(y+1)24=1

Therefore, the projection of the intersection is the ellipse x28+(y+1)24=1.
