A particle moves along a circular path centered at O in the xy-plane in the counterclockwise direction with a constant angular velocity of 14 rad/s. Given that the object is at the position (1,0) when t=0, find the position function of the particle.

a
 r(t)=cos⁡t4i−sin⁡t4j
b
r(t)=cos⁡t4i+sin⁡t4j
c
 r(t)=14cos⁡ti+sin⁡tj
d
 r(t)=14(cos⁡ti+sin⁡tj)
e
 r(t)=14(cos⁡ti−sin⁡tj)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The position function of a particle moving in a circular path of radius R in the xy-plane with a constant angular velocity ω is given by r(t)=R(cos⁡ωti+sin⁡ωtj), where it is assumed that the circle is centered at O, and the particle is at the position (R,0) when t=0.

Since the object's initial position is (1,0), we have that r(0)=i, and we can conclude that the circle's radius is R=1.

Therefore, since we have R=1 and ω=14 rad/s, we get that the position function is given by r(t)=cos⁡t4i+sin⁡t4j.
