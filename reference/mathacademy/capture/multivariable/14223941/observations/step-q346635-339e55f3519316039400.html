The container is being emptied so that the distance h from the top of the container to the grain surface increases at a rate of 253hcm/min. EXPLANATION

First, let's consider the dimensions of the container. Let's call H=450cm the height of the conical container, and R=90cm its radius at the bottom. These quantities are fixed and do not change with time.

Now, let's consider the grain level. We call h the distance from the top of the container to the surface of the grain, r the radius of the cone at the grain surface, and v the volume of the empty space in the container. All of these quantities change with time. In particular, we are given

dhdt=253hcm/min,

which is positive because the distance from the top of the container to the grain surface increases as the level of the grain in the container decreases.

We want to know dvdt when the level of the grain is 180cm. At this moment, we have h=H−180=450−180=270cm.

We need to find a relation between v and the variable for which we know the rate of change, which is h. This is given by the formula for the volume of a cone:

v=13πr2h

Before we differentiate the equation with respect to time, note that r also appears in the formula. So, we need to simplify the formula by writing r in terms of h.

Note that the cones formed by the container and the empty space located between the container's top and the grain surface must be similar. Therefore, we have

rh=RHrh=90450r=h5.

Therefore, we can write the volume formula as

v=13π(h5)2hv=175πh3.

Now that we have an equation relating v and h, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule:

ddtv=ddt(175πh3)dvdt=125πh2dhdtdvdt=125πh2(253h)dvdt=13πh

Finally, substituting the known values, we get

dvdt=13π(270)dvdt=90πcm3/min.

Therefore, the volume of grain is decreasing at a rate of 90πcm3/min.
