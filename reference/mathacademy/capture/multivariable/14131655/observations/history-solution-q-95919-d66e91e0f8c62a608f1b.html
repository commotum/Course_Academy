Recall that if A=QR is a QR factorization of an m×n matrix A, then

Am×n=Qm×n×Rn×n,

where

  • Q is an m×n matrix whose columns form an orthonormal basis of Col(A)

  • R is an n×n upper triangular matrix

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is false. Q should be an m×n matrix, so Q is not necessarily a square matrix.

  • Statement II is true. Since the columns of Q form an orthonormal basis of Col(A), they must span Col(A).

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, R must be an m×n matrix.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
