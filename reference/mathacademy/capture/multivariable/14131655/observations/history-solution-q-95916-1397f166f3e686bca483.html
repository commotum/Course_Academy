Recall that if A=QR is a QR factorization of an m×n matrix A, then

Am×n=Qm×n×Rn×n,

where

  • Q is an m×n matrix whose columns form an orthonormal basis of Col(A)

  • R is an n×n upper triangular matrix

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since the columns of Q form an orthonormal basis, they are orthonormal.

  • Statement II is true. Since Q must be a 2×2 matrix, it is square.

  • Statement III is true. Since R must be a 2×2 matrix, it is square.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
