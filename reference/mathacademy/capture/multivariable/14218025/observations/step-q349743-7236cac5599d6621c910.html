EXPLANATION

The standard Maclaurin expansion for ln⁡(1+x) is

ln⁡(1+x)=x−x22+x33−x44+⋯.

Replacing x with −4x2 in the above expansion and simplifying gives

ln⁡(1−4x2)=(−4x2)−(−4x2)22+(−4x2)33−(−4x2)44+⋯=−4x2−16x42−64x63−256x84+⋯=−4x2−8x4−643x6−64x8+⋯.
