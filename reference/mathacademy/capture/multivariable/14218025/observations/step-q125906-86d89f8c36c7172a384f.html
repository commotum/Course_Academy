Find the first four non-zero terms of the Maclaurin expansion of ex+sin⁡3x.

a
 ex+sin⁡3x=1+4x+4x2−7x33+⋯
b
ex+sin⁡3x=1+4x+x22−13x33+⋯
c
ex+sin⁡3x=1+14x+2x2−11x33+⋯
d
 ex+sin⁡3x=1+2x3+4x29−16x327+⋯
e
 ex+sin⁡3x=1+2x+x24−3x316+⋯
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The Maclaurin expansion of ex is ex=1+x+x22!+x33!+⋯=1+x+x22+x36+⋯.

On the other hand, the Maclaurin series for sin⁡x is sin⁡x=x−x33!+x55!−⋯.

Replacing x with 3x, we get sin⁡3x=3x−(3x)33!+(3x)55!−⋯=3x−9x32+81x540−⋯.

Adding the expansions for ex and sin⁡3x, we get ex+sin⁡3x=(1+x+x22+x36+⋯)+(3x−9x32+81x540−⋯)=1+(1+3)x+x22+(16−92)x3+⋯=1+4x+x22−13x33+⋯.
