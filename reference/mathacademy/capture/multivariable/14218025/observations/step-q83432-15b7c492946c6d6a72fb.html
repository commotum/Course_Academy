Find the first three non-zero terms of the Maclaurin expansion of sin⁡x1−x.

a
sin⁡x1−x=x+x2+56x3+⋯
b
sin⁡x1−x=x−x3+16x5−⋯
c
sin⁡x1−x=−1+x2−56x4+⋯
d
sin⁡x1−x=−1+x−53x2+⋯
e
sin⁡x1−x=x+2x3+23x5+⋯
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The standard Maclaurin expansion for sin⁡x is sin⁡x=x−13!x3+15!x5−⋯=x−16x3+1120x5−⋯.

The standard Maclaurin expansion for 11−x is 11−x=1+x+x2+⋯.

To find the expansion of sin⁡x1−x, we multiply the expansion of sin⁡x by the expansion of 11−x, as follows: sin⁡x1−x=(x−16x3+1120x5−⋯)(1+x+x2+⋯)

Let's work out each term individually:

  • There is a single x term, given by x⋅1=x.

  • There is a single x2 terms, given by x⋅x=x2.

  • There are two x3 terms, and their sum is given by x⋅x2+(−16x3)⋅1=x3−16x3=56x3.

So, the first three nonzero terms of the expansion are

sin⁡x1−x=x+x2+56x3+⋯.
