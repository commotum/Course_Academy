The population P(t) of turtles on a certain island follows the logistic growth model

dPdt=4.8P−0.0012P2,P(0)=200,

where t is the time, measured in years. For which value of P is the turtle population growing at the fastest possible rate?

a
500
b
1000
c
2000
d
900
e
1500
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A logistic growth equation has the form

P′=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

For a logistic growth model, the fastest rate of growth occurs when P=12a.

First, let's rewrite the right-hand side of the given equation so that it matches the form above:

dPdt=4.8P−0.0012P2=0.0012P(4000−P)

From here, we see that a=4000. Hence, the carrying capacity is 4000, and P grows at its fastest possible rate when

P=12a=12⋅4000=2000.
