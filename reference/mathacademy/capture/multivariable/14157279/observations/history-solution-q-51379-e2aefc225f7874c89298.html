A logistic growth equation has the form

P′=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

For a logistic growth model, the fastest rate of growth occurs when P=12a.

First, let's rewrite the right-hand side of the given equation so that it matches the form above:

dPdt=4P−0.002P2=0.002P(2000−P)

From here, we see that a=2000. Hence, the carrying capacity is 2000, and P grows at its fastest possible rate when

P=12a=12⋅2000=1000.
