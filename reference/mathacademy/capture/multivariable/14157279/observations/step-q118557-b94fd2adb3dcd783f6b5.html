The population y(t) of turtles on a certain island follows the logistic growth model y′=1.5y−0.001y2,y(0)=500, where t is the time, in years, since the observations began. According to this model, what is the maximum possible population of turtles?

a
2500
b
1500
c
3000
d
1000
e
500
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A logistic growth equation has the form y′=ky(a−y),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>y is the carrying capacity.

The carrying capacity a represents the maximum possible population. A diagram showing a typical logistic growth curve is shown below.

First, let's rewrite the right-hand side of the given equation so that it matches the form above:

y′=1.5y−0.001y2=0.001y(1500−y)

From here, we see that a=1500. Hence, the carrying capacity is 1500, and we conclude that the number of turtles cannot exceed 1500.
