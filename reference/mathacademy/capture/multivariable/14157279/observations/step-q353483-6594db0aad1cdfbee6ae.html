The download count P(t) of a viral mobile game follows the logistic growth model

dPdt=4P(8000−P),P(0)=600.

where t is the time, measured in years. For which value of P is the download count growing at the fastest possible rate?

P=
4000
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A logistic growth equation has the form

P′=kP(a−P),

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>P is the carrying capacity.

For a logistic growth model, the fastest rate of growth occurs when P=12a.

From our equation, we see that a=8000. Hence, the carrying capacity is 8000, and P grows at its fastest possible rate when

P=12a=12⋅8000=4000.
