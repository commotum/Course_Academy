EXPLANATION

First, let f(y)=1+y and g(y)=1+y3.

We start by determining the points where the curves intersect by solving the following equation for y:

f(y)=g(y)1+y=1+y3y−y3=0y(1−y2)=0y(1−y)(1+y)=0y=−1,0,1

The solutions corresponding to our region are y=0 and y=1. So, we need to integrate from y=0 to y=1.

Now, let's draw our usual horizontal arrow through the region to determine the left and right functions.

The horizontal arrow

  • enters the region through the left function x=g(y)=1+y3, and

  • leaves through the right function x=f(y)=1+y.

Therefore, using the shell method, the volume of the solid can be expressed as follows:

V=2π∫cdy([right]−[left])dy=2π∫01y([1+y]−[1+y3])dy=2π∫01y(y−y3)dy
