A surveyor stands 15 ft away from the base of a radio tower. An elevator inside the tower moves straight up at a constant speed of 20 ft/s. How fast is the distance between the surveyor and the elevator changing when the elevator is 20 ft above the ground?
The distance between the surveyor and the elevator is
16
ft/s.

Correct
EXPLANATION

We start by drawing a diagram of the problem.

In the diagram,

  • x is the distance between the surveyor and the base of the tower,

  • y is the distance between the elevator and the ground,

  • z is the distance between the surveyor and the elevator.

As shown in the diagram, x, y, and z form a right triangle with z as the hypotenuse. We wish to find the rate of change of z, which is dzdt.

We know that x=15ft and y=20ft. We can find z using the Pythagorean Theorem, as follows:

x2+y2=z2152+202=z2625=z2z=25ft

We also know that:

  • dydt=20ft/s,

  • the distance between the surveyor and the base of the tower remains the same, so dxdt=0.

Finally, we differentiate both sides of x2+y2=z2 with respect to t and then substitute in the known values, as follows:

ddt(x2+y2)=ddt(z2)2xdxdt+2ydydt=2zdzdt2(15)(0)+2(20)(20)=2(25)dzdt800=50dzdtdzdt=16

Therefore, the distance between the surveyor and the elevator is 16ft/s.
