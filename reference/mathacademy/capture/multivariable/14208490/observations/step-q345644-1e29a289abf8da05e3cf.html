Alex and Brooke agree to meet at the library. Alex arrives on time, but Brooke is late. After waiting for a while, Alex decides to leave and calls Brooke. They agree to head home instead.

Alex travels north from the library at a speed of 6km/h. Brooke travels east away from the library. When Alex is 4km from the library, Brooke is 3km from the library. If the distance between them is increasing at a rate of 6km/h, what is Brooke’s speed?

EXPLANATION

We start by drawing a diagram of the problem.

In the diagram,

  • x is the distance between Brooke and the library,

  • y is the distance between Alex and the library,

  • z is the distance between Alex and Brooke.

As shown in the diagram, x, y, and z form a right triangle with z as the hypotenuse. We wish to find the rate of change of x, which is dxdt.

We know that x=3km and y=4km. We can find z using the Pythagorean Theorem, as follows: x2+y2=z232+42=z29+16=z2z2=25z=5km

We also know that:

  • dydt=6km/h,

  • dzdt=5km/h.

Finally, we differentiate both sides of x2+y2=z2 with respect to t and then substitute in the known values: ddt(x2+y2)=ddt(z2)2xdxdt+2ydydt=2zdzdt2(3)dxdt+2(4)(6)=2(5)(6)6dxdt+48=606dxdt=12dxdt=2

Therefore, Brooke's speed is 2km/h.
