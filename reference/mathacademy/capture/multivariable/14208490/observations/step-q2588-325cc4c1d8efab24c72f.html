A boat B is being pulled in toward a dock D with a rope by a fisherman F standing on the dock. His hands are 4 ft above the ground, and he is pulling the rope at a rate of 3 ft/s, how fast is the distance between the dock and the boat decreasing when the boat is 3ft from the dock?

8 ft/s
b
5 ft/s

4 ft/s

11 ft/s

EXPLANATION

In the diagram,

  • x is the distance between the dock and the boat,

  • y is the distance between the fisherman's hands and the ground, and

  • z is the distance between the fisherman's hands and the boat mooring.

As shown in the diagram, x, y, and z form a right triangle with z as the hypotenuse. We wish to find the rate of change of x, which is dxdt.

We know that x=3ft and y=4ft . We can find z using the Pythagorean Theorem, as follows:

z2=x2+y2z2=32+42z2=9+16z2=25z=5ft

We also know that:

  • dzdt=−3ft/h,

  • the distance between the fisherman's hands and the ground remains the same, so dydt=0.

Finally, we differentiate both sides of x2+y2=z2 with respect to t and then substitute in the known values:

ddt(x2+y2)=ddt(z2)2xdxdt+2ydydt=2zdzdt2(3)dxdt+2(4)(0)=2(5)(−3)6dxdt=−30dxdt=−5.

Therefore, the distance between the dock and the boat is decreasing at a rate of 5ft/s.
