James and Mary have a date at the cinema theater. James arrived at the agreed time, but Mary is late. After 20 minutes of waiting, James decides to go home.

James travels north from the theater with a speed of 3.2km/h. Mary travels west toward the theater. When James is 0.8km from the theater, Mary is 1.5km from the theater. If the distance between them increases at a speed of 2.3km/h, what is Mary's speed?

EXPLANATION

We start by drawing a diagram of the problem.

In the diagram,

  • x is the distance between Mary and the cinema theater,

  • y is the distance between James and the cinema theater,

  • z is the distance between James and Mary.

As shown in the diagram, x, y, and z form a right triangle with z as the hypotenuse. We wish to find the rate of change of x, which is dxdt.

We know that x=1.5km and y=0.8km. We can find z using the Pythagorean Theorem, as follows:

x2+y2=z21.52+0.82=z2z2=2.89z=1.7km

We also know that:

  • dydt=3.2km/h,

  • dzdt=2.3km/h.

ddt(x2+y2)=ddt(z2)2xdxdt+2ydydt=2zdzdt2(1.5)dxdt+2(0.8)(3.2)=2(1.7)(2.3)3dxdt=7.82−5.12dxdt=0.9

Therefore, Mary is approaching the cinema theater with a speed of 0.9km/h.
