Which of the following is equivalent to the integral ∫Cxyds, where C is the section of the polar curve r=2cos⁡θ for 0≤θ≤π2?

a
8∫0π/2cos⁡2θsin⁡θdθ
b
8∫0π/2cos3⁡θsin⁡θdθ
c
4∫0π/2cos2⁡θsin⁡θdθ
d
4∫0π/2sin2⁡θcos⁡θdθ
e
4∫0π/2sin⁡2θdθ
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

In polar coordinates, the formula for the line integral is given by

∫Cf(x,y)ds=∫θ1θ2f(rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ)r2+(drdθ)2dθ.

The connection between polar and Cartesian coordinates gives

x=rcos⁡θ=2cos⁡θ⋅cos⁡θ=2cos2⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ=2cos⁡θ⋅sin⁡θ=2sin⁡θcos⁡θ.

So, along our curve, we have x=2cos2⁡θ and y=2sin⁡θcos⁡θ, where 0≤θ≤π2.

Now, if f(x,y)=xy, we obtain

f(rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ)=2cos2⁡θ⋅2sin⁡θcos⁡θ=4cos3⁡θsin⁡θ.

Next, we have

drdθ=ddθ(2cos⁡θ)=−2sin⁡θ,

and therefore

r2+(drdθ)2dθ=(2cos⁡θ)2+(−2sin⁡θ)2=4(cos2⁡θ+sin2⁡θ)=4=2.

Finally, we can write the integral as

∫Cxyds=∫0π/2f(rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ)r2+(drdθ)2dθ=∫0π/24cos3⁡θsin⁡θ⋅2⋅dθ=8∫0π/2cos3⁡θsin⁡θdθ.
