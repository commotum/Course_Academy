Which of the following Diophantine equations have solutions?

  1. 4x+3y=5
  2. 4x+8y=9
  3. 5x+10y=12
a
I only
b
I and III only
c
I and II only
d
II only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that a linear Diophantine equation ax+by=c has a solution if and only if d|c, where d=gcd(a,b).

With that in mind, let's examine each equation in turn.

  • Equation I has solutions. We have a=4, b=3, and c=5. Since d=gcd(4,3)=1 and 1|5, our equation has solutions.

  • Equation II has no solution. We have a=4, b=8, and c=9. Since d=gcd(4,8)=4 and 4⧸|9, our equation has no solution.

  • Equation III has no solution. We have a=5, b=10, and c=12. Since d=gcd(5,10)=5 and 5⧸|12, our equation has no solution.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
