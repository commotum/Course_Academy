Which of the following Diophantine equations have solutions?

  1. 4x+12y=6
  2. 2x+12y=4
  3. −3x+5y=2
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
II only
d
I and III only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a linear Diophantine equation ax+by=c has a solution if and only if d|c, where d=gcd(a,b).

With that in mind, let's examine each equation in turn.

  • Equation I has no solution. We have a=4, b=12, and c=6. Since d=gcd(4,12)=4 and 4⧸|6, our equation has no solution.

  • Equation II has solutions. We have a=2, b=12, and c=4. Since d=gcd(2,12)=2 and 2|4, our equation has solutions.

  • Equation III has solutions. We have a=−3, b=5, and c=2. Since d=gcd(−3,5)=1 and 1|2, our equation has solutions.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
