Which of the following pairs (x,y) are solutions of the Diophantine equation 3x+6y=18?

  1. (10,−1)
  2. (4,1)
  3. (−6,6)
a
II only
b
I and III only
c
III only
d
I and II only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's check each pair in turn.

  • (10,−1) is not a solution of our equation. Substituting x=10 and y=−1, we obtain 3x+6y=183⋅10+6⋅(−1)=1830−6=1824≠18×

  • (4,1) is a solution of our equation. Indeed, substituting x=4 and y=1, we obtain 3x+6y=183⋅4+6⋅1=1812+6=1818=18✓

  • (−6,6) is a solution of our equation. Indeed, substituting x=−6 and y=6, we obtain 3x+6y=183⋅(−6)+6⋅6=18−18+36=1818=18✓

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
