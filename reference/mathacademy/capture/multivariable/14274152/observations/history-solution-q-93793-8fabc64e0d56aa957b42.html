Recall that a linear Diophantine equation ax+by=c has a solution if and only if d|c, where d=gcd(a,b).

With that in mind, let's examine each equation in turn.

  • Equation I has no solution. We have a=14, b=−21, and c=8. Since d=gcd(14,−21)=7 and 7⧸|8, our equation has no solution.

  • Equation II has no solution. We have a=4, b=−16, and c=6. Since d=gcd(4,−16)=4 and 4⧸|6, our equation has no solution.

  • Equation III has solutions. We have a=−2, b=7, and c=5. Since d=gcd(−2,7)=1 and 1|5, our equation has solutions.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
