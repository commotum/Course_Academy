Explanation

To convert from polar coordinates to Cartesian coordinates, we use

x=rcos⁡θ and y=rsin⁡θ,

where x, y, and r are functions of θ.

Using the fact that r=6sin⁡θ, we get

x=6sin⁡θ⋅cos⁡θ=3sin⁡(2θ)

and

y=6sin⁡θ⋅sin⁡θ=6sin2⁡θ.
