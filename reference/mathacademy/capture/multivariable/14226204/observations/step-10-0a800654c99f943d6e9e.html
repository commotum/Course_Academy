Given r(θ)=6sin⁡θ, find the third derivative of r(θ) with respect to θ.
r(3)(θ)=
−6cosθ
Submit
Correct
Explanation

Computing the first derivative, we get

r′(θ)=ddθ(6sin⁡θ)=6cos⁡θ.

Computing the second derivative, we get

r″(θ)=ddθ(6cos⁡θ)=−6sin⁡θ.

Finally, computing the third derivative, we get r(3)(θ)=ddθ(−6sin⁡θ)=−6cos⁡θ.
