Let R be the event that the selected marble is red, and

  • let A be the event that the selected marble is from box A,

  • let B be the event that the selected marble is from box B,

  • let C be the event that the selected marble is from box C.

Then the disjoint union A⊔B⊔C covers the entire sample space.

Recall that the law of total probability states that

P(R)=P(R∩A)+P(R∩B)+P(R∩C).

We wish to find P(R∩C):

  • To calculate P(R∩A), we take the number of red marbles in box A (16) and divide by the total number of marbles (80). So, we have P(R∩A)=1680.

  • To calculate P(R∩B), we take the number of red marbles in box B (14) and divide by the total number of marbles (80). So, we have P(R∩B)=1480.

  • Also, we're given that P(R)=50%=4080.

Finally, substituting the above information into the law of total probability and solving for P(R∩C), we get the following:

P(R)=P(R∩A)+P(R∩B)+P(R∩C)4080=1680+1480+P(R∩C)P(R∩C)=4080−1680−1480P(R∩C)=1080P(R∩C)=12.5%
