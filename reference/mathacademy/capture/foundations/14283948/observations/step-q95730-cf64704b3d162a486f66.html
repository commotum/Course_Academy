EXPLANATION

The spectral decomposition of a 2×2 symmetric matrix A is given by

A=λ1u1⋅u1T+λ2u2⋅u2T, where u1 and u2 are orthonormal eigenvectors of A corresponding to the eigenvalues λ1 and λ2, respectively.

First, let's normalize our eigenvectors:

u1=v1‖v1‖=15[21]u2=v2‖v2‖=125[−24]

Therefore, we have

A=λ1u1u1T+λ2u2u2T=5⋅15[21]⋅15[21]+(−5)⋅125[−24]⋅125[−24]=5⋅15[21][21]+(−5)⋅120[−24][−24]=15(5⋅[21][21]+(−5)⋅14[−24][−24])=15(5⋅[4221]+(−5)⋅14[4−8−816])=15(5⋅[4221]+(−5)⋅[1−2−24])=15[152020−15]=[344−3].
