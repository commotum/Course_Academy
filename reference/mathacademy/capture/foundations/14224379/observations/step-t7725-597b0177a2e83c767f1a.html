The flux measures the total flow of through as depicted below.

Flux is always measured with respect to a preselected unit normal vector and we have two choices of orientation for this vector. If we measure the flux relative to the unit normal pointing in the opposite direction, our flux will have the same magnitude but opposite sign.

This lesson aims to extend the concept of flux to three-dimensional vector fields. But first, we need a brief discussion on surface orientations.
