The vector-valued function f(θ,ϕ), given by f(θ,ϕ)=⟨_____,sinh⁡ϕsin⁡θ,cosh⁡ϕ⟩

parametrizes one sheet of the hyperboloid of two sheets defined in Cartesian coordinates as x2+y2−z2=−1. By expressing the equation of the hyperboloid in cylindrical polar coordinates, determine the first component of f.

Hint: You may use the fact that the hyperbola z2−r2=k2 can be parametrized as ⟨z,r⟩=⟨kcosh⁡ϕ,ksinh⁡ϕ⟩ for ϕ∈R.

a
sin⁡θ
b
sinh⁡ϕsin⁡θ
c
sinh⁡ϕ
d
sinh⁡ϕcos⁡θ
e
cosh⁡ϕcos⁡θ
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

If (r,θ,z) are the cylindrical coordinates of a point and (x,y,z) are the corresponding Cartesian coordinates of that point, then we have x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ,z=z.

Substituting this into our equation, we get x2+y2−z2=−1(rcos⁡θ)2+(rsin⁡θ)2−z2=−1r2(cos2⁡θ+sin2⁡θ)−z2=−1r2−z2=−1z2−r2=1.

Here, the hyperbola z2−r2=1 represents the intersection of our hyperboloid with the plane that passes through the z-axis and makes the angle of θ with the positive x-axis. Using the hint, a parametrization of such hyperbola can be given as ⟨z,r⟩=⟨cosh⁡ϕ,sinh⁡ϕ⟩,ϕ∈R.

Now, for any point (x,y,z) on our surface, we obtain the following:

f(θ,ϕ)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ,z⟩=⟨(sinh⁡ϕ)cos⁡θ,(sinh⁡ϕ)sin⁡θ,cosh⁡ϕ⟩=⟨sinh⁡ϕcos⁡θ,sinh⁡ϕsin⁡θ,cosh⁡ϕ⟩

where θ∈[0,2π) and ϕ∈R.

Therefore, the first component of f is sinh⁡ϕcos⁡θ.
