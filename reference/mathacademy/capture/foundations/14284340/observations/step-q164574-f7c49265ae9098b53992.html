The vector-valued function f(θ,ϕ), given by

f(θ,ϕ)=⟨3cosh⁡ϕcos⁡θ,_______,3sinh⁡ϕ⟩

parametrizes the hyperboloid of one sheet defined in Cartesian coordinates as x2+y2−z2=9. By expressing the equation of the hyperboloid in cylindrical polar coordinates, determine the second component of f.

Hint: You may use the fact that the hyperbola r2−z2=k2 can be parametrized as ⟨r,z⟩=⟨kcosh⁡ϕ,ksinh⁡ϕ⟩ for ϕ∈R.

a
 cosh⁡ϕcos⁡θ
b
3cosh⁡ϕsin⁡θ
c
 cosh⁡ϕsin⁡θ
d
 3sin⁡ϕsin⁡θ
e
 3sinh⁡ϕcos⁡θ

Correct
EXPLANATION

If (r,θ,z) are the cylindrical coordinates of a point and (x,y,z) are the corresponding Cartesian coordinates of that point, then we have

x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ,z=z.

Substituting this into our equation, we get

x2+y2−z2=9(rcos⁡θ)2+(rsin⁡θ)2−z2=9r2(cos2⁡θ+sin2⁡θ)−z2=9r2−z2=9.

Here, the hyperbola r2−z2=9 represents the intersection of our hyperboloid with the plane that passes through the z-axis and makes the angle of θ with the positive x-axis. Using the hint, a parametrization of such hyperbola can be given as

⟨r,z⟩=⟨3cosh⁡ϕ,3sinh⁡ϕ⟩,ϕ∈R.

Now, for any point (x,y,z) on our surface, we obtain the following:

f(θ,ϕ)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨rcos⁡θ,rsin⁡θ,z⟩=⟨(3cosh⁡ϕ)cos⁡θ,(3cosh⁡ϕ)sin⁡θ,3sinh⁡ϕ⟩=⟨3cosh⁡ϕcos⁡θ,3cosh⁡ϕsin⁡θ,3sinh⁡ϕ⟩

where θ∈[0,2π) and ϕ∈R.

Therefore, the second component of f is 3cosh⁡ϕsin⁡θ.
