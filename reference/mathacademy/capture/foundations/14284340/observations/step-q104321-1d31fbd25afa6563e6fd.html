EXPLANATION

Solving our equation for x, we obtain

5x=10y2+2z2⟹x=2y2+25z2.

Now, for any point (x,y,z) on our surface, we obtain the following:

f(y,z)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨2y2+25z2,y,z⟩

where y∈(−∞,∞) and z∈(−∞,∞).

Therefore, the first component of f is 2y2+25z2.
