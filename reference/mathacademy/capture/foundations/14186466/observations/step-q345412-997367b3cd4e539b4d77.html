The diagram shows a series RCL circuit with a source electromotive force (EMF) of 13sin⁡(3t)V, a resistor of resistance 4Ω, a capacitor of capacitance 0.25F, and an inductor of inductance 1H.

The current I, measured in amperes, is defined as the rate of flow of charge q, measured in coulombs. I=dqdt

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RCL circuit, there are three voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality R=4Ω. So, VR=4I.

  • The voltage drop across the capacitor is related to its charge by q=CVC. Therefore, VC=qC=q0.25=4q.

  • The voltage drop across the inductor is proportional to the rate of change of current, with a constant of proportionality L=1H. Hence, VL=dIdt.
