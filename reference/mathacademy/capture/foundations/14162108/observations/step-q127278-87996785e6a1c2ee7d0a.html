EXPLANATION

The osculating plane passes through the point P, which is at the tip of the vector r(0). The vectors T(0) and N(0) are parallel to this plane. Therefore, a parametric equation for the plane can be written as R(s,u)=r(0)+sT(0)+uN(0),s,u∈(−∞,∞).

We have that r(0)=⟨0,cos⁡0−sin⁡0,cos⁡0+sin⁡0⟩=⟨0,1,1⟩.

Hence, a parametric equation for the osculating plane at the point (0,1,1) is given by R(s,u)=r(0)+sT(0)+uN(0)=⟨0,1,1⟩+s⋅33⟨−1,−1,1⟩+u⋅22⟨0,−1,−1⟩=⟨−s33,1−s33−u22,1+s33−u22⟩.
