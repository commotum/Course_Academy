Consider the vector field F(x,y) shown above, and let C be the path where the semiellipse is traversed from A to B. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. ∫CF⋅dr>0
  2. ∫CF⋅dr=0
  3. ∫−CF⋅dr=0
a
 III only
b
 II only
c
 I only
d
 II and III only
e
 I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that ∫CF⋅dr gives the total work done by a force F in moving a particle along C:

  • If the force works with the motion of the particle (i.e., the force helps to push the particle along the path), then ∫CF⋅dr>0.

  • If the force works against the motion of the particle (i.e., the force impedes the motion of the particle as it moves along the path), then ∫CF⋅dr<0.

With that in mind, let's check each statement.

  • Statement I is false, while statement II is true. Since the force F acts perpendicular to the motion of the particle as it moves from A to B, it neither helps nor hinders its motion.
    So, ∫CF⋅dr=0.
  • Statement III is true. If we reverse the orientation of C, then the situation with regards to the work done by F is similar to the previous case.
    So, ∫−CF⋅dr=0.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
