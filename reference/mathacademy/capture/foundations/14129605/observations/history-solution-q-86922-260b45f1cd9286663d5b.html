A random variable is a variable whose value is determined by the outcome of a trial or experiment. A continuous random variable is a random variable whose possible values are represented by an interval of real numbers.

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given statements.

  • Statement I does not describe a continuous random variable. Its value is determined by the result of an experiment, but its possible values are the integers 1,2,3,…,10, which do not correspond to an interval of real numbers.

  • Statement II does not describe a continuous random variable. The number of aces in a standard deck of playing cards is not the outcome of a trial or experiment. (By definition, there are 4 aces in a standard deck of playing cards.)

  • Statement III describes a continuous random variable. Its value is determined by the result of an experiment, and its possible values correspond to the real numbers in the interval (0,∞).

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
