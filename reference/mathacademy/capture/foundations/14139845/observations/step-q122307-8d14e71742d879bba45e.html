A particle P moves along the x-axis where x>0. When the displacement of P from the origin O is x meters, its velocity is vm/s and the magnitude of its acceleration is 1x+1m/s2. Given that the acceleration is directed toward O, which differential equation describes this motion?

a
12v2dvdx=1x+1
b
vdvdx=1x+1
c
vdvdx=−1x+1
d
2vdvdx=−1x+1
e
v2dvdx=−1x+1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the acceleration of the particle P has magnitude 1x+1 and is directed toward O, we have dvdt=−1x+1.

Using the chain rule for differentiation, we can rewrite this equation as dvdx⋅dxdt=−1x+1.

Now, since v=dxdt, we obtain the differential equation vdvdx=−1x+1.
