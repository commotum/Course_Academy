EXPLANATION

Let T(t) denote the unit tangent to the given curve as a function of the parameter t. Since we are given the curve r(t) parametrized by the parameter t (not the arc length s), we will use the following formula for the curvature: κ(t)=‖T′(t)‖‖r′(t)‖

Now, we calculate r′(t) and find its magnitude:

r′(t)=⟨ddt(sin⁡3t),ddt(cos⁡3t),ddt(t)⟩=⟨3cos⁡3t,−3sin⁡3t,1⟩‖r′(t)‖=(3cos⁡3t)2+(−3sin⁡3t)2+(1)2=9(cos2⁡(3t)+sin2⁡(3t))+1=10

Hence, the corresponding unit tangent vector is

T(t)=r′(t)‖r′(t)‖=110⟨3cos⁡3t,−3sin⁡3t,1⟩.

Next, we calculate T′(t) and find its magnitude: T′(t)=110⟨ddt(3cos⁡3t),ddt(−3sin⁡3t),ddt(1)⟩=110⟨−9sin⁡3t,−9cos⁡3t,0⟩‖T′(t)‖=110(9sin⁡3t)2+(−9cos⁡t)2+02=11092(sin2⁡3t+cos2⁡3t)=910

Finally, the curvature is

κ(t)=‖T′(t)‖‖r′(t)‖=91010=910.
