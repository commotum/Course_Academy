Let v=p+qi, where p,q∈R2, be an eigenvector corresponding to the eigenvalue λ=a+bi.

Then, a complex solution of the system is given by xcomplex(t)=veλt.

Using Euler's formula, we obtain xcomplex(t)=ve(a+bi)t=eat(cos⁡(bt)+isin⁡(bt))(p+qi)=eat(cos⁡(bt)p−sin⁡(bt)q)+ieat(sin⁡(bt)p+cos⁡(bt)q).

Therefore, the general solution of the system is x(t)=c1eat(cos⁡(bt)p−sin⁡(bt)q)+c2eat(sin⁡(bt)p+cos⁡(bt)q), where the parts (cos⁡(bt)p−sin⁡(bt)q) and (sin⁡(bt)p+cos⁡(bt)q) are bounded.

Now, notice that the given curve is bounded (and closed), representing an ellipse (circle) that is traversed clockwise.

But we have that:

  • eat→∞ as t→∞ when a>0, and

  • eat→0 as t→∞ when a<0.

Thus, we must have a=0.
