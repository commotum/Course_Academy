EXPLANATION

The volume of the parallelepiped spanned by three vectors is given by the absolute value of their triple product.

First, we compute the triple product:

a⋅(b×c)=|12435−7102|=1⋅|5−702|−2⋅|3−712|+4⋅|3510|=(5⋅2−0⋅(−7))−2(3⋅2−1⋅(−7))+4(3⋅0−1⋅5)=10−26−20=−36

Finally, we take the absolute value. So the volume of the corresponding parallelepiped is V=|−36|=36.
