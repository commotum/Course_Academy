EXPLANATION

Notice that if R covers D completely and we define

f(x,y)={y1+x2,(x,y)∈R∩D0,(x,y)∈R∖D,

we obtain

∬Dy1+x2dA=∬Rf(x,y)dA.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the given statements.

  • Statement I is true. The rectangle R covers the region D completely.

  • Statement II is true. The rectangle R covers the region D completely.

  • Statement III is false. The integrals have not been stated correctly with respect to the domains R and D (they have been swapped).
