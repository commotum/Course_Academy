Compute the Wronskian W(y1,y2) for y1(x)=ex and y2(x)=cos⁡x.

a
sin⁡x−excos⁡x
b
−2exsin⁡x
c
ex(sin⁡x−cos⁡x)
d
exsin⁡x
e
−ex(sin⁡x+cos⁡x)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The Wronskian W(y1,y2) of two functions y1(x) and y2(x) is defined as W(y1,y2)=|y1y2y1′y2′|=y1y2′−y2y1′.

In our case, we have y1(x)=ex and y2(x)=cos⁡x. Therefore W(y1,y2)=(ex)(cos⁡x)′−(cos⁡x)(ex)′=(ex)(−sin⁡x)−(cos⁡x)(ex)=−exsin⁡x−excos⁡x=−ex(sin⁡x+cos⁡x).
