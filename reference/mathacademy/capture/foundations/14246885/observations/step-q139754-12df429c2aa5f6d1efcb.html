Ax=QTA

Let A=QR be a QR decomposition of an m×n matrix A with linearly independent columns, and the matrix equation above gives the least-squares solution of Ax=b. From left to right, what are the missing parts of the equation?

a
RT and bT
b
R and mb
c
R and b
d
R−1 and mb
e
R−1 and b
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The least-squares solution of Ax=b satisfies the normal equation

ATAx=ATb.

Substituting A=QR, we obtain

ATAx=ATb(QR)TQRx=(QR)TbRT(QTQ)Rx=RTQTb.

Since Q has orthonormal columns, QTQ=I, and we get

RTRx=RTQTb.

Finally, since RT is invertible, pre-multiplying by (RT)−1 gives

(RT)−1⋅RTRx=(RT)−1⋅RTQTbRx=QTb.

Therefore, the missing parts of the equation are R and b.
