  1. f(x+12)=f(x) for all x∈R
  2. f(x)=F(x) for all x∈R
  3. f(x+3)=f(x) for all x∈R

EXPLANATION

Since f is the 6-periodic extension of F, we have

  • f(x+6)=f(x) for all x∈R, and

  • f(x)=F(x) for all x∈[0,6).

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. We have f(x+12)=f((x+6)+6)=f(x+6)=f(x). for all x∈R.

  • Statement II is false. For example, take x=7. Then, f(7)=f(1+6)=f(1)=F(1)=3, while F(7)=27. Hence, f(x)≠F(x) for x=7.

  • Statement III is false. For example, take x=2. Then, f(2+3)=f(5)=F(5)=19 while f(2)=F(2)=7. Hence, f(x+3)≠f(x) for x=2.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only".
