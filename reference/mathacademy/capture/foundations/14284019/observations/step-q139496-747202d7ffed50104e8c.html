Which of the following statements are true?

  1. Any 5×3 matrix A has at most 3 distinct singular values.
  2. Any 3×5 matrix A has at most 3 distinct singular values.
  3. The singular values of the matrix A=[−100−2] are negative.
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
I and III only
d
I and II only
e
II only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The singular values of the matrix A are the square roots of the eigenvalues of the matrix ATA.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since A is a 5×3 matrix and AT is a 3×5 matrix, ATA must be a 3×3 matrix: AT3×5×A5×3=ATA3×3 Hence, A has at most 3 distinct singular values.

  • Statement II is false. Since A is a 3×5 matrix and AT is a 5×3 matrix, ATA must be a 5×5 matrix: AT5×3×A3×5=ATA5×5 Hence, A has at most 5 distinct singular values.

  • Statement III is false. All singular values of any matrix are non-negative.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
