Find the locations of all of the critical points of the transformation T, where T is defined as

T:[uv]⟶[3u2+2v2u+v].

a
The critical points lie on the parabola 2v=3u2.
b
The critical points lie on the line v=32u.
c
The critical points lie on the circle u2+v2=6.
d
The critical points lie on the hyperbola 2v2−3u2=1.
e
The critical points lie on the line v=6u.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Given a transformation

T:[uv]→[x(u,v)y(u,v)],

the Jacobian determinant of T, denoted ∂(x,y)∂(u,v), is given by

∂(x,y)∂(u,v)=|∂x∂u∂x∂v∂y∂u∂y∂v|.

To find the locations of the critical points of T, we need to solve ∂(x,y)∂(u,v)=0.

Here, we have x(u,v)=3u2+2v2 and y(u,v)=u+v.

First, we find the partial derivatives:

∂x∂u=6u∂x∂v=4v∂y∂u=1∂y∂v=1

Therefore, the corresponding Jacobian determinant is

∂(x,y)∂(u,v)=|∂x∂u∂x∂v∂y∂u∂y∂v|=|6u4v11|=6u⋅1−4v⋅1=6u−4v.

The function has critical points when ∂(x,y)∂(u,v)=0. So, we obtain

∂(x,y)∂(u,v)=06u−4v=0v=32u.

The equation v=32u defines a line in the uv-plane.
