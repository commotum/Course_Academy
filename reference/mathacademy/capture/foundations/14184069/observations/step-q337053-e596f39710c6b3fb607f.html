EXPLANATION

The diagram shows a series RC circuit with a source electromotive force (EMF) of 15V, a resistor of resistance 250Ω, and a capacitor of capacitance 0.004F.

The current I, measured in amperes, is defined as the rate of flow of charge q, measured in coulombs. I=dqdt

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RC circuit, there are two voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality R=250Ω. So, VR=250I=250dqdt.

  • The voltage drop across the capacitor is related to its charge by q=CVC. Therefore, VC=q0.004=250q.

Therefore, by Kirchhoff's voltage law, the differential equation governing the amount of charge q on the capacitor is 15=250dqdt+250q, which gives dqdt+q=350.
