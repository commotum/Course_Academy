Which of the following random variables could be modeled by an exponential distribution?

  1. The time between customer calls, in minutes, for a particular business
  2. The number of winning lottery tickets in a set of 10 tickets
  3. The number obtained when picking an integer number between 0 and 15 at random
a
 II only
b
 I only
c
 III only
d
 I and II only
e
 I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The exponential distribution can be thought of as the continuous analog of the geometric distribution. It models the amount of continuous time or space until a particular event occurs, where the events should occur independently and at a constant average rate.

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given random variables.

  • Random variable I can be modeled using an exponential distribution. Calls can be modeled as independent events with a constant average rate, and we wish to know the amount of time until the next call occurs.

  • Random variable II does not follow an exponential distribution. There is no interval of continuous time or space. The number of winning lottery tickets is discrete, not continuous.

  • Random variable III does not follow an exponential distribution. There is no interval of continuous time or space. The number obtained is discrete, not continuous.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
