Which of the following random variables could be modeled by an exponential distribution?

  1. The time it takes for a taxi to reach any address in a city, starting from the same point
  2. The time it takes for a taxi to reach a particular address in a city, starting from the same point
  3. The number of defective electronic components produced in a factory every week
a
II only
b
I and II only
c
I only
d
I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The exponential distribution can be thought of as the continuous analog of the geometric distribution. It models the amount of continuous time or space until a particular event occurs, where the events should occur independently and at a constant average rate.

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given random variables.

  • Random variable I does not follow an exponential distribution. The times needed to reach different destinations will likely have different average rates.

  • Random variable II could be modeled using an exponential distribution. Journeys along a particular route can be modeled as independent events with a constant average rate, and we wish to know the amount of time needed to reach the destination.

  • Random variable III does not follow an exponential distribution. There is no interval of continuous time or space. The number of defective components is discrete, not continuous.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
