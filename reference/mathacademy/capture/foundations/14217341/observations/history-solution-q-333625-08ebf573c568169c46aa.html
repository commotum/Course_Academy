The general solution of the system is [x(t)y(t)]=c1e2t[11]+c2e−2t[−13],c1,c2∈R.

  • The line that passes through the origin and is parallel to v1=[11] has the equation y=x. The straight-line solutions of the form c1e2t[11] lie on that line and approach infinity as t→∞. So, y=x is an unstable line.

  • The line that passes through the origin and is parallel to v1=[−13] has the equation y=−3x. The straight-line solutions of the form c2e−t[−13] lie on that line and approach the origin as t→∞. So, y=−3x is a stable line.

Therefore, the equilibrium point (origin) is a saddle point.
