The general solution of the system is [x(t)y(t)]=c1e4t[21]+c2et[−11],c1,c2∈R.

  • The line that passes through the origin and is parallel to v1=[21] has the equation y=12x. The straight-line solutions of the form c1e4t[21] lie on that line and approach infinity as t→∞. So, y=12x is an unstable line.

  • The line that passes through the origin and is parallel to v2=[−11] has the equation y=−x. The straight-line solutions of the form c2et[−11] lie on that line and approach infinity as t→∞. So, y=−x is an unstable line.

Therefore, the equilibrium point (origin) is an unstable node (source).
