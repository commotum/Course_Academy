The characteristic polynomial of A is given by p(λ)=λ2−tr(A)λ+det(A).

{λ1+λ2=tr(A)λ1⋅λ2=det(A).

So, the product of the eigenvalues of A is equal to the determinant of A. Therefore, λ1⋅λ2=det(A)=|53−47|=5⋅7−(−4)⋅3=35+12=47.
