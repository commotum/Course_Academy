The conditional probability mass function fY|X(y|x) is defined as fY|X(y|x)=P(Y=y|X=x).

Therefore, we're required to compute the conditional probability fY|X(0|1)=P(Y=0|X=1).

From the given table, f(1,0)=0.25.
