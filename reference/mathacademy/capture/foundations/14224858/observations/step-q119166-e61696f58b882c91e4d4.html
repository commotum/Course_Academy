EXPLANATION

Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

The volume of a cylinder decreases at a rate that's proportional to the square of its radius.

We note the following:

  • The question describes the rate at which the volume V of a cylinder varies. Therefore, this is a statement about the quantity dVdt.

  • We're told that dVdt decreases at a rate that's proportional to the square of its radius. Therefore, dVdt=−kr2, where k>0.

We wish to use this information to find an expression for drdt.

The volume V of a cylinder is given by V=πr2h.
