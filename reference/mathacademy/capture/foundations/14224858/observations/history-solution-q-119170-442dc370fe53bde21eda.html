Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

The volume of a cylinder decreases at a rate proportional to its volume.

We note the following:

  • The question describes the rate at which the volume V of a cylinder varies. Therefore, this is a statement about the quantity dVdt.

  • We're told that dVdt decreases at a rate proportional to the volume. Therefore, dVdt=−kV, where k>0.

We wish to use this information to find an expression for dhdt.

The volume V of a cylinder is given by V=πr2h.

Differentiating this equation with respect to t, and using the fact that the radius of the cylinder does not change, we get dVdt=ddt(πr2h)dVdt=πr2dhdt.

Therefore, dVdt=−kVπr2dhdt=−k(πr2h)dhdt=−kπr2hπr2dhdt=−kh.
