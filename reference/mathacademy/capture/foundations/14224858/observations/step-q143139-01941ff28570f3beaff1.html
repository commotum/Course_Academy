At each point P on a particular curve, the derivative y′(x) is proportional to the shortest distance from P to the y-axis. The derivative at the point (3,−5) equals 24. What differential equation describes this curve?

a
y′(x)=x2+y23
b
y′(x)=8|x|
c
y′(x)=3|x|
d
y′(x)=4x2+y2
e
y′(x)=24|y|5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

At each point P on a particular curve, the derivative y′(x) is proportional to the shortest distance from P to the y-axis.

We note the following:

  • The shortest distance from P to the y-axis is given by |x|.

  • Since the derivative is proportional to the shortest distance from P to the y-axis, we have y′(x)=k⋅|x| where k is a constant.

So, our differential equation is

y′(x)=k|x|.

In addition, we're told that y′(x)=24 when at the point (3,−5). We can use this to determine the value of the coefficient k:

24=k|3|⟹k=8

Finally, the differential equation describing the curve is y′(x)=8|x|.
