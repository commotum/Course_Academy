The usual inner product of functions f and g over x∈[0,2π) is given by ⟨f(x),g(x)⟩=∫02πf(x)g(x)dx.

For a 2π-periodic function f(x) that is piecewise continuous over x∈[0,2π), the Fourier series of f is given by f(x)∼a02+∑n=1∞(ancos⁡(nx)+bnsin⁡(nx)), where a0, an, and bn are constants to be determined.

Now, we find the inner product of f(x) with cos⁡(4x):
