Let D be the region enclosed by the curve C parametrized as r(t)=⟨cos⁡t−cos⁡2t,sin⁡t−sin⁡2t⟩ for t∈[π3,5π3]. The area of D can be written as

Area(D)=∬D(∂Q∂x−∂P∂y)dA=∫π/35π/3AAAAAAAdt,

where P(x,y)=−y and Q(x,y)=0. Determine the missing part of the equation.

a
(sin⁡t−sin⁡2t)(cos⁡t−2cos⁡2t)
b
(cos⁡t−cos⁡2t)(cos⁡t−2cos⁡2t)
c
(cos⁡2t−cos⁡t)(cos⁡t−2cos⁡2t)
d
(sin⁡2t−sin⁡t)(sin⁡t−2sin⁡2t)
e
(sin⁡t−sin⁡2t)(sin⁡t−2sin⁡2t)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Our simple, smooth, closed path C is positively oriented, and

∂Q∂x−∂P∂y=0−(−1)=1.

So, according to Green's theorem, we can write the area of the region D enclosed by the curve C as follows:

Area(D)=∬DdA=∬D(∂Q∂x−∂P∂y)dA=∮CPdx+Qdy

Substituting P(x,y)=−y and Q(x,y)=0, we get

∮CPdx+Qdy=∮C−ydx.

The derivative of r(t) is r′(t)=⟨−sin⁡t+2sin⁡2t,cos⁡t−2cos⁡2t⟩.

Therefore, we can write our line integral as

∮C−ydx=∫π/35π/3−(sin⁡t−sin⁡2t)⋅(−sin⁡t+2sin⁡2t)dt=∫π/35π/3(sin⁡t−sin⁡2t)(sin⁡t−2sin⁡2t)dt.
