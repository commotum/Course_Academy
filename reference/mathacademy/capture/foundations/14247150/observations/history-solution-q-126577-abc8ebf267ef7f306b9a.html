According to Green's theorem, if F(x,y)=⟨P(x,y),Q(x,y)⟩, where

∂Q∂x−∂P∂y=1,

we can write the area of the region D enclosed by the curve C as follows:

Area(D)=∬DdA=∬D(∂Q∂x−∂P∂y)dA=∮CPdx+Qdy=∮CF⋅dr

With that in mind, let's examine each pair in turn.

  • Vector field I does not give the required area. In this case, ∂P∂y=1 and ∂Q∂x=0, which gives ∂Q∂x−∂P∂y=−1≠1.

  • Vector field II does not give the required area. Notice that ∂P∂y=17 and ∂Q∂x=67, which gives ∂Q∂x−∂P∂y=57≠1.

  • Vector field III gives the required area. Notice that ∂P∂y=e−y and ∂Q∂x=e−y+1, which gives ∂Q∂x−∂P∂y=1.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
