EXPLANATION

If we set f(x,y,z)=x2+y2+z2, our surface can be viewed as the level surface f(x,y,z)=3.

A normal vector to the level surface that passes through the point (−1,−1,1) can be found by computing ∇f(−1,−1,1).

So, we find the gradient and evaluate it at (−1,−1,1):

∇f(x,y,z)=fx(x,y,z)i+fy(x,y,z) j+fz(x,y,z) k=∂∂x(x2+y2+z2)i+∂∂y(x2+y2+z2)j+∂∂z(x2+y2+z2)k=2x i+2y j+2z k∇f(−1,−1,1)=2(−1) i+2(−1) j+2(1) k=−2i−2j+2k=⟨−2,−2,2⟩
