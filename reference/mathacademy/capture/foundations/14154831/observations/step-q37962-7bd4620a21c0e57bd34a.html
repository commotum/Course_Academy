EXPLANATION

Notice that the dividend is missing the x2-term and the constant term. So, we write it using a zero coefficient, as follows:

x3+0x2−4x+0

Using the method of polynomial division, we obtain the following:

x+1x2−3x+5x+3x3+0x2−4x+10x+1x3+3x2―x+1x3−3x2−4x+10x+1x3−3x2−9x―x+1x3−3x2−5x+10x+1x3−3x2−5x+15―x+1x3−3x2+5x−15

Therefore, the missing expressions are

x2−3x+5 and 5x+0.
