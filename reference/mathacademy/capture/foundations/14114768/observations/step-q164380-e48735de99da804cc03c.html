The projection of the intersection between the hyperboloid of two sheets −(x+1)218+y22+(z−2)2=−1 and the plane z=3 onto the xy-plane is a hyperbola. What are the lengths of the semi-axes of the hyperbola?

a
6 and 3
b
6 and 2
c
9 and 4
d
32 and 2
e
18 and 2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the intersection of the hyperboloid and the plane z=3, we substitute z=3 into the equation of the hyperboloid: −(x+1)218+y22+(3−2)2=−1−(x+1)218+y22+1=−1−(x+1)218+y22=−2(x+1)218−y22=2(x+1)236−y24=1(x+1)262−y222=1

This gives us the equation of a hyperbola with semi-axes of lengths 6 and 2.
