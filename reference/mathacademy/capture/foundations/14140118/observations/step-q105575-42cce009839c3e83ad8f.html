Consider the vector field F shown above. Which of the following statements are true everywhere in the domain of F?

  1. ∇⋅F=0
  2. ∇⋅F>0
  3. ∇⋅F<0
  4. F is solenoidal
a
I and IV only
b
II only
c
I only
d
III only
e
II and IV only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that divF=∇⋅F denotes the divergence of the vector field F.

Intuitively, the divergence of F at a point P measures how much F acts like a source or a sink at P:

  • If F acts like a source at P, then ∇⋅F>0 at P.

  • If F acts like a sink at P, then ∇⋅F<0 at P.

  • If F acts like neither a source nor a sink at P, then ∇⋅F=0 at P.

If ∇⋅F=0 everywhere in the domain of F, we say that F is solenoidal.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement:

  • Statement I is true, whereas statements II and III are false. For any point P, the sum of the inward-pointing vectors equals the sum of the outward-pointing vectors. Therefore, F acts neither like a source nor a sink.

  • Statement IV is true. Since ∇⋅F=0 everywhere, the vector field F is solenoidal.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and IV only."
