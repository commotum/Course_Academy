Let T=[2−1−63] be the standard matrix of the linear transformation T. Then T maps R2 onto

a
y=−12x
b
y=R2

d

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Notice that T has proportional rows, and yet T is not the zero matrix.

Therefore, rank(T)=1 and T maps R2 onto a line that passes through the origin.

Moreover, the line must pass through the origin (0,0) and the points (2,−6) and (−1,3), which are defined by the columns of the matrix T.

Computing the slope m of this line, we get m=3−(−6)−1−2=−3.

Therefore, T maps R2 onto the line y=−3x.
