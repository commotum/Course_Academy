Which of the following statements are true?

  1. Any 3×4 matrix A has at most 4 distinct singular values.
  2. Any 8×6 matrix A has at most 8 distinct singular values.
  3. The matrix A=[−100−1] has one negative singular value.
a
I only
b
II only
c
III only
d
II and III only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The singular values of the matrix A are the square roots of the eigenvalues of the matrix ATA.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since A is a 3×4 matrix and AT is a 4×3 matrix, ATA must be a 4×4 matrix: AT4×3×A3×4=ATA4×4 Hence, A has at most 4 distinct singular values.

  • Statement II is false. Since A is a 8×6 matrix and AT is a 6×8 matrix, ATA must be a 6×6 matrix: AT6×8×A8×6=ATA6×6 Hence, A has at most 6 distinct singular values.

  • Statement III is false. All singular values of any matrix are non-negative. So, A can't have a negative singular value.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
