Recall that F(x,y)=P(X≤x,Y≤y) is the probability that the pair (X,Y) will lie inside the infinite rectangular region that has its top-right corner at (x,y) and whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axes.

Let's sketch the region corresponding to the probability we wish to find.

Geometrically, this region can be obtained by taking the infinite rectangle that has its top-right corner at (3,6) (the blue region below) and subtracting the infinite rectangle that has its top-right corner at (−2,6) (the red region below).

Therefore, we have

P(−2≤X≤3,Y≤6)=P(X≤3,Y≤6)⏞top-right corner at (3,6)−P(X≤−2,Y≤6)⏞top-right corner at (−2,6)=F(3,6)−F(−2,6)=0.8−0.15=0.65.
