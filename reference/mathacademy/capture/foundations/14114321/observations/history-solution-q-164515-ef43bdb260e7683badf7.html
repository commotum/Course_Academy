Recall that the equation of an elliptic cone centered at O whose axis of symmetry is the z-axis is given by

z2c2=x2a2+y2b2.

Thus, an elliptic cone centered at O whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis is given by

y2b2=x2a2+z2c2.

Therefore, our equation is an elliptic cone whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis and b=5,a=c=2.
