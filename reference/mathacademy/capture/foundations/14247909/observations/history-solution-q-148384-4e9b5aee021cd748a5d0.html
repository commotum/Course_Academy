Recall that the correlation coefficient of the random variables X and Y is defined as

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[Y],

where

  • Cov[X,Y] is the covariance, and

  • SD[X] and SD[Y] are the standard deviations of X and Y.

In our case, we have

SD[X]=Var[X]=5,SD[X]=Var[Y]=20=25.

Substituting our data in the formula, we obtain

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[X]=85⋅25=45.
