1.Computing the Flux of a Vector Field Through a Tilted Plane?

Consider the vector field F(x,y,z)=⟨x,y,2z⟩ and the surface S defined in Cartesian coordinates as z=2x+3y for 0≤x≤1 and 0≤y≤1. Calculate the flux of F through S with respect to the upward-pointing unit normal vector n.

Hint: You're given that the fundamental vector product of S with respect to its canonical parametrization is ||rx×ry||=14.
