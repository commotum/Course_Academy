We have to sketch the vector field at the points (1,4),(2,−2), and (4,−2).

To sketch the vector field at the given points, we substitute their coordinates into the function F(x,y)=−xi−yj:

F(1,4)=−(1)i−4j=−i−4jF(2,−2)=−(2)i−(−2)j=−2i+2jF(4,−2)=−(4)i−(−2)j=−4i+2j
