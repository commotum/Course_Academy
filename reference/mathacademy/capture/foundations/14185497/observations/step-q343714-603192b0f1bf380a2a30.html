A series RCL circuit has a source electromotive force (EMF) of 12V, a resistance of 40Ω, a capacitance of 0.005F, and an inductance of L=20H. Find the differential equation governing the amount of charge q (in coulombs) on the capacitor at time t (in seconds).

Hint: The voltage drop VC across the capacitor is related to its charge by q=CVC, where C is the capacitance measured in Farads.

a
d2qdt2+2dqdt+10q=125
b
d2qdt2+2dqdt+10q=12
c
d2qdt2+2dqdt+110q=35
d
d2qdt2+12dqdt+10q=35
e
d2qdt2+2dqdt+10q=35

Correct
EXPLANATION

We have the following circuit diagram.

The current I, measured in amperes, is defined as the rate of flow of charge q, measured in coulombs. I=dqdt

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RCL circuit, there are three voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality R=40Ω. So, VR=40I=40dqdt.

  • The voltage drop across the capacitor is related to its charge by q=CVC. Therefore, VC=qC=q0.005=200q.

  • The voltage drop across the inductor is proportional to the rate of change of current, with a constant of proportionality L=20H. Hence, VL=20dIdt=20ddt(dqdt)=20d2qdt2.

Therefore, by Kirchhoff's voltage law, the differential equation governing the amount of charge q on the capacitor is

12=40dqdt+200q+20d2qdt2,

which gives

d2qdt2+2dqdt+10q=35.
