We focus on the term with the exponential e−πs/3 (which corresponds to the step function in the time domain) and identify the rational function part: F(s)=s(s2+9)(s+2).

We'll solve this in three steps.

Step 1. Partial fraction decomposition

Since the denominator contains a linear factor (s+2) and an irreducible quadratic factor (s2+9), the decomposition takes the form s(s2+9)(s+2)=As+2+Bs+Cs2+9. To find the constants, we clear the denominators: s=A(s2+9)+(Bs+C)(s+2). Now, we find the constants A,B, and C.
