Peter wants to cut a piece of square-shaped glass while maintaining its shape. The area of this glass is decreasing at a rate of 1.5 in2/min. At what rate are the diagonals of the glass decreasing if the length of these diagonals is 50 in?

a
0.1 in/min
b
0.125 in/min
c
0.03 in/min
d
0.05 in/min
e
0.2 in/min
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let h and A be the length of the diagonals and the area of the square, respectively. Using the Pythagorean theorem, it can be shown that the relationship between A and h is

A=h22.

We want to find

dhdt.

Differentiating this relationship with respect to t using implicit differentiation, we get

ddt(A)=ddt(h22)dAdt=dhdt⋅ddh(h22)dAdt=dhdt⋅hdhdt=1h⋅dAdt.

We're told that A decreases at a rate of 1.5 in2/min, so

dAdt=−1.5.

Also, we want to compute the rate of change of the diagonals when the diagonal has length h=50. Substituting this information into our expression for dhdt, we get

dhdt=150⋅(−1.5)dhdt=−1.550dhdt=−0.03in/min.

We conclude that the diagonals of the glass are decreasing at a rate of 0.03 in/min.
