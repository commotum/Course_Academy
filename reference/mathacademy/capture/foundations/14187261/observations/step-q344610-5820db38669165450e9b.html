The diagonals of a square are decreasing at a rate of 72 m/min. At what rate is the perimeter of the square decreasing?
The perimeter is decreasing at a rate of
28
m/min.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let p be the perimeter of the square, and h be the length of the diagonal. The relationship between p and h is

p=22h.

Differentiating this relationship with respect to t using implicit differentiation, we get

ddt(p)=ddt(22h)dpdt=dhdt⋅ddh(22h)dpdt=dhdt⋅22.

We're told that each diagonal of the square is decreasing at a rate of 72 m/min, so we have dhdt=−72.

Substituting this information into the above expression for dpdt, we get

dpdt=(−72)(22)=−28 m/min.

Therefore, the perimeter of the square decreases at 28m/min.
