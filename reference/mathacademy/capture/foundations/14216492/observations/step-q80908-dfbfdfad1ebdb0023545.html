EXPLANATION

Given a decoupled system of first-order linear ODEs in the form

[x1′(t)x2′(t)]=[λ00λ][x1(t)x2(t)], the formula for the general solution is x(t)=c1[10]eλt+c2[01]eλt,c1,c2∈R.

Since the matrix of our differential equation is diagonal, the system is decoupled.

Therefore, the general solution is given by x(t)=c1[10]e−t/3+c2[01]e−t/3,c1,c2∈R.
