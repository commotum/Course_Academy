For two random variables X and Y, we have Var[X]=7, Var[Y]=5, and Cov[X,Y]=3. Find Var[X+Y].

a
20
b
18
c
9
d
15
e
6
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X and Y are random variables, then

Var[X±Y]=Var[X]+Var[Y]±2Cov[X,Y].

Substituting the given data into the formula above, we obtain

Var[X+Y]=7+5+2⋅3=18.
