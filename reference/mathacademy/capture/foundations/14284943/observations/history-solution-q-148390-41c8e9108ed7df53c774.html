Recall that if X1,…,Xn are random variables, and a1,…,an are constants, then

Var[∑i=1naiXi]=∑i=1nai2Var[Xi]+2∑i<jaiajCov[Xi,Xj].

Hence, for n=3 random variables and constants a1=a2=a3=1, we have

Var[X1+X2+X3]=Var[X1]+Var[X2]+Var[X3]=+2Cov[X1,X2]+2Cov[X1,X3]+2Cov[X2,X3].

Substituting the given data into the formula above, we obtain

Var[X1+X2+X3]=2+5+3+2⋅4+2⋅1+2⋅3=26.
