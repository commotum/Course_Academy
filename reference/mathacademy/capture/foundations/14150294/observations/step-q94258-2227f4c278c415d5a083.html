Given that the differential equation y″+8y′+16y=−8 has the general solution y=Ae−4x+Bxe−4x−12, solve the initial value problem y″+8y′+16y=−8,y(0)=12,y′(0)=0.

 y=e−4x+2xe−4x−12

 y=e−4x+4xe−4x−12

 y=2e−4x+13xe−4x−12

Correct
EXPLANATION

We find the constants A and B using the initial conditions.

  • Substituting y(0)=12 into the general solution gives A−12=12A=1.

  • To apply the condition y′(0)=0, we first differentiate y to get y′=−4Ae−4x+Be−4x−4Bxe−4x, and then we substitute y′(0)=0 into the above to get −4A+B=0−4(1)+B=0B=4.

Therefore, the solution to the initial value problem is y=e−4x+4xe−4x−12.
