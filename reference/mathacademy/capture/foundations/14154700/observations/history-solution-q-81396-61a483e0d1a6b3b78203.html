We find the constants A and B using the initial conditions.

  • Substituting y(0)=2 into the general solution gives B=2.

  • To apply the condition y′(0)=5, we first differentiate y to get y′=5Acos⁡5x−5Bsin⁡5x. Substituting y′(0)=5 into the above gives 5=5A⟹A=1.

Therefore, the solution to the initial value problem is

y=sin⁡5x+2cos⁡5x.
