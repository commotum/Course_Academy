We would like to find a rectangular region of the form

Δ={(u,v):a≤u≤b,c≤v≤d}={(u,v):a≤u(x,y)≤b,c≤v(x,y)≤d},

where the equations of the left, right, bottom, and top sides are

u(x,y)=a,u(x,y)=b,v(x,y)=c,v(x,y)=d.

With that in mind, let's analyze the boundary of our region D.

Rewriting the equations

x=3y,x=7y,ex=2ey,ex=6ey

by moving both variables x and y to the left-hand side, we obtain

xy=3,xy=7,ex+y=2,ex+y=6.

Therefore, if we put u(x,y)=xy and v(x,y)=ex+y, we can define the transformation T−1 as

T−1:(x,y)→(xy,ex+y).

To find T, we should express x and y in terms of u and v. Recall that we have

{u=xyv=ex+y.

From the first equation, x=uy. Substituting this into the second equation, we get

v=euy+y⟹ln⁡v=uy+y⟹y=ln⁡vu+1.

Finally, substituting y=ln⁡vu+1 back into x=uy, we obtain

x=uln⁡vu+1.

Therefore, the transformation T can be given by

T:(u,v)→(uln⁡vu+1,ln⁡vu+1).
