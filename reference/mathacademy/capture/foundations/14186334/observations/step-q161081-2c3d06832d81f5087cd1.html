A transformation T, given by T:(u,v)→(x(u,v),y(u,v)), maps a rectangular region Δ with sides parallel to the u- and v-axes from the uv-plane onto D in the xy-plane. EXPLANATION

We would like to find a rectangular region of the form

Δ={(u,v):a≤u≤b,c≤v≤d}={(u,v):a≤u(x,y)≤b,c≤v(x,y)≤d},

where the equations of the left, right, bottom, and top sides are

u(x,y)=a,u(x,y)=b,v(x,y)=c,v(x,y)=d.

With that in mind, let's analyze the boundary of our region D.

Re-writing the equations

y=2x2,y=4x2,y=3x,y=5x

by moving both variables x and y to the left-hand side, we obtain

x2y=2,x2y=4,yx=3,yx=5.

Therefore, if we put u(x,y)=x2y and v(x,y)=yx, we can define the transformation T−1 as

T−1:(x,y)→(x2y,yx).
