The vector-valued function r(z,θ) above parametrizes the surface of revolution generated by rotating the curve x=z−z2 by 2π radians about the z-axis for z∈[0,1]. What is the second component of r?

z

z−z2

(z−z2)cos⁡θ

(z−z2)sin⁡θ

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A point (x,y,z) on the surface generated by rotating the graph of x=f(z) by 2π radians about the z-axis has the position vector

r=⟨f(z)cos⁡θ,f(z)sin⁡θ,z⟩,z∈[a,b],θ∈[0,2π).

First, notice that x=f(z)=z−z2≥0 for all z∈[0,1].

So, the parametrization of our surface of revolution is

r(z,θ)=⟨(z−z2)cos⁡θ,(z−z2)sin⁡θ,z⟩,z∈[0,1],θ∈[0,2π).

Therefore, the second component of r is (z−z2)sin⁡θ.
