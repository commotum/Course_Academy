A point (x,y,z) on the surface generated by rotating the graph of y=f(x) by 2π radians about the x-axis for x∈[a,b] has the position vector

r=⟨x,f(x)cos⁡θ,f(x)sin⁡θ⟩,x∈[a,b],θ∈[0,2π).

First, notice that y=f(x)=1−x2≥0 for all x∈[−1,1].

So, the parametrization of our surface of revolution is

r(x,θ)=⟨x,1−x2cos⁡θ,1−x2sin⁡θ⟩,x∈[−1,1],θ∈[0,2π).
