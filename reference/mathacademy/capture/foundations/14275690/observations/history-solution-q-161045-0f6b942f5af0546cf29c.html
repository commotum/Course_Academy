A point (x,y,z) on the surface generated by rotating the graph of y=f(z) by 2π radians about the z-axis has the position vector

r=⟨f(z)sin⁡θ,f(z)cos⁡θ,z⟩,z∈[a,b],θ∈[0,2π).

First, notice that y=f(z)=(z−1)3+1≥0 for all z∈[1,2].

So, the parametrization of our surface of revolution is

r(z,θ)=⟨((z−1)3+1)sin⁡θ,((z−1)3+1)cos⁡θ,z⟩,z∈[1,2],θ∈[0,2π).

Therefore, the second component of r is ((z−1)3+1)cos⁡θ.
