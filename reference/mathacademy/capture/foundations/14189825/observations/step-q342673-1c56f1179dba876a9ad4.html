EXPLANATION

The definition of tanh⁡x is tanh⁡x=ex−e−xex+e−x=e2x−1e2x+1=1−e−2x1+e−2x.

Using the second definition, we get tanh(32ln⁡x)=e2(32ln⁡x)−1e2(32ln⁡x)+1=e3ln⁡x−1e3ln⁡x+1=eln⁡x3−1eln⁡x3+1=x3−1x3+1.
