A geometric random variable X with probability of success p has the following probability mass function: f(x)=(1−p)x−1p

Let X be the number of attempts made until the page successfully loads. Since the chance of successfully loading the page is 18%, the number of attempts until the page successfully loads can be modeled as a geometric random variable with a probability of success p=18%=18100=0.18.

So, X∼Geom(0.18). In this case, X has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=(1−0.18)x−1(0.18)=(0.82)x−1(0.18)

Therefore, the probability that the page will successfully load for the first time on the 7th attempt is P(X=7)=f(7)=(0.82)7−1(0.18)=(0.82)6(0.18)≈0.055,

rounded to 3 decimal places.
