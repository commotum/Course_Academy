A geometric random variable X with probability of success p has the following probability mass function: f(x)=(1−p)x−1p

Let X be the number of draws until a red card is drawn. The probability of getting a red card (heart or diamond) on any draw is p=2652=12.

So, the number of draws until a red card came up can be modeled as a geometric random variable with a probability of success p=12.

So, X∼Geom(12). In this case, X has the following probability mass function: f(x)=(1−12)x−1(12)=(12)x−1(12)

Therefore, the probability of finding a red card by the 5th attempt is P(X≤5)=P(X∈{1,2,3,4,5})=f(1)+f(2)+f(3)+f(4)+f(5)=(12)1−1(12)+(12)2−1(12)+(12)3−1(12)+(12)4−1(12)+(12)5−1(12)=(12)+(12)(12)+(12)2(12)+(12)3(12)+(12)4(12)=[1+(12)+(12)2+(12)3+(12)4](12)=[1+12+14+18+116](12)≈0.969,

rounded to 3 decimal places.
