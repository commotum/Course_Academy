Which of the following random variables follow a geometric distribution?

  1. The number of free throws a basketball player attempts before making a basket
  2. The number of families without children among 25 families interviewed
  3. The number of people watching a movie in a movie theater
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
III only
d
II only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A geometric distribution is used to model the number of binomial experiments needed before the first success.

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given random variables.

  • Random variable I follows a geometric distribution. Making a free throw is a binomial experiment, and the number of free throws needed before making a basket can be interpreted as the number of binomial experiments needed before the first success.

  • Random variable II does not follow a geometric distribution. Asking a family whether they have children is a binomial experiment, but the number of families without children cannot be interpreted as the number of binomial experiments needed before the first success.

  • Random variable III does not follow a geometric distribution. The number of people watching a movie in a movie theater is not a binomial experiment.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
