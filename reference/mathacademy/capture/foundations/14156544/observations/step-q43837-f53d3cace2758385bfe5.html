EXPLANATION

Every linear transformation in R2 can be represented as a 2×2 matrix. The standard matrix representation of the transformation has columns that are the images of the vectors i and j under the action of the transformation.

From the diagram we see that i′=[12] and j′=[−1−1].

Therefore, the standard matrix for the linear transformation is

T=[1−12−1].
