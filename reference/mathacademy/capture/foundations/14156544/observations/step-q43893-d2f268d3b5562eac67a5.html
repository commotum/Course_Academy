The linear transformation T has the standard matrix T=[4−451]. What are the respective images of the standard basis vectors {i,j} of R2 under the action of T?

a
 [−45] and [41]
b
 [45] and [−41]
c
 [−41] and [45]
d
 [4−4] and [51]
e
 [41] and [−45]
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Method 1

The images of the vectors in the standard basis {i,j} are the first and second columns of T, respectively.

Therefore, we have the following:

T(i)=T[10]=[45]T(j)=T[01]=[−41]

Method 2

We can find the images of the vectors in the standard basis {i,j} by computing the matrix products T⋅i and T⋅j:

T(i)=T⋅i=[4−451][10]=[45]T(j)=T⋅j=[4−451][01]=[−41]
