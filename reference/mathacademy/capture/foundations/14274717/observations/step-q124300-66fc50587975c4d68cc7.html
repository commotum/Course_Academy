EXPLANATION

The line integral of unity with respect to x along a path C is equal to the net change in x between the terminal and initial points:

∫Cdx=x2−x1, where x2 is the x-coordinate of the terminal point, and x1 is the x-coordinate of the initial point.

In our case, x2=1 and x1=−3. Therefore,

∫Cdx=x2−x1=1−(−3)=4.
