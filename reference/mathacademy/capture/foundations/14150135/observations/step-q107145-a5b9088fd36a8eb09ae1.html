Consider the vector space C[0,1] of all continuous functions f:[0,1]→R and its subset G={f∈C[0,1]|f(0)≥0}.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. G is closed under function addition
  2. G is closed under scalar multiplication
  3. G is a subspace of C[0,1]
  4. G is not a subspace of C[0,1]
a
 II and IV only
b
 I, II, and III only
c
IV only
d
II only
e
 I and IV only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a subset of a vector space is a subspace if it's closed under addition and scalar multiplication.

With that in mind, let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, if f(x),g(x)∈G, then (f+g)(0)=f(0)+g(0)≥0. So, G is closed under addition.

  • Statement II is false. For example, if f(x)=1 for all x∈[0,1] and k=−1∈R, then (kf)(0)=((−1)f)(0)=−f(0)=−1<0. So, G is not closed under scalar multiplication.

  • Statement III is false, while statement IV is true. G is not a subspace because it's not closed under scalar multiplication.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and IV only."
