EXPLANATION

Suppose the functions y1(x) and y2(x) are defined on some interval I. The Wronskian W(y1,y2) of these functions is defined as W(y1,y2)=|y1y2y1′y2′|=y1y2′−y2y1′.

Recall the following:

  • If W≠0 for at least one x∈I, then y1 and y2 are linearly independent.

  • If W=0 and it’s known that y1 and y2 are solutions to the same second-order linear differential equation, then y1 and y2 are linearly dependent.

In our case, the Wronskian is given by

W(y1,y2)=|x+1x2(x+1)′(x2)′|=|x+1x212x|=(x+1)⋅(2x)−x2⋅1=2x(x+1)−x2=2x2+2x−x2=x2+2x=x(x+2)

which is nonzero for at least one x∈R.

Therefore, the set {y1(x),y2(x)} is linearly independent on R.
