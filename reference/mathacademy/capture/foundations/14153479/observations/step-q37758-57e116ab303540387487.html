Correct
EXPLANATION

The region D can be partitioned into two type I regions D1 and D2, as shown below.

As a result, we get

D1={(x,y):0≤x≤2,x2≤y≤7x}

and

D2={(x,y):2≤x≤4,x2≤y≤x+12}.

Therefore, using the additivity property of double integrals, we obtain

∬Df(x,y)dA=∬D1f(x,y)dA+∬D2f(x,y)dA=∫02∫x27xf(x,y)dydx+∫24∫x2x+12f(x,y)dydx.
