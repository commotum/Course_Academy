  2. y″+7y′+9y=0 represents an oscillator undergoing critical damping
  3. y″+7y′+14y=0 represents an oscillator undergoing light damping

EXPLANATION

Remember that, given the equation of a damped harmonic oscillator, the type of damping depends on the discriminant of the characteristic equation:

  • D>0 leads to heavy damping,

  • D=0 leads to critical damping, and

  • D<0 leads to light damping.

With that in mind, let's inspect each equation.

  • The differential equation y″+22y′+2y represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is λ2+22λ+2=0. The discriminant of this quadratic equation is D=(22)2−4(1)(2)=0. Thus, the given equation represents critical damping, and so statement I is false.

  • The differential equation y″+7y′+9y=0 represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is λ2+7λ+9=0. The discriminant of this quadratic equation is D=72−4(1)(9)=13>0. Thus, the given equation represents heavy damping, and so statement II is false.

  • The differential equation y″+7y′+14y represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is λ2+7λ+14=0. The discriminant of this quadratic equation is D=72−4(1)(14)=−7<0. Thus, the given equation represents light damping, and so statement III is true.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "III only".
