The given equation represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. We are looking for solutions of the form y=eλt.

The auxiliary equation for λ is

λ2+2λ+10=0.

The auxiliary equation can be solved by completing the square: λ2+2λ+10=0(λ2+2λ+1)−1+10=0(λ+1)2+9=0(λ+1)2=−9λ+1=±3iλ=−1±3i

So, the general solution is yc=e−t(Acos⁡3t+Bsin⁡3t).

From the solution, the value of the angular frequency is Ω=3s−1.

The period of the damped oscillation T is related to the angular frequency as follows:

T=2πΩ

So the period is

T=2π3s.
