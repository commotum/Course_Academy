EXPLANATION

If F=Pi+Qj is a vector field and C is a piecewise-smooth, simple, closed curve, then the flux of F across C is given by ∮CF⋅nds=∮CPdy−Qdx, where n denotes the outward-pointing unit normal vector to C.

Along the curve C, we have x=5cos⁡t,y=6sin⁡t.

Therefore,

P(x,y)=x=5cos⁡t,Q(x,y)=y=6sin⁡t.

Computing the derivatives of x(t) and y(t) along the curve, we get

dxdt=ddt(5cos⁡t)=−5sin⁡t,dydt=ddt(6sin⁡t)=6cos⁡t.

Finally, we evaluate the integral as follows:

∮CF⋅nds=∮CPdy−Qdx=∫02π(P⋅dydt−Q⋅dxdt)dt=∫02π(5cos⁡t)⋅(6cos⁡t)−(6sin⁡t)⋅(−5sin⁡t)dt=30∫02π(cos2⁡t+sin2⁡t)dt=30∫02π1dt=30(2π−0)=60π
