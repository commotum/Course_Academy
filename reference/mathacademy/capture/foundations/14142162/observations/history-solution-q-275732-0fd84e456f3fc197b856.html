For a continuous random variable X and a function r(x), the rule of the lazy statistician states that

E[r(x)]=∫−∞∞r(x)f(x) dx,

where f(x) is the probability density function of X.

In this problem, X is chosen uniformly from [0,1], so the probability density function is

f(x)=1,0≤x<1.

Let A denote the area of the triangle formed by the three points (0,0),(2,0), and (2,X). The triangle has a fixed base of length 2 along the x-axis, and a height of length X from (2,0) to (2,X). Using the standard formula for the area of a triangle, we have

A=12⋅base⋅height=12⋅2⋅X=X.

So, we define

r(x)=x.

We now apply the rule of the lazy statistician:

E[A]=∫−∞∞r(x)f(x)dx=∫01x⋅1dx=∫01xdx=[x22]01=12.
