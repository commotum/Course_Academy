To map the entire region Δ onto the xy-plane, we consider the transformation of each side. However, in this case, we consider only the side S1.

For the side S1, we have v=0 and 0≤u≤1. Substituting v=0 into our transformation equations, we obtain

(x,y)=T(u,v)=(v−u,v3+u)=(0−u,03+u)=(−u,u).

So, the image of S1 can be parametrized by

x=−u,y=u,0≤u≤1.

Writing this in the usual notation, we have

x(t)=−t,y(t)=t,0≤t≤1.

This is a line segment with endpoints (0,0) and (−1,1) in the xy-plane.
