To map the entire region Δ onto the xy-plane, we consider the transformation of each side. However, in this case, we consider only the side S1.

For the side S1, we have 0≤u≤1 and v=0. Substituting v=0 into our transformation equations, we obtain (x,y)=T(u,v)=(v+2u,u(v2−1))=(0+2u,u(0−1))=(2u,−u).

So, the image of S1 can be parametrized by x=2u,y=−u,0≤u≤1.

Writing this in the usual notation, we have x(t)=2t,y(t)=−t,0≤t≤1.
