To map the entire region Δ onto the xy-plane, we consider the transformation of each side. However, in this case, we consider only the side S2.

For the side S2, we have u=1 and 0≤v≤1. Substituting u=1 into our transformation equations, we obtain

(x,y)=T(u,v)=(v,u(1+v2))=(v,1(1+v2))=(v,1+v2).

So, the image of S2 can be parametrized by

x=v,y=1+v2,0≤v≤1.

Writing this in the usual notation, we have

x(t)=t,y(t)=1+t2,0≤t≤1.
