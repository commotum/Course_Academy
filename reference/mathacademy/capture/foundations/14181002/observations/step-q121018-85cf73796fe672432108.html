The position of the particle at t=6 is equal to its position at t=1 plus the total displacement over t∈[1,6]. Therefore, we can calculate it as follows:

x(6)=x(1)+∫16v(t)dt

We are told that x(1)=3, and we can compute the integral ∫16v(t)dt by finding the signed area under the graph from t=1 to t=6. Computing the signed area, we get

∫16v(t)dt=1.

Therefore,

x(6)=3+1=4.

So, the position of the particle at t=6 is x=4.
