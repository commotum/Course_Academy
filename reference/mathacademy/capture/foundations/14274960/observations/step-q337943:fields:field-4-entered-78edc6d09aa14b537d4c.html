EXPLANATION

Given a second-order linear inhomogeneous differential equation, the variation of parameters method requires that we seek a particular solution of the form yp(x)=u1(x)y1(x)+u2(x)y2(x) that satisfies the constraint yp′=u1y1′+u2y2′. Here, y1 and y2 are linearly independent solutions of the corresponding homogeneous equation, and u1 and u2 are functions to be determined.

In this case, we're told that the fundamental solutions are

y1=ex,y2=e−4x.

Substituting the fundamental solutions into our expression for yp, we get

yp=u1ex+u2e−4x.
