A tank initially holds 450L of a brine (salt-water) solution containing 9kg of salt. Fresh water is poured into the tank at a rate of 21L/min, while the completely mixed brine leaves the tank at a rate of 24L/min. Which differential equation best describes the amount of salt Q, in kilograms, in the tank t minutes after the fresh water begins to be added?

a
dQdt−7150−tQ=0
b
dQdt+8150−tQ=0
c
dQdt+7150−tQ=0
d
dQdt−8150−tQ=0
e
dQdt+1150−tQ=0
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Since Q is the amount of salt in the tank at time t, the derivative dQdt describes how this amount changes with respect to time t, in minutes. This is equal to the rate at which salt enters the tank minus the rate at which salt leaves the tank.

dQdt=Qin′−Qout′

Note that the units of dQdt,Qin′ and Qout′ are kg/min. Now, let's compute the required rates of change in steps.

Step 1: Calculating the rate at which salt enters the tank.

First, note that fresh water (i.e., a brine solution containing 0kg/L of salt) enters the tank at a rate of 21L/min. So, the rate at which the salt enters the tank is

Qin′=(0kgL)⋅(21Lmin)=(0kgL)⋅(21Lmin)=0kgmin.

Step 2: Calculating the rate at which salt exits the tank.

Now, to find the rate at which salt leaves the tank, we must first determine the volume of brine in the tank at time t.

  • The tank initially contains 450L of brine.

  • Fresh water enters the tank at a rate of 21L/min. So, after t minutes, 21tL have entered the tank.

  • The completely mixed brine leaves the tank at a rate of 24L/min. So, after t minutes, 24tL have left the tank.

As a result, the volume of brine in the tank after t minutes is 450+21t−24t=(450−3t)L.

Since Q is the mass of salt in the tank, the concentration of the brine at time t is Q450−3tkgL.

Therefore, as the completely mixed brine leaves at a rate of 24L/min, salt leaves at a rate of Qout′=(24Lmin)⋅(Q450−3tkgL)=(24Lmin)⋅(Q450−3tkgL)=24Q450−3tkgmin=8Q150−tkgmin.

Step 3: Calculate the overall rate of change.

Finally, the rate of change equals the rate of entry minus the rate of exit:

dQdt=Qin′−Qout′=0−8Q150−t which finally gives

dQdt+8150−tQ=0.
