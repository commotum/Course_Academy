A tank initially holds 260L of a dye solution containing 18kg of dye. Pure water is poured into the tank at a rate of 13L/min, while the completely mixed solution leaves the tank at a rate of 13L/min. Find an equation for the amount of dye Q(t), in kilograms, in the tank t minutes after the pure water is first added.
Q(t)=
18e−t/20

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since Q is the amount of dye in the tank at time t, the derivative dQdt describes how this amount changes with respect to time t, in minutes. This is equal to the rate at which dye enters the tank minus the rate at which dye leaves the tank.

dQdt=Qin′−Qout′

Note that the units of dQdt,Qin′ and Qout′ are kg/min. Now, let's compute the required rates of change in steps.

Step 1: Calculating the rate at which dye enters the tank.

First, note that pure water (i.e., a solution containing 0kg/L of dye) enters the tank at a rate of 13L/min. So, the rate at which the dye enters the tank is

Qin′=(0kgL)⋅(13Lmin)=(0kgL)⋅(13Lmin)=0kgmin.

Step 2: Calculating the rate at which dye exits the tank.

Note that the rate of flow in equals the rate of flow out, so the volume of solution in the tank remains 260L. Then, since Q is the amount of dye in the tank, the concentration of the solution at time t is Q260kgL.

Therefore, as the completely mixed solution leaves at a rate of 13L/min, dye leaves at a rate of Qout′=(13Lmin)⋅(Q260kgL)=(13Lmin)⋅(Q260kgL)=13Q260kgmin=Q20kgmin.

Step 3: Calculate the overall rate of change.

The rate of change equals the rate of entry minus the rate of exit. Therefore,

dQdt=Qin′−Qout′dQdt=0−Q20dQdt=−120Q.

Step 4: Integrate the equation.

Separating variables and integrating both sides with respect to t, we get

1Q⋅dQdt=−120∫1Q⋅dQdtdt=∫(−120)dt∫1Q⋅dQ=−120∫dtln⁡|Q|=−t20+C|Q|=e−t/20+CQ(t)=Ae−t/20,

where A=±eC is a constant of integration.

We're told that the tank initially contains 18kg of dye. So, we can apply the initial condition Q(0)=18, and solve for A:

Q(0)=18Ae−0/20=18A=18

Finally, we obtain the following expression for Q(t):

Q(t)=18e−t/20
