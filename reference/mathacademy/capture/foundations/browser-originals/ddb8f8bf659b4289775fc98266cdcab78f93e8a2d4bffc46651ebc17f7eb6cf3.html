Question 1

The inverse of the matrix $N$ is found using row operations, as shown below. What are the row operations corresponding to the blank spaces?

\begin{align*} [N\,|\,I] & = \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} -2 & 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right] & R_1 &\leftrightarrow R_2\\[5pt] &\sim \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ -2 & 2 & 1 & 0 \\ \end{array} \right] & & \text{___________} \\[5pt] &\sim \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 & 2 \end{array} \right] & & \text{___________} \\[5pt] &\sim \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & \frac12 & 1 \end{array} \right] \end{align*}

a
 $R_2:=R_2- 2R_1, \quad R_2 := 2R_2$
b
 $R_2:=R_2- R_1, \quad R_2 := \dfrac14R_2$
c
 $R_2:=R_2+ \dfrac12R_1, \quad R_2 := 2R_2$
d
 $R_2:=R_2+ R_1, \quad R_2 := R_2-\dfrac12R_1$
e
 $R_2:=R_2+ 2R_1, \quad R_2 := \dfrac12R_2$
