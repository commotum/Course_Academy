a

Question 4

Let $V=\mathbb{R}_2[t],$ the vector space of real polynomials of degree at most $2,$ and let the inner product on $V$ be defined as \[ \langle p(t),q(t) \rangle =p(-1)q(-1)+p(0)q(0)+p(1)q(1). \]

Now consider the following elements of $V\mathbin{:}$

\[ p_1(t)=t^2-t,\quad p_2(t)=t^2+t, \quad q(t)=4t^2-t-1 \]

Find the orthogonal projection of $q(t)$ onto $S=\text{Span}\{p_1(t), p_2(t)\}$ given that $p_1(t) \perp p_2(t)$ with respect to the inner product, and that the orthogonal projection of $q(t)$ onto $\textrm{Span}\{p_1(t)\}$ equals $2t^2-2t.$

a
 $t^2 -3t$
b
 $-t^2+t$
c
 $t^2-2t$
d
 $t^2+2t$
e
 $3t^2 -t$
