Question 2

Assume that $x_1,x_2,$ and $x_3$ are real numbers. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The vectors $\mathbf{a}_1,\mathbf{a}_2,$ and $\mathbf{a}_3$ are linearly dependent.
  2. The equation $x_1\mathbf{a}_1+x_2\mathbf{a}_2+x_3\mathbf{a}_3=\mathbf{0}$ has an infinite number of nonzero solutions.
  3. The vectors $\mathbf{a}_1,\mathbf{a}_2,$ and $\mathbf{a}_3$ are linearly independent.
a
 None of the statements are true
b

c

d
 II and III only
e
