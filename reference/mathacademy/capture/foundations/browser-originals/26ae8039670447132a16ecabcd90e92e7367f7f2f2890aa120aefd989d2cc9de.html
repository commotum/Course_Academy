Question 2

a
 $\dfrac{\textrm d }{\textrm d x}\left(v^2\right) = 3e^x+1$
b
 $\dfrac{\textrm d v}{\textrm d x} = 3e^x+1$
c
 $\dfrac{\textrm d }{\textrm d x}\left(2v^2\right) = 3e^x+1$
d
 $\dfrac12 \dfrac{\textrm d v}{\textrm d x} = 3e^x+1$
e
