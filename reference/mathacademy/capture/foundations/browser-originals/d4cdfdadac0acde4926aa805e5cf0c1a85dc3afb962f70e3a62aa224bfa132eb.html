Question 1

$F$ is not a vector space (with respect to function addition and scalar multiplication of a function by a real number) because $\textrm{_________}\,.$

a
 addition of $f,g \in F$ is not commutative
b
 addition and scalar multiplication of functions in $F$ do not satisfy the distributive laws
c
 $1 \cdot f \neq f$ for some $f \in F$
d

e
 $F$ does not contain the zero element

  2. $\displaystyle \iint\limits_{\large R} e^{x-y} \: \text{d}A = \iint\limits_{\large D} f(x,y) \: \text{d}A,\quad$ where $R=[-2,2] \times [-3,3]$
  3. $\displaystyle \iint\limits_{\large D} e^{x-y} \: \text{d}A = \iint\limits_{\large R} f(x,y) \: \text{d}A,\quad$ where $R=[-3,3] \times [-2,2]$

b

c

d
 I only
e
