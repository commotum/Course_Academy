f(θ,ϕ)=⟨5cos⁡θsin⁡ϕ,5sin⁡θsin⁡ϕ,_____⟩

The vector-valued function f(θ,ϕ) above parametrizes the sphere defined in Cartesian coordinates as x2+y2+z2=5. What is the third component of f?

a
5sin⁡θcos⁡ϕ
b
5cos⁡θcos⁡ϕ
c
5cos⁡ϕ
d
5sin⁡ϕ
e
5cos2⁡ϕ
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The given equation generates a sphere of radius 5 centered at the origin. In spherical polar coordinates, this sphere has the equation ρ=5.

If (ρ,θ,ϕ) are the spherical polar coordinates of a point, then its Cartesian coordinates (x,y,z) can be found using the following formulas: x=ρcos⁡θsin⁡ϕ,y=ρsin⁡θsin⁡ϕ,z=ρcos⁡ϕ.

Now, for any point (x,y,z) on our surface, we obtain the following:

f(θ,ϕ)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨ρcos⁡θsin⁡ϕ,ρsin⁡θsin⁡ϕ,ρcos⁡ϕ⟩=⟨5⋅cos⁡θsin⁡ϕ,5⋅sin⁡θsin⁡ϕ,5⋅cos⁡ϕ⟩=⟨5cos⁡θsin⁡ϕ,5sin⁡θsin⁡ϕ,5cos⁡ϕ⟩

where θ∈[0,2π) and ϕ∈[−π,π].

Therefore, the third component of f is 5cos⁡ϕ.
