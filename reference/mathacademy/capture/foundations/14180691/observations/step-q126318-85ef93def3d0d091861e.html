Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The modes of a random variable X with probability density function f(x) are the values of x at the global maxima of f(x).

From the graph, we can tell that the global maxima of f(x) are at x=−1,3,9.

Therefore, the modes of X are −1,3 and 9.
