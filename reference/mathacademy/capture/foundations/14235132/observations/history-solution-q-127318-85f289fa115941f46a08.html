Let C be a positively oriented, piecewise-smooth, simple closed curve in the plane, and let D be the region bounded by C. If F=Pi+Qj, and P and Q have continuous first derivatives on an open region containing D, then the flux form of Green's theorem states that

∮CF⋅nds=∬DdivFdA, which is equivalent to ∮CPdy−Qdx=∬D(∂P∂x+∂Q∂y)dA.

We have that P(x,y)=x+y and Q(x,y)=y+x. Therefore,

∂P∂x=1,∂Q∂y=1.

D={(x,y):−π2≤x≤π2,0≤y≤cos⁡x}

Therefore, applying Green's theorem in flux form, we get
