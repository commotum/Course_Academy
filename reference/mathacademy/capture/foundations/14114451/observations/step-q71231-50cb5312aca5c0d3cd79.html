The curve C is the intersection of a surface S, defined by z=f(x,y), with the plane y=π4. Given that the point P(1,π4,0) lies on C, and that fx=2sin⁡(2y)cos⁡y, which of the following gives a tangent vector to C at P?

a
⟨1,0,2⟩
b
⟨1,0,1⟩
c
⟨−2,0,1⟩

⟨1,0,2⟩

⟨−1,0,1⟩

The curve C is the intersection of the surface with the plane y=π4. Hence, the slope of the tangent to the curve C at the point P will be given by the partial derivative fx(1,π4).

To get the slope, we evaluate the partial derivative fx(x,y) at (1,π4):

fx(1,π4)=2sin⁡(2⋅π4)cos⁡π4=2⋅1⋅12=1

Now, we use the point-slope form of a straight line:

z−f(1,π4)=fx(1,π4)(x−1)z−0=1(x−1)z=x−1
