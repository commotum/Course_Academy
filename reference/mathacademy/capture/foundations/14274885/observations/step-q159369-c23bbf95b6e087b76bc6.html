Consider two random variables X and Y with expectations E[X]=3, E[Y]=1, mixed moment E[XY]=4, and second raw moments E[X2]=10 and E[Y2]=4. Find the covariance matrix for X and Y.

Correct
EXPLANATION

For two random variables X and Y, the covariance matrix Σ is given by

Σ=[Var[X]Cov[X,Y]Cov[Y,X]Var[Y]].

Recall that the variance of a random variable X is determined by the formula

Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2,

and the covariance of two random variables X and Y is given by the formula

Cov[X,Y]=E[XY]−E[X]⋅E[Y].

First, let's find Var[X]:

Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2=10−32=1.

Similarly,

Var[Y]=E[Y2]−E[Y]2=4−12=3.

Now lets find Cov[X,Y]:

Cov[X,Y]=E[XY]−E[X]⋅E[Y]=4−3⋅1=1.

Therefore, the covariance matrix is given by

Σ=[Var[X]Cov[X,Y]Cov[Y,X]Var[Y]]=[1113].
