Consider two random variables X and Y with expectations E[X]=6, E[Y]=7, mixed moment E[XY]=40, and variances Var[X]=2 and Var[Y]=5. Find the covariance matrix for X and Y.

a
[2−2−25]

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

For two random variables X and Y, the covariance matrix Σ is given by

Σ=[Var[X]Cov[X,Y]Cov[Y,X]Var[Y]].

Recall that the covariance of two random variables X and Y is given by the formula

Cov[X,Y]=E[XY]−E[X]⋅E[Y].

Using the above formula, we have

Cov[X,Y]=E[XY]−E[X]⋅E[Y]=40−6⋅7=−2.

Therefore, the covariance matrix is given by

Σ=[Var[X]Cov[X,Y]Cov[Y,X]Var[Y]]=[2−2−25].
