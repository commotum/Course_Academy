Consider the square region Δ in the uv-plane, given by Δ={(u,v):2≤u≤4,10π9≤v≤4π3},

and the transformation T, given by T:(u,v)→(ucos⁡v,usin⁡v).

The image of the top side of Δ under the action of T is a line segment. Which of the following statements are true regarding the image?

  1. The image makes the angle of 10π9 with the positive x-axis.
  2. The image makes the angle of 4π3 with the positive x-axis.
  3. The endpoints of the image lie on the circles of radii 2 and 2 centered at O.
a
I only
b
II only
c
I and III only
d
III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To map the entire region Δ onto the xy-plane, we consider the transformation of each side. However, in this case, we consider only the top side.

For the top side, we have v=4π3 and 2≤u≤4.

Substituting v=4π3 into our transformation equations, we obtain (x,y)=T(u,v)=(ucos⁡v,usin⁡v)=(ucos⁡(4π3),usin⁡(4π3)),2≤u≤4.

This is a parametric equation of the line segment shown below.

Note that the line that this segment lies on passes through the origin.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is false, while statement II is true. The slope of the line is k=sin⁡(4π3)cos⁡(4π3)=tan⁡(4π3). As a result, our line makes an angle of 4π3 radians with the positive x-axis.

  • Statement III is false.

    • When u=2, we obtain the point (2cos⁡(4π3),2sin⁡(4π3)), whose distance to the origin is 2.

    • When u=4, we obtain the point (4cos⁡(4π3),4sin⁡(4π3)), whose distance to the origin is 4.

    So, the endpoints of the line segment lie on the circles of radii 2 and 4.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
