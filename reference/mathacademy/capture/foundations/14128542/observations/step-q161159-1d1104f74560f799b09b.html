EXPLANATION

For our transformation T, we have

x=ucos⁡v,y=usin⁡v.

Notice that if we change u to r and v to θ, we obtain

{x=rcos⁡θy=rsin⁡θ

which represents the usual formulas for changing from polar to Cartesian coordinates.

Therefore, the pre-image Δ of D under the action of T is the region that can be written as

Δ={(u,v):12≤u≤3,5π8≤v≤7π8}.

This is the rectangular region enclosed between

  • two vertical lines with Cartesian equations u=12 and u=3, and

  • two horizontal lines with Cartesian equations v=5π8 and v=7π8,
