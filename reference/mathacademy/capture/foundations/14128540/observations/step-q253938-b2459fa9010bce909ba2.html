Given that the random variable X∼Exp(2), calculate P(X≤9|X>6).

a
 3e−2
b
 1−e−6

 1−2e−3

 1−3e−2

Correct
EXPLANATION

Given a random variable X∼Exp(λ), and two positive real numbers s,t, the memoryless property of the exponential distribution states that P(X>s+t|X>s)=P(X>t)=e−λt.

We can compute the complementary conditional probability as follows: P(X≤s+t|X>s)=1−P(X>s+t|X>s)=1−e−λt

In our case, we have λ=2, s=6, and s+t=9. From this, we can find t as follows: t=(t+s)−s=9−6=3

Substituting these values into the above formula, we get P(X≤9|X>6)=1−e−λt=1−e−(2)⋅(3)=1−e−6
