Let T=[1428] be the standard matrix of the linear transformation T. Then T maps R2 onto

a
 (0,0)
b
 y=12x
c
 y=2x
d
 R2
e
 y=4x
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that T has proportional rows, and yet T is not the zero matrix.

Therefore, rank(T)=1 and T maps R2 onto a line that passes through the origin.

Moreover, the line must pass through the origin (0,0) and the points (1,2) and (4,8), which are defined by the columns of the matrix T.

Computing the slope m of this line, we get

m=8−24−1=2.

Therefore, T maps R2 onto the line y=2x.
