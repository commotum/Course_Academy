Which of the following vector spaces has a dimension of 12?

  1. R12
  2. M3×4(R), the vector space of all 3×4 matrices over R
  3. R11[t], the vector space of all polynomials in the variable t of degree at most 11
a
II and III only
b
I, II, and III
c
I only
d
I and II only
e
I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of the vector spaces in turn.

  1. Recall that dim(Rn)=n. Therefore, dim(R12)=12.✓
  2. Recall that dim(Mn×m(R))=n⋅m. Therefore, dim(M3×4(R))=3⋅4=12.✓
  3. Recall that dim(Rn[t])=n+1. Therefore, dim(R11[t])=11+1=12.✓
Hence, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
