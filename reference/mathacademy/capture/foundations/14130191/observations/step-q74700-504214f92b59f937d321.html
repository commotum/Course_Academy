  1. The zero-polynomial belongs to U.
  2. For any p(x),q(x)∈U, we have p(x)+q(x)=q(x)+p(x).
  3. For any p(x),q(x),r(x)∈U, we have [p(x)−q(x)]−r(x)=p(x)−[q(x)−r(x)].

EXPLANATION

Let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, the zero-polynomial can be represented as 0=0⋅x3. Hence, 0∈U.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, for any polynomials p(x),q(x)∈U, we obtain that p(x)+q(x)=q(x)+p(x) since polynomial addition is commutative.

  • Statement III is false. For example, consider polynomials p(x)=x3, q(x)=2x3, and r(x)=3x3 from U. Then: [p(x)−q(x)]−r(x)=[x3−2x3]−3x3=−x3−3x3=−4x3p(x)−[q(x)−r(x)]=x3−[2x3−3x3]=x3−[−x3]=2x3 Hence, [p(x)−q(x)]−r(x)≠p(x)−[q(x)−r(x)].

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
