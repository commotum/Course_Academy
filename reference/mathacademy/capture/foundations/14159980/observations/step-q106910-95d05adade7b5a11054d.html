Which of the following vector spaces are finite-dimensional?

  1. C[0,1], the vector space of all continuous functions f:[0,1]→R
  2. M200×300(R), the vector space of all 200×300 matrices over R
  3. R20[t], the vector space of all polynomials in the variable t of degree at most 20
a
II and III only
b
I and III only
c
I only
d
I and II only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's examine our vector spaces.

  • C[0,1] is not finite-dimensional. To understand why, we first note that the following set of (polynomial) functions is linearly independent: {1,t,t2,…,tn},n∈N. Indeed, we have that ∑i=0nxiti=0 for all t∈[0,1] if and only if xi=0 for all i=0,1,2,…n. Therefore, for any n∈N, there exists a linearly independent subset of C[0,1] of size n, which imples that C[0,1] cannot have finite dimension.

  • M200×300(R) and R20[t] are finite-dimensional. Indeed, dim(M200×300(R))=200⋅300=60000, and dim(R20[t])=20+1=21 are both finite numbers.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
