A particle of mass 5kg is attached to a massless spring. The spring constant associated with the spring is k=30kg/s2. The position of the particle is y(t) (measured in meters), and t>0 is the time in seconds.

If the particle moves with simple harmonic motion, then which of the following statements are true?

  1. The governing differential equation is y″+6y=0
  2. The frequency f of the oscillation is f=32πs−1
  3. The general solution can be expressed as y=Rsin⁡(6t+ϕ), where R is the amplitude and ϕ is the phase.
a
I only
b
II only
c
I and II only
d
II and III only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement.

  • The equation of motion for a particle of mass m moving with simple harmonic motion (SHM) under the action of a spring with spring constant k is given by my″=−ky. Rearranging the above equation gives y″+kmy=0. Comparing this to the SHM equation y″+ω2y=0, we see that ω2=km. Substituting our values for k and m gives ω2=305=6⟹ω=6 So the governing differential equation is y″+6y=0. Hence, statement I is true.

  • The frequency f (measured in s−1) is given by f=1T=ω2π, where T is the period. So in our case, we have f=62π. So the frequency f=62πs−1. Hence, statement II is false.

  • The general solution for y(t) can take one of three forms.

    1. Acos⁡ωt+Bsin⁡ωt

    2. Rcos⁡(ωt+ϕ1)

    3. Rsin⁡(ωt+ϕ2)

    Writing ω=6 and ϕ1=ϕ in form 3 gives y(t)=Rsin⁡(6t+ϕ). Hence, statement III is false.

So, the correct answer is "I only".
