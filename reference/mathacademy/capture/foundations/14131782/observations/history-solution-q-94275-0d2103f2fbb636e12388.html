The SHM equation is

y″+ω2y=0.

The general solution to the SHM equation can be written in three different forms:

  1. y=Rcos⁡(ωt+ϕ1)

  2. y=Rsin⁡(ωt+ϕ2)

  3. y=Acos⁡ωt+Bsin⁡ωt

In our case

ω2=4⟹ω=2.

Thus, we have:

  1. y=Rcos⁡(2t+ϕ1)

  2. y=Rsin⁡(2t+ϕ2)

  3. y=Acos⁡2t+Bsin⁡2t

Therefore, only function III could be used to represent y(t).
