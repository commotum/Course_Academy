The position y(t) of a particle oscillating with simple harmonic motion is governed by the equation

y″+64y=0,

where t>0 is the time, in seconds. What is the period of the oscillation?

a
 π2s
b
π16s
c
π4s
d
πs
e
 π8s
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The simple harmonic motion (SHM) equation is

y″+ω2y=0.

In our case

ω2=64⟹ω=8.

The period T is given by:

T=2πω

Therefore, in our case, the period (in seconds) is

T=2π8=π4s.
