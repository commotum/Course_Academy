EXPLANATION

The 100pth percentile of a random variable Y is the number yp such that

P(Y≤yp)=p.

In our case, we want to find the 60th percentile, which corresponds to the decimal p=0.6. We have

P(Y≤y0.6)=∫1y0.61y2dy=−1y|1y0.6=1−1y0.6.

Therefore, the 60th percentile is given by

1−1y0.6=0.61y0.6=0.4y0.6=10.4y0.6=52.
