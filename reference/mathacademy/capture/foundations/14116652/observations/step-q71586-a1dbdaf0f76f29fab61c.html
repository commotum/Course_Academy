Which of the following describes the level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z)=(x+y+z)2?

a
A family of surfaces given by z=(x+y+c)2
b
A family of hyperboloids of one sheet: c2x2+c2y2−c2z2=1
c
A family of spheres: x2+y2+z2=c2
d
A family of planes: x+y+z±c=0
e
A family of planes: x+y−z2−c=0
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z) are given by the following equation:

f(x,y,z)=c(x+y+z)2=cx+y+z=±cx+y+z±c=0

Notice that f(x,y,z) can't be negative. Therefore, we get a family of planes: x+y+z±c=0,c∈[0,∞).
