Which of the following describes the level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z)=−x2−3y2+z+3?

a
A family of elliptic paraboloids: z=−x2−3y2+C
b
A family of planes: z=x+3y+C
c
A family of elliptic paraboloids: z=x2+3y2+C
d
A family of hyperbolic paraboloids: z=x2−3y2+C
e
A family of elliptic cones: z2=x2+3y2+C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z) are given by the following equation: f(x,y,z)=c−x2−3y2+z+3=cz=x2+3y2+c−3

If we let c−3=C, we get a family of elliptic paraboloids: z=x2+3y2+C,C∈(−∞,∞)
