The size of a bush growing in a schoolyard is being monitored. The area Am2 of the bush at any given moment grows at a rate that's directly proportional to the area at that moment. Additionally, 2m2 of the bush is cut away every month. What is the differential equation that describes the rate of change in the area of the schoolyard bush at time t, where t is the time in months since it started growing?

a
dAdt=−kA+2,k>0
b
dAdt=kA−2,k>0
c
dAdt=kA,k>0
d
dAdt=kA+2,k>0
e
dAdt=kA−2,k>0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The rate of change in the area of the bush per month increases in proportion to A and decreases by 2. The differential equation that describes this is

dAdt=kA−2,

where k is the (positive) constant of proportionality.
