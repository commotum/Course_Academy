A cyclist has a small puncture in one of her tires. The volume of air V, in centimeters cubed, in the tire decreases at a rate that's proportional to the volume at that moment. Every day, the cyclist pumps 10cm3 back into the tire. What is the differential equation describing the rate of change of the volume of air in the tire at time t, where t is the time in days?

a
dVdt=−V−10,k>0
b
dVdt=kV+10,k>0
c
dVdt=−kV−10,k>0
d
dVdt=V−10k,k>0
e
dVdt=−kV+10,k>0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The rate of change in the volume of air per day decreases in proportion to V and increases by 12. The differential equation that describes this is

dVdt=−kV+10,

where k is the (positive) constant of proportionality.
