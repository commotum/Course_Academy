EXPLANATION

Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

The side length of a square decreases at a rate proportional to its area.

We note the following:

  • The question describes the rate at which the side length s of a square varies. Therefore, this is a statement about the quantity dsdt.

  • We're told that dsdt decreases at a rate proportional to the area of the square. In other words, dsdt=−kA where k>0.

We wish to use this information to find an expression for dAdt.

The side length of a square is given by s=A.

Differentiating this equation with respect to t, we get dsdt=ddt(A)dsdt=12AdAdt.

Therefore,

dsdt=−kA12AdAdt=−kAdAdt=−2kA3/2.

We now introduce a new constant: K=2k>0

Therefore, our equation becomes dAdt=−KA3/2.
