A wildlife reserve is monitoring the population of a newly introduced species of deer. The habitat can sustain a maximum population of 850 deer, but the herd is still below this limit. Since biologists began tracking the growth, the population reached 490 deer on month 4 and 600 deer on month 12. On what month will the population reach 800 deer? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

Hint: You may assume that the population of deer, P(t), increases at a rate that obeys an inhibited growth model, where t is the time, in months.

EXPLANATION

The rate at which the population P(t) changes is proportional to the difference between the maximum population and its current population. Therefore, we have the inhibited growth equation dPdt=k(a−P), where a is the maximum production of the farm, and k>0 is a constant of proportionality.

In our case, a=850 deer, and we have dPdt=k(850−P).

First, we integrate our differential equation by separating the variables:

dP850−P=kdt∫dP850−P=∫kdt−ln⁡|850−P|=kt+C

Since this is the solution to an inhibited growth equation, we have 850−P>0 for all t≥0. Next, we solve for P(t), as follows:

850−P=e−kt−C850−P=e−Ce−kt850−P=Ke−ktP=850−Ke−kt

Note that K=e−C is a constant of integration.

We're told that, on month 4, the population reached 490 deer. So, applying the condition P(4)=490, we have P(4)=490850−Ke−4k=490Ke−4k=360.

We're also told that, on month 12, the population was 600 deer. So, applying the condition P(12)=600, we get P(12)=600850−Ke−12k=600Ke−12k=250.

Then, by dividing the result of the first condition by the result of the second, we can solve for k:

Ke−4kKe−12k=360250e8k=36258k=ln⁡(3625)k=18ln⁡(3625)

Now, substituting back into the result of the first condition, we solve for K:

Ke−4⋅(1/8)ln⁡(36/25)=360K=360e(1/2)ln⁡(36/25)K=3603625K=360⋅65K=432

Therefore, we have the following expression for P(t):

P(t)=850−432e−(t/8)ln⁡(36/25)

Finally, let m be the month when the population reaches 800 deer. Then, we have

P(m)=800850−432e−km=80050=432e−kme−km=50432−km=ln⁡(25216)m=−1kln⁡(25216).

Substituting in our value for k, we conclude that the population will be 500 deer on month m=−ln⁡(25216)18ln⁡(3625)=47.309…≈47,

rounded to the nearest month.
