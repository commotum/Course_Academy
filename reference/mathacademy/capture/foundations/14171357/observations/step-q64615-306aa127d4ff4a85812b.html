Which of the following statements are true?

  1. y″+4y′+8y=0 represents an oscillator undergoing light damping.
  2. y″+6y′+9y=0 represents an oscillator undergoing heavy damping.
  3. y″+3y′+2y=0 represents an oscillator undergoing critical damping.

b
III only
c
II only
d
II and III only

Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that, given the equation of a damped harmonic oscillator, the type of damping depends on the discriminant of the characteristic equation:

  • D>0 leads to heavy damping,

  • D=0 leads to critical damping, and

  • D<0 leads to light damping.

With that in mind, let's inspect each equation.

  • The differential equation y″+4y′+8y=0 represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is λ2+4λ+8=0 The discriminant of this quadratic equation is: D=42−4(1)(8)=−16<0 Thus, the given equation represents light damping, and so statement I is correct.

  • The differential equation y″+6y′+9y=0 represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is: λ2+6λ+9=0 The discriminant of this quadratic equation is D=62−4(1)(9)=0 Thus, the given equation represents critical damping, and so statement II is false.

  • The differential equation y″+3y′+2y=0 represents the equation of damped harmonic motion. The auxiliary equation for λ is: λ2+3λ+2=0 The discriminant of this quadratic equation is D=32−4(1)(2)=1>0 Thus, the given equation represents heavy damping, and so statement III is false.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "I only".
