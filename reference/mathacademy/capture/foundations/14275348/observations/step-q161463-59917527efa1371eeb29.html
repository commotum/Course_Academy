For a surface S given by z=g(x,y) for (x,y)∈D, the flux of the vector field F(x,y,z)=⟨P,Q,R⟩ over S is given by ∬SF⋅dS=∬D(−P∂gA−Q∂gA+R)dA. From left to right, what are the missing parts of the formula?

a
∂x and ∂z
b
∂y and ∂x
c
∂y and ∂z
d
∂x and ∂y
e
∂z and ∂x

Correct
EXPLANATION

For a surface S with parametrization r(u,v) for (u,v)∈D, the flux of a vector field F over S is given by

∬SF⋅dS=∬SF⋅ndS=∬DF(r(u,v))⋅(ru′×rv′)dA. Since our surface S is of the form z=g(x,y), it can be parameterized as

r(x,y)=⟨x,y,g(x,y)⟩.

We now compute the tangent vectors to the grid curves:

rx′=⟨1,0,∂g∂x⟩ry′=⟨0,1,∂g∂y⟩

Now, the fundamental vector product is

rx′×ry′=|ijk10∂g∂x01∂g∂y|=⟨−∂g∂x,−∂g∂y,1⟩.

Therefore, the surface integral can be written as

∬SF⋅dS=∬DF(r(x,y))⋅(rx′×ry′)dA=∬D⟨P,Q,R⟩⋅⟨−∂g∂x,−∂g∂y,1⟩dA=∬D(−P∂g∂x−Q∂g∂y+R)dA.
