The mode of a random variable X with probability density function f(x) is the value of x at the global maximum of f(x).

To find the global maximum of f(x), we find the critical points of f(x) and then apply the candidates test.

First, we find the critical points.

  • The piece of f(x) defined on x≥0 is f(x)=1(1+x)2. Taking the derivative, we get f′(x)=−2(1+x)3<0. So, there are no solutions of f′(x)=0.

  • The derivative doesn't exist at the endpoint x=0. So, this is the only critical point.

Since f′(x)<0 for x>0, then f(0) is the global maximum of f(x). Therefore, the mode of X is 0.
