Let X be a random variable with the probability density function shown above. What are the modes of X?

a
3,7
b
3,7,10

d
1,5,10
e
1,10
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The modes of a random variable X with probability density function f(x) are the values of x at the global maxima of f(x).

From the graph, we can tell that the global maxima of f(x) are at x=1,5,10.

Therefore, the modes of X are 1,5 and 10.
