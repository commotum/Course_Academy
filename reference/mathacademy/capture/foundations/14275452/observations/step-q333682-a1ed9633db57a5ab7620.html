A linear system of ODEs has the equilibrium at x∗=[1,−1]T. Two linearly independent eigenvectors of the system's matrix are v1 and v2. After shifting using u=x−x∗, the system becomes u′=Au. Which of the following is guaranteed to be an eigenvector of the matrix A?

a
−v2
b
x∗
c
v2−x∗
d
v1+x∗
e
0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

After shifting a linear system, the eigenvalues and the corresponding eigenvectors remain unchanged.

Thus, the matrix A in the shifted system has the same eigenvectors as the system's matrix in the original coordinates. These eigenvectors are v1 and v2, or any nonzero vectors that are parallel to them.

Among the given options, the only vector that is nonzero and is guaranteed to be parallel to either v1 or v2 is the vector −v2.
