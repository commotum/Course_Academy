The joint CDF of two continuous random variables X and Y, for (x,y)∈[0,1]×[0,1], is given by

F(x,y)=2xy−1.

Find FX(x), the marginal CDF of X, for 0≤x≤1.

a
2x−1
b
log2⁡(1+x2)
c
2x
d
1−2−x
e
log2⁡(1+x)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The marginal CDF of X is given by

FX(x)=F(x,∞)=limy→∞F(x,y).

Therefore, for 0≤x≤1, we have

FX(x)=F(x,∞)=F(x,1)=2x(1)−1=2x−1.
