Find the general solution to the equation d2ydx2+2dydx+3y=0.

a
y=e−x(Pcos⁡3x+Qsin⁡3x)
b
y=e−x(Pcos⁡2x+Qsin⁡2x)
c
y=e−2x(Pcos⁡2x+Qsin⁡2x)
d
y=e−2x(Pcos⁡3x+Qsin⁡3x)
e
y=ex(Pcos⁡2x+Qsin⁡2x)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let y=eλx. Differentiating y with respect to x gives

dydx=λeλx,d2ydx2=λ2eλx.

Substituting the above into our differential equation gives

λ2eλx+2λeλx+3eλx=0eλx(λ2+2λ+3)=0.

The auxiliary equation is therefore

λ2+2λ+3=0.

The auxiliary equation can be solved by completing the square:

λ2+2λ+3=0(λ2+2λ+1)−1+3=0(λ+1)2+2=0(λ+1)2=−2λ+1=±2iλ=−1±2i

Therefore, the general solution to the differential equation is

y=e−x(Pcos⁡2x+Qsin⁡2x).
