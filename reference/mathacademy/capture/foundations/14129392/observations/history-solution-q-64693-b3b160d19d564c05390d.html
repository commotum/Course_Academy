Assuming y=eλx and differentiating y with respect to x gives

y′=λeλx,y″=λ2eλx.

Substituting the above into our differential equation gives

λ2eλx+49eλx=0eλx(λ2+49)=0.

The auxiliary equation is therefore

λ2+49=0.

Solving the auxiliary equation, we get

λ2+49=0λ2=−49λ=±7i.

Therefore, λ=±7i are the (purely imaginary) roots of the auxiliary equation.
