In the picture, we are given a sphere of radius 3 centered at the origin.

We write the position of a point (vector) in spherical polar coordinates as (ρ,θ,ϕ), where

  • ρ≥0 is the distance from the point to the origin,

  • θ∈[0,2π) is the angle between the point and the positive x-axis, and

  • ϕ∈[0,π] is the angle the corresponding vector makes with the positive z-axis.

The distance from any point to the origin on the given surface equals 3. On the other hand, θ and ϕ vary arbitrarily in their respective domains.

Therefore, the spherical polar equation of the surface is ρ=3.
