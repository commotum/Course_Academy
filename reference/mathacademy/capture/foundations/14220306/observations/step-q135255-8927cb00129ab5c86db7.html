EXPLANATION

In the picture, we are given a half-cone with the z-axis as its axis.

We write the position of a point (vector) in spherical polar coordinates as (ρ,θ,ϕ), where

  • ρ≥0 is the distance from the point to the origin,

  • θ∈[0,2π) is the angle between the point and the positive x-axis, and

  • ϕ∈[0,π] is the angle the corresponding vector makes with the positive z-axis.

Every point on the given half-cone makes an angle of π12 radians with the positive z-axis. On the other hand, ρ and θ can vary arbitrarily in their respective domains.

Therefore, the spherical polar equation of the surface is ϕ=π12.
