We write the position of a point (vector) in spherical polar coordinates as (ρ,θ,ϕ), where

  • ρ≥0 is the distance from the point to the origin,

  • θ∈[0,2π) is the angle between the point and the positive x-axis, and

  • ϕ∈[0,π] is the angle the corresponding vector makes with the positive z-axis.

With that in mind, let's examine our equation:

θ=4π3

This equation specifies that for any point on the surface, the angle that the corresponding vector makes with the positive x-axis must equal 4π3. On the other hand, ρ and ϕ can vary arbitrarily in their respective domains.

Therefore, since ρ≥0, our surface is a half-plane.
