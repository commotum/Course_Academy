Some researchers want to test the belief that teenagers that play an instrument are more likely to be good at math. They collect data on a large sample of teenagers that play an instrument.

  • 20% of teenagers play the piano, and 60% of those teenagers are good at mathematics.
  • 15% of teenagers play the violin.
  • 65% of teenagers play other instruments, and 35% of those teenagers are good at mathematics.

If 12% of teenagers started to play music before 6 years old, 27% of teenagers who started to play before 6 years old are good at math, and 8% of students who are good at math started to play an instrument before 6 years old, what is the probability that a randomly chosen teenager that plays the violin is good at math? EXPLANATION

Let B be the event that a teenager started to play music before 6 years old, M be the event that a teenager is good at math, and T1,T2,T3 be the events that a teenager plays piano, violin, or another instrument, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(T1)=0.2P(M|T1)=0.6P(T2)=0.15P(T3)=0.65P(M|T3)=0.35 The disjoint union T1⊔T2⊔T3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(T1)+P(T2)+P(T3)=0.2+0.15+0.65=1.

  • Since 12% of teenagers started to play before 6 years old, we have P(B)=0.12.

  • Since 27% of teenagers who started to play before 6 years old are good at math, we have P(M|B)=0.27.

  • Since 8% of students who are good at math started to play an instrument before 6 years old, we have P(B|M)=0.08.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen teenager who plays the violin is good at math, which is represented as P(M|T2).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(B|M)=P(M|B)P(B)P(M)P(B|M)=P(M|B)P(B)P(M|T1)P(T1)+P(M|T2)P(T2)+P(M|T3)P(T3)0.08=(0.27)(0.12)(0.2)(0.6)+P(M|T2)(0.15)+(0.35)(0.65)0.08=0.03240.3475+0.15P(M|T2)

Solving this equation for P(M|T2), we have

0.08(0.3475+0.15P(M|T2))=0.03240.3475+0.15P(M|T2)=0.03240.080.3475+0.15P(M|T2)=812000.15P(M|T2)=81200−0.3475P(M|T2)=10.15(81200−0.3475)P(M|T2)≈0.383 rounded to three decimal places.
