An English teacher believes that students who prefer print handwriting tend to perform better in essay writing. To prove her belief, she puts some students into three groups according to their average writing grades and asks them if they usually write in print.

  • 20% of students have low grades.
  • 65% of students have average grades, and 55% of those students usually write in print.
  • 15% of students have high grades, and 52% of those students usually write in print.

If 42% of students are female, 80% of female students write in print, and 56% of students that write in print are female, what is the probability that a randomly chosen student with low grades writes in print? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

EXPLANATION

Let F be the event that a student is female, W be the event that a student writes in print, and L,A,H be the event that a student has low, average, or high writing grades, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(L)=0.2P(A)=0.65P(W|A)=0.55P(H)=0.15P(W|H)=0.52 The disjoint union L⊔A⊔H covers the entire sample space, and we have P(L)+P(A)+P(H)=0.2+0.65+0.15=1.

  • Since 42% of students in the entire school are female, we have P(F)=0.42.

  • Since 80% of females write in print, we have P(W|F)=0.8.

  • Since 56% of students that write in print are female, we have P(F|W)=0.56.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen student with low grades writes in print, which is represented as P(W|L).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(F|W)=P(W|F)P(F)P(W)P(F|W)=P(W|F)P(F)P(W|L)P(L)+P(W|A)P(A)+P(W|H)P(H)0.56=(0.8)(0.42)P(W|L)(0.2)+(0.55)(0.65)+(0.52)(0.15)0.56=0.3360.2P(W|L)+0.4355.

Solving this equation for P(W|L), we have

0.56(0.2P(W|L)+0.4355)=0.3360.2P(W|L)+0.4355=0.3360.560.2P(W|L)+0.4355=350.2P(W|L)=35−0.4355P(W|L)=10.2(35−0.4355)P(W|L)≈0.823

rounded to three decimal places.
