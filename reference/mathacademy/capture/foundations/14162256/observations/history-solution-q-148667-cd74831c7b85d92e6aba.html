Let R be the event that a copy has a printing defect, S be the event that a copy is sold, and D1,D2,D3 be the event that a copy is bought by distributor 1, 2, or 3, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(D1)=0.33P(S|D1)=0.9P(D2)=0.25P(S|D2)=0.87P(D3)=0.42P(S|D3)=0.82 The disjoint union D1⊔D2⊔D3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(D1)+P(D2)+P(D3)=0.33+0.25+0.42=1.

  • Since 5% of all copies has some printing defect, we have P(R)=0.05.

  • Since 70% of defective copies are sold, we have P(S|R)=0.7.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen sold copy has a printing defect, which is represented as P(R|S).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(R|S)=P(S|R)P(R)P(S)=P(S|R)P(R)P(S|D1)P(D1)+P(S|D2)P(D2)+P(S|D3)P(D3)=(0.7)(0.05)(0.9)(0.33)+(0.87)(0.25)+(0.82)(0.42)=0.0350.8589≈0.041,

rounded to 3 decimal places.
