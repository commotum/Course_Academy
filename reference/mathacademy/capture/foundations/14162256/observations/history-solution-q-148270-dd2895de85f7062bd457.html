Let F be the event that a student is female, W be the event that a student writes in print, and L,A,H be the event that a student has low, average, or high writing grades, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(L)=0.2P(A)=0.65P(W|A)=0.55P(H)=0.15P(W|H)=0.52 The disjoint union L⊔A⊔H covers the entire sample space, and we have P(L)+P(A)+P(H)=0.2+0.65+0.15=1.

  • Since 42% of students in the entire school are female, we have P(F)=0.42.

  • Since 80% of females write in print, we have P(W|F)=0.8.

  • Since 56% of students that write in print are female, we have P(F|W)=0.56.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen student with low grades writes in print, which is represented as P(W|L).
