An English teacher believes that students who prefer print handwriting tend to perform better in essay writing. To prove her belief, she puts some students into three groups according to their average writing grades and asks them if they usually write in print.

  • 20% of students have low grades.
  • 65% of students have average grades, and 55% of those students usually write in print.
  • 15% of students have high grades, and 52% of those students usually write in print.

If 42% of students are female, 80% of female students write in print, and 56% of students that write in print are female, what is the probability that a randomly chosen student with low grades writes in print? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
