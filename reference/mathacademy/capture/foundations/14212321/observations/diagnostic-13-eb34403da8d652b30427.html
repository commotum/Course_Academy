EXPLANATION

Applying the multivariable chain rule for the function h(x,y,z) with three independent variables r,θ, and z, where x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ, and z=z, we have

∂h∂θ=∂h∂x⋅∂x∂θ+∂h∂y⋅∂y∂θ+∂h∂z⋅∂z∂θ=−∂h∂xrsin⁡θ+∂h∂yrcos⁡θ.

First, we find the partial derivatives of h with respect to x and y:

∂h∂x=yz,∂h∂y=xz

Writing the above results in terms of r,θ, and z, we get

∂h∂x=rzsin⁡θ,∂h∂y=rzcos⁡θ.

Finally, substituting these into our chain rule formula, we get

∂h∂θ=−(rzsin⁡θ)rsin⁡θ+(rzcos⁡θ)rcos⁡θ=−r2zsin2⁡θ+r2zcos2⁡θ=r2z(cos2⁡θ−sin2⁡θ)=r2zcos⁡2θ.
