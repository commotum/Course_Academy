Let A denote the area under the curve y=C(t) over [0,15]. In this case, we have n=4 rectangles. Using the right Riemann sum, the area is approximated as

A≈C(3)Δt1+C(7)Δt2+C(12)Δt3+C(15)Δt4,

where each Δti is calculated by considering the step between a particular t-value and the previous one, i.e.,

Δti=ti−ti−1.

We set up a value table that includes the Δt's.

t 0 3 7 12 15
Δt − 3 4 5 3
C(t) − 4 3 5 2

We plug in the numbers to approximate the area under the curve and get A≈4⋅3+3⋅4+5⋅5+2⋅3=12+12+25+6=55.

Finally, in the given context, the area under the curve represents the total number of customers served over a period of 15 minutes. So, the total number of customers is approximately 55.
