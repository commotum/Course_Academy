EXPLANATION

We wish to find the limits of integration such that our integral can be expressed as ∫ab∫v1(z)v2(z)∫u1(y,z)u2(y,z)f(x,y,z)dxdydz.

From the repeated integrals, we see that the domain of integration R=R1∪R2, where R1 and R2 are type I regions, is given by

R1={(x,y,z):−2≤y≤2,0≤z≤y+2,−z≤x≤y2},R2={(x,y,z):2≤y≤6,0≤z≤6−y,−z≤x≤y2}.

Since all repeated integrals first integrate with respect to x, the x-limits remain the same as before:

∫ab∫v1(z)v2(z)∫−zy2f(x,y,z)dxdydz

Now, we consider the projection D of R onto the yz-plane shown below.

The region D consists of two type I regions in the yz-plane:

D={(y,z):−2≤y≤2,0≤z≤y+2}∪{(y,z):2≤x≤6,0≤z≤6−y}.

To change the order of integration from dxdzdy to dxdydz, we write D as a type II region.

Notice that the left boundary of the region, written in the form y=y(z), is z=y+2⟹y=z2−2 and the right boundary of the region is z=6−y⟹y=6−z2.

Then, the type II representation of the region is

D={(y,z):0≤z≤2,z2−2≤y≤6−z2},

as shown in the diagram below.

Therefore, by swapping the order of integration, we obtain

∫02∫z2−26−z2∫−zy2f(x,y,z)dxdydz.
