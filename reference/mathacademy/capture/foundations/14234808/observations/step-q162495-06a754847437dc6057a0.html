EXPLANATION

From the given repeated integral, we see that R can be written as a type III region as follows:

R={(x,y,z):0≤z≤2,3−3z2≤x≤3,8−4x3−2z≤y≤4}

We wish to rewrite our triple integral as follows:

∭Rf(x,y,z)dV=∫ab∫v1(x)v2(x)∫u1(x,z)u2(x,z)f(x,y,z)dydzdx

Since both repeated integrals first integrate with respect to y, the y-limits remain the same as before:

∭Rf(x,y,z)dV=∫ab∫v1(x)v2(x)∫8−4x/3−2z4f(x,y,z)dydzdx

Now, we consider the projection D of R onto the xz-plane shown below.

The region D is a type II region in the xz-plane:

D={(x,z):0≤z≤2,3−3z2≤x≤3}

To change the order of integration from dydxdz to dydzdx, we write D as a type I region.

Notice that the bottom boundary of the region, written in the form z=z(x), is

x=3−3z2⟹z=2−2x3

and the top boundary of the region is

z=2.

Then, the type I representation of the region D is

D={(x,z):0≤x≤3,2−2x3≤z≤2},
