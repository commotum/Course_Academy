EXPLANATION

From the given repeated integral, we see that R can be written as a type II region as follows:

R={(x,y,z):0≤z≤1,0≤y≤z3,1+y−z≤x≤2}

We wish to rewrite our triple integral as follows:

∭Rf(x,y,z)dV=∫ab∫v1(y)v2(y)∫u1(y,z)u2(y,z)f(x,y,z)dxdzdy

Since both repeated integrals first integrate with respect to x, the x-limits remain the same as before:

∭Rf(x,y,z)dV=∫ab∫v1(y)v2(y)∫1+y−z2f(x,y,z)dxdzdy

Now, we consider the projection D of R onto the yz-plane shown below.

The region D is a type II region in the yz-plane:

D={(y,z):0≤z≤1,0≤y≤z3}

To change the order of integration from dxdydz to dxdzdy, we write D as a type I region.

Notice that the bottom boundary of the region, written in the form z=z(y), is

y=z3⟹z=y3

and the top boundary of the region is

z=1.

Then, the type I representation of the region D is

D={(y,z):0≤y≤1,y3≤z≤1},

as shown below.

Therefore, by swapping the order of integration, we obtain ∭Rf(x,y,z)dV=∫01∫y31∫1+y−z2f(x,y,z)dxdzdy.
