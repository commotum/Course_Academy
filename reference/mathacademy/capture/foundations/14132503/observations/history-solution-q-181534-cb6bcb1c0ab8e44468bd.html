The intersection of a family of indexed sets {Ai}i∈I contains all elements that belong to all sets in our family:

⋂i∈IAi={x∈U:x∈Aifor all Ai,i∈I} where U is a universal set.

Let's now write down a few instances of our indexed sets:

  • if i=1, we have S1=(0,2)

  • if i=2, we have S2=(0,43)

  • if i=3, we have S3=(0,65)

  • if i=4, we have S4=(0,87)

and so on.

Notice that as i increases,

  • the left endpoint of the intervals is 0 for all the sets,

  • the right endpoint of the intervals is greater than 1 and approaches 1 from the right, never reaching it.

Therefore,

⋂i∈ISi=(0,1].
