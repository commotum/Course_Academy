Let T be the set of all 2×2 matrices (aij) with real entries, such that a11+a22 is rational. Complete the following sentence:

T is not a vector space (with respect to addition of matrices and multiplication of matrices by a real number) because ____.

a
multiplication by a scalar is not closed
b
there exists a matrix A∈T such that A+B≠O for any B∈T.
c
addition of matrices in T is not commutative
d
addition of matrices in T is not associative
e
T does not contain the zero matrix O
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The correct answer is

T is not a vector space (with respect to addition of matrices and multiplication of matrices by a real number) because multiplication by a scalar is not closed.

For example, the identity matrix I=[1001], lies in T, but 2⋅I=[2002]∉T because 2+2 is not a rational number.

On the other hand,

  • The zero matrix belongs to T. ✓

  • Addition of matrices with real entries is commutative, in general. ✓

  • Addition of matrices with real entries is associative, in general. ✓

  • Given any A∈T, the additive inverse matrix −A=(−1)⋅A is also in T, and A+(−A)=O. ✓
