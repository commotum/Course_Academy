EXPLANATION

The second shifting theorem states that if a≥0, then L{u(t−a)f(t−a)}=e−asF(s), where F(s)=L{f(t)}.

In our case, we wish to find the transform of u(t−1)e−6(t−1).

By comparing this expression to the form in the theorem, we can identify the following:

  • The shift is a=1.

  • The shifted function is f(t−1)=e−6(t−1).

From the shifted function, we can deduce the original unshifted function f(t) by simply replacing (t−1) with t: f(t)=e−6t.

Now, we calculate the Laplace transform F(s) of the unshifted function: F(s)=L{e−6t}=1s−(−6)=1s+6.

Finally, we apply the shifting theorem formula L{u(t−a)f(t−a)}=e−asF(s) by multiplying F(s) by the exponential delay factor e−s:

L{u(t−1)e−6(t−1)}=e−s⋅F(s)=e−s⋅1s+6.
