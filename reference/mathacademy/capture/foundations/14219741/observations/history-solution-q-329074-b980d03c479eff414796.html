Recall that the general solution of an inhomogeneous system can be written as x(t)=xc(t)+xp(t) where xc(t) is the complementary solution (solution of the corresponding homogeneous system) and xp(t) is a particular solution.

The forcing term [1−2t+1] of the inhomogeneous system of ODEs is polynomial of degree 1. Therefore, we assume that the particular solution is also polynomial of degree 1, i.e. xp(t)=[a1t+b1a2t+b2]=[a1a2]t+[b1b2], where a1,a2,b1,b2 are constants that are to be determined.

Calculating the derivative of xp with respect to t gives xp′(t)=[a1a2].
