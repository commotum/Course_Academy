The time X, in minutes, between two customers arriving at a bank can be modeled as an exponential random variable X∼Exp(5). If a customer has just arrived at the bank, what is the average time until the next customer arrives at the bank?

a
5 minutes
b
12 seconds
c
5 seconds
d
1 minute
e
10 minutes
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X∼Exp(λ) is an exponential random variable, then

E[X]=1λ.

We're given that X∼Exp(5). Therefore, λ=5, which means that 5 customers arrive each minute. Hence,

E[X]=15min=12s.

Therefore, the average time until the next customer is 12 seconds.
