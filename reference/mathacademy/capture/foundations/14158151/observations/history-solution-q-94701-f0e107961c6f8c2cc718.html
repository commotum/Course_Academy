The 100pth percentile of a random variable Y is the number yp such that

P(Y≤yp)=p.

In our case, we want to find the 30th percentile, which corresponds to the decimal p=0.3. We have

P(Y≤y0.3)=∫0y0.3e−ydy=−e−y|0y0.3=1−e−y0.3.

Therefore, the 30th percentile is given by

1−e−y0.3=0.3e−y0.3=0.7e−y0.3=710−y0.3=ln⁡(710)y0.3=ln⁡(107)y0.3=ln⁡(10)−ln⁡(7).
