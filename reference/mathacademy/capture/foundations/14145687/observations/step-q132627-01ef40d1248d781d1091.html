The time X, in minutes, between two visitors entering a restaurant can be modeled as an exponential random variable X∼Exp(12). If a visitor has just entered the restaurant, what is the average time until the next visitor enters?

a
 15 seconds
b
 2 minutes
c
 1 minute
d
 4 minutes
e
 30 seconds
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X∼Exp(λ) is an exponential random variable, then

E[X]=1λ.

We're given that X∼Exp(12). Therefore, λ=12, and

E[X]=1(12)min=2min.

Therefore, the average time until the next visitor is 2 minutes.
