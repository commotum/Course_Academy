The time X, in days, between two doses of medication in a patient's treatment can be modeled as an exponential random variable X∼Exp(6). If a dose has just been taken by the patient, what is the average time until the next dose?

a
6 days
b
4 hours
c
8 hours
d
6 hours
e
4 days
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X∼Exp(λ) is an exponential random variable, then

E[X]=1λ.

We're given that X∼Exp(6). Therefore, λ=6, which means that 6 doses are taken each day. Hence,

E[X]=16day=4h.

Therefore, the average time until the next dose is 4 hours.
