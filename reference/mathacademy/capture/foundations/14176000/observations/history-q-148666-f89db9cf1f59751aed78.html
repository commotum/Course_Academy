2.Solving for an Unknown Conditional Probability?

A manufacturing company has three sites, factory 1, factory 2, and factory 3. They all produce two types of light bulbs: A and B.

  • Factory 1 produces 45% of the entire production, and 5% of those bulbs are defective.
  • Factory 2 produces 45% of the entire production.
  • Factory 3 produces 10% of the entire production, and 1% of those bulbs are defective.

If 57% of all light bulbs are of type A, 3% of type A light bulbs are defective, and 41% of defective light bulbs are of type A, what is the probability that a randomly chosen light bulb from factory 2 is defective? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
