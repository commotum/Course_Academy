Assuming y=eλx and differentiating y with respect to x gives y′=λeλx,y″=λ2eλx.

Substituting the above into our differential equation gives λ2eλx+100eλx=0eλx(λ2+100)=0.

The auxiliary equation is therefore λ2+100=0.

Solving the auxiliary equation, we get λ2+100=0λ2=−100λ=±10i.

So, λ=±10i are the (purely imaginary) roots of the auxiliary equation.

Therefore, any linear combination of the functions Re(e10ix)=cos⁡10x and Im(e10ix)=sin⁡10x is a solution to the original differential equation.

Among the given options, only cos⁡5x10 is not a solution.
