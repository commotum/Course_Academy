Jennie fills an aquarium, in the shape of a rectangular prism that is 50cm long and 25cm wide, with water from a tap. What is the rate of change of the water level if the volume is increasing at a rate of 250cm3/h?
The water level is increasing at a rate of

EXPLANATION

Let x,y,z be the dimensions of the rectangular prism, let V be its volume. We are given that x=50cm,y=25cm.

Also, we know that dVdt=250cm3/h.

We would like to find dzdt when x=50cm and y=25cm.

We start with the formula for the volume of the rectangular prism: V=xyz

Substituting the known values, we get V=(50)(25)z=1250z.

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get dVdt=ddt(1250z)dVdt=1250(dzdt).

We can now plug in the known value into the above expression. This gives 250=1250(dzdt)dzdt=0.2cm/h.

Therefore, the water level is increasing at a rate of 0.2cm/h.
