John is a carpenter who wants to modify a wooden box. The length, width and height of the box are decreased at a rate of 1 cm/s, 2 cm/s and 4 cm/s, respectively. Determine the rate at which the volume of the box decreases, if its dimensions are 15 cm long, 10 cm wide and 15 cm high.

a
1200 cm3/s
b
525 cm3/s
c
1750 cm3/s
d
820 cm3/s
e
670 cm3/s
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let L, W, H and V be the length, the width, the height, and the volume of the box, respectively. We know that

L=15 cm,W=10 cm,H=15 cm,dLdt=−1 cm/s,dWdt=−2 cm/s,dHdt=−4 cm/s.

We would like to find dVdt.

The relation between the four variables is given by the formula for the volume of a rectangular prism: V=LWH

We differentiate both sides of V=LWH with respect to t, using the product rule, we have

ddtV=ddt(LWH)dVdt=W⋅H(dLdt)+L⋅H(dWdt)+L⋅W(dHdt).

Finally, we can plug in the known values and get dVdt=(10)(15)(−1)+(15)(15)(−2)+(15)(10)(−4)=−1200 cm3/s.

Therefore, the volume of the box is decreasing at a rate of 1200 cm3/s.
