If the surface area of a cube is increasing at a rate of 144 cm2/s, at what rate are the edges changing when each edge is 4 cm? Assume that each edge is increasing at the same rate.

a
6cm/s
b
4cm/s
c
2cm/s
d
3cm/s
e
12cm/s
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let x be the side of the cube, let S be its surface area, and let V be its volume. All of the variables change with time. In particular, we are given that dSdt=144cm2/s.

We would like to find dxdt when x=4cm.

We want to find the rate of change of x knowing the rate of change of S. So, we start with the formula for the surface area of the cube:

S=6x2

Now, we differentiate with respect to time using the chain rule, and we get

ddt(S)=ddt(6x2)dSdt=12xdxdt.

We can now plug in the known values into the above expression. This gives dSdt=12xdxdt144=12(4)dxdtdxdt=3.

Therefore, the edges are changing at a rate of 3cm/s.
