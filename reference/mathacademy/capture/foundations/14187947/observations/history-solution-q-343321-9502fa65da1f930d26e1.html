We define the following variables:

  • let L and W be the length and width of the aquarium, respectively, and

  • let h and V be the height and volume of the water, respectively.

Note that h and V change with time, while L and W are constants.

We are given that L=50m,W=25m,dhdt=0.2m/min.

We would like to find dVdt.

The relation between the two time-dependent variables, V and h, is given by the formula for the volume of water: V=LWh

We differentiate both sides of V=LWh with respect to t, treating both L and W as constants, and get ddtV=ddt(LWh)dVdt=LWdhdt.

Finally, we can plug in the known values and get dVdt=(50)(25)dhdtdVdt=1250⋅0.2dVdt=250.

Therefore, the volume of water in the aquarium is increasing at a rate of 250cm3/h.
