A coastal research station is monitoring the population of brine shrimp in a controlled seawater tank. The tank can sustain a maximum population of 500 shrimp, but the population is still below this limit. Since researchers began tracking the growth, the population reached 452 shrimp on day 3 and 473 shrimp on day 9. On what day will the population reach 490 shrimp? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

Hint: You may assume that the population of shrimp, P(t), increases at a rate that obeys an inhibited growth model, where t is the time, in days.

EXPLANATION

The rate at which the population P(t) changes is proportional to the difference between the maximum population and its current population. Therefore, we have the inhibited growth equation dPdt=k(a−P), where a is the maximum population the habitat can sustain, and k>0 is a constant of proportionality.

In our case, a=500 shrimp, and we have dPdt=k(500−P).

First, we integrate our differential equation by separating the variables:

dP500−P=kdt∫dP500−P=∫kdt−ln⁡|500−P|=kt+C

Since this is the solution to an inhibited growth equation, we have 500−P>0 for all t≥0. Next, we solve for P(t), as follows:

500−P=e−kt−C500−P=e−Ce−kt500−P=Ke−ktP=500−Ke−kt

Note that K=e−C is a constant of integration.

We're told that, on day 3, the population was 452 shrimp. So, applying the condition P(3)=452, we have P(3)=452500−Ke−3k=452Ke−3k=48.

We're also told that, on day 9, the population was 473 shrimp. So, applying the condition P(9)=473, we get P(9)=473500−Ke−9k=473Ke−9k=27.

Then, by dividing the result of the first condition by the result of the second, we can solve for k:

Ke−3kKe−9k=4827e6k=1696k=ln⁡(169)k=16ln⁡(169)

Now, substituting back into the result of the first condition, we solve for K:

Ke−3⋅(1/6)ln⁡(16/9)=48K=48e(1/2)ln⁡(16/9)K=48169K=48⋅43K=64

Therefore, we have the following expression for P(t):

P(t)=500−64e−(t/6)ln⁡(16/9).

Finally, let d be the day when the population reaches 490 shrimp. Then, we have

P(d)=490500−64e−kd=49010=64e−kde−kd=1064=532−kd=ln⁡(532)d=−1kln⁡(532).

Substituting in our value for k, we conclude that the population will be 490 shrimp on day d=−ln⁡(532)16ln⁡(169)=19.3578…≈19, rounded to the nearest day.
