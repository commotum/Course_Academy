An inhibited growth initial value problem has the form dydt=k(a−y),y(0)=y0

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>y for all t≥0.

From the given options, the only inhibited growth problem is dPdt=1−0.00005P,P(0)=10000,

since

dPdt=1−0.00005P=0.00005(20000−P).

In this case, k=0.00005,a=20000, and y0=10000. Notice that a>y0, which is required for inhibited growth.

The other options do not represent inhibited growth. In particular, the initial value problems dPdt=3−0.0004P=0.0004(7,500−P),P(0)=13000, and dPdt=5−0.005P=0.005(1000−P),P(0)=2000 do not represent an inhibited growth model because y0=13000>a=7500, and y0=2000>a=1000.
