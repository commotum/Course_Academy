Given that y<200 and t≥0, which of the following is an inhibited growth equation?

a
dydt=−0.3(y+200)
b
dydt=200(y+0.3)
c
dydt=200(y−0.3)
d
dydt=0.3(y−200)
e
dydt=0.3(200−y)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

An inhibited growth equation has the form dydt=k(a−y), where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>y for all t≥0.

From the given options, the only inhibited growth equation is dydt=0.3(200−y).

In this case, the constant of proportionality is k=0.3, and a=200. Note that for inhibited growth, we must have y<200 for all t≥0.
