A wildlife reserve can support a total of 800 deer. At the beginning of a disease study in the reserve, a parasitic infection has affected 80 deer. After 10 days, the number of deer affected by the parasite increases to 300. To the nearest day, how long does it take for the infected deer population to reach 600 animals?

Hint: You may assume that the number of deer affected by the parasite, P(t), increases at a rate that obeys an inhibited growth model, where t≥0 is the time, in days.
