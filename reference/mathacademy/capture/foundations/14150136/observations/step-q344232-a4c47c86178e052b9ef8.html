A parking garage initially contains 70 vehicles. The number of vehicles in the garage V(t) increases at a rate that obeys an inhibited growth model, where t≥0 is the time in hours. If the maximum capacity of the garage is 250 vehicles, which differential equation could be used to model the number of vehicles in the garage? Assume that k>0 is a constant of proportionality.

a
dVdt=k(250−V)
b
dVdt=k(300−V)
c
dVdt=k(500−V)
d
dVdt=k(70−V)
e
dVdt=k(120−V)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the number of vehicles in the garage obeys an inhibited growth model, the rate at which the number of vehicles changes is proportional to the difference between the maximum capacity and the number of vehicles in the garage. Therefore, we have the differential equation dVdt=k(a−V),

where a is the maximum capacity of the garage, and k>0 is a constant of proportionality.

In our case, a=250 vehicles, and we have

dVdt=k(250−V).
