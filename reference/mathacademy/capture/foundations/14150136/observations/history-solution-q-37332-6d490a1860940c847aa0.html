An inhibited growth initial value problem has the form

dydt=k(a−y),y(0)=y0

where k>0 is a constant of proportionality, and a>y for all t≥0.

From the given options, the only inhibited growth problem is

dPdt=638−0.02P,P(0)=31200,

since

dPdt=638−0.02P=0.02(31900−P).

In this case, k=0.02,a=31900, and y0=31200. Notice that a>y0, which is required for inhibited growth.

The other options do not represent inhibited growth. In particular, the initial value problems dPdt=2216−8P=8(277−P),P(0)=291,

and

dPdt=26−0.005P=0.005(5200−P),P(0)=5600

do not represent an inhibited growth model because y0=291>a=277 and y0=5600>a=5200.
