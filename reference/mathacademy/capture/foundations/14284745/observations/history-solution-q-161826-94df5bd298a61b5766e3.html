We write the position of a point (vector) in spherical polar coordinates as (ρ,θ,ϕ), where

  • ρ≥0 is the distance from the point to the origin,

  • θ∈[0,2π) is the angle between the point and the positive x-axis, and

  • ϕ∈[0,π] is the angle the corresponding vector makes with the positive z-axis.

Notice that inside our region, the spherical polar coordinates vary within the following domains:

  • ρ∈[0,2]

  • θ∈[0,2π]

  • ϕ∈[π/2,π]

Therefore, we have

{(ρ,θ,ϕ):0≤ρ≤2,0≤θ≤2π,π/2≤ϕ≤π}.
