The arc length of a curve y=f(x) between x=a and x=b can be found using the formula L=∫ab1+[f′(x)]2dx.

In our case, f(x)=cos2⁡x, a=0, and b=π.

First, we find the derivative of f(x): f′(x)=−2cos⁡xsin⁡x=−sin⁡(2x)

Substituting f′(x)=−sin⁡2x into the arc length formula, we get L=∫0π1+[f′(x)]2dx=∫0π1+(−sin⁡(2x))2dx=∫0π1+sin2⁡(2x)dx.
