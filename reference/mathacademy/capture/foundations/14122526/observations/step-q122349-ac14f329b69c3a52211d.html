A particle P moves along the x-axis in the direction of increasing x where x≥0. When the displacement of P from the origin O is xm, the magnitude of its acceleration is 32(x+2)5m/s2 and is directed toward O. Initially, P is at the origin O and has velocity v=1m/s. Find x as a function of time t.

a
 x(t)=2t+13−1
b
 x(t)=(3t+2)3−8
c
x(t)=12t+83−2
d
 x(t)=t−83+2
e
 x(t)=(12t−1)3+1

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Since the acceleration of the particle P has magnitude 32(x+2)5m/s2 and is directed toward O, we have dvdt=−32(x+2)5.

Using the fact that

dvdt=ddx(12v2)

we can rewrite our equation as

ddx(12v2)=−32(x+2)5.

Integrating both sides of the above equation with respect to x, we have ∫ddx(12v2)dx=∫−32(x+2)5dx

which gives the general solution

12v2=8(x+2)4+K.

Now, since v=1m/s when x=0m, we can find the value of the constant K, as follows: 12v2=8(x+2)4+K12(1)2=8(0+2)4+K12=12+KK=0

Therefore,

12v2=8(x+2)4v2=16(x+2)4v=±4(x+2)2.

Since the particle is moving in the direction of increasing x, we have that v(x)>0, so we can discard the negative square root. Therefore,

v(x)=4(x+2)2.

Now, we know that v=dxdt. Therefore, we have the equation dxdt=4(x+2)2.

Separating the variables and integrating both sides with respect to t, we have

dxdt=4(x+2)2(x+2)2dxdt=4∫(x+2)2dxdtdt=∫4dt∫(x+2)2dx=∫4dt13(x+2)3=4t+C1(x+2)3=12t+C where C=3C1.

We're given that x=0 when t=0. Therefore, C=8, and the particular solution is

(x+2)3=12t+8.

Finally, writing x in terms of t, we have

(x+2)3=12t+8x+2=12t+83x=12t+83−2.
