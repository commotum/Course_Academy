The diagram shows a series RL circuit with a source electromotive force (EMF) of 6sin⁡(2t)V, a resistor of resistance 12Ω, and an inductor of inductance 32H.

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RL circuit, there are two voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality R=12Ω. VR=12I

  • The voltage drop across the inductor is proportional to the rate of change of current, with a constant of proportionality L=32H. VL=32⋅dIdt

Therefore, by Kirchhoff's voltage law, the differential equation governing the amount of current I in the circuit is

6sin⁡(2t)=12I+32⋅dIdt

which gives the equation dIdt+8I=4sin⁡(2t).

This is a first-order linear equation with constant coefficients. So, to find the general solution, we must find the sum of the complementary and particular solutions.

  • To find the complementary solution Ic, we solve the corresponding homogeneous equation, given by dIcdt+8Ic=0. Solving using an integration technique, such as separation of variables, we obtain the complementary solution Ic(t)=Ae−8t, where A is a constant of integration.

  • Next, we find the particular solution. Note that the right-hand side of the inhomogeneous equation is sinusoidal with argument 2t. So, we assume that the particular solution is also sinusoidal with argument 2t, i.e., Ip(t)=αcos⁡(2t)+βsin⁡(2t). To find the values of α and β, we substitute Ip=αcos⁡(2t)+βsin⁡(2t),dIpdt=−2αsin⁡(2t)+2βcos⁡(2t), into the inhomogeneous equation and group like terms on the left-hand side: (−2αsin⁡(2t)+2βcos⁡(2t))+8(αcos⁡(2t)+βsin⁡(2t))=4sin⁡(2t)(−2α+8β)sin⁡(2t)+(8α+2β)cos⁡(2t)=4sin⁡(2t) Equating the coefficients, we get the following system of equations: {−2α+8β=4(equating the coefficients ofsin⁡(2t))8α+2β=0(equating the coefficients ofcos⁡(2t)) Solving this system gives α=−217, β=817. Therefore, the particular solution is Ip(t)=−217cos⁡(2t)+817sin⁡(2t).

Now, since the general solution is given by the sum of the complementary solution and the particular solution, the general solution in our case is

I(t)=Ic(t)+Ip(t)=Ae−8t−217cos⁡(2t)+817sin⁡(2t).
