Correct
EXPLANATION

The diagram shows a series RL circuit with a source electromotive force (EMF) of E(t)=3sin⁡(2t)V, a resistor with resistance 9Ω, and an inductor with an inductance of 3H.

Kirchhoff's voltage law states that in a series circuit, the source voltage (EMF) equals the sum of the voltage drops.

In an RL circuit, there are two voltage drops:

  • The voltage drop across the resistor is proportional to the current, with a constant of proportionality R=9Ω. VR=9I

  • The voltage drop across the inductor is proportional to the rate of change of current, with a constant of proportionality L=3H. VL=3dIdt

Therefore, by Kirchhoff's voltage law, the differential equation governing the amount of current I in the circuit is

3sin⁡(2t)=9I+3dIdt, which gives dIdt+3I=sin⁡(2t).
