EXPLANATION

Since the set {p1(t),p2(t)} is orthogonal, the orthogonal projection of q(t) onto the subspace S is given by

projSq(t)=projp1(t)q(t)+projp2(t)q(t).

We are given that projp1(t)q(t)=t, so let's now find the orthogonal projection of q(t) onto Span{p2(t)}. This can be computed as

projp2(t)q(t)=⟨q(t),p2(t)⟩⟨p2(t),p2(t)⟩p2(t).

In our case, we have

⟨q(t),p2(t)⟩=q(0)p2(0)+q(1)p2(1)=(4t−3)|0⋅(t−1)|0+(4t−3)|1⋅(t−1)|1=(−3)⋅(−1)+1⋅0=3,

and

⟨p2(t),p2(t)⟩=p2(0)p2(0)+p2(1)p2(1)=(t−1)2|0+(t−1)2|1=(−1)2+02=1.

Therefore,

projp2(t)q(t)=31⋅(t−1)=3t−3.

Finally,

projSq(t)=projp1(t)q(t)+projp2(t)q(t)=t+(3t−3)=4t−3.
