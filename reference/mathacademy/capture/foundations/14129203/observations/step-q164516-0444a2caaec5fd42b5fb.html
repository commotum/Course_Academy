Identify the following surface:

y2+z2=1,−∞<x<∞.

a
An elliptic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis
b
A hyperbolic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the y-axis
c
A hyperbolic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis
d
An elliptic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis
e
A hyperbolic paraboloid whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the equation of an elliptic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the z-axis is given by

x2a2+y2b2=1.

Thus, an elliptic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis is given by

y2b2+z2c2=1.

Therefore, our equation is an elliptic cylinder whose axis of symmetry is the x-axis and b=c=1.
