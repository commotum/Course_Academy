Which of the following global truncation error expressions represent a second-order numerical method?

  1. e(Δx)=6(Δx)2
  2. e(Δx)=0.3Δx+2(Δx)2
  3. e(Δx)=9(Δx)2+4(Δx)5
a
I, II, and III
b
II and III only
c
I only
d
II only
e
I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A numerical method has order p if p is the largest positive integer such that the global truncation error (GTE) satisfies e(Δx)=O((Δx)p)asΔx→0. The order describes how fast errors shrink as Δx decreases.

With that in mind, let's check each option in turn:

  • For e(Δx)=6(Δx)2, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 6(Δx)2. So, statement I corresponds to an order 2 method.

  • For e(Δx)=0.3Δx+2(Δx)2, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 0.3Δx. So, statement II corresponds to an order 1 method.

  • For e(Δx)=9(Δx)2+4(Δx)5, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 9(Δx)2. So, statement III corresponds to an order 2 method.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only".
