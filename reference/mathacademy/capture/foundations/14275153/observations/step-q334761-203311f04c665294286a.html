Which of the following expressions for the global truncation error (e) or local truncation error (τ) represent a third-order numerical method?

  1. e(Δx)=6(Δx)3+4(Δx)4
  2. τ(Δx)=0.5(Δx)4+3(Δx)7
  3. e(Δx)=2(Δx)2+(Δx)5
a
II only
b
I only
c
I and II only
d
I, II, and III
e
II and III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

For a given numerical method, if p is the largest positive integer such that

  • the global truncation error (GTE) satisfies
    e(Δx)=O((Δx)p)asΔx→0,

  • the local truncation error (LTE) satisfies
    τ(Δx)=O((Δx)p+1)asΔx→0,

then the method is of order p.

With that in mind, let’s check each option:

  • For e(Δx)=6(Δx)3+4(Δx)4, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 6(Δx)3=6(Δx)3.
    So, statement I corresponds to an order 3 method.

  • For τ(Δx)=0.5(Δx)4+3(Δx)7, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 0.5(Δx)4=0.5(Δx)3+1.
    So, statement II corresponds to an order 3 method.

  • For e(Δx)=2(Δx)2+(Δx)5, the dominant term as Δx→0 is 2(Δx)2=2(Δx)2.
    So, statement III corresponds to an order 2 method.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only".
