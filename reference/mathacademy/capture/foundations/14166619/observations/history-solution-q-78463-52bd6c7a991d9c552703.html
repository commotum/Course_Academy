The number of generalized eigenvectors of rank greater than 1 that correspond to an eigenvalue λ is equal to the difference between the algebraic multiplicity and the geometric multiplicity of λ.

In the characteristic polynomial p(λ)=(1+λ)4(1−λ)2(4−λ)3, we see that the root λ=−1 has multiplicity 4. So the algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalue λ=−1 is 4.

However, there is only 1 eigenvector corresponding to the eigenvalue λ=−1, so the geometric multiplicity of the eigenvalue λ=−1 is 1.

Therefore, the number of generalized eigenvectors of rank greater than 1 corresponding to λ=−1 is 4−1=3.
