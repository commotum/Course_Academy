(A−7I)5v=0and(A−7I)4v≠0.

Since Null(A−7I)4 is a subspace of dimension 9 in Null(A−7I)5, which is a subspace of dimension 11, our basis can contain only 2 vectors that lie in Null(A−7I)5 but do not lie in Null(A−7I)4.

Therefore, there will be nullity(A−7I)5−nullity(A−7I)4=11−9=2 generalized eigenvectors of rank 5 corresponding to the eigenvalue λ=7.
