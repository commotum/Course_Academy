If C is a 4×4 invertible matrix, then which of the following could be the characteristic equation of C?

  1. λ4+2λ3+3λ2+λ=0
  2. λ(λ−2)2(λ+3)=0
  3. λ4−3λ2+4=0
a
I only
b
I and III only
c
I and II only
d
II only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Since C is invertible, according to the invertible matrix theorem, 0 can't be an eigenvalue of C. As a result, λ=0 should not be a solution to the corresponding characteristic equation.

With that in mind, let's examine the given equations in turn.

  • Equation I cannot be the characteristic equation of C since λ=0 is a root of the equation.

  • Equation II cannot be the characteristic equation of C since λ=0 is a root of the equation.

  • Equation III could be the characteristic equation of C. Indeed, λ=0 is not a root of the equation.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
