A matrix B has the characteristic equation λ4−2λ2+1=0. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. λ=1 is a root of the characteristic equation
  2. λ=2 is an eigenvalue of B
  3. B is invertible
a
I and II only
b
I only
c
I, II, and III
d
I and III only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The invertible matrix theorem in terms of eigenvectors states the following:

The square matrix A is invertible if and only if 0 is not an eigenvalue of A.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, substituting λ=1 into the equation, we obtain λ4−2λ2+1=0(1)4−2(1)2+1=01−2+1=00=0✓

  • Statement II is false. Since substituting λ=2 into the equation, we obtain λ4−2λ2+1=0(2)4−2(2)2+1=016−8+1=09≠0×

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, λ=0 is not an eigenvalue of B. As a result, according to the invertible matrix theorem, B is invertible.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
