EXPLANATION

The position of a point in cylindrical polar coordinates is written as (r,θ,z), where

  • r≥0 and θ∈[0,2π) are the usual plane polar coordinates, and

  • z∈(−∞,∞) is the usual Cartesian z-coordinate.

The picture shows the xy-plane.

We note the following:

  • Since the z-coordinate of any point on our surface equals 0, we must have z=0 for every point on the surface.

  • On the other hand, r and θ can vary arbitrarily in their respective domains.

Therefore, the equation of the surface in cylindrical polar coordinates is z=0.
