What surface is defined by the equation z=3 in cylindrical polar coordinates?

a
A half-cone
b
A plane that is parallel to the xy-plane
c
A circular cylinder
d
A cone
e
A half-plane that passes through the z-axis
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The position of a point in cylindrical polar coordinates is written as (r,θ,z), where

  • r≥0 and θ∈[0,2π) are the usual plane polar coordinates, and

  • z∈(−∞,∞) is the usual Cartesian z-coordinate.

With that in mind, let's examine our equation:

z=3

This equation requires that z-coordinate of any point on our surface equals 3, while r and θ can vary arbitrarily in their respective domains.

Therefore, our surface is the plane that is parallel to the xy-plane shown below.
