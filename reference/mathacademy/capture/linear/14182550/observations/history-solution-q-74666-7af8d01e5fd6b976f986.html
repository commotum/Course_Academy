Notice that the points A, B, and C lie on the circle. From the diagram, the radius of the circle is r=OC.

We first notice that AB=AO+OB.

Now, AO― and OB― are both segments between the center of the circle and the circle itself. So, they are both radii of the circle, and because AB=15, we have AB=15AO+OB=15r+r=152r=15r=7.5.

Finally, we obtain OC=r=7.5.
