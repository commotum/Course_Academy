Notice that the points A, B, and C lie on the circle. From the diagram, the radius of the circle is r=OC=16.

To find AB, we first notice that AB=AO+OB.

Now, AO― and OB― are both segments between the center of the circle and the circle itself. So, they are both radii of the circle, and because the radius is 16, we have

AO=16,OB=16.

Finally, we obtain

AB=AO+OB=16+16=32.
