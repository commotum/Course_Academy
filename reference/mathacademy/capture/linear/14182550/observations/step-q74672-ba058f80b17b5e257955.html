The diagram above shows a circle of radius 17, a point C that lies on the circle, and a third point Q on the plane.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. OQ<17
  2. OQ>17
  3. OC>17

b

Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The point C lies on the circle itself. So, the distance from O to C is equal to the radius of the circle: OC=17

On the other hand, the point Q lies outside the circle. So, the distance between Q and the center O of the circle must be greater than the radius: OQ>OC=17

Therefore, statement II is true, while statements I and III are false.
