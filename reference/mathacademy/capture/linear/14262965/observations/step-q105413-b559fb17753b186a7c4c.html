EXPLANATION

The correct answer is the following.

We see that q(x) is continuous on the interval (−∞,3], and since (−∞,2] lies completely inside (−∞,3], it is also continuous on (−∞,2].

Notice that

  • f(x) and g(x) are not continuous at x=−1, and so are not continuous on (−∞,2].

  • p(x) and h(x) are not continuous at x=0, and so are not continuous on (−∞,2].
