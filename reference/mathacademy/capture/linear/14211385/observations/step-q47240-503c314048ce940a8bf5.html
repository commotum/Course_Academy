Evaluate ln⁡(e2)+2ln⁡(e).

EXPLANATION

The natural logarithm reverses the action of the exponent. The principle is: ln⁡(ex)=x

In the first and second term, we have x=2 and x=1, respectively. Therefore: ln⁡(e2)=2ln⁡(e)=1

Hence: ln⁡(e2)+2ln⁡(e)=2+2⋅1=4
