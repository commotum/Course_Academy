A cyclist travels toward their destination. The rate at which their remaining distance R decreases is proportional to the time, in minutes, since their journey started.

We note the following:

  • We're told that dRdt is proportional to the time since their journey started. In other words, dRdt∝t. Recall that ∝ means "is proportional to."
