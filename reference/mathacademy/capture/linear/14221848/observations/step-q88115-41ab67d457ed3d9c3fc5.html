EXPLANATION

A dilation with center at the origin and a scale factor of 13 can be represented by the function

(x,y)↦(13x,13y).

Applying this function to the endpoints A(−6,−3) and B(3,6), we have the following:

(−6,−3)↦(13⋅(−6),13⋅−3)=(−2,−1)(3,6)↦(13⋅(3),13⋅6)=(1,2)

Therefore, the resulting points are A′(−2,−1) and B′(1,2).
