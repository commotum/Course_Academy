EXPLANATION

A dilation with center at the origin and a scale factor of 12 can be represented by the function

(x,y)↦(12x,12y).

Applying this function to the point (8,−6), we have

(8,−6)↦(12⋅8,12⋅(−6))=(4,−3).

Therefore, the resulting point is (4,−3).
