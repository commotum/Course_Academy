EXPLANATION

A dilation with center at the origin and a scale factor of 12 can be represented by the function

(x,y)↦(12x,12y).

Applying this function to the point (8,−4), we have

(8,−4)↦(12⋅8,12⋅(−4))=(4,−2).

Therefore, the resulting point is (4,−2).
