The graph of a periodic function y=f(x) is shown above. What is the period of this function?

a
4
b
18
c
1
d
8
e
2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Note that if we shift the graph 8 units left or right, the graph remains unchanged.

On the other hand, if we shift the graph horizontally by any distance other than 8 (or a multiple of 8), we get a different graph.

Therefore, the period of the function is 8.
