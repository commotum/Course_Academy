1

2

Correct
EXPLANATION

Note that if we shift the graph 2 units left or right, the graph remains unchanged.

On the other hand, if we shift the graph horizontally by any distance other than 2 (or a multiple of 2), we get a different graph.

Therefore, the period of the function is 2.
