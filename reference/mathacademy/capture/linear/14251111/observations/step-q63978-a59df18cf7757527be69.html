EXPLANATION

This geometric sequence is given as a recurrence relation, i.e.,

f(n+1)=r⋅f(n).

Therefore: f(1)=10,r=4.

We're interested in finding n such that f(n)=640. Using the formula for the nth term, we get:

f(n)=f(1)⋅rn−1640=10⋅4n−164010=4n−164=4n−143=4n−1

Equating the exponents gives: 3=n−1⟹n=4

Therefore, 640 is the 4th term of the sequence.
