A surd is a number containing a radical symbol 0 that cannot be written as a positive or negative whole number or fraction.

Surds can be square roots 0, cube roots 03, or higher roots such as 04, 05,…, etc.

With that in mind, let's look at each number in turn.

  • 103 is a surd because 10 is not a perfect cube. In fact 103=2.154434…

  • 1003 is a surd because 100 is not a perfect cube. In fact 1003=4.641588…

  • 10003 is NOT a surd. Since 1000 is a perfect cube, we can get rid of the cube root symbol (03) and obtain a whole number. 10003=1033=10

Therefore, the correct answer is: 103 and 1003 only.
