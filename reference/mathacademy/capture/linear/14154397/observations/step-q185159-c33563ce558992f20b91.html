Which of the following numbers are NOT surds? 16,181,1100

a
1100 only
b
181 and 1100 only
c
16 only
d
181 only
e
16 and 181 only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A surd is a number containing a radical symbol 0 that cannot be written as a positive or negative whole number or fraction.

With that in mind, let's look at each number in turn.

  • 16 is a surd because 16 is not a perfect square. In fact 16=0.408248…

  • 181 is NOT a surd. Since 1 and 81 are perfect squares, we can get rid of the square root symbol (0) and obtain a regular fraction. 181=1292=1⋅19⋅9=19⋅19=(19)2=19

  • 1100 is NOT a surd. Since 1 and 100 are perfect squares, we can get rid of the square root symbol (0) and obtain a regular fraction. 1100=12102=1⋅110⋅10=110⋅110=(110)2=110

Therefore, the correct answer is: 181 and 1100 only.
