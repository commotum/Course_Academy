Calculate the derivative of y=2sec⁡(6x−2).

a
−6csc⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
b
2sec⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
c
12sec⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
d
−12sec⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
e
12csc⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we express y as the composite function

y=2sec⁡u,u=6x−2.

Differentiating gives

dydu=2sec⁡utan⁡u,dudx=6.

We now apply the chain rule, and get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=2sec⁡utan⁡u⋅6=12sec⁡utan⁡u=12sec⁡(6x−2)tan⁡(6x−2)
