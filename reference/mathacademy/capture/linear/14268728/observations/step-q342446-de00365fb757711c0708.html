EXPLANATION

First, the slope of y=f(x) at x=π2 is given by f′(π2).

We will calculate f′(x) using the chain rule. First, we express f(x) as the composite function f=3sin⁡(u),u=cos⁡(x).

Differentiating gives

dfdu=3cos⁡(u),dudx=−sin⁡(x).

We now apply the chain rule, and get dfdx=dfdu⋅dudx=3cos⁡(u)⋅(−sin⁡(x))=−3sin⁡(x)cos⁡(cos⁡(x)).

Evaluating at x=π2, we find f′(π2)=−3sin⁡(π2)cos⁡(cos⁡(π2))=−3(1)(1)=−3.
