The recursive formula for a geometric sequence is f(n+1)=r⋅f(n).

To complete the formula, we must determine the common ratio r and state the first term f(1). The first two terms are f(1)=10⋅(−5)1=10⋅(−5)=−50,f(2)=10⋅(−5)2=10⋅25=250.
