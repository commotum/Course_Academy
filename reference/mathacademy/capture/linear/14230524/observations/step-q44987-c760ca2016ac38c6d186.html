What is the explicit formula for the nth term of the geometric sequence given by an+1=an7,a1=3,n≥1?

a
an=17⋅3n,n≥1
b
an=37n,n≥1
c
an=73n,n≥1
d
an=3⋅7n,n≥1
e
an=217n,n≥1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The explicit formula for the nth term of a geometric sequence is

an=a1⋅rn−1,n≥1.

So, we must determine the first term a1 and the common ratio r.

We are given that the first term is a1=3. To find the common ratio, we compare the given sequence with the general recursive formula for a geometric sequence:

an+1=r⋅an

From the above, we see that r=17.

Substituting a1=3 and r=17 into the formula for the nth term, we obtain an=a1⋅rn−1=3⋅(17)n−1.

We can simplify further by manipulating the exponents as follows: an=3⋅(17)n−1=3⋅(17)−1⋅(17)n=3⋅7⋅(1n7n)=21⋅17n=217n

Therefore, the formula for the nth term is an=217n,n≥1.
