The graph above shows the relationship between x and y. Given that y varies inversely with x, find the inverse variation equation between x and y.

a
y=4x
b
y=6x
c
y=9x
d
y=52x
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since y varies inversely with x, we use the equation y=kx.

In the graph, we notice a point with x=3 and y=2. We substitute this data into the equation to find k: 2=k32⋅3=k6=k

Therefore, the inverse variation equation is given by y=6x.
