In an inverse variation, y=38 when x=2. Find an equation that relates x and y.
y=
34x​
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since y varies inversely with x, we use the equation y=kx.

Next, we use the given data y=38 when x=2 to find k: 38=k238⋅2=k68=k34=k

Therefore, our inverse variation equation is y=34x.
