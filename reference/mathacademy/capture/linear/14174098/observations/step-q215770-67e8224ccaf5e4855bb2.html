EXPLANATION

We need to isolate all terms with p on one side of the equation. To do this, we subtract 8p from both sides:

5pq=1+8p5pq−8p=1+8p−8p5pq−8p=1

Notice that all terms containing p are on the left-hand side, and all terms independent of p are on the right-hand side.

Next, we factor out p in the expression on the left-hand side. 5pq−8p=1(5q−8)p=1

Finally, we divide both sides by (5q−8) to isolate p: (5q−8)p=1(5q−8)p5q−8=15q−8(5q−8)p5q−8=15q−8p=15q−8

Note: This result assumes that 5q−8≠0.
