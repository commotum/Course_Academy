Dividing a whole number by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying the whole number by the reciprocal of the fraction.

First, we write 6 as an improper fraction: 6=61

Next, we note that the reciprocal of 34 is 43.

So, we have 6÷34=61÷34=61×43.

Now, we multiply our fractions.

61×43=6×41×3=243

Finally, we simplify: 24÷3=8
