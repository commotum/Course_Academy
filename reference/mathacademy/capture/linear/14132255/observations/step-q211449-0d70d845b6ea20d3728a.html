Expressed as a whole number, 7÷78=
8

EXPLANATION

Dividing a whole number by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying the whole number by the reciprocal of the fraction.

First, we write 7 as an improper fraction: 7=71

Next, we note that the reciprocal of 78 is 87.

So, we have 7÷78=71÷78=71×87.

Now, we multiply our fractions.

71×87=7×81×7=567

Finally, we simplify: 56÷7=8
