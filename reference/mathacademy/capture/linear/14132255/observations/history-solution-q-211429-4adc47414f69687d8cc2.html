Dividing a whole number by a fraction is equivalent to multiplying the whole number by the reciprocal of the fraction.

First, we write 8 as an improper fraction: 8=81

Next, we note that the reciprocal of 57 is 75.

So, we have 8÷57=81÷57=81×75.

Now, we multiply our fractions. 81×75=8×71×5=565
