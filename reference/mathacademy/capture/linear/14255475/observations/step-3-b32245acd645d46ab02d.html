From the graph, we see that the maximum value is y=3.

Method 2

We know that y=sin⁡x has a maximum value of 1.

sin⁡x≤1

Adding 2 to both sides of the above inequality gives:

sin⁡x+2≤1+2sin⁡x+2≤3

Therefore, the maximum value of sin⁡x+2 is A=3.
