The graph shows that the function makes 2 complete cycles on the interval [0,2π]. So, the period is π.

We know that for the function y=sin⁡(Bx), the horizontal scaling factor is 1B, and the period is multiplied by the scaling factor:

period=2π⋅1B

For our function, the period is π, so we have
