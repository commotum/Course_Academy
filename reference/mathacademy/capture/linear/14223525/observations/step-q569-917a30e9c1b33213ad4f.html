EXPLANATION

Using the addition law for exponents, we have

ab=i2⋅i9=i2+9=i11.

To find the value of i raised to some positive integer power n, we divide n by 4, and raise i to the remainder.

In our case, we have n=11. Dividing the power by 4, we get

11÷4=2R3.

So the remainder is 3. Therefore,

i11=i3=−i.
