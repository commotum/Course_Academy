EXPLANATION

We need to solve the following system consisting of the equations of the curves: {y=x2+2x+2y=2−2x

Setting the equations equal to one another and solving, we get x2+2x+2=2−2xx2+4x=0x(x+4)=0x=−4,0.

Since x1<x2, the x-coordinates of the intersection points are x1=−4 and x2=0.

To find the y-coordinates of the intersection points, we plug these values into either curve. Let's choose the line since it's simpler.

  • Substituting x1=−4, we get y1=2−2x1=2−2(−4)=10, so one point of intersection is (x1,y1)=(−4,10).

  • Substituting x2=0, we get y2=2−2x2=2−2(0)=2, so the other point of intersection is (x2,y2)=(0,2).

Therefore, y1=10,y2=2, and y1+y2=10+2=12.
