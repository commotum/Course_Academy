If sin⁡x=53, then 5sin⁡(x−20π)=

EXPLANATION

The period of y=sin⁡x is 2π. Therefore, for integer n we have

sin⁡x=sin⁡(x+2nπ).

Substituting n=−10 into the right-hand-side gives

sin⁡x=sin⁡(x−20π).

Therefore, if sin⁡x=53, then sin⁡(x−20π)=53.

Finally, then 5sin⁡(x−20π)=5(53)=53.
