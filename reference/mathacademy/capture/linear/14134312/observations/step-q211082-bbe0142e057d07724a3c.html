EXPLANATION

Dividing a fraction by a whole number is equivalent to multiplying the fraction by the reciprocal of that whole number.

The reciprocal of 10 is 110. So, we have 78÷10=78×110.

Now, we multiply fractions. We multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators: 78×110=7×18×10=780
