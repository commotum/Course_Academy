EXPLANATION

If the ratio of vanilla flavored cakes to total cakes is 5:9, it means that there are 5 vanilla flavored cakes for every 9 cakes in total.

Another way of writing this is to say that "out of every 9 cakes in the bakery, 5 of them are vanilla flavored cakes."
