EXPLANATION

Suppose a and b are two lines. Then,

  • a∥b means a and b are parallel, and

  • a⊥b means a and b are perpendicular.

In the given diagram, there are two pairs of perpendicular lines:

  • q is perpendicular to r, which we write as q⊥r

  • q is perpendicular to s, which we write as q⊥s.

Therefore, the correct statement is q⊥r and q⊥s.
