EXPLANATION

Let's recall our definitions.

  • The natural numbers consist of all positive whole numbers: 1,2,3,4,5,…

  • The integers consist of all of the positive and negative whole numbers, and zero: …,−3,−2,−1,0,1,2,3,…

  • The rationals consist of all numbers that can be written as the ratio of two integers.

Now, let's go through each number in turn.

  • −19 is not a natural number.

  • −19 is not an integer.

  • −19 is a rational number because it can be written as (−1)9.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
