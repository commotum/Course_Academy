EXPLANATION

To subtract two mixed numbers, we subtract whole numbers from whole numbers and fractions from fractions.

Subtracting the whole numbers, we get 6−1=5.

Now we subtract the fractions:

78−56

To subtract the fractions, we need to find the least common multiple of the denominators (8 and 6).

  • Multiples of 8: 8,16,24,…

  • Multiples of 6: 6,12,18,24,…

Therefore, the least common denominator of 8 and 6 is 24.

  • To put 78 over a denominator of 24, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 3: 78=7×38×3=2124

  • To put 56 over a denominator of 24, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 4: 56=5×46×4=2024

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators:

78−56=2124−2024=124

Therefore,

678−156=5124
