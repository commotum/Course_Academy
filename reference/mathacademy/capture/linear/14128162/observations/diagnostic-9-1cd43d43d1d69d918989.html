Determine the least common multiple of 4, 6 and 8.

a
 16
b
 24
c
 32
d
 48
e
 12
Don't Know
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's list the multiples of each number. Then, we find the smallest number that appears in all three lists:

  • Multiples of 4: 4,8,12,16,20,24,28…

  • Multiples of 6: 6,12,18,24,30…

  • Multiples of 8: 8,16,24,32…

We conclude that the least common multiple of 4, 6 and 8 is 24.
