The point A with coordinates (2,ln⁡2) lies on the curve y=ln⁡x, and the point A′ with coordinates (6,ln⁡2) lies on the curve y=ln⁡(ax), shown above. What is the value of a?

a
3
b
12
c
2
d
13
e
16
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The graph of y=ln⁡(ax) can be obtained from y=ln⁡x by a horizontal stretch of scale factor 1a.

From the diagram, we deduce that under such a transformation, the point (2,ln⁡2) that lies on y=ln⁡x becomes

(1a⋅2,ln⁡2)=(6,ln⁡2)

which lies on y=ln⁡(ax).

Therefore, a=13.
