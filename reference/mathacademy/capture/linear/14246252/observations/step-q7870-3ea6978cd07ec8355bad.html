EXPLANATION

The graph of y=ln⁡(ax) can be obtained from y=ln⁡x by a horizontal stretch of scale factor 1a.

From the diagram, we deduce that under such a transformation, the point (4,ln⁡4) that lies on y=ln⁡x becomes

(1a⋅4,ln⁡4)=(1,ln⁡4)

which lies on y=ln⁡(ax).

Therefore, a=4.
