EXPLANATION

The product rule for derivatives gives p′(x)=f′(x)⋅g(x)+f(x)⋅g′(x).

As a result, we have that p′(1.5)=f′(1.5)⋅g(1.5)+f(1.5)⋅g′(1.5).

From the graphs above, we obtain that f(1.5)=−2 and g(1.5)=−0.5.

Additionally,

  • The slope of f(x) on x∈(1,3) equals 1. So, f′(1.5)=1.

  • The slope of g(x) on x∈(1,3) equals 1. So, g′(1.5)=1.

Therefore, p′(1.5)=f′(1.5)⋅g(1.5)+f(1.5)⋅g′(1.5)=1⋅(−0.5)+(−2)⋅1=−2.5.
