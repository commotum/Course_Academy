Then, we apply the addition and multiplication principles, as usual: 4z=6z−84z−6z=6z−8−6z−2z=−8−2z−2=−8−2z=4

Since z=4 is different from z=0 and z=43, we can accept it as a solution. Thus, the solution is z=4.
