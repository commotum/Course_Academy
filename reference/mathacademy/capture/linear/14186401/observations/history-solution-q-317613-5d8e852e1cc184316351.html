The number of views V(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 120%.

Let's write down what we know:

  • a=50 is the total number of views initially

  • r=1.2 is the growth rate (120% expressed as a decimal)

  • t= the time (in hours) after the video was posted

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

V=a(1+r)t.

Therefore, the correct expression for V(t) is

V=50⋅(1+1.2)t.

Simplifying the above, we get

V(t)=50⋅(2.2)t.

To find the total number of views after 3 hours, we evaluate the above for t=3:

V(3)=50⋅(2.2)3≈532

Therefore, the estimated number of views after 3 hours is 532.
