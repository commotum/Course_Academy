The number of subscribers y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 35%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

We have:

  • a=5000 is the initial number of subscribers.

  • r=0.35 is the growth rate (35% expressed as a decimal).

  • t= the time, in years.

Therefore, the correct expression for y is

y=5000⋅(1+0.35)t=5000⋅(1.35)t.

Evaluating the expression for the number of subscribers after 15 years, we get

y(15)=5,000⋅(1.35)15≈450792.

We round to the nearest whole number because we are talking about people.
