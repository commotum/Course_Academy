When a new article was first published on a news website, it had 40 views. An exponential model estimates that at the end of each hour after publication, the number of views increased by 190% of the number of views at the end of the previous hour.

Which of the following equations best represents this model, where V is the estimated number of views t hours after the article was published, and t≤6?

a
V(t)=40⋅(19)t
b
V(t)=40⋅(9)t
c
V(t)=40⋅(3.9)t
d
V(t)=40⋅(2.9)t
e
V(t)=40⋅(0.9)t
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The number of views V(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 190%.

Let's write down what we know:

  • a=40 is the total number of views initially

  • r=1.9 is the growth rate (190% expressed as a decimal)

  • t= the time (in hours) after publication

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

V=a(1+r)t.

Therefore, the correct expression for V(t) is

V=40⋅(1+1.9)t.

Simplifying the above, we get

V(t)=40⋅(2.9)t.
