The formula to calculate the distance that an object in uniform rectilinear motion has traveled is given by d=v⋅t,

where v represents the velocity of the moving object and t represents time.

What is the formula for velocity in terms of distance and time?

a
v=d⋅t
b
v=dt
c
v=tv
d
v=d−t
e
v=d+t
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We need to isolate v, so we will treat the other variables d and t as constants. Since v is being multiplied by t, we need to divide both sides of the equation by t:

d=v⋅tdt=v⋅ttdt=v⋅ttdt=v

Thus, v=dt.

Note: This result assumes that t≠0.
