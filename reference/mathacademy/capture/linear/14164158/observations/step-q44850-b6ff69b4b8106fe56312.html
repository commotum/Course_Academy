In business, the economic profit is defined as E=PQ−C,

where P is the sale price of a good, Q is the number of goods sold, and C is the cost of producing Q goods.

What is the formula for the price in terms of the other variables?

a
C=PQ−E
b
P=Q(E+C)
c
P=E+CQ
d
Q=E+CP
e
P=E−CQ
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We need to isolate P, so we will treat the other variables E, Q, and C as constants. We can use the addition and multiplication principles to solve for P.

First, we add C to both sides:

E=PQ−CE+C=PQ−C+CE+C=PQ

Then, we divide by Q to get P alone:

E+C=PQE+CQ=PQQE+CQ=P

Thus, P=E+CQ.

Note: This result assumes that Q≠0.
