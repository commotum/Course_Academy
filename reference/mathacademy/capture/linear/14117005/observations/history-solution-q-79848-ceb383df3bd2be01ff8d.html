64=28⋅2+8↙↙28=8⋅3+4↙↙8=4⋅2+0

As a result, gcd(64,28)=4.

Solving for the remainders (the rightmost terms) in the equations above, we get

8=64−28⋅24=28−8⋅3.

Then, we back-substitute:

4=28−8⋅3=28−(64−28⋅2)⋅3=28⋅7−64⋅3=64⋅(−3)+28⋅7

Therefore, u=−3 and v=7.

Finally, u+v=(−3)+7=4.
