EXPLANATION

We will use the extended Euclidean algorithm. First, we apply the forward reduction:

79=35⋅2+9↙↙35=9⋅3+8↙↙9=8⋅1+1

As a result, gcd(79,35)=1.

Solving for the remainders (the rightmost terms) in the equations above, we get

9=79−35⋅28=35−9⋅31=9−8.

Then, we back-substitute:

1=9−8=9−[35−9⋅3]=9⋅4−35=[79−35⋅2]⋅4−35=79⋅4−35⋅9=79⋅4+35⋅(−9)

Therefore, u=4 and v=−9.

Finally, u+v=4+(−9)=−5.
