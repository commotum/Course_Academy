EXPLANATION

The remainder theorem states that when q(x) is divided by (x−4), the remainder equals q(4).

Evaluating q(4) directly gives q(4)=5(4)−(4)2=20−16=4.

So, when q(x) is divided by (x−4), the remainder is 4.
