EXPLANATION

Using the quotient rule, we get ddx(f(x)g(x))=f′(x)g(x)−f(x)g′(x)(g(x))2.

At the point where x=−2, this gives

ddx(f(x)g(x))|x=−2=f′(−2)g(−2)−f(−2)g′(−2)g2(−2)=(−3)⋅2−1⋅622=−3.
