t h(t) z(t) h′(t) z′(t)
4 1 −2 3 1

If f(t)=2h(t)−51−z(t), compute f′(4). Do not round the answer.

53​

EXPLANATION

The quotient rule states that ddt(uv)=u′v−uv′v2.

Let u=2h(t)−5 and v=1−z(t). Differentiating each, we get dudt=2h′(t)anddvdt=−z′(t).

Substituting into the quotient rule gives

f′(t)=2h′(t)(1−z(t))−(2h(t)−5)(−z′(t))(1−z(t))2.

At t=4, this gives

f′(4)=2h′(4)(1−z(4))−(2h(4)−5)(−z′(4))(1−z(4))2=2(3)(1−(−2))−(2(1)−5)(−1)(1−(−2))2=2(3)(3)−(−3)(−1)(3)2=18−39=159=53.
