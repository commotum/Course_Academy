Consider a system of n linear equations in n variables, written in matrix form as Dz=c. Here, D is the non-singular coefficient matrix of the system, z is the column vector containing the variables, and c is the column vector containing the constants. The unique solution of the system can be found using the method shown below:

Dz=cD−1Dz=D−1cInz=D−1cz=D−1c

From top to bottom and right to left, what are the missing parts of the derivation?

a
D−1 and D
b
D−1 and D−1
c
D and z
d
D−1 and In
e
D and In
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since D is not singular, it must have an inverse D−1. Now, pre-multiplying both sides of the equation Dz=c by D−1, we obtain the following:

Dz=cD−1Dz=D−1cInz=D−1cz=D−1c

Therefore, the missing parts are D−1 and In.
