In January 2022, a social media account had approximately 53,000 followers. In December of that year, the account had 120% the number of followers it had in January. Determine the number of followers the account had in December, 2022.

a
63,600
b
59,400
c
61,800
d
58,000
e
60,600
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula: part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the number of followers the account had in January, 2022, which is 53,000. The percentage is 120%, and the part is the number of followers the account had in December 2022.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal: 120%=120÷100=1.2

Applying our formula, we have: part=percentage×whole=120%×53,000=1.2×53,000=63,600

Therefore, 120% of 53,000 is 63,600. So, the social media account had 63,600 followers in December 2022.

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction: 120%=120100=120÷20100÷20=65

Applying our formula, we have: part=percentage×whole=120%×53,000=65×53,000=6×53,0005=6×10,600=63,600

Therefore, 120% of 53,000 is 63,600. So, the social media account had 63,600 followers in December 2022.
