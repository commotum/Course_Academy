EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the number of balloons in the room, which is 200. The percentage is 7.5%, and the part is the number of white balloons.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

7.5%=7.5÷100=0.075

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=7.5%×200=0.075×200=15

Therefore, 7.5% of 200 is 15. Thus, there are 15 white balloons.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

7.5%=7.5100

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=7.5%×200=7.5100×200=7.5×200100=7.5×2=15

Therefore, 7.5% of 200 is 15. Thus, there are 15 white balloons.
