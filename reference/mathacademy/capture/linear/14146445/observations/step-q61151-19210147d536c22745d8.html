In Maria's class, there are 30 children. Of this amount, 10% walk to school. How many children walk to school?

a
5 children
b
4 children

3 children

EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the total number of children in Maria's class, which is 30. The percentage is 10%, and the part is the number of children who walk to school.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

10%=10÷100=0.1

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=10%×30=0.1×30=3

Therefore, 10% of 30 is 3. Thus, 3 children walk to school.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

10%=10100=10÷10100÷10=110

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=10%×30=110×30=3010=3

Therefore, 10% of 30 is 3. Thus, 3 children walk to school.
