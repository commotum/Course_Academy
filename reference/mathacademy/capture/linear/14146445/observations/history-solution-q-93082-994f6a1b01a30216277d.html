To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the capacity of the first location, which is 10,000 people. The percentage is 115%, and the part is the capacity of the second location.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

115%=115÷100=1.15

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=115%×10,000=1.15×10,000=11,500

Therefore, 115% of 10,000 is 11,500. So, the second location has a capacity of 11,500 people.
