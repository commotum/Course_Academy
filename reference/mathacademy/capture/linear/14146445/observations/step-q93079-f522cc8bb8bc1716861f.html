A restaurant originally had enough capacity to serve 80 customers. Following a renovation, the restaurant increased its capacity to 120% of the original. What was the capacity of the restaurant after the renovation?

a
100 customers
b
96 customers
c
104 customers
d
90 customers
e
92 customers
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the original number of customers the restaurant was able to receive, which is 80 customers. The percentage is 120%, and the part is the number of customers the restaurant can receive after the renovation.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

120%=120÷100=1.2

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=120%×80=1.2×80=96

Therefore, 120% of 80 is 96. So, the restaurant can receive 96 customers after the renovation.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

120%=120100=120÷20100÷20=65

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=120%×80=65×80=6×805=6×16=96

Therefore, 120% of 80 is 96. So, the restaurant can receive 96 customers after the renovation.
