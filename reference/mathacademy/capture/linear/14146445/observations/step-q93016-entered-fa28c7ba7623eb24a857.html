Question 1
?

50% of 24 students in Joan's class have brown eyes. How many students have brown eyes?

a
 8
b
 12
c
 14
d
 10
e
 16
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the total number of students in Joan's class, which is 24. The percentage is 50%, and the part is the number of students that have brown eyes.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

50%=50÷100=0.5

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=50%×24=0.5×24=12

Therefore, 50% of 24 is 12. So, 12 students in Joan's class have brown eyes.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

50%=50100=50÷50100÷50=12

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=50%×24=12×24=242=12

Therefore, 50% of 24 is 12. So, 12 students in Joan's class have brown eyes.
