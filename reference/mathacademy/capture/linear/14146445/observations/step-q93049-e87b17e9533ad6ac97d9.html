EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the amount of money Emma earns annually, which is $19,500. The percentage is 12.6%, and the part is the amount she must pay in taxes.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

12.6%=12.6÷100=0.126

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=12.6%×19,500=0.126×19,500=2,457

Therefore, 12.6% of 19,500 is 2,457. Hence, Emma must pay $2,457 in taxes.
