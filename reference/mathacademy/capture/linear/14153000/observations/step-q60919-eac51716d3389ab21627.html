In a supermarket, 16 out of 200 watermelons were unripe. What percentage of watermelons were unripe?

a
 16%
b
 8%
c
 10%
d
 12%
e
 32%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We're given a part and a whole, and we must find a percentage. So, we can use the following formula: percentage=part÷whole

In this case,

  • the part is the number of unripe watermelons, which is 16, and

  • the whole is the total number of watermelons, which is 200.

Substituting our numbers into the above formula, we get

percentage=16÷200=16200.

To write this as a percentage, we create an equivalent fraction with 100 as the denominator:

16200=16÷2200÷2=8100

Now we can write this fraction as a percentage:

8100=8%

Therefore, 8% of the watermelons were unripe.
