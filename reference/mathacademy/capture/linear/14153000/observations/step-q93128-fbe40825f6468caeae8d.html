On average, George exercises for 120 minutes each day. Of this time, he spends 54 minutes running. What percentage of his exercise time does George spend running?

a
60%
b
55%
c
45%
d
40%
e
50%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We're given a part and a whole, and we must find a percentage. So, we can use the following formula: percentage=part÷whole

In this case,

  • the part is the number of minutes George runs, which is 54, and

  • the whole is the total number of minutes George exercises, which is 120.

Substituting our numbers into the above formula, we get

percentage=54÷120=54120.

We can simplify this fraction as follows:

54120=54÷6120÷6=920

To write this as a percentage, we create an equivalent fraction with 100 as the denominator:

920=9×520×5=45100

Now we can write this fraction as a percentage:

45100=45%

Therefore, George runs for 45% of his overall training time.
