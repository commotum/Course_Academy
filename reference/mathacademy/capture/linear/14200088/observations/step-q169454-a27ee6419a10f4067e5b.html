Minutes (m) Words (w)

3.5 245
5 350

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. There is a proportional relationship between m and w.
  2. There is not a proportional relationship between m and w.
  3. The constant of proportionality is 35.

EXPLANATION

First, let's add a new column to our table and compute the ratio of pages per minute for each row.

Minutes (m) Words (w) Words / Minutes
1 70 70
3.5 245 70
5 350 70

The ratio of words per minute is the same for each row. Therefore, there is a proportional relationship between m and w, and the constant of proportionality is 70.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
