EXPLANATION

Every proportional relationship takes the form y=kx, where k is the constant of proportionality.

To compute the constant of proportionality, we first need to choose a point (x,y) from the table. Here, let's choose the point (4,2), which corresponds to the first row of the table.

Now, we can find the constant of proportionality by computing the ratio of the y-coordinate to the x-coordinate, as follows:

k=yx=24=2÷24÷2=12

Therefore, the equation is

y=12x.
