EXPLANATION

First, recall the hierarchy diagram of quadrilaterals, where each shape below another is a specific type of the one above it.

Let's now look at the terms one by one.

  • The given figure is a quadrilateral where all sides are congruent and all angles are right, so it is a square.

  • Since the quadrilateral has two pairs of opposite sides that are parallel to each other, not one pair, it is not a trapezoid.

  • The quadrilateral is a parallelogram where all sides are congruent. So it is a rhombus.

Consequently, the given shape is described by terms I (square) and III (rhombus).
