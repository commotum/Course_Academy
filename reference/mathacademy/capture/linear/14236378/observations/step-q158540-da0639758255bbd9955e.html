The point (−1,1) lies on the curve y=f(x). Which of the following points must lie on y=3f(x+2)?

a
(−3,13)
b
(3,1)
c
(2,13)
d
(−3,3)
e
(2,3)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Method 1

To plot y=3f(x+2), we apply the following steps:

  • First, we take the graph y=f(x).

  • Then, shift the graph horizontally by 2 unit to the left and get the graph of y=f(x+2). After this transformation, the point (−1,1) will be mapped to the point
    (−1−2,1)=(−3,1).

  • Finally, we stretch the result by a scale factor of 3 parallel to the y-axis and get the graph of y=3f(x+2). After this transformation, the point (−3,1) will be mapped to the point (−3,3⋅1)=(−3,3).

Method 2

Since the point (−1,1) lies on the graph of y=f(x), we have that f(−1)=1.

Now consider the graph of y=3f(x+2). Substituting x=−3, we get

y=3f(−3+2)=3f(−1)=3⋅1=3.

Therefore, the point (−3,3) must lie on y=3f(x+2).
