The surface area of a cube is 294cm2. Find the length of the edges of the cube.

a
1cm
b
6cm
c
7cm
d
9cm
e
2cm
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The surface area S of a cube is made up of 6 congruent squares. So, the area A of one face is A=S6=2946=49cm2.

Since A=s2, where s is the length of an edge, we can find s as follows: s=A=49=7cm

Therefore, the length of the edges is 7cm.
