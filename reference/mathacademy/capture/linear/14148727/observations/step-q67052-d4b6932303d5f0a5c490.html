EXPLANATION

We put an addition symbol (+) between each part and separate using parentheses: (5a)+(−43b)+(−12c)+(12d) Counting off the terms:

  • the first term is 5a,
  • the second term is −43b,
  • the third term is −12c, and
  • the fourth term is 12d.

In particular, we are interested in the fourth term. The fourth term is 12d.
