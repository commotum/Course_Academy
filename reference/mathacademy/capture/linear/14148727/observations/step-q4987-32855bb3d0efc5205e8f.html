a
2d, 5f, and 11
b
2d, −9e, and 5f
c
2d, −9e, 5f, and 11
d
2, −9, 5, and 11
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

We put an addition symbol (+) between each part and separate using parentheses:

(2d)+(−9e)+(5f)+(11)

So, the expression has four terms: 2d, −9e, 5f, and 11.
