EXPLANATION

We want to find a "path" from Q to T consisting only of the vectors a,b, and c.

According to the triangle law of addition,

QT→=QS→+SP→+PT→

as shown below.

Now, from the original diagram, we have that

QS→=−b,SP→=−a,PT→=c.

Let's add these to our diagram.

Therefore, we obtain

QT→=QS→+SP→+PT→=−b+(−a)+c=c−a−b.
