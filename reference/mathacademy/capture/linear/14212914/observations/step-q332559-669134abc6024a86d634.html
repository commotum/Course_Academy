EXPLANATION

We want to find a "path" from C to B consisting only of the vectors a,b,c, and d.

According to the triangle law of addition,

CB→=CE→+EF→+FA→+AB→

as shown below.

Now, from the original diagram, we have that

CE→=d,EF→=−c,FA→=b,AB→=−a.

Let's add these to our diagram.

Therefore, we obtain

CB→=CE→+EF→+FA→+AB→=d−c+b−a.
