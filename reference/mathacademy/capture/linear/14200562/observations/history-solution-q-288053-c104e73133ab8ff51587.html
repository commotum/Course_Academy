Since the equation is true for all values of x, the expressions on both sides must be exactly the same. That means the coefficients of the matching powers of x must be equal.

Let's compare the coefficients:

  • The coefficient of x on the left-hand side is a, and on the right-hand side it is 10. So, we have a=10.

  • The constant term on the left-hand side is b, and on the right-hand side it is 24. So, we have b=24.

Now, we compute the sum: a+b=10+24=34
