EXPLANATION

Since the equation is true for all values of x, the expressions on both sides must be exactly the same. That means the coefficients of the matching powers of x must be equal.

Let's compare the coefficients:

  • The coefficient of x on the left-hand side is u, and on the right-hand side it is −1. So, we have u=−1.

  • The constant term on the left-hand side is v, and on the right-hand side it is 1. So, we have v=1.

Now, we compute the sum: u+v=−1+1=0
