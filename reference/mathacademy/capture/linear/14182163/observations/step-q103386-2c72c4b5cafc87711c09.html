Mark is mowing the lawn. He wants to determine how much area he can mow as time goes on. What are appropriate units for Mark's purpose, in terms of base units?

a
m2s
b
in3s
c
m2s2
d
ms
e
fts
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We measure the change in the area, as we change the time. Consequently, any appropriate unit must take the form unit of areaunit of time.

The base unit for area is m2, and the base unit for time is s. Therefore, the appropriate units are m2s, or, square meters per second.
