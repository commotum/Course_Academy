EXPLANATION

To plot the given function, we follow these steps:

  • We take the curve y=5x.
  • Reflect it on the x-axis to give y=−5x. The y-intercept moves from (0,1) to (0,−1) but the horizontal asymptote stays at y=0.
  • Then, vertically shift it by 3 units down to give y=−5x−3. The point (0,−1) moves to (0,−4) and the horizontal asymptote moves from y=0 to y=−3.
