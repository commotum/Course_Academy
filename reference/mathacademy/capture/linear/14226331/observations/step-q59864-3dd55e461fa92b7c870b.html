Gerry, a gardener, is planting roses. He knows he needs 1 cup of water for every 4 seeds he plants. So, Gerry creates a ratio table to determine the ratio of water to seeds for different quantities.


Water (cups) Seeds
1 4
7 ?
12 48

How many seeds can Gerry plant with 7 cups of water?

a
26
b
32
c
28
d
24
e
30
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We start with the ratio 1:4 and write it as a fraction:

14

So the numerator represents the number of cups of water (1), and the denominator represents the number of seeds (4).

We can make a numerator of 7 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 7:

1×74×7=728

We add this to our ratio table:


Water (cups) Seeds
1 4
7 28
12 48

So, Gerry can plant 28 seeds with 7 cups of water.
