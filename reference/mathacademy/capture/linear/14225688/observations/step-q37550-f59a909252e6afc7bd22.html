EXPLANATION

The sum of cubes formula is given by

a3+b3=(a+b)(a2−ab+b2).

Notice that we can write our expression as a sum of cubes, as follows: 8x6+27y3=(2x2)3+(3y)3

Now, we factor our expression using the sum of cubes formula:

8x6+27y3=(2x2)3+(3y)3=(2x2+3y)((2x2)2−2x2⋅3y+(3y)2)=(2x2+3y)(4x4−6x2y+9y2)
