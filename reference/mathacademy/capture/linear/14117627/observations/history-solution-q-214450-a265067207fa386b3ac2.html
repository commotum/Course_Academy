The residue class [4]7 consists of the elements that are congruent to 4 modulo 7. So, we have

[4]7={x∈Z|x≡4(mod7)}={x∈Z|x=4+7k,k∈Z}={4+7k|k∈Z}={…,4−2⋅7,4−7,4,4+7,4+2⋅7,…}={…,−10,−3,4,11,18,…}.

We see that −3 and 18 belong to [4]7, while 6 does not.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only".
