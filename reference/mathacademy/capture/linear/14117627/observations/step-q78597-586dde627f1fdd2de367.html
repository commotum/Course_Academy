Which of the following integers belong to the residue class [−7]11?

  1. 15
  2. −4
  3. 7

EXPLANATION

The residue class [−7]11 consists of the elements that are congruent to −7 modulo 11. So, we have

[−7]11={x∈Z|x≡−7(mod11)}={x∈Z|x=−7+11k,k∈Z}={−7+11k|k∈Z}={…,−7−2⋅11,−7−11,−7,−7+11,−7+2⋅11,…}={…,−29,−18,−7,4,15,…}.

We see that 15 belongs to [−7]11, while −4 and 7 do not.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only".
