We can solve this problem using modular arithmetic.

First, we note the number of complete months between January 1st, 1995, and September 1st, 1995.

  • There were 5 months with 31 days (January, March, May, July, and August).

  • There were 2 months with 30 days (April and June).

  • There was 1 month with 28 days (February).
