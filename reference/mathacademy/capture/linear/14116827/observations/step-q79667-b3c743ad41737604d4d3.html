Which of the following is equivalent to x+5≡11(mod15)?

a
x≡11(mod5)
b
x≡4(mod10)
c
x≡6(mod15)
d
x≡5(mod4)
e
x≡11(mod15)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The addition property of congruences states that

a≡b⟺a+c≡b+c(modn).

Similarly, the subtraction property states that

a≡b⟺a−c≡b−c(modn).

We use the subtraction property to subtract 5 from both sides of the given congruence, as follows:

x+5≡11(mod15)x+5−5≡11−5(mod15)x≡6(mod15)
