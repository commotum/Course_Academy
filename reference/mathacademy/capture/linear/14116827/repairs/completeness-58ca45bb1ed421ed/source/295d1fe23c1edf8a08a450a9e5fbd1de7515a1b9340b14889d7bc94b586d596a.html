Which of the following is congruent to 0 modulo 17?

a
15
b
−51
c
12
d
−21
e
−35
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, recall that

a≡0(modn)⟺a=kn,

where k∈Z. In other words, when a is divided by n, there is no remainder. This means that any integer multiple of 17 is congruent to 0 modulo 17.

Therefore, since 17|(−51), we obtain

−51≡(−3)⋅17≡(−3)⋅0≡0(mod17).

None of the other options are multiples of 17.
