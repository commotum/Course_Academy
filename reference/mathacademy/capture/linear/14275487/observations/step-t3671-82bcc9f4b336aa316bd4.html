In probability, we say that two events are independent when the outcome of one event doesn't influence the outcome of the other event. Otherwise, the events are dependent.

Let's imagine a situation where we have blue marbles and orange marbles in a bag, and we draw two marbles from the bag, one after the other. Let the event be the outcome of the first draw, and let be the outcome of the second draw.

  • If the marbles are drawn with replacement (meaning that each marble is put back into the bag after drawing), then event is not influenced by event In this case, and are independent.
  • On the other hand, if the marbles are drawn without replacement (meaning that each marble is not put back into the bag after drawing), then event is influenced by event In this case, and are dependent.
