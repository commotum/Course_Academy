A fair spinner has one orange section and six red sections. The spinner is spun 3 times. What is the probability of getting the orange section on the first spin, a red section on the second spin, and the orange section on the third spin?

a
 1343
b
 6343
c
 142
d
 17
e
 7343
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's call O1 the event "the first outcome is orange", R2 the event "the second outcome is red" and O3 the event "the third outcome is orange". Then the required probability is P(O1∩R2∩O3).

Since the events O1, R2 and O3 are mutually independent, we can use the multiplication law for independent events, which states that P(O1∩R2∩O3)=P(O1)⋅P(R2)⋅P(O3).

Let's find P(O1),P(R2) and P(O3).

  • The spinner has 1 orange section among the 1+6=7 sections. So, we have P(O1)=P(O3)=17,P(R2)=67.

Therefore, substituting the above into the multiplication law for independent events, we get P(O1∩R2∩O3)=17⋅67⋅17=6343.
