What is the missing number in the following equality?

137×6=07

a
60
b
33
c
45
d
42
e
6
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we write 137 as an improper fraction:

137=(1×7)+37=107

We also write 6 as an improper fraction:

6=61

Now, we can multiply the numbers. We multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators:

107×61=10×67×1=607

Therefore, the missing number is 60.
