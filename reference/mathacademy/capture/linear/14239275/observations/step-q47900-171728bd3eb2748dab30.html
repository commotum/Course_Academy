f′(x)=limh→0(−5(x+h)2+2−(−5x2+2)h)=limh→0(−5(x2+2xh+h2)+2+5x2−2)h)=limh→0(−5x2−10xh−5h2+2+5x2−2h)=limh→0(−10xh−5h2h)=limh→0h(−10x−5h)h=limh→0(−10x−5h)=−10x.
