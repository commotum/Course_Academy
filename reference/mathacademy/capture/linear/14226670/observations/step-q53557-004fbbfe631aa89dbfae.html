Ken's tree has a diameter of 4ft. What length of chain does Ken need for it to wrap around the tree perfectly?

a
πft
b
8ft
c
8πft
d
4πft
e
2πft
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The length of the chain needed to wrap around the tree is its circumference.

The formula for the circumference of a circle is C=πd, where d=4ft is the diameter.

Therefore, we have C=π⋅4=4πft.
