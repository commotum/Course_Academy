A circular plot of land has a diameter of 20m. What length of fence is needed to border this plot of land?

a
5πm
b
20πm
c
10πm
d
40πm
e
50πm
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The length of the fence needed to border the circular land is its circumference.

The formula for the circumference of a circle is C=πd, where d=20m is the diameter.

Therefore, we have C=π⋅20=20πm.
