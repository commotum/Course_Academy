What are the coordinates of the minimum point of the function y=x2+5?

a
(−5,5)
b
(5,5)
c
(0,−5)
d
(0,5)
e

Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In general, the graph of y=x2+k looks like the standard parabola y=x2 but translated k units up. The vertex of y=x2+k is located at (0,k).

For the given function y=x2+5, we have k=5.

Therefore, the given curve is the parabola y=x2 after being translated 5 units up.

The vertex is located at (0,5), and these are the coordinates of the minimum point.
