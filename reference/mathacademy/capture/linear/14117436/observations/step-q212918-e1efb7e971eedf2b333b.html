EXPLANATION

Let b=12a+11. Note that b is positive. Now, let d3, d2, and d1 be the last three digits of the number b:

b=…d3d2d1

Then, b can be written in expanded form as follows:

b=⋯+100d3+10d2+d1=10(⋯+10d3+d2)+d1

Now, we compute the residue of b modulo 10:

b≡10(⋯+10d3+d2)+d1(mod10)≡0⋅(⋯+10d3+d2)+d1(mod10)≡0+d1(mod10)≡d1(mod10)

Notice that d1 is the last digit of b.

Finally, since b=12a+11, and 12≡2,11≡1(mod10) we have the following: b≡d1(mod10)12a+11≡d1(mod10)2a+1≡d1(mod10)

Therefore, the expression that gives the last digit of 12a+11 is the following:

2a+1mod10
