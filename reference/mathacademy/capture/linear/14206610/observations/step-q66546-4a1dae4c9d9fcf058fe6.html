Correct
EXPLANATION

The common logarithm of a is defined as follows:

log⁡a=x is equivalent to a=10x

If we compare log⁡a=12 with log⁡a=x, we get that x=12.

Therefore, the given equation can be expressed as 101/2=a.

Finally, since 101/2=10, the equation log⁡a=12 is equivalent to 10=a.
