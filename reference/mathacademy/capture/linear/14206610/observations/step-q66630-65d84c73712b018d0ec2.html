First, we rewrite 103 as a power of 10 (the base of the common logarithm) as follows:

103=101/3

Therefore, we have:

log⁡(103)=log⁡(101/3)

The common logarithm reverses the action of the exponent. The principle is:

log⁡(10x)=x

In our case, we have x=13. Therefore:

log⁡(101/3)=13
