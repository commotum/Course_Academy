EXPLANATION

The point of intersection consists of the coordinates (x,y) that satisfy both equations y=5x+2 and y=−2x−5. So, we need to solve the system of equations consisting of the two lines: {y=5x+2y=−2x−5

We can solve this system by substitution. Substituting the first equation y=5x+2 into the second equation y=−2x−5 yields an equation that we can solve for x: 5x+2=−2x−55x=−2x−77x=−7x=−1

This is the x-coordinate of the point of intersection. To find the y-coordinate, we substitute x=−1 back into one of the equations, say y=5x+2, as follows: y=5x+2=5(−1)+2=−5+2=−3

Therefore, the solution to the system is x=−1,y=−3. So, the coordinates of the intersection point are (−1,−3).
