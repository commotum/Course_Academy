The initial investment was $11280, and this decreased by 18 every month.

If the investment value is reduced by 18, then 78 of its value remained every month. So the decay factor is 78.

The formula for exponential decay (in terms of a decay factor b) is

p=abt.

In our case, we have the following:

  • a=11280 is the initial investment.

  • b=78 is the decay factor.

  • t= the time, in months.

Therefore, the correct expression for p(t) is

p(t)=11280⋅(78)t.

So the missing number is 78.
