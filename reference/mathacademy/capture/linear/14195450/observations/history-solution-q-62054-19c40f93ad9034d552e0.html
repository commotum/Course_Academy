The formula for exponential decay (in terms of a decay rate r) is

p(t)=a(1−r)t.

In our case, we have the following:

  • a=1130 is the initial daily milk production.

  • r=0.12 is the decay rate (12% expressed as a decimal).

  • t= the time, in months.

Therefore, the correct expression for p is

p(t)=1130⋅(1−0.12)t=1130⋅(0.88)t.
