Explanation

The slope of the tangent at x=1 is equal to f′(1). In part 1, we found

f′(x)=−2(x2+2x−8)(2x2−11x+5)2.

Now, we evaluate at x=1, and we get

f′(1)=−2(12+2⋅1−8)(2⋅12−11⋅1+5)2=10(−4)2=1016=58.

Therefore, the slope of the tangent line to the curve at x=1 is 58.
