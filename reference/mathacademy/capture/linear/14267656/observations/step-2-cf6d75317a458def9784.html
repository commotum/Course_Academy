Explanation

Recall that the horizontal asymptote of y=2x−a+b is y=b.

In the figure above, the horizontal asymptote is y=4. Hence, b=4.

Next, we use the point (5,6) to determine the value of a. Substituting (5,6) into the equation of the curve, we get

25−a+4=625−a=25−a=1a=4.

Therefore, a=4 and b=4.
