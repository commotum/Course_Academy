The function h is part of the graph of the function y=2cos⁡(πx3)+6. Find the exact value of y when x=7.
y=
7

Correct
Explanation

First, we find an angle that's coterminal with 7π3 that lies in the range [0,2π).

7π3−2π=π3✓

Therefore, cos⁡(7π3)=cos⁡(π3).

Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle π3 corresponds to the point (12,32).

Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡(π3)=12.

Therefore, y=2cos⁡(π3)+6=2⋅12+6=7.
