EXPLANATION

First, we factor the quadratic denominator, giving us the equation

1r(r−1)−1r−1=13r.

We now make the following observations:

  • The least common denominator of our equation is given by LCM(r(r−1),r−1,3r)=3r(r−1).

  • The values r=0 and r=1 cannot be solutions to our equation. These values make at least one denominator in our original equation equal zero, and division by zero is undefined.
