Next, we apply the addition principle, subtracting 3 from both sides: 23=5x+323−3=5x+3−320=5x+020=5x

Next, we apply the multiplication principle, dividing both sides by 5: 20=5x205=5x54=5x54=x

Since 4=x, we conclude that the solution is x=4.
