If P(x) and Q(x) are polynomials of degree 11 and 10, respectively, the degree of P+Q is
11
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since P is a polynomial of degree 11, the leading term contains an x11.

Since Q is a polynomial of degree 10, the leading term contains an x10.

In P+Q, the highest-order term still contains an x11. No x11 term in P could possibly cancel out the x11 term in Q.

Therefore, the degree of P+Q must be 11.
