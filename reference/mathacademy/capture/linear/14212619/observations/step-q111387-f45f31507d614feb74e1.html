If P(x) and Q(x) are polynomials of degree 3 and 7, respectively, which of the following statements are true?

  1. P+Q is a polynomial of degree 3
  2. P−Q is always a polynomial
  3. 2x5⋅PQ is sometimes a polynomial
a
 I and II only
b
 III only
c
 II only
d
 I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. The highest-degree terms in P and Q must contain x3 and x7, respectively. Since there is no x7 term in P that could possibly cancel the x7 term in Q, the leading term in P+Q still contains x7. Therefore, the degree of P+Q must always be 7.

  • Statement II is true. Polynomials are closed under subtraction.

  • Statement III is true. For example, suppose P=x3 and Q=x7. Then, 2x5⋅PQ=2x5⋅x3x7=2x, which is a polynomial.

In conclusion, only statements II and III are true.
