Remember that the equation of an ellipse centered at the origin with a horizontal radius of length a and a vertical radius of length b is given by x2a2+y2b2=1.

In the given equation, we have a2=25 and b2=9.

  • Since a2=25, the length of the horizontal radius must be a=25=5.

  • Since b2=9, the length of the vertical radius must be b=9=3.

So the semi-major axis has length a=5, and the semi-minor axis has length b=3.

Therefore, the major axis has length 2a=2⋅5=10, and the minor axis has length 2b=2⋅3=6.
