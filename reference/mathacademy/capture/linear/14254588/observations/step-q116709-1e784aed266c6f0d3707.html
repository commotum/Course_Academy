Which expression gives the equations of all the asymptotes of y=csc⁡x? Assume that n represents any integer.

a
x=π2+2nπ
b
x=nπ
c
x=π4+nπ2
d
x=nπ2
e
x=π2+nπ
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's recall the graph of y=csc⁡x.

Note that the asymptotes of the function occur at points not in the domain: 0,±π,±2π,…

Therefore, the equations of the asymptotes of y=csc⁡x are described with the expression x=nπ, where n is any integer.
