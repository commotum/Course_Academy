EXPLANATION

Let's recall some of the basic properties of y=cot⁡x:

  • The domain is all real numbers except at the integer multiples of π: x=0,±π,±2π,…

  • The range of the function is y∈(−∞,∞).

  • The period of the function is π.

  • There is no minimum or maximum value.

  • The zeros occur at x=±π2,±3π2,…

  • The graph has vertical asymptotes at the points that are not in the domain: x=0,±π,±2π,…, and at each asymptote, the graph tends to −∞ from the left and ∞ from the right.
