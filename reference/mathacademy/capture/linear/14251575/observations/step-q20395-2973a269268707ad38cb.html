EXPLANATION

Since the vectors are equal, we can write

−3a+13b=λ(12a−52b).

Next, we factor the left-hand side, which gives

−14(12a−52b)=λ(12a−52b).

Notice that we have (12a−52b) on both sides. So, let's define a new vector u as u=12a−52b.

Then, our equation becomes

−14u=λu.

Therefore, λ=−14.
