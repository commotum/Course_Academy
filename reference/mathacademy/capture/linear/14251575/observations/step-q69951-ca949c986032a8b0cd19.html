EXPLANATION

  • Statement I is false. The magnitude of m is greater than the magnitude of g. So, m≠−g.

  • Statement II is false. The vectors a and z have opposite directions. So, a≠z.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, vectors g and m are parallel and have opposite directions. Also the magnitude of g is 12 times the magnitude of m. So, g=−12m.

Therefore, only statement III is true.
