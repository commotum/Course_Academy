If a total of 9 people made all the cakes for the sale, and each person made the same number of cakes, then they each made
24
cakes.

Explanation

We know that there are 216 cakes in total. So, each person made 216÷9 cakes.

We start by writing down the question using long division notation:

9 2|1|6

Notice that 2 (the number of hundreds in 216) is less than the divisor 9. Therefore, we should consider both the hundreds and tens:

Divide: 21÷9=2R3
Write over tens: 2
Multiply: 9×2=18
Subtract: 21−18=3
Bring down: 6

Subtract: 36−36=0

We get the remainder 0, so the division is done.

Therefore, 216÷9=24.
