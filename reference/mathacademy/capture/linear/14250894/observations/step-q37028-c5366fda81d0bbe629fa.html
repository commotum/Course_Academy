What is the range of the function h defined by the following table?

x 0 1 2 12 3 13 4 14
h(x) 6 6 6 0 6 0 6 0
a
(0,6)
b
[−6,6]
c
{0,6}
d
{−6,0,6}
e
[0,6]
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The range of h(x) consists of all the outputs that have a corresponding x-input.

In a table, the range consists of all numbers that feature in the row corresponding to h(x).

Therefore, the range is the set {0,6}.
