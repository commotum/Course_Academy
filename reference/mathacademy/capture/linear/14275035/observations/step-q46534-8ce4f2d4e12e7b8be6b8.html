The expression 45m2+5 is equivalent to

a
5(3m+i)(3m−i)

15(m+i)(m−i)

(3m+i)(3m−i)

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's factor 5 from the expression. We have

45m2+5=5(9m2+1).

Let's now focus on factoring the expression (9m2+1).

To factor a sum of squares, we first write it as a difference. Then, we rewrite the second term using i2=−1. Finally, we factor as a difference of squares.

9m2+1=9m2−(−1)=(3m)2−i2=(3m+i)(3m−i)

Finally, substituting this back into our main expression, we get

5(9m2+1)=5(3m+i)(3m−i).
