Which set represents all integers divisible by 5 from −21 to 11?

a
{−25,−20,−15,…,10}
b
{−20,−10,0,10}
c
{−20,−15,−10,…,10}
d
{−20,−15,−10,…,5}
e
{−20,−15,−10,…,15}
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To express the desired set, we start with the first integer divisible by 5 greater than or equal to −21, which is −20. The next integer divisible by 5 is −15, the one after that is −10, and so on up to 10 (which is the last number that is divisible by 5 and less than or equal to 11).

Therefore, we have {−20,−15,−10,…,10}.
