Which set represents all positive integers less than or equal to 15?

a
{…,13,14,15}
b
{1,2,3,…,14}
c
{1,2,3,…,15}
d
{…,12,13,14}
e
{1,3,5,…,14}
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To express the desired set, we start with the first positive integer less than or equal to 15, which is 1. The next positive integer is 2, the one after that is 3, and so on up to 15 (which is the last number that is less than or equal to 15).

Therefore, we have {1,2,3,…,15}.
