EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence is

an=a1+(n−1)d.

First, we will write the formula for the nth term of this sequence. From the first few terms

4,8,12,16,…,

we see that the first term is a1=4 and the common difference is

d=8−4=4.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

an=a1+(n−1)d=4+(n−1)(4)=4+4n−4=4n

Now, we can use our formula to find the 25th term. All we have to do is substitute n=25:

an=4na25=4(25)=100

Therefore, the 25th term of the sequence is a25=100.
