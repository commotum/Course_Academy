The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence (in function notation) is

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d.

We are given the first term f(1)=−1. Also, the common difference of the sequence can be found as d=−3−(−1)=−2.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d=−1+(n−1)(−2)=−1−2n+2=1−2n

Therefore, the formula for the nth term is f(n)=1−2n.
