The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence (in function notation) is

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d.

We are told that the first term is f(1)=−5 and the common difference is d=−1. So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d=−5+(n−1)(−1)=−5−n+1=−4−n

Therefore, the formula for the nth term is f(n)=−4−n.
