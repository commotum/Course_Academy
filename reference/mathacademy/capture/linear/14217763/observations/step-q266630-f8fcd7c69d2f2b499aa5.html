6sec⁡60∘+3csc⁡30∘+cot⁡30∘=

Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that sec is the reciprocal of cos, csc is the reciprocal of sin and cot is the reciprocal of tan. So, we have sec⁡60∘=1cos⁡60∘,csc⁡30∘=1sin⁡30∘,cot⁡30∘=1tan⁡30∘.

The angle 60∘ and 30∘ are special, and its trigonometric ratios have the values cos⁡60∘=12,sin⁡30∘=12,tan⁡30∘=33.

Therefore, 6sec⁡60∘+3csc⁡30∘+cot⁡30∘=6(1cos⁡60∘)+3(1sin⁡30∘)+1tan⁡30∘=6cos⁡60∘+3sin⁡30∘+1tan⁡30∘=6(12)+3(12)+1(33)=12+6+33=18+33=18+33⋅33=18+3.
