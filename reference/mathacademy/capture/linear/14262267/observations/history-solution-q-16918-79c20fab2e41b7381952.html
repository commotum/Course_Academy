The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence (in function notation) is

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d.

From the first few terms

2,11,20,…,

we see that the first term is f(1)=2 and the common difference is d=11−2=9.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d=2+(n−1)(9)=2+9n−9=9n−7

Therefore, the formula for the nth term is f(n)=9n−7.
