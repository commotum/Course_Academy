We start with the ratio 32:2 and write it as a fraction:

322

32÷22÷2=161

So the numerator represents the area of the fence in m2 (16), and the denominator represents the volume of paint in liters (1).

16×51×5=805

So, Liam needs 5 liters of paint for 80 square meters of the fence.
