We need to factor the following expression: −12a2−6a+6

First, notice that all the terms have a common factor of −6. So, we can simplify the calculation by first factoring out −6: −6(2a2+a−1)

Now, we just need to factor the trinomial 2a2+a−1 in parentheses. To do this, we find two numbers that

  • multiply to the product of the quadratic coefficient and the constant (which is 2×(−1)=−2), and

  • add to the linear coefficient (which is 1).

The factor pairs of −2 are (1,−2),(−1,2).

Notice the numbers in the factor pair (−1,2) add to 1: −1+2=1✓

Then, we write the linear term as −a+2a, and factor the result by grouping: 2a2+a−1=2a2−a+2a−1=(2a2−a)+(2a−1)=a(2a−1)+(2a−1)=(a+1)(2a−1)

Therefore, the original trinomial factors as follows: −12a2−6a+6=−6(2a2+a−1)=−6(a+1)(2a−1)
