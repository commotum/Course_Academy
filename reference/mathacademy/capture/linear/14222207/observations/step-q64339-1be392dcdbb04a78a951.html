The diagram above shows the unit circle. Given that the x-coordinate of the point P is 22, what is the measure of the angle θ?

a
45∘
b
50∘
c
75∘
d
40∘
e
30∘

Correct
EXPLANATION

Any point (x,y) on the unit circle is related to the central angle θ as follows:

x=cos⁡θ,y=sin⁡θ

We're given that x=22 at the point P. Therefore, we have

cos⁡θ=22.

We compute the angle θ using the inverse cosine:

θ=arccos⁡(22)=45∘.
