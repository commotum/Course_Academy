The diagram above shows a unit circle. Rounding to two significant figures, the x-coordinate of the point P is
0.94
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Any point (x,y) on the unit circle is related to the central angle θ as follows: x=cos⁡θ,y=sin⁡θ

We're given that θ=20∘ at the point P. Therefore, we have x=cos⁡20∘=0.93969…≈0.94.
