To plot y=−2x−2, we apply the following steps:

  • Take the curve y=−1x.
  • Translate it by 2 units to the right to get the graph of y=−1x−2.
  • Then, stretch the result by a scale factor of 2 parallel to the y-axis to get the graph of y=−2x−2.
