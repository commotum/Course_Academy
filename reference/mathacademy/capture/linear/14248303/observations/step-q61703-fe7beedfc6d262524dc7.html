A soccer club started with 120 members. The number of members in the club then decreased by 15 every year. Which expression gives approximately the number of members after 3 years?

a
1203⋅(45)
b
120⋅(15)3
c
120⋅(65)3
d
120⋅(45)3
e
1203⋅(15)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the number of members reduced by 15 every year, it means that 45 of members remained every year.

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, there were 120⋅(45) members.

  • After 2 years, there were approximately 120⋅(45)2 members.

  • After 3 years, there were approximately 120⋅(45)3 members.
