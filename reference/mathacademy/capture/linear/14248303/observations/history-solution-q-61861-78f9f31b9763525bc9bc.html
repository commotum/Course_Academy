The number 7% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.07. Therefore, a decrease of 7% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.07=0.93

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, the value of the investment was 5600⋅(0.93) dollars.

  • After 2 years, the value of the investment was 5600⋅(0.93)2 dollars.

  • After 3 years, the value of the investment was 5600⋅(0.93)3 dollars.
