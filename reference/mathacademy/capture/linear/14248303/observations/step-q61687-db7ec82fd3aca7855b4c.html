A radioactive substance decays exponentially such that its mass reduces by 14 every hour. If the mass of the substance was initially 2kg, what is the mass of the substance after 9 hours?

a
29⋅(14)kg
b
2⋅(34)kg
c
29⋅(34)kg
d
2⋅(34)9kg
e
2⋅(14)9kg
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the mass reduces by 14, then 34 of its mass remains every hour.

Therefore:

  • After 1 hour, the mass is 2⋅(34).

  • After 2 hours, the mass is 2⋅(34)2.

  • After 3 hours, the mass is 2⋅(34)3.

  • …

Repeating this pattern, we see that the mass after 9 hours is 2⋅(34)9kg.
