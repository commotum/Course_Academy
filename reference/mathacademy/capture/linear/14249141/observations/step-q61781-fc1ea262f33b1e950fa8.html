Nash paints a box that has the form of a rectangular prism. What is the total area that Nash needs to paint if the box is 6cm long, 3cm wide, and 3cm tall?

a
90cm2
b
108cm2
c
72cm2
d
86cm2
e
54cm2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's consider the net of our box.

From the diagram, we have squares and rectangles.

The rectangles are 6cm long and 3cm high. So, the area of one rectangle is Area of the rectangle=6×3=18cm2.

The squares have sides of length 3cm. Hence, the area of one square is Area of the square=3×3=9cm2.

Finally, the surface area of the bar consists of 4 rectangles and 2 squares.

Therefore, we get

Surface area=4×(Area of the rectangle)+2×(Area of the square)=4×18+2×9=72+18=90cm2.
