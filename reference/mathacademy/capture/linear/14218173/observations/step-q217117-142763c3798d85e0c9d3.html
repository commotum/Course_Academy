EXPLANATION

The recursive formula is f(n+1)=7−3f(n), where f(1)=1.

We use this formula to compute the terms f(2) and f(3) as follows:

  • To compute f(2), we substitute n=1 into the recursive formula: f(n+1)=7−3f(n)f(1+1)=7−3f(1)f(2)=7−3f(1)f(2)=7−3(1)f(2)=4

  • To compute f(3), we substitute n=2 into the recursive formula: f(n+1)=7−3f(n)f(2+1)=7−3f(2)f(3)=7−3f(2)f(3)=7−3(4)f(3)=−5

Therefore, f(3)=−5.
