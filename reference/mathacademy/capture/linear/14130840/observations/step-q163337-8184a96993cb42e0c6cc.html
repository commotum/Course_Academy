A dumbbell in a gym has a mass of 1kg. What is this mass in grams?

a
0.1g
b
1000g
c
100g
d
10g
e
0.001g
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A table showing the units of mass in metric units is given below.

Name Symbol Conversion
Kilogram kg 1kg=1000g
Gram g Base Unit
Milligram mg 1000mg=1g

So, 1kg is equivalent to 1000g.
