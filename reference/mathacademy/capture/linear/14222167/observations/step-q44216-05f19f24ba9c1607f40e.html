EXPLANATION

First, we move all of the terms to the left-hand side and collect the like terms: 100x2=7100x2−7=0

Now, using the difference of squares formula, we factor the left-hand side of our quadratic equation: 100x2−7=0(10x)2−(7)2=0(10x−7)(10x+7)=0

Applying the zero product rule, we have 10x−7=0or10x+7=0.

Then, we solve each equation separately:

10x−7=010x+7=010x=710x=−7x=710x=−710

Therefore, the solutions are x=−710,710. We can write this even more concisely as x=±710.
