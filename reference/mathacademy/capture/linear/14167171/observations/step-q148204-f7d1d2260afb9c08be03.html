It takes Douglas 20 minutes to shear 5 sheep. How many minutes will it take to shear 9 sheep?

a
36 minutes
b
34 minutes
c
38 minutes
d
32 minutes
e
40 minutes
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine the number of minutes it will take Douglas to shear 9 sheep, we first find the unit rate of minutes per sheep and then multiply it by 9 sheep.

To find the unit rate of minutes per sheep, we divide the number of minutes, 20, by the number of sheep, 5, as follows: 20÷5=4

So, the unit rate is 4 minutes per sheep. To find the number of minutes it takes Douglas to shear 9 sheep, we multiply the unit rate by 9 sheep and get 4×9=36

Therefore, it takes Douglas 36 minutes to shear 9 sheep.
