Farhan can buy 2 slices of pizza for a dollar. If he has 14 dollars, then how many slices of pizza can he buy?

a
42 slices
b
28 slices
c
35 slices
d
24 slices
e
21 slices
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The unit rate is 2 slices per dollar. To find the number of slices Farhan can buy for 14 dollars, we multiply the unit rate by 14 dollars and get

2×14=28.

Therefore, Farhan can buy 28 slices for 14 dollars.
