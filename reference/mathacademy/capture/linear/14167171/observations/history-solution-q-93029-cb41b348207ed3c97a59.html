To find the number of kilometers that Sophie walks in 2 hours, we can first find the unit rate of kilometers per hour and multiply the unit rate by 2 hours.

To find the unit rate of kilometers per hour, we divide the number of kilometers, 35, by the number of hours, 110, as follows:

35÷110

Dividing one fraction by another is equivalent to multiplying the fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction:

35÷110=35×101=3×105×1=305=30÷5=6

So, the unit rate is 6 kilometers per hour. To find the number of kilometers that Sophie walks in 2 hours, we multiply the unit rate by 2 hours and get

6×2=12.

Therefore, Sophie walks 12 kilometers in 2 hours.
