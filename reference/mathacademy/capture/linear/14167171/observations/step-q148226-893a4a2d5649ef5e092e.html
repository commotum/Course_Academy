Laura can decorate 5 cakes in 52 hours. How many cakes can she decorate in 3 hours?

EXPLANATION

To determine the number of cakes Laura can decorate in 3 hours, we first find the unit rate of cakes per hour and then multiply it by 3 hours.

To find the unit rate of cakes per hour, we divide the number of cakes, 5, by the number of hours, 52, as follows: 5÷52

To divide by a fraction, we multiply by the fraction's reciprocal: 5÷52=5×25=51×25=5×21×5=105=10÷5=2

So, the unit rate is 2 cakes per hour. To determine the number of cakes Laura can decorate in 3 hours, we multiply the unit rate by 3 hours and get 2×3=6.

Therefore, Laura can decorate 6 cakes in 3 hours.
