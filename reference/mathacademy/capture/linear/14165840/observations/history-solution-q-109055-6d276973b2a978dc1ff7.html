We need to find two distinct points that lie on our line. Any two points will do!

  • For the first point, we will let the x-coordinate be x=0. Then the y-coordinate is y=2−12x=2−12(0)=2−0=2. So, the first point is (0,2).

  • For the second point, we will let the x-coordinate be x=8. Then the y-coordinate is y=2−12x=2−12(8)=2−4=−2. So, the second point is (8,−2).

Finally, we plot those two points on the xy-plane and draw a line through them. This is the graph of y=2−12x.
