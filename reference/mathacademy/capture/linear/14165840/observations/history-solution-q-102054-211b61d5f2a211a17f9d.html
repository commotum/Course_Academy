We need to find two distinct points that lie on our line. Any two points will do!

  • For the first point, we will let the x-coordinate be x=0. Then the y-coordinate is y=−2x=−2(0)=0. So, the first point is (0,0).

  • For the second point, we will let the x-coordinate be x=1. Then the y-coordinate is y=−2x=−2(1)=−2. So, the second point is (1,−2).

Finally, we plot those two points on the xy-plane and draw a line through them. This is the graph of y=−2x.
