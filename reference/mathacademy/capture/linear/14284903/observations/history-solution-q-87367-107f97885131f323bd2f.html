Let us call R the event "we get a prime number," and E the event "we get an even number." Then the probability of getting a prime number or an even number is P(R∪E), and we can apply the addition law:

P(R∪E)=P(R)+P(E)−P(R∩E)

First, let's find P(R), P(E), and P(R∩E).

  • There are 6 prime numbers among the 15 labels of the spinner (2,3,5,7,11,13), so we have P(R)=615.

  • There are 7 even numbers among the 15 labels of the spinner (2,4,6,8,10,12,14), so we have P(E)=715.

  • There is only a single number, 2, that is both prime and even. So, we have P(R∩E)=115.

Therefore, applying the addition law, we have

P(R∪E)=P(R)+P(E)−P(R∩E)=615+715−115=45.
