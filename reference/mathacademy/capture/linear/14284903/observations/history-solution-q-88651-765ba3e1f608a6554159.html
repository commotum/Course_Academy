Let's call G the event "a card labeled with a number greater than 7 is selected" and N the event "a non-spade card is selected."

The required probability is P(G∪N), which we can find using the addition law P(G∪N)=P(G)+P(N)−P(G∩N).

Let's find P(G) and P(N).

  • There are 12 cards labeled with a number greater than 7 in a standard deck. So, we have P(G)=1252=313.

  • There are 39 non-spade cards in a standard deck. So, we have P(N)=3952=34.

  • There are 9 non-spade cards labeled with a number greater than 7. Indeed, the cards labeled with a number greater than 7 are 8, 9, and 10, giving 3 such cards per suit. Since there are 3 non-spade suits, we have

P(G∩N)=952.

Finally, substituting all of the known information into the addition law, we get P(G∪N)=P(G)+P(N)−P(G∩N)=313+34−952=2126.
