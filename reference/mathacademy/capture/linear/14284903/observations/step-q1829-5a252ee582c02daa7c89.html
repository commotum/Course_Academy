A card is drawn at random from a standard 52-card deck. What is the probability of drawing a card labeled with a number less than 4 or a spade card?

*Hint: A standard 52-card deck has 4 suits with 13 cards in each suit. Spades is one of the suits. Number cards are the cards labeled 2 through 10 in each suit.

EXPLANATION

Let's call A the event "a card labeled with a number less than 4 is selected" and B the event "a spade card is selected."

The required probability is P(A∪B), which we can find using the addition law P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B).

Let's find P(A) and P(B).

  • There are 2⋅4=8 cards labeled with a number less than 4 in a standard deck. So, we have P(A)=852=213.

  • There are 13 spade cards in a standard deck. So, we have P(B)=1352=14.

  • There are 2 spade cards labeled with a number less than 4 (the 2 of spades and the 3 of spades). Therefore,

P(A∩B)=252=126.

Finally, substituting all of the known information into the addition law, we get P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)=213+14−126=1952.
