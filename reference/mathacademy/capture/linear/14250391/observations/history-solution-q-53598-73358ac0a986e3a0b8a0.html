A similarity transformation is any transformation made up of rigid motions (rotations, reflections, and translations) and dilations.

A similarity transformation will map any polygon to a similar polygon.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the transformations in turn.

  • Transformation I represents a stretch of factor 2 in the x-direction and a stretch of factor 4 in the y-direction. This is not a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation II represents a dilation of scale factor 3 and center O. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation III represents a reflection in the x-axis. Thus, it is a rigid motion and, therefore, a similarity transformation.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
