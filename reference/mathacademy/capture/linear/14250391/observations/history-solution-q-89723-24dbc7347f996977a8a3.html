A similarity transformation is any transformation made up of rigid motions (rotations, reflections, and translations) and dilations.

A similarity transformation will map any polygon to a similar polygon.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the transformations in turn.

  • Transformation I represents a translation by 3 units to the left and 1 unit upward. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation II represents a dilation of scale factor 2 and center O. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation III represents a rotation by 90∘ clockwise around the point O. Thus, it is a rigid motion and, therefore, a similarity transformation.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
