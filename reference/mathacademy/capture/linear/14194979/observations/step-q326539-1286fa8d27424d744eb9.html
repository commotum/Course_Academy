At a furniture store, all tables are discounted by 25% during a weekend sale. What is the sale price of a table that originally costs $64?

a
$48
b
$40
c
$49
d
$56
e
$52
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find an amount that's 25% less than $64, we apply a new percentage of

100%−25%=75%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

75%=75100=0.75

So, to find 75% of $64, we multiply $64 by 0.75:

0.75⋅$64=$48

Therefore, the sale price of the table is $48.
