EXPLANATION

To find a number 15% less than 100, we apply a new percentage of

100%−15%=85%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

85%=85100=0.85

So, to find a number that is 85% of 100, we multiply 100 by 0.85:

0.85⋅100=85
