An office chair is marked down by 15% during a promotion. What is the sale price of the chair if its original price is $85?

a
$74.25
b
$72.25
c
$71.50
d
$73.00
e
$70.00
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find an amount that's 15% less than $85, we apply a new percentage of

100%−15%=85%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

85%=85100=0.85

So, to find 85% of $85, we multiply $85 by 0.85:

0.85⋅$85=$72.25

Therefore, the sale price of the chair is $72.25.
