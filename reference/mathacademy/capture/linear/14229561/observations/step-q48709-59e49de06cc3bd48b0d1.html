The function V(t) models the number of visitors, in thousands, to a new website, t hours after the website's launch. What is the best interpretation of the statement below?

The slope of the tangent line to the graph of V(t) at t=10 is equal to 1.5.

a
10 hours after launching, the number of visitors decreased by a total of 1500 visitors
b
10 hours after launching, the number of visitors increased at a rate of 1500 visitors per hour
c
10 hours after launching, the number of visitors increased at a constant rate of 150 visitors per hour
d
10 hours after launching, the number of visitors increased by a total of 1500 visitors
e
10 hours after launching, the number of visitors decreased at a rate of 1500 visitors per hour
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The slope of the line tangent to the graph of V(t) at t=10 gives the derivative of V(t) at t=10. So, we have that

V′(10)=1.5.

We can interpret the above statement as

10 hours after launching, the number of visitors increased at a rate of 1500 visitors per hour.
