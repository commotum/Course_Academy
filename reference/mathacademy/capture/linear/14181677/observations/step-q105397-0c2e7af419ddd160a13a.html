The magnitude of a force F applied to an object is related to the work W and the distance d by the formula F=Wd. Given that W is measured in kgm2s2 and the distance is measured in meters, determine an appropriate measurement unit for the force.

a
 kgsm
b
 kgms2
c
 kgms2
d
 kgms
e
 kgs2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the unit of the force, we substitute the given units of work and distance into the formula and simplify. F=Wd=(kgm2s2)m=1m⋅(kgm2s2)=kg⋅m2m⋅s2=kg⋅m⋅mm⋅s2=kgms2

Thus, the unit of the force is kgms2.
