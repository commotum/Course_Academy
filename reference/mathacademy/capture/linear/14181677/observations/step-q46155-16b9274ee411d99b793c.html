The work applied to an object is the result of multiplying the force by the distance. Given that the force is measured in Newtons (kgms2), determine the unit of work in terms of kilograms, meters and seconds.

a
kgm2s
b
kgms
c
kgm2s2
d
kgm2s2
e
kgms
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The force is measured in Newtons (kgms2) and distance is measured in meters (m). Since the work is the multiplication of force by distance, we must multiply force units by distance units. Thus, we have

kgms2⋅m=kgm2s2.

Thus, the unit of work is kgm2s2.
