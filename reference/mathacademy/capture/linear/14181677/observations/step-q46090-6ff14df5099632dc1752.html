A printer prints 1250 letters in 60 minutes. To determine the number of letters that the printer prints on average, the owner uses the formula N=lt, where l is the number of letters printed and t the time in minutes.

In words, what is the appropriate measurement unit for N?

a
Letters per squared minute
b
Minutes per letter
c
Cubic letters per minute
d
Letters per minute
e
Letters per cubic minute
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are solving for measurement units, so we can ignore the quantities 1250 and 60. They do not matter, if we are just solving for units.

Since l represents letters and t represents minute, the appropriate measurement unit for N=lt is lettersminute. In words, this is 'letters per minute'.
