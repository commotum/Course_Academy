EXPLANATION

The parameter C represents a horizontal shift of the function.

First, we rewrite the function by factoring the horizontal stretch factor, as follows: y=tan⁡(2x+C)=tan⁡(2(x+C2))

The factor x+C2 indicates that the graph of y=tan⁡(2(x+C2)) is a horizontal translation of y=tan⁡(2x). Comparing the two graphs below allows us to determine the amount of this shift.

From the diagram, we see that our curve has been obtained by shifting y=tan⁡(2x) by π12 units to the left. Therefore C2=π12⟹C=π6.
