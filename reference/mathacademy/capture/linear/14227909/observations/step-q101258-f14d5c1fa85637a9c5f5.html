What is the recursive formula for the following geometric sequence? 20,15,454,…

an+1=34an,a1=20,n≥1

an+1=38an,a1=20,n≥1

Correct
EXPLANATION

The recursive formula for a geometric sequence with common ratio r takes the form an+1=r⋅an.

In this case, the first term is a1=20, and we can calculate the common ratio r as

r=a2a1=1520=34.

Consequently, the recursive formula for this sequence is

an+1=34an,a1=20,n≥1.
