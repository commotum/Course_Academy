We need to find the difference in the population of condors between t=2 and t=3 decades. To calculate this, we find the population after 2 decades, and we subtract the population after 3 decades. So we need to evaluate

C(2)−C(3).

The number of condors after t=2 decades is equal to

C(2)=1000000⋅(0.25)2=1000000⋅0.0625=62500.

The number of condors after t=3 decades is equal to C(3)=1000000⋅(0.25)3=1000000⋅0.015625=15625.

So, the number of condors lost between the second and third decades is

C(3)−C(2)=62500−15625=46875.

Therefore, 46875 condors vanished between the second and third decades.
