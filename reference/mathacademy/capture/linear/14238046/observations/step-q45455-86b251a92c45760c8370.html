The market value of a sports car, in dollars, can be modeled according to the rule 30000⋅(0.85)t, where t is the number of years since the car was manufactured. What does the base of the exponential expression (0.85) mean in this context?

a
The value of the car depreciates by 15% each year
b
The car increases its value by 85% each year
c
The value of the car depreciates by 85% each year
d
The initial price of the car is $25500
e
The car increases its value by 15% each year
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The given expression is an exponential decay expression of the form y(t)=a⋅(1−r)t, where:

  • a=30000 represents the initial value of the car (in dollars).

  • 1−r=0.85, where r is the decay rate.

  • t is the time since the start of the observations, in years.

Solving for r, we get

1−r=0.85⟹r=0.15.

Now, 0.15 (expressed as a percent) is equal to 15%.

So, the base of the exponential expression 0.85 means that the value of the car depreciates by 15% each year.
