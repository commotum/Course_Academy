  2. A rectangle is always a quadrilateral.
  3. A square is never a rhombus.

EXPLANATION

First, recall the hierarchy diagram of quadrilaterals, where each shape below another is a specific type of the one above it.

Let's now look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. In a rectangle, its four angles are right angles. The same is true for a square. A square is a specific type of rectangle, where the four angles are right angles and the four sides are congruent.

  • Statement II is true. A rectangle is always a quadrilateral because it has four sides.

  • Statement III is false. A rhombus is a parallelogram where all sides are congruent. A square is a specific type of rhombus, where the four angles are right angles and the four sides are congruent.

In conclusion, only statement II is true.
