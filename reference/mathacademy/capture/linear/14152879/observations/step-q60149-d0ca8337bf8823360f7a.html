There are 60 peaches in Sophia's store. The ratio of peaches to pears is 6:5. How many pears are there in Sophia's store?

a
50
b
45
c
40
d
48
e
42

Correct
EXPLANATION

We start with the ratio 6:5 and write it as a fraction:

65

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of peaches and the denominator represents the number of pears.

We can make a numerator of 60 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 10:

6×105×10=6050

Therefore, there are 50 pears in Sophia's store.
