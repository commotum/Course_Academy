Sophie is baking a pie. The recipe for the pie dough states that the ratio of sugar to melted butter is 32. If she decides to use 4 cups of melted butter, how many cups of sugar does she need to use?

a
9
b

c
6
d

Correct
EXPLANATION

We are given that the ratio of sugar to melted butter is 32.

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of cups of sugar and the denominator represents the number of cups of melted butter.

We can make a denominator of 4 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 2:

3×22×2=64

Therefore, for 4 cups of melted butter, Sophie must use 6 cups of sugar.
