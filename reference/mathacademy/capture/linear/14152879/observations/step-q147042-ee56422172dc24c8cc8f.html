The ratio of T-shirts to pairs of jeans in David's closet is 52. If David has 4 pairs of jeans in his closet, how many T-shirts does David have?

a
12
b
15
c
10
d
24
e
40
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The ratio of T-shirts to pairs of jeans in David's closet is 52.

In this fraction, the numerator represents the number of T-shirts and the denominator represents the number of pairs of jeans.

We can make a denominator of 4 by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 2:

5×22×2=104

Therefore, David has 10 T-shirts in his closet.
