Notice that ∠W≅∠Z.

Let's determine the vertices in the second pentagon that correspond to Z. First, notice the following:

YZ=MNZV=NJ∠Z≅∠N

Therefore,

  • vertex Z corresponds to vertex N,

  • consequently, vertex W corresponds to vertex K.

As for the other three vertices, notice we have two more pairs of congruent angles:

∠X≅∠L∠Y≅∠M.

Similarly, we have two more pairs of congruent sides:

XY=MLWX=LK.

Therefore,

  • vertex X corresponds to vertex L,

  • vertex Y corresponds to vertex M, and

  • vertex V corresponds to vertex J because they both correspond to the right angle.

Therefore, we conclude that a correct congruence statement is VWXYZ≅JKLMN.
