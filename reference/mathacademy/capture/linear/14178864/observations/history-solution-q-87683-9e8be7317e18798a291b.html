In general, congruent polygons have the same shape. In other words:

  • they have the same number of sides,

  • all corresponding sides are congruent, and

  • all corresponding interior angles are congruent.

With that in mind, let's compare the given polygons.

  • Statement I is false. Notice that the polygons C and V have the same number of sides, but they don't seem to have the same shape. In particular, we notice they don't have corresponding congruent sides.

  • Statement II is false. By the same reasoning as statement I, we can see that the polygons V and B are not congruent.

  • Statement III is true. Since the polygons B and C have the same number of sides and all corresponding sides and angles are congruent, we conclude that B≅C.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
