EXPLANATION

We have four pairs of congruent angles: ∠R≅∠F∠S≅∠E∠T≅∠H∠U≅∠G.

Similarly, we have four pairs of congruent sides:

RS=FEST=EHTU=HGUR=GF.

Since all corresponding sides and angles are congruent, the two quadrilaterals are congruent. In particular

  • vertex R corresponds to vertex F,

  • vertex S corresponds to vertex E,

  • vertex T corresponds to vertex H, and

  • vertex U corresponds to vertex G.

Therefore, we conclude that a correct congruence statement is RSTU≅FEHG.
