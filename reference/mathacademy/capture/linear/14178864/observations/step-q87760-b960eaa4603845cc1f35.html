MN=DEMP=DG∠M≅∠D

Therefore,

  • vertex M corresponds to vertex D,

  • consequently, vertex O corresponds to vertex F.

As for the other two vertices, notice we have two more pairs of congruent angles:

∠N≅∠E∠P≅∠G.

Similarly, we have two more pairs of congruent sides:

Therefore,

  • vertex N corresponds to E, and
