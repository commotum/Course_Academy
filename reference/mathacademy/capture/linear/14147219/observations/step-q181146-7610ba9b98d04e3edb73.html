EXPLANATION

Here, we are dividing a negative number (−11) by a positive one (3). So the result will be negative: (−11)÷3=−3

Now, to compute the number that goes in the box, we divide 11 by 3 without any signs: 11÷3=113. Therefore, we get (−11)÷3=−113.
