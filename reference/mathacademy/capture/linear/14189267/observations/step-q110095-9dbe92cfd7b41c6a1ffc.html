EXPLANATION

For two lines to be parallel, they must have the same slope.

  • The line y=−3x+1 is in slope-intercept form, and its slope is −3.

  • To find the slope of the line kx−2y=12, we convert to slope-intercept form: kx−2y=12−2y=−kx+122y=kx−12y=k2x−6 In slope-intercept form, the slope is the x-coefficient. So, the slope is k2.

Since the slopes of the two lines must be equal, we must have k2=−3. Consequently, k=−6.
