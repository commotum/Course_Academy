The lines y=23x+4 and y=mx+2 are parallel. What is the value of m?
m=
23​
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

For two lines to be parallel, they must have the same slope.

Both lines are in slope-intercept form, so we can pick out the slope of each equation by looking at the x-coefficient.

  • The slope of the line y=23x+4 is 23.

  • The slope of the line y=mx+2 is m.

Because the slopes must be the same, we must have m=23.
