The graph above shows the image of the graph y=|x| under the action of a single translation. What is the equation of this curve?
The equation of this curve is y=
|x|−8
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that y=|x|+a is a vertical translation of the absolute value graph y=|x| by a units up.

Here, the graph is moved down by 8 units. This corresponds to a=−8.

Therefore, the equation of the curve is y=|x|−8.
