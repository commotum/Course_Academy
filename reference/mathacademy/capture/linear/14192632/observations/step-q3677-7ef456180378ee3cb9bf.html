What is the equation of the curve shown above?

a
y=2|x|
b
y=|x−2|
c
y=|x+2|
d
y=|x|−2
e
y=|x|+2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that the graph of y=|x−a| is a horizontal translation of the absolute value graph y=|x| by a units right.

Here, the graph is moved right by 2 units. This corresponds to a=2. So, the equation of the graph is y=|x−2|.
