If y=|x−4|+3, what are the coordinates of the minimum point on the graph?

a
(3,4)
b
(4,3)
c
(−4,3)
d
(4,−3)
e
(−3,4)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that y=|x−h|+k is a vertical translation of the absolute value graph y=|x| by h units right and k units up.

The function y=|x−4|+3 has h=4 and k=3. Therefore, it is a translation of the absolute value graph y=|x| by 4 units right and 3 units up.

Looking at the graph, we can see that the coordinates of the minimum point are (4,3).
