The first term of a geometric sequence is 1, and the common ratio is −2. The 6th term of this sequence is
−32
.

Correct
EXPLANATION

The first term is a1=1, and the common ratio is r=−2, so we can write the formula for the nth term of the sequence:

an=a1⋅rn−1=1⋅(−2)n−1

To find the 6th term, we can use this formula with n=6:

an=1⋅(−2)n−1a6=1⋅(−2)6−1=1⋅(−2)5=1⋅(−32)=−32

Therefore, the 6th term is a6=−32.
