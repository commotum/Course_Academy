A tree sapling grows by 2 centimeters per day. Assuming that one year has 365 days, how many meters does the tree grow in one year?

a
8.1 meters
b
6.5 meters
c
7.3 meters
d
6.9 meters
e
7.8 meters
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are given the units cmday, and we want to convert this into myear. We're converting two units instead of just one, so we're going to have to multiply by two conversion factors: cmday⋅??⋅??=myear

One of the conversion factors should change cm to m. cmday⋅mcm⋅??=myear

The other conversion factor should change day into year. cmday⋅mcm⋅daysyear=myear

So, our conversion formula is cmday⋅mcm⋅daysyear=myear.

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use, and apply it.

  • The rate given in the problem statement is 2cmday.
  • There are 100 centimeters in one meter, therefore, there are 1100 meters in one centimeter.
  • There are 365 days in one year.

Applying our conversion formula, we have 2cmday=2cmday⋅1100mcm⋅365daysyear=2⋅1100⋅365(cmday⋅mcm⋅daysyear)=7.3myear.

Consequently, 2 centimeters per day is equivalent to 7.3 meters per year. In one year, the tree grows by 7.3 meters.
