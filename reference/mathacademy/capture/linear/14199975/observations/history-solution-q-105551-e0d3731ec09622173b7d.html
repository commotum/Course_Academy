We are given the units cms, and we want to convert this into ms. To figure out what we need to multiply by, we can set up an equation to help us: cms⋅??=ms

We want to replace cm with m. To get rid of cm, we can put cm in the denominator so that it cancels out. To introduce m, we can put m in the numerator. cms⋅mcm=ms

So, our conversion formula is cms⋅mcm=ms.

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use, and apply it.

  • The speed given in the problem statement is 972cms.
  • There are 100 centimeters per meter. Taking the reciprocal, there are 1100 meters per centimeter.

Applying our conversion formula, we have 972cms=972cms⋅1100mcm=972⋅1100(cms⋅mcm)=9.72ms.

Thus, the speed of the cruise is 9.72m/s.
