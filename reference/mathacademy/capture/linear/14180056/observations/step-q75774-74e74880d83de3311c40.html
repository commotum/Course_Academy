Sophie reserves a hotel room. The price of the room is T dollars and, additionally, she must pay a 9% luxury tax.

Which expression gives the total cost, in dollars, of the hotel room after tax is applied?

a
0.09T
b
T+0.9T
c
T+0.09T
d
T−0.09T
e
T+0.09
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The price before taxes of a hotel room is T dollars.

The amount of taxes Sophie pays is 9% of the price of the hotel room, or 0.09T.

Therefore, the expression that represents the total cost (in dollars) of the hotel room after luxury tax is:

T+0.09T
