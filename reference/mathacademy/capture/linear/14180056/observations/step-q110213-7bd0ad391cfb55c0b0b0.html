The fee a plumber charges for a callout is given by the expression 45h+50, where h is the time, in hours, it takes him to complete the job.

What does the constant term of the expression represent in this context?

a
The plumber charges $50 per hour.
b
The plumber's fee for working zero hours is $50.
c
The plumber charges $45 per hour.
d
The plumber charges $95 per hour.
e
The plumber's hourly rate.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The expression tells us that the plumber charges $45 every hour, plus a 'base fee' of $50.

The constant term is 50. This represents the fee (in dollars) the plumber charges for working zero hours.
