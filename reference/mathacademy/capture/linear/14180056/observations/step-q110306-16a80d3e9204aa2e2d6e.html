The retail price of a particular model of cell phone, at Jordan's Electronics, is C dollars and the purchaser also has to pay an 11% tax.

Which expression represents the total cost, in dollars, of the cell phone after tax is applied?

a
C+1.1C
b
C+0.11
c
C+11
d
C+11C
e
C+0.11C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The price of the cell phone before taxes is C dollars.

The amount of tax the purchaser has to pay is 11% of the price of a phone or 0.11C dollars.

Therefore, the expression that represents the total cost (in dollars) of the cell phone after tax is:

C+0.11C.
