In a cinema, a snack combo consists of four hot dogs, two medium fries, and two medium soft drinks. The cost of one hot dog is H dollars, the cost of one portion of fries is F dollars and the cost of one drink is D dollars. Alan is purchasing some combos for his friends and the total bill comes to 5(4H+2F+2D) dollars.

What does the factor 5 mean in this context?

a
Each combo costs 5(4H+2F+2D) dollars
b
Alan purchased 5 combos in total
c
Alan has purchased 5(4H+2F+2D) combos in total
d
Each hot dog costs $5
e
Alan has purchased 4H+2F+2D hot dogs in total
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A hot dog costs H dollars, one medium fries costs F dollars, and a medium soft drink costs D dollars. Thus, the cost, in dollars, of a combo is

4H+2F+2D.

Alan's bill, in dollars, comes to 5(4H+2F+2D).

Hence, the factor 5 must be the total number of combos that he's purchased.
