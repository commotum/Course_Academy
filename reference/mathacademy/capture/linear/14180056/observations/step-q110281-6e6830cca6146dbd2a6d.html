Mike is going to buy a baseball bat. The price of the bat is P dollars, and Mike also has to pay a tax of 12%.

Which expression represents the total cost of the bat, in dollars, after applying the tax?

a
P+12
b
P+0.12P
c
P+1.2P
d
P+0.12
e
P+12P
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The price of the bat before taxes is P dollars.

The amount of tax Mike has to pay is 12% of the price of the bat, or 0.12P.

Therefore, the expression that represents the total cost (in dollars) of the bat after tax is:

P+0.12P
