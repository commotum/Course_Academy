Find the y-intercept of AB↔, and express the equation of this line in slope-intercept form.
The y-intercept of the line is b=
−7
The equation is y=
3x−7
Submit
Correct
Explanation

We will write the equation of the line in the slope-intercept form

y=mx+b.

As we found previously, the slope is m=3.

Substituting m=3 into the slope-intercept form above, we get

y=3x+b.

We now need to find the y-intercept b. We can find this by substituting the coordinates of a point on the line. Any point will do, so let's substitute (1,−4):

y=3x+b−4=3(1)+b−4=3+b−7=bb=−7

Therefore, the equation of the line is

y=3x−7.
