Previously we found that the line AB↔ has the equation

y=3x−7.

Now, we need to decide which inequality symbol to use.

  • Since the line is dashed, we will use a strict inequality.

  • Since the shaded region is above the line, the inequality refers to the y-values that are greater than those on the line.

Therefore, the corresponding inequality is

y>3x−7.
