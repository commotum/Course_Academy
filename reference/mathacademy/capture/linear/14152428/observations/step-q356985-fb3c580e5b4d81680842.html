Let u=4 and v=−9. What point on the number line corresponds to u+v?

a
It's the number 5 located 9 units to the left of 4.
b
It's the number −5 located 9 units to the right of 4.
c
It's the number −5 located 9 units to the left of 4.
d
It's the number −5 located 4 units to the right of 9.
e
It's the number 5 located 9 units to the right of 4.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The value of u+v is the number located a distance |v| from u on the number line,

  • moving to the right if v is positive, and

  • moving to the left if v is negative.

In our situation:

  • We start at u=4 on the number line.

  • Here, v=−9, so the distance we move is |v|=9.

  • Because v is negative, we move 9 units to the left from 4.

This point corresponds to the number 4+(−9)=−5.
