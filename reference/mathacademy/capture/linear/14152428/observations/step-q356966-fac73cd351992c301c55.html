A bank account had an initial balance of $0. During the day, there was a deposit of $20. Then there was a withdrawal of $20 later. What is the resulting amount in the account?

a
$0
b
$20
c
−$40
d
$40
e
−$20
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

In our situation:

  • During the day, the account had a deposit of $20, which is a change of +$20.

  • Later, the account had a withdrawal of $20, which is a change of −$20.

These two changes are opposite quantities. When opposite quantities combine, they make 0: 20+(−20)=0

Therefore, the balance returns to its original value of $0.
