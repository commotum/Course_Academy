Let p=1 and q=−6. What point on the number line corresponds to p+q?

a
It's the number −5 located 6 units to the right of 1.
b
It's the number 5 located 6 units to the left of 1.
c
It's the number 5 located 6 units to the right of 1
d
It's the number −5 located 1 unit to the right of 6.
e
It's the number −5 located 6 units to the left of 1.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The value of p+q is the number located a distance |q| from p on the number line,

  • moving to the right if q is positive, and

  • moving to the left if q is negative.

In our situation:

  • We start at p=1 on the number line.

  • Here, q=−6, so the distance we move is |q|=6.

  • Because q is negative, we move 6 units to the left from 1.

This point corresponds to the number 1+(−6)=−5.
