The temperature inside an oven dropped by 25∘C when the door was opened. Then it rose by 25∘C when the door was closed.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The temperature is now lower than the initial value.
  2. The temperature returned to the initial value.
  3. The temperature is now larger than the initial value.
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
II only
d
III only
e
None
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In our situation:

  • When the door was opened, the temperature dropped by 25∘C, which is a change of −25∘C.

  • When the door was closed, the temperature rose by 25∘C, which is a change of +25∘C.

These two changes are opposite quantities. When opposite quantities combine, they make 0: −25+25=0

This means the total temperature change is 0∘C, so the temperature returns to its original value.

So, statements I and III are false, while statement II is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only".
