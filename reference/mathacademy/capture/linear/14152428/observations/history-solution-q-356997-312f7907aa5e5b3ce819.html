In our situation:

  • While flying up to a roof, the drone’s elevation rose by 85 m, which is a change of +85 m.

  • While descending back to the pilot, the drone’s elevation dropped by 85 m, which is a change of −85 m.

These two changes are opposite quantities. When opposite quantities combine, they make 0: 85+(−85)=0

This means the total elevation change is 0 m, so the drone returns to the original elevation.

So, statements I and III are false, while statement II is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only".
