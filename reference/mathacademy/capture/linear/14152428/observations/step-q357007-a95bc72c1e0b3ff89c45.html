Let p=−6 and q=9. What point on the number line corresponds to p+q?

a
It's the number 3 located 6 units to the right of 9.
b
It's the number 3 located 9 units to the right of −6.
c
It's the number 3 located 9 units to the left of −6.
d
It's the number −3 located 9 units to the left of −6.
e
It's the number −3 located 9 units to the right of −6.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The value of p+q is the number located a distance |q| from p on the number line,

  • moving to the right if q is positive, and

  • moving to the left if q is negative.

In our situation:

  • We start at p=−6 on the number line.

  • Here, q=9, so the distance we move is |q|=9.

  • Because q is positive, we move 9 units to the right from −6.

This point corresponds to the number −6+9=3.
