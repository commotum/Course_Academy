On the left side of the graph, we see that as the values of x decrease and approach −∞, the graph of the function grows without bound to ∞.

Consequently, f(x) approaches ∞ as x approaches −∞, and we have

limx→−∞f(x)=∞.

On the right side of the graph, as the values of x increase and approach ∞, the graph of the function moves closer and closer to the horizontal asymptote y=−2. Therefore,

limx→∞f(x)=−2.
