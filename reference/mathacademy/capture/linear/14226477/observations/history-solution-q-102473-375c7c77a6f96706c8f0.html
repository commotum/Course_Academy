On the left side of the graph, we see that as the values of x decrease and approach −∞, the graph of the function moves closer and closer to the horizontal asymptote y=0.

Consequently, f(x) approaches the value 0 as x approaches −∞, and we have

limx→−∞f(x)=0.

On the right side of the graph, as the values of x increase and approach ∞, the graph of the function grows without bound to ∞ as well. Therefore,

limx→∞f(x)=∞.
