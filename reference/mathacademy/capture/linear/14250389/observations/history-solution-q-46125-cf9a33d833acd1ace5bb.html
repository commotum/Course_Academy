We are given the units mmin, and we want to convert this into kmhour. We're converting two units instead of just one, so we're going to have to multiply by two conversion factors: mmin⋅??⋅??=kmhour

One of the conversion factors should change m to km. mmin⋅kmm⋅??=kmhour

The other conversion factor should change min into hour. mmin⋅kmm⋅minhour=kmhour

So, our conversion formula is mmin⋅kmm⋅minhour=kmhour.

Now that we have our conversion formula, we need to determine the correct numbers to use, and apply it.

  • The rate given in the problem statement is 1000mmin.
  • There are 1000 meters per kilometer, therefore, there are 11000 kilometers per meter.
  • There are 60 minutes in one hour.

Applying our conversion formula, we have 1000mmin=1000mmin⋅11000kmm⋅60minhour=1000⋅11000⋅60(mmin⋅kmm⋅minhour)=60kmhour.

Consequently, a speed of 1000 meters per minute is equivalent to 60 kilometers per hour. Thus, the car will travel 60 kilometers in one hour.
