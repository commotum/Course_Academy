We wish to compute the probability that a student plays violin given that they don't play guitar. Therefore, we need to compute P(V|G′).

To compute P(V|G′), we need to ask, "what is the probability of V occurring given that G has not occurred?"

Since we are assuming that G has not occurred, we only consider the students outside G.

In total, the number of students in the set G′ is

35+10=45.

Of these 45 students, 35 lie inside V.

Since each outcome is equally likely, P(V|G′) can be interpreted as the proportion of V that lies inside G′, relative to the size of G′. Therefore, we have

P(V|G′)=3545=79.
