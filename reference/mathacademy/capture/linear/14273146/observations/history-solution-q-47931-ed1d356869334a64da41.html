To compute P(Y|X), we need to ask, "what is the probability of Y occurring given that X has occurred?"

Since we are assuming that X has occurred, we only consider the events inside X.

The probability of event X occurring is

P(X)=0.42+0.18=0.6.

Now, P(Y|X) can be interpreted as the proportion of Y that lies inside X, relative to the size of X. So, we have

P(Y|X)=0.180.6=0.3.
