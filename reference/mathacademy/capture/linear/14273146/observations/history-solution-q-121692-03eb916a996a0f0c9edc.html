To compute P(B′|A), we need to ask, "what is the probability of B not occurring given that A has occurred?"

Since we are assuming that A has occurred, we only consider the elements inside A.

In total, the number of elements inside A is

8+2=10.

Of these 10 elements, 8 lie outside of B.

Since each outcome is equally likely, P(B′|A) can be interpreted as the proportion of B′ that lies inside A, relative to the size of A. Therefore, we have

P(B′|A)=810=45.
