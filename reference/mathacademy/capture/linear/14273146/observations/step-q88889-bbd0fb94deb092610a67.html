EXPLANATION

We wish to compute the probability that a student studies German given that they study French. Therefore, we need to compute P(G|F).

To compute P(G|F), we need to ask, "what is the probability of G occurring given that F has occurred?"

Since we are assuming that F has occurred, we only consider the events inside F.

The probability of event F occurring is

P(F)=0.3+0.2=0.5.

Now, P(G|F) can be interpreted as the proportion of G that lies inside F, relative to the size of F. So, we have

P(G|F)=0.30.5=0.6.
