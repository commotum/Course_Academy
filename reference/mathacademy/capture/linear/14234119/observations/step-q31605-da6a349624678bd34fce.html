A horizontal line l passes through the point (5,4). Calculate the coordinates of the y-intercept of l.

a
There is no y-intercept
b
(4,0)
c
(5,0)
d
(0,5)
e
(0,4)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

On any horizontal line, all points have the same y-coordinate.

The y-coordinate of the point (5,4) is 4, so the y-coordinate of the y-intercept is also 4.

Therefore, the coordinates of the y-intercept are (0,4).
