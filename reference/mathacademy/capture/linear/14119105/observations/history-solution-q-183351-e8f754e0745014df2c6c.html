Let's denote by P(x) and Q(x) the following predicates:

  • P(x): x is a multiple of 10.

  • Q(x): x is a multiple of 5.

Then, our initial sentence can be written as P(x)⇒Q(x), which is logically equivalent to

¬P(x)∨Q(x).

The negation of P(x) can be written as

  • ¬P(x): x is not is a multiple of 10.

Therefore, the given sentence is logically equivalent to the following:

x is not a multiple of 10, or x is a multiple of 5.
