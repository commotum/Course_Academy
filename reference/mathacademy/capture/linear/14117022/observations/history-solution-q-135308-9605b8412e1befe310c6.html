Recall that a steady-state vector of a stochastic matrix P is a stochastic vector q such that Pq=q.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, q is a stochastic vector since its entries are non-negative and add up to 1.

  • Statement II is false. Note that Pq=[0.30.80.70.2][0.10.9]=[0.03+0.720.07+0.18]=[0.750.25]≠q.

  • Statement III is false. Since Pq≠q, it is not a steady-state vector of P.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
