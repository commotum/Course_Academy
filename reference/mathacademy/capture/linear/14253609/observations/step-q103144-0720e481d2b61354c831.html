EXPLANATION

First, we find an angle that's coterminal with 9π2 that lies in the range [0,2π).

9π2−2π=5π25π2−2π=π2✓

Therefore, cot⁡(π2)=cot⁡(9π2).

The angle 90∘ is a quadrantal angle. Let's draw out the quadrantal angles on the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle π2 corresponds to the point (0,1).

  • Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡(π2)=0.

  • Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡(π2)=1.

Therefore, using the fact that cot⁡θ=cos⁡θsin⁡θ, we have cot⁡(π2)=cos⁡(π2)sin⁡(π2)=01=0.

Therefore, cot⁡(9π2)=0.
