The point P with coordinates (0,3) lies on the curve y=f(x). Which of the following points must lie on y=f(x−1)−2?

a
(3,0)
b
(−1,−1)
c
(1,1)
d
(−1,1)
e
(1,−1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The graph of y=f(x−1)−2 is a translation of the graph of y=f(x) by 1 unit to the right and 2 units down. So the y-coordinate decreases by 2 and the x-coordinate increases by 1.

We know that the point (0,3) lies on y=f(x). Therefore, the point (0+1,3−2)=(1,1) must lie on y=f(x−1)−2.
