The point P with coordinates (1,1) lies on the curve y=f(x). Which of the following points must lie on y=f(x−4)?

a
(−3,1)
b
(1,5)
c
(−1,1)
d
(1,−3)
e
(5,1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The graph of y=f(x−4) is a translation of the graph of y=f(x) by 4 units to the right. So the y-coordinate remains unchanged while the x-coordinate increases by 4.

We know that the point (1,1) lies on y=f(x). Therefore, the point (1+4,1)=(5,1) must lie on y=f(x−4).
