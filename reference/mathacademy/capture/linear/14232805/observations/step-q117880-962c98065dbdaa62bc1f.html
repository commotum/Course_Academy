Find an expression for the volume V of a cylinder in terms of its height h, given that the ratio of the radius of the base to the height of the cylinder is 8.

a
V=164πh3
b
V=8πh
c
V=18πh
d
V=8πh3
e
V=64πh3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Using the fact that the ratio of the radius r of the base to the height h is 8, we have that rh=8.

Our goal is to express V in terms of h. Therefore, we need to isolate r in the above equation: rh=8r=8h

The volume of a cylinder with radius r and height h is given by V=πr2h.

Therefore, substituting r=8h into the formula, we get V=πr2h=π(8h)2⋅h=π⋅64h2⋅h=64πh3.
