Calculate the volume of a cylinder given that the circumference of the base is 14πm and the ratio of its height to its radius is 149.

a
49πm3
b
πm3
c
7πm3
d
343πm3
e
14πm3
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, we find the radius r using the formula C=2πr, where C is the circumference, as follows:

C=2πr14π=2πrr=7m

To find the height h, we use the fact that the ratio of the height to the radius is 149: hr=149h7=149h=17m

The volume of a cylinder with radius r and height h is given by

V=πr2h.

Substituting r=7m and h=17m into the formula, we get

V=π⋅72⋅17=7πm3.
