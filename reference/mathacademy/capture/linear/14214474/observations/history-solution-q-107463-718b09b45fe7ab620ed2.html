First, we express y as the composite function

y=4u,u=2x+1.

Differentiating gives

dydu=4uln⁡(4),dudx=2.

We now apply the chain rule, and get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=4uln⁡(4)⋅2=2⋅42x+1ln⁡(4).

Now, to find the slope of the tangent to the curve at x=−1, we have to evaluate dydx at that point. dydx|x=−1=(2⋅42x+1ln⁡4)|x=−1=2⋅4−1ln⁡(4)=12ln⁡(4)=ln⁡((4)1/2)=ln(4)=ln⁡(2).

Also, the y-value of the point on the curve where x=x1=−1 is y1=(42x+1)|x=−1=42(−1)+1=4−1=14.

Finally, we find the equation of the tangent at that point:

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−14=ln⁡(2)⋅(x+1)y−14=ln⁡(2)x+ln⁡(2)y=ln⁡(2)x+ln⁡(2)+14
