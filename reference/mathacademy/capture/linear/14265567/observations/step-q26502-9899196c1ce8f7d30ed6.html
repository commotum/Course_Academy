An object accelerates with constant acceleration from 0m/s to 20m/s in 50 seconds. Find its acceleration.

a
1000m/s2
b
0.4m/s2
c
1.2m/s2
d
2.5m/s2
e
1m/s2

Correct
EXPLANATION

We can find the acceleration of an object by computing the slope of its speed-time graph. To plot a speed-time graph, we need two points.

  • The object starts at a speed of 0m/s. This corresponds to the point (0,0).

  • In 50 seconds, the car reaches a speed of 20m/s. This corresponds to the point (50,20).

Since the acceleration is constant, we plot the two points and draw a line through them as follows:

The slope of the object's speed-time graph gives its acceleration. The slope of this line is

m=20 m/s−0 m/s50 s−0 s=20 m/s50 s=0.4ms2.

Therefore, the acceleration of the object is 0.4m/s2.
