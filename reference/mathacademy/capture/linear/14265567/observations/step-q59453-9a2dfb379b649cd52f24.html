The speed-time graph shown above represents the motion of a car. At what times is the car traveling at a constant speed?

a
From 0s to 20s
b
From 0s to 50s
c
From 0s to 20s and from 50s to 70s
d
From 20s to 50s
e
From 50s to 70s
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The car is traveling at a constant speed when its acceleration is zero. In a speed-time graph, the acceleration is given by the slope of the line. So, we need to find the times for which the slope is zero.

We see from the graph that the slope is zero from t=20s to t=50s, because the graph is horizontal there. The car is traveling at a constant speed in this time interval.
