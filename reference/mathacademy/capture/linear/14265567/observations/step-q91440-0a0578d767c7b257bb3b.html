If the speed of an object is measured in feet/min, what are the units of its acceleration?

a
feetmin3
b
feetmin
c
feet2min2
d
feet2min
e
feetmin2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We can find the acceleration of an object by computing the slope of its speed-time graph. So, let's draw an example of a speed-time graph where the speed is measured in feet/min and the time is measured in min.

In this case, the slope is computed as

m=change in speedchange in time,

and so the units of the slope are as follows:

units of speedunits of time=feet/minmin=feetmin⋅1min=feetmin2

Therefore, the units of acceleration are feetmin2.
