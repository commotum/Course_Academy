The addition and constant multiple rules for derivatives give h′(x)=f′(x)−2g′(x).

As a result, we have that h′(3)=f′(3)−2g′(3).

Also, notice that x=3 corresponds to

  • the interval x∈(2,4) for the function f, and

  • the interval x∈(0,4) for the function g,

as shown below.

  • The slope of y=f(x) is constant on x∈(2,4) and equals ΔfΔx=−2−24−2=−2. Therefore, f′(3)=−2.

  • The slope of y=g(x) is constant on x∈(0,4) and equals ΔgΔx=−2−04−0=−12. Therefore, g′(3)=−12.

Finally, we have h′(3)=f′(3)−2g′(3)=−2−2⋅(−12)=−1.
