From the diagram above, which of the following statements is true?

a
s⊥w and u⊥v
b
v⊥t and v⊥w
c
u⊥t and v⊥t
d
u⊥t and u⊥w
e
s⊥t and w⊥t
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Suppose a and b are two lines. Then,

  • a∥b means a and b are parallel, and

  • a⊥b means a and b are perpendicular.

In the given diagram, there are two pairs of perpendicular lines:

  • s is perpendicular to t, which we write as s⊥t

  • w is perpendicular to t, which we write as w⊥t.

Therefore, the correct statement is s⊥t and w⊥t.
