To find this year's cereal production, we apply a 300% increase to last year's figure.

Increasing by 300% means that we must apply a new percentage of

100%+300%=400%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal: 400%=400100=4

Now, we multiply the original amount by 4: 900×4=3,600

So, the factory produced 3600 boxes of cereal this year.
