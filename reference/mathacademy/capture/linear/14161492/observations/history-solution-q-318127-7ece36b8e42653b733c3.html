To find a number 125% greater than 64, we apply a new percentage of

100%+125%=225%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

225%=225100=2.25

So, to find a number that is 125% greater than 64, we multiply 64 by 2.25:

2.25⋅64=144

Therefore, the number that is 125% greater than 64 is 144.
