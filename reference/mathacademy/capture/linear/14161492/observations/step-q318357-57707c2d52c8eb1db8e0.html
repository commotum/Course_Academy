What number is 155% greater than 40?

a
95
b
120
c
80
d
108
e
102
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find a number 155% greater than 40, we apply a new percentage of

100%+155%=255%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

255%=255100=2.55

So, to find a number that is 155% greater than 40, we multiply 40 by 2.55:

2.55⋅40=102

Therefore, the number that is 155% greater than 40 is 102.
