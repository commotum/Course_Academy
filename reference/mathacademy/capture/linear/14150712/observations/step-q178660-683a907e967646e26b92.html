EXPLANATION

The number that can be added to −4 to give 0 is the additive inverse of −4.

To find the additive inverse of the number −4, we change its sign by placing a minus symbol (−) in front of it. So, we obtain −(−4)=4.

To check our work, we simply add the inverse to the original number. If we're correct, we should get zero. (−4)+4=−4+4=0✓
