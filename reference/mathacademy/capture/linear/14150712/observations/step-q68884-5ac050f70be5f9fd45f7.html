EXPLANATION

To find the additive inverse of the number −82, we change its sign by placing a minus symbol (−) in front of it. So, we obtain

−(−82)=82.

To check our work, we simply add the inverse to the original number. If we're correct, we should get zero.

(−82)+82=−82+82=0✓
