EXPLANATION

The number that can be added to 23 to give 0 is the additive inverse of 23.

To find the additive inverse of the number 23, we change its sign by placing a minus symbol (−) in front of it. So, we obtain

−(23)=−23.

To check our work, we simply add the inverse to the original number. If we're correct, we should get zero.

23+(−23)=23−23=0✓
