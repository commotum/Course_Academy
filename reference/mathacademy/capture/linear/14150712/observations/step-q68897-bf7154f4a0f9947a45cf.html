EXPLANATION

The number that can be added to −12 to give 0 is the additive inverse of −12.

To find the additive inverse of the number −12, we change its sign by placing a minus symbol (−) in front of it. So, we obtain

−(−12)=12

To check our work, we simply add the inverse to the original number. If we're correct, we should get zero.

(−12)+12=−12+12=0✓
