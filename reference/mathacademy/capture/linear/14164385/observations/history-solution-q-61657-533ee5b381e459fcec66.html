The number of infected files y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 5%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

We have:

  • a=20 is the initial number of infected files.

  • r=0.05 is the growth rate (5% expressed as a decimal).

  • t= the time, in days.

Therefore, the correct expression for y is

y=20⋅(1+0.05)t=20⋅(1.05)t.

So, the missing number is 1.05.
