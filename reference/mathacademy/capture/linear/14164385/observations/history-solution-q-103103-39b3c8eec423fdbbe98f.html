The number of bacteria y(t) grows exponentially with a growth factor of 5.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of the growth factor b) is

y=abt.

We have:

  • a=7 is the initial number of bacteria.

  • b=5 is the growth factor (the number of bacteria increases fivefold every hour).

  • t= the time, in hours.

Therefore, the correct expression for y is

y=7⋅5t.

So, the missing number is 7.
