The number of views V(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 250%.

Let's write down what we know:

  • a=90 is the total number of views initially

  • r=2.5 is the growth rate (250% expressed as a decimal)

  • t= the time (in hours) after upload

The formula for exponential growth is

V=a(1+r)t.

Therefore, the correct expression for V(t) is

V=90⋅(1+2.5)t.

Simplifying the above, we get

V(t)=90⋅(3.5)t.

To find the total number of views after 3 hours, we evaluate the above for t=3:

V(3)=90⋅(3.5)3≈3859

Therefore, the estimated number of views after 3 hours is 3859.
