The number of bacteria y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 15%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

We have:

  • a=14 is the initial number of bacteria.

  • r=0.15 is the growth rate (15% expressed as a percent).

  • t= the time, in minutes.

Therefore, the correct expression for y(t) is

y(t)=14⋅(1+0.15)t=14⋅1.15t.
