Our goal is to write the equation in the form ax+by=c, where a,b, and c are integers with no common factors and a≥0.

We begin by moving all of the variables to the left-hand side and all of the constants to the right-hand side. This gives

−2x+y=−10

Now, we multiply both sides of the equation by −1:

−2x+y=−10(−1)⋅(−2x+y)=(−1)⋅(−10)2x−y=10.

Now, a,b, and c are integers with no common factors, and a≥0. So we are done!
