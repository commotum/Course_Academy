Explanation

To estimate the length of piping used for each of the 5 houses, we need to find an approximation for 104.7÷5.

So, we round the dividend to the nearest whole number: 104.7→105

Now, we divide 105 by 5 as usual:

105÷5=21

Therefore, the construction of each house used approximately 21ft of pipes.
