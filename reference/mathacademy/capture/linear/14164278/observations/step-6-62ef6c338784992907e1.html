Explanation

The length of the remaining pipe is (518−334)ft.

Writing 518 as an improper fraction, we have

518=5×8+18=418.

Writing 334 as an improper fraction, we have

334=3×4+34=154.

Therefore, our problem now is to work out the following:

418−154

To subtract two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator.

In this question, we can make a common denominator of 8.

To put 154 over a denominator of 8, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 2:

154=15×24×2=308

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators:

418−308=118

Finally, we convert back to a mixed number:

118=1R3=138

Therefore, 138ft of pipe remains.
