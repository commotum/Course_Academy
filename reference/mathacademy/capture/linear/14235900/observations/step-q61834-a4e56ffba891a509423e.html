One month, a greengrocer sold 40 kilograms of watermelon. If the number of kilograms of sold watermelon doubles each month, how many kilograms of watermelon will the greengrocer sell after 4 months?

a
(40⋅4)2
b
40⋅24
c
(40⋅2)4
d
40⋅2⋅4
e
40⋅42
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In a particular month, the greengrocer sold 40 kilograms of watermelon, and then this amount doubles every month.

Therefore, we have:

  • After 1 month, the greengrocer will sell 40⋅2 kilograms of watermelon.

  • After 2 months, the greengrocer will sell 40⋅22 kilograms of watermelon.

  • After 3 months, the greengrocer will sell 40⋅23 kilograms of watermelon.

  • After 4 months, the greengrocer will sell 40⋅24 kilograms of watermelon.

So the correct expression is 40⋅24.
