a
(23)−2

−(23)2

(23)2

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we write 94 as a base raised to a power:

94=3×32×2=32×32=(32)2

Now, we find the reciprocal of the base (32), and then raise that reciprocal to the same power but with the opposite sign (−2), as follows: (32)2=(23)−2
