Earlier, we found that f″(x)=−12x+12. To find the points of inflection, we solve f″(x)=0. This gives −12x+12=0⟹x=1. Finally, we need to check that the concavity changes at x=1. To do that, we take two points, one to the left of x=1 and another to the right of x=1. For example, let's take the points x=0 and x=2. We compute the sign of f″(x) at the selected points: f″(0)=−12(0)+12=12>0f″(2)=−12(2)+12=−12<0 Therefore, the second derivative changes sign at x=1. Since the concavity changes at x=1, we conclude that x=1 is indeed a point of inflection.
