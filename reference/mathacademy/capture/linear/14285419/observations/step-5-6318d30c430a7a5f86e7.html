We have the derivative

f′(x)=−6x2+12x+18=−6(x+1)(x−3)

and the two critical points x=−1 and x=3. We use the first derivative test to determine whether these points are local maxima or minima.

Let's choose the test points x=−2,0,4 and evaluate the first derivative at these points. f′(−2)=−6(−2+1)(−2−3)=−30<0f′(0)=−6(0+1)(0−3)=18>0f′(4)=−6(4+1)(4−3)=−30<0

Finally, let's use the table above to classify the critical points.

  • At x=−1, the first derivative changes sign from negative to positive. Therefore, x=−1 is a local minimum.

  • At x=3, the first derivative changes sign from positive to negative. Therefore, x=3 is a local maximum.
