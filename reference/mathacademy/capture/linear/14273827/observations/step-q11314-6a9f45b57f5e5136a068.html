EXPLANATION

First, we want to determine the limits of integration. To do this, we equate the two functions and solve for x:

2−x2−2x=2x2+6x−13x2+8x−3=0x=−8±82−4⋅3⋅(−3)2⋅3=−8±106

Hence, we need to integrate from x=−3 to x=13.

To determine which is the lower function and which is the upper, we draw our vertical arrow.

The vertical line

  • enters the region through the lower function y=2x2+6x−1, and

  • leaves through the upper function y=2−x2−2x.

Therefore, the integral that gives our area is

A=∫−31/3[(upper function)−(lower function)]dx=∫−31/3[(2−x2−2x)−(2x2+6x−1)]dx=∫−31/3(−3x2−8x+3)dx.
