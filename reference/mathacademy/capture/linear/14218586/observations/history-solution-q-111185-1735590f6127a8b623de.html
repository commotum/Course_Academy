The function y=13f(x) represents a compression of the function y=f(x) by a factor of 3 parallel to the y-axis. This has the effect of dividing the y-coordinates of all points on y=f(x) by 3.

Therefore, the point (3,6) on f(x) is mapped to the point (3,63)=(3,2), and this point must lie on the graph of y=13f(x).
