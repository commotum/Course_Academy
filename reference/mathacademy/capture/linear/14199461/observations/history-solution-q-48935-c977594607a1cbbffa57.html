The rate of change in the crocodile population t years since 2000 is equal to the birth rate minus the death rate, that is

B(t)−D(t).

Since 2012 is 12 years after 2000, we have t=12. Substituting t=12, the rate of change of the crocodile population in the river in 2012 was

B(12)−D(12)=6⋅e0.25(12)−4⋅e0.25(12)=6e3−4e3=2e3≈40.

Therefore, the rate of change of the crocodile population in the river in 2012 is approximately 40 crocodiles per year.
