The birth rate of crocodiles in a river is modeled by B(t)=6⋅e0.25t crocodiles per year, while the rate of death is modeled by D(t)=4⋅e0.25t crocodiles per year, where t represents the number of years since the year 2000.

What was the rate of change of the crocodile population in 2012? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

a
55 crocodiles per year
b
77 crocodiles per year
c
66 crocodiles per year
d
40 crocodiles per year
e
−6 crocodiles per year
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The rate of change in the crocodile population t years since 2000 is equal to the birth rate minus the death rate, that is

B(t)−D(t).

Since 2012 is 12 years after 2000, we have t=12. Substituting t=12, the rate of change of the crocodile population in the river in 2012 was

B(12)−D(12)=6⋅e0.25(12)−4⋅e0.25(12)=6e3−4e3=2e3≈40.

Therefore, the rate of change of the crocodile population in the river in 2012 is approximately 40 crocodiles per year.
