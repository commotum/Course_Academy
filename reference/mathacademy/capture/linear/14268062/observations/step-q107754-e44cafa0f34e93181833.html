The lateral surface area of a cylinder is 864πcm2 and the ratio of its height to its radius is 3. Calculate the radius of the cylinder.

a
8cm
b
16cm
c
12cm
d
7cm
e
11cm

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let r and h be the radius and height of the cylinder, respectively.

We are given that

hr=3⟹h=3r.

We can find the lateral surface area of a cylinder using the formula SL=2πrh.

Substituting the values

SL=864π,h=3r

into the formula for SL, we obtain the following:

SL=2πrh864π=2πr(3r)864π=6πr2r2=864π6πr2=144r=12cm

Notice that we disregard the negative solution since length must be positive.
