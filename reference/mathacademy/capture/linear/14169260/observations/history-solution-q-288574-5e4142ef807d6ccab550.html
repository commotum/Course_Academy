Suppose we have an equation of the form ax+b=cx+d where a,b,c, and d are constants. This equation has infinitely many solutions if a=candb=d.

To determine the values of k and r such that the equation (k+2)x+r=5x+3 has infinitely many solutions, we equate the coefficients of x, and we equate the constants:

  • Equating the coefficients of x, we have k+2=5. Solving this equation for k, we get k=3.

  • Equating the constants, we get r=3.

Finally, since k=3 and r=3, we have k+r=3+3=6.
