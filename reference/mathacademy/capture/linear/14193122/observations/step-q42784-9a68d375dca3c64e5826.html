EXPLANATION

We put both fractions over a common denominator of 5 and then add the numerators. This gives −1+85=−55+85=−5+85.

To compute the numerator, we can use either Method 1 or Method 2, shown below.

Method 1 - Picturing a Number Line

To add 8 to −5, we start at −5 on a number line and move 8 spaces to the right.

So, −5+8=3. Therefore, −5+85=35.

Method 2 - Subtraction in Reverse

We can swap the order of the numbers in the numerator, and we get −5+85=8−55=35.

So, we conclude that −1+85=35.
