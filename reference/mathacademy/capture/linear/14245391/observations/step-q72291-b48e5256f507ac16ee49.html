The diagram above shows the graphs of y=f(x) and y=g(x), where g(x)=f(x+a)+b. What are the values of a and b?

a
a=2,b=−3
b
a=3,b=−2

a=−2,b=3
d
a=3,b=2
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that every point on the graph y=g(x) is three units to the left and two units down of the corresponding point on the graph of y=f(x).

So, we conclude that g(x)=f(x+3)−2, and therefore a=3 and b=−2.
