Let us call F the event "both dice landed on 5" and E the event "both dice landed on the same number."

The required probability is P(F|E), which is given by

P(F|E)=P(F∩E)P(E).

Let's find P(E) and P(F∩E).

  • There are 6 possibilities of getting the same number on both dice among the 6×6=36 possible combinations. So, we have P(E)=636=16.

  • There is only one possibility to get 5 on both dice. So, we have P(F∩E)=136.

Therefore, substituting the above into our formula for P(F|E), we get P(F|E)=(136)(16)=16.
