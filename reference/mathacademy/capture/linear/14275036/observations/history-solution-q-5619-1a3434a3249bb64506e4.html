Let us call B the event "the black bag is chosen" and N the event "a ball labeled N is drawn."

The required probability is P(B∩N), which is given by

P(B∩N)=P(B)⋅P(N|B).

Let's find P(B) and P(N|B).

  • There is 1 black bag among the 2 bags, so we have P(B)=12.

  • There is 1 ball labeled N among the 4 items in the black bag. So, we have P(N|B)=14.

Substituting the above data into the multiplication law for conditional probability, we get

P(B∩N)=12⋅14=18.
