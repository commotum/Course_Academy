Two cards are randomly drawn from a pack of 52 cards one by one without replacement. What is the probability that both the cards are diamonds?

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let us call D1 the event "the first card drawn is a diamond" and D2 the event "the second card drawn is a diamond."

The required probability is P(D1∩D2), which is given by

P(D1∩D2)=P(D1)⋅P(D2|D1).

Let's find P(D1) and P(D2|D1).

  • There are 13 diamonds among the 52 cards, so we have P(D1)=1352=14.

  • If the first card drawn is a diamond, there are 12 diamonds left in the pack among the remaining 51 cards. So, we have P(D2|D1)=1251.

Substituting the above data into the multiplication law for conditional probability, we get

P(D1∩D2)=14⋅1251=117.
