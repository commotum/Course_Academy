To find the slope at a given point, we have to find dfdx at that point.

This question requires the product rule. Let u(x)=x4 and v(x)=csc⁡(3x−1). Then

u′(x)=4x3,v′(x)=−3csc⁡(3x−1)cot⁡(3x−1).

Note that we used the chain rule to differentiate v.

Now, applying the product rule and simplifying, we have

dfdx=u′(x)⋅v(x)+u(x)⋅v′(x)=(4x3)(csc⁡(3x−1))+(x4)(−3csc⁡(3x−1)cot⁡(3x−1))=4x3csc⁡(3x−1)−3x4csc⁡(3x−1)cot⁡(3x−1)=x3csc⁡(3x−1)(4−3xcot⁡(3x−1)).

Therefore, the slope at x=1 is

(dfdx)|x=1=(1)3csc⁡(3(1)−1)(4−3(1)cot⁡(3(1)−1))=csc⁡2(4−3cot⁡2).
