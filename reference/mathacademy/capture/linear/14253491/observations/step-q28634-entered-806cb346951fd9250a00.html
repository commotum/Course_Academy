−52​cot(2)
csc⁡(2)

EXPLANATION

First, we express y as the composite function

y=u,u=csc⁡v,v=5x+2.

Differentiating gives

dydu=12u,dudv=−csc⁡vcot⁡v,dvdx=5.

Applying the chain rule twice, we get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=dydu⋅dudv⋅dvdx=12u⋅(−csc⁡vcot⁡v)⋅5=12csc⁡(5x+2)⋅(−csc⁡(5x+2)cot⁡(5x+2))⋅5=−52cot⁡(5x+2)csc⁡(5x+2).

Now we compute y′(0): y′(0)=−52cot⁡(5(0)+2)csc⁡(5(0)+2)=−52cot⁡(2)csc⁡(2).
