A transformed reciprocal function of the form f(x)=abx+c+d has the horizontal asymptote y=d, and therefore, its range is f(x)∈(−∞,d)∪(d,∞).

In our case, d=5. Therefore, the horizontal asymptote is y=5, and the range of the function is f(x)∈(−∞,5)∪(5,∞).
