The notation a∣b means a divides b. So, 5∣10 means 5 divides 10.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a divides b means that there exists an integer k such that b=ak.

Therefore, 5∣10 is equivalent to the statement that there exists an integer k such that 10=5k.
