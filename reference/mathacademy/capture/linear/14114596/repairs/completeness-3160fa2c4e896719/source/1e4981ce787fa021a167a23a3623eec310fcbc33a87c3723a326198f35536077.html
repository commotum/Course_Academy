  2. 9∣(60x−45y)
  3. 5∣(60x−45y)

EXPLANATION

The notation a∣b means a divides b.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a divides b means that there exists an integer k such that b=ak. With that in mind, let's examine our statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. For example, if x=0 and y=1, we have 60(0)−45(1)=−45, which is not divisible by 2.

  • Statement II is false. For example, if x=1 and y=1, we have 60(1)−45(1)=15, which is not divisible by 9.

  • Statement III is true. Notice that 60x−45y=5(12x−9y). Since x and y are integers, 12x−9y must be an integer, too. So, 5∣(60x−45y).

Therefore, the correct answer is " III only."
