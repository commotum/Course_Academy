The notation a∣b means a divides b. So, 4∣16 means 4 divides 16.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a divides b means that there exists an integer k such that b=ak.

Therefore, 4∣16 is equivalent to the statement that there exists an integer k such that 16=4k.
