The notation a∣b means a divides b.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a divides b means that there exists an integer k such that b=ak. With that in mind, let's examine our statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Notice that 42x+24y=6(7x+4y). Since x and y are integers, 7x+4y must be an integer, too. So, 6∣(42x+24y).

  • Statement II is false. For example, if x=0 and y=1, we have 42(0)+24(1)=24, which is not divisible by 7.

  • Statement III is true. Notice that 42x+24y=2(21x+12y). Since x and y are integers, 21x+12y must be an integer, too. So, 2∣(42x+24y).

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
