The notation a∣b means a divides b. So, 2∣8 means 2 divides 8.

According to the definition of integer divisibility, the fact that a divides b means that there exists an integer k such that b=ak.

Therefore, 2∣8 is equivalent to the statement that there exists an integer k such that 8=2k.
