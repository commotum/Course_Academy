Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's call P(x) the polynomial shown in the diagram.

The end behavior of P(x) is

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→−∞, and

  • P(x)→∞ as x→∞.

Consequently,

  • the degree of P(x) is odd, and

  • the leading coefficient of P(x) is positive.

  • the y-intercept is positive, which means that P(0)>0.

Therefore, of the given options, the only one that satisfies all three criteria is y=x3−2x2+x+2.
