EXPLANATION

Let's call P(x) the given polynomial:

P(x)=2x3−6x2+8

The degree (n=3) is odd and the leading coefficient (a3=1) is positive. Therefore, the polynomial has the following end behavior:

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→−∞.

  • P(x)→∞ as x→∞.

Only the graph A satisfies these conditions.
