The graph of the polynomial y=P(x) is shown above. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The degree of P(x) is even.
  2. The coefficient of the leading term is positive.
  3. The constant term is positive.
a
I and II only
b
I and III only
c
II and III only
d
I only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The end behavior of P(x) is

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→−∞, and

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→∞.

Consequently,

  • the degree of P(x) is even, and

  • the leading coefficient of P(x) is negative.

So, statement I is true, while statement II is false.

Lastly, note that the y-intercept is positive, and therefore the constant term must be positive. So, III is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only".
