  1. The degree of P(x) is odd.
  2. P(x)→∞ as x→∞.

EXPLANATION

The end behavior of P(x) is

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→−∞, and

  • P(x)→−∞ as x→∞.

Consequently,

  • the degree of P(x) is even, and

  • the leading coefficient of P(x) is negative.

Therefore, statement III is true, while statements I and II are false.
