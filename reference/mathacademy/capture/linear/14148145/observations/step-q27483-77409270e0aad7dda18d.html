The algebraic expression 4w−3 can be translated into a sentence as

a
The difference between twice a number and three
b
The difference between a number and three
c
The difference between four times a number and three
d
The difference between three times a number and four
e
The difference between four times a number and five
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The first term of the expression is 4w and this can be written as "four times a number".

Since we have the subtraction 4w−3, the whole expression can be written as "the difference between four times a number and three".
