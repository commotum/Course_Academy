Translate the phrase "one-quarter of a number minus three-sevenths of another number" into an algebraic expression.

a
17x−34y
b
37x−14x
c
37y
d
37x+14y
e
14x−37y
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

If we denote our first number by x and our second number by y, then we can write "one-quarter of a number " as 14x and "three-sevenths of a second number" as 37y. So, the whole sentence is 14x−37y.
