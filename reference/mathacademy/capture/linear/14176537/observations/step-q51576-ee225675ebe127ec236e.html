Quartic binomial

Quadratic trinomial

Cubic binomial

EXPLANATION

The polynomial 5y3−2y+1 has three terms, so it is a trinomial.

Also, its degree is 3, so it is a cubic.

Therefore, the polynomial is a cubic trinomial.
