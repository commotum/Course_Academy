The point (4,7) is stretched in x-direction and factor −2, and then stretched in the y-direction and factor −1. What is the resulting point?

a
(8,7)
b
(3,5)
c
(2,6)
d
(−8,−7)
e
(−7,−8)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's apply the given transformation.

  • A stretch in the x-direction of factor −2 can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(−2x,y).

  • A stretch in the y-direction of factor −1 can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(x,−y).

Therefore, the combination of the stretches is given by (x,y)↦(−2x,−1y).

Applying the combined stretch to our point, we obtain (4,7)↦(−2⋅4,−1⋅7)=(−8,−7).
