A farmer divided his field into 3 smaller lots of equal area. What is the area of each lot if the total area of the field is 12 acres?
Expressed as a fraction, the area of each lot is
16​
acres.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine the area of each lot, we divide the total field area by the number of lots.

12÷3

To divide a unit fraction by a whole number, we multiply the fraction's denominator by that whole number:

12÷3=12×3=16

Therefore, the area of each lot is 16 acres.
