The diagram above shows the unit circle. Given that the y-coordinate of the point P is 54, use the Pythagorean identity to find cos⁡θ

1−54

Correct
EXPLANATION

Any point (x,y) on the unit circle is related to the central angle θ as follows:

x=cos⁡θ,y=sin⁡θ

We're given that y=54 at the point P. Therefore, we have

sin⁡θ=54.

We can use the Pythagorean identity to find cos⁡θ:

sin2⁡θ+cos2⁡θ=1cos2⁡θ=1−sin2⁡θcos⁡θ=±1−sin2⁡θ

Substituting sin⁡θ=54 gives

cos⁡θ=±1−(54)2=±1−516=±1116=±114.

Finally, we select the positive answer since cos⁡θ is positive in the first quadrant. So our final answer is cos⁡θ=114.
