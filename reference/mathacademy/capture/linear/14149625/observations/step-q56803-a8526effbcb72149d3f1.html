How many pairs of like terms are in the expression a+2b+3−10b−9−8a?

a
4
b
3
c
1
d
0
e
2

Correct
EXPLANATION

We write the terms of the expression as follows:

(a)+(2b)+(3)+(−10b)+(−9)+(−8a).

There are three pairs of like terms:

  • a and −8a are like terms because they both contain the variable a.

  • 2b and −10b are like terms because they both contain the variable b.

  • 3 and −9 are like terms because they both contain no variable.
