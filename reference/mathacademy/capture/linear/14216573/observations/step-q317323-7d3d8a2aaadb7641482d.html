EXPLANATION

To plot the given function, we follow these steps:

  • We take the curve y=(14)x.
  • Reflect it on the x-axis to give y=−(14)x. The y-intercept moves from (0,1) to (0,−1) but the horizontal asymptote stays at y=0.
  • Then, vertically shift it by 6 units up to give y=−(14)x+6. The point (0,−1) moves to (0,5) and the horizontal asymptote moves from y=0 to y=6.
