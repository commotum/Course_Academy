Let C(0,2) and D(4,−1). Find |CD→|.

a
26
b
20
c
29
d
6
e
5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We have

OC→=c=0i+2j,OD→=d=4i−j.

Expressing CD→ as the difference of position vectors, we obtain:

CD→=OD→−OC→=d−c=(4i−j)−(2j)=4i−3j

Now, using the formula for the magnitude of a vector, we get

|CD→|=42+(−3)2=16+9=25=5.
