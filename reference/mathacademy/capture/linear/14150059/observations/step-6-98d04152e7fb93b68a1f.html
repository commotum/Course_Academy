Carmen cuts a piece of drill fabric with a length of 214m to make a tablecloth. If the fabric has a width of 112m, what will be the area of the tablecloth? Express your answer as a **mixed number** in simplest form.

Explanation

The tablecloth will have an area of (214×112)m2.

First, we write 214 as an improper fraction: 214=(2×4)+14=94

We also write 112 as an improper fraction: 112=(1×2)+12=32

Now, we can multiply the two numbers. We multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators: 94×32=9×34×2=278

Finally, we write the resulting improper fraction as a mixed number: 278=3R3=338.
