Carmen has a piece of fabric with a length of 514m. She accidentally spills coffee on this piece, so she decides to cut out and discard 115m of fabric. How long is the remaining piece of fabric? Express your answer as a **mixed number** in simplest form.

Explanation

The new piece of fabric has a length of (514−115)m long.

To subtract two mixed numbers, we subtract whole numbers from whole numbers and fractions from fractions.

Subtracting the whole numbers, we get 5−1=4.

Now we subtract the fractions: 14−15

Let's look at the denominators. The least common denominator of 4 and 5 is 20.

To put 14 over a denominator of 20, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 5: 14=1×54×5=520

To put 15 over a denominator of 20, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 4: 15=1×45×4=420

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators:

14−15=520−420=120

Therefore, 514−115=4120.
