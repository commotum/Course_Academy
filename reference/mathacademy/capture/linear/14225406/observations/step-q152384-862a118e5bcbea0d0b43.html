EXPLANATION

First, we bring the quotient under a single radical and then simplify the result: 32z58z3=32z58z3=4z2

Now, notice that the expression inside the radical is a perfect square. So, we can continue simplifying by writing the expression as this perfect square: 4z2=22z2=(2z)2=|2z|=|2|⋅|z|=2⋅|z|

Lastly, we are told that z>0, so we have |z|=z. Therefore, the final answer is 2⋅|z|=2⋅z=2z.
