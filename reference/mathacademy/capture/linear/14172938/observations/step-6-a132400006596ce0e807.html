Writing 1034 as an improper fraction, we have 1034=10×4+34=434.

Writing 927 as an improper fraction, we have 927=9×7+27=657.

Therefore, our problem now is to work out the following: 434−657

To subtract two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator.

In this question, we can make a common denominator of 28.

To put 434 over a denominator of 28, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 7: 434=43×74×7=30128

To put 657 over a denominator of 28, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 4: 657=65×47×4=26028

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators: 30128−26028=4128

Finally, we convert back to a mixed number: 4128=1R13=11328

Therefore, the volume of distilled water remaining equals 11328 liters.
