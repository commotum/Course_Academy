Explanation

To estimate the price of each of the 6 perfumes, we need to find an approximation for 90.33÷6.

So, we round the dividend to the nearest whole number: 90.33→90

Now, we divide 90 by 6 as usual: 90÷6=15

Therefore, the approximate price for each bottle is $15.
