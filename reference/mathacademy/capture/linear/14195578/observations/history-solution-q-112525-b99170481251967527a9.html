We need to find the level of caffeine in the blood between t=2 and t=3 hours after drinking a cup of coffee. To calculate this, we find the level of caffeine in the blood after 2 hours, and we subtract from it the level of caffeine in the blood after 3 hours. So we need to evaluate

C(2)−C(3).

The level of caffeine in the blood after 2 hours is C(2)=140⋅(0.9)2=140⋅0.81=113.4.

The level of caffeine in the blood after 3 hours is C(3)=140⋅(0.9)3=140⋅0.729=102.06.

Thus, the level of caffeine metabolized between t=2 and t=3 hours after drinking a cup of coffee is

113.4−102.06=11.34.
