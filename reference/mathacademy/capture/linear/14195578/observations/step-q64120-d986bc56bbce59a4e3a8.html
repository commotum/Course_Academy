The value of a boat, in dollars, depreciates according to the function p(t)=10000⋅(0.9)t, where t is the number of years since the boat was purchased. Calculate p(4), and determine its meaning in this context.

a
The value of the boat after 4 years is $5901.
b
The value of the boat after 4 years is $10000.
c
The value of the boat depreciates by $10000 every four years
d
The value of the boat after 4 years is $6561.
e
The value of the boat after 4 years is $7290.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The value p(4) represents the value of p(t) at t=4. In this context, it represents the value of the boat 4 years after it was purchased.

Substituting t=4 into the expression for p(t) gives

p(4)=10000⋅(0.9)4=10000⋅(0.6561)=6561.

Therefore, the value of the boat after four years is $6,561.
