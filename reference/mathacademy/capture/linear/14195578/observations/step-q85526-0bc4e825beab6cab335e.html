Due to acaricides, the population of ticks in a certain park decreases according to the expression 20000⋅(0.7)t, where t is the time, in weeks, since acaricides were first applied. What does the base of the exponential expression (0.7) mean in this context?

a
The population of ticks decreases by 70% every week
b
The population of ticks increases by 30% every week
c
The population of ticks increases by 70% every week
d
The population of ticks decreases by 30% every week
e
There were 70 ticks initially
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The given expression is an exponential decay expression of the form y(t)=a⋅(1−r)t, where:

  • a=20000 represents the initial number of ticks.

  • 1−r=0.7, where r is the decay rate.

  • t is the time, in weeks, since the acaricides were first applied.

Solving for r, we get 1−r=0.7⟹r=0.3.

Now, 0.3 (expressed as a percent) is equal to 30%.

So, the base of the exponential expression 0.7 means that the population of ticks decreases by 30% every week.
