EXPLANATION

The inequality x≤0 or x>115 means that x can take any value less than or equal to 0, or greater than 115.

To represent this on a number line, we graph the two separate inequalities:

  • the inequality x≤0 consists of a closed circle at 0 and an arrow to the left.

  • the inequality x>115 consists of an open circle at 115 and an arrow to the right.
