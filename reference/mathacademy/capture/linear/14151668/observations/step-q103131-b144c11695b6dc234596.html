Which inequality corresponds to the highlighted region shown on the number line above?

a
x≤−2 or x≤2
b
x<−2 or x>2
c
x>−2 or x<2
d
x≤−2 or x≥2
e
x<−2 or x<2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The red rays contain all values less than −2 or greater than 2. In addition,

  • the open circle at −2 implies that x cannot be equal to −2, and

  • the open circle at 2 implies that x cannot be equal to 2.

Therefore, the highlighted region represents x<−2 or x>2.
