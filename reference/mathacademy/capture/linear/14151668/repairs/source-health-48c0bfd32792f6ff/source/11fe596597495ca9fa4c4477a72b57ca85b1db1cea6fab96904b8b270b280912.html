The inequality x<−5 or x>−3 means that x can take any value less than −5, or greater than −3.

To represent this on a number line, we graph the two separate inequalities:

  • the inequality x<−5 consists of an open circle at −5 and an arrow to the left.

  • the inequality x>−3 consists of a open circle at −3 and an arrow to the right.
