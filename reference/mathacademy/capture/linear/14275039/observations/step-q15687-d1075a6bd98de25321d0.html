Find the x-intercepts of the circle (x−2)2+(y−2)2=20, shown above.

a
 (−1.5,0) and (5.5,0)
b
 (−1,0) and (6,0)
c
 (−2,0) and (7,0)
d
 (−2,0) and (6,0)
e
 (−1,0) and (4,0)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We must have y=0 at the x-intercepts.

So, we need to substitute y=0 into the equation of the circle and solve for x, as follows: (x−2)2+(y−2)2=20(x−2)2+(0−2)2=20(x−2)2+4=20(x−2)2=16x−2=±16x=2±4

Therefore, the x-intercepts occur at the points (−2,0) and (6,0).
