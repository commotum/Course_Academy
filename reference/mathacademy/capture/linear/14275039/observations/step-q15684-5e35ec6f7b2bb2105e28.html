Find the x-intercepts of the circle (x−1)2+(y−3)2=45, shown above.

a
(−6,0) and (8,0)
b
(−6,0) and (7,0)
c
(−5,0) and (8,0)
d
(−5,0) and (7,0)
e
(−5.5,0) and (7.5,0)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We must have y=0 at the x-intercepts.

So, we need to substitute y=0 into the equation of the circle and solve for x, as follows: (x−1)2+(y−3)2=45(x−1)2+(0−3)2=45(x−1)2+9=45(x−1)2=36x−1=±36x=1±6

Therefore, the x-intercepts occur at the points (−5,0) and (7,0).
