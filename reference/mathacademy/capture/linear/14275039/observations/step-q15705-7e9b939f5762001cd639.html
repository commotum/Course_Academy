(0,−3) and (0,8)

(0,−1.5) and (0,6.5)

Correct
EXPLANATION

We must have x=0 at the y-intercepts.

So, we need to substitute x=0 into the equation of the circle and solve for y, as follows: (x−2)2+(y−2)2=20(0−2)2+(y−2)2=204+(y−2)2=20(y−2)2=16y−2=±16y=2±4 Therefore, the y-intercepts occur at the points (0,−2) and (0,6).
