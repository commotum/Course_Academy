Since (x−1) is a factor of f(x), we know that (x−1) divides f(x) with no remainder. So, to begin factoring f(x), we can divide it by (x−1) using synthetic division:

x3 x2 x1 x0

x3+x2−10x+8x−1=x2+2x−8,

x3+x2−10x+8=(x−1)(x2+2x−8).

(x−1)(x2+2x−8)=(x−1)(x−2)(x+4).
