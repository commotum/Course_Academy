To complete another job, Mike bought 4 identical cans of paint, paying a total of $95.64. Estimate the price of each can of paint by rounding the amount of money to the nearest dollar.
The approximate price of each can of paint is $
24
.

Correct
Explanation

To estimate the price of each can of paint, we need to find an approximation for 95.64÷4.

So, we round the dividend to the nearest whole number: 95.64→96

Now, we divide 96 by 4 as usual: 96÷4=24

Therefore, the approximate price of each can of paint is $24.
