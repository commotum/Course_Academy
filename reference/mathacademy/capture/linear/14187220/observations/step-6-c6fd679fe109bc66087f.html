Explanation

After finishing, Mike had (445−3310) liters of paint left.

Writing 445 as an improper fraction, we have 445=4×5+45=245.

Writing 3310 as an improper fraction, we have 3310=3×10+310=3310.

Therefore, our problem now is to work out the following: 245−3310

To subtract two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator.

In this question, we can make a common denominator of 10.

To put 245 over a denominator of 10, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 2: 245=24×25×2=4810

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators: 4810−3310=1510

Simplifying, we have 1510=15÷510÷5=32.

Finally, we convert back to a mixed number: 32=1R1=112

Therefore, Mike had 112 liters of paint left.
