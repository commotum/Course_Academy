The least-squares solution of Ax=b satisfies the normal equation

ATAx=ATb.

Substituting A=QR, we obtain

ATAx=ATb(QR)TQRx=(QR)TbRT(QTQ)Rx=RTQTb.

Since Q has orthonormal columns, QTQ=I, and we get

RTRx=RTQTb.

Then, since RT is invertible, pre-multiplying by (RT)−1 gives

(RT)−1⋅RTRx=(RT)−1⋅RTQTbRx=QTb.

Finally, since R is invertible, pre-multiplying by R−1 gives

R−1⋅Rx=R−1⋅QTbx=R−1QTb.

Therefore, the missing part of the equation is R−1.
