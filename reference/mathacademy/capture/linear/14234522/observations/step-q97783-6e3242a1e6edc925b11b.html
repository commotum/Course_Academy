The graph of y=f(−(x+5)) can be obtained from the graph of y=f(x) by applying some of the following transformations:

  • (T1) - Reflection across the x-axis

  • (T2) - Translation by 5 units to the left

  • (T3) - Stretch of scale factor 5 parallel to the y-axis

  • (T4) - Reflection across the y-axis

Which of the following could be a possible ordering of the appropriate transformations?

a
(T1)→(T3)
b
(T2)→(T3)
c
(T1)→(T2)
d
(T4)→(T2)
e
(T4)→(T3)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To perform transformations of functions with multiple operations, we must remember the following rule:

  1. First, we perform the transformations inside the argument of f. We first carry out horizontal stretches and reflections, followed by horizontal translations.

  2. Then, we perform the transformations outside the argument of f. We first carry out vertical stretches and reflections, followed by vertical translations.

To get y=f(−(x+5)), we can apply the following steps:

  • Take the curve y=f(x).

  • Apply transformation (T4): Reflect y=f(x) across the y-axis to get the graph of y=f(−x).

  • Apply transformation (T2): Translate y=f(−x) to the left by 5 units to get the graph of y=f(−(x+5)).

Therefore, a possible order of transformations is

(T4)→(T2).
