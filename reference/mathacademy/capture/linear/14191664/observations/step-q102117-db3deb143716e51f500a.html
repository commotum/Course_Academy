EXPLANATION

First, we graph the two intervals on a number line:

The interval (−∞,−2] can be expressed as the inequality x≤−2.

Likewise, the interval (−1,∞) can be expressed as the inequality x>−1.

So, the union of these intervals represents the compound inequality x≤−2 or x>−1.
