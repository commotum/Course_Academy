x<3 or x>6

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we graph the two intervals on a number line:

The interval (−∞,3] can be expressed as the inequality x≤3.

Likewise, the interval [6,∞) can be expressed as the inequality x≥6.

So, the union of these intervals represents the compound inequality

x≤3 or x≥6.
