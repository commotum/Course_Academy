If the depth reduces by 110 every month, then 910 of the depth remains every year. So the decay factor is 910.

The formula for exponential decay (in terms of a decay factor b) is

d(t)=abt.

In our case, we have the following:

  • a=320 is the initial depth.

  • b=910 is the decay factor.

  • t= the time, in years.

Therefore, the correct expression for d(t) is

d(t)=320⋅(910)t.

So the missing number is 910.
