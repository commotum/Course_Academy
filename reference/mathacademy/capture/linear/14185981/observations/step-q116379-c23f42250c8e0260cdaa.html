The amount of bleach b, measured in gallons (gal), in a certain detergent product varies directly with the amount of water w, measured in gallons, in the product. When the amount of bleach is b=1.25gal, the amount of water is w=3.75gal. Find the amount of bleach when the amount of water is w=9gal.

a
2.25gal
b
27gal
c
3gal
d
9gal

EXPLANATION

The amount of bleach b is directly proportional to the amount of water w, so we have

b=kw.

We are told that when the amount of bleach is b=1.25gal, the amount of water is w=3.75gal. We can use this information to solve for k, as follows:

1.25=k⋅(3.75)1.25=3.75k1.253.75=k13=k

We now have the equation

b=13w.

We can use this equation to find the amount of bleach when the amount of water is w=9gal, as follows:

b=13⋅(9)=3

Therefore, the amount of bleach when w=9gal is b=3gal.
