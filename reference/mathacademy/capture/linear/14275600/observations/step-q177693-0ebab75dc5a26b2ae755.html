The 7th and 8th terms of an arithmetic sequence are −39 and −31, respectively. Determine the formula for the nth term of the sequence.

a
an=8n−87
b
an=7n−88
c
an=6n−81
d
an=7n−80
e
an=8n−95
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term is given by an=a1+(n−1)d, so we need to figure out the first term a1 and the common difference d.

Since the terms a7=−39 and a8=−31 are consecutive, we can subtract them to find the common difference: d=a8−a7=−31−(−39)=−31+39=8

Now, we can use one of our known values, say, a7=−39, to determine the first term of the sequence. Since a7 is 6 jumps after a1, we have a7=a1+6d−39=a1+6(8)−39=a1+48−39−48=a1a1=−87.

So, the formula for the nth term of this arithmetic sequence is an=a1+(n−1)d=−87+(n−1)(8)=−87+8n−8=8n−95.
