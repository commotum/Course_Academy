Find the GCF of 9m, 15n and 21n.

a
15
b
9
c
None
d
1
e
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 9 are 1,3,9.

The factors of 15 are 1,3,5,15.

The factors of 21 are 1,3,7,21.

The common factors of 9, 15, and 21 are 1,3.

The greatest of these common factors is 3.

Now we look at the variable factors. The expressions 9m, 15n, and 21n do not all share a variable factor.

Thus, the GCF of 9m, 15n, and 21n is 3.
