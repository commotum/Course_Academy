To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 12 are 1,2,3,4,6,12.

The factors of 8 are 1,2,4,8.

The common factors of 12 and 8 are 1,2,4.

The greatest of these common factors is 4.

Now we look at the variable factors. Both 12z and 8z contain z.

Thus, the greatest common factor of 12z and 8z is 4z.
