To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 16 are 1,2,4,8,16.

The factors of 24 are 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24.

The common factors of 16 and 24 are 1,2,4,8.

The greatest of these common factors is 8.

Now we look at the variable factors. Since 16 has no variable and 24t contains t, there are no variable factors in common.

Thus, the greatest common factor of 16 and 24t is 8.
