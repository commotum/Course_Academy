To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 12 are 1,2,3,4,6,12.

The factors of 18 are 1,2,3,6,9,18.

The common factors of 12 and 18 are 1,2,3,6.

The greatest of these common factors is 6.

Now we look at the variable factors. Since 12 has no variable and 18z contains z, there are no variable factors in common.

Thus, the greatest common factor of 12 and 18z is 6.
