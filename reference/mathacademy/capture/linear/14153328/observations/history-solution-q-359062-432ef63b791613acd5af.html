To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 9 are 1,3,9.

The factors of 6 are 1,2,3,6.

The common factors of 9 and 6 are 1,3.

The greatest of these common factors is 3.

Now we look at the variable factors. Both 9y and 6y contain y.

Thus, the greatest common factor of 9y and 6y is 3y.
