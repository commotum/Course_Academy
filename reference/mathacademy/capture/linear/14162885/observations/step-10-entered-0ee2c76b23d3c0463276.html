Question 1 ?
Expressed as a polynomial in terms of x, the length of Paul's plot of land is
3x
yards.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

The width of the plot is x+5 yards, and its area is 3x2+15x square yards. Since the area=length×width, we factor the expression for the area to find the length.

First, we find the GCF of the two terms:

GCF(3x2,15x)=3x

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the GCF:

3x2+15x=(3x⋅x)+(3x⋅5)

Next, we write the GCF outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

(3x⋅x)+(3x⋅5)=3x(x+5)

As a result, we conclude that the length of the plot must be 3x yards.
