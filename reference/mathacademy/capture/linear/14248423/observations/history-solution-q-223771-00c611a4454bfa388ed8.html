Let's examine each of our numbers in turn.

  • 0.1222 is a rational number because it can be expressed as a ratio of two integers: 0.1222=122210000

  • 1.34― is a rational number. The notation 4― tells us that the digit 4 after the decimal point repeats indefinitely. 1.34―=1.34444444444… Any decimal with repeating digits can be represented as a ratio of two integers. In this case, 1.34―=12190.

  • 1.981354… is not a rational number. The notation "…" tells us that digits after the decimal point extend forever. However, there are no repeating digits.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
