Explanation

First, notice that we have a right triangle with legs a=11m and b=14m.

Let c be the length of the hypotenuse. Then, according to the Pythagorean theorem, we obtain

c2=a2+b2c=a2+b2.

Substituting the values for a and b gives

c=112+142=121+196=317m.
