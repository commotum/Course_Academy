Given that f(x)=−7x, what is f′(x) according to the definition of the derivative?

a
f′(x)=limh→0(−7x−(x+h)h)
b
f′(x)=limh→0(−7(x+h)−(−7x)h)
c
f′(x)=limh→0((x+h)+(−7x)h)
d
f′(x)=limh→0(x−7(x+h)h)
e
f′(x)=limh→0((x+h)+xh)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The derivative f′(x) is given by the limit

limh→0(f(x+h)−f(x)h).

Since f(x)=−7x, we have that f(x+h)=−7(x+h). Hence, we get

f′(x)=limh→0(−7(x+h)−(−7x)h).
