For a fixed temperature, the pressure P of a gas varies inversely with the volume V that it occupies. When V=3m3, the pressure is P=2 megapascal (MPa). What will be the pressure if the gas is compressed to a container with volume V=2m3?

a
3.8MPa
b
2.8MPa
c
2.5MPa
d
3MPa
e
4MPa
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The pressure P is inversely proportional to the volume V, so we have

P=kV,

where k is the constant of variation.

We are told that when the volume is V=3m3, the pressure is P=2 megapascal (MPa). We can use this information to solve for k as follows:

2=k32⋅3=k6=k

We now have the equation

P=6V.

We can use this equation to find the pressure when the volume is V=2m3, as follows:

P=62=3

Therefore, the pressure of the gas when V=2m3 is 3MPa.
