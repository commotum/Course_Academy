Example: Classifying Even Functions Graphically
?

Which of the above could be the graph of an even function?

EXPLANATION

Recall that the graph of an even function must be symmetric about the -axis.

  • Graphs II, III, and IV are not symmetric about the -axis. So, they are not even functions.

  • On the other hand, graph I is symmetric about the -axis. That is to say, if we reflect graph I over the -axis, we get the same picture. This means that graph I is an even function.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
