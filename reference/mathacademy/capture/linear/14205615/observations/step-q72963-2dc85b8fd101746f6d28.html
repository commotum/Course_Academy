Which of the above could be graphs of odd functions?

a
I and IV only
b
I, II, III, and IV
c
II and IV only
d
II only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the graph of an odd function must have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin.

  • Graphs I, III, and IV do not have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin. So, they are not odd functions.

  • On the other hand, graph II does have rotational symmetry about the origin. If we rotate the graph 180∘ around the origin, we get the same picture. This means that graph II shows an odd function.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
