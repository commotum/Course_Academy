Recall that the graph of an odd function must have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin.

  • Graph IV does not have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin. So, it is not an odd function.

  • On the other hand, graphs I, II, and III do have rotational symmetry about the origin. If we rotate the graph 180∘ around the origin, we get the same picture. This means that graphs I, II, and III show odd functions.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II and III only."
