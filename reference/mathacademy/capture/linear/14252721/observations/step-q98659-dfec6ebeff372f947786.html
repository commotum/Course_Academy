What is the range of f(x)=−44−3x6+2?

a
 y∈(−∞,∞)
b
 y∈[2,∞)
c
 y∈(−∞,43]
d
 y∈[43,∞)
e
 y∈(−∞,2]
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The range of x6 is

x6≥0.

Horizontal shifts and stretches do not affect the range. Hence,

4−3x6≥0.

Multiplying the above inequality by −4 and then adding 2, we get

4−3x6≥0−44−3x6≤0−44−3x6+2≤2.

Therefore, the range of the given function is f(x)∈(−∞,2].
