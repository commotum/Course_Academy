If log8⁡x+2log8⁡x=2, then what is the value of x?

a
4
b
21
c
24
d
64
e
6
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we rearrange our equation to isolate the logarithmic term:

log8⁡x+2log8⁡x=2(1+2)log8⁡x=23log8⁡x=2log8⁡x=23

To remove the logarithm, we exponentiate both sides of the equation. The base of the logarithm is 8, so we use this base for the exponentiation as well:

log8⁡x=238log8⁡x=82/3

Now, since exponentiation is the inverse function of the logarithm, we get 8log8⁡x=x.

Therefore, x=82/3x=643x=4.
