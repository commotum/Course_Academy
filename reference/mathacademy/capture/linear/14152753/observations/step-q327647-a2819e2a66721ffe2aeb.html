A number x is 400% greater than 120. Find the value of x.
x=
600
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find a number 400% greater than 120, we apply a new percentage of

100%+400%=500%.

We begin by converting our percentage to a decimal:

500%=500100=5

So, to find a number that is 400% greater than 120, we multiply 120 by 5:

5⋅120=600

Therefore, x=600.
