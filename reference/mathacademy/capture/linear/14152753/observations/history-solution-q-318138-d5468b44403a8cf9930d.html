To find a number 145% greater than 80, we apply a new percentage of

100%+145%=245%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

245%=245100=2.45

So, to find a number that is 145% greater than 80, we multiply 80 by 2.45:

2.45⋅80=196

Therefore, the number that is 145% greater than 80 is 196.
