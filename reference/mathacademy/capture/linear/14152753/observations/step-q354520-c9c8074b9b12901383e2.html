An artist painted 60 portraits last year. This year, her output is 400% greater than last year. How many portraits did the artist paint this year?
The artist painted
300
portraits this year.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find this year's portrait output, we apply a 400% increase to last year's figure.

Increasing by 400% means that we must apply a new percentage of

100%+400%=500%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal: 500%=500100=5

Now, we multiply the original amount by 5: 60×5=300

So, the artist painted 300 portraits this year.
