What number is 45% greater than 220?

a
315
b
322
c
318
d
320
e
319
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find a number 45% greater than 220, we apply a new percentage of

100%+45%=145%.

We begin by converting the percentage to a decimal:

145%=145100=1.45

So, to find a number that is 45% greater than 220, we multiply 220 by 1.45:

1.45⋅220=319

Therefore, the number that is 45% greater than 220 is 319.
