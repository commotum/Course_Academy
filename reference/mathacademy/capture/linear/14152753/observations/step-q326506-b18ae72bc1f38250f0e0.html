What number is 12% greater than 1,500?
The increased number is

.

Correct
EXPLANATION

To find a number 12% greater than 1,500, we apply a new percentage of 100%+12%=112%.

We convert this percentage to a decimal: 112%=112100=1.12

Now, we multiply the original number by 1.12: 1,500×1.12=1,680

So, the number that is 12% greater than 1,500 is 1,680.
