Speed is a special unit rate applied to moving objects. It is defined as the distance traveled per unit of time:

speed=distancetime

To find the speed, in feet per month, we divide the number of feet 49, by the number of months, 14. This gives,

4914=49÷714÷7=72=7÷2=3.5.

Therefore, the speed of the glacier is 3.5 feet per month.
