A spacecraft traveled for 15 seconds at a constant speed of 12 kilometers per second. Find the distance covered by the spacecraft.

a
234 kilometers
b
180 kilometers
c
2.5 kilometers
d
90 kilometers
e
1.25 kilometers
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The speed is 12 kilometers per second. To find the distance covered in 15 seconds, we multiply the speed by 15. This gives

distance=speed⋅time=12×15=180.

Therefore, the spacecraft covered 180 kilometers in 15 seconds.
