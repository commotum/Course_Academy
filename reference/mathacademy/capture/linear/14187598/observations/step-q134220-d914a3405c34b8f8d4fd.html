A jet ski traveled 3960 meters at a constant speed of 660 meters per minute. For how long was the jet ski in motion?

a
 6.5 minutes
b
 5 minutes
c
 6 minutes
d
 7 minutes
e
 7.5 minutes
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The speed is 660 meters per minute. To find the time it took the jet ski to cover 3960 meters, we divide the distance by 660. This gives

time=distancespeed=3960660=3960÷60660÷60=6611=66÷11=6.

Therefore, the jet ski was in motion for 6 minutes.
