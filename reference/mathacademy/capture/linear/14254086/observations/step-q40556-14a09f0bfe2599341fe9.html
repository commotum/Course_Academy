The range of (23)x is

(23)x>0.

Horizontal shifts and stretches do not affect the range. Therefore,

(23)x−1>0.

Multiplying the above inequality by −5 and then adding 3, we get the following:

(23)x−1>0−5(23)x−1<0−5(23)x−1+3<0+3f(x)<3

Therefore, the range is f(x)<3. Thus, of the given values, only y=−3 and y=2 are outputs of y=f(x).
