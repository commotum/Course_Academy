Let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. An icosahedron is a regular polyhedron that

    • consists of 20 congruent faces, each in the form of an equilateral triangle, and

    • exactly 5 faces meet at each vertex.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, since there are F=20 faces each in the form of a triangle, we have S=20⋅3=60 sides combined. Now, each edge of the polyhedron is created when two face sides coincide. So, the number of edges of the icosahedron must be equal to S2=602=30.

  • Statement III is false. Substituting the number of edges E=30 and the number of faces F=20 into Euler's formula, we can solve for the number of vertices V as follows: V−E+F=2V−(30)+(20)=2V−10=2V=12≠14

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
