  1. A tetrahedron has 4 congruent faces, each in the form of an equilateral triangle
  2. An octahedron has 12 congruent faces, each in the form of an equilateral triangle
  3. A dodecahedron has 12 congruent faces, each in the form of an equilateral triangle

EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. A tetrahedron has 4 congruent faces, where each face is an equilateral triangle.

  • Statement II is false. An octahedron is a regular polyhedron consisting of 8 (not 12) congruent faces, where each face is an equilateral triangle.

  • Statement III is false. A dodecahedron is a regular polyhedron consisting of 12 congruent faces, where each face is a regular pentagon (not an equilateral triangle).

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
