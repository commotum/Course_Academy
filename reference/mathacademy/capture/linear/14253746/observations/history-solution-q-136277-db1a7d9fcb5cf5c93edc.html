Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.

  • Let's first consider the angle 60∘. From the unit circle, we see that the angle 60∘ corresponds to the point (12,32). Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡60∘=12.

  • Now, let's consider the angle 90∘. From the unit circle, we see that the angle 90∘ corresponds to the point (0,1). Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡90∘=1.

Finally, we combine the results:

cos⁡60∘−sin⁡90∘=12−1=−12
