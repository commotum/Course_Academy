A bag contained 97kg of soil. Annie poured 13 of the bag into a pot. How many kilograms of soil did Annie pour into the pot?

a
314kg
b
37kg
c
67kg
d
910kg
e
914kg
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find out the weight of soil that Annie poured, we need to calculate 97×13.

To multiply the two numbers, we multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators:

97×13=9×17×3=921

Next, we simplify: 921=9÷321÷3=37

So, Annie poured 37kg of soil into the pot.
