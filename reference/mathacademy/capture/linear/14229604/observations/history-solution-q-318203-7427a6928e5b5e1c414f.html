The number of downloads D(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 120%.

Let's write down what we know:

  • a=60 is the total number of downloads initially
  • r=1.2 is the growth rate (120% expressed as a decimal)
  • t= the time (in days) after launch

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

D=a(1+r)t.

Therefore, the correct expression for D(t) is

D=60⋅(1+1.2)t.

Simplifying the above, we get

D(t)=60⋅(2.2)t.
