Which of the following equations best represents this model, where D is the estimated number of downloads t days after the app was launched, and t≤7?

a
 D(t)=60⋅(0.2)t
b
 D(t)=60⋅(2.2)t
c
 D(t)=60⋅(1.2)t
d
 D(t)=60⋅(12)t
e
 D(t)=60⋅(22)t
