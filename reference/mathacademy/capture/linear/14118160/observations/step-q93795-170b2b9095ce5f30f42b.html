Which of the following Diophantine equations have solutions?

  1. 3x+7y=11
  2. −3x−9y=2
  3. −12x+15y=21
a
I and III only
b
I and II only
c
III only
d
I only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that a linear Diophantine equation ax+by=c has a solution if and only if d|c, where d=gcd(a,b).

With that in mind, let's examine each equation in turn.

  • Equation I has solutions. We have a=3, b=7, and c=11. Since d=gcd(3,7)=1 and 1|11, our equation has solutions.

  • Equation II has no solution. We have a=−3, b=−9, and c=2. Since d=gcd(−3,−9)=3 and 3⧸|2, our equation has no solution.

  • Equation III has solutions. We have a=−12, b=15, and c=21. Since d=gcd(−12,15)=3 and 3|21, our equation has solutions.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
