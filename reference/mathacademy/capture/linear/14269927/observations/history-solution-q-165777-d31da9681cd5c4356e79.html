Let A represent the event described in the question. Then

P(A)=nN, where N is the total number of ways to pick 3 jelly beans out of 25, and n is the number of ways to choose exactly 2 green and 1 red.

Since the order in which Ted picks the jelly beans does not matter, we use combinations.

First, we compute N. The number of ways to pick 3 jelly beans from a total of 25 is

N=25C3=25!22!⋅3!=2300.

Now, we compute n. The computation of n can be divided into two steps:

  • the number of ways to choose 2 (out of 10) green jelly beans is 10C2=10!8!⋅2!=45.

  • the number of ways to choose 1 (out of 15) red jelly beans is 15C1=15!14!⋅1!=15.

Multiplying the results from the above steps, we get

n=10C2⋅15C1=45⋅15=675.

Finally, P(A)=6752300=2792≈0.29=29%.
