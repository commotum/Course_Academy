Alex has 10 marbles in a bag, 6 blue, and 4 red. He randomly chooses 3 of them. What is the probability that Alex picks exactly 1 blue and 2 red marbles? Round your answer to the nearest percent.

a
33%
b
30%
c
40%
d
42%
e
21%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let A represents the event described in the question. Then

P(A)=nN, where N is the total number of ways to pick 3 marbles out of 10, and n is the number of ways to choose exactly 1 blue and 2 red marbles.

Since the order in which he picks the marbles does not matter, we use combinations.

First, we compute N. The number of ways to pick 3 marbles from a total of 10 is

N=10C3=10!7!⋅3!=120.

Now, we compute n. The computation of n can be divided into two steps:

  • the number of ways to choose 1 (out of 6) blue marble is 6C1=6!5!⋅1!=6.

  • the number of ways to choose 2 (out of 4) red marbles is 4C2=4!2!⋅2!=6.

Multiplying the results from the above steps, we get

n=6C1⋅4C2=6⋅6=36.

Finally, P(A)=36120=310=0.3=30%.
