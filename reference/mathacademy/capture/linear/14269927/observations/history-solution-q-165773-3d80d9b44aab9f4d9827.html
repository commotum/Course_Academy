Let A represent the event described in the question. Then P(A)=nN,

where N is the total number of ways to pick 4 people out of 18, and n is the number of picks where 2 are men and 2 are women.

Since the order in which people will be chosen does not matter, we use combinations.

First, we compute N. The number of ways to pick 4 people from a total of 18 is

N=18C4=18!4!⋅14!=3060.

Now, we compute n. The computation of n can be divided into two steps:

  • the number of ways to choose 2 (out of 8) women is 8C2=8!2!⋅6!=28

  • the number of ways to choose 2 (out of 10) men is 10C2=10!2!⋅8!=45

Multiplying the results from the above steps, we get n=8C2⋅10C2=28⋅45=1260.

Finally, P(A)=12603060=717≈0.41=41%.
