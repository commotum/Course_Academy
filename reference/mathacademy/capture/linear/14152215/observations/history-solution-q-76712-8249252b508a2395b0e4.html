First, consider a ray AB→.

We place the midpoint of the protractor at the origin of AB→, that is, at the point A. Then we align this ray with the line that passes through the midpoint and zero of the protractor.

In the internal scale of the protractor, we locate the mark associated with the given measure (in this case, 50∘) and then we draw a point to the margin of the protractor. We will call this point C.

Finally, we draw the ray AC→.

Hence, the angle ∠BAC has a measure of 50∘.
