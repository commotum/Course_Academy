We convert the absolute value inequality into a compound inequality to remove the absolute value.

The inequality specifies every number 14−x whose distance from zero is less than 74. This corresponds to the following compound inequality:

−74<14−x<74

Now, we can solve the compound inequality using the usual methods. (Remember that whenever we multiply or divide by a negative number, we have to flip the inequality signs.)

−74<14−x<74−2<−x<322>x>−32

To make the final result easier to read, we will rewrite it so that the smaller number is on the left: −32<x<2
