EXPLANATION

The area of the triangle is defined as A=b⋅h2, where b is a base and h is the corresponding height.

From the diagram, we see that the base is b=AB, and the corresponding height is

h=CH=14m.

Therefore, using the formula for the area, we can solve for the base as follows:

A=b⋅h2140=b⋅142280=b⋅14b=28014b=20m

As a result, AB=20m and hence, we have

HB=AB−AH=20−15=5m.
