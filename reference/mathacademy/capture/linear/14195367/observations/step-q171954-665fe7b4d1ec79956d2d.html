EXPLANATION

The area of the triangle is defined as A=b⋅h2, where b is a base and h is the corresponding height.

From the diagram, we see that the base is b=AB=12cm and the corresponding height is h=6cm.

Therefore, the area is

A=b⋅h2=12⋅62=722=36cm2.
