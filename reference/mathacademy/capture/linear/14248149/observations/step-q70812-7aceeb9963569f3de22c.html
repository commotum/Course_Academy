EXPLANATION

Let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The root x=0 has an odd multiplicity because at x=0 the graph passes through the x-axis at this point.

  • Statement II is false. The root x=0 is not a simple root because the graph crosses the x-axis at that point, and the curve is tangent to the x-axis at that point.

  • Statement III is true. As x moves right in the positive direction towards +∞, the y value moves up in the positive direction towards +∞.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only".
