EXPLANATION

Let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The root x=2 is a simple root because the graph crosses the x-axis at the point and the curve is not tangent to the x-axis at that point.

  • Statement II is true. The root x=−1 has an even multiplicity because at x=−1 the graph just touches the x-axis and reverses direction.

  • Statement III is false. As x moves right in the positive direction towards +∞, the y value moves up in the positive direction towards +∞.
