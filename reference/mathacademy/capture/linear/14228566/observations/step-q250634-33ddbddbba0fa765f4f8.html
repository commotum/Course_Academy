EXPLANATION

The given parabola is written in standard form, y=ax2+bx+c,

with a=1>0, b=−6, and c=10.

Because the leading coefficient is positive, the parabola opens upward. Consequently, there is no maximum output, and the vertex corresponds to the minimum output.

The x-coordinate of the vertex is given by x=−b2a=−(−6)2⋅1=3.

Now, we substitute x=3 back into the function to find the y-value of the vertex: y=x2−6x+10=(3)2−6(3)+10=9−18+10=1

Therefore, the vertex is (3,1), and the range of the function is [1,∞), i.e. 1≤f(x)<∞.
