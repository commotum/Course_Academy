If the expression x2+14x+31 is written as (x+p)2+q, then what are the values of p and q?
p=
7
, q=
−18
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we rewrite the coefficient of the linear term as a product with 2 as a factor:

x2+2⋅7x+31

Now, here's the trick. We add and then subtract 72, writing the constant term 31 last:

x2+2⋅7x+72⏟−72+31

The first three terms form a perfect square, which we factor as follows:

(x+7)2−72+31

Finally, evaluating the last two terms, we get

(x+7)2−18

Therefore, p=7 and q=−18.
