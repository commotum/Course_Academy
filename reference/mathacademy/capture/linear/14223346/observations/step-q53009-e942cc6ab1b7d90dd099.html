EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. A kite is a quadrilateral with two pairs of congruent sides that are adjacent to each other. In the figure, we can see that AB=AD.

  • Statement II is true. A kite always has a pair of congruent opposite angles.

  • Statement III is false. A kite is a quadrilateral with two pairs of congruent sides that are adjacent to each other. However, not all sides are congruent, for instance, AB≠BC.

In conclusion, statements I and II are true.
