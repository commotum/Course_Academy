EXPLANATION

The first term is a1=40 and the second term is a2=20. Using these two terms, we compute the common ratio:

r=a2a1=2040=12

Therefore, the general term can be expressed as

an=a1(r)n−1=40(12)n−1=40(12)−1(12)n=40(21)(12)n=80(12)n.

Finally, the infinite geometric series can be written in sigma notation as 40+20+10+5+⋯=∑n=1∞an=∑n=1∞80(12)n.
