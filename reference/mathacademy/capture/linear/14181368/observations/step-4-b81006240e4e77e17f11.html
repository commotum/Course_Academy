Explanation

To produce x cakes, the pastry shop uses (5x+32) packs of chocolate. So, we have that

5x+32=9.

We need to solve this linear equation for x. First, we cross-multiply:

5x+32=91(5x+3)⋅1=9⋅2

Then, we proceed as usual to solve the equation:

(5x+3)⋅1=9⋅25x+3=185x+3−3=18−35x=155x5=155x=3

So, the pastry shop can produce 3 cakes.
