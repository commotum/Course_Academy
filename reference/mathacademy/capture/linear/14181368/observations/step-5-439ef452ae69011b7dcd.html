How many cakes does the pastry shop produce if they use equal numbers of packs of flour and butter?

Explanation

To produce x cakes, the pastry shop uses the following amounts:

  • 4x+16 packs of butter

  • 13x+520 packs of flour

If the pastry shop uses the same number of packs of butter and flour, we have that

4x+16=13x+520.

We need to solve this linear equation for x. First, we cross-multiply:

4x+16=13x+520(4x+1)⋅20=6⋅(13x+5)

Then, we proceed as usual to solve the equation:

(4x+1)⋅20=6⋅(13x+5)80x+20=78x+3080x+20−78x=78x+30−78x2x+20=302x+20−20=30−202x=10x=5.

Thus, x=5 cakes are produced when the pastry shop uses the same number of packs of butter and flour.
