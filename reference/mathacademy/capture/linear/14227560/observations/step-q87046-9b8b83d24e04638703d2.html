The graph of y=7f(2x−2)+5, can be obtained from the graph of y=f(x) by applying some of the following transformations:

  • (T1) - Translate up by 5 units

  • (T2) - Stretch by a scale factor of 7 parallel to the y-axis

  • (T3) - Translate right by 1 unit

  • (T4) - Stretch by a scale factor of 0.5 parallel to the x-axis

  • (T5) - Translate right by 2 units

Which of the following could be a possible ordering of the appropriate transformations?

a
(T4)→(T5)→(T3)
b
(T4)→(T3)→(T2)→(T1)
c
(T3)→(T4)→(T2)→(T1)
d
(T4)→(T3)→(T1)→(T2)
e
(T4)→(T3)→(T5)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To perform transformations of functions with multiple operations, we must remember the following rule:

  1. First, we perform the transformations inside the argument of f. We first carry out horizontal stretches, followed by horizontal translations.

  2. Then, we perform the transformations outside the argument of f. We first carry out vertical stretches, followed by vertical translations.

First, let's factor out the coefficient next to the x inside the function:

y=7f(2x−2)+5=7f(2(x−1))+5

To get y=7f(2(x−1))+5, we could apply the following steps:

  • Take the curve y=f(x).

  • Apply transformation (T4). Stretch y=f(x) by a scale factor of 12=0.5 parallel to the x-axis to get the graph of y=f(2x).

  • Apply transformation (T3). Translate y=f(2x) right by 1 unit to get the graph of y=f(2(x−1)).

  • Apply transformation (T2). Stretch y=f(2(x−1)) by a scale factor of 7 parallel to the y-axis to get the graph of y=7f(2(x−1)).

  • Apply transformation (T1). Translate y=7f(2(x−1)) up by 5 units to get the graph of y=7f(2(x−1))+5.

Therefore, a possible order of transformations is

(T4)→(T3)→(T2)→(T1).
