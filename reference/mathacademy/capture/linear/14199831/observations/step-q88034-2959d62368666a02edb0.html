Given that △ABC∼△LMN, BC=4ft and CA=2ft, which of the following could be the values of MN and NL?

  1. MN=2ft,NL=1ft
  2. MN=12ft,NL=6ft
  3. MN=10ft,NL=8ft
a
II only
b
II and III only
c
I and III only
d
I and II only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

For similar polygons, the ratios of lengths of the corresponding sides should be the same.

  • Let's check option I: BCMN=42=2,CANL=21=2. So, option I does give possible values of MN and NL.

  • For option II, we have BCMN=412=13,CANL=26=13. So, option II does give possible values of MN and NL.

  • For option III, we have BCMN=410=25,CANL=28=14. So, option III does not give possible values of MN and NL.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
