The two quadrilaterals shown above are similar. What is the correct similarity statement?

a
RTUV∼NOQP
b
RTUV∼NOPQ
c
RTUV∼QPON
d
RTUV∼ONQP
e
RTUV∼ONPQ

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's find the measures of unknown angles. Since the sum of the measures of internal angles of a quadrilateral is 360∘, we have

m∠R+m∠T+m∠U+m∠V=360∘m∠R+130∘+50∘+110∘=360∘m∠R+290∘=360∘m∠R=70∘.

Similarly,

m∠O+m∠N+m∠Q+m∠P=360∘70∘+130∘+m∠Q+110∘=360∘m∠Q+310∘=360∘m∠Q=50∘.

Hence, the quadrilaterals are similar, and

∠R≅∠O,∠T≅∠N,∠U≅∠Q,∠V≅∠P.

So, we conclude that

  • R corresponds to O,

  • T corresponds to N,

  • U corresponds to Q,

  • V corresponds to P.

Therefore, RTUV∼ONQP.
