Which of the above quadrilaterals are similar to P (other than P itself)?

a
R, S, and T
b
S only
c
T only
d
S and T only
e
R only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that P is a kite with a pair of opposite congruent angles and 2 pairs of congruent sides.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the given polygons.

  • R is not similar to P because R is not a kite.

  • S is similar to P. Indeed, S is a kite. Furthermore, the corresponding angles are congruent, and the ratios of the lengths of the corresponding sides are the same: 32.5=65

  • T is similar to P. Indeed, T is a kite. Furthermore, the corresponding angles are congruent, and the ratios of the lengths of the corresponding sides are the same: 2.52.5=55

Therefore, the correct answer is "S and T only".
