The two quadrilaterals shown above are similar. What is the correct similarity statement?

a
ABCD∼MNKL
b
ABCD∼LKNM
c
ABCD∼NKLM
d
ABCD∼NMLK
e
ABCD∼MLKN

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let's find the measures of unknown angles. Since the sum of the measures of internal angles of a quadrilateral is 360∘, we have

m∠A+m∠B+m∠C+m∠D=360∘68∘+m∠B+85∘+111∘=360∘m∠B+264∘=360∘m∠B=96∘.

Similarly,

m∠M+m∠N+m∠K+m∠L=360∘68∘+96∘+m∠K+111∘=360∘m∠K+275∘=360∘m∠K=85∘.

Hence, the quadrilaterals are similar, and

∠A≅∠M,∠B≅∠N,∠C≅∠K,∠D≅∠L.

So, we conclude that

  • A corresponds to M,

  • B corresponds to N,

  • C corresponds to K,

  • D corresponds to L.

Therefore, ABCD∼MNKL.
