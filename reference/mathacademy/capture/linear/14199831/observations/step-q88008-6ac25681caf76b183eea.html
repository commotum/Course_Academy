EXPLANATION

First, let's find the measures of unknown angles. Since the sum of the measures of internal angles of a triangle is 180∘, we have

m∠H+m∠J+m∠L=180∘m∠H+69∘+40∘=180∘m∠H+109∘=180∘m∠H=71∘.

Similarly,

m∠V+m∠W+m∠Y=180∘71∘+m∠W+40∘=180∘m∠W+111∘=180∘m∠W=69∘.

Hence, the triangles are similar, and

∠H≅∠V,∠J≅∠W,∠L≅∠Y.

So, we conclude that

  • H corresponds to V,

  • J corresponds to W,

  • L corresponds to Y.
