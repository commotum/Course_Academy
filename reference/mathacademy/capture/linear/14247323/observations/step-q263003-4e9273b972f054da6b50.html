−88
.

EXPLANATION

We substitute n=3 and n=5 into the definition of the sequence:

a3=7−3(3)2=−20a5=7−3(5)2=−68

Therefore, the sum of the third and fifth terms is a3+a5=(−20)+(−68)=−88.
