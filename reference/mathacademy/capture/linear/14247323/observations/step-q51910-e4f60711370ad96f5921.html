−9,−6,−3,0,6,12,18

EXPLANATION

For this sequence:

  • The first term is −9

  • The second term is −6

  • The third term is −3

  • The fourth term is 0

  • The fifth term is 6

  • The sixth term is 12

  • The seventh term is 18

Therefore, the sum of the fifth, sixth, and seventh terms is

6+12+18=36.
