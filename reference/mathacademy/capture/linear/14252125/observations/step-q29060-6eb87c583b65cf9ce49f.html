Solve the inequality −2x−8≤4.

a
x≥−4
b
x≤−6
c
x≤−4
d
x≥−6
e
x≥2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we apply the addition principle, adding 8 to both sides: −2x−8≤4−2x−8+8≤4+8−2x+0≤12−2x≤12

Then, we apply the multiplication principle, dividing both sides by −2.

Remember, when multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number, we need to flip the inequality sign:

−2x≤12−2x−2≥12−2−2x−2≥−6x≥−6

Therefore, the solution is x≥−6.
