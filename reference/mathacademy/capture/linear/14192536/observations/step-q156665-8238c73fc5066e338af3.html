EXPLANATION

The variable t is being multiplied by −5. To isolate t, we can perform the opposite operation, which is dividing by −5. So, we divide both sides of the equation by −5.

Remember, when multiplying or dividing by a negative number, we need to flip the inequality:

−5t>15−5t−5<15−5−5t−5<−3

Now, we can cancel a common factor of −5 from both the numerator and denominator.

−5t−5<−3−5t−5<−3t<−3
