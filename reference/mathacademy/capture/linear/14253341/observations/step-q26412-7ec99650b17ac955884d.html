The point Q with coordinates (1,1) lies on the curve y=f(x). Which of the following points must lie on y=f(−x)?

a
(−1,−1)
b
(−1,1)
c
(1,1)
d
(1,−1)
e
(0,1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The transformation y=f(−x) reflects the curve y=f(x) in the y-axis.

First, notice that the point P(1,1) lies 1 unit to the right from the y-axis. So, under the action of the transformation, this point will be reflected 1 unit to the left from the y-axis.

We conclude that the point (−1,1) must lie on the graph of y=f(−x).
