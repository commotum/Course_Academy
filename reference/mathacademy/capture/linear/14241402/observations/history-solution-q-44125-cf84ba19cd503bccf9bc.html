Remember that the graph of y=(x−h)2+k is a translation of the standard parabola y=x2 by

  • h units right, and
  • k units up.

The vertex of y=(x−h)2+k is located at (h,k).

Here, the parabola is translated 5 units left, so we have h=−5. The parabola is also translated 3 units up, so we have k=3.

The vertex is located at (−5,3), and these are the coordinates of the minimum point.
