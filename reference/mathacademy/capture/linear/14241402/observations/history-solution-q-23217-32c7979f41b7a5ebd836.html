In general, the graph of y=(x−h)2 looks like the standard parabola y=x2 but translated h units right. The vertex of y=(x−h)2 is located at (h,0).

For the given function y=(x−1)2, we have h=1.

Therefore, the given curve is the parabola y=x2 after being translated 1 unit right.

The vertex is located at (1,0), and these are the coordinates of the minimum point.
