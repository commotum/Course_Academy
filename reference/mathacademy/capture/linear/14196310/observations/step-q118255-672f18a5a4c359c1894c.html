Two vectors are equal if and only if they lie on parallel lines and have both the same magnitude and the same direction.

The vectors b and d have opposite directions, hence, b≠d.

On the other hand, c≠b and c≠d because they are not parallel.

So, the only pair of equal vectors is a=e.
