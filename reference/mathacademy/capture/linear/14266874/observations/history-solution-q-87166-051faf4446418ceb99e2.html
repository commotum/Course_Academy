Given the graph of a quadratic function:

  • if the graph has 2 roots, then D>0,

  • if the graph has 1 root, then D=0, and

  • if the graph has no roots, then D<0.

For the given graph of the parabola y=ax2+bx+c:

  • Notice that a is the leading coefficient. Since the parabola opens downward, we have that a<0. Therefore, statement I is true.

  • Also, notice that the parabola doesn't intersect the x-axis, so it has no roots. Therefore, we must have D<0. Hence, statement II is true, while statement III is false.

In conclusion, only statements I and II are true.
