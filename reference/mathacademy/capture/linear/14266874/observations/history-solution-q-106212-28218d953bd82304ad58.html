Given the graph of a quadratic function:

  • if the graph has 2 roots, then D>0,

  • if the graph has 1 root, then D=0, and

  • if the graph has no roots, then D<0.

For the given graph of the parabola y=ax2+bx+c:

  • Notice that a is the leading coefficient. Since the parabola opens upward, we have that a>0. Therefore, statement III is true.

  • Also, notice that the parabola intersects the x-axis twice, so it has 2 roots. Therefore, we must have D>0. Hence, statement I is false, while statement II is true.

In conclusion, only statements II and III are true.
