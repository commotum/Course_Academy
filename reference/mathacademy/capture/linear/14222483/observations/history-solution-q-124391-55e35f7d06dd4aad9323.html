To help us to visualize the angle, we first convert the measure of the angle into degrees.

To convert the measure of an angle in radians to an equivalent measure in degrees, we multiply the measure in radians by 180∘π. This gives

(−5π9)⋅(180∘π)=−100∘.

Therefore, −5π9 is equivalent to −100∘.
