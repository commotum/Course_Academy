A school's softball team lost 18% of the games they played last year. If the team lost 9 games last year, how many games did the team win?

EXPLANATION

We need to know the total number of games played to determine the number of games won.

We're given a part and a percentage, and we need to find the whole.

In this case, the part is the number of games the team lost, which is 9. The percentage is 18%, and the whole is the total number of games played.

To find the whole, we can use the following formula: whole=part÷percentage

Before we can substitute our numbers into the formula, we need to express 18% as a fraction:

18100=18÷2100÷2=950

We can now substitute our numbers into the formula, as follows:

whole=9÷18%=9÷950=9×509=99×50=50 Therefore, the total number of games played by the softball team last year was 50. The total number of games won is 50−9=41. Hence, the softball team won 41 games last year.
