EXPLANATION

Our statement can be written as

∀n∈Z,9n−4 is even.

Since this statement is false, its negation is true.

A counterexample is a particular case that disproves the statement. Equivalently, it's a case that proves the negation is true.

The negation of our statement is

¬(∀n∈Z,9n−4 is even)≡∃n∈Z,9n−4 is odd.

Therefore, we have the following:

A counterexample for the above statement would be an integer n such that 9n−4 is odd.
