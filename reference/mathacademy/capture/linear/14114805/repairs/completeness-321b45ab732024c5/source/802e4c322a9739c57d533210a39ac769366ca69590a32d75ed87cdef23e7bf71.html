EXPLANATION

Our statement can be written as

∀n∈Z,n2−6n+8≥0.

Since this statement is false, its negation is true.

A counterexample is a particular case that disproves the statement. Equivalently, it's a case that proves the negation is true.

The negation of our statement is ¬(∀n∈Z,n2−6n+8≥0)≡∃n∈Z,n2−6n+8<0.
