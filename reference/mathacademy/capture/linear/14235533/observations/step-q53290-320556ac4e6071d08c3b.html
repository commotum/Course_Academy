The perimeter of the rectangle LMNP above equals 22cm. What is the length of NP―?

a
8cm
b
6cm
c
7cm

10cm

9cm

Correct
EXPLANATION

The opposite sides of a rectangle are congruent, so we have

PL=MN=3cm.

For the same reason, we also have

NP=LM.

Now, since the perimeter is p=22cm, we have

p=LM+MN+NP+PL22=2NP+2PL22=2NP+616=2NP8=NP.

Therefore, NP=8cm.
