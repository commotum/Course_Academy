To make waffles, Joshua mixes 5 eggs, 1 cup of sugar, 200 grams of butter, and 1 cup of flour. What ratio describes the amount of sugar to the amount of flour?

a
1:2
b
3:1
c
2:1
d
1:1
e
1:3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We can express the ratio of the amount of sugar to the amount of flour as follows:

amount of sugar:amount of flour

We compute our ratio as follows:

  • First, we count the amount of sugar. There is 1 cup of sugar.

  • Next, we count the amount of flour. There is 1 cup of flour.

Therefore, the ratio of sugar to flour is 1:1.
