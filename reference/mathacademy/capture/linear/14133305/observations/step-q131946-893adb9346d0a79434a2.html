To make a fruit sponge, Dimo mixes 3 pounds of cherries with 2 pounds of raisins. What ratio describes the amount of cherries to the amount of raisins?

a
3:5
b
2:5
c
3:2
d
5:2
e
2:3
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We can express the ratio of the amount of cherry to the amount of raisins as follows:

amount of cherry:amount of raisins

We compute our ratio as follows:

  • First, we count the amount of cherries. There are 3 pounds of cherries.

  • Next, we count the amount of raisins. There are 2 pounds of raisins.

Therefore, the ratio of cherries to raisins is 3:2.
