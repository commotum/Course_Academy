The ratio of chocolate chip cookies to total cookies in a bakeshop is 8:10. What is the meaning of this ratio?

a
The bakeshop has exactly 2 sugar-coated cookies.
b
Out of every 8 cookies in the shop, 10 of them are chocolate chip.
c
The bakeshop has exactly 18 cookies.
d
Out of every 10 cookies in the shop, 8 of them are chocolate chip.
e
The bakeshop has exactly 10 chocolate chip cookies.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the ratio of chocolate chip cookies to total cookies is 8:10, it means that there are 8 chocolate chip cookies for every 10 cookies in total.

Another way of writing this is to say that "out of every 10 cookies in the shop, 8 of them are chocolate chip."
