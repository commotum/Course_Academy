Matt decides to distribute some candy to his best friends. The following table shows the number of candies each of his friends received.

Friends Rose Peter Adriana Vanessa Helen Luis Jason
Candies 5 6 10 9 4 3 6

What is the ratio of friends who received at least 8 pieces of candy to those who received less than 8 pieces of candy?

The required ratio is
2
:
5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

From the table, there are 2 friends (Adriana and Vanessa) who received at least 8 candies.

On the other hand, there are 5 friends (Rose, Peter, Helen, Luis, and Jason) who received less than 8 candies.

So the ratio of friends who received at least 8 pieces of candy to those who received less than 8 pieces of candy is 2:5.
