The number of visitors V(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 300%.

Let's write down what we know:

  • a=25 is the total number of visitors initially

  • r=3.0 is the growth rate (300% expressed as a decimal)

  • t= the time (in days) after launch

The formula for exponential growth is

V=a(1+r)t.

Therefore, the correct expression for V(t) is

V=25⋅(1+3.0)t.

Simplifying the above, we get

V(t)=25⋅(4)t.

To find the total number of visitors after 3 days, we evaluate the above for t=3:

V(3)=25⋅(4)3=25⋅64=1600

Therefore, the estimated number of visitors after 3 days is 1600.
