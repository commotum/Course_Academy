At a wedding, the guests may have ice cream or custard with their dessert. There are 80 guests at the wedding. It was found that 35 guests prefer ice cream, 38 guests custard, and 10 prefer both. Draw a Venn diagram representing this sample space. If a person is selected at random among the 80 guests, what is the probability that they prefer ice cream but not custard with dessert? Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

a
0.13
b
0.31
c
0.22

0.33

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let us call A the event "the guest prefers ice cream with dessert" and B the event "the guest prefers custard with dessert."

Note that there are 10 people who prefer both. We draw two overlapping circles representing the events A and B and write the number 10 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of people who prefer only ice cream with dessert is 35−10=25, so we write the number 25 in the part of circle A that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of people who prefer only custard with dessert is 38−10=28, so we write the number 28 in the part of the circle B that does not overlap with circle A.

The total number of guests who prefer either an ice cream or a custard with dessert is 10+25+28=63, and there are 80 people in total, so the number of remaining people is 80−63=17.

To represent these 17 people who do not prefer ice cream or custard, we write the number 17 outside of the circles A and B.
