An optician registered 80 customers last week. Of the customers he registered, 55 purchased corrective glasses, 32 purchased sunglasses, and 18 purchased both types of glasses. Draw a Venn diagram representing this sample space. If a customer is selected at random among the 80 customers, what is the probability that the customer purchased neither corrective glasses nor sunglasses? Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

a
 0.23
b
 0.34
c
 0.14
d
 0.18
e
 0.19
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let us call A the event "customer purchased corrective glasses" and B the event "customer purchased sunglasses."

Note that there are 18 customers that bought both types of glasses. We draw two overlapping circles representing the events A and B and write the number 18 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of customers that bought only corrective glasses is 55−18=37, so we write the number 37 in the part of circle A that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of customers that bought only sunglasses is 32−18=14, so we write the number 14 in the part of circle B that does not overlap with circle A.

The total number of customers that bought corrective glasses or sunglasses is 18+37+14=69, and there are 80 customers in total, so the number of remaining customers is 80−69=11.

To represent these 11 customers that purchased neither corrective glasses nor sunglasses, we write the number 11 outside of the circles A and B.
