Calculate dfdx for f(x)=5ln⁡(3−2x)+7.

a
123−2x
b
5ln⁡(3−2x)
c
−10ln⁡(3−2x)
d
53−2x
e
−103−2x
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we express f(x)=5ln⁡(3−2x)+7 as the composite function

f=5ln⁡u+7,u=3−2x. Differentiating gives

dfdu=5u,dudx=−2.

We now apply the chain rule, and we get

dfdx=dfdu⋅dudx=5u⋅(−2)=−10u=−103−2x.

?

Calculate dfdx for f(x)=ln⁡(4−3x)+3x.

a
−34−3x+3
b
34−3x
c
3x4−3x
d
94−3x
e
4−3xx+3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let g(x)=ln⁡(4−3x) and h(x)=3x. Then,

f(x)=g(x)+h(x).

First, we differentiate g(x). We express g(x)=ln⁡(4−3x) as the composite function g=ln⁡u,u=4−3x.

Differentiating gives

dgdu=1u,dudx=−3.

We now apply the chain rule, and we get dgdx=dgdu⋅dudx=1u⋅(−3)=−3u=−34−3x

Next, we differentiate h(x)=3x:

dhdx=3

Hence, f′(x)=g′(x)+h′(x)=−34−3x+3.
