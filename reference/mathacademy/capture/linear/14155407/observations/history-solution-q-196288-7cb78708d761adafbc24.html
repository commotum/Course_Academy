Dividing a fraction by another fraction is equivalent to multiplying the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

The reciprocal of 49 is 94. So we have 23÷49=23×94

Now, we multiply fractions. We multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators: 23×94=2×93×4=1812

Finally, we simplify: 1812=18÷612÷6=32
