Question 1 ?
Maria, Esther, and Albert each bought 1 pound of canary seed and 3 pounds of sunflower seeds. In total, they bought
12
pounds of seeds.

Correct
Explanation

We must write and evaluate an expression representing the total number of pounds of seeds.

Since each of them bought a pound of canary seed and 3 pounds of sunflower seeds, the number of pounds each person bought is: 1⏟canary seeds+3⏟sunflower seeds

Since each person purchased this amount of seeds, and there are 3 people, we must multiply the number of pounds of seeds by 3. Therefore, the total amount of seeds is: (1+3)⏟each person×3⏟people

To evaluate the expression, we follow the order of operations with parentheses.

First, we perform the operations inside the parentheses:

(1+3)×3=(1+3)×3=4×3.

Now, we multiply: 4×3=12
