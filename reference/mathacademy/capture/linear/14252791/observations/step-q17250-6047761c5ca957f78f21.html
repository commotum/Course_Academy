Given that f(x)=2⋅3x, find the slope of the tangent at x=1.

a
2ln⁡3
b
5ln⁡3
c
2ln⁡3+3
d
ln⁡3+6
e
6ln⁡3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Applying the formula, we get f′(x)=ddx(2⋅3x)=2ddx(3x)=2ln⁡3⋅3x.

Therefore, the slope of the tangent at x=1 is dfdx|x=1=2ln⁡3⋅3x|x=1=2ln⁡3⋅31=2ln⁡3⋅3=6ln⁡3.
