To find the GCF, we list the factors of the coefficients and identify any common variable factors in the expressions.

The factors of 25 are 1,5,25.

The factors of 10 are 1,2,5,10.

The common factors of 25 and 10 are 1,5.

The greatest of these common factors is 5.

Now we look at the variable factors. Both 25z and 10z contain z.

Thus, the greatest common factor of 25z and 10z is 5z.
