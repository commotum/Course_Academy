Which of the above quadrilaterals are similar to T (other than T itself)?

a
K and M only
b
M and R only
c
R only
d
M only
e
K only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that T is a right trapezoid with one pair of parallel opposite sides.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the given polygons.

  • K is not similar to T. The polygon K is a right trapezoid, and the corresponding angles are congruent, but the ratios of the lengths of the corresponding sides are not the same: 714≠66

  • R is not similar to T because R is not a trapezoid (it's a rectangle since it has 2 pairs of opposite parallel sides, and 2 pairs of right angles).

  • M is similar to T. Indeed, M is a right trapezoid. Furthermore, the corresponding angles are congruent, and the ratios of the lengths of the corresponding sides are the same: 714=36

Therefore, the correct answer is "M only".
