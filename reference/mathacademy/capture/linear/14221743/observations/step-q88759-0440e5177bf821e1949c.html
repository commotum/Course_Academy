What function represents a stretch of factor 5 and invariant x-axis, followed by a stretch of factor 2 and invariant y-axis?

a
(x,y)↦(2x,5y)
b
(x,y)↦(x+2,y+5)
c
(x,y)↦(5x,2y)
d
(x,y)↦(x+5,y+2)
e
(x,y)↦(x,5y)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

  • A stretch of factor 5 and invariant x-axis can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(x,5y).

  • A stretch of factor 2 and invariant y-axis can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(2x,y).

Therefore, the combination of the stretches is given by

(x,y)↦(2x,5y).
