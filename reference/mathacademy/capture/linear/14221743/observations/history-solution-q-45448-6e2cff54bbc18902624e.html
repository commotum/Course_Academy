A stretch of factor 3 in the x-direction and 2 in the y-direction can be represented by the following function: (x,y)↦(3x,2y)

To stretch a polygon, we

  • find the images of the vertices, and then

  • draw segments connecting the image vertices.

So, our three vertices (1,3), (1,−2), (−2,2) are mapped to the following points by the stretch:

(1,3)↦(3⋅1,2⋅3)=(3,6)(1,−2)↦(3⋅1,2⋅(−2))=(3,−4)(−2,2)↦(3⋅(−2),2⋅2)=(−6,4)
