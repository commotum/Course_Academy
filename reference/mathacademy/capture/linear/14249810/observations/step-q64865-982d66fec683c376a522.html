EXPLANATION

The angle 3π2 is a quadrantal angle. Let's draw out the quadrantal angles on the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle 3π2 corresponds to the point (0,−1).

  • Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡(3π2)=0.

  • Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡(3π2)=−1.

Therefore, using the fact that tan⁡θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θ, we have

tan⁡(3π2)=sin⁡(3π2)cos⁡(3π2)=−10.

However, division by zero is undefined. Therefore, tan⁡(3π2) is undefined.
