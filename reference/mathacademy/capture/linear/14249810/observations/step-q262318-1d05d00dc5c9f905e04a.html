The angle 90∘ is a quadrantal angle. Let's draw out the quadrantal angles on the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle 90∘ corresponds to the point (0,1).

Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡(90∘)=1.
