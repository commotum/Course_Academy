Either the positive or negative of the expression inside the absolute value must be equal to the right-hand side of the equation.

So, we set up the two corresponding equations and solve them independently:

  • If |3−23y|=3−23y, we obtain: |3−23y|=53−23y=5−23y=2 Multiplying both sides of the equation by −32 gives −23y=2(−32)⋅(−23y)=(−32)⋅(2)(−32)⋅(−23y)=−62y=−3.

  • If |3−23y|=−(3−23y), we obtain: |3−23y|=5−(3−23y)=5−3+23y=523y=8 Multiplying both sides of the equation by 32 gives 23y=8(32)⋅(23y)=(32)⋅(8)(32)⋅(23y)=242y=12.

Therefore, the solutions are y=−3 and y=12.
