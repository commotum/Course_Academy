EXPLANATION

First, using the power law for exponents, we can write our expression as

53/4=(53)1/4.

The fractional exponent x1/n can be written as the radical xn.

Comparing x1/n with our expression above, we get x=53 and n=4. Therefore,

(53)1/4=534.
