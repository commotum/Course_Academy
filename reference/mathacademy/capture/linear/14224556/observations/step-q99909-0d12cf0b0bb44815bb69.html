Express 8ln⁡7+8ln⁡3 as a single logarithm.

a
ln⁡80
b
ln⁡32
c
4ln⁡21
d
8ln⁡4
e
8ln⁡21
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let's recall the product rule for logarithms:

logn⁡a+logn⁡b=logn⁡(ab)

If we factor the given expression and use the product rule for logarithms, we get

8ln⁡7+8ln⁡3=8(ln⁡7+ln⁡3)=8ln⁡(7⋅3)=8ln⁡21.

Therefore, 8ln⁡7+8ln⁡3=8ln⁡21.
