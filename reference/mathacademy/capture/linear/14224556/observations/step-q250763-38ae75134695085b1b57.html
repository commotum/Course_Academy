EXPLANATION

First, let's recall the product rule for logarithms:

logn⁡a+logn⁡b=logn⁡(ab)

If we factor the given expression and use the product rule for logarithms, we get 4log⁡t+4log⁡(t2)+4log⁡(t3)=4(log⁡t+log⁡(t2)+log⁡(t3))=4log⁡(t⋅t2⋅t3)=4log⁡(t1+2+3)=4log⁡(t6).
