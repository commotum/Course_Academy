What is the magnitude of the number z=7i?

a
14
b
77
c
7
d
27
e
7

Correct
EXPLANATION

The imaginary number 7i is equivalent to the complex number z=0+7i.

We know that for a complex number z=a+bi, the magnitude of z is given by |z|=a2+b2.

So, the magnitude of z=0+7i is given by

|z|=a2+b2=(0)2+(7)2=0+7=7.
