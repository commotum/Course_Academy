Since the exponent of the binomial expression is n=5, we see that the exponents of the binomial expansion must follow the pattern s0,s1,s2,s3,s4,s5. Of these, the term that returns s5 is the fifth. So, we are looking for the fifth term in the binomial expansion.

The 5th row in Pascal's Triangle is (1,5,10,10,5,1). Therefore, the fifth term in the binomial expansion is 1(3)0(2s)5=32s5.

So, the coefficient of s5 in the expansion of (3+2s)5 is 32.
