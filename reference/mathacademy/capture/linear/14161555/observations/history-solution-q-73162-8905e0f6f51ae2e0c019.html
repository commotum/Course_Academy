Since the exponent of the binomial expression is n=4, we see that the exponents of the binomial expansion must follow the pattern z4x0,z3x1,z2x2,z1x3,z0x4,

Of these, the term that returns x4 is the fifth. So, we are looking for the fifth term in the binomial expansion.

The 4th row in Pascal's Triangle is (1,4,6,4,1). Therefore, the fifth term in the binomial expansion is

1(z)0(2x)4=1(1)(16x4)=16x4.

So, the coefficient of x4 in the expansion of (z+2x)4 is 16.
