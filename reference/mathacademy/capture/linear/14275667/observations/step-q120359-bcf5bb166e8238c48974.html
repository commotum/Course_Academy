Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The addition law for probability states that

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B).

So, to calculate P(A∪B), we apply the addition law: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)=15+14−18=1340
