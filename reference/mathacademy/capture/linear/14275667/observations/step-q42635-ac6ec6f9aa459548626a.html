Suppose that A and B are two events such that P(A)=0.6, P(B)=0.3, and P(A∩B)=0.2, find P(A∪B).

a
0.2
b
0.9
c
0.8
d
0.7
e
0.5
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The addition law for probability states that

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B).

So, to calculate P(A∪B), we apply the addition law: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)=0.6+0.3−0.2=0.7
