EXPLANATION

The "square of a difference" formula states that

a2−2ab+b2=(a−b)2.

We will use this formula to factor the given trinomial. Our trinomial is

9x2−12x+4.

First, we write both the quadratic term and the constant term as perfect squares: (3x)2−12x+(2)2

Next, we factor a 2 out of the linear term: (3x)2−2(6x)+(2)2

Then, we write the middle parentheses (6x) as a product of 3x and 2: (3x)2−2(3x⋅2)+(2)2

Finally, we factor the trinomial using the square of difference formula with a=3x and b=2. This gives (3x−2)2.
