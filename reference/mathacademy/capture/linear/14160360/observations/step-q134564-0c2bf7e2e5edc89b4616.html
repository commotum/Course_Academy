First, we use the product rule for radicals, as follows:

5003=125⋅43=1253⋅43=543

As a result,

45003+43−3=4(543)+43−3=2043+43−3.

Now, we can combine the two 43 terms together:

2043+43−3=(2043+43)−3=(20+1)43−3=2143−3
