EXPLANATION

The variable z is being divided by 6. To isolate z and remove the negative sign, we multiply by −6

Remember, when multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number, we need to flip the inequality sign: 4<−z6(−6)⋅4>(−6)⋅(−z6)−24>(−6)⋅(−z6)

Since two negative numbers multiply to give a positive number, we first cancel the negatives: −24>(−6)⋅(−z6)−24>(−6)⋅(−z6)−24>6⋅z6

Now, we can cancel a common factor of 6 from both the numerator and denominator. −24>6⋅z6−24>6z6−24>6z6−24>z

Finally, we rewrite the inequality with the variable on the left-hand side: z<−24

Therefore, the solution is z<−24.
