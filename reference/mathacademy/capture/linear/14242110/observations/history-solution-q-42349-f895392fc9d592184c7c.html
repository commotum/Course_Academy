Note that there are 10 students who study both languages. So, we draw two overlapping circles representing the events A and B and write the number 10 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of students who only study English is 43−10=33, so we write the number 33 in the part of circle A that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of students who only study Spanish is 25−10=15, so we write the number 15 in the part of circle B that does not overlap with circle A.

There are 63 students in total, so the number of remaining students is 63−10−33−15=5.

To represent the 5 remaining students, we write the number 5 outside of the circles A and B.
