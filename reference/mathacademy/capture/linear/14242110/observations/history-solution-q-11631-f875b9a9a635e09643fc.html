Let us call A the event "the singer sings country music" and B the event "the singer sings pop music."

Note that there are 6 singers singing both country and pop music. So, we draw two overlapping circles representing the events A and B and write the number 6 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of singers who only sing country music is 27−6=21, so we write the number 21 in the part of circle A that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of singers who only sing pop music is 23−6=17, so we write the number 17 in the part of circle B that does not overlap with circle A.

The total number of singers who sing country or pop music is 6+21+17=44, and there are 50 singers in total, so the number of remaining singers is 50−44=6.

To represent these 6 singers who did not sing country or pop music, we write the number 6 outside of the circles A and B.
