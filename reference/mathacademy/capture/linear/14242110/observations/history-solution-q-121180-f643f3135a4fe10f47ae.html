Let us call A the event "the student has black hair" and B the event "the student has blue eyes."

Note that there are 7 students who have both black hair and blue eyes. So, we draw two overlapping circles representing the events A and B and write the number 7 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of students who only have black hair is 18−7=11, so we write the number 11 in the part of circle A that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of students who only have blue eyes is 9−7=2, so we write the number 2 in the part of circle B that does not overlap with circle A.

There are 30 students in total, so the number of remaining students is 30−11−7−2=10.

To represent these 10 students that have not black hair or blue eyes, we write the number 10 outside of the circles A and B.
