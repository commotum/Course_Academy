Note that there are 10 students who traveled by both train and bus. We draw two overlapping circles representing the events T and B and write the number 10 where the circles overlap.

  • The number of students who traveled by train only is 17−10=7, so we write the number 7 in the part of circle T that does not overlap with circle B.

  • The number of students who traveled by bus only is 23−10=13, so we write the number 13 in the part of circle B that does not overlap with circle T.

There are 40 students in total, so the number of remaining students is 40−7−10−13=10.

To represent the 10 remaining students, we write the number 10 outside of the circles T and B.
