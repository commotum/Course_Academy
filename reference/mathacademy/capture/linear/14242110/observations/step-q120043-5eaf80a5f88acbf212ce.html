EXPLANATION

To calculate the probability of an event using a Venn diagram, we count the number of outcomes in the region that represents the event, and divide by the total number of outcomes of the sample space S.

In the Venn diagram, A∪B is represented by the area that is inside both circles (see the illustration below).

We count up the outcomes:

  • The number of outcomes in A∪B is 6+1+4=11.

  • The total number of outcomes in S is 6+1+4+12=23.

So, we have P(A∪B)=1123≈0.48 to 2 decimal places.
