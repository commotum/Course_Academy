Let's recall the functional representations of our two transformations:

  • A reflection across the y-axis can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(−x,y).

  • Our translation is represented by the function (x,y)↦(x−7,y−3).

Let's apply the given transformations to the point P using our functions:

First, we apply the reflection. The image of the point (−6,4) under the reflection is

(−6,4)↦(6,4).

Then, we apply the translation. The image of the point (6,4) under the translation is

(6,4)↦(6−7,4−3)=(−1,1).

Therefore, the resulting point is (−1,1).
