In △OPR shown above, we have

  • OP=3|p|
  • OP→ has the opposite direction to p

Hence, OP→=−3p.

Similarly:

  • PR=2|q|
  • PR→ has the opposite direction to q

Hence, PR→=−2q.

Additionally,

r=OP→+PR→=−3p−2q.

Consequently, r=λp+μq=−3p−2q.

Since p∦q, the linear combination can only be written in one way. Equating the coefficients, we get:

  • λ=−3 and μ=−2
