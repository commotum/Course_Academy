To solve this problem, we can use the formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is the total number of emergencies that firefighters attended in the first year, which is 40 emergencies. The percentage is 165%, and the part is the total number of emergencies firefighters attended in the second year.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

165%=165÷100=1.65

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=165%×40=1.65×40=66

Therefore, 165% of 40 is 66. So, firefighters attended 66 emergencies in the second year.
