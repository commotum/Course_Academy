Find the midpoint of the line segment with endpoints (−w,7z) and (5w,−3z).

a
(−2w,−2z)
b
(−w,2z)
c
(−w,−z)
d
(2w,2z)
e
(2w,3z)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let (xm,ym) be the midpoint between (−w,7z)⏟(x1,y1) and (5w,−3z)⏟(x2,y2).

We use the midpoint formula as follows:

(xm,ym)=(x1+x22,y1+y22)=(−w+5w2,7z+(−3z)2)=(−w+5w2,7z−3z2)=(4w2,4z2)=(2w,2z).
