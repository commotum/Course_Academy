A vertical line l passes through the point (−4,−5). Calculate the coordinates of the x-intercept of l.

a
(0,−5)
b
There is no x-intercept
c
(−4,0)
d
(0,−4)
e
(−5,0)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

On any vertical line, all points have the same x-coordinate.

The x-coordinate of the point (−4,−5) is −4, so the x-coordinate of the x-intercept is also −4

Therefore, the coordinates of the x-intercept are (−4,0).
