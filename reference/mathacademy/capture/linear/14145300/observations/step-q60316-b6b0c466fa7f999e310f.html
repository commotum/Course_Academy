Theresa pours out 78 of the contents of an 8-cup jug of orange juice for her family's breakfast. How many cups of orange juice did Theresa pour from the jug?

a
6 cups
b
7 cups
c
87 cups
d
4 cups
e
5 cups
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find out how many cups of orange juice Theresa poured from the jug, we need to multiply 8 by 78.

To multiply a fraction by a whole number, we multiply the fraction's numerator by the whole number and keep the denominator the same:

78×8=7×88=568

Finally, we simplify:

568=56÷8=7

So, Theresa poured 7 cups of orange juice from the jug.
