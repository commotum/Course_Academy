Sandra is reading a book. She can read 34 of a page in one minute. Compute how many pages she can read in 10 minutes. Between what two whole numbers does your answer lie?

a
Between 7 and 8 pages
b
Between 6 and 7 pages
c
Between 8 and 9 pages
d
Between 4 and 5 pages
e
Between 3 and 4 pages
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find out how many pages she can read in 10 minutes, we need to multiply 34 by 10.

To multiply a fraction by a whole number, we multiply the fraction's numerator by the whole number and keep the denominator the same:

10×34=10×34=304

We then reduce this fraction to its lowest terms: 304=30÷24÷2=152.

Finally, we then convert 152 to a mixed number: 15÷2=7R1=712.

So, in 10 minutes, Sandra can read a total of 712 pages of the book, which lies between 7 and 8 pages.
