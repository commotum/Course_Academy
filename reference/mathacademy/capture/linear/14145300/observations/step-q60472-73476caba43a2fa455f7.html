A wooden slat is 3ft long. Eddie needs to cut a piece that's 58 the length of the slat. How long is the piece he has to cut?

a
218ft
b
358ft
c
178ft
d
338ft
e
138ft
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the length of the piece that Eddie has to cut, we have to multiply 3 by 58.

To multiply a fraction by a whole number, we multiply the fraction's numerator by the whole number and keep the denominator the same:

58×3=5×38=158

Finally, we then convert 158 to a mixed number: 15÷8=1R7=178.

So, Eddie has to cut a piece 178ft long.
