George rides his bike 910 of a mile each day, going from home to school and back. Compute how many miles he rides in 5 days just by going to school and back. Between what two whole numbers does your answer lie?

a
Between 4 and 5 miles
b
Between 2 and 3 miles
c
Between 3 and 4 miles
d
Between 1 and 2 miles
e
Between 5 and 6 miles

EXPLANATION

To find out how many kilometers George travels in 5 days just by going to school and back, we have to multiply 910 by 5.

To multiply a fraction by a whole number, we multiply the fraction's numerator by the whole number and keep the denominator the same:

5×910=5×910=4510

We then reduce this fraction to its lowest terms: 4510=45÷510÷5=92.

Finally, we then convert 92 to a mixed number: 9÷2=4R1=412.

So, in 5 days, George rides a total of 412mi, which lies between 4 and 5 miles.
