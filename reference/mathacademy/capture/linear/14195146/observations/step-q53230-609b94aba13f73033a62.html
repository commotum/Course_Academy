Correct
EXPLANATION

The area of a rectangle is given by

A=a⋅b,

where a and b are the lengths of the sides.

From the figure, we obtain that

a=AB=9cm,b=AD=5cm.

Therefore, the area of the rectangle is

A=a⋅b=9⋅5=45cm2.
