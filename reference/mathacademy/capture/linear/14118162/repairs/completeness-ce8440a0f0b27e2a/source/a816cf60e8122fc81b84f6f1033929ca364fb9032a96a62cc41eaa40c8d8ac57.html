EXPLANATION

De Morgan's law for disjunctions states that

¬(A∨B)=¬A∧¬B.

So, the negation of "a≤−1 or b=0" is

¬(a≤−1 or b=0)≡¬(a≤−1∨b=0)≡¬(a≤−1)∧¬(b=0)≡a>−1∧b≠0≡a>−1 and b≠0.
