EXPLANATION

De Morgan's law for disjunctions states that

¬(A∨B)=¬A∧¬B.

So, the negation of "a is even or b is odd" is

¬(a is even or b is odd )≡¬(a is even ∨b is odd )≡¬(a is even )∧¬(b is odd )≡a is odd ∧b is even ≡a is odd and b is even.
