First, we write 334 as an improper fraction:

334=(3×4)+34=154

We also write 225 as an improper fraction:

225=(2×5)+25=125

Now, we can multiply the two numbers.

154×125

We can simplify this product by swapping the denominators:

154×125=155×124=3×3=9

Therefore, we conclude that

334×225=9
