What is the missing digit in the following equality?

225×113=05

a
16
b
13
c
12
d
4
e
6
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we write 225 as an improper fraction:

225=(2×5)+25=125

We also write 113 as an improper fraction:

113=(1×3)+13=43

Now, we can multiply the two numbers. 125×43

We can simplify this product by swapping the denominators: 125×43=123×45=4×45=165

Therefore, the missing number is 16.
