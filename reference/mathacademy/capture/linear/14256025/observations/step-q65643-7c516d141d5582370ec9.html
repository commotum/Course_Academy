The graph above shows the function y=Asin⁡(5x4), where A is a positive constant. What is the value of A?

a
5
b
2
c
110
d
20
e
10
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The constant A is the amplitude of the function.

From the graph, we see that the maximum value is ymax=10 and the minimum value is ymin=−10. Therefore, the amplitude is A=ymax−ymin2=10−(−10)2=202=10.
