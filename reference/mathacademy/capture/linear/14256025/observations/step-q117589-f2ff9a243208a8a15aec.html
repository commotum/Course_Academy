The graph above shows the function y=4sin⁡(x+C). What is the value of C?

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The constant C is the horizontal shift of the function.

To plot the given graph, we follow these steps:

  • We take the curve y=sin⁡x.

  • Vertically stretch it with scale factor 4 to give y=4sin⁡x.

  • Then, shift it by 32 units right to give y=4sin⁡(x−32).

Therefore C=−32.
