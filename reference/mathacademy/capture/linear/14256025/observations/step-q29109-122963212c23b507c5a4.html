EXPLANATION

The period T of the function is

T=14π3−2π3=12π3=4π.

The formula for the period T of the function y=Acos⁡(Bx+C)+D is given by

T=2πB.

Therefore, applying the formula for T gives

4π=2πB⟹B=2π4π=12.

So B=12. Therefore, the equation of the function is
