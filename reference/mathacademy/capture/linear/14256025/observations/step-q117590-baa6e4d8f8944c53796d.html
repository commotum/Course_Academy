Correct
EXPLANATION

The constant D is the vertical shift of the function.

To plot the given graph, we follow these steps:

  • We take the curve y=sin⁡x.

  • Stretch it horizontally by a stretch factor of 2 to give y=sin⁡(x2).

  • Then, shift it by 2 units up to give y=sin⁡(x2)+2.

Therefore D=2.
