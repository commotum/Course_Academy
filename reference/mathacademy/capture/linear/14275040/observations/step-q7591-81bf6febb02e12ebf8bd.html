EXPLANATION

The major axis is the longest diameter of the ellipse, which is the line segment bounded by the points (2,1) and (2,−4). So, the length of the major axis is 1−(−4)=5.

The length of the semi-major axis is half the length of the major axis. So, the length of the semi-major axis is 52=2.5.
