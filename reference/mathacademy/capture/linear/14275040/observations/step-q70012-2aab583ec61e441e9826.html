The length of the semi-minor axis is half the length of the minor axis.

  • The minor axis is the shortest diameter of the ellipse, which is the line segment bounded by the points (−3,1) and (1,1). So, the length of the minor axis is 1−(−3)=4.

  • The length of the semi-minor axis, then, is 42=2.
