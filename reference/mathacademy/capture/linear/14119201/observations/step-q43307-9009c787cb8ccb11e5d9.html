Let A be a 3×3 matrix such that |A|=0. Which of the following statements must be true?

  1. A has a zero column
  2. A has two proportional rows
  3. One of the rows/columns of A is a linear combination of other rows/columns of A
a
 I and II only
b
 I, II, and III
c
 II only
d
 I only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's consider our statements in turn.

  • Statements I and II are false. For example, consider the matrix A=[112011123]. Expending across the 1st column, we have that |A|=1⋅|1123|+1|1211|=(3−2)+(1−2)=0 but A has neither a zero column nor two proportional rows.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, we can make use of the following fact:

    |A|=0 if and only if one of the rows/columns of A is a linear combination of other rows/columns of A.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only".
