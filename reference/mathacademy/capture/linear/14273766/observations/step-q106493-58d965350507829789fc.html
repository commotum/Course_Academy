EXPLANATION

Let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. We see from the graph that limx→−2−f(x)=limx→−2+f(x)=−2. Therefore, limx→−2f(x)=−2.

  • Statement II is true. Both limits limx→1−f(x) and limx→1+f(x) do not exist because the function f(x) is not given in the interval [0,2). Therefore,
    limx→1f(x)=DNE.

  • Statement III is false. As x approaches 2 from the left and right we get limx→2−f(x)=DNE,limx→2+f(x)=−1. Therefore, limx→2−f(x)≠limx→2+f(x).

In conclusion, only statements I and II are true.
