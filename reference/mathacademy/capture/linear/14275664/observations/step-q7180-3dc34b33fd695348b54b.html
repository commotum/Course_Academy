The displacement s of a particle moving in a straight line is given by s(t)=7t3+t2. Find the particle's acceleration at time t.

a
a(t)=84t5+2
b
a(t)=7t3+t2
c
a(t)=−21t5+2
d
a(t)=−21t4+2t
e
a(t)=84t5+4t
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we calculate v(t) by differentiating s(t):

v(t)=dsdt=ddt(7t3+t2)=−21t4+2t

Now, we differentiate v(t) to find a(t): a(t)=dvdt=ddt(−21t4+2t)=84t5+2
