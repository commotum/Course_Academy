An engineer increased a battery’s capacity to 135% of its original rating. Then, a safety reserve equal to 20% of that new capacity was set aside. If the original capacity was 4000 mAh, what is the safety reserve in mAh?
The safety reserve is
1080
mAh.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, the new capacity is 135% of the original rating (4000 mAh): 135%×4000=135100×4000=1.35×4000=5400

So, the new capacity is 5400 mAh. Then, the safety reserve is 20% of this new capacity:

20%×5400=20100×5400=0.20×5400=1080

Therefore, the safety reserve is 1080 mAh.
