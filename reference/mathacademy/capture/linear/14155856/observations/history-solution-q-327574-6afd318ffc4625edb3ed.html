Let x be the baseline enrollment.

We're told that enrollment grew to 180% of the baseline. Thus, the new enrollment is 1.8x.

We are also told that of those enrolled, 15% chose yoga. So, the number of people who chose yoga is 0.15(1.8x)=0.27x.

The expression 0.27x represents 27% of x. Therefore, 27% of the baseline chose yoga.
