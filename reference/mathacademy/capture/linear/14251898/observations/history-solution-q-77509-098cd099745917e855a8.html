First, recall that

−4a−3b=−4a+(−3b).

  • The vector −4a has the direction that is opposite to a and 4 times the magnitude.

  • The vector −b has the direction that is opposite to b and 3 times the magnitude.

Now, we can draw −3b starting from the head of −4a. The resultant −4a−3b goes from the tail of −4a to the head of −3b.
