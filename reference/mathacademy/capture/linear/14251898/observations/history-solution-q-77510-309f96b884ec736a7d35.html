First, recall that

−2p−4q=−2p+(−4q).

  • The vector −2p has the direction that is opposite to p and 2 times the magnitude.

  • The vector −4q has the direction that is opposite to q and 4 times the magnitude.

Now, we can draw −4q starting from the head of −2p. The resultant −2p−4q goes from the tail of −2p to the head of −4q.
