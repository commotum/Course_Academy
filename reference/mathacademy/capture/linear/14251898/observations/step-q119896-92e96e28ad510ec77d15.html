EXPLANATION

First, recall that

2a−2b=2a+(−2b).

  • The vector 2a has the same direction as a but twice the magnitude.

  • The vector −2b has the direction that is opposite to b but twice the magnitude.

Now, we can draw −2b starting from the head of 2a. The resultant 2a−2b goes from the tail of 2a to the head of −2b.
