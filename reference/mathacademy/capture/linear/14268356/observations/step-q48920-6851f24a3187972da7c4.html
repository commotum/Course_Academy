The values of functions f(x) and g(x) and their derivatives at x=2 are given in the table below.

x f(x) g(x) f′(x) g′(x)
2 4 3 5 1

If h(x)=(f(x)+1)(2g(x)−2), compute h′(2).

a
17
b
32
c
25
d
30
e
24
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The product rule states that ddx(uv)=udvdx+vdudx.

Let u=f(x)+1 and v=2g(x)−2. Differentiating each, we get dudx=f′(x)anddvdx=2g′(x).

Substituting this into the product rule gives

h′(x)=udvdx+vdudx=(f(x)+1)⋅2g′(x)+(2g(x)−2)⋅f′(x)=2g′(x)(f(x)+1)+2f′(x)(g(x)−1).

At x=2, this gives

h′(2)=2g′(2)(f(2)+1)+2f′(2)(g(2)−1)=2⋅1⋅(4+1)+2⋅5⋅(3−1)=2⋅5+10⋅2=30.
