Which of the following is equivalent to 7log⁡(5x)−2log⁡(5x)+5log⁡(3x)?

a
5log⁡(5x)−5log⁡(3x)
b
7log⁡(5x)−3log⁡(3x)
c
10log⁡(5x)
d
10log⁡(10x)
e
5log⁡(5x)+5log⁡(3x)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Both the first and the second logarithmic terms have a base of 10 and an argument of 5x. Therefore, we can combine them by adding their coefficients.

7log⁡(5x)−2log⁡(5x)+5log⁡(3x)=(7−2)log⁡(5x)+5log⁡(3x)=5log⁡(5x)+5log⁡(3x)
