Which of the following is the best upper bound for the length of BD―, obtained using the triangle inequality theorem only?

(Note: Figure not drawn to scale)

a
BD<9cm
b
BD<5cm
c
BD<7cm
d
BD<10cm
e
BD<8cm
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The triangle inequality theorem states that the length of any one side must be smaller than the sum of the other two sides.

  • Applying the triangle inequality theorem to △BAD, we get BD<BA+ADBD<4cm+5cmBD<9cm.

  • Applying the triangle inequality theorem to △BCD, we get BD<BC+CDBD<2cm+7cmBD<9cm.

Since in both cases BD<9cm must be satisfied, we conclude that

BD<9cm.
