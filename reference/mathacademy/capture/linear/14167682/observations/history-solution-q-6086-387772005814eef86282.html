The triangle inequality theorem states that the length of any one side must be smaller than the sum of the other two sides.

In particular, for the numbers 17,9,8 to represent the sides of a triangle, the number 17 must be smaller than the sum of the numbers 9 and 8. But this is not the case: 9+8=17≯17 Therefore, the three numbers 17,9,8 cannot represent the sides of a triangle.
