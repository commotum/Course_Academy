Which of the following is the best upper bound for the length of BC―, obtained using the triangle inequality theorem only?

(Note: Figure not drawn to scale)

a
BC<10cm
b
BC<9cm
c
BC<12cm
d
BC<13cm
e
BC<11cm
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The triangle inequality theorem states that the length of any one side must be smaller than the sum of the other two sides.

  • Applying the triangle inequality theorem to △BAC, we get BC<BA+ACBC<8cm+3cmBC<11cm.

  • Applying the triangle inequality theorem to △BDC, we get BC<BD+DCBC<4cm+9cmBC<13cm.

Since both BC<11cm and BC<13cm must be satisfied, we conclude that

BC<11cm.
