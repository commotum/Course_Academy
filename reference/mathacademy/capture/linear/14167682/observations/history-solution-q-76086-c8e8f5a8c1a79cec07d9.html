The triangle inequality theorem states that the length of any one side must be smaller than the sum of the other two sides.

In particular, for the numbers 8,6,14 to represent the sides of a triangle, the number 14 must be smaller than the sum of the numbers 8 and 6. But this is not the case: 8+6=14≯14 Therefore, the three numbers 8,6,14 cannot represent the sides of a triangle.
