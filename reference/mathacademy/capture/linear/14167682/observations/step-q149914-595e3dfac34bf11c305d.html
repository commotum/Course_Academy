In a gym, the tennis court is 15m away from the front desk, and the swimming pool is 22m away from the front desk. Assuming that the three locations form a triangle, what is the range of all possible values for the distance between the tennis court and the swimming pool?

a
between 5m and 32m
b
between 8m and 40m
c
between 7m and 37m
d
between 10m and 39m
e
between 9m and 41m
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we let:

  • F be the position of the front desk

  • T be the position of the tennis court

  • P be the position of the swimming pool

Let's draw △FTP.

We know that FT=15m,FP=22m.

According to the triangle inequality theorem,

FP−FT<PT<FP+FT22−15<PT<22+157<PT<37.

Therefore, the distance from the tennis court to the swimming pool must be between 7m and 37m.
