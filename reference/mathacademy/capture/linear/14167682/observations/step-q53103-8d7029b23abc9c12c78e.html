Abe knows that he lives 13km away from Becky's house and 7km away from Chris' house, but doesn't know the distance between their houses. Assuming that the three locations form a triangle, what is the range of all possible values for the distance between Becky's house and Chris's house?

a
between 6km and 18km
b
between 6km and 24km
c
between 8km and 20km
d
between 8km and 24km
e
between 6km and 20km
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we let:

  • A be the position of Abe's house

  • B be the position of Becky's house

  • C be the position of Chris's house

Let's draw △ABC.

We know that

AB=13km,AC=7km.

According to the triangle inequality theorem,

AB−AC<BC<AB+AC13−7<BC<13+76<BC<20.

Therefore, the distance between Becky's house and Chris's house, BC, must be between 6km and 20km.
