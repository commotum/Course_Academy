Sana and Talia are students. In the previous semester, the decimal proportion of Sana’s homework assignments that were turned in early was 0.4, while 45% of Talia’s homework assignments were turned in early.

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. Less than half of Sana's homework assignments were turned in early.
  2. More than half of Talia's homework assignments were turned in early.
  3. Sana had a greater percentage of homework assignments turned in early compared to Talia.

EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, since 0.4<0.5, less than half of Sana's homework assignments were turned in early.

  • Statement II is false. Notice that 45%<50%. So, less than half of Talia's homework assignments were turned in early.

  • Statement III is false. We can compare 0.4 and 45% by converting the decimal into a percent.

    To do this, we multiply it by 100: 0.4×100=40% Since 40%<45%, Sana did not have a greater percentage of homework assignments turned in early compared to Talia.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only".
