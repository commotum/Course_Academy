EXPLANATION

To convert the percent to a fraction, we first write 20% as a fraction with a denominator 100:

20%=20100

We can simplify the fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by 10:

20100=20÷10100÷10=210 Then, we divide the numerator and denominator by 2:

210=2÷210÷2=15

Now, notice that 15<25.

Therefore, 20% is equivalent to 15, which is smaller than 25.
