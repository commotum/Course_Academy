Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.
From the unit circle, we see that the angle π4 corresponds to the point (22,22). Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡(π4)=22. Therefore, using the fact that csc⁡θ=1sin⁡θ, we have csc⁡(π4)=1sin⁡(π4)=1(22)=2. Finally, evaluating v(t) at t=π4 we have 3csc2⁡(π4)=3(2)2=6.
