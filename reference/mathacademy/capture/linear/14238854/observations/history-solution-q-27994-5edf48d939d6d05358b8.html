The equation is already in factored form, so by the zero-product rule, we deduce that it has a single root at x=2 and a double root at x=1.

We also find the y-intercept of the curve by substituting the value x=0 into the equation: y=(2−0)(1−0)2=(2)(1)2=2.

So, the curve

  • intercepts the y-axis at the point (0,2),

  • touches the x-axis and turns around at the double root x=1, and

  • passes through the x-axis at the single root x=2.
