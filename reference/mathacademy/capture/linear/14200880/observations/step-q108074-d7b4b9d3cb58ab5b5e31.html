EXPLANATION

First, we rewrite 256 as a power of 2 (the base of the logarithm) as follows:

256=28

Therefore, we have:

log2⁡256=log2⁡(28)

The logarithm reverses the action of the exponent. The principle is:

logb⁡(bx)=x

In our case, we have b=2 and x=8. Therefore:

log2⁡(28)=8
