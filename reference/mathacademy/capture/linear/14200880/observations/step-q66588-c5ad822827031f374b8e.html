EXPLANATION

First, we rewrite 64 as a power of 4 (the base of the logarithm) as follows:

64=43

Therefore, we have:

log4⁡64=log4⁡(43)

The logarithm reverses the action of the exponent. The principle is:

logb⁡(bx)=x

In our case, we have b=4 and x=3. Therefore:

log4⁡(43)=3
