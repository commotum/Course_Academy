Given that the minor of m21 is 11, find all the possible values of y in the matrix M=[y32y254−y12yy2−6y].

a
y=75
b
y=0
c
y=−2
d
y=23
e
y=−1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we remove the second row and first column:

M=[∗32y25∗∗∗2−6y]

The minor of m21 is

det[32y2−6y]=|32y2−6y|=32⋅(−6y)−y⋅2=−11y.

We're told that the minor of m21 is 11, so we must have:

−11y=11y=−1

Therefore, the solution is y=−1.
