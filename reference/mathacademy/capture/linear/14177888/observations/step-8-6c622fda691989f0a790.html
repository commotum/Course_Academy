Explanation

We know that Susan has 179 of a pound of walnuts.

To calculate the amount that she ate, we need to multiply 13 by 179.

To multiply two fractions, we multiply the numerators, and we multiply the denominators: 13×179=1×173×9=1727.

Therefore, Susan ate 1727 of a pound of walnuts.
