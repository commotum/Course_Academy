We cannot compare the fractions directly as they don't have a common denominator.

We can make a common denominator of 5×9=45.

To put 225 over a denominator of 45, we first convert to an improper fraction and then multiply the numerator and denominator by 9: 225=125=12×95×9=10845

We can now compare the fractions 10845 and 8545 by looking at the numerators only.
