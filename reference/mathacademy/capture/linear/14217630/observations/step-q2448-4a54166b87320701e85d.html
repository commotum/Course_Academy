A curve is defined as y=3x−5. Find the average rate of change of y over the interval [3,7].

a
11
b
6
c
−6
d
14
e
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let f(x)=3x−5. Applying the formula for the average rate of change, we get ΔyΔx=f(b)−f(a)b−a=f(7)−f(3)7−3=[3(7)−5]−[3(3)−5]]7−3=16−47−3=124=3.
