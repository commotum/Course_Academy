If g(x) is given by the table shown below, what are its roots?

x 10 20 30 40 50 60 70 80
g(x) 5 15 25 0 −15 −25 0 5
a
30 and 60
b
40 and 70
c
10 and 80
d
20 and 50
e
50 and 60
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The roots of the function are the values of x such that g(x)=0. In the given table, we see that g(40)=0 and g(70)=0.

Therefore, the roots of the function are x=40 and x=70.
