Find the slope of y=f(x) at x=3 if f(x)=11cos⁡x−sec⁡x+1x.

a
11sin⁡(3)−sec⁡(3)tan⁡(3)−13
b
−sin⁡(3)−csc⁡(3)cot⁡(3)−13

−11sin⁡(3)+sec⁡(3)tan⁡(3)+13

−11sin⁡(3)−csc⁡(3)cot⁡(3)−1
e
−11sin⁡(3)−sec⁡(3)tan⁡(3)−13

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we calculate the derivative using the sum rule:

f′(x)=ddx(11cos⁡x−sec⁡x+1x)=ddx(11cos⁡x−sec⁡x+x−1)=11(−sin⁡x)−sec⁡xtan⁡x−1x2=−11sin⁡x−sec⁡xtan⁡x−1x2

Then, we substitute x=3 in the derivative and get f′(3)=−11sin⁡(3)−sec⁡(3)tan⁡(3)−1(3)2=−11sin⁡(3)−sec⁡(3)tan⁡(3)−13.
