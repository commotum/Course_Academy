Explanation

If r is the inverse of g, then

r(g(x))=x.

To find r(g(x)), we write down the function r and replace every occurrence of x with g(x):

r(x)=12x+kr(g(x))=12g(x)+k

Then, we substitute g(x)=2x−6 into the right-hand side of our expression and simplify, as follows:

r(g(x))=12g(x)+k=12(2x−6)+k=x−3+k

However, we know that r(g(x))=x. Equating the expression above with x and solving for k gives

r(g(x))=xx−3+k=xx−3+k=x−3+k=0k=3
