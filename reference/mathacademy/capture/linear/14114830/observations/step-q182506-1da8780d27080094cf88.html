EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of our statements:

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, A⇒¬B is logically equivalent to the disjunction ¬A∨¬B, which by De Morgan's law is equivalent to ¬(A∧B).

  • Statement II is true, while statement III is false. From part I, we have the following equivalence: A⇒¬B≡¬(A∧B) By negating this statement, we get ¬(A⇒¬B)≡¬(¬(A∧B)). Canceling out double negation, we reach A∧B. So, ¬(A⇒¬B) is equivalent to A∧B.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
