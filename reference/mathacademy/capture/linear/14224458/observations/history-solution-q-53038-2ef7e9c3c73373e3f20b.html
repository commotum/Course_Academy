First, recall the hierarchy diagram of quadrilaterals, where each shape below another is a specific type of the one above it.

Remember that a rectangle is a quadrilateral where all angles are right with two pairs of parallel sides.

With that in mind, let's look at each shape in turn.

  • The shape P is an isosceles trapezoid. It has no right angles, therefore, it is not a rectangle.

  • The shape Q is a square. Therefore, all its angles are right, so it is a rectangle.

  • The shape R is a rectangle since all its angles are right.

  • The shape S is a kite. Since there are no pairs of parallel sides, it is not a rectangle.

Therefore, shapes Q and R are rectangles. The shapes P and S are not rectangles.
