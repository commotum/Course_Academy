EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. A rectangle is a parallelogram where all angles are right. Therefore, ∠BCD is a right angle.

  • Statement II is true. The opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel, so AD― and BC― are parallel.

  • Statement III is false. Opposite sides of a rectangle are congruent, however, BC and CD are adjacent and we see that BC≠CD.

In conclusion, only statement II is true.
