The "square of a sum" formula states that

x2+2xy+y2=(x+y)2.

We will use this formula to factor the given trinomial. Our trinomial is

9b2+24b+16.

First, we write both the quadratic term and the constant term as perfect squares: (3b)2+24b+(4)2

Next, we factor a 2 out of the linear term: (3b)2+2(12b)+(4)2

Then, we write the middle parentheses (12b) as a product of 3b and 4: (3b)2+2(3b⋅4)+(4)2

Finally, we factor the trinomial using the square of a sum formula with x=3b and y=4. This gives (3b+4)2.
