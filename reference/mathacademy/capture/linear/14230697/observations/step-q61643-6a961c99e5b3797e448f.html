EXPLANATION

The polyhedron is a cube since all its faces are squares.

Also, notice that:

  • The solid is not a pyramid since a pyramid must have triangular faces (except, possibly, for the base).

  • The solid is not a triangular prism because it does not have two triangular bases connected by parallel lines.
