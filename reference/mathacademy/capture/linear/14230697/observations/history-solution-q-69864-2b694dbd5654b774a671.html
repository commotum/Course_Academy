The polyhedron is a hexagonal pyramid since it is a pyramid whose base is a hexagon.

Also, notice that:

  • The solid is not a rectangular pyramid because it does not have a rectangle as its base.

  • The solid is not a triangular pyramid because not all of its faces are triangles.

  • The solid is not a hexagonal prism since it has only one hexagonal face.

  • The solid is not a rectangular prism since not all of its faces are rectangles.
