Correct
EXPLANATION

The polyhedron is a pentagonal prism since it has two identical pentagonal faces connected by equal parallel line segments.

Also, notice that:

  • The solid is not a pyramid since a pyramid must have triangular faces (except, possibly, for the base) that meet at a single point.

  • The solid is not a rectangular prism since not all of its faces are rectangles.
