Pyramids
?

Pyramids are polyhedrons made of a polygonal base, with the other triangular faces meeting at a single point.

Let's take a look at some common pyramids.

  • A triangular pyramid has a triangular base with other triangular faces that meet at a single point.
  • A rectangular pyramid has a rectangular base with triangular faces that meet at a single point. If the base is a square, we call this a square pyramid.
  • A pentagonal pyramid has a pentagonal base with triangular faces that meet at a single point.

Similarly to prisms, we can add more sides to the polygonal base to make more types of pyramids.
