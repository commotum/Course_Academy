Recall that:

  • the product of two rational numbers is always rational: rational×rational=rational

  • the product of a nonzero rational number and an irrational number is always irrational: rational×irrational=irrational

  • the product of two irrational numbers is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

With that in mind, let's examine the statements.

  • Expression I is irrational. Since 1012 is rational and 5 is irrational, their product must be irrational.

  • Expression II is irrational. We have 6135=1135⋅6, and since 1135 is rational and 6 is irrational, their product must be irrational.

  • Expression III is irrational. Since 3.777 is rational and 31 is irrational, their product must be irrational.

Therefore, the correct answer is "None".
