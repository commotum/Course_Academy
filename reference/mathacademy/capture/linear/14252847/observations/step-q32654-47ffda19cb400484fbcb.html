A sphere has a diameter of 83mm. Find the surface area of the sphere.

a
192πmm2
b
921πmm2
c
2193πmm2
d
12932πmm2
e
912πmm2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We can calculate the surface area of a sphere using the formula

S=4πr2,

where r is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting r=832=43mm into the formula, we obtain

S=4π(43)2=192πmm2.
