A sphere has a surface area of 25πmm2. Calculate the diameter of the sphere.

a
52mm
b
53mm
c
10mm
d
103mm
e
5mm

Correct
EXPLANATION

We can calculate the surface area of a sphere using the formula

S=4πr2,

where r is the radius of the sphere.

Substituting S=25πmm2 into the formula, we can solve for r as follows:

S=4πr225π=4πr225π4π=r2r2=254r=254=52mm

Therefore, the diameter is

d=2r=2⋅52=5mm.
