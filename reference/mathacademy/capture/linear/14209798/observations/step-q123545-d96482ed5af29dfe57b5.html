  2. cos⁡32∘=cos⁡(8π35)

EXPLANATION

Let's examine the statements one by one.

  • Statement I is true. Converting 57∘ from degrees to radians, we get 57∘=57⋅π180=19π60radians.

  • Statement II is false. Converting 32∘ from degrees to radians, we get 32∘=32⋅π180=8π45radians. The statement is false because 8π45≠8π35.

  • Statement III is false. Converting 14∘ from degrees to radians, we get 14∘=14⋅π180=7π90radians. The statement is false because 7π90≠7π80.

So, the correct answer is 'I only'.
