A five gallons water bottle is leaking its content. The number of gallons of water left inside the bottle after t minutes is given by the formula W(t)=5⋅(15)t. How many gallons of water will be inside the bottle after 3 minutes?

a
0.02 gallons
b
0.2 gallons
c
0.04 gallons
d
1 gallon
e
0.4 gallons
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To get the number of gallons of water inside the bottle after 3 minutes, we evaluate the function at t=3. Substituting t=3 into the function W(t) gives

W(3)=5⋅(15)3=5⋅(1353)=5⋅1125=5125=0.04.

Therefore, after 3 minutes, there will be 0.04 gallons of water inside the bottle.
