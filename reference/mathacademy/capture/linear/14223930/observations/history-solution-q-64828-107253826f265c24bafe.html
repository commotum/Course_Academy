First, we find an angle that's coterminal with 19π4 by subtracting integer multiples of 2π until we get an angle that lies in the range [0,2π).

  • 19π4−2π=11π4

So the angle 19π4 is coterminal with 3π4. Let's draw this coterminal angle in the coordinate plane.
