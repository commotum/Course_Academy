The transformation y=−f(x) reflects the curve y=f(x) in the x-axis.

First, notice that the point P(−4,6) lies 6 units above the x-axis. So, under the action of the transformation, this point will be reflected 6 units below the x-axis.

We conclude that the point (−4,−6) must lie on the graph of y=−f(x).
