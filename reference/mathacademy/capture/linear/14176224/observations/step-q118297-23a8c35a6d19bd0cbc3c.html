Let ∠N and ∠S be two adjacent angles. Given that ∠N is a null angle and ∠S is straight, what type of angle is their sum?

a
Full
b
Obtuse
c
Right
d
Straight
e
Reflex
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since ∠N is a null and a right angle, we have m∠N=0∘ and since ∠S is a straight angle, we have m∠S=180∘.

So, their sum is

m∠N+m∠S=0∘+180∘=180∘.

Consequently, the sum of ∠N and ∠S is a straight angle.
