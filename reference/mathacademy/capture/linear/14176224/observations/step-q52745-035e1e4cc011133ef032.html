Let ∠S and ∠T be two adjacent angles. Given that ∠S is a straight angle and ∠T is obtuse, what type of angle is their sum?

a
Obtuse
b
Acute
c
Full
d
Reflex
e
Straight
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since ∠S is a straight angle, we have m∠S=180∘.

Since ∠T is obtuse, we have

90∘<m∠T<180∘.

So, the sum of these two angles satisfies by the following inequalies:

180∘+90∘<m∠S+m∠T<180∘+180∘270∘<m∠S+m∠T<360∘.

Consequently, the sum of ∠S and ∠T is a reflex angle.
