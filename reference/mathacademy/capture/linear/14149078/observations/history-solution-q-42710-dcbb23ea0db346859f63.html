To subtract 5.5 from 3.5, we break up 5.5 into its whole number part (5) and its decimal part (0.5) and proceed in two steps.

  • We start at 3.5 on the number line and move 5 places to the left, which brings us to −1.5.

  • Then, we move 0.5 places to the left, leaving us at −2.

This gives 3.5−5.5=−2.
