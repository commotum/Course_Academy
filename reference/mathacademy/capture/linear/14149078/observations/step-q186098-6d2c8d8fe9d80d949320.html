EXPLANATION

To subtract 2.5 from 4, we break up 2.5 into its whole number part (2) and its decimal part (0.5) and proceed in two steps.

  • We start at 4 on the number line and move 2 places to the left, which brings us to 2.

  • Then, we move the remaining 0.5 places to the left, leaving us at 1.5.

This gives 4−2.5=1.5.
