EXPLANATION

The given equation is equivalent to tan⁡(x2−π3)=−1.

So, the general solution to our equation is x2−π3=−π4+nπ.

Solving the above equation for x, we get x2=π3−π4+nπx2=π12+nπx=π6+2nπ.

Therefore, we have that a=16.
