EXPLANATION
  • Q,S,T are collinear since there is a straight line that passes through all of them.
  • M,O,Q are not collinear since we can't draw a straight line that passes through all of them.
  • N,P,T are not collinear since we can't draw a straight line that passes through all of them.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
