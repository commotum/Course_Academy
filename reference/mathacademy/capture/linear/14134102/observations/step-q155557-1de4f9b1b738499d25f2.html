Given the picture shown above, which of the following statements are true?

  1. M, R, and N are collinear
  2. T, S, and P are collinear
  3. M, R, and S are not collinear
a
I only
b
III only
c
II only
d
I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Three (or more) points are collinear if a straight line passes through all points.

With that in mind, let's examine our diagrams in turn.

  • Statement I is true. There is a straight line that passes through these three points.
  • Statement II is false. We can't draw a straight line that passes through these three points.
  • Statement III is true. We can't draw a straight line that passes through these three points.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
