  1. M,N,R
  2. P,S,T

II and III only
b
I and III only

Incorrect
EXPLANATION
  • M,N,R are collinear since there is a straight line that passes through all of them.
  • P,S,T are not collinear since we can't draw a straight line that passes through all of them.
  • O,P,S are collinear since there is a straight line that passes through all of them.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
