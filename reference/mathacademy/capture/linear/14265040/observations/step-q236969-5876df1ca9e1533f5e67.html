So, we need to calculate X-values at the maxima of

y=12cos⁡X.

The X-values at the maxima are given by the following expression:

X=360∘⋅n where n is an integer.

To find the x-values at the maxima of our original function, we rewrite X in terms of the original variable x and solve the resulting equation for x: X=360∘⋅n4x=360∘⋅nx=90∘⋅n
