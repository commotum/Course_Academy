First, recall the hierarchy diagram of quadrilaterals, where each shape below another is a specific type of the one above it.

Remember that a parallelogram is a quadrilateral with 2 pairs of parallel sides.

With that in mind, let's look at each shape in turn.

  • The shape P is a parallelogram since it has 2 pairs of parallel sides.

  • The shape Q is a square. Therefore, its opposite sides are parallel, so it is a parallelogram.

  • The shape R is a rhombus. Since the opposite sides of a rhombus are parallel, it is a parallelogram.

  • The shape S is a kite. It has no pairs of opposite sides that are parallel, therefore, it is not a parallelogram.

Therefore, shapes P, Q, and R are parallelograms. The shape S is not parallelogram.
