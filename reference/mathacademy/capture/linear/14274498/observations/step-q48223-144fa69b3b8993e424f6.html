EXPLANATION

Both the numerator and denominator approach ∞ as x→∞. However, since ex≫6x for large values of x, the denominator is growing much faster than the numerator. Consequently, we conclude that limx→∞6xex=0.

Therefore

limx→∞(6xex+3)=0+3=3.
