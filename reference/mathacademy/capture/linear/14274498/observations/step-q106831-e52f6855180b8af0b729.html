EXPLANATION

First, we rewrite the limit as

limx→∞−9cos⁡x1−4x=limx→∞9cos⁡x4x−1.

The numerator here is a bounded, oscillating function with

|9cos⁡x|≤9.

However, the denominator 4x−1 grows without bound as x→∞. So the denominator is growing much faster than the numerator, and we conclude that

limx→∞−9cos⁡x1−4x=limx→∞9cos⁡x4x−1=0.
