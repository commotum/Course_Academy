Notice that we can rewrite the first term as

limx→∞ln⁡(2x)e−3x=limx→∞ln⁡(2x)e3x.

Both the numerator and denominator approach ∞ as x→∞. However, since e3x≫ln⁡(2x) for large values of x, the denominator is growing much faster than the numerator. Consequently, we conclude that

limx→∞ln⁡(2x)e3x=0.

Therefore,

limx→∞ln⁡(2x)e−3x+2=limx→∞(ln⁡(2x)e3x+2)=0+2=2.
