Which value of k gives precisely one solution to the equation t2+k−8=−2?

a
−8
b
−6
c
6
d
6
e
8
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, we solve for t as follows:

t2+k−8=−2t2=−2−k+8t2=6−k|t|=6−k

The equation has only one solution if the right-hand side is equal to zero, and the right-hand side is zero only if the expression underneath the radical is zero. So, we solve for the value of k that makes the expression under the radical equal to zero:

6−k=0k=6

Therefore, k=6 will give only one solution to the equation.
