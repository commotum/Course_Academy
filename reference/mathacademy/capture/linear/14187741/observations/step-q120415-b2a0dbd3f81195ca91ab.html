Mark's expenditure of money E, in dollars, is inversely proportional to his savings S, also in dollars. If Mark spends $500 when he saves $200, what will be Mark's expenditure (to the nearest dollar) when he saves $300?

a
$250
b
$400
c
$100
d
$333
e
$33
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Mark's expenditure of money E is inversely proportional to his savings S, so we have

E=kS,

where k is the constant of variation.

We are told that Mark spends $500 when he saves $200. We can use this information to solve for k as follows:

500=k200500⋅200=k100000=k

We now have the equation

E=100000S.

We can use this equation to find Mark's expenditure when he saves $300, as follows:

E=100000300≈333

Therefore, Mark's expenditure when S=$300 is E=$333.
