Using the quotient rule, we have limx→2f(x)+2g(x)1+xg(x)=limx→2(f(x)+2g(x))limx→2(1+xg(x)).

We find from the graph that limx→2f(x)=6 and limx→2g(x)=2. Calculating the limits of the numerator and denominator separately, we have limx→2(f(x)+2g(x))=6+2(2)=10 and limx→2(1+xg(x))=1+2(2)=5. Therefore,

limx→2f(x)+2g(x)1+xg(x)=105=2.
