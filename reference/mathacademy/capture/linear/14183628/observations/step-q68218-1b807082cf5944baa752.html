The relationship between the variables h and t is shown in the following table:

t h
4 8
5 15
6 24

Which of the following statements are true?

  1. There is not a proportional relationship between h and t.
  2. The constant of proportionality is 6.
  3. There is a proportional relationship between h and t.
a
I only
b
II and III only
c
I and II only
d
III only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's add a new column to our table and compute the ratio ht:

t h ht
4 8 2
5 15 3
6 24 4

The ratio ht is not the same for each row. Therefore, there is not a proportional relationship between h and t. Consequently, there is no constant of proportionality.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
