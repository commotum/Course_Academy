We're told that w and c have a proportional relationship.

To find the constant of proportionality, we first need to choose a point (w,c) from the table. Let's choose the point (2,3), which corresponds to the first row of the table.

We can find the constant of proportionality as follows:

k=cw=32.

The units of k are "dollars per pound" since we divided the cost c by the weight w. Therefore, the relationship between c and w is

c=32w.

Now, with that in mind, let's look at each statement.

  • Statement I is false. We know that k=32, which means that the unit rate is 32 dollars per pound.

  • Statement II is false. Since k=32=1.5, one pound of apples costs $1.50.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, if we substitute w=5 into the relationship c=32w, we get c=32(5)=7.5 which means that the cost of 5 pounds of apples is $7.50.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only".
