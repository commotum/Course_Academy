EXPLANATION

We're told that x and y have a proportional relationship.

Every proportional relationship can be written in the form y=kx, where k is the constant of proportionality.

To compute the constant of proportionality, we first need to choose a point (x,y) from the table. Let's choose the point (6,50), which corresponds to the second row of the table.

Now, we can find the constant of proportionality as follows:

k=yx=506=253

Therefore, the equation is

y=253x.
