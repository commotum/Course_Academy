The function N(t) gives the number of people inside the park t minutes after opening. Then, N′(t)=dN(t)dt=Change in NChange in t. Thus, N′(t) gives the rate at which the number of people inside the park changes t minutes after opening.

In addition, we can compute the rate of change of the number of people inside the park at time t by finding the difference between the entry and exit rates at time t. That is, N′(t)=E(t)−L(t).
