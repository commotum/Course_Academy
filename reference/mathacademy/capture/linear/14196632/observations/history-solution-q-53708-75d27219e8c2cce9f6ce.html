A similarity transformation is any transformation made up of rigid motions (rotations, reflections, and translations) and dilations.

A similarity transformation will map any polygon to a similar polygon.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the transformations in turn.

  • Transformation I includes stretching with different factors in the x- and y-direction. Thus, it is not a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation II is a combination of two translations, which are rigid motions. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation III is a combination of dilation of scale factor 2 and a reflection. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
