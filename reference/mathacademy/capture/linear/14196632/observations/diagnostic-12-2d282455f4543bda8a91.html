  3. 2⋅2−15

EXPLANATION

Recall that:

  • the product of two rational numbers is always rational: rational×rational=rational

  • the product of a nonzero rational number and an irrational number is always irrational: rational×irrational=irrational

  • the product of two irrational numbers is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

With that in mind, let's examine the statements.

  • Expression I is rational. Notice that 36=6 is a rational number. The number 85 is rational too. So, their product must be rational.

  • Expression II is rational. Since both 320 and 7.0523 are rational, their product must be rational.

  • Expression III is irrational. Since 2 is irrational and 2−15 is rational, their product must be irrational.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
