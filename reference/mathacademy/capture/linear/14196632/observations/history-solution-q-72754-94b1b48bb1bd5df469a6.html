The area of the quadrilateral ABCD is the sum of the areas of the triangles △ABD and △BDC (see the illustration below).

First, we find the area of △ABD:

AABD=12⋅6⋅8⋅sin⁡85∘=24⋅sin⁡85∘≈23.9087,

rounded to four decimal places.

Now we find the area of △BCD:

ABCD=12⋅14⋅16⋅sin⁡36.4∘=112⋅sin⁡36.4∘≈66.4629,

rounded to four decimal places.

Finally, we add together the areas of our two triangles to determine the area of ABCD:

AABCD=AABD+ABCD≈23.9087+66.4629≈90.3716.

Consequently, to the nearest integer, the area of the quadrilateral ABCD is 90.
