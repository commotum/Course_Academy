f′(x)=ddx(6x+5x−13)=ddx(6x1/2+5x)=3x−1/2+5

So, the slope of the tangent at our point is m=f′(1)=3(1)−1/2+5=8.

Therefore, the equation of the tangent is

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−(−2)=8(x−1)y=8x−8−2y=8x−10.

To find where the tangent line intercepts the x-axis, we set y=0 and solve:

0=8x−10x=54

Therefore, the point of intersection is (54,0).
