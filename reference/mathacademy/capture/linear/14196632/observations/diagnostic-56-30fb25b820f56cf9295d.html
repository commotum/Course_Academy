EXPLANATION

First, we rewrite e4 as a power of e (the base of the natural logarithm) as follows: e4=e1/4

Therefore, we have:

12ln⁡(e4)=12ln⁡(e1/4)

The natural logarithm reverses the action of the exponent. The principle is: ln⁡(ex)=x

In our case, we have x=14. Therefore: 12ln⁡(e1/4)=12⋅14=18
