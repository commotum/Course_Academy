The total amount of fuel consumed consists of two parts.

  • For the first 2 hours, the generator consumes

3×2=6liters.

  • For each additional hour beyond the first 2, the generator consumes

5(t−2)liters.

Therefore, the total amount of fuel F consumed after t hours, where t≥2, is

F(t)=6+5(t−2)=6+5t−10=5t−4.
