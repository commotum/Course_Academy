Caden sold half of his comic books and then bought sixteen more. If he now has 36, how many comic books did he have at the start?

a
52
b
20
c
26
d
40

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x= the number of comic books Caden had at the start.

We note that:

  • Half the number of comic books corresponds to 12x.
  • The number of comic books remaining after selling half of them corresponds to x−12x.
  • The number of comic books remaining after selling half and buying sixteen more corresponds to x−12x+16.

The final number of comic books is 36, so we have the equation x−12x+16=36.

We can solve this equation using the addition and multiplication principles: x−12x+16=36(1−12)x+16=3612x+16=3612x+16−16=36−1612x=202⋅12x=2⋅20x=40

Thus, Caden had 40 comic books at the start.
