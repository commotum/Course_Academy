A hot air balloon descends 73m each second. What is the total change in altitude after 97 seconds?

a
3m
b
−3m
c
−7621m
d
−207m
e
7621m
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The hot air balloon descends 73m each second.

Since the altitude decreases, the change each second is −73.

The descent lasts 97 seconds, so the total change in altitude is

97×(−73).

Evaluating the product, we get

97×(−73)=−9×77×3=−93=−3.

Therefore, the total change in altitude is −3m.
