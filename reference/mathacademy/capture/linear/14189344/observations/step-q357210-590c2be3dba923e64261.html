A diver descends a total of 32.5m over 5 minutes at a constant rate. What is the change in elevation each minute?

a
6.5m
b
5.5m
c
−5.5m
d
−6.5m
e
−27.5m
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the diver's elevation decreases by 32.5m over 5 minutes, the total change is −32.5m.

This change occurs evenly over 5 minutes, so we divide the total change by 5:

−32.5÷5

Evaluating the quotient, we get

−32.5÷5=−6.5.

Therefore, the change in elevation each minute is −6.5m.
