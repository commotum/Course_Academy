EXPLANATION

To raise the fraction to the power of 3, we must raise both the numerator and the denominator to the power of 3. So we have

(25)3=2353=8125.

Therefore,

10(25)3=10⋅8125=80125.

Finally, we simplify:

80125=16⋅525⋅5=1625
