EXPLANATION

For any integers a, b, and c, we have transitivity of divisibility:

a∣b and b∣c ⟹ a∣c

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements.

  • Statement I is false. As a counterexample, let k=11. Then, 11∣11 but 33∤11.

  • Statement II is true. First, note that 11∣33. Therefore, we have 11∣33 and 33∣k, which implies that 11∣k.

  • Statement III is true. First, note that 3∣33. Therefore, we have 3∣33 and 33∣k, which implies that 3∣k.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
