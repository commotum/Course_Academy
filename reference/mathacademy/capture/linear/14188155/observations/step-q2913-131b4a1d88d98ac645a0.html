EXPLANATION

The square root of a squared quantity can be simplified using absolute value.

x2=|x|

In our case, we can simplify the given expression as follows:

t8y6=t4y3⋅t4y3=(t4y3)2=|t4y3|=|t4|⋅|y3|=|t4|⋅|y|3

An even power of a number is always positive or zero, and the absolute value of any positive (or zero) number is itself, so we have |t4|=t4.

On the other hand, we are told that y>0, and we know that the absolute value of any positive number is just itself, so we have |y|=y. Therefore, our final answer is |t4|⋅|y|3=t4⋅y3=t4y3.
