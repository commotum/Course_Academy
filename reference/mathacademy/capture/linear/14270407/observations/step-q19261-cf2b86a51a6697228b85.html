The point A with coordinates (7,ln⁡7) lies on the curve y=ln⁡x, and the point A′ with coordinates (3.5,ln⁡7) lies on the curve y=ln⁡(ax), shown above. What is the value of a?

EXPLANATION

The graph of y=ln⁡(ax) can be obtained from y=ln⁡x by a horizontal stretch of scale factor 1a.

From the diagram, we deduce that under such a transformation, the point (7,ln⁡7) that lies on y=ln⁡x becomes

(1a⋅7,ln⁡7)=(3.5,ln⁡7)

which lies on y=ln⁡ax.

Therefore, a=2.
