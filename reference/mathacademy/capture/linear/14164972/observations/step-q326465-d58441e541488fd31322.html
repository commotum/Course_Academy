According to a recent report, 60% of tablet owners said they used their tablets regularly. Of those, 50% said they used them primarily for drawing. What percentage of all tablet owners used tablets primarily for drawing?

a
60%
b
30%
c
50%
d
40%
e
35%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x be the total number of tablet owners.

We're told that 60% of tablet owners used their tablets regularly. Thus, the number of tablet owners who use them regularly is 0.6x.

We are also told that of those owners, 50% used them for drawing. So, the number of tablet owners who used tablets primarily for drawing is 0.50(0.6x)=0.30x.

The expression 0.30x represents 30% of x. Therefore, 30% of all tablet owners used tablets primarily for drawing.
