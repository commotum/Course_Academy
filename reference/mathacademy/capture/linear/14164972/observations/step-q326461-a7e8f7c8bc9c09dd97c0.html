In an online study, 60% of participants reported enjoying painting. Of those, 25% said they preferred painting landscapes. What percentage of all participants preferred landscape painting?

a
25%
b
30%
c
15%
d
20%
e
45%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let x be the total number of participants.

We're told that 60% of participants enjoy painting. Thus, the number of participants who enjoy painting is 0.6x.

We are also told that of those participants, 25% preferred landscapes. So, the number of participants who preferred landscape painting is 0.25(0.6x)=0.15x.

The expression 0.15x represents 15% of x. Therefore, 15% of all participants preferred landscape painting.
