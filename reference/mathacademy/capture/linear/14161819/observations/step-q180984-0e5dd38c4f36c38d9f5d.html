EXPLANATION

First, let's swap the left and right-hand sides so that the variable is on the left-hand side: 24+x=9

Now, let's rewrite the left-hand side so that it's similar to what we've seen before. x+24=9

To isolate x, we need to perform the opposite of adding 24 to x, which is subtracting 24 from x. Remember to perform the same operation on the right-hand side.

x+24=9x+24−24=9−24x+0=−15x=−15
