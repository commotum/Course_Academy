If each cube represents 1 cubic centimeter, what is the volume of the figure?

a
20 cubic centimeters
b
21 cubic centimeters
c
30 cubic centimeters
d
10 cubic centimeters
e
24 cubic centimeters
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of 4 rows, and each row has 6 cubes.

So, there are 6×4=24 cubes in total.

Therefore, the volume of the figure is 24 cubic centimeters.
