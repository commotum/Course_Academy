An object is assembled as shown below. Given that each small cube represents one cubic centimeter, what is the volume of the object?

a
10 cubic centimeters
b
6 cubic centimeters
c
7 cubic centimeters
d
8 cubic centimeters
e
5 cubic centimeters
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of three layers:

  • The bottom layer consists of 5 cubes.

  • The middle layer consists of 2 cubes.

  • The top layer consists of 1 cube.

Therefore, the volume is 5+2+1=8 cubic centimeters.
