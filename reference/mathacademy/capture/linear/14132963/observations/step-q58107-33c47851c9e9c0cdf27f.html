If each cube represents 1 cubic unit, what is the volume of the figure?

a
18 cubic units
b
21 cubic units
c
20 cubic units
d
22 cubic units
e
19 cubic units
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of 3 rows, and each row has 7 cubes.

So, there are 7×3=21 cubes in total.

Therefore, the volume of the figure is 21 cubic units.
