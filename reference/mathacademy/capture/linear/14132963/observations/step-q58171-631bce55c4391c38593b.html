If each cube represents 1 cubic foot, what is the volume of the figure?

a
28 cubic feet
b
18 cubic feet
c
25 cubic feet
d
21 cubic feet
e
20 cubic feet
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of 4 rows, and each row has 5 cubes.

So, there are 5×4=20 cubes in total.

Therefore, the volume of the figure is 20 cubic feet.
