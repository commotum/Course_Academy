If each cube represents 1 cubic unit, what is the volume of the figure?

a
20 cubic units
b
18 cubic units
c
16 cubic units
d
14 cubic units
e
24 cubic units
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of 3 rows, and each row has 6 cubes.

So, there are 6×3=18 cubes in total.

Therefore, the volume of the figure is 18 cubic units.
