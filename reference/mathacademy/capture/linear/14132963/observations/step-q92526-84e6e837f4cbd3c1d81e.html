An object is assembled as shown below. Given that each small cube represents one cubic inch, what is the volume of the object?

a
11 cubic inches
b
10 cubic inches
c
6 cubic inches
d
7 cubic inches
e
8 cubic inches
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of three layers:

  • The bottom layer consists of 6 cubes.

  • The middle layer consists of 3 cubes.

  • The top layer consists of 1 cube.

Therefore, the volume is 6+3+1=10 cubic inches.
