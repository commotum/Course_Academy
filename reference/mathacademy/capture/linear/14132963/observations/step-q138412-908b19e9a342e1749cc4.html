If each cube represents 1 cubic centimeter, what is the volume of the figure?

a
4 cubic centimeters
b
12 cubic centimeters
c
6 cubic centimeters
d
10 cubic centimeters
e
8 cubic centimeters
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The figure consists of 4 rows, and each row has 2 cubes.

So, there are 2×4=8 cubes in total.

Therefore, the volume of the figure is 8 cubic centimeters.
