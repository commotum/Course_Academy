To express tan⁡(−225∘) in terms of tan⁡θR, we proceed as follows:

  • Step 1: Find the corresponding coterminal angle that lies in the range [0∘,360∘). We do this by adding integer multiples of 360∘ until we get our desired angle. −225∘+360∘=135∘✓ Therefore, tan⁡(−225∘)=tan⁡135∘.

  • Step 2: Compute the reference angle. Since θ=135∘ is in the 2nd quadrant, the reference angle is θR=180∘−135∘=45∘.

  • Step 3: Determine the correct sign. The tangent function is negative (−) in the second quadrant. Therefore, tan⁡(−225∘)=−tan⁡θR=−tan⁡45∘.

Therefore, we conclude that
