Speed is a special unit rate applied to moving objects. It is defined as the distance traveled per unit of time:

speed=distancetime

To find the speed in meters per minute, we divide the number of meters, 190, by the number of minutes, 2. This gives,

1902=190÷2=95.

So, the speed of the person is 95 meters per minute.

To find the distance covered in 5 minutes, we multiply the speed by 5. This gives

distance=speed×time=95×5=475.

Therefore, the person can cover 475 meters in 5 minutes.
