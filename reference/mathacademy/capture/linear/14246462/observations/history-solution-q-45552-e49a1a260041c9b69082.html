We know that the number of rabbits increases sixfold every decade. So the number of rabbits, which we'll call y(t), grows exponentially with a growth factor of 6.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth factor b) is

y(t)=abt.

From the given information, we have the following:

  • a=100 is the initial number of rabbits.

  • b=6 is the growth factor.

  • t is the time, in decades, since the opening of the nature reserve.

Therefore, the expression for y(t) is

y(t)=100⋅6t.

So, to find the time it takes for the population to increase to 129600, we would need to solve the equation

y(t)=129600100⋅6t=129600.
