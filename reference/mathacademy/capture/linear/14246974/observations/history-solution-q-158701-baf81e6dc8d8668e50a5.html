Notice that ∠BAF and ∠AFC are alternate interior angles formed when the transversal AF↔ cuts the parallel lines AB↔ and CF↔. Hence, m∠AFC=m∠BAF=71∘.

Also, notice that ∠FBE and ∠BFC are alternate interior angles formed when the transversal BF↔ cuts the parallel lines AB↔ and CF↔. Hence, m∠BFC=m∠FBE=139∘.

Now, using the angle addition postulate, we can find ∠AFB: m∠AFB=m∠BFC−m∠AFC=139∘−71∘=68∘
