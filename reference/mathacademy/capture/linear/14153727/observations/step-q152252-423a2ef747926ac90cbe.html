Which of the following statements are true?

  1. 5x2−7x+1 is cubic trinomial.
  2. x3+x2+x is cubic trinomial.
  3. Some trinomials are cubic.
a
I only
b
I and II only
c
II and III only
d
II only
e
I, II, and III
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. Since the degree of the trinomial 5x2−7x+1 is 2, it is a quadratic trinomial.

  • Statement II is true. Since the degree of the trinomial x3+x2+x is 3, it is a cubic trinomial.

  • Statement III is true. A trinomial could be cubic (see, for example, statement II).

    However, a trinomial is not always cubic (see, for example, statement I).

    So, some trinomials are cubic.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
