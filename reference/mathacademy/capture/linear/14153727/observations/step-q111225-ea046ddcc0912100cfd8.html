a
quartic trinomial
b
quartic monomial

cubic binomial
d
cubic monomial
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

The polynomial 3h4 has only one term, so it is a monomial.

Also, its degree is 4, so it is a quartic.

Therefore, the polynomial is a quartic monomial.
