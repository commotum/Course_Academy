Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The product of 7x and 3x2 is a binomial.
  2. The product of 4x and 5 is a monomial.
  3. The product of two monomials is always a binomial.
a
II only
b
I only
c
III only
d
II and III only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. We have (7x)×(3x2)=21x3, which is not a binomial (it's a monomial).

  • Statement II is true. We have (4x)×(5)=20x, which is a monomial.

  • Statement III is false. There are products of two monomials that are not binomials (see, for example, statement I and II).

    In fact, the product of two monomials is never a binomial.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
