For two lines to be parallel, they must have the same slope.

  • To find the slope of the line 3x+4y=8, we convert to slope-intercept form, as follows: 3x+4y=84y=−3x+8y=−34x+2 In slope-intercept form, the slope is the x-coefficient. So, the slope is −34.

  • To find the slope of the line kx+4y=−20, we convert to slope-intercept form, as follows: kx+4y=−204y=−kx−20y=−k4x−5 In slope-intercept form, the slope is the x-coefficient. So, the slope is −k4.

Since the slopes of the two lines must be equal, we must have −k4=−34. We solve for k:

−k4=−34k4=34k=3
