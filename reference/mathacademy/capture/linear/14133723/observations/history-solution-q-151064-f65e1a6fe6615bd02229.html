A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1.

The only number that satisfies this definition is 3. It has only two factors: 1 and 3.

3=1×3=3×1

All the other given numbers have at least one factor distinct from itself, and 1:

  • 9 has 3 as a factor: 9=3×3

  • 27 has 3 and 9 as factors: 27=3×9

  • 8 has 2 and 4 as factors: 8=2×4

  • 32 has 2 and 16 as factors: 32=2×16

Therefore, the correct answer is 3.
