Which of the following numbers are not prime? 12,13,19,23,29

a
19 and 29 only
b
12 only
c
13, 19, and 23 only
d
13 and 23 only
e
12 and 23 only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1. A composite number is a number that has more than two distinct factors.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the numbers in turn.

  • 12 is not prime as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 2 is a factor of 12: 12=2×6

  • 13 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 13.

  • 19 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 19.

  • 23 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 23.

  • 29 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 29.

Therefore, the correct answer is "12 only."
