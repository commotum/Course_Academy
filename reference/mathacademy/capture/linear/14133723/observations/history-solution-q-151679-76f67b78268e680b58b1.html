A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1. A composite number is a number that has more than two distinct factors.

The number 1 is neither prime nor composite.

With that in mind, let's examine the numbers in the list.

  • 40 is not prime as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 10 is a factor of 40: 40=10×4

  • 1 is not prime since it has only one factor. Moreover, 1 is not composite either.

  • 23 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 23.

  • 36 is not prime as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 6 is a factor of 36: 36=6×6

  • 19 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 19.

Therefore, the correct answer is "19 and 23 only."
