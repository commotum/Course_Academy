Which of the following numbers are composite? 14,39,1,19,2

a
2 and 14 only
b
14 and 39 only
c
19 only
d
None of the numbers are composite
e
1, 14, and 39 only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1. A composite number is a number that has more than two distinct factors.

The number 1 is neither prime nor composite.

With that in mind, let's examine the numbers in the list.

  • 14 is composite as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 2 is a factor of 14: 14=2×7

  • 39 is composite as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 3 is a factor of 39: 39=3×13

  • 1 is not composite since it has only one factor. Moreover, 1 is not prime either.

  • 19 is not composite since it has only two factors: 1 and 19.

  • 2 is not composite since it has only two factors: 1 and 2.

Therefore, the correct answer is "14 and 39 only."
