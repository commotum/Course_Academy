Which of the following numbers are composite? 4,15,5,17,21

a
4, 15, and 21 only
b
5, and 17 only
c
4 only
d
17 and 21 only
e
4 and 15 only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1. A composite number is a number that has more than two distinct factors.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the numbers in turn.

  • 4 is composite as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 2 is a factor of 4: 4=2×2

  • 15 is composite as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 3 is a factor of 15: 15=3×5

  • 5 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 5.

  • 17 is prime since it has only two factors: 1 and 17.

  • 21 is composite as it has factors distinct from itself and 1. For example, 3 is a factor of 21: 21=3×7

Therefore, the correct answer is "4, 15, and 21 only."
