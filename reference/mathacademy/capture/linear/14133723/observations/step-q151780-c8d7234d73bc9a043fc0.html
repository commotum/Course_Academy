Which of the following numbers is prime?

a
9
b
15
c
7
d
24
e
21
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A prime number is a positive whole number with exactly two distinct whole number factors: itself and 1.

The only number that satisfies this definition is 7. It has only two factors: 1 and 7.

7=1×7=7×1

All the other given numbers have at least one factor distinct from itself, and 1:

  • 21 has 3 and 7 as factors: 21=3×7

  • 9 has 3 as a factor: 9=3×3

  • 24 has 3 and 8 as factors: 24=3×8

  • 15 has 3 and 5 as factors: 15=3×5

Therefore, the correct answer is 7.
