Method 1

The graph of y=f(x) is obtained by translating the graph of y=f(x)+1 by 1 unit down.

Hence, if the point (−3,1) lies on y=f(x)+1, then the point (−3,1−1)=(−3,0) lies on y=f(x).

Method 2

As (−3,1) lies on y=f(x)+1, we have:

f(−3)+1=1f(−3)=1−1f(−3)=0

Now, if x=−3 then

So, the point (−3,0) must lie on y=f(x).
