Method 1

The graph of y=f(x) is obtained by translating the graph of y=f(x)+4 by 4 units down.

Hence, if the point (−2,3) lies on y=f(x)+4, then the point (−2,3−4)=(−2,−1) lies on y=f(x).

As (−2,3) lies on y=f(x)+4, we have:
