First, we need to factor the polynomial. For this polynomial, we can factor by grouping:

p(x)=x3+x2−x−1=x2(x+1)−(x+1)=(x2−1)(x+1)=(x−1)(x+1)(x+1)=(x−1)(x+1)2

To find the roots and their multiplicities, we look at the factors:

  • The factor (x+1) has an exponent of 2 in the factored form of p(x). So the root x=−1 has multiplicity 2.

  • The factor (x−1) has an exponent of 1 in the factored form of p(x). So the root x=1 has multiplicity 1.

In conclusion, only the root x=−1 has multiplicity 2.
