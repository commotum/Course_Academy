−30

EXPLANATION

Since p varies directly with q, we use the equation

p=kq.

We are told that p=−35 when q=−7. We substitute this data into the equation to find k: −35=k⋅(−7)(−35)(−7)=k357=k5=k

So, the direct variation equation is given by p=5q.

Finally, substituting the value of q=−6 into this equation gives p=5⋅(−6)=−30.
