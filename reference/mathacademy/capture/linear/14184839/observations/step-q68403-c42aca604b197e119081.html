Caroline bought a new car. The company claims she can drive the vehicle for 120 miles with 4 gallons of gasoline. If there is a proportional relationship between the volume of gas x (in gallons) and the distance traveled y (in miles), what is the constant of proportionality for the relationship between x and y?

a
0.3 gallons per mile
b
3 miles per gallon
c
40 miles per gallon
d
30 miles per gallon
e
48 gallons per mile
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are told that Caroline can drive the car for y=120 miles with x=4 gallons of gasoline.

We can find the constant of proportionality by computing the ratio of the y-value to the x-value, as follows:

k=yx=1204=120÷44÷4=301=30

Therefore, the constant of proportionality is 30, and its units are "miles per gallon."
