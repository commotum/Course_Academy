Andry is planning a party. He made 30 sandwiches for 12 people at the last party. If there is a proportional relationship between the number of people at the party x and the number of sandwiches y, what is the constant of proportionality for the relationship between x and y?

a
52 sandwiches per person
b
112 sandwiches per person
c
25 sandwiches per person
d
12 people per sandwich
e
30 people per sandwich
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are told that Andry made y=30 sandwiches for x=12 people.

We can find the constant of proportionality by computing the ratio of the y-value to the x-value, as follows:

k=yx=3012=30÷612÷6=52

Therefore, the constant of proportionality is 52, and its units are "sandwiches per person."
