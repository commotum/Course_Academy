Paul plants a sunflower, which grows at a rate of 1t+1 feet per month. If the sunflower is 12 feet tall when Paul planted it at time t=0, write an expression for A(t), the height of sunflower at time t, where t is the time measured in months.

a
A(t)=65+6t−ln⁡(5t+1)
b
A(t)=12+ln⁡t
c
A(t)=65+5t−ln⁡(t+1)
d
A(t)=12+5t−ln⁡(5t)
e
A(t)=12+ln⁡(t+1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let f(t)=1t+1 be the rate at which the sunflower grows, and let A(T) be the height of the sunflower after T months.

Using our formula for the accumulation of change, we have A(T)=A(0)+∫0Tf(t)dt.

Substituting the known information and carrying out the necessary integration gives A(T)=A(0)+∫0Tf(t)dt=12+∫0T1t+1dt=12+∫0T(1t+1)dt=12+(ln⁡(t+1))|0T=12+ln⁡(T+1).

Finally, let's rewrite our final answer in terms of the original variable t. We have A(t)=12+ln⁡(t+1).
