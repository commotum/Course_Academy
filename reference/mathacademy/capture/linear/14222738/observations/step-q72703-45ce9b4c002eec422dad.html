Which of the above could be graphs of odd functions?

a
I and II only
b
III and IV only
c
I and III only
d
II and IV only
e
I and IV only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the graph of an odd function must have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin.

  • Graphs II and III do not have rotational symmetry of order 2 about the origin. So, they are not odd functions.

  • On the other hand, graphs I and IV do have rotational symmetry about the origin. If we rotate the graphs 180∘ around the origin, we get the same pictures. This means that graphs I and IV show odd functions.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and IV only."
