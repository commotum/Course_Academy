We need to factor the following expression:

−50x2+50x−8

First, notice that all the terms have a common factor of −2. So, we can simplify the calculation by first factoring out −2: −2(25x2−25x+4)

Now, we just need to factor the trinomial 25x2−25x+4 in parentheses. To do this, we find two numbers that

  • multiply to the product of the quadratic coefficient and the constant (which is 25×4=100), and

  • add to the linear coefficient (which is −25).

Since the linear coefficient is negative and the constant is positive, we only need to consider the negative factor pairs of 100.

The negative factor pairs of 100 are

(−1,−100),(−2,−50),(−4,−25),(−5,−20),(−10,−10).

Notice the numbers in the factor pair (−5,−20) add to −25:

−5−20=−25✓
