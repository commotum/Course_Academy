EXPLANATION

We must factor the quadratic expression 10m2+13m+4.

First, we find two numbers that

  • multiply to the product of the quadratic coefficient and the constant (10×4=40), and

  • add to the linear coefficient (13).

Since all coefficients are positive, we only need to consider the positive factor pairs of 40.

The positive factor pairs of 40 are

(1,40),(2,20),(4,10),(5,8).

Notice the numbers in the factor pair (5,8) add to 13:

5+8=13✓

Then, we write the linear term as 5m+8m, and factor the result by grouping: 10m2+13m+4=10m2+5m+8m+4=(10m2+5m)+(8m+4)=5m(2m+1)+4(2m+1)=(5m+4)(2m+1)
