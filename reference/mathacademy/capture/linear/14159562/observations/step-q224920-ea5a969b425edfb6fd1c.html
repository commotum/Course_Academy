EXPLANATION

We must factor the quadratic expression 4x2−15x+9.

First, we find two numbers that

  • multiply to the product of the quadratic coefficient and the constant (4×9=36), and

  • add to the linear coefficient (−15).

Since the linear coefficient is negative and the product of the quadratic coefficient and constant is positive, we only need to consider the negative factor pairs of 36.

The negative factor pairs of 36 are (−1,−36),(−2,−18),(−3,−12),(−4,−9),(−6,−6).

Notice the numbers in the factor pair (−3,−12) add to −15: (−3)+(−12)=−15✓

Then, we write the linear term as −3x−12x, and factor the result by grouping: 4x2−15x+9=4x2−3x−12x+9=(4x2−3x)−(12x−9)=x(4x−3)−3(4x−3)=(x−3)(4x−3)
