EXPLANATION

We must factor the quadratic expression 5h2−12h−9.

First, we find two numbers that

  • multiply to the product of the quadratic coefficient and the constant (5×−9=−45), and

  • add to the linear coefficient (−12).

The factor pairs of −45 are

(1,−45),(−1,45),(3,−15),(−3,15),(5,−9),(−5,9).

Notice the numbers in the factor pair (3,−15) add to −12:

3−15=−12✓

Then, rewrite the linear term as 3h−15h, and factor the result by grouping:

5h2−12h−9=5h2+3h−15h−9=5h2−15h+3h−9=(5h2−15h)+(3h−9)=5h(h−3)+3(h−3)=(5h+3)(h−3)
