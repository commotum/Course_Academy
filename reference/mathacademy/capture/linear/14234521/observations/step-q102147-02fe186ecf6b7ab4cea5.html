Let X and Y be the set of all non-negative integer powers of 3 and the set of all multiples of 4 greater than 0, respectively. What is X∪Y?

a
{3,4,6,8,9,12,15,16,18,20,…}
b
{1,3,4,6,8,9,12,15,18,20,21,25,…}
c
{1,3,4,8,9,12,16,20,24,27,…}
d
{1,4,8,12,16,20,24,28,…}
e
{1,3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24,…}
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The non-negative integer powers of 3 are

X={1,3,9,27,…}.

Likewise, multiples of 4 that are greater than 0 are

Y={4,8,12,16,20,24,…}.

Now, X∪Y is the union of X and Y, which means that we want to find the elements that are in either X or Y, or both. In other words, X∪Y denotes the set of natural numbers that are either powers of 3 or multiples of 4.

X∪Y={1,3,9,27,…}∪{4,8,12,16,20,24,…}={1,3,4,8,9,12,16,20,24,27,…}.
