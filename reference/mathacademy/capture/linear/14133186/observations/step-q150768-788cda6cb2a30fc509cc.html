Here, the letter x represents the measure of the unknown angle ∠BAC.

Note the following:

  • The angles ∠BAC and ∠CAD are adjacent because they share the side AC→.

  • So, the measure of the large angle (∠BAD) must be the sum of the measures of the two smaller angles (∠BAC and ∠CAD).

  • This means that the measure of ∠BAC must be the difference between the large angle (∠BAD) and the smaller known angle (∠CAD).

Therefore,

x=140∘−80∘=60∘.
