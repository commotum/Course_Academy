EXPLANATION

First, we notice that the function is a negative cubic curve because the coefficient of x3 is negative.

We are told that (x+1) is a factor of f(x), so to find the other roots of f(x) we use synthetic division to divide f(x) by (x+1):

x3x2x1x0−1−10761−1−6−1160

We obtain f(x)=(x+1)(−x2+x+6)=−(x+1)(x2−x−6)=−(x+1)(x+2)(x−3).

Setting the factors equal to zero, we find that the roots of f(x) are x=−1,x=−2, and x=3.

Now, let's find the y-intercept. To do that, we substitute x=0, and we get y=−(0+1)(0+2)(0−3)=−(1)(2)(−3)=6.
