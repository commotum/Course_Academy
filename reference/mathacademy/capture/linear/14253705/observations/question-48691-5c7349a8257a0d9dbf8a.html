The slope of the tangent line to the graph of P(v) at v=4 is equal to 0.5.

a
 When 4 liters of lemonade are sold the previous day, the price increases at a rate of 50 cents per liter
b
 When 4 liters of lemonade are sold the previous day, the price decreases at a rate of 50 cents per liter
c
 When the price of lemonade equals 50 cents, the amount sold increases at a rate of 4 liters per dollar
d
 When 4 liters of lemonade were sold the previous day, the price of lemonade is equal to 50 cents.
e
 When the price of lemonade equals 50 cents, the amount sold decreases at a rate of 4 liters per dollar
