The number of bees y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 20%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

From the given information, we have the following:

  • a=2000 is the initial number of bees.

  • r=0.2 is the growth rate.

  • t is the time, in months, since the start of the study

So, the expression for y(t) is

y(t)=2000⋅(1+0.2)t=2000⋅(1.2)t.

Therefore, to find the time it takes for the hive to increase to 5000 bees, we would need to solve the equation

y(t)=50002000⋅(1.2)t=5000.
