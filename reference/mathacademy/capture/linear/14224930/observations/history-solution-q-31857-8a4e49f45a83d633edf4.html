The "square of a sum" formula states that

x2+2ax+a2=(x+a)2.

We will use this formula to factor the given trinomial. Our trinomial is

x2+18x+81.

First, we write the constant term as a perfect square: x2+18x+92

Then, we factor a 2 out of the linear term:

x2+2⋅9x+92

Finally, we factor the trinomial using the square of sum formula with a=9. This gives

(x+9)2.
