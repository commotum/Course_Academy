a
 (T2)→(T5)→(T4)→(T3)
b
 (T2)→(T3)→(T4)→(T5)

 (T1)→(T2)→(T4)→(T5)
d
 (T1)→(T2)→(T4)→(T3)

 (T2)→(T1)→(T4)→(T3)
