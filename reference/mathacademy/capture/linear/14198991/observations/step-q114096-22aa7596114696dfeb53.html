  • Substituting 4 for x in the polynomial and evaluating, we get the following result: 43−42−21(4)+45=64−16−84+45=9 The polynomial does not equal 0 when 4 is substituted for x. Therefore, 4 is not a root.

  • Substituting 5 for x in the polynomial and evaluating, we get the following result: 53−52−21(5)+45=125−25−105+45=40 The polynomial does not equal 0 when 5 is substituted for x. Therefore, 5 is not a root.

Therefore, the values that are not roots are x=4 and x=5, so the correct answer is "II and III only".
