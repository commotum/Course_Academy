EXPLANATION

The difference of squares formula is given by

a2−b2=(a+b)(a−b)

Notice that all of the terms have a common factor of 6. To simplify the computation, we can factor out the 6, as follows:

486−6x4=6⋅81−6⋅x4=6(81−x4)

The expression in parentheses can be written as a difference of squares: 6(81−x4)=6(92−(x2)2)

Now, we factor our expression using the difference of squares formula: 6(92−(x2)2)=6(9+x2)(9−x2) Finally, we notice that the expression in the second set of parentheses is also a difference of squares. Therefore, the expression can be factored further, as follows: 6(9+x2)(9−x2)=6(9+x2)(32−x2)=6(9+x2)(3+x)(3−x)
