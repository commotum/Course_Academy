The graph above shows that the distance d an electric train travels is directly proportional to the number of hours t since its journey started. Find a direct variation equation that describes the relationship between d and t.

a
d=100t
b
d=800t+40
c
d=240t
d
d=210t

d=840t

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since d varies directly with t, we use the equation

d=kt.

Next, we notice that there is a point on the graph with t=4 and d=840. So, we substitute this data into the equation to find k:

840=k⋅48404=k210=k

Therefore, the direct variation equation is given by d=210t.
