Which of the graphs above represent proportional relationships?

a
I and II only
b
I and III only
c
IV only
d
None
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that the graph of a proportional relationship is always a straight line that passes through the origin (0,0).

Of the given graphs, none of the graphs fits this description.

  • Graphs I, II, and III are not straight lines.

  • Graph IV does not pass through the origin (0,0).

Therefore, the correct answer is "None".
