EXPLANATION

Given a frequency table containing continuous data split into K classes, the mean can be estimated as follows: x―≈1n∑i=1Kfi⋅mi where

  • fi is the frequency of the ith class,

  • mi is the midpoint of the ith class, and

  • n is the total number of observations.

To estimate the mean, we represent the given data as a table and add columns for the midpoints of each class and the products of the midpoints and frequencies.
