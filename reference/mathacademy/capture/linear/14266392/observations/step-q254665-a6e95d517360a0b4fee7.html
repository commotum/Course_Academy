A restaurant manager records the total time 20 customers waited before they were seated. The results are presented in the table below.


Waiting time (minutes) Frequency
0≤x<10 4
10≤x<15 6
15≤x<20 7
20≤x<30 3

Assuming that the waiting times within each class interval are uniformly distributed, find an estimate of the mean waiting time x―, rounded to one decimal place.

Rounded to one decimal place, x―≈
14.6
minutes.

Correct
EXPLANATION

Given a frequency table containing continuous data split into K classes, the mean can be estimated as follows: x―≈1n∑i=1Kfi⋅mi where

  • fi is the frequency of the ith class,

  • mi is the midpoint of the ith class, and

  • n is the total number of observations.

To estimate the mean, we add columns to our table for the midpoints of each class and the products of the midpoints and frequencies.

Waiting time (minutes) fi mi fi⋅mi
0≤x<10 4 5 20
10≤x<15 6 12.5 75
15≤x<20 7 17.5 122.5
20≤x<30 3 25 75

Finally, we sum the values in the last column and divide by 20 (the total number of observations). x―≈20+75+122.5+7520=292.520≈14.6
