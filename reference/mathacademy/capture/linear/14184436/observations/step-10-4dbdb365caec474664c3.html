The value V(t) of Diana's plot of land, in thousands of dollars, can be modeled by the function V(t)=30⋅(1.1)t, where t is the time in years since she bought the land. What is the growth in the value of the plot over the first three years? Round your answer to the nearest thousand dollars.
Diana's land grew in value by approximately
10
thousand dollars over the first three years.

Correct
Explanation

To find the growth in the value over the first three years, we need to find the value after 3 years and then subtract the initial cost:

V(3)−V(0)

In the previous part, we were given the initial value of the land:

V(0)=30

Next, we find V(3), the value of the land after 3 years:

V(3)=30⋅(1.1)(3)=30⋅(1.331)=39.93

Therefore, the growth in the first three years is

V(3)−V(0)=39.93−30=9.93≈10.

Therefore, the growth in the value of the land over the first three years was approximately 10 thousand dollars.
