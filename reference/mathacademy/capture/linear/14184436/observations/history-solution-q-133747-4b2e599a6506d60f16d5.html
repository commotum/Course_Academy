To find the growth in the value over the first three years, we need to find the value after 3 years and then subtract the initial cost: V(3)−V(0) In the previous part, we were given the initial value of the land: V(0)=30 Next, we find V(3), the value of the land after 3 years: V(3)=30⋅(1.1)(3)=30⋅(1.331)=39.93 Therefore, the growth in the first three years is V(3)−V(0)=39.93−30=9.93≈10. Therefore, the growth in the value of the land over the first three years was approximately 10 thousand dollars.
