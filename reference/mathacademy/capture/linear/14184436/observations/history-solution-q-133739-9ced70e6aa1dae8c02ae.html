The width of the plot is x−1 meters, and its area is 2x2−2x square meters. Since the area=length×width, we factor the expression for the area to find the length. First, we find the GCF of the two terms: GCF(2x2,−2x)=2x Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the GCF: 2x2−2x=(2x⋅x)+(2x⋅(−1)) Next, we write the GCF outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside: (2x⋅x)+(2x⋅(−1))=2x(x−1) As a result, we conclude that the length of the plot must be 2x meters.
