First, we find the GCF of the two terms:

GCF(2x2,−2x)=2x

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the GCF:

2x2−2x=(2x⋅x)+(2x⋅(−1))

Next, we write the GCF outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

(2x⋅x)+(2x⋅(−1))=2x(x−1)

As a result, we conclude that the length of the plot must be 2x meters.

Correct
Explanation

To find the required width, we need to factor the expression x2+x−6.

First, we find the factor pairs of the constant term (−6). The constant term is negative, so that means one of the factors is negative, and the other is positive:

1×(−6)(−1)×62×(−3)(−2)×3

Now, we find which factor pair adds up to the coefficient of the linear term 3:

1+(−6)=−5×−1+6=5×2+(−3)=−1×−2+3=1✓

Using the factor pair identified above, write the linear term as x=−2x+3x:

x2+x−6=x2−2x+3x−6

Finally, we factor the first two terms and the last two terms separately, and then factor the expression completely:

x2−2x+3x−6=(x2−2x)+(3x−6)=x(x−2)+3(x−2)=(x−2)(x+3)

As a result, we conclude that the width must be x−2 meters.
