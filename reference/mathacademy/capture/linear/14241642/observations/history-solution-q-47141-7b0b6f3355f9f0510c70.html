To estimate the probability that a randomly chosen student preferred apples or bananas or is a girl, we

  • add up the numbers in the columns "Apple" or "Banana" or the row "Girls", and

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

The numbers in the columns "Apple" or "Banana" or the row "Girls" are highlighted below.

Apple Orange Banana Totals
Girls 11 8 7 26
Boys 12 6 6 24
Totals 23 14 13 50

Adding up these numbers and then dividing by the total number of data points in the table (50), we get

p=11+12+8+7+650=4450=0.88.
