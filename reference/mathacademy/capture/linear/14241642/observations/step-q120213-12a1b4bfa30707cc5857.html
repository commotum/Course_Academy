A survey asks a group of students about their eye color. The findings are summarized in the two-way frequency table below.

Green Blue Brown Black Totals
Girls 10 12 8 10 40
Boys 13 12 15 20 60
Totals 23 24 23 30 100

Let B be the event that a randomly selected student is a "Boy", and C be the event that a randomly selected student has "Brown" eyes. Estimate P(B∩C).

a

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, note that B∩C is the event that a randomly selected student is a boy and has brown eyes.

To estimate the probability of B∩C, we

  • find the number at the intersection of the row "Boys" and the column "Brown", and then

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

The number at the intersection of the row "Boys" and the column "Brown" is highlighted in the table below.

Green Blue Brown Black Totals
Girls 10 12 8 10 40
Boys 13 12 15 20 60
Totals 23 24 23 30 100

Dividing by the total number of data points in the table (100), we get

P(B∩C)=15100=320.
