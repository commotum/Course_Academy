To estimate the probability that a randomly chosen student in the school has either a cat or a dog but not both, we

  • add up the numbers in the row " Has a cat" or the column "Has a dog" but not in their intersection, and

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

The numbers in the row "Has a cat" or the column "Has a dog" but not in their intersection are highlighted below.

Has a dog Doesn't have a dog Totals
Has a cat 6 9 15
Doesn't have a cat 10 7 17
Totals 16 16 32

Adding up these numbers and then dividing by the total number of data points in the table (32), we get

p=9+1032=1932.
