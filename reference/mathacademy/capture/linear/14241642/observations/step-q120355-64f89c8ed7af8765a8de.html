Some customers in a pizzeria were asked about the type of pizza they prefer between spicy pizza and vegetarian pizza. The results of the survey are summarized in the two-way frequency table below.

Prefers Spicy Prefers Vegetarian Totals
Children 10 22 32
Adults 41 27 68
Totals 51 49 100

Estimate the probability that a randomly chosen person is an adult or prefers spicy pizza (or both).

a
0.51
b
0.78
c
0.37

0.68

Correct
EXPLANATION

To estimate the probability that a randomly chosen person is an adult or prefers spicy pizza, we

  • add up the numbers in the row "Adults" or the column "Prefers Spicy", and

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

The numbers in the row "Adults" or the column "Prefers Spicy" are highlighted below.

Prefers Spicy Prefers Vegetarian Totals
Children 10 22 32
Adults 41 27 68
Totals 51 49 100

Adding up these numbers and then dividing by the total number of data points in the table (100), we get

p=10+41+27100=78100=0.78.
