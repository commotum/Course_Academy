Let F be the event that a randomly selected student in the high school is a "Girl" whose favorite sport is "Football", and S be the event that a randomly selected student in the high school is a "Girl" whose favorite sport is "Soccer". Estimate P(F∪S).

EXPLANATION

First, note that F∪S is the event that a randomly selected student in the high school is a girl whose favorite sport is football or a girl whose favorite sport is soccer.

To estimate the probability of F∪S, we

  • add up the numbers in the columns "Football" or "Soccer" and the row "Girls", and

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

The numbers in the columns "Football" or "Soccer" and the row "Girls" are highlighted below.

Baseball Football Basketball Soccer Totals
Girls 10 19 10 12 51
Boys 10 13 20 6 49
Totals 20 32 30 18 100
