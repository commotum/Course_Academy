EXPLANATION

To estimate the number of students who like biology or chemistry but not both, we

  • add up the numbers in the row "Like chemistry" or the column " Like biology", but not in both, and then

  • divide by the total number of data points in the table.

Like biology Doesn't like biology Totals
Like chemistry 25 19 44
Doesn't like chemistry 8 10 18
Totals 33 29 62

Adding up these numbers and then dividing by the total number of data points in the table (62), we get

p=8+1962=2762=0.435.

This represents the estimated probability that a randomly selected student likes biology or chemistry but not both.

Finally, to find the estimated number of students of Edison High School who like biology or chemistry but not both, we multiply the total number of students in the Edison High School (500) by the probability above: n=500⋅p=500⋅0.435=217.5=218, rounded to the nearest integer.
