Given the graph of y=f(x) above, which of the following statements is true?

a
f′(−2)<f′(−1)<f′(−4)
b
f′(−4)<f′(−1)<f′(−2)
c
f′(−1)<f′(−2)<f′(−4)
d
f′(−2)<f′(−4)<f′(−1)
e
f′(−4)<f′(−2)<f′(−1)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

From the graph, we see that

  • The slope of the tangent at x=−4 is negative, so f′(−4)<0.

  • The slope of the tangent at x=−2 is positive, so f′(−2)>0.

  • The slope of the tangent at x=−1 is zero, so f′(−1)=0.

Therefore, f′(−4)<f′(−1)<f′(−2).
