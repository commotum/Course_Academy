Given the graph of y=f(x) above, which of the following statements is true?

a
f′(1)<f′(3)<f′(−3)
b
f′(3)<f′(−3)<f′(1)
c
f′(−3)<f′(1)<f′(3)
d
f′(−3)<f′(3)<f′(1)
e
f′(3)<f′(1)<f′(−3)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

From the graph, we see that

  • The slope of the tangent at x=−3 is negative, so f′(−3)<0.

  • The slope of the tangent at x=1 is positive, so f′(1)>0.

  • The slope of the tangent at x=3 is zero, so f′(3)=0.

Therefore, f′(−3)<f′(3)<f′(1).
