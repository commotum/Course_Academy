The powers of 2 that are greater or equal to 1 are

A={1,2,4,8,16,32,64,…}.

Multiples of 4 that are greater than 0 are

B={4,8,12,16,20,24,…}.

Now, A∩B is the intersection of A and B, which means that we want to find the elements that are in both A and B. In other words, A∩B denotes the set of natural numbers that are both powers of 2 and multiples of 4. So, we have

A∩B={1,2,4,8,16,32,64,…}∩{4,8,12,16,20,24,…}={4,8,16,32,64,…}.
