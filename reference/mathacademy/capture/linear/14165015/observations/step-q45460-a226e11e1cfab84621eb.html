Max initially had $2 in his piggy bank. Then, thanks to a generous relative, the amount of money in Max's piggy bank doubled at the end of every day. How much money will Max have in the piggy bank after 6 days?

a
$48
b
$256
c
$64
d
$32
e
$128
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Initially, Max has $2 in the piggy bank, and this amount doubles every day.

Therefore, we have:

  • After 1 day, Max has 2⋅2 dollars.

  • After 2 days, Max will have 2⋅(2)2 dollars.

  • After 3 days, Max will have 2⋅(2)3 dollars.

  • …

  • After 6 days, Max will have 2⋅(2)6 dollars.

Evaluating the expression for its value after 6 days, we get:

2⋅(2)6=2⋅64=128

Thus, Max will have $128 after 6 days.
