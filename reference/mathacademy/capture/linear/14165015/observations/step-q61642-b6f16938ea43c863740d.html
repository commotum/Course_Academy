The price of a gallon of gasoline is $2. Some economists estimate that its price will rise by 2% weekly. What will be the price of a gallon of gasoline after 4 weeks?

a
2⋅(1.02)4
b
2⋅(0.02)4
c
2⋅4+1.02
d
 2⋅1.02+4
e
2⋅1.02⋅4
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The price of a gallon of gasoline is $2, and this amount will increase by 2% each week.

2% as a decimal is 0.02. Therefore, an increase of 2% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of

1+0.02=1.02.

Therefore, we have:

  • After 1 week, the price will be 2⋅(1.02) dollars.

  • After 2 weeks, the price will be 2⋅(1.02)2 dollars.

  • After 3 weeks, the price will be 2⋅(1.02)3 dollars.

  • After 4 weeks, the price will be 2⋅(1.02)4 dollars.
