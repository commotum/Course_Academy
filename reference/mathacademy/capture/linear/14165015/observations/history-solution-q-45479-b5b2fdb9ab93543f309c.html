Initially, there were 3,000 ants, and this number triples every month.

Therefore, we have:

  • After 1 month, there will be 3000⋅3 ants.

  • After 2 months, the will be 3000⋅(3)2 ants.

  • After 3 months, there will be 3000⋅(3)3 ants.

Evaluating the expression for the number of ants after 3 months, we get:

3,000⋅(3)3=3,000⋅27=81,000

Therefore, after 3 months, there will be 81,000 ants
