The second and third terms of an arithmetic sequence are 45 and 80, respectively. Determine the formula for the nth term of the sequence.

a
an=20n+5
b
an=5n+25
c
an=9n+27
d
an=15n+15
e
an=35n−25

Correct
EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term is given by

an=a1+(n−1)d,

so we need to figure out the first term a1 and the common difference d.

Since the terms a2=45 and a3=80 are consecutive, we can subtract them to find the common difference:

d=a3−a2=80−45=35

Now, we can use one of our known values, say, a2=45, to determine the first term of the sequence. Since a2 is 1 jump after a1, we have:

a2=a1+1⋅d45=a1+3545−35=a1a1=10

So the formula for the nth term of this arithmetic sequence is:

an=a1+(n−1)d=10+(n−1)⋅35=10+35n−35=35n−25
