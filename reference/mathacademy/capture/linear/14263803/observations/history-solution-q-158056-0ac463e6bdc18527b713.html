First, let's recall the graph of y=sec⁡x.

From the graph, we note the following:

  • The period of sec⁡x is 2π.

  • Our graph is symmetric about the y-axis. This means that sec⁡x is an even function, and we have sec⁡(−x)=sec⁡x.

Let's now evaluate our expression:

  • Firstly, using the periodicity of sec⁡x, we have sec⁡(−x−10π)=sec⁡(−x−2π⋅5)=sec⁡(−x).

  • Secondly, using the evenness of sec⁡x, we have sec⁡(−x)=sec⁡x=3.

Therefore, 3sec⁡(−x−10π)−5=3(3)−5=9−5=4.
