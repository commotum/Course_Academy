If sec⁡x=76, then what is 18sec⁡(x−18π)+2?

Correct
EXPLANATION

The period of y=sec⁡x is 2π. Therefore, for integer n we have sec⁡x=sec⁡(x+2nπ).

Substituting n=−9 into the right-hand-side of the above gives sec⁡x=sec⁡(x+2⋅(−9)⋅π)=sec⁡(x−18π).

Therefore, if sec⁡x=76, then sec⁡(x−18π)=76.

Finally, then 18sec⁡(x−18π)+2=18(76)+2=21+2=23.
