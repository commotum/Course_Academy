The standard form equation of a line is

ax+by=c,

where

  • a,b, and c are all integers,

  • a≥0, and

  • a,b, and c have no common factors greater than 1.

  • Equation I is not in standard form because the coefficients (4,2, and 14) have a common factor of 2.

  • Equation II is not in standard form because the coefficients (5 and 10) have a common factor of 5.
