Notice that the coefficient of t in the first equation is 2, and the coefficient of t in the second equation is −2.

Since these numbers add to zero, we can add the two equations, eliminate the variable t, and solve the resulting equation for u:

−u+2t=−23u−2t=10(−u+3u)=(−2+10)2u=8u=4

So, the solution must have u=4. However, we still need to find t. To do this, we substitute u=4 back into one of the equations. Let's choose the first equation:

−u+2t=−2−(4)+2t=−22t=2t=1

Therefore, the solution to the system of equations is u=4,t=1, which can be expressed as (4,1).
