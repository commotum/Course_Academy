Expressed as a nonnegative integer smaller than 17, the additive inverse of 34 modulo 17 is
0
.

Correct
EXPLANATION

The additive inverse of 34 modulo 17 is the integer 0≤x<17 such that

x+34≡0(mod17).

First, note that 34≡2⋅17≡0(mod17), and 0+0≡0(mod17).

Therefore, the additive inverse of the element 34 modulo 17 is 0.

We can verify this result as follows: 34+0≡2⋅17+0≡2⋅0+0≡0✓(mod17)

Note: In general, the additive inverse of a modulo n is simply the residue of −a modulo n, i.e. −amodn.
