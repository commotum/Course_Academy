EXPLANATION

The additive inverse of 29 modulo 42 is the integer 0≤x<42 such that

x+29≡0(mod42).

Notice that −29+29≡0, so x≡−29(mod32). To write this in the required form, we use the following sequence of congruences:

x≡−29(mod42)≡0−29(mod42)≡42−29(mod42)≡13(mod42)

Therefore, the additive inverse of the element 19 modulo 42 is 13.

Note: The additive inverse of a modulo n is simply the residue of −a modulo n, i.e. −amodn.
