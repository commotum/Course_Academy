The additive inverse of −10 modulo 14 is the integer 0≤x<14 such that

x+(−10)≡0(mod14).

Notice that 10+(−10)≡0, so x≡10(mod14), which is already in the required form.

Therefore, the additive inverse of the element −10 modulo 14 is 10.

Note: In general, the additive inverse of a modulo n is simply the residue of −a modulo n, i.e. −amodn.
