Which of the following polynomials IS NOT written in standard form?

a
1−x−5x2
b
−3
c
3x2−5x
d
x2−1
e
4x4+2x2+1
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A polynomial is written in standard form when the terms are arranged in descending order of the variable's exponent. The first term should have the largest exponent, and the last term should have the smallest exponent.

The polynomial 1−x−5x2 is not in standard form. The highest exponent term is −5x2, followed by −x, and finally 1. So, the standard form is −5x2−x+1 which is different from the given arrangement.
