A polynomial is written in standard form when the terms are arranged in descending order of the variable's exponent. The first term should have the largest exponent, and the last term should have the smallest exponent.

Let's analyze each polynomial.

  • Polynomial I is not in standard form. The highest exponent term is x7, followed by −7x5, and finally 5x2. So, the standard form is x7−7x5+5x2, which is different from the given arrangement.

  • Polynomial II is not in standard form. The highest exponent term is x8, followed by 3x6, and finally 9x4. So, the standard form is x8+3x6+9x4, which is different from the given arrangement.

  • Polynomial III is in standard form. The highest exponent term is x7 followed by 5x2, and finally −7. So, the standard form is indeed x7+5x2−7.

In conclusion, only polynomial III is given in standard form.
