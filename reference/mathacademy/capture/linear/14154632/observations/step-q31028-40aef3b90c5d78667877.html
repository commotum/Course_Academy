EXPLANATION

Here, 4x2 and −5x22 are like terms, and the −5x3 and 3x are also like terms. So, we group and add each pair of like terms separately:

−5x3+4x2−5x22+3x=(4x2−5x22)+(3x−5x3)=(4−52)x2+(3−53)x=32x2+43x.
