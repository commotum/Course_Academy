Which of the following are logically equivalent to R?

  1. (T∨R)∧T
  2. R∧(T∨R)
  3. (R∨T)∧R
a
I, II, and III

Correct
EXPLANATION

The law that conjunction absorbs disjunction is stated as

R∧(R∨T)≡R.

With that in mind, let's inspect each of the given statements.

  • Statement I is not logically equivalent to R. We have (T∨R)∧T≡T∧(T∨R)≡T≢R.×

  • Statement II is logically equivalent to R. We have R∧(T∨R)≡R∧(R∨T)≡R.✓

  • Statement III is logically equivalent to R. We have (R∨T)∧R≡R∧(R∨T)≡R.✓

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
