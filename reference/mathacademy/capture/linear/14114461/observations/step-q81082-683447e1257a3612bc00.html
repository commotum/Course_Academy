EXPLANATION

The law that disjunction absorbs conjunction is stated as

X∨(X∧Y)≡X.

With that in mind, let's inspect each of the given statements.

  • Statement I is not logically equivalent to X. This statement cannot be simplified using the absorption laws.

  • Statement II is logically equivalent to X. We have (Y∧X)∨X≡X∨(Y∧X)≡X∨(X∧Y)≡X.✓

  • Statement III is not logically equivalent to A. We have Y∨(X∧Y)≡Y∨(Y∧X)≡Y≢X.×

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
