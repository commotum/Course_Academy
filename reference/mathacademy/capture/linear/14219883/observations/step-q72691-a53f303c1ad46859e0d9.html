Chantelle did an experiment in her Chemistry class. She heated a liquid whose initial temperature was −5.3∘C. After several minutes, the temperature of the liquid increased by 24.7∘C, at which point she decided to end the experiment. What was the final temperature of the liquid?

a
18.0∘C
b
18.4∘C
c
19.4∘C
d
19.0∘C
e
20.0∘C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine the final temperature of the liquid, we add the variation in temperature to the initial temperature of the liquid. This gives −5.3∘C+24.7∘C=19.4∘C.

Therefore, the temperature of the liquid at the end of the experiment was 19.4∘C.
