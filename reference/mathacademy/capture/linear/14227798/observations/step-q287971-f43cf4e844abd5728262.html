Consider the equation (p−3)x=4x where p is a constant. Given that this equation has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of p?

Correct
EXPLANATION

Suppose we have an equation of the form ax=bx where a and b are constants. If a=b, then the equation has infinitely many solutions.

Notice that this equation is in the form ax=bx. In this case, a=p−3,b=4.

Therefore, the equation has infinitely many solutions if p−3=4.

Solving this for p, we get p=7.
