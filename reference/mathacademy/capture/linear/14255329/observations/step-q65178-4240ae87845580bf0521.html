If cos⁡x=53, then 5cos⁡(x−2π)−2=

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The period of y=cos⁡x is 2π. Therefore, for an integer n we have

cos⁡x=cos⁡(x+2nπ).

Substituting n=−1 into the right-hand-side gives

cos⁡x=cos⁡(x−2π).

Therefore, if cos⁡x=53, then cos⁡(x−2π)=53.

Finally, then

5cos⁡(x−2π)−2=5(53)−2=(5)23−2=53−2=−13.
