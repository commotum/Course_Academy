Mark has 235 liters of strawberry juice and 74 liters of orange juice remaining.

We cannot compare the fractions directly as they don't have a common denominator.

We can make a common denominator of 5×4=20.

To put 235 over a denominator of 20, we first convert to an improper fraction and then multiply the numerator and denominator by 4: 235=135=13×45×4=5220

To put 74 over a denominator of 20, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 5:

74=7×54×5=3520

We can now compare the fractions 5220 and 3520 by looking at the numerators only.

Since 52>35, the correct statement must be:

5220>3520

So, the correct answer is

"After Rose takes her part of strawberry juice, Mark's remaining amount of strawberry juice is > than the remaining amount of orange juice because 235>74."
