What is the explicit formula for the nth term of the geometric sequence given by f(n+1)=f(n)2,f(1)=12,n≥1?

a
f(n)=12n,n≥1
b
f(n)=2n−1,n≥1
c
f(n)=12n−1,n≥1
d
f(n)=14n,n≥1
e
f(n)=2n,n≥1

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's rewrite our recursive formula, as follows:

f(n+1)=f(n)2=12f(n)

The explicit formula for the nth term of a geometric sequence is

f(n)=f(1)⋅rn−1,n≥1.

So, we must determine the first term f(1) and the common ratio r.

We are given that the first term is f(1)=12. To find the common ratio, we compare the given sequence with the general recursive formula for a geometric sequence:

f(n+1)=r⋅f(n)

From the above, we see that r=12.

Substituting f(1)=12 and r=12 into the formula for the nth term, we obtain f(n)=f(1)⋅rn−1=12⋅(12)n−1.

We can simplify further by manipulating the exponents as follows: f(n)=12⋅(12)n−1=12⋅(12)−1⋅(12)n=12⋅21⋅(12)n=12n

Therefore, the formula for the nth term is f(n)=12n,n≥1.
