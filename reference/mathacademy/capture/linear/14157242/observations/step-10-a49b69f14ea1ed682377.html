The value V(t) of Fred's plot of land, in thousands of dollars, can be modeled by the function V(t)=200⋅(1.1)t, where t is the time in years since he bought the land. What is the growth in the value of the plot over the first two years? Round your answer to the nearest thousand dollars.

Explanation

To find the growth in the value over the first two years, we need to find the value after 2 years and then subtract the initial cost:

V(2)−V(0)

In the previous part, we were given the initial value of the land:

V(0)=200

Next, we find V(2), the value of the land after 2 years:

V(2)=200⋅(1.1)(2)=200⋅(1.21)=242

Therefore, the growth in the first two years is

V(2)−V(0)=242−200=42.

Therefore, the growth in the value of the land over the first two years was 42 thousand dollars.
