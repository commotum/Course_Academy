The land initially costs 200 thousand, which increases at a rate of 10% per year. 10% as a decimal is 0.1. Therefore, an increase of 10% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of 1+0.1=1.1. Therefore, we have: * After 1 year, the value will be 200⋅(1.1) thousand dollars. * After 2 years, the value will be 200⋅(1.1)2 thousand dollars. * After 3 years, the value will be 200⋅(1.1)3 thousand dollars. * After 4 years, the value will be 200⋅(1.1)4 thousand dollars. Evaluating the expression for the value after 4 years, we get the following: 200⋅(1.1)4=292.82≈293, rounded to the nearest integer. Therefore, after 4 years, the land will be worth approximately 293 thousand dollars.
