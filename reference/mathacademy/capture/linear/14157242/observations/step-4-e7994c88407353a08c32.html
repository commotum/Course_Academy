Explanation

In part 1, we found that the length of Fred's plot is 2x meters.

To find the required distance, we need to subtract polynomials 2x and x−5:

We put a minus sign between the two polynomials, distribute it over the second polynomial, collect like terms, and then simplify:

(2x)−(x−5)=2x−x+5=(2−1)x+5=x+5
