b
−5≤f(x)≤1

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we find the lowest and highest y-values in the graph.

  • The lowest y-value is 1. This occurs at the point (−2,1).

  • The highest y-value is 5. This occurs at the point (2,5).

The function covers all the y-values between 1 and 5, including both 1 and 5.

Therefore, the range is 1≤y≤5, which can also be written as 1≤f(x)≤5.
