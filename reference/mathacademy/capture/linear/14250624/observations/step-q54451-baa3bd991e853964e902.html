EXPLANATION

Let's sketch the graph of y=log2⁡(x+4).

The function is not defined for x≤−4. Therefore,

limx→−4−log2⁡(x+4)=DNE.

However, the function is defined for x>−4. Moreover,

limx→−4+log2⁡(x+4)=−∞.

Since the left and right-sided limits are not equal, we have limx→−4log2⁡(x+4)=DNE.
