The polygon A′B′C′D′E′ is the image of the polygon ABCDE under the transformation (x,y)↦(y,−x). Which of the following statements are true?

  1. BC=B′C′.
  2. AB=A′B′.
  3. CD<C′D′.
a
 I and III only
b
II and III only
c
I and II only
d
I only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The given transformation is a rotation of 90∘ about the origin, which is a rigid motion. Rigid motions preserve the distances between all points and all angle measures.

With that in mind, let's check each statement.

  • Statement I is true. Under the action of the transformation, the distance between all corresponding points is preserved. Therefore, BC=B′C′.

  • Statement II is true. Under the action of the transformation, the distance between all corresponding points is preserved. Therefore, AB=A′B′.

  • Statement III is false. Under the action of the transformation, the distance between all corresponding points is preserved. Therefore, CD=C′D′.

As a result, the correct answer is "I and II only."
