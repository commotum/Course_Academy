If cot⁡x=−4 for some x, then find the value of 3cot⁡(x+17π)+6.

a
−6
b
−18
c
18
d
−9
e
6
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The period of y=cot⁡x is π. Therefore, for any integer n, we have

cot⁡x=cot⁡(x+nπ).

Substituting n=17 into the above gives

cot⁡x=cot⁡(x+17⋅π)=cot⁡(x+17π).

Therefore, if cot⁡x=−4, then cot⁡(x+17π)=−4.

Finally, then

3cot⁡(x+7π)+6=3(−4)+6=−12+6=−6.
