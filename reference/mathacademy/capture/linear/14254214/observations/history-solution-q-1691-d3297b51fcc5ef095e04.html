First, we find an angle that's coterminal with −300∘ that lies in the range [0,360∘).

−300∘+360∘=60∘✓

Therefore, tan⁡(−300∘)=tan⁡60∘.

Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle 60∘ corresponds to the point (12,32).

  • Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡60∘=12.

  • Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡60∘=32.

Therefore, using the fact that tan⁡θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θ, we have tan⁡60∘=sin⁡60∘cos⁡60∘=(32)(12)=3.
