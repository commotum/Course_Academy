First, we find an angle that's coterminal with −3π4 that lies in the range [0,2π).

−3π4+2π=5π4✓

Therefore, sec⁡(−3π4)=sec⁡(5π4).

Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle 5π4 corresponds to the point (−22,−22).

Since the cosine of the angle corresponds to the x-coordinate, we have cos⁡(5π4)=−22.

Therefore, using the fact that sec⁡θ=1cos⁡θ, we have sec⁡(5π4)=1cos⁡(5π4)=1(−22)=−22=−2.

Therefore, sec⁡(−3π4)=−2.
