EXPLANATION

The variable z is being multiplied by −2. To isolate z, we perform the opposite operation, which is dividing by −2. Remember to perform the same operation on both sides.

Also, when multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative number, we need to flip the inequality sign: −2z<7−2z−2>7−2−2z−2>−72

Now, we can cancel a common factor of −2 from both the numerator and denominator. −2z−2>−72−2z−2>−72z>−72

Therefore, the solution is z>−72.
