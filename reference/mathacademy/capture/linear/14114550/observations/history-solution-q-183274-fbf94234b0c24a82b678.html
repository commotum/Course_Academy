In our conditional statement:

  • The antecedent is A: 3 divides 6.

  • The consequent is B: 6 is even.

The inverse of a conditional statement A⇒B is given by

¬A⇒¬B.

First, we write down the negations of A and B:

  • ¬A: 3 doesn't divide 6.

  • ¬B: 6 is not even.

Therefore, the inverse is the following:

If 3 doesn't divide 6, then 6 is not even.

Your Answer:
If 6 is not even, then 3 divides 6.
