If you are sick, then you are not going to school.

Which of the following statements are equivalent to the conditional statement above?

  1. If you are not going to school, then you are sick.
  2. If you are not sick, then you are going to school.
  3. If you are going to school, then you are not sick.
a
I and II only
b
II and III only
c
II only
d
III only
e
I only

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's recall the following facts:

  • The contrapositive is logically equivalent to the original statement.

  • The inverse and the converse are logically equivalent to each other, but not to the original statement.

So, we are looking for the contrapositive.

  • Statement I is the converse, not the contrapositive. ×

  • Statement II is the inverse, not the contrapositive. ×

  • Statement III is the contrapositive. ✓

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
