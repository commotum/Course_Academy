EXPLANATION

To plot y=45x+1, we apply the following steps:

  • Take the curve y=1x.
  • Then, stretch the result by a scale factor of 45 parallel to the y-axis to get the graph of y=45x.
  • Translate it by 3 units up to get the graph of y=45x+3.
