A similarity transformation is any transformation made up of rigid motions (rotations, reflections, and translations) and dilations.

A similarity transformation will map any polygon to a similar polygon.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the transformations in turn.

  • Transformation I represents a translation by 5 units to the left. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation II represents a rotation by 90∘ counterclockwise around the origin. Thus, it is a similarity transformation.

  • Transformation III represents a stretch of factor 3 in the y-direction. This is not a rigid motion.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
