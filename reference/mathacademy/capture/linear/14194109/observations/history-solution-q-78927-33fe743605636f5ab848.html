The "square of a difference" formula states that

x2−2ax+a2=(x−a)2.

We will use this formula to factor the given trinomial. Our trinomial is

x2−53x+2536.

First, we write the constant term as a perfect square:

x2−53x+(56)2

Then, we factor a 2 out of the linear term:

x2−2⋅56x+(56)2

Finally, we factor the trinomial using the square of difference formula with a=56. This gives

(x−56)2.
