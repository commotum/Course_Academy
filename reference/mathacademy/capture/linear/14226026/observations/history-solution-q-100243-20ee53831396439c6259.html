The laws of logarithms are:

  • logn⁡(a)+logn⁡(b)=logn⁡(ab) - the product rule

  • logn⁡(a)−logn⁡(b)=logn⁡(ab) - the quotient rule

  • logn⁡(cm)=mlogn⁡(c) - the power rule

Using the power rule and then the quotient rule for logarithms, we obtain 13log4⁡(x)−log4⁡(y)=log4⁡(x1/3)−log4⁡(y)=log4⁡(x3)−log4⁡(y)=log4⁡(x3y).
