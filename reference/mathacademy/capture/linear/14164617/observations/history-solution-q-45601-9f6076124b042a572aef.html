To find the growth of the patch between day 1 and day 2, we need to subtract the area after day 1 from the area after day 2. So we need to calculate

A(2)−A(1).

First, we find the area after the first day, A(1): A(1)=10⋅(1.1)1=10⋅1.1=11

Next, we find A(2), the area after the second day:

A(2)=10⋅(1.1)2=10⋅1.21=12.1

Therefore, the growth of the patch between day 1 and day 2 is A(2)−A(1)=12.1−11=1.1.

So, the area increased by 1.1 square kilometer between day 1 and day 2.
