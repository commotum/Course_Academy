Owen enrolled in an online course that has 12 topics. He completed 16 of the remaining topics every week. Which expression gives approximately the number of topics that were incomplete after 3 weeks?

a
123⋅(16)
b
12⋅(76)3
c
12⋅(56)3
d
12⋅(16)3
e
123⋅(56)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the number of incomplete topics decreased by 16 every week, then every week 56 of the topics remained incomplete.

Therefore:

  • After 1 week, 12⋅(56) topics were incomplete.

  • After 2 weeks, approximately 12⋅(56)2 topics were incomplete.

  • After 3 weeks, approximately 12⋅(56)3 topics were incomplete.

Elena has 400 TVs in her store. She expects that the number of TVs will decrease by 2% daily due to sales. Approximately how many TVs will be in her store after 3 days?

a
400⋅(0.98)3
b
400⋅(0.8)3
c
4003⋅(0.98)
d
400⋅(1.02)3
e
400⋅(0.02)3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The number 2% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.02. Therefore, a decrease of 0.02% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.02=0.98

Therefore:

  • After 1 day, there will be 400⋅(0.98) TVs remaining.

  • After 2 days, there will be approximately 400⋅(0.98)2 TVs remaining.

  • After 3 days, there will be approximately 400⋅(0.98)3 TVs remaining.
