The number 2% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.02. Therefore, a decrease of 2% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.02=0.98

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, there will be 11000⋅(0.98) rabbits.

  • After 2 years, there will be approximately 11000⋅(0.98)2 rabbits.

  • After 3 years, there will be approximately 11000⋅(0.98)3 rabbits.

  • …

Repeating this pattern, we see that there will be approximately 11000⋅(0.98)10 rabbits in 10 years.
