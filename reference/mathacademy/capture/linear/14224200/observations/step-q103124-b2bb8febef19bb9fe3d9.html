A company initially had 40320 followers on a social media platform. If the number of followers declines by 18 every month after some bad publicity, which expression gives approximately the number of followers after 4 months?

a
40320⋅(78)4
b
40320⋅(18)4
c
40320⋅(98)4
d
403204⋅(18)
e
403204⋅(78)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

If the number of followers reduces by 18 every month, then 78 of the followers remains every month.

Therefore:

  • After 1 month, there will be 40320⋅(78) followers.

  • After 2 months, there will be 40320⋅(78)2 followers.

  • After 3 months, there will be approximately 40320⋅(78)3 followers.

  • After 4 months, there will be approximately 40320⋅(78)4 followers.
