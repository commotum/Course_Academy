Determine the weekly profit if the company produces 24 wooden tables in a week.
The company will earn
5184
dollars.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

From part 3, we have the function p(x)=−6x2+360x.

We substitute x=24 into the function definition and then simplify:

p(24)=−6(24)2+360(24)=−3456+8640=5184

Therefore, the company will earn $5184.
