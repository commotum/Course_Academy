Express P⇔Q as a sentence given the following:

  • P: A triangle is right.

  • Q: A triangle has two complementary angles.

a
A triangle is right and it has two complementary angles.
b
A triangle is right, provided that it has two complementary angles.
c
A triangle has two complementary angles only if it is a right triangle.
d
A triangle has two complementary angles, if it is a right triangle.
e
A triangle is right if and only if it has two complementary angles.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's recall the following grammatical constructions for the biconditional P⇔Q:

  • P if and only if Q

  • P iff Q

  • P is necessary and sufficient for Q

  • P is equivalent to Q

  • If P then Q, and conversely

Therefore, among the given options, the correct grammatical construction of P⇔Q is the following:

A triangle is right if and only if it has two complementary angles.
