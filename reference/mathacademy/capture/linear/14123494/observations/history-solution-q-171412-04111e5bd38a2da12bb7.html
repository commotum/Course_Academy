Let's recall the following grammatical constructions for the biconditional P⇔Q:

  • P if and only if Q

  • P iff Q

  • P is necessary and sufficient for Q

  • P is equivalent to Q

  • If P then Q, and conversely

Therefore, among the given options, the correct grammatical construction of A⇔B is the following:

4 is even if and only if 4 is divisible by 2.

Your Answer:
4 is even if 4 is divisible by 2.
