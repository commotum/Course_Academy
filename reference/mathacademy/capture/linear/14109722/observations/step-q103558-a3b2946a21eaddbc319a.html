Which of the following statements are true?

  1. If 12 is divisible by 3, then 9 is divisible by 4.
  2. If 5 is divisible by 2, then 10 is divisible by 5.
  3. If 9 is divisible by 5, then 6 is divisible by 3.
a
II and III only
b
I and II only
c
III only
d
I only
e
I and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

An implication A⇒B is true unless A is true and B is false. In other words, the implication is true unless the "promise" is broken.

  • Statement I is false. The antecedent "12 is divisible by 3" is true, and the consequent "9 is divisible by 4" is false. So the promise is broken, and the statement is false.

  • Statement II is true. The antecedent "5 is divisible by 2" is false, so the promise does not apply. Regardless of the consequent, the promise is not broken, and the statement is true.

  • Statement III is true. Again, the antecedent "9 is divisible by 5" is false, so the promise does not apply. Regardless of the consequent, the promise is not broken, and the statement is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
