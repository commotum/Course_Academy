EXPLANATION

First, notice that −15=(−1)5.

To raise the fraction to the power of 2, we must raise both the numerator and the denominator to the power of 2:

(−15)2=((−1)5)2=(−1)252

Now, using (−1)2=1 and 52=25, we get

(−1)252=125.
