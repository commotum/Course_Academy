EXPLANATION

The following triangle illustrates the information given.

The Law of Cosines states that y2=x2+z2−2xzcos⁡Y, Here, we have m∠Y=139∘,x=3,z=4.

Substituting the numbers into the formula gives y2=32+42−2(3)(4)cos⁡139∘=9+16−24cos⁡139∘=25−24(−0.75470…)=25+18.11303=43.11303 rounded to 5 decimal places.

Taking the square root gives y=43.11303=6.57 rounded to two decimal places.
