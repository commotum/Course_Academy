EXPLANATION

Since we have an overall common factor of 3, we start by dividing our equation through by 3. This gives

t3−5t2−3t+15=0.

Next, we factor by grouping. Notice that when grouping the smaller terms, we factor out the negative sign.

t3−5t2−3t+15=(t3−5t2)−(3t−15)=t2(t−5)−3(t−5)=(t−5)⋅t2−(t−5)⋅3=(t−5)(t2−3).

Finally, we set each factor equal to 0 and solve:

  • Setting the factor (t−5) equal to 0, we get t−5=0t=5

  • Setting the factor (t2−3) equal to 0, we get t2−3=0t2=3t=±3

Therefore, the solutions to the given equation are 5 and ±3.
