To divide powers of the same base, we simply subtract the exponents: 2−222=2−2−2=2−4

To evaluate 2−4, we find the reciprocal of the base (2) and then raise that reciprocal to the same power but with the opposite sign: 2−4=(12)4=12×12×12×12=116
