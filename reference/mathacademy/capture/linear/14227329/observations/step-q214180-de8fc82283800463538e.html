Find the formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence if the first term is −2 and the second term is 3.
f(n)=
5n−7
,n≥1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence (in function notation) is

f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d.

We are given the first term f(1)=−2. Also, the common difference of the sequence can be found as d=3−(−2)=5.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify: f(n)=f(1)+(n−1)d=−2+(n−1)(5)=−2+5n−5=5n−7
