EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence is

an=a1+(n−1)d.

First, we will write the formula for the nth term of this sequence. From the first few terms

13,8,3,−2,…

we see that the first term is a1=13 and the common difference is

d=8−13=−5.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

an=a1+(n−1)d=13+(n−1)(−5)=13−5n+5=18−5n

Now, we can use our formula to find the 33rd term. All we have to do is substitute n=33:

an=18−5na33=18−5(33)=18−165=−147

Therefore, the 33rd term of the sequence is a33=−147.
