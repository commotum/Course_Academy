Explanation

We know that Darryl has 43 of an apple pie.

To calculate the fraction of a whole pie that Darryl's daughter has, we need to multiply 25 by 43.

To multiply two fractions, we multiply the numerators and we multiply the denominators:

25×43=2×45×3=815.

Therefore, Darry's daughter have 815 of a pie.
