The quadratic coefficient of a particular quadratic equation equals 1. The sum of the solutions equals 4, and the product of the solutions equals −45. Find the equation.
The equation is
x2−4x−45
=0.

EXPLANATION

Suppose the quadratic equation ax2+bx+c=0 has the solutions x=x1 and x=x2, where a≠0.

Then, Vieta's formulas state the following:

  • The sum of the solutions is given by x1+x2=−ba.

  • The product of the solutions is given by x1⋅x2=ca.

Now, let's use the given information to determine the values of a,b, and c in this case:

  • We're told that the quadratic coefficient equals 1. Therefore, we have a=1.

  • We're told that the sum of the roots equals 4. Therefore, we have 4=−ba4=−b1−4=bb=−4.

  • We're told that the product of the roots equals −45. Therefore, we have −45=ca−45=c1c=−45.

Therefore, our equation has coefficients a=1,b=−4,c=−45 and the equation is x2−4x−45=0.
