Find dfdx when f(x)=(1−7x)4.
dfdx=
−28(1−7x)3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we express f(x) as a composite function: f=g4,g=1−7x

The chain rule states that dfdx=dfdg⋅dgdx.

Differentiating, we have dfdg=4g3, and dgdx=−7.

Finally, multiplying the two together and substituting for g gives dfdx=4g3⋅(−7)=−28(1−7x)3.
