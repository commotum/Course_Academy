Consider the sequence starting from a1=9 with the property an+1an=13 for all n≥1. Write the sum to infinity S of the terms of this series using sigma notation in terms of index n.
S=∑n=1∞
9(13​)n−1

Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that the sequence is a geometric sequence since the ratio of any two successive terms is constant.

The first term of the sequence is a1=9, and the common ratio is r=an+1an=13.

Therefore, the general term an can be expressed as

an=a1(r)n−1=9(13)n−1=9(13)−1(13)n=27(13)n.

Finally, the infinite geometric series S can be written in sigma notation as S=∑n=1∞an=∑n=1∞27(13)n.
