A triangle △ABC is rotated by 180∘ clockwise around the point A and then translated by 3 units downward. If the resulting triangle is △PQR, which of the following statements must be true?

  1. △ABC∼△PQR
  2. △ABC≅△PQR
  3. ∠B≅∠Q
a
I and III only
b
I and II only
c
II and III only
d
I, II, and III
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A similarity transformation is any transformation made up of rigid motions (rotations, reflections, and translations) and dilations.

A similarity transformation will map any polygon to a similar polygon.

With that in mind, let's examine each of the statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. We are given a combination of a rotation and a translation. As a result, we obtain that △ABC∼△PQR.

  • Statement II is true. Since our combined transformation consists of rigid motions only (namely, rotation and translation), the distance between all points is preserved under the transformation. Therefore, we obtain that ABC≅PQR.

  • Statement III is true. Since △ABC∼△PQR, all corresponding angles of the triangles must be congruent. In particular, ∠B≅∠Q.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
