The number of machines infected by a computer virus is represented by the function f(t)=4t3−2t2+5t+11, where t is the time, in days, since the virus was first detected. What is the average rate of growth of f(t) over the first week?

a
18 machines
b
78 machines per day
c
1309 machines per day
d
187 machines per day
e
178 machines per day
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let f(t)=4t3−2t2+5t+11 be the number of machines infected by the computer virus after t days. To find the average rate of growth of f(t) over the first week, we use the average rate of change formula on the interval [0,7]:

ΔfΔt=f(b)−f(a)b−a=f(7)−f(0)7−0=[4(7)3−2(7)2+5(7)+11]−[4(0)3−2(0)2+5(0)+11]7=13097=187

Therefore, the average rate of growth over the first week is 187 machines per day.
