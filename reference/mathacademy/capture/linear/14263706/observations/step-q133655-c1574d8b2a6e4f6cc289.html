Which of the following expressions are rational?

  1. 89+98
  2. 510+1200
  3. 28+3
a
II only
b
I and II only
c
II and III only
d
I and III only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that:

  • the sum of two rational numbers is always rational: rational+rational=rational

  • the sum of a rational number and an irrational number is always irrational: rational+irrational=irrational

  • the sum of two irrational numbers is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

With that in mind, let's examine the statements.

  • Expression I is rational. Since both 89 and 98 are rational, their sum must be rational.

  • Expression II is rational. Since both 510 and 1200 are rational, their sum must be rational.

  • Expression III is not rational. Since 28 is rational and 3 is irrational, their sum must be irrational.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
