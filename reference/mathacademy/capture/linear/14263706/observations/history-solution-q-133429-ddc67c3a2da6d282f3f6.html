Recall that:

  • the sum of two rational numbers is always rational: rational+rational=rational

  • the sum of a rational number and an irrational number is always irrational: rational+irrational=irrational

  • the sum of two irrational numbers is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

With that in mind, let's examine the statements.

  • Statement I is true.

  • Statement II is false. For example, if a=−5, then 5+a=5−5=0, which is rational.

  • Statement III is true. From part II, we know that 5+a is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational. Since addition is commutative, we conclude that a+5 is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
