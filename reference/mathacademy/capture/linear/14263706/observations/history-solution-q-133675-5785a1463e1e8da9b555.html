The sum (or difference) of two irrational numbers is sometimes rational and sometimes irrational.

For example:

  • 2+2=2.828427125… is irrational, yet

  • 2+(−2)=0 is rational.

With that in mind, let's examine the statements.

  • Statement I is false.

  • Statement II is false, while statement III is true.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
