Order the angles of the triangle △XYZ from smallest to largest.

a
∠X,∠Y,∠Z
b
∠Z,∠X,∠Y
c
∠Y,∠Z,∠X
d
∠X,∠Z,∠Y
e
∠Y,∠X,∠Z
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The longest side is XZ―, which means that the opposite angle, ∠Y, must be the largest one.

The shortest side is YZ―, so the smallest interior angle is ∠X.

Therefore, the required order must be

∠X,∠Z,∠Y.
