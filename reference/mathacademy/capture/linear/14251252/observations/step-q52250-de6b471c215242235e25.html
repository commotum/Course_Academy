Factor the polynomial expression x(w−6)−10(w−6).

a
10(w−6)
b
(x−6)(w−10)
c
(x−10)(w−6)
d
x(w−6)
e
10x(w−6)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we find the GCF of the two terms:

GCF(x(w−6),10(w−6))=w−6

Each term is already written as a product with the GCF:

x⋅(w−6)−10⋅(w−6)

Finally, we write the GCF outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

x⋅(w−6)−10⋅(w−6)=(x−10)(w−6)
