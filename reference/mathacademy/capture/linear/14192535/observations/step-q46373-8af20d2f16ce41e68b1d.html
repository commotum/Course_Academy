Correct
EXPLANATION

We will determine each branch separately.

  • On the interval (−∞,−2), we have f(x)=3. Notice that the point x=−2 is excluded.

  • On the interval [−2,1), we have f(x)=−2. Notice that the point x=−2 is included and the point x=1 is excluded.

  • On the interval (1,∞), we have f(x)=2. Notice that the point x=1 is included.

Hence the definition of f(x) is

f(x)={3,x<−2−2,−2≤x<12,x≥1
