Correct
EXPLANATION

We will determine each branch separately.

  • On the interval (−∞,1], we have f(x)=0. Notice that the point x=1 is included.

  • On the interval (1,∞), we have f(x)=1. Notice that the point x=1 is excluded.

Hence the definition of f(x) is

f(x)={0,x≤11,x>1
