The point Q with coordinates (−2,1) lies on the curve y=f(x) and the point R with coordinates (−2,−1) lies on the curve y=g(x). If the curve y=g(x) is related to y=f(x) via the transformation g(x)=f(x)+k, then what is the value of k?

a
1
b
3
c
2
d
−2
e
−1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that the point R(−2,−1) lies 2 units below the point Q(−2,1). So, the graph of y=g(x) can be obtained from the graph of y=f(x) by shifting it 2 units down.

As a result, k=−2.
