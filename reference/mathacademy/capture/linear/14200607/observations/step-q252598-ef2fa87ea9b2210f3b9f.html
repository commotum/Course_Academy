If the equation log25⁡5=12 is equivalent to b=5, then b=
25
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The logarithm of a with base b is defined as follows:

logb⁡a=x is equivalent to a=bx

If we compare log25⁡5=12 with logb⁡a=x, we get a=5, b=25, and x=12.

Therefore, the given equation is equivalent to 251/2=5, which is also equivalent to 25=5. Consequently, b=25.
