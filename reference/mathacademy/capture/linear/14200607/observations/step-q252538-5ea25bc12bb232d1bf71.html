EXPLANATION

The logarithm of a with base b is defined as follows:

logb⁡a=x is equivalent to a=bx

If we compare log2⁡(128)=7 with logb⁡a=x, we get a=128, b=2, and x=7.

Therefore, the given equation is equivalent to 27=128. Consequently, b=2.
