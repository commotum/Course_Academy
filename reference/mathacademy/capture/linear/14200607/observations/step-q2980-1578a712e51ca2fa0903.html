EXPLANATION

The logarithm of a with base b is defined as follows:

logb⁡a=x is equivalent to a=bx

If we compare log5⁡25=2 with logb⁡a=x, we get a=25, b=5, and x=2.

Therefore, the given equation is equivalent to 52=25. Consequently, x=2.
