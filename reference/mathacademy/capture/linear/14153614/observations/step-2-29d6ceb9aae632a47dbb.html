Expressed as a polynomial in terms of x, the length of Dan's plot of land is
2x
yards.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

The width of the plot is x+2 yards, and its area is 2x2+4x square yards. Since the area=length×width, we factor the expression for the area to find the length.

First, we find the GCF of the two terms:

GCF(2x2,4x)=2x

Then, we rewrite each term in the original expression as a product with the GCF:

2x2+4x=(2x⋅x)+(2x⋅2)

Next, we write the GCF outside the parentheses and keep the remaining terms inside:

(2x⋅x)+(2x⋅2)=2x(x+2)

As a result, we conclude that the length of the plot must be 2x yards.
