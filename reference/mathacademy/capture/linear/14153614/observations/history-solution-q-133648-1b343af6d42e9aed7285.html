The land initially costs 160 thousand, which increases at a rate of 10% per year. 10% as a decimal is 0.1. Therefore, an increase of 10% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of 1+0.1=1.1. Therefore, we have: * After 1 year, the value will be 160⋅(1.1) thousand dollars * After 2 years, the value will be 160⋅(1.1)2 thousand dollars * After 3 years, the value will be 160⋅(1.1)3 thousand dollars * After 4 years, the value will be 160⋅(1.1)4 thousand dollars * After 5 years, the value will be 160⋅(1.1)5 thousand dollars Evaluating the expression for the value after 5 years, we get the following: 160⋅(1.1)5=257.68≈258 rounded to the nearest integer. Therefore, after 5 years, the land will be worth approximately 258 thousand dollars.
