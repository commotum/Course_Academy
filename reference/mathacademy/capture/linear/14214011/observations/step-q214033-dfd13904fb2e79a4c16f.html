First, we write the equation x+3y=12 in its slope-intercept form, as follows:

x+3y=123y=−x+12y=−13x+4

Since perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other, the slope of the new line should be the negative reciprocal of the slope of the given line y=−13x+4. The given line has a slope of −13, so our perpendicular line must have a slope of 3.

Our perpendicular line must have the slope m=3 and pass through the point (x1,y1)=(1,−5). Using point-slope form and simplifying, we obtain the equation of the line in slope-intercept form:

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−(−5)=3(x−1)y+5=3x−3y=3x−8
