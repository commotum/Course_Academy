Which of the following numbers are rational?

  1. 1.353124…
  2. 1.521―
  3. 4.055

I and II only
b

EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of our numbers in turn.

  • 1.353124… is not a rational number. The notation "…" tells us that digits after the decimal point extend forever. However, there are no repeating digits.

  • 1.521― is a rational number. The notation 21― tells us that the digits 2 and 1 after the decimal point repeat indefinitely. 1.521―=1.52121212121… Any decimal with repeating digits can be represented as a ratio of two integers. In this case, 1.521―=1506990.

  • 4.055 is a rational number because it can be expressed as a ratio of two integers: 4.055=40551000

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
