A social media influencer had 100 followers the day she created her account. Since then, the number F(t) of followers after t weeks can be modeled by the function

F(t)=100⋅3t.

How many weeks will it take for the followers to increase to 72900?

a
5
b
6
c
3
d
−6
e
7
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the time it takes for the number of followers to increase to 72900, we need to solve the equation F(t)=72900 for t, as follows:

F(t)=72900100⋅3t=729003t=729001003t=7293t=36t=6

Therefore, the number of followers increases to 72900 after 6 weeks.
