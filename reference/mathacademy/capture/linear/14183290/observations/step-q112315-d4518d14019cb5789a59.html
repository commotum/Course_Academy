In a certain country, the number of people P(t) who know a second language, t years after 2005, is given by

P(t)=P0⋅3t,

where P0 was the number of people who knew a second language in 2005. If in 2010 the number of people who knew a second language was 4860000, how many people knew a second language in 2005?

a
2000
b
30000
c
200000
d
3000
e
20000
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The number of people who knew a second language in 2010 was 4860000, this is 5 years after 2005. Thus, P(5)=4860000.

To find P0, the number of people who knew a second lenguage in 2005, we substitute t=5 and P(5)=4860000 into the given equation and solve for P0, as follows:

P(5)=P0⋅354860000=P0⋅35P0=4860000243P0=20000

Therefore, in 2005 there were 20000 people who knew a second language in that country.
