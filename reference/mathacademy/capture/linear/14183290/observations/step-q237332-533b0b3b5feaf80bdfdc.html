EXPLANATION

The amoeba population y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 65%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

From the given information, we have the following:

  • a=140 is the initial number of amoebas.

  • r=0.65 is the growth rate.

  • t is the time, in days, since the beginning of the observation.

So, the expression for y(t) is

y(t)=140⋅(1+0.65)t=140⋅(1.65)t.

Therefore, to find the time it will take for the amoeba population to increase to 800, we would need to solve the equation y(t)=800140⋅(1.65)t=800.
