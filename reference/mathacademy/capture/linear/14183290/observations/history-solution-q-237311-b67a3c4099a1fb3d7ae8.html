We know that the duck population triples every year. Thus, the duck population, which we will call y(t), grows exponentially with a growth factor of 3.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth factor b) is

y=abt.

From the given information, we have the following:

  • a=8 is the initial population of ducks.

  • b=3 is the growth factor.

  • t is the time, in years, since the beginning of the observation.

Therefore, the expression for y(t) is

y(t)=8⋅3t.

Now, to find the time it takes for the population of ducks to increase to 5832, we need to solve the equation y(t)=5832 for t, as follows:

y(t)=58328⋅3t=58323t=583283t=7293t=36t=6

Therefore, the population of ducks increases to 5832 after 6 years.
