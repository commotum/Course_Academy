A fungi culture increases by 30% each week. If the culture started with 5 fungi, what equation should we solve to determine how many weeks will it take for the fungi culture to increase to 100?

a
5⋅(1.3)t=100
b
5⋅(0.3)t=100
c
(5⋅1.3)t=100
d
5⋅(1.03)t=100
e
5⋅3t=100
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The fungi culture y(t) grows exponentially with a growth rate of 30%.

The formula for exponential growth (in terms of a growth rate r) is

y=a(1+r)t.

From the given information, we have the following:

  • a=5 is the initial number of fungi.

  • r=0.3 is the growth rate.

  • t is the time, in weeks, since the culture started.

So, the expression for y(t) is

y(t)=5⋅(1+0.3)t=5⋅(1.3)t.

Therefore, to find the number of weeks it takes for the fungi culture to increase to 100, we would need to solve the equation

y(t)=1005⋅(1.3)t=100.
