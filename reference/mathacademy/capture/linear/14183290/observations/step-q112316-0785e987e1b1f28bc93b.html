The profits of a store P(t), t years after 2018, is given by

P(t)=P0⋅(1.1)t,

where P0 was the profit in 2018. If the profits in 2020 were of $48400, what was the profit in 2018?

a
$1000
b
$40000
c
$10000
d
$4000
e
$11000
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The store profits in 2020 were of $48400, this is 2 years after 2018. Thus, P(2)=48400.

To find the profit P0 in 2018, we substitute t=2 and P(2)=48400 into the given equation and solve for P0, as follows:

P(2)=P0⋅(1.1)248400=P0⋅(1.1)2P0=484001.21P0=40000

Therefore, the profits in 2018 were of $40000.
