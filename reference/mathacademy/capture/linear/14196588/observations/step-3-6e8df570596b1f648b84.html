Explanation

Notice that the points A,C, and D lie on the circle. We know that the radius of the circle is r=OC.

We first notice that

AD=AO+OD.

Now, AO― and OD― are both segments between the center of the circle and the circle itself. So, they are both radii of the circle, and because AD=8, we have

AD=8AO+OD=8r+r=82r=8r=4.

Finally, we obtain

OC=r=4.
