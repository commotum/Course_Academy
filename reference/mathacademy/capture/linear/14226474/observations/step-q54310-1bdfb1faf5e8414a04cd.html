EXPLANATION

In this case, the area of the right triangle is A=12AB⋅AC.

We are given that tan⁡θ=45, where θ=m∠B. Notice we're also given the length of the side AB― adjacent to ∠B. Hence, we can use the tangent ratio to find the length of the opposite side AC―:

tan⁡θ=ACAB45=AC10AC=45⋅10AC=8

Therefore, the area of △ABC is

A=12AB⋅AC=12⋅10⋅8=40.
