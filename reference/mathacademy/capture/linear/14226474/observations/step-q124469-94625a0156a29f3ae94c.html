In this case, the area of the right triangle is A=12BC⋅AB.

We are given that m∠A=30∘. Notice that relative to ∠A, we have expressions for the lengths of the opposite side BC― and adjacent side AB―. Hence, we can use the tangent ratio to solve for x:

tan⁡(m∠A)=BCABtan⁡(30∘)=2x3x+333=2x3x+33(3x+3)=3⋅2x3x+33=6x33=3xx=3

So, the side lengths are

BC=2x=23,AB=3x+3=3(3)+3=6.

Therefore, the area of △ABC is

A=12⋅BC⋅AB=12⋅23⋅6=63.
