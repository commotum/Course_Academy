The dimensions of a stamp are 2.1cm×2.4cm, where each dimension is measured to the nearest 0.1cm. Rounded to the nearest tenth of a square centimeter, what is the upper bound for the area of the stamp?
To the nearest tenth of a square centimeter, the upper bound is
5.3
cm2.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Each dimension is given to the nearest 0.1cm. So, the greatest possible error of these measurements is 0.1cm2=0.05cm.

We wish to find the upper bound for the area of the stamp. Therefore, we require the upper bound of each dimension.

The upper bounds for each dimension are as follows: 2.1cm+0.05cm=2.15cm2.4cm+0.05cm=2.45cm

So, the upper bound for the area of the stamp is A=2.15cm×2.45cm=5.2675cm2≈5.3cm2, rounded to the nearest tenth of a square centimeter.
