Explanation

The function f′(x) models the rate of change of the value of the profits, applicable in the domain of f(x).

Thus, the rate of change of the value of the profit at x=4 months is

f′(4)=234=23⋅2=13.

Therefore, the rate of change in the profits was 13 thousand dollars per month.
