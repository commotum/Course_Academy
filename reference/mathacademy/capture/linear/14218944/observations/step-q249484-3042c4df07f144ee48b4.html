EXPLANATION

Since f(x)=f(17⋅(7x)), the function y=f(x) represents a stretch of the function y=f(7x) by a factor of 7 parallel to the x-axis. This has the effect of multiplying the x-coordinates of all points on y=f(7x) by 7.

Therefore, the point (2,−5) on f(7x) is mapped to the point (7⋅2,−5)=(14,−5), and this point must lie on the graph of y=f(x).
