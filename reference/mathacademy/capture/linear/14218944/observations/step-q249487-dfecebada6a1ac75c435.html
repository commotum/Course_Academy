The point P with coordinates (−15,6) lies on the curve y=f(13x). Find the point Q that must lie on y=f(x).
The coordinates of Q are (
−5
,
6
).

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since f(x)=f(3⋅(13x)), the function y=f(x) represents a compression of the function y=f(13x) by a factor of 3 parallel to the x-axis. This has the effect of dividing the x-coordinates of all points on y=f(13x) by 3.

Therefore, the point (−15,6) on f(13x) is mapped to the point (−153,6)=(−5,6), and this point must lie on the graph of y=f(x).
