The function y=f(14x) represents a stretch of the function y=f(x) by a factor of 4 parallel to the x-axis. This has the effect of multiplying the x-coordinates of all points on y=f(x) by 4.

Therefore, the point (5,3) on f(x) is mapped to the point (4⋅5,3)=(20,3), and this point must lie on the graph of y=f(14x)
