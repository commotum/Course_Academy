The point P with coordinates (18,−12) lies on the curve y=f(16x). Which of the following points must lie on y=f(x)?

a
(108,−12)
b
(3,−72)
c
(108,−2)
d
(3,−12)
e
(3,−2)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Since f(x)=f(6⋅(16x)), the function y=f(x) represents a compression of the function y=f(16x) by a factor of 6 parallel to the x-axis. This has the effect of dividing the x-coordinates of all points on y=f(16x) by 6.

Therefore, the point (18,−12) on f(16x) is mapped to the point (186,−12)=(3,−12), and this point must lie on the graph of y=f(x).
