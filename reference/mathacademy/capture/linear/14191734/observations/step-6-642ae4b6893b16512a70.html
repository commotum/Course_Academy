In preparation for his entire week's work, the painter purchased 856 liters of thinner. Of this amount, he used 723 liters. How much thinner did he have left? Express your answer as a mixed fraction.

Explanation

The painter had (856−723) liters of thinner left over.

Writing 856 as an improper fraction, we have 856=8×6+56=536.

Writing 723 as an improper fraction, we have 723=7×3+23=233.

Therefore, our problem now is to work out the following: 536−233

To subtract two fractions with unlike denominators, we need to express each fraction as an equivalent fraction with a common denominator.

In this question, we can make a common denominator of 6.

To put 233 over a denominator of 6, we multiply the numerator and denominator by 2: 233=23×23×2=466

We can now subtract the fractions. We keep the denominator the same, and we subtract the numerators: 536−466=76

Finally, we convert back to a mixed number: 76=1R1=116

Therefore, the painter had 116 liters of thinner left over.
