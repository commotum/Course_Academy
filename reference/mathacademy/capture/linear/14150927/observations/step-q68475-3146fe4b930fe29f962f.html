An airplane flies over the Pacific Ocean at 850 meters above sea level. At the same time, a submarine is in the ocean at 125 meters below sea level. What is the difference in the altitude of the submarine compared to the airplane?

a
725m
b
925m
c
760m
d
975m
e
845m
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine how far below the submarine is compared to the airplane, we must calculate the difference between the location of the submarine and the location of the plane. This gives

850−(−125)=850+125=975.

Thus, the submarine is 975m below the airplane.
