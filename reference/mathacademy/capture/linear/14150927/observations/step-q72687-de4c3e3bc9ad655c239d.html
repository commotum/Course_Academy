The average temperature in the Arctic in December of a particular year was 3.2∘C. Due to plummeting temperatures in the winter, the average temperature in February of the next year decreased by 6.9∘C compared to the average temperature in December. What was the average temperature in the Arctic in February?

a
−3.5∘C
b
−3.7∘C
c
−2.9∘C
d
−4.2∘C
e
−3.2∘C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine the average temperature of the Arctic in February, we subtract the change in temperature (from December to February) from the average temperature in December. This gives 3.2∘C−6.9∘C=−3.7∘C.

Consequently, the average temperature in the Arctic at the end of February was −3.7∘C.
