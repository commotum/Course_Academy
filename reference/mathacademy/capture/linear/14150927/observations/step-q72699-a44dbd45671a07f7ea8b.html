In Oslo, Norway, the average temperature in November is 3.3∘C. In January, the average temperature decreases by 6.2∘C compared to November. Finally, in March, the average temperature increases by 4.6∘C compared to January. Determine the average temperature in Oslo in March.

a
2.1∘C
b
1.9∘C
c
2.3∘C
d
1.7∘C
e
0.9∘C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we must determine the average temperature in Oslo during the month of January. Since we know that the temperature decreases on average by 6.2∘C compared to November, we subtract the variation in temperature from the average temperature in November. This gives 3.3∘C−6.2∘C=−2.9∘C.

Now, we must determine the temperature during the month of March. Since we know that the temperature increases on average by 4.6∘C compared to January, we add the variation in temperature to the average temperature in January. This gives −2.9∘C+4.6∘C=1.7∘C.

Therefore, the average temperature in Oslo in March is 1.7∘C.
