Angela has a bank account with ASB Bank, and she has an overdraft facility authorized by this bank. At the start of a certain week, her balance was $75.75. At the end of that week, she decided to withdraw $80 to buy a sound system. What was Angela's balance at the end of that week?

a
−$5.25
b
−$3.25
c
−$3.50
d
−$4.75
e
−$4.25
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find Angela's bank balance at the end of that week, we subtract the amount of money she withdrew from the starting balance. This gives $75.75−$80=−$4.25.

Therefore, Angela's bank balance at the end of the week was −$4.25.
