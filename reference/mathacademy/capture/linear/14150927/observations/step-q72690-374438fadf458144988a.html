Ffion has an authorized overdraft facility with her bank. Her bank balance on Monday was −$43.74. To reduce her debt, Ffion decides to make a deposit of $29.50 into her bank account on the following Wednesday. What was Ffion's balance at the end of the day on Wednesday?

a
−$14.24
b
−$15.94
c
−$12.74
d
−$11.14
e
−$13.54
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To determine the balance at the end of the day on Wedensday, we must add the amount of money Ffion deposited to the starting balance. This gives −$43.74+$29.50=−$14.24

Thus, Ffion's balance at the end of the day on Wednesday was −$14.24.
