On a certain day in December 2019, the temperature in Toronto (Ontario) was −7.4∘C. That same day, the temperature in Seattle (Washington) was 3.6∘C. How many degrees higher was Seattle's temperature compared to the temperature in Toronto?

a
11.2∘C
b
10.5∘C
c
11.0∘C
d
10.8∘C
e
10.2∘C
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find how many degrees higher Seattle's temperature was compared to the temperature in Toronto, we must calculate the difference between the temperatures of Seattle and Toronto. This gives 3.6∘C−(−7.4∘C)=3.6∘C+7.4∘C=11.0∘C.
