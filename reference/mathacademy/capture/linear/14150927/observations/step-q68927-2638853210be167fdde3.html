As a part of a scientific experiment, Santiago placed a liquid, whose initial temperature was 15.3∘C, into a refrigerator. After an hour, he removed the liquid from the refrigerator and placed it on a table. He observed that its temperature decreased by 17.1∘C.

After 15 minutes, Santiago measured the temperature of the liquid again and concluded that it increased by 6∘C. What was the final temperature of the liquid?

a
5.1∘C
b
3.7∘C
c
2.8∘C
d
3.3∘C
e
4.2∘C
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we must determine the temperature of the liquid when removing it from the refrigerator. Since its temperature decreased by 17.1∘C compared to its initial temperature, we must subtract the variation in temperature from the initial temperature. This gives 15.3∘C−17.1∘C=−1.8∘C.

Now, we must determine the temperature of the liquid after the 15 minutes outside the refrigerator. Since the temperature of the liquid increased by 6∘C compared to the temperature it had when it was removed from the refrigerator, we add the variation in temperature to the temperature of the liquid when it was removed from the refrigerator. This gives −1.8∘C+6∘C=4.2∘C.

Therefore, the final temperature of the liquid was 4.2∘C.
