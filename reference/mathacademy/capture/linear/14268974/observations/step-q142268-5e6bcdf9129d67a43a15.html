Given the graph of y=f(x), we apply the following transformations in the order given:

  1. Stretch it by a scale factor of 13 parallel to the x-axis

  2. Stretch it by a scale factor of 4 parallel to the y-axis

  3. Translate it to the right by 5 units

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

a
y=f(12x+15)
b
y=4f(13x−5)
c
y=4f(3x−5)
d
y=4f(3x−15)
e
y=4f(13x−53)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

  1. Stretching the graph of y=f(x) by a scale factor of 13 parallel to the x-axis is equivalent to replacing every instance of x with 3x. Applying this transformation, we obtain y=f(3x).

  2. Stretching the graph of y=f(3x) by a scale factor of 4 parallel to the y-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by 4. Applying this transformation, we obtain y=4(f(3x))=4f(3x).

  3. Translating the graph of y=4f(3x) by 5 units to the right is equivalent to replacing every instance of x by (x−5). Applying this transformation, we obtain y=4f(3(x−5))=4f(3x−15).

Therefore, the final function expression is y=4f(3x−15).
