Given the graph of y=f(x), we apply the following transformations in the order given:

  1. Translate it down by 2 units

  2. Translate it to the right by 1 unit

  3. Stretch it by a scale factor of 5 parallel to the y-axis

What is the final function expression after applying all the transformations?

a
y=f(5x+1)−10
b
y=f(x+5)+10
c
y=5f(x−1)−10
d
y=5f(x+1)−2
e
y=5f(x−1)+2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the expression, let's apply each of the transformations in turn.

  1. Translating the graph of y=f(x) down by 2 units is equivalent to subtracting 2 from the function expression. Applying this transformation, we obtain y=f(x)−2.

  2. Translating the graph of y=f(x)−2 by 1 unit to the right is equivalent to replacing every instance of x with (x−1). Applying this transformation, we obtain y=f(x−1)−2.

  3. Stretching the graph of y=f(x−1)−2 by a scale factor of 5 parallel to the y-axis is equivalent to multiplying the function expression by 5. Applying this transformation, we obtain y=5(f(x−1)−2)=5f(x−1)−10.

Therefore, the final function expression is y=5f(x−1)−10.
