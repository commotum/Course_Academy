The point Q with coordinates (2,−4) lies on the curve y=f(x). Which of the following points must lie on y=f(−x)?

a
(−2,4)
b
(−4,2)
c
(2,4)
d
(−4,−2)
e
(−2,−4)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The transformation y=f(−x) reflects the curve y=f(x) in the y-axis.

First, notice that the point P(2,−4) lies 2 units to the right from the y-axis. So, under the action of the transformation, this point will be reflected 2 units to the left from the y-axis.

We conclude that the point (−2,−4) must lie on the graph of y=f(−x).
