The transformation y=f(−x) reflects the curve y=f(x) in the y-axis.

First, notice that the point P(4,1) lies 4 units to the right from the y-axis. So, under the action of the transformation, this point will be reflected 4 units to the left from the y-axis.

We conclude that the point (−4,1) must lie on the graph of y=f(−x).
