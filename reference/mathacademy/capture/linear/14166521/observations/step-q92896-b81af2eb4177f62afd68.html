John bought 25 candies for $3. What is the price per candy?

a
$0.12
b
$0.09
c
$0.20
d
$0.15
e
$0.25
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The ratio of dollars to candies is 3:25. Expressing this as a fraction, we have:

325

We can convert this fraction to a decimal by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 4:

3×425×4=12100=0.12

Consequently, the cost is $0.12 per candy.
