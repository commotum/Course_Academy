The parabola y=x2+x and the line y=3x−1 intersect at a single point with coordinates (p,q). What is p+q?

EXPLANATION

We need to solve the following system consisting of the equations of the curves: {y=x2+xy=3x−1

Setting the equations equal to one another and solving, we get

x2+x=3x−1x2+x−3x+1=0x2−2x+1=0(x−1)(x−1)=0x=1.

So, the x-coordinate of the intersection point is x=1.

To find the y-coordinate of the intersection point, we plug this value into either curve. Let's choose the equation of the line. Substituting x=1, we get y=3x−1=3(1)−1=2.

So, the only point of intersection is (1,2). Therefore, p=1,q=2, and

p+q=1+2=3.
