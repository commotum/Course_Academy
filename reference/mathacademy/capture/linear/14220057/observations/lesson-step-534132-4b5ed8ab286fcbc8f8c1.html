Example: Identifying Radical Equations With No Real Solution
?

Which of the following equations have no real solution?

EXPLANATION

Remember that a square root is always positive or zero. So, a radical equation has no real solution if it states that a square root is equal to a negative number.

Of the given equations, only I and III state that a square root is equal to a negative number. Therefore, equations I and III have no real solution.

So, the correct answer is "I and III only".
