Question 3

The graph of $y=5f(3x-6)+2,$ can be obtained from the graph of $y=f(x)$ by applying some of the following transformations:

  • $(\textrm{T}1)$ - Translate right by $6$ units

  • $(\textrm{T}2)$ - Stretch by a scale factor of $\dfrac13$ parallel to the $x$-axis

  • $(\textrm{T}3)$ - Translate up by $2$ units

  • $(\textrm{T}4)$ - Stretch by a scale factor of $5$ parallel to the $y$-axis

  • $(\textrm{T}5)$ - Translate right by $2$ units

Which of the following could be a possible ordering of the appropriate transformations?

a
 $(\textrm{T}2) \to (\textrm{T}5) \to (\textrm{T}4) \to (\textrm{T}3)$
b
 $(\textrm{T}2) \to (\textrm{T}3) \to (\textrm{T}4) \to (\textrm{T}5)$
c
 $(\textrm{T}1) \to (\textrm{T}2) \to (\textrm{T}4) \to (\textrm{T}5)$
d
 $(\textrm{T}1) \to (\textrm{T}2) \to (\textrm{T}4) \to (\textrm{T}3)$
e
 $(\textrm{T}2) \to (\textrm{T}1) \to (\textrm{T}4) \to (\textrm{T}3)$
