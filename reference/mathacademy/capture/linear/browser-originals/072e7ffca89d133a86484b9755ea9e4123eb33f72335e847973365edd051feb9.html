Question 1

The population of an ant colony grew exponentially, and within $4$ months, the population was $1\,000 \cdot (1.5)^4.$ What does the expression $(1.5)^4$ mean in this context?

a
 The ant population grew by a factor of $(0.5)$ every month
b
 The ant population grew by a factor of $(1.5)$ over the first $4$ months
c
 The ant population grew by a factor of $(0.5)^4$ over the first $4$ months
d
 The ant population grew by a factor of $(1.5)^4$ over the first $4$ months
e
 The ant population grew by a factor of $(1.5)^4$ every month
