The card's value is $50, and this number increases by 8% each year.

8% as a decimal is 0.08. Therefore, an increase of 8% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of 1+0.08=1.08.

Therefore, we have:

  • After 1 year, the value of the card is 50⋅(1.08) dollars.

  • After 2 years, the value of the card is 50⋅(1.08)2 dollars.

  • After 3 years, the value of the card is 50⋅(1.08)3 dollars.

    ⋮

  • After 10 years, the value of the card is 50⋅(1.08)10 dollars.
