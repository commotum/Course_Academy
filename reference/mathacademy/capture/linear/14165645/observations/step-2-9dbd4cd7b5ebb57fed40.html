Peter, Steve, Chelsea, and Laura each bought 3 ounces of gummies and 4 ounces of chocolates. In total, they bought
28
ounces of candy.

Explanation

We must write and evaluate an expression representing the total amount of candy.

Since each of them bought 3 ounces of gummies and 4 ounces of chocolates, the amount of candy each person bought, in ounces, is: 3⏟gummies+4⏟chocolates

Since each person purchased this amount of candy, and there are 4 people, we must multiply the amount of candy by 4. Therefore, the total amount of candy is: (3+4)⏟each person×4⏟people

To evaluate the expression, we follow the order of operations with parentheses.

First, we perform the operations inside the parentheses: (3+4)×4=(3+4)×4=7×4.

Now, we multiply:

7×4=28
