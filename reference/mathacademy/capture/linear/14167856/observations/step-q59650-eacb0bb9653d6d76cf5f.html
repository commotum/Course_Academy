What are the slope and y-intercept of the line y=−2x+3?

a
The slope is −2 and the y-intercept is 3.
b
The slope is 3 and the y-intercept is −2.
c
The slope is 2 and the y-intercept is 3.
d
The slope is −2 and the y-intercept is −3.
e
The slope is 3 and the y-intercept is 2.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The equation is in slope-intercept form y=mx+b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.

For the equation y=−2x+3, we see that the slope is m=−2 and the y-intercept is b=3.
