Determine the equation of the line shown above if its slope is 12.

a
y=12x
b
y=12x+3
c
y=x−3
d
y=12x+4
e
y=12x−3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We will write the equation of the line in the slope-intercept form y=mx+b. We know that the slope is m=12, so we just need to work out y-intercept b.

From the graph, we see that the line passes through the point (2,4). Substituting this into y=mx+b with m=12 and solving for b, we get

y=12x+b4=12(2)+b4=1+bb=3.

Therefore, the equation of the line is y=12x+3.
