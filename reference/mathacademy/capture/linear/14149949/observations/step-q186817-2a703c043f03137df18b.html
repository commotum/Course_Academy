EXPLANATION

We put both fractions over a common denominator of 15 and then add the numerators. This gives −715+35=−715+915=−7+915.

To compute the numerator, we can use either Method 1 or Method 2, shown below.

Method 1 - Picturing a Number Line

To add 9 to −7, we start at −7 on a number line and move 9 spaces to the right.

So, −7+9=2. Therefore, −7+915=215.

Method 2 - Subtraction in Reverse

We can swap the order of the numbers in the numerator, and we get −7+915=9−715=215.

So, we conclude that −715+35=215.
