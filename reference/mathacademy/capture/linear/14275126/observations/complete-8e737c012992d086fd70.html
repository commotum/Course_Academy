The displacement s, in meters, relative to a fixed origin O of a particle moving in a straight line along the x-axis is given by s(t)=3t2−9t+1, where t>0 is the time, measured in seconds. Find the time intervals in which the particle is moving in the direction of increasing x.

a
(3,∞)
b
(32,∞)
c
(0,32)
d
(2,∞)
e
(0,2)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the particle moves in the direction of increasing x, then its velocity is positive.

First, we calculate the velocity function by differentiating the position function:

v(t)=dsdt=ddt(3t2−9t+1)=6t−9

To calculate time intervals in which the particle is moving in the direction of increasing x, we solve v(t)>0: v(t)>06t−9>06t>9t>32

Hence, the time interval in which the particle is moving to the direction of increasing x is t∈(32,∞).
