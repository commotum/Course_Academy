Consider the picture above. Given that h=λf+μg, find λ and μ.

a
λ=−2 and μ=−1
b
λ=2 and μ=−2
c
λ=−2 and μ=12
d
λ=−12 and μ=−2
e
λ=12 and μ=12

Correct
EXPLANATION

In △OFH shown above, we have

  • OF=12|f|
  • OF→ has the opposite direction to f

Hence, OF→=−12f.

Similarly:

  • FH=2|g|
  • FH→ has the opposite direction to g

Hence, FH→=−2g.

Additionally,

h=OF→+FH→=−12f−2g.

Consequently, h=λf+μg=−12f−2g.

Since f∦g, the linear combination can only be written in one way. Equating the coefficients, we get:

  • λ=−12 and μ=−2
