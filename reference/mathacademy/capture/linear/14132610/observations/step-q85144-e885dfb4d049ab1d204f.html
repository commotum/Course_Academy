EXPLANATION

A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.

For the number 173◻32338 the sum of the digits is

1+7+3+◻+3+2+3+3+8=30+◻.

Now, let's consider each of the given digits that could be inserted into the blank.

  • If we insert 4 into the blank, then the sum of the digits is 30+4=34, which is not divisible by 9.

  • If we insert 6 into the blank, then the sum of the digits is 30+6=36, which is divisible by 9.

  • If we insert 9 into the blank, then the sum of the digits is 30+9=39, which is not divisible by 9.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
