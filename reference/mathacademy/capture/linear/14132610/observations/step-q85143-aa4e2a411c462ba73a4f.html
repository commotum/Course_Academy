Which of the following numbers are divisible by 3?

  1. 187349
  2. 3121557
  3. 99125041

II and III only
b
I, II, and III
c
I and II only
d

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 187349 is 1+8+7+3+4+9=32, which is not divisible by 3. So, 187349 is not divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 3121557 is 3+1+2+1+5+5+7=24, which is divisible by 3. So, 3121557 is divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 99125041 is 9+9+1+2+5+0+4+1=31, which is not divisible by 3. So, 99125041 is not divisible by 3.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
