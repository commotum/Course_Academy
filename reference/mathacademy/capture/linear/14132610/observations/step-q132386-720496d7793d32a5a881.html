Which of the following numbers are divisible by 3?

  1. 935784
  2. 7391123
  3. 12144801
a
I and III only
b
II only
c
None
d
I, II, and III
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 935784 is 9+3+5+7+8+4=36, which is divisible by 3. So, 935784 is divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 7391123 is 7+3+9+1+1+2+3=26, which is not divisible by 3. So, 7391123 is not divisible by 3.

  • The sum of the digits of 12144801 is 1+2+1+4+4+8+0+1=21, which is divisible by 3. So, 12144801 is divisible by 3.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
