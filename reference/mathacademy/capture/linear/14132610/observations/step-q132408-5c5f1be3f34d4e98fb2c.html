EXPLANATION

A number is divisible by 6 if it is divisible by both 2 and 3.

  • The number 345989191 is not divisible by 2, so it is not divisible by 6.

  • The number 55355676 is divisible by 2, and it is also divisible by 3 since the sum of its digits is divisible by 3: 5+5+3+5+5+6+7+6=42 So, 55355676 is divisible by 6.

  • The number 137252124 is divisible by 2, and it is also divisible by 3 since the sum of its digits is divisible by 3: 1+3+7+2+5+2+1+2+4=27 So, 137252124 is divisible by 6.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
