Let's call D the event "a diamond card is selected" and F the event "a face card is selected."

The required probability is P(D∪F), which we can find using the addition law P(D∪F)=P(D)+P(F)−P(D∩F).

Let's find P(D) and P(F).

  • There are 13 diamond cards in a standard deck. So, we have P(D)=1352=14.

  • There are 12 face cards in a standard deck. So, we have P(F)=1252=313.

  • There are 3 diamond face cards (the Jack, Queen, and King of diamonds). Therefore,

P(D∩F)=352.

Finally, substituting all of the known information into the addition law, we get P(D∪F)=P(D)+P(F)−P(D∩F)=14+313−352=1126.
