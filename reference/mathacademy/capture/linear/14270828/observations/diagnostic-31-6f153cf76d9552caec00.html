Therefore, the general solution to our equation is x2+π6=3π4+2πn,x2+π6=5π4+2πn.

Solving the two equations above for x, we get x2=−π6+3π4+2πn,x2=−π6+5π4+2πnx2=7π12+2πn,x2=13π12+2πnx=7π6+4πn,x=13π6+4πn.
