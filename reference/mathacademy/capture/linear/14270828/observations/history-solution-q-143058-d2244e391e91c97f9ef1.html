Let's highlight the important words in the given statement:

The height H of a tree increases at a rate that's inversely proportional to the tree's height H at that moment.

We note the following:

  • The question describes the rate at which the height varies. Therefore, this is a statement about the quantity dHdt.

  • We're told that dHdt is inversely proportional to the tree's height. In other words, dHdt∝1H. Recall that ∝ means "is proportional to."
