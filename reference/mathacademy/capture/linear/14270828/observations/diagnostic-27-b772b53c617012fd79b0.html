In order for a point (a,b) to lie on the graph of the function y=arcsin⁡x, the following conditions must hold:

  • The reflected point (b,a) must lie on the graph of y=sin⁡x, meaning a=sin⁡b.

  • The y-value of the point must lie in the range of y=arcsin⁡x, which is y∈[−π2,π2].

With this in mind, let's check each of the points.

  • First, we consider the point (12,5π6). We note the following:

    • The point (5π6,12) lies on the graph of y=sin⁡x.✓

    • The range of y=arcsin⁡x is y∈[−π2,π2], and y=5π6 does not lie within this range. ×

    Therefore, the point (12,5π6) does not lie on the graph of y=arcsin⁡x.

  • Next, we consider the point (0,π). We note the following:

    • The point (π,0) lies on the graph of y=sin⁡x.✓

    • The range of y=arcsin⁡x is y∈[−π2,π2], and y=π does not lie within this range. ×

    Therefore, the point (0,π) does not lie on the graph of y=arcsin⁡x.

  • Finally, we consider the point (−32,−π3). We note the following:

    • The point (−π3,−32) lies on the graph of y=sin⁡x.✓

    • The range of y=arcsin⁡x is y∈[−π2,π2], and y=−π3 lies within this range. ✓

    Therefore, the point (−32,−π3) lies on the graph of y=arcsin⁡x.
