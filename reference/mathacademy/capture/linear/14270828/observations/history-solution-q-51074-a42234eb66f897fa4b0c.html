In this context, the area A under the curve y=r(t) over the interval [0,60] represents the number of corn kernels popped by the machine in one minute

The general formula for the trapezoidal rule states that A≈12[r(a)+2(r(t1)+r(t2)+⋯+r(tn−1))+r(b)]Δt, where n is the number of trapezoids and Δt=b−an is the step size.

Here, we have the following values:

  • The number of trapezoids is n=3.

  • The corresponding interval is [0,60], so we have a=0 and b=60.

  • The step size is given by Δt=b−an=60−03=20.

Using the above values, the general formula becomes A≈12[r(0)+2(r(20)+r(40))+r(60)]⋅20.

Substituting the appropriate numbers from the given table in the above expression gives

A≈12[40+2(50+50)+30]⋅20=10(270)=2700.

Therefore, the machine pops approximately 2700 in the given one-minute period.
