To find the lowest common multiple of two numbers, we first find the prime factorization of both numbers. Since the numbers 7 and 19 are prime numbers, they are already each their respective prime factorizations.

Now, we write down every prime factor that is shown above, and we raise each prime factor to the highest power given in either factorization:

  • the highest power of 7 is 1

  • the highest power of 19 is 1

Therefore, the lowest common multiple of 7 and 19 is LCM(7,19)=71⋅191=133.
