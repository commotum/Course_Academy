  1. x=−3
  2. x=0
  3. x=2

EXPLANATION

Let's consider each point separately.

  • The function f(x) is not continuous at x=−3. Since there is an asymptote at x=−3, it is impossible to draw the function in the neighborhood of x=−3 without taking our pencil off of the paper.

  • The function f(x) is continuous at x=0. The graph can be drawn in the neighborhood of x=0 without taking our pencil off of the paper.

  • The function f(x) is also continuous at x=2. The graph can be drawn in the neighborhood of x=2 without taking our pencil off of the paper.

Hence, the correct answer is "II and III only".
