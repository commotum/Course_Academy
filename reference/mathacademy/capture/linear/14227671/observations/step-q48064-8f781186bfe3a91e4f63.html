EXPLANATION

By inspecting the graph, we can conclude the following:

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, f(2)=1.

  • Statement II is false. Indeed, from the graph we see that limx→2−f(x)=1,limx→2+f(x)=−2. Hence, limx→2f(x) doesn't exist

  • Statement III is false. The graph of the function breaks at x=2 and it's impossible to draw the graph without taking our pencil off of the paper.
