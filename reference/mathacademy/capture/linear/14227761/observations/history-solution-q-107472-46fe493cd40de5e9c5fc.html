First, we need to find the slope of the tangent line. The slope is equal to the derivative of the curve at the given point:

m=dydx|x=1

To compute dydx, we first express y as the composite function

y=log⁡(u),u=4x+6.

Differentiating gives

dydu=1uln⁡(10),dudx=4.

We now apply the chain rule, and get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=1uln⁡(10)⋅4=4(4x+6)ln⁡(10)=2(2x+3)ln⁡(10).

Therefore, the slope m of the tangent line at the point where x=1 is

m=dydx|x=1=2(2x+3)ln⁡(10)|x=1=2(2(1)+3)ln⁡(10)=25ln⁡(10).

Now we know that the slope of the tangent line at the point (1,1) is m=25ln⁡(10). Finally, we find the equation of the tangent line at that point:

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−1=25ln⁡(10)(x−1)y=2x5ln⁡(10)−25ln⁡10+1.
