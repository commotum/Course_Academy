Let U be the event that the chosen pen is blue, and let A be the event that the pen was selected from drawer A. Then A′ is the event that the pen was selected from drawer B.

To calculate the probability that a randomly selected pen is blue, we can use the law of total probability:

P(U)=P(U|A)P(A)+P(U|A′)P(A′)

Let's go through the problem statement and translate each bit of information into mathematical notation.

  • Since 34 of the pens in drawer A are blue, we have P(U|A)=34.

  • Since 23 of the pens on the desk drawers are blue, we have P(U)=23.
