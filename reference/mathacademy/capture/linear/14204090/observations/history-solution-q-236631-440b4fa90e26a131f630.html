The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence is an=a1+(n−1)d.

First, we will write the formula for the nth term of this sequence. From the first few terms −13,−9,−5,−1,…,

we see that the first term is a1=−13 and the common difference is d=−9−(−13)=4.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify: an=a1+(n−1)d=−13+(n−1)(4)=−13+4n−4=4n−17

Now, we can use our formula to find the 36th term. All we have to do is substitute n=36: an=4n−17a36=4(36)−17=144−17=127
