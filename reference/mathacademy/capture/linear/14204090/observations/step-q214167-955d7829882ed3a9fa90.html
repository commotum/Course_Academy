Find the formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence if the first term is 3 and the second term is 9.
an=
6n−3
,n≥1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The formula for the nth term of an arithmetic sequence is

an=a1+(n−1)d.

We are given the first term a1=3. Also, the common difference of the sequence can be found as d=9−3=6.

So, we substitute into the formula and simplify:

an=a1+(n−1)d=3+(n−1)(6)=3+6n−6=6n−3
