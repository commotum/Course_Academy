Given an arithmetic sequence that has a8=8 and the common difference d=4, what is the general formula for the nth term of the sequence?

a
an=4n−24
b
an=−4n+40
c
an=4n−28
d
an=4n−20
e
an=−4n+36
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since a8=8 is 7 jumps after a1, we can determine the first term of the sequence:

a8=a1+7d8=a1+7(4)8=a1+28−20=a1

So, the formula for the nth term is:

an=a1+(n−1)d=−20+(n−1)(4)=−20+4n−4=4n−24
