The rate, in degrees Farhenheit per month, at which the average midday temperature in New York City varies, does so according to the function R(t)=t3+3, where t is the number of months since the measurements began. Estimate the total increase in air temperature over the first three months using the trapezoidal rule with 3 trapezoids of equal height.

a
50∘F
b
24.5∘F
c
31.5∘F
d
48.5∘F
e
45∘F
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In this context, the area A under the curve y=R(t) over the interval [0,3] represents the total increase in air temperature over the first three months.

The general formula for the trapezoidal rule states that A≈12[R(a)+2(R(t1)+R(t2)+⋯+R(tn−1))+R(b)]Δt, where n is the number of trapezoids and Δt=b−an is the step size.

Here, we have the following values:

  • The number of trapezoids is n=3.

  • The corresponding interval is [0,3], so we have a=0 and b=3.

  • The step size is given by Δt=b−an=3−03=1.

Using the above values, the general formula becomes A≈12[R(0)+2(R(1)+R(2))+R(3)]⋅1.

To identify the function values, we now set up a table with the values of t and R(t):

t 0 1 2 3
R(t) 3 4 11 30

Substituting the appropriate numbers into the above expression gives

A≈12[3+2(4+11)+30]⋅1=632=31.5.

Therefore, the total air temperature increase is approximately 31.5∘F.
