EXPLANATION

We use the triangle law of addition to add the vectors a and b.

Notice that we obtain a right triangle. So, we can use the Pythagorean theorem as follows:

|a+b|2=|a|2+|b|2=152+92=306

Hence, |a+b|=306=334.
