The vector a is directed due west and |a|=16. The vector b is directed vertically up and |b|=8. Find |a+b|.

a
311
b
510
c
85
d
16
e
24
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the triangle law of addition to add the vectors a and b.

Notice that we obtain a right triangle. So, we can use Pythagorean theorem as follows:

|a+b|2=|a|2+|b|2=162+82=320

Hence, |a+b|=320=85.
