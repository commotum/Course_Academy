Each side of an ice cube has length 12in. What is the volume of the ice cube?

a
32in3
b
14in3
c
8in3
d
18in3
e
2in3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We need to find the volume of a cube.

The volume V of a cube can be found using the formula

V=s3,

where s is the side length of the cube.

Substituting s=12in into the formula, we get V=(12in)3=18in3.

Therefore, the volume of the ice cube is 18in3.
