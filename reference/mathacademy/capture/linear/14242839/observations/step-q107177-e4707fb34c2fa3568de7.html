Find the volume of the cube whose sides each have length 4in.

a
64in3
b
16in3
c
4000in3
d
400in3
e
24in3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The volume V of a cube can be found using the formula

V=s3,

where s is the side length of the cube.

Substituting s=4in into the formula, we get V=(4in)3=64in3.
