The volume of a cube is 64000cm3. Find the length of the edges of the cube.

a
800cm
b
16cm
c
400cm
d
40cm
e
80cm
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the volume of a cube is

V=s3,

where s is the length of an edge, we can find s as follows:

s=V3=640003=40

Therefore, the length of the edges is 40cm.
