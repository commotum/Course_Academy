Method 2

We know that y=sin⁡x has a minimum value of −1.

−1≤sin⁡x

Subtracting 2 from both sides of the above inequality gives:

−1−2≤sin⁡x−2−3≤sin⁡x−2

Therefore, the minimum value of sin⁡x−2 is −3.
