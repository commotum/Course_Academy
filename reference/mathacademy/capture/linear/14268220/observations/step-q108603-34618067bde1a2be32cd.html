EXPLANATION

A polyhedron is convex if and only if the following condition holds:

If we take any two distinct points A and B that belong to the polyhedron, then all the points on the line segment AB― must also belong to the polyhedron.

With that in mind, let's examine our solids in turn.

  • Solids I and II are convex. Indeed, the above condition holds for any two points that belong to the solids.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
