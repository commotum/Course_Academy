EXPLANATION

To quickly determine the number of solutions, we can compute the discriminant. We have a=4, b=−12, and c=9, so the discriminant is as follows: D=b2−4ac=(−12)2−4(4)(9)=144−144=0

Since the discriminant is zero (D=0), the equation has one distinct real solution.
