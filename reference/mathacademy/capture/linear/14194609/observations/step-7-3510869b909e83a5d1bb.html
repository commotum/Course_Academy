Since the two bases of the trapezoid D are parallel, angles δ and γ in the graph are alternate interior angles and consequently, they have the same measure. So, we have

δ=γ=45∘.

Finally, δ=45∘(3y−15)∘=45∘3y−15=453y=60y=20.
