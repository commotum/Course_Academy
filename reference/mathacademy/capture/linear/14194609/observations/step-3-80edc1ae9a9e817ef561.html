Explanation

In general, congruent polygons have the same shape. In other words:

  • they have the same number of sides,

  • all corresponding sides are congruent, and

  • all corresponding interior angles are congruent.

With that in mind, let's compare polygons B and D to A.

  • The polygon A is an octagon (it has 8 sides). So, the polygons B and D can't be congruent to the polygon A because they do not have 8 sides.

  • On the other hand, A and C have the same shape: they have congruent corresponding sides, and since they are regular polygons, congruent corresponding angles. This implies that A≅C.
