Let P, Q and R be three collinear points such that PQ=11 cm and QR=16 cm. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. P cannot lie between Q and R
  2. Q cannot lie between P and R
  3. R cannot lie between P and Q
a
I and II only
b
I and III only
c
III only
d
I only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's check each statement.

If P is between Q and R, then, by the segments addition postulate, we have:

QR=PQ+PR16=11+PR16−11=PRPR=5

Therefore, P can lie between Q and R, so statement I is false.

If Q is between P and R, then again, by the segments addition postulate, we have:

PR=PQ+QR=11+16=27

Therefore, Q can lie between P and R, so statement II is false.

If R is between P and Q, then, by the segments addition postulate, we would have:

PQ=PR+QR11=PR+16PR=−5

This can't happen since the length of a segment can't be negative.

Therefore, R cannot lie between P and Q, so statement III is true.
