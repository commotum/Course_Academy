The remainder of dividing 2x4+x3−2x2−3x−7 by x+1 is
−5
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let p(x)=2x4+x3−2x2−3x−7.

The remainder theorem states that when p(x) is divided by (x+1), the remainder equals p(−1).

Evaluating p(−1) directly gives p(−1)=2(−1)4+(−1)3−2(−1)2−3(−1)−7=2−1−2+3−7=−5.

So, when p(x) is divided by (x+1), the remainder is −5.
