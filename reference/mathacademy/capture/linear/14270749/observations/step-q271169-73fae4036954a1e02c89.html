EXPLANATION

Let f(x)=x4−px3+2x2+5x+7.

The remainder theorem states that when f(x) is divided by (x+2), the remainder equals f(−2). Since we're told that the remainder is q, then, we must have f(−2)=q.

Evaluating f(−2) directly gives f(−2)=(−2)4−p(−2)3+2(−2)2+5(−2)+7=16+8p+8−10+7=21+8p,

which means that

q=21+8p.

Solving for p in the equation above, we find that p=q−218.
