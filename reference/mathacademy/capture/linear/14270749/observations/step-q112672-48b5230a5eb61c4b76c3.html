EXPLANATION

Let p(x)=−3x4+7x3−4x2−x+7.

The remainder theorem states that when p(x) is divided by (x−2), the remainder equals p(2).

Evaluating p(2) directly gives p(2)=−3(2)4+7(2)3−4(2)2−2+7=−48+56−16−2+7=−3.

So, when p(x) is divided by (x−2), the remainder is −3.
