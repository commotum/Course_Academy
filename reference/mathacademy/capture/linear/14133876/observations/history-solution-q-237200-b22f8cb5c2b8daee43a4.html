A polygon is convex if it has no internal angle larger than 180∘. In plain words, a convex polygon has no angles pointing inwards. Otherwise, the polygon is called concave.

A polygon is simple if its boundary does not intersect itself. Otherwise, the polygon is called complex.

In our case, the polygon is

  • simple since the boundary doesn't intersect itself, and

  • convex since it has no angles pointing inwards.
