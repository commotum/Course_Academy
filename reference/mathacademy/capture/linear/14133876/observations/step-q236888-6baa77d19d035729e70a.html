Which of the above polygons are complex?

a
II only
b
II and III only
c
I and II only
d
I only
e
III only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A polygon is simple if its boundary does not intersect itself. Otherwise, the polygon is called complex.

Among the given polygons, only polygon II has intersecting sides.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
