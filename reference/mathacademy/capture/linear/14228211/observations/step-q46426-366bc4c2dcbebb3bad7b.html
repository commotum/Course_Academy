If P(x) and Q(x) are polynomials of degree 3 and 5, respectively, which of the following statements are true?

  1. P+Q is always a polynomial
  2. P⋅Q is always a polynomial
  3. PQ is always a polynomial
a
I and II only
b
II and III only
c
I, II, and III
d
None
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Polynomials are closed under addition.

  • Statement II is true. Polynomials are closed under multiplication.

  • Statement III is false. For example, suppose P=x3 and Q=x5. Then PQ=x3x5=1x2, which is not a polynomial.

In conclusion, only statements I and II are true.
