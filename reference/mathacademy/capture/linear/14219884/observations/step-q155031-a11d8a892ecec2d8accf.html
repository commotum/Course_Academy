Which of the diagrams above shows three collinear points?

a
III only
b
I and II only
c
II only
d
I and III only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Three (or more) points are collinear if a straight line passes through all points.

With that in mind, let's examine our diagrams in turn.

  • Diagram I shows three collinear points. There is a straight line that passes through all three of these points.
  • Diagram II shows three points that are not collinear. We can't draw a straight line that passes through all three points.
  • Diagram III shows three collinear points. There is a straight line that passes through all three of these points.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
