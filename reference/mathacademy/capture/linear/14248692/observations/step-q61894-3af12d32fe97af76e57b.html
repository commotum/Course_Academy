Which of the statements is true about the solids depicted above?

a
The net of III consists of 1 rectangle and 4 triangles
b
The net of II consists of triangles only
c
The net of I has one triangle
d
The net of I consists of exactly 8 squares
e
The net of III consists of 4 triangles and 2 rectangles
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's sketch the nets of each solid.

As we can see, the correct answer is:

"The net of III consists of 1 rectangle and 4 triangles."

Other statements are false.

  • "The net of II consists of triangles only" is false since the net of II has 3 rectangles (the side faces).

  • "The net of I has one triangle" is not true because the net of I contains only squares.

  • "The net of III consists of 4 triangles and 2 rectangles" is false since the net of III only has 1 rectangle (the base of the solid).

  • "The net of I consists of exactly 8 squares" is not true because the net of I only has 6 squares.
