Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's sketch the nets of each solid.

As we can see, the correct answer is:

"The net of II consists of triangles only"

Other statements are false.

  • "The net of III consists of triangles only" is false since the net of III has one rectangle (the base of the solid).

  • "The net of I consists of 3 triangles and 3 rectangles" is not true because the net of I contains only 2 triangles (the base and the top of the solid).

  • "The net of III consists of 3 triangles and 1 rectangle" is false since the net of III has 4 triangles (the side faces).

  • "The net of II consists of 3 triangles and 1 rectangle" is not true because the net of II does not contain rectangles.
