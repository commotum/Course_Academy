What is 1.5% of 60?

0.6

Correct
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is 60, and the percentage is 1.5%.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

1.5%=1.5÷100=0.015

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=1.5%×60=0.015×60=0.9

Therefore, 1.5% of 60 is 0.9.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

1.5%=1.5100=1.5×10100×10=151,000

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=1.5%×60=151,000×60=9001,000=0.9

Therefore, 1.5% of 60 is 0.9.
