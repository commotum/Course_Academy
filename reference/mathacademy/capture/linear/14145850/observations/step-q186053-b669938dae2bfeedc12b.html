EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula: part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is 200, and the percentage is 124%.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal: 124%=124÷100=1.24

Applying our formula, we have: part=percentage×whole=124%×200=1.24×200=248

Therefore, 124% of 200 is 248.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction: 124%=124100=124÷4100÷4=3125

Applying our formula, we have: part=percentage×whole=124%×200=3125×200=31×20025=31×8=248

Therefore, 124% of 200 is 248.
