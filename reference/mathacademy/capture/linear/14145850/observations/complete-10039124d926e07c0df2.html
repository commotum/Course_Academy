Question 1
?
16% of 75 is equal to
13
.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is 75, and the percentage is 16%.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

16%=16÷100=0.16

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=16%×75=0.16×75=12

Therefore, 16% of 75 is 12.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

16%=16100=16÷4100÷4=425

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=16%×75=425×75=4×7525=4×3=12

Therefore, 16% of 75 is 12.
