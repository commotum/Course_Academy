To solve this problem, we can use the following formula:

part=percentage×whole

In our case, the whole is 150, and the percentage is 0.4%.

Method 1

First, we convert the percentage to a decimal:

0.4%=0.4÷100=0.004

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=0.4%×150=0.004×150=0.6

Therefore, 0.4% of 150 is 0.6.

Method 2

First, we convert the percentage to a fraction:

0.4%=0.4100=0.4×10100×10=41,000

Applying our formula, we have:

part=percentage×whole=0.4%×150=41,000×150=6001,000=0.6

Therefore, 0.4% of 150 is 0.6.
