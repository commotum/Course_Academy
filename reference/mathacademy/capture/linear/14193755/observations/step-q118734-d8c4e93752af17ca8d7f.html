EXPLANATION

Note, that the full angle is the sum of the measure of interior angles of an equilateral triangle, a regular octagon, a regular pentagon, and the measure of the shaded angle.

For a regular polygon with n sides, the measure of each interior angle must be 180∘⋅(n−2)n.

In an equilateral triangle, there are 3 equivalent interior angles, so each has a measure of 180∘(3−2)3=60∘.

Likewise, in a regular octagon, there are 8 equivalent interior angles, so each has a measure of 180∘(8−2)8=135∘.

A regular pentagon has 5 equivalent interior angles, so each has a measure of 180∘(5−2)5=108∘.

Let us call x the measure of the shaded angle. So, we have

60∘+135∘+108∘+x=360∘303∘+x=360∘x=57∘.
