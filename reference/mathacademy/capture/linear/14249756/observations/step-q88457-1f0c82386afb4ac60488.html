EXPLANATION

First, we factor the equation:

x2e3x−4xe3x−12e3x=0(x2−4x−12)e3x=0

We can now apply the zero product property, which states that ab=0 if and only if a=0 or b=0. This gives us two equations,

x2−4x−12=0ande3x=0.

We now solve each of these equations separately.

  • The equation x2−4x−12=0 can be factored as (x+2)(x−6)=0, which leads to the solutions x=−2,6.

  • The equation e3x=0 has no solutions because e3x is positive for every real number x.

Therefore, the original equation has the solutions x=−2,6, and their product is (−2)⋅6=−12.
