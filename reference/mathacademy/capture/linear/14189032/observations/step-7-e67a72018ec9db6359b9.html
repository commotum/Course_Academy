We cannot compare the fractions directly as they don't have a common denominator.

We can make a common denominator of 4×7=28.

To put 334 over a denominator of 28, we first convert to an improper fraction and then multiply the numerator and denominator by 7: 334=154=15×74×7=10528

We can now compare the fractions 10528 and 4428 by looking at the numerators only.
