First, we determine the common ratio r for this geometric series:

r=a2a1=153=5

The sum of the first N terms of a geometric series can be determined by the formula

SN=a1(1−rN)1−r.

In this case a1=3 and r=5. Substituting these values into the formula for SN gives the following:

SN=a1(1−rN)1−r=3(1−5N)1−5=3(1−5N)(−4)=−34(1−5N)=34(5N−1)
