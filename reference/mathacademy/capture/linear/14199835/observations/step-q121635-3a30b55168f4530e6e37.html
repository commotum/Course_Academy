Express csc⁡(52∘−θ) as a cofunction of a complementary angle.

a
cot⁡(θ+38∘)
b
tan⁡(38∘−θ)
c
 sec⁡(θ+38∘)
d
 sin⁡(38∘−θ)
e
 sec⁡(38∘−θ)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We know that the cofunction of csc⁡θ is sec⁡θ, and the angle complementary to 52∘−θ is 90∘−(52∘−θ)=90∘−52∘+θ=θ+38∘.

So we have

csc⁡(52∘−θ)=sec⁡(90∘−(52∘−θ))=sec⁡(θ+38∘).
