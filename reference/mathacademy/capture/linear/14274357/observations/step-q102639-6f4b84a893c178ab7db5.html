Find the values of θ at which the curve x=23cos⁡θ,y=2sin⁡θ,0≤θ<2π intersects the line x−y=0.

a
π6,7π6
b
π2,3π2
c
π3,4π3
d
π6,5π6
e
π4,5π4
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the coordinates of the points of intersection, we take the equation of the line, replace x and y with the parametric formulas, and then solve for θ. x−y=023cos⁡θ−2sin⁡θ=023cos⁡θ=2sin⁡θ13cos⁡θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θ3cos⁡θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θtan⁡θ=13tan⁡θ=33.

The principal value of θ is

θ=arctan⁡(33)=π6.

This value lies inside the domain 0≤θ<2π, so our first solution is θ1=π6.

To get a second solution, we add π to θ1:

θ1=π6+π=7π6

Therefore, our solutions are π6,7π6.
