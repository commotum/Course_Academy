The number 15% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.15. Therefore, a decrease of 15% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.15=0.85

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, the car's value was 7500⋅(0.85) dollars.

  • After 2 years, the car's value was 7500⋅(0.85)2 dollars.

  • After 3 years, the car's value was 7500⋅(0.85)3 dollars.

Evaluating the last expression gives

7500⋅(0.85)3≈4606.

So the car was worth $4606 (to the nearest dollar) after 3 years.
