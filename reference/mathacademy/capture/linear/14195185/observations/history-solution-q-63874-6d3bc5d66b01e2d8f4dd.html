The number 16% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.16. Therefore, a decrease of 16% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.16=0.84

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, value of the home was 150000⋅(0.84) dollars.

  • After 2 years, value of the home was 150000⋅(0.84)2 dollars.

  • After 3 years, value of the home was 150000⋅(0.84)3 dollars.

  • After 4 years, value of the home was 150000⋅(0.84)4 dollars.

Evaluating the last expression gives:

150000⋅(0.84)4≈74681

So, the home was worth $74681 (to the nearest dollar) after 4 years.
