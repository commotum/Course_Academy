Since the number of mice reduced by 56 per day, then only 16 of the mice from the previous day remained.

So we have:

  • At the start, there were 720 mice.

  • After 1 day, there were 720⋅(16) mice.

  • After 2 days, there were 720⋅(16)2 mice.

Evaluating the last expression, we get

720⋅(16)2=20.

Therefore, after 2 days only 20 mice remained.
