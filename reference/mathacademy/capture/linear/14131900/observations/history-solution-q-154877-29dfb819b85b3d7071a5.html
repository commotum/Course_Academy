Here, the letter x represents the measure of the unknown angle ∠DAE.

Note the following:

  • The angles ∠BAD and ∠DAE are adjacent because they share the side AD→.

  • So, the measure of the large angle (∠BAE) must be the sum of the measures of the two smaller angles (∠BAD and ∠DAE).

  • This means that the measure of ∠DAE must be the difference between the large angle (∠BAE) and the smaller known angle (∠BAD).
