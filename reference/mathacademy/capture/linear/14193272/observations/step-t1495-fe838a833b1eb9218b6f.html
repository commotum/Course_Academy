When we deal with functions, we are often interested in their largest or smallest values.

  • The global maximum of a function is the largest -value that the function attains on its domain. There can only be (at most) one global maximum. It's also called the absolute maximum.

  • The global minimum of a function is the smallest -value that the function attains on its domain. There can only be (at most) one global minimum. It's also called the absolute minimum.

If one or both exist, the global maximum and minimum values of a function are collectively called the global extrema of the function.
