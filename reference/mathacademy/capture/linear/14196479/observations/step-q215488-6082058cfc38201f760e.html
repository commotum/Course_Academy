EXPLANATION

First, we isolate the absolute value on the left side of the inequality:

35+|1−3x5|<2|1−3x5|<75

Now, we convert the absolute value inequality into a compound inequality to remove the absolute value.

The inequality specifies every number 1−3x5 whose distance from zero is less than 75. This corresponds to the following compound inequality:

−75<1−3x5<75

Now, we can solve the compound inequality using the usual methods. (Remember that whenever we multiply or divide by a negative number, we have to flip the inequality signs.)

−75<1−3x5<75−7<1−3x<7−8<−3x<68>3x>−683>x>−2
