Recall that when n is a positive integer,

(1+x)n=1+nx+12!n(n−1)x2+⋯+xn.

Therefore,

(1+x)7=1+7x+12!⋅7⋅6⋅x2+⋯+xn=1+7x+21x2+⋯+xn.

So, for small x, we have (1+x)7≈1+7x+21x2.

Now, we substitute x=0.02 into the above approximation of (1+x)7: (1.02)7=(1+0.02)7≈1+7(0.02)+21(0.02)2=1+7(0.02)+21(0.0004)=1+0.14+0.0084=1.1484

Finally, using a calculator, we can compute the percentage error, as follows: %error=amount of errorexact value×100=|(1.02)7−1.1484|(1.02)7×100≈0.02% rounded to two decimal places.
