EXPLANATION

Note that plugging in x=0 gives the indeterminate form 00. However, we can use L'Hopital's rule to find this limit:

limx→0ln(1+sin⁡(4x))tan⁡(2x)=limx→0[ln(1+sin⁡(4x))]′[tan⁡(2x)]′=limx→0(4cos⁡(4x)1+sin⁡(4x))2sec2⁡(2x)=(4cos⁡(4(0))1+sin⁡(4(0)))2sec2⁡(2(0))=(4(1)1+0)2(1)=2
