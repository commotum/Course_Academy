Let's recap some of the rules for exponents.

  • Product rule: to multiply powers of the same base, add the exponents.

  • Quotient rule: to divide powers of the same base, subtract the exponents.

  • Power rule: to raise a power to another power, multiply the exponents.

  • Power of product rule: to raise the product of two numbers to a power, raise each number to that power, and then multiply.

  • Power of quotient rule: to raise a fraction to a power, raise both the numerator and the denominator to that power.
