Consider the equivalence relation ∼ on C, defined by x∼y if x2=y2. Which of the following are in the equivalence class [−4]?

  1. −4
  2. 4
  3. 4i
a
II and III only
b
II only
c
I only
d
I and III only
e
I and II only

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The equivalence class [−4] consists of the elements that are equivalent to −4 under the given equivalence relation. So, we have

[−4]={x∈C|x∼−4}={x∈C|x2=(−4)2}={x∈C|x2=16}={−4,4}.

We see that −4 and 4 are in [−4], while 4i is not.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only".
