If m is the number of miles that the car is driven, and the car rental agency charges a fee of $0.08 per mile, then the cost for m miles must be 0.08m.

There is also a fixed fee of $25. Consequently, the constant term of the expression should be 25.

Therefore, the expression that represents the total cost (in dollars) when the car is driven for m miles must be

0.08m+25
