The number 4% (expressed as a decimal) is equal to 0.04. Therefore, a decrease of 4% corresponds to multiplication by a factor of:

1−0.04=0.96

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, there were 1200⋅(0.96) subscribers.

  • After 2 years, there were approximately 1200⋅(0.96)2 subscribers.

  • After 3 years, there were approximately 1200⋅(0.96)3 subscribers.

  • …

Repeating this pattern, we see that there were approximately 1200⋅(0.96)7 subscribers after 7 years.
