A car is purchased for $18000 and its value depreciates (decreases) by 15 per year. What will be the value of the car in 8 years?

a
$180008⋅(45)
b
$180008⋅(15)
c
$18000⋅(65)8
d
$18000⋅(45)8
e
$18000⋅(15)8
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the value reduces by 15, it means that 45 of its value remains every year.

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, the value of the car will be $18000⋅(45).

  • After 2 years, the value of the car will be $18000⋅(45)2.

  • After 3 years, the value of the car will be $18000⋅(45)3.

  • …

Repeating this pattern, we see that the value of the car will be $18000⋅(45)8 in 8 years.
