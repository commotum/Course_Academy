The value of a new sewing machine is $640. If the machine's value depreciates by one half every year, what will be its value after three years?

a
$58
b
$32
c
$102
d
$80
e
$64
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The initial value is 640 dollars, and its value reduces by 12 every year.

Therefore:

  • After 1 year, the value will be 640⋅(12) dollars.

  • After 2 years, the value will be 640⋅(12)2 dollars.

  • After 3 years, the value will be 640⋅(12)3 dollars.

Evaluating the last expression, we get

640⋅(12)3=80.

Therefore, the sewing machine will be worth $80 after 3 years.
