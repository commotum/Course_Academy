Find the greatest common divisor of 490 and 154.

a
12
b
23
c
11
d
14
e
17
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We use the Euclidean algorithm, as follows:

490=154(3)+28↙↙154=28(5)+14↙↙28=14(2)+0

The last positive remainder is 14. Therefore, we conclude that the greatest common divisor of 490 and 154 is 14. Symbolically, we write

gcd(490,154)=14.
