Which of the following statements are true?

  1. gcd(765,221)=17
  2. gcd(765,221)=1
  3. 765 and 221 are coprime
a
II and III only
b
II only
c
I and III only
d
III only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that two integers are coprime if their greatest common divisor is 1.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true, while statement II is false. Indeed, let's use the Euclidean algorithm, as follows: 765=221⋅3+102↙↙221=102⋅2+17↙↙102=17⋅6+0 The last positive remainder is 17. So, the greatest common divisor of 765 and 221 is 17: gcd(765,221)=17.

  • Statement III is false. Since gcd(765,221)>1, the numbers are not coprime.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
