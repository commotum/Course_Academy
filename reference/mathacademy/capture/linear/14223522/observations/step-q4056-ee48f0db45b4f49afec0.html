The point Q(−3,1) is shifted 2 units up, reflected across the y-axis, and then shifted 1 unit to the left. What is the resulting point?

a
(−2,−3)
b
(2,3)
c
(−4,−3)
d
(1,0)
e
(−3,−4)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's recall the functional representations of our three transformations:

  • A shift of 2 units up can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(x,y+2).

  • A reflection in the y-axis can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(−x,y).

  • A shift of 1 unit to the left can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(x−1,y).

Let's apply the given transformations to the point Q using our functions:

First, we apply the shift. The image of the point (−3,1) under the shift is

(−3,1)↦(−3,1+2)=(−3,3).

Then, we apply the reflection. The image of the point (−3,3) under the reflection is

(−3,3)↦(3,3).

Finally, we apply the second shift by 1 unit to the left. The image of the point (3,3) under the shift is

(3,3)↦(3−1,3)=(2,3).

Therefore, the resulting point is (2,3).
