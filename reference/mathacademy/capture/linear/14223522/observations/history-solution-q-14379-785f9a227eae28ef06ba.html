Let's recall the functional representations of our two transformations:

  • A shift of 7 units down can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(x,y−7).

  • A reflection across the line y=x can be represented by the function (x,y)↦(y,x).

Let's apply the given transformations to the point P using our functions:

First, we apply the shift. The image of the point (−21,17) under the shift is (−21,17)↦(−21,17−7)=(−21,10).

Then, we apply the reflection. The image of the point (−21,10) under the reflection is (−21,10)↦(10,−21).

Therefore, the resulting point is (10,−21).
