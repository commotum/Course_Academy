A group of students is interviewed. The scatter plot above shows the correspondence between the hours they spent playing video games daily the week before the literature exam and the grade they got on the exam. Which of the following best describes the correlation between the variables in the scatter plot?

a
 strong positive linear correlation
b
 weak negative linear correlation
c
 weak positive linear correlation
d
 strong negative linear correlation
e
 no linear correlation
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We note the following:

  • The points in the scatter plot seem to follow a straight line with a negative slope. So, we have a negative correlation.

  • The points lie pretty close to the corresponding trend line. So, we have a strong correlation.

Therefore, the variables have a strong negative linear correlation.
