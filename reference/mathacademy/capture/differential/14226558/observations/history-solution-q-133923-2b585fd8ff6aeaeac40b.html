Recall that the linear correlation coefficient ρ satisfies the inequality −1≤ρ≤1, where ρ=1 indicates a perfect positive correlation, and ρ=−1 indicates a perfect negative correlation.

Therefore, since ρ≈0.9, there is a strong positive linear correlation in the data.

  • The positive linear correlation means that the data points should be scattered along a line with a positive slope.

  • Since the correlation is strong, most of the points should lie close to the trend line.
