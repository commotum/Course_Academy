Recall that the linear correlation coefficient ρ satisfies the inequality −1≤ρ≤1, where ρ=1 indicates a perfect positive correlation, and ρ=−1 indicates a perfect negative correlation.

With that in mind, let's examine our scatter plot.

We note the following:

  • The points in the scatter plot seem to follow a straight line with a negative slope. So, the correlation coefficient must be negative. Thus, we disregard the following values: ρ≈0.9,ρ≈0.1,ρ≈0

  • The points lie pretty close to the corresponding trend line. So, the correlation coefficient must be closer to −1. Thus, we disregard the following value: ρ≈−0.2

Therefore, the best approximation of the correlation coefficient must be ρ≈−0.8.
