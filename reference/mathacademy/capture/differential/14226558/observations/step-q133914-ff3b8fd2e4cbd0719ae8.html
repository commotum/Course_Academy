Which of the following best describes the correlation between the variables in the scatter plot above?

a
strong positive linear correlation
b
weak positive linear correlation
c
weak negative linear correlation
d
no linear correlation
e

Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We note the following:

  • The points in the scatter plot seem to follow a straight line with a negative slope. So, we have a negative correlation.

  • The points are scattered quite far from the corresponding trend line. So, we have a weak correlation.

Therefore, the variables have a weak negative linear correlation.
