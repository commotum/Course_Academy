Consider the function f(x,y)=2xy. What is the rate of change of f(x,y) at the point (8,2) in the direction in which the function decreases most rapidly?

a
172

Correct
EXPLANATION

At a given point, a function f(x,y) decreases most rapidly in the opposite direction of its gradient at that point.

In our case, we compute −∇f(x,y) and evaluate it at the point (8,2): −∇f(x,y)=−∂∂x(2xy)i−∂∂y(2xy)j=−yxi−xyj−∇f(8,2)=−28i−82j=−12i−2j

Finally, the rate of change of f(x,y) at the point (8,2) in the direction of −∇f(8,2) is given by ‖−∇f(8,2)‖=‖−12i−2j‖=(−12)2+(−2)2=14+4=174=172.
