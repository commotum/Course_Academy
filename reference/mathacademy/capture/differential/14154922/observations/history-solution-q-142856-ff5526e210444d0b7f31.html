If X and Y are two continuous random variables with joint probability density function f(x,y), then the marginal density functions for X and Y are

fX(x)=∫−∞∞f(x,y)dy,fY(y)=∫−∞∞f(x,y)dx.

First, let's sketch the region D={(x,y):y2+1≤x}.

In our case, the marginal density function of Y for −∞<y<∞ is

fY(y)=∫y2+1∞1πx2dx=1π∫y2+1∞1x2dx=1πlimb→∞∫y2+1b1x2dx=1πlimb→∞[−1x]x=y2+1x=b=−1πlimb→∞[1b−1y2+1]=−1π[0−1y2+1]=1π(y2+1)

Therefore, the full expression for fY(y) is given by

fY(y)=1π(y2+1).
