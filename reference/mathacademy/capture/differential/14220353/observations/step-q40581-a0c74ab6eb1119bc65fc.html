Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's make a sketch of our region.

We need to represent D as a type II region. Writing y=x−2 and y=x in terms of y, we obtain

y=x−2⟹x=y+2y=x⟹x=y2

Now, let's determine the limits of integration.

  • To determine the y-limits, we need to find the y-coordinates of the points at which the curves intersect. So, we set the following equation and solve it for y: y2=y+2y2−y−2=0(y−2)(y+1)=0 So, since our region lies above the x-axis the y-limits are 0≤y≤2.

  • To determine the left and right functions, we draw a horizontal arrow from left to right through the region, as usual.

As a result, we get

D={(x,y):0≤y≤2,y2≤x≤y+2}.

Therefore, we can express the integral as follows:

∬Df(x,y)dA=∫02∫y2y+2f(x,y)dxdy
