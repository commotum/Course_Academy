EXPLANATION

Let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, the constant polynomial 1 can be represented as 1=0⋅x2+1. Hence, 1∈E.

  • Statement II is false. For example, consider the polynomial p(x)=x2+1∈E. Then, 1+p(x)=1+x2+1=x2+2≠p(x). The zero-polynomial 0 is the unique polynomial such that for any p(x)∈E we have 0+p(x)=p(x).

  • Statement III is false. For any polynomials p(x),q(x)∈E, we have [p(x)+q(x)]+r(x)=p(x)+[q(x)+r(x)], since polynomial addition is associative.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
