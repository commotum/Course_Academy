Let V be the set of all 2×2 matrices with real entries and determinant 0. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The zero-matrix [0000] belongs to V.
  2. Scalar multiplication of matrices in V is associative.
  3. For any A,B∈V and α∈R, we have α(A+B)=αA+αB.
a
I and II only
b
I and III only
c
I, II, and III
d
II and III only
e
I only

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, |0000|=0. Hence, [0000]∈V.

  • Statements II is true. Indeed, for any 2×2 matrix A=(aij)∈V, the entries are just real numbers. Thus, for any α,β∈R, we obtain that α(βaij)=(αβ)aij, and hence, α(βA)=(αβ)A.

  • Statements III is true. Indeed, for any 2×2 matrices A=(aij) and B=(bij) from V, the entries are just real numbers. Thus, for any α,β∈R, we obtain that α(aij+bij)=αaij+αbij, and hence, (α+β)A=αA+βA.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III."
