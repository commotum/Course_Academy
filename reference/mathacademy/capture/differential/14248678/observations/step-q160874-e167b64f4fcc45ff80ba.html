Let C be the set of all polynomials with real coefficients whose degree is less than or equal to 2. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. g(x)∈C, where g(x)=x3
  2. For every p(x),q(x)∈C, we have p(x)+q(x)∈C.
  3. The set is closed under addition.
a
III only
b
I and II only
c
II only
d
II and III only
e
I only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's examine our statements one at a time.

  • Statement I is false. The set C contains only polynomials whose degree is less than or equal to 2, however deg⁡(x3)=3>2. So, g(x)=x3∉C.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, suppose p(x),q(x)∈C, then deg⁡(p(x))≤2 and deg⁡(q(x))≤2. Since deg⁡(p+q) is less than or equal to the maximum of the degrees of p and q, we deduce that deg⁡(p+q)≤2. Hence, p(x)+q(x)∈C.

  • Statement III is true. Since statement II is true, the set is closed under addition.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
