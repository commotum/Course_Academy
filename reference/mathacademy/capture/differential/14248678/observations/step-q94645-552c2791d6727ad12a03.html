EXPLANATION

Let's examine each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. The set R contains only matrices of the form [ab00], where a and b are real numbers. However, I2=[1001] is not a matrix of this form. So, I2∉R.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, suppose A=[ab00]∈R and B=[cd00]∈R, where a,b,c,d are real numbers. Then, A+B=[ab00]+[cd00]=[a+cb+d00]. Since (a+c) and (b+d) are real numbers, we conclude that [a+cb+d00]∈R, i.e. A+B∈R.

  • Statement III is true. Since statement II is true, the set is closed under addition.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
