First, let's make t the subject of the parametric equations. This gives

t2=4x,t=2y.

Squaring both sides of the second equation, we have

t2=4y2.

We can now eliminate the parameter t by substituting the above into the equation t2=4x and rearranging to find the Cartesian equation of the parabola, as follows:

4y2=4xy2=x

Therefore, the Cartesian equation of the parabola is y2=x.
