First, let's make t the subject of the parametric equations. This gives

t2=−2x,t=−y.

Squaring both sides of the second equation, we have

t2=y2.

We can now eliminate the parameter t by substituting the above into the equation t2=−2x and rearranging to find the Cartesian equation of the parabola, as follows:

y2=−2x

Therefore, the Cartesian equation of the parabola is y2=−2x.
