EXPLANATION

We use the following facts:

  • The determinant of a product is equal to the product of the determinants.

  • The determinant of an orthogonal matrix P is equal to ±1.

Applying these ideas, we get the following:

|det(PQ)|=|det(P)⋅det(Q)|=|(±1)⋅det(Q)|=|±1|⋅|det(Q)|=|det(Q)|=|2⋅2−8⋅(−4)|=34
