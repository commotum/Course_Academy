Let the random variable X be the number of times we get a number divisible by 5.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=80 rolls

  • the probability of getting a number divisible by 5 (i.e., a 5 or a 10) on any roll of the die is p=212=16.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(80,16).

For a binomial random variable X, the standard deviation is given by SD[X]=Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the standard deviation of the number of times we get a number divisible by 5 is SD[X]=np(1−p)=80⋅16⋅(1−16)=80⋅16⋅56=1009=103.
