Let the random variable X be equal to the number of clients that bought a piece of cake.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=40 customers

  • the probability of a customer buying a piece of cake is p=35%=0.35

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(40,0.35).

For a binomial random variable X, the variance is given by Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the variance of the number of clients that bought a piece of cake is

Var[X]=np(1−p)=(40)(0.35)(1−0.35)=(14)(0.65)=9.1.
