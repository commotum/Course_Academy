For a particular balloon brand, the probability of a randomly selected balloon not bursting when blown up is 90%. What is the mean number of balloons that will not burst in a randomly selected packet of 20 balloons?

a
9
b
19
c
18
d
16
e
20
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of balloons that will not burst.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=20 balloons

  • the probability of not bursting each balloon is p=90%=0.9

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(20,0.9).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean of the number of balloons that do not burst is

E[X]=np=(20)(0.9)=18.
