Let the random variable X be equal to the number of correct answers.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=17 questions

  • the probability of selecting the correct answer in each question is p=12.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(17,12).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of correct answers is E[X]=np=17⋅12=8.5.
