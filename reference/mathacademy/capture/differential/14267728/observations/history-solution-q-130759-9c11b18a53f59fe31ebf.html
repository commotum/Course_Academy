Let the random variable X be the number of groups that do not arrive.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=30 reservations

  • the probability that a group doesn't arrive is p=15%=0.15

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(30,0.15).

For a binomial random variable X, the variance is given by Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the variance of the number of groups that do not arrive is

Var[X]=np(1−p)=(30)(0.15)(1−0.15)=(30)(0.15)(0.85)=3.825.
