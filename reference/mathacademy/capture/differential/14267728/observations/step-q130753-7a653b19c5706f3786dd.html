A restaurant knows that 13% of reservations are canceled by the customer. If the restaurant receives 30 reservations, what is the mean number of canceled reservations?

a
3.5
b
4
c
3.7
d
3.9
e
3
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be the number of canceled reservations.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=30 reservations

  • the probability that a reservation is canceled is p=13%=0.13

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(30,0.13).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of canceled reservations is

E[X]=np=(30)(0.13)=3.9.
