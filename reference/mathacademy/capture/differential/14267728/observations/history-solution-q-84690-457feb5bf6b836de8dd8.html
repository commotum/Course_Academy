Let the random variable X be equal to the number of times the coin lands on heads.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=15 tosses

  • the probability of landing on heads for each toss is p=0.7

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(15,0.7).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean of the number of times the coin lands on heads is

E[X]=np=(15)(0.7)=10.5.
