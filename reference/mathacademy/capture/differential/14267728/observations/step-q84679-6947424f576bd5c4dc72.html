A bolt manufacturer determines that 1.5% of the bolts produced are defective. A quality control inspection will check 200 randomly selected bolts. What is the mean number of defective bolts in the sample?

a
3
b
30
c
15
d
5
e
2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of defective bolts in the sample.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=200 bolts

  • the probability of selecting a defective bolt is p=1.5%=0.015

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(200,0.015).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of defective bolts is E[X]=np=(200)(0.015)=3.
