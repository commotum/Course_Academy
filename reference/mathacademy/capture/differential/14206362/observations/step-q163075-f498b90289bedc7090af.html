Suppose a transformation f:Rn→Rm is defined by

f:[x1x2⋮xn]⟶[f1(x1,x2,…,xn)f2(x1,x2,…,xn)⋮fm(x1,x2,…,xn)],

where f1,f2,…,fm are scalar functions with n variables. If f is differentiable at x, then the total derivative of f at x, denoted f′(x), is given by the m×n matrix

f′(x)=[∂f1∂x1∂f1∂x2⋯∂f1∂xn∂f2∂x1∂f2∂x2⋯∂f2∂xn⋮⋮⋮⋮∂fm∂x1∂fm∂x2⋯∂fm∂xn].

In our case, we have the following:

f′(x)=[∂f∂x∂f∂y]

First, we compute the partial derivatives:

∂f∂x=∂∂x(ytan⁡x)=ycos2⁡x,∂f∂y=∂∂y(ytan⁡x)=tan⁡x

Therefore, we have

f′(x)=[ycos2⁡xtan⁡x].

Evaluating f and its derivative at x=a=[π42], we have

f(a)=2,f′(a)=[41].

The tangent to f at x=a is given by

z−f(a)=f′(a)(x−a).

Substituting f(a), f′(a), and a in the equation above, we obtain

z−2=[41]([xy]−[π42]).

Therefore, the sum of the missing values is

2+4+1=7.
