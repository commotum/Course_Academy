EXPLANATION

First, let's add a Y=X2−4 row to our table.

x 0 2 4 6 8
y −4 −3 −2 −1 0
fX(x) 110 15 25 15 110

Now, fX(x)=fY(y) for corresponding values of X and Y. Therefore, the distribution of Y is as follows:

y −4 −3 −2 −1 0
fY(y) 110 15 25 15 110

Therefore, we conclude that P(Y=−2)=25.
