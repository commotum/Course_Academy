The random variable X has the following probability distribution.

x 0 10 20 30 40
fX(x) 512 112 112 14 16

Let Y=X10−1. Calculate P(Y=1).

a
0
b
16
c
512
d
14
e
112
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's add a Y=X10−1 row to our table.

x 0 10 20 30 40
y −1 0 1 2 3
fX(x) 512 112 112 14 16

Now, fX(x)=fY(y) for corresponding values of X and Y. Therefore, the distribution of Y is as follows:

y −1 0 1 2 3
fY(y) 512 112 112 14 16

Therefore, we conclude that P(Y=1)=112.
