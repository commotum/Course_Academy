Let X=[X1,X2]T be a bivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ given by μ=[03],Σ=[2114]. Find the distribution of Y=X1+X2.

a
Y∼N(6,8)
b
Y∼N(1,2)
c
Y∼N(6,4)
d
Y∼N(3,4)
e
Y∼N(3,8)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X=[X1,X2]T is a bivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ, given by μ=[μ1μ2],Σ=[σ12σ12σ21σ22], then the random variable Y, defined as

Y=aX1+bX2

is a normally distributed random variable for all values of a and b, where

E[Y]=aμ1+bμ2,Var[Y]=a2σ12+b2σ22+2abσ12.

In our case, we have μ1=0 and μ2=3. Therefore, E[Y]=E[X1+X2]=E[X1]+E[X2]=μ1+μ2=0+3=3.

Also, we have σ12=2,σ22=4, and σ12=σ21=1. Therefore, Var[Y]=Var[X1+X2]=12⋅2+12⋅4+2⋅1⋅1⋅1=2+4+2=8.

Therefore, we conclude that Y∼N(3,8).
