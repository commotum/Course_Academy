Recall that if X=[X1,X2,…,Xk]T is a multivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ, then the random variable Y, defined as

Y=cTX=∑i=1kciXi

is a normally distributed random variable, where c is any k×1 column vector, and

E[Y]=cTμ,Var[Y]=cTΣc.

In our case, since we wish to know the distribution of Y=−X1+3X2+2X3, we have

c=[−132].

Therefore, for the mean of Y, we have

E[Y]=cTμ=[−132][032]=0+9+4=13,

and for the variance of Y, we have

Var[Y]=cTΣc=[−132][321242125][−132]=[−132][51415]=−5+42+30=67.

Therefore, we conclude that

Y∼N(13,67).
