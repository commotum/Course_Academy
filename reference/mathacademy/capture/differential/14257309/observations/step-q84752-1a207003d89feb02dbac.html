Which of the following random variables could be modeled as a binomial distribution?

  1. The number of families without children among 20 families interviewed
  2. The number of players who score a goal in a soccer game among a total of 10 players if each player has the same probability of scoring a goal.
  3. The number of spectators in a soccer stadium
a
I and II only
b
I only
c
I and III only
d
II and III only
e
III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A binomial distribution models the number of successes in a sequence of n independent experiments (where each outcome is either success or failure).

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given random variables.

  • Random variable I could be modeled as a binomial distribution. The number of families without children among 20 families interviewed can be interpreted as the number of successes in a sequence of 20 independent experiments.

  • Random variable II could be modeled as a binomial distribution. The number of players who score a goal among 10 players can be interpreted as the number of successes in a sequence of 10 independent experiments.

  • Random variable III could not be modeled as a binomial distribution. The number of spectators cannot be interpreted as "success" or "failure."

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
