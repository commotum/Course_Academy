Find the second derivative of f(t)=2t3i−(cos⁡t+t2)j+(5t3+5t−25)k with respect to t.

a
12ti+(cos⁡t−2)j+30tk
b
12ti+(sin⁡t−2)j+30tk
c
12ti+(cos⁡t−2)j+15tk
d
12ti+(2−cos⁡t)j+30tk
e
12i−(sin⁡t−2)j+30tk
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, we find the derivative of f(t) by differentiating each component.

f′(t)=ddt(2t3)i−ddt(cos⁡t+t2)j+ddt(5t3+5t−25)k=6t2i−(−sin⁡t+2t)j+(15t2+5)k=6t2i+(sin⁡t−2t)j+(15t2+5)k

Then, we calculate f″(t) by differentiating f′(t):

f″(t)=ddt(6t2)i+ddt(sin⁡t−2t)j+ddt(15t2+5)k=12ti+(cos⁡t−2)j+30tk
