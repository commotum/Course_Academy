EXPLANATION

The statement A⇒B is true unless A is true and B is false.

Let's break this down into cases:

  • When A=T and B=T, we have (A⇒B)≡(T⇒T)≡T.

  • When A=T and B=F, we have (A⇒B)≡(T⇒F)≡F.

Therefore, if A is true, then A⇒B is sometimes true.
