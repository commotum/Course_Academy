The solid is a pentagonal pyramid since it is a pyramid whose base is a pentagon.

Also, notice that:

  • The solid is not a triangular pyramid since not all its faces are triangular.

  • The solid is not a pentagonal prism since it has only one pentagonal face.

  • The solid is not a rectangular prism since not all of its faces are rectangles. Due to the same reasons, the solid is not a cube.
