For a function f:Rn→R, given by f:[x1x2⋮xn]⏟x⟶f(x1,x2,…,xn),

the second derivative of f is given by the n×n matrix

f″(x)=[∂2f∂x12∂2f∂x1∂x2⋯∂2f∂x1∂xn∂2f∂x2∂x1∂2f∂x22⋯∂2f∂x2∂xn⋮⋮⋮⋮∂2f∂xn∂x1∂2f∂xn∂x2⋯∂2f∂xn2].

The Hessian determinant H(x,y) is the determinant of the second derivative:

H(x)=det(f″(x))

In our case, we have

f″(x,y)=[∂2f∂x2∂2f∂x∂y∂2f∂y∂x∂2f∂y2].

First, notice that since f′(x,y)=[−eysin⁡xeycos⁡x], we must have

∂f∂x=−eysin⁡x,∂f∂y=eycos⁡x.

Now, we compute the second partial derivatives:

∂2f∂x2=∂∂x(−eysin⁡x)=−eycos⁡x∂2f∂x∂y=∂∂x(eycos⁡x)=−eysin⁡x∂2f∂y∂x=∂∂y(−eysin⁡x)=−eysin⁡x∂2f∂y2=∂∂y(eycos⁡x)=eycos⁡x

So, the second derivative is f″(x,y)=[∂2f∂x2∂2f∂x∂y∂2f∂y∂x∂2f∂y2]=[−eycos⁡x−eysin⁡x−eysin⁡xeycos⁡x].

Evaluating the second derivative at (0,0), we obtain

f″(0,0)=[−1001].

Finally, we have H(0,0)=(−1)⋅1−02=−1.
