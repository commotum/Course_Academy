Let the random variable X be equal to the number of times a 2 or 5 or 7 is rolled.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=6 rolls

  • the probability of getting a 2,5, or 7 on any roll of the die is p=38.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(6,38).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of times we get a 2 or a 5 or a 7 is E[X]=np=6⋅38=94.
