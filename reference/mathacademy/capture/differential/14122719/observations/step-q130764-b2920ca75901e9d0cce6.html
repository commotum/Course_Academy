A fair octahedral die with sides labeled with numbers 1 through 8 is rolled 25 times. What is the variance of the number of times we get a number divisible by 3?

a
 754
b
 2516
c
 7516
d
 12516
e
 7225
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be the number of times we get a number divisible by 3.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=25 rolls

  • the probability of getting a number divisible by 3 (i.e., a 3 or a 6) on any roll of the die is p=28=14.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(25,14).

For a binomial random variable X, the variance is given by Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the variance of the number of times we get a number divisible by 3 is Var[X]=np(1−p)=25⋅14⋅(1−14)=25⋅14⋅34=7516.
