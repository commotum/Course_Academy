A fair coin is tossed 20 times. What is the mean number of tails that occur?

a
 13
b
 10
c
 11
d
 12
e
 9
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be the number of tails that occur.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=20 tosses

  • since the coin is fair, the probability of landing on tails for each toss is p=12

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(20,12).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of tails that occur is

E[X]=np=(20)(12)=10.
