Let the random variable X be equal to the number of matches that the player wins.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=5 matches

  • the probability of winning each match is p=60%=0.6

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(5,0.6).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of matches that the player wins is

E[X]=np=(5)(0.60)=3.
