A fair six-sided die is rolled 7 times. What is the mean of the number of times that the outcome of a single roll is a 2 or a 3?

a
53
b
72
c
259
d
73
e
499
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of times a 2 or 3 is rolled.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=7 rolls

  • the probability of getting a 2 or 3 on any roll of the die is p=26=13.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(7,13).

For a binomial random variable X, the mean is given by E[X]=np, where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the mean number of times we get a 2 or a 3 is E[X]=np=7⋅13=73.
