An infectious flu virus is spreading through a school. The probability of a randomly selected student having the flu next week is 0.2. If a class has 23 students, what is the standard deviation of the number of students having the flu next week? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

a
 1.918
b
 2.268
c
 2.459
d
 5.250
e
 2.897
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be the number of students having the flu next week.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=23 students in the class

  • the probability of selecting a student having the flu next week is 0.2

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(23,0.2).

For a binomial random variable X, the standard deviation is given by SD[X]=Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the standard deviation of the number of students having the flu next week is SD[X]=np(1−p)=(23)(0.2)(1−0.2)=(23)(0.2)(0.8)=3.68≈1.918 rounded to 3 decimal places.
