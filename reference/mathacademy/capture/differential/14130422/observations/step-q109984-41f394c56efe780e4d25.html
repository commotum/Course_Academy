EXPLANATION

Recall that if X and Y are independent random variables and a and b are constants, then

Var[aX+bY]=a2Var[X]+b2Var[Y].

Also, the variance of X is determined by the formula Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2.

First, let's find Var[X]. If X is defined on a sample space SX, then

E[X]=∑x∈SXx⋅f(x)=(−1)⋅0.25+0⋅0.25+1⋅0.5=0.25

and

E[X2]=∑x∈SXx2⋅f(x)=1⋅0.25+0⋅0.25+1⋅0.5=0.75.

Therefore,

Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2=0.75−0.252=0.6875.

Similarly, if Y is defined on a sample space SY, then

E[Y]=∑y∈SYy⋅f(y)=10⋅0.1+11⋅0.7+12⋅0.2=11.1

and

E[Y2]=∑y∈SYy2⋅f(y)=100⋅0.1+121⋅0.7+144⋅0.2=123.5.

Therefore,

Var[Y]=E[Y2]−E[Y]2=123.5−11.12=0.29.

Finally, we obtain

Var[5X−0.5Y]=52Var[X]+(−0.5)2Var[Y]=25(0.6875)+0.25(0.29)=17.26.
