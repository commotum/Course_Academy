Which of the following describes the level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z)=8x−2y+3z?

a
A family of surfaces given by z=83(x−2y)
b
A family of planes: cx−2cy+cz−5=0
c
A family of surfaces given by z=8x−2y+3
d
A family of planes: cx−2cy+3cz−8=0
e
A family of planes: cx−3cz−8=0
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z) are given by the following equation:

f(x,y,z)=c8x−2y+3z=c8=c(x−2y+3z)cx−2cy+3cz−8=0

Notice that f(x,y,z) can't be zero. Therefore, we get a family of planes: cx−2cy+3cz−8=0,c∈(−∞,0)∪(0,∞).
