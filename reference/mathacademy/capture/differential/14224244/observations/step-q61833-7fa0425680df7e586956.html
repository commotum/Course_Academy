Which of the following is a polyhedron with exactly 4 faces?

a
I only
b
II and III only
c
II only
d
III only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's examine the solids one-by-one.

  • I is a polyhedron and it has exactly 6 faces.

  • II is a polyhedron and it has exactly 5 faces.

  • III is a polyhedron but it has exactly 4 faces.

Therefore, the answer is "III only".
