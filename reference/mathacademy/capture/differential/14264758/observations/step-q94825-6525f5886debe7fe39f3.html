First, we convert the given probability into a cumulative probability: P(X<3)=0.6368⇒P(X≤3)=0.6368

Then, we transform to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X≤3)=0.6368P(Z≤3−μ4)=0.6368Φ(3−μ4)=0.63683−μ4=Φ−1(0.6368)

According to the table, we have

Φ(0.35)=0.6368⇒Φ−1(0.6368)=0.35.
