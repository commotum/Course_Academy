A softball team has a 70% chance of winning each game in a 10-game season. What is the probability that the team wins 9 of the 10 games? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

a
 0.092

 0.159

 0.257

 0.121

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of games that the team wins.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=10 games

  • the probability of winning each game is p=70%=0.7

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(10,0.7). We want to find the probability of winning x=9 games.

The number of successes of a binomial random variable has the probability mass function

P(X=x)=(nx)px(1−p)n−x,

where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

Substituting the above information into the formula for probability mass function, we get

P(X=9)=(109)(0.7)9(1−0.7)10−9=10!9!(10−9)!(0.7)9(0.3)1=10(0.7)9(0.3)=0.121

rounded to 3 decimal places.
