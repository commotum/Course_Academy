EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of baskets that the player makes.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=10 free throws

  • the probability of making a basket in each free throw is p=80%=0.8

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(10,0.8).

The number of successes of a binomial random variable has the probability mass function

P(X=x)=(nx)px(1−p)n−x,

where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

Substituting the above information into the formula for probability mass function, we get

P(X=x)=(10x)(0.8)x(1−0.8)10−x=(10x)(0.8)x(0.2)10−x.

We want to find the probability that the basketball player makes more than 8 baskets. So, we compute

P(X>8)=P(X=9)+P(X=10)=(109)(0.8)9(0.2)1+(1010)(0.8)10(0.2)0=10(0.8)9(0.2)+1(0.8)10=0.376

rounded to 3 decimal places.
