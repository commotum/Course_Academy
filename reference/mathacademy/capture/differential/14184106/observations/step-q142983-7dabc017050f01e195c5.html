EXPLANATION

Recall that if X1,X2, and X3 are mutually independent random variables, where

X1∼N(μ1,σ12),X2∼N(μ2,σ22),X3∼N(μ3,σ32),

and a1,a2,a3 are real constants, then

Y=a1X1+a2X2+a3X3∼N(a1μ1+a2μ2+a3μ3,a12σ12+a22σ22+a32σ32).

In our case, we have X1∼N(2,2),X2∼N(1,32),X3∼N(3,8), and Y=4X1+3X2−X3.

Since X1,X2, and X3 are mutually independent, we have

E[Y]=a1μ1+a2μ2+a3μ3=4⋅2+3⋅1+(−1)⋅3=8,Var[Y]=a12σ12+a22σ22+a32σ32=42⋅2+32⋅32+(−1)2⋅8=16⋅2+9⋅9+1⋅8=121.

Therefore,

Y∼N(8,121).

We want to find P(5<Y<10). Thus, we convert Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(5<Y<10)=P(5−8121<Z<10−8121)≈P(−0.27<Z<0.18)=P(Z<0.18)−P(Z<−0.27)=Φ(0.18)−Φ(−0.27)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(−0.27)≈0.3936,Φ(0.18)≈0.5714.

Therefore,

P(5<Y<10)=Φ(0.18)−Φ(−0.27)≈0.5714−0.3936=0.1778.
