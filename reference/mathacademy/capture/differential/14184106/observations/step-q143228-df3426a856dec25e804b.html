EXPLANATION

Recall that if X1,X2, and X3 are mutually independent random variables, where

X1∼N(μ1,σ12),X2∼N(μ2,σ22),X3∼N(μ3,σ32),

and a1,a2,a3 are real constants, then

Y=a1X1+a2X2+a3X3∼N(a1μ1+a2μ2+a3μ3,a12σ12+a22σ22+a32σ32).

In our case, we have

X1∼N(−1,7),X2∼N(3,2),X3∼N(5,9),

and

Y=X1−3X2+4X3.

Since X1,X2, and X3 are mutually independent, we have

E[Y]=a1μ1+a2μ2+a3μ3=1⋅(−1)+(−3)⋅3+4⋅5=10Var[Y]=a12σ12+a22σ22+a32σ32=12⋅7+(−3)2⋅2+42⋅9=1⋅7+9⋅2+16⋅9=169.

Therefore,

Y∼N(10,169).

We want to find P(Y>22). Thus, we convert Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(Y>22)=P(Z>22−10169)≈P(Z>0.92)=1−P(Z<0.92)=1−Φ(0.92)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(0.92)≈0.8212.

Therefore,

P(Y>22)=1−Φ(0.92)=1−0.8212≈0.1788.
