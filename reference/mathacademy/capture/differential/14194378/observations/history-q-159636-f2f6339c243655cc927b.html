3.Applications of Confidence Intervals?

The lengths of traditional baguettes and brown baguettes made at a particular French bakery have standard deviations of 4cm and 3cm, respectively. The baker wants to determine whether the population mean μx of the lengths of traditional baguettes is larger than the population mean μy of the lengths of brown baguettes. He constructs a 90% confidence interval for the difference between the population means.

Given that the baker sampled 50 traditional baguettes and 40 brown baguettes and found that their mean lengths were 65cm and 63.5cm, respectively, which of the following statements are true?

  1. The end-points of the 90% confidence interval for the difference between the population means are approximately 1.5±1.421.
  2. All values contained within the 90% confidence interval are positive.
  3. The confidence interval data suggests that μx>μy.
