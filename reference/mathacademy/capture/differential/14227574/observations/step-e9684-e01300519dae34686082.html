Example: Using Trend Lines to Make Predictions
?

The scatter plot above shows the distances some athletes in a sports club cover during a training session, and the time (in minutes) it takes. Use the trend line to estimate how long it takes a club athlete to run km.

EXPLANATION

To make our estimation, we proceed as follows:

  • We start at on the vertical axis.
  • Then, we move right until we intersect the trend line.
  • Finally, we move down until we intersect the horizontal axis.

Therefore, a club athlete can run kilometers in approximately minutes.
