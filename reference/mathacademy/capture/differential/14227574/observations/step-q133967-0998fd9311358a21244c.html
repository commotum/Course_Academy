The scatter plot above shows the number of matches won by some chess players in a championship and the hours they spend training per day. Use the trend line to estimate how much time players need to train each day to win 30 championship games.

a
 5 hours
b
 6 hours
c
 4.5 hours

 4 hours

 5.5 hours
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To make our estimation, we proceed as follows:

  • We start at y=30 on the vertical axis.
  • Then, we move right until we intersect the trend line.
  • Finally, we move down until we intersect the horizontal axis.

Therefore, a chess player that wins 30 games spends around 5 hours training per day.
