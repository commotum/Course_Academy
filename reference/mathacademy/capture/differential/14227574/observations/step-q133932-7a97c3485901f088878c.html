b
31∘C

32∘C

EXPLANATION

To predict the outside temperature knowing that they served 53 customers, we need to substitute y=53 into the equation of the trend line and solve for x: y=2.25x−1953=2.25x−192.25x=72x=32

Therefore, according to this model, the outside temperature is approximately 32∘C.

Note: The point where y=53 is not shown on our trend line. Therefore, our result may be unreliable.
