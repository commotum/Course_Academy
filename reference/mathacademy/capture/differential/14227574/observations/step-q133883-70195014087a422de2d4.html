EXPLANATION

To make our prediction, we proceed as follows:

  • First, we start at y=80 on the y-axis.
  • Then, we move right until we intersect the trend line.
  • Finally, we move down until we intersect the x-axis.

Therefore, when y=80, we obtain x≈15.
