The scatter plot above shows the weight loss per week (in pounds) vs. the number of hours spent training per week for a group of people. Use the trend line to estimate how much weight a person loses if they train for 4 hours per week.

a
 1.5 pounds
b
 2.5 pounds
c
 4 pounds
d
 3 pounds
e
 2 pounds
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To make our estimation, we proceed as follows:

  • We start at x=4 on the horizontal axis.
  • Then, we move up until we intersect the trend line.
  • Finally, we move left until we intersect the vertical axis.

Therefore, a person training 4 hours a week will lose around 3 pounds of weight.
