The scatter plot above gives the correspondence between the variables x and y for some data points. The equation of the trend line is y=5−0.25x. Assuming this trend continues, estimate the value of y when x=10.

a
2.5
b
3
c
1.5
d
2
e
1
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We can estimate the required value by substituting x=10 into the equation of the trend line:

y=5−0.25x=5−0.25(10)=5−2.5=2.5

Therefore, according to this model, when x=10, we have y≈2.5.

Note: The point where x=10 is not shown on our trend line. Therefore, our result may be unreliable.
