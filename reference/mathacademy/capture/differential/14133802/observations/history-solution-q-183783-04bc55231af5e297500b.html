Notice that the elements of our set −5,−10,−20,−40,… form an infinite geometric sequence with a common ratio r=2:

r=2=−10−5=−20−10=−40−20=⋯

Therefore, the nth term an of our sequence for n∈N, is given by an=a1⋅rn−1=(−5)⋅2n−1=(−5)⋅2n⋅2−1=−52⋅2n.

Therefore, we can define our set using a constructive definition as follows:

S={−52⋅2n:n∈N}
