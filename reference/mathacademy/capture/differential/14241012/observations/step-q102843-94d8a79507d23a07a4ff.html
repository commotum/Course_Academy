Which of the following is a mathematical statement?

a
Compute the sum of the first two natural numbers.
b
1 is a rational number.
c
The set of rational numbers.
d
The diagonal of a quadrilateral.
e
 What is a prime number?
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A mathematical statement is a sentence or mathematical expression that is either true or false.

Of the given sentences, the only sentence that is either true or false is "1 is a rational number." (This sentence is true.)

All the other sentences are neither true nor false.
