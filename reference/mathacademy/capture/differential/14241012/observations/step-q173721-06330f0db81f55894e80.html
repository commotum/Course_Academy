Which of the following statements are false?

  • P: 5 is a real number.

  • Q: 10 is divisible by 3.

  • R: 2+3≤1

a
 P and Q only
b
 P,Q, and R
c
 Q and R only
d
 P only
e
 Q only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A mathematical statement is a sentence or mathematical expression that is either true or false.

With that in mind, let's evaluate the truth value of each statement:

  • Statement P is true. Indeed, 5 is a real number.

  • Statement Q is false because 10 is not divisible by 3.

  • Statement R is false because 2+3=5≰1.

Therefore, the correct answer is "Q and R only."
