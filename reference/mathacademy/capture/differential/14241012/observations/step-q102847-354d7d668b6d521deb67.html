Which of the following is a mathematical statement?

a
 How do you find the perimeter of a square?
b
 Sum of the measures of the angles in a pentagon.
c
 The sides of a triangle.
d
 A rectangle has four angles.
e
 The diameter of a circle.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

A mathematical statement is a sentence or mathematical expression that is either true or false.

Of the given sentences, the only sentence that is either true or false is "A rectangle has four angles." (This sentence is true.)

All the other sentences are neither true nor false.
