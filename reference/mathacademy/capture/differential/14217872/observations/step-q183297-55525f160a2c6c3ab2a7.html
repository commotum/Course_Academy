EXPLANATION

De Morgan's law for conjunctions states that

¬(A∧B)=¬A∨¬B.

So, the negation of "x≤0 and y is odd" is

¬(x≤0 and y is odd)≡¬(x≤0∧y is odd)≡¬(x≤0)∨¬(y is odd)≡x>0∨y is even≡x>0 or y is even.
