EXPLANATION

De Morgan's law for conjunctions states that

¬(A∧B)=¬A∨¬B.

So, the negation of "x≤13 and y>12" is

¬(x≤13 and y>12)≡¬(x≤13∧y>12)≡¬(x≤13)∨¬(y>12)≡x>13∨y≤12≡x>13 or y≤12.
