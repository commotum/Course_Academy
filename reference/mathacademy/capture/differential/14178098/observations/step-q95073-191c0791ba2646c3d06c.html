EXPLANATION

Let X represent the grade obtained by a student. Then we have X∼N(81,σ2), and we want to find P(X<70). But first, we need to find the standard deviation σ.

We know that P(X>91)=0.05. First, we convert this to a cumulative probability:

P(X≤91)=1−P(X>91)=1−0.05=0.95

Next, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring. We get

P(X≤91)=0.95P(Z≤91−81σ)=0.95Φ(10σ)=0.9510σ=Φ−1(0.95)

From the table,

Φ−1(0.95)≈1.6449.

Therefore, we have

10σ=1.64491σ=0.16449σ=10.16449≈6.0794.

Finally, we can solve for P(X<70) as follows:

P(X<70)=P(X≤70)=P(Z≤70−816.0794)≈P(Z≤−1.81)=Φ(−1.81)=0.0351≈3.5%

Therefore, approximately 3.5% of the students scored lower than 70.
