Find a Cartesian equation of the plane that passes through the point P(−1,3,2) and is perpendicular to the vector n=⟨4,0,1⟩.

a
4x+z+2=0
b
4x−y+z+2=0
c
4x+y+z+2=0
d
4x+z−2=0
e
x+4y+z+2=0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we note that the position vector of the point P is p=⟨−1,3,2⟩ and the normal vector of the plane is n=⟨4,0,1⟩.

Using the dot product we can find an equation of the plane as follows: (r−p)⋅n=0r⋅n=p⋅nr⋅⟨4,0,1⟩=⟨−1,3,2⟩⋅⟨4,0,1⟩r⋅⟨4,0,1⟩=−2

Now, to obtain the Cartesian equation of the plane, we substitute r=⟨x,y,z⟩ into the equation above. Hence, ⟨x,y,z⟩⋅⟨4,0,1⟩=−24x+z=−24x+z+2=0.
