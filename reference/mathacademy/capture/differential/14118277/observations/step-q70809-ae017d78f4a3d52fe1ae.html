Find a Cartesian equation of the plane that passes through the point P(2,3,−1) and is perpendicular to the line r=[−201]+t[32−2].

a
3x+2y−2z−2=0
b
−2x+y+z−2=0
c
2x+3y−z−14=0
d
2x−z−14=0
e
3x+2y−2z−14=0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we note that the position vector of P is p=⟨2,3,−1⟩. From the vectorial equation, we find that the line is parallel to the vector ⟨3,2,−2⟩. So, the normal vector of the plane is

n=⟨3,2,−2⟩.

Using the dot product, we can find the equation of the plane as follows:

(r−p)⋅n=0r⋅n=p⋅nr⋅⟨3,2,−2⟩=⟨2,3,−1⟩⋅⟨3,2,−2⟩r⋅⟨3,2,−2⟩=14.

Now, to obtain the Cartesian equation of the plane, we substitute r=⟨x,y,z⟩ into the equation above. Hence, ⟨x,y,z⟩⋅⟨3,2,−2⟩=143x+2y−2z=143x+2y−2z−14=0.
