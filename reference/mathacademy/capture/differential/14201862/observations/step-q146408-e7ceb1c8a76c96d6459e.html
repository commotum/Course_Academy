Suppose X and Y are discrete random variables. Let the joint CDF of X and Y be F(x,y), where the supports of X and Y, respectively, are

SX={0,2,4,6},SY={0,1,3}.

Find an expression for the corresponding joint CDF when x>6,y>3.

a
6x
b
3y
c
0
d
6x+3y
e
1
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If X and Y are discrete random variables with the joint probability mass function f(x,y), then the corresponding joint CDF is given by

F(x,y)=P(X≤x,Y≤y)=∑xi≤x∑yi≤yf(xi,yi).

Note the following:

  • We can think of each point where f(x,y)≠0 as a point mass in the plane, where the "probability mass" at each point equals the probability associated with that point.

  • The joint CDF F(x,y)=P(X≤x,Y≤y) is the total "probability mass" that lies in the infinite rectangular region that has its top-right corner at (x,y) and whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axis.

Given the supports of X and Y, the joint support S is a subset of

SX×SY={0,2,4,6}×{0,1,3}.

Let's sketch the points in SX×SY, which contains the joint support. Let's also consider a point (x,y) where x>6 and y>3, and a region Δ that lies below and to the left of this point.

Since every value in the support of X is at most 6 and x>6, every possible value of X satisfies X≤x. Similarly, every value in the support of Y is at most 3 and y>3, so every possible value of Y satisfies Y≤y.

Therefore, the region Δ contains the entire joint support, and

F(x,y)=P(X≤x,Y≤y)=1.
