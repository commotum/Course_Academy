Let X represent the duration (in seconds) of the selected song. Then we have X∼N(230,402).

We want to find P(X<180). First, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X<180)=P(Z≤180−23040)=P(Z≤−1.25)=Φ(−1.25)

From the z-table, we see that

Φ(−1.25)=0.1056≈0.11.

Therefore, the probability that a randomly selected song recorded by this company has a duration less than or equal to 180s is approximately 0.11.
