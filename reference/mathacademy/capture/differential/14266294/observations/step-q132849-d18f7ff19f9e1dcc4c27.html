EXPLANATION

Let X represent the amount (in grams) of sugar in a cookie. Then we have X∼N(15,σ2), and we want to find the standard deviation σ.

We know that P(X≤14)=0.10. Converting X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring, we get

P(X≤14)=0.10P(Z≤14−15σ)=0.10Φ(−1σ)=0.10−1σ=Φ−1(0.10).

From the table,

Φ−1(0.10)=−1.2816.

Finally then,

−1σ=−1.28161σ=1.2816σ=11.2816≈0.78.
