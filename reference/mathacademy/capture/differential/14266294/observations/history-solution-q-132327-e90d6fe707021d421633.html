Let X represent the voltage (in volts) of a randomly selected adapter. Then we have X∼N(19,0.052).

We want to find P(X<18.97). First, we write this as a cumulative probability:

P(X<18.97)=P(X≤18.97)

Then, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X≤18.97)=P(Z≤18.97−190.05)=P(Z≤−0.6)=Φ(−0.6)

From the z-table, we see that

Φ(−0.6)=0.2743≈0.27.

Therefore, the probability that the output voltage of a randomly selected adapter is less than 18.97 volts is approximately 0.27.
