EXPLANATION

Let X represent the amount of sugar (in grams) on the cookies. Then we have X∼N(15,0.82).

We want to find P(X<14). First, we write this as a cumulative probability:

P(X<14)=P(X≤14)

Then, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(X≤14)=P(Z≤14−150.8)=P(Z≤−1.25)=Φ(−1.25)

From the z-table, we see that

Φ(−1.25)=0.1056≈0.11.

Therefore, the probability that the amount of sugar on a randomly selected cookie is less than 14 grams is approximately 0.11.
