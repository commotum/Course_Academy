Let W be a 9×10 matrix that has 8 pivot columns. What is the dimension of the null space of W?

a
1
b
4
c
5
d
8
e
2
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We know that dim(Null(W)) is the number of non-pivot columns in W.

Since W has 10 columns and 8 of them are pivots, then the number of non-pivot columns in W is 10−8=2.

Therefore, dim(Null(W))=2.
