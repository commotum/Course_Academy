Let Q be a 5×8 matrix that has 5 pivot columns. What is the dimension of the null space of Q?

a
7
b
5
c
6
d
4
e
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We know that dim(Null(Q)) is the number of non-pivot columns in Q.

Since Q has 8 columns and 5 of them are pivots, then the number of non-pivot columns in Q is 8−5=3.

Therefore, dim(Null(Q))=3.
