Which of the following pairs of sets are disjoint?

  1. {x,y,z} and {3,6,9}
  2. {a,b,c,d,e} and {a,e,i,o,u}
  3. {1,3,6} and {3,5,7}
a
III only
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
I and II only
e
I only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Two sets A and B are disjoint if they have no element in common, i.e., their intersection is empty: A∩B=∅

With that in mind, let's examine our sets.

  • Pair I is disjoint. Indeed, the intersection of the sets is empty: {x,y,z}∩{3,6,9}=∅✓

  • Pair II is not disjoint since the intersection of the sets is not empty: {a,b,c,d,e}∩{a,e,i,o,u}={a,e}≠∅×

  • Pair III is not disjoint since the intersection of the sets is not empty: {1,3,6}∩{3,5,7}={3}≠∅×

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
