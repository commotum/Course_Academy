Find the disjoint union of the sets A1={v,w} and A2={w,x,y}.

a
{v,w,x,y}
b
{(v,x),(v,y)}
c
{(v,w),(v,x),(w,x),(w,y)}
d
{(v,1),(w,1),(w,2),(x,2),(y,2)}
e
{v,x,y}
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Notice that the sets are overlapping since {v,w}∩{w,x,y}={w}≠∅.

So, for each of our sets, we form the associated set, where each element is tagged with the corresponding index, as follows: A1→A1∗={(v,1),(w,1)}A2→A2∗={(w,2),(x,2),(y,2)}

Now, the disjoint union of our sets is simply the union of the associated tagged sets: A1⊔A2={v,w}⊔{w,x,y}=A1∗∪A2∗={(v,1),(w,1)}∪{(w,2),(x,2),(y,2)}={(v,1),(w,1),(w,2),(x,2),(y,2)}
