EXPLANATION

First, recall the associative law ABC=(AB)C=A(BC).

Notice that A is a 3×2 matrix, B is a 2×3 matrix, and C is a 3×1 matrix. Therefore,

  • multiplying A by B gives a 3×3 matrix, which would require finding 9 entries, while

  • multiplying B by C gives a 2×1 matrix, which requires finding 2 entries.

So, we find the product BC first:

BC=[−2103−30][120]=[0−3]

Now, we multiply A by BC:

ABC=A(BC)=[3−2−1541][0−3]=[6−15−3]
