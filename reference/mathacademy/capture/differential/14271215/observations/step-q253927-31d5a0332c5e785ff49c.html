Given that the random variable X∼Exp(25), calculate P(X>7|X>2).

a
 1e2
b
 12e4
c
 1e6
d
 13e8
e
 1e7

Correct
EXPLANATION

Given a random variable X∼Exp(λ), and two positive real numbers s,t, the memoryless property of the exponential distribution states that P(X>s+t|X>s)=P(X>t)=e−λt.

In our case, we have λ=25, s=2, and s+t=7. From this, we can find t as follows: t=(t+s)−s=7−2=5

Substituting these values into the above formula, we get P(X>7|X>2)=e−λt=e−(2/5)⋅(5)=e−2=1e2.
