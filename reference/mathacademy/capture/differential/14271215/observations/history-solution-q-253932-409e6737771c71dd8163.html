Given a random variable X∼Exp(λ), and two positive real numbers s,t, the memoryless property of the exponential distribution states that P(X>s+t|X>s)=P(X>t)=e−λt.

We can compute the complementary conditional probability as follows: P(X≤s+t|X>s)=1−P(X>s+t|X>s)=1−e−λt

In our case, we have λ=5, s=4, and s+t=6. From this, we can find t as follows: t=(t+s)−s=6−4=2

Substituting these values into the above formula, we get P(X≤6|X>4)=1−e−λt=1−e−(5)⋅(2)=1−e−10
