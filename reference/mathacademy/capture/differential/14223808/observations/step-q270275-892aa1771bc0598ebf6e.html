Using the chain rule, we have

ddxf(y(x))=dfdy⋅dydx.

Let's consider each of the factors in turn.

  • The function f acts on a vector y∈R2 and outputs a vector f(y)∈R3. Thus, the total derivative of f with respect to y is a 3×2 matrix dfdy=[∂f1∂y1∂f1∂y2∂f2∂y1∂f2∂y2∂f3∂y1∂f3∂y2]=[∂∂y1(y1−y2)∂∂y2(y1−y2)∂∂y1(y22)∂∂y2(y22)∂∂y1(2y1+y23)∂∂y2(2y1+y23)]=[1−102y223y22].
