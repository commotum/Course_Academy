Consider the function g:X→Y given by the mapping diagram shown above. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The domain of g is {1,2,3}
  2. The codomain of g is {1,2,3}
  3. The range of g is {1,2,3}

Correct
EXPLANATION

The notation g:X→Y means that the function g maps the set X to the set Y. In this case,

  • the domain of g is X,

  • the codomain of g is Y.

The range of g is the subset of the codomain that contains all values y∈Y that are mapped to by at least one element x∈X.

With that in mind, let's inspect our diagram:

  • the domain of g is X={1,2,3},

  • the codomain of g is Y={1,2,3,4},

  • the range of g is {1,2,3}, the subset of elements of Y that have at least one incoming arrow.

As a result, statements I and III are true, while statement II is false.

Therefore, the correct answer "I and III only."
