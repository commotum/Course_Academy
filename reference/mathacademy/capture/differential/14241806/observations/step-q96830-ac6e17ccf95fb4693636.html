Consider the function f:X→Y given by the mapping diagram shown above. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The domain of f is {5,10,15}
  2. The codomain of f is {5,10,12,13,14,15}
  3. The range of f is {12,13,14}
a
I only
b
I and III only

II and III only
d

Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The notation f:X→Y means that the function f maps the set X to the set Y. In this case,

  • the domain of f is X,

  • the codomain of f is Y.

The range of f is the subset of the codomain that contains all values y∈Y that are mapped to by at least one element x∈X.

With that in mind, let's inspect our diagram:

  • the domain of f is X={5,10,15},

  • the codomain of f is Y={12,13,14},

  • the range of f is {12,13}, the subset of elements of Y that have at least one incoming arrow.

As a result, statements II and III are false, while statement I is true.

Therefore, the correct answer "I only."
