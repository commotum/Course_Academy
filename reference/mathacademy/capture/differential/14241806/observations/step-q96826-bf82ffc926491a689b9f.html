Which of the following is the correct interpretation of g:R→R?

  1. The domain of g is R
  2. The codomain of g is R
  3. The range of g is R
a
II only
b
I and II only
c
I only
d
I and III only
e
II and III only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The notation

g:R→R

means that the function g maps the set R to the set R. In this case,

  • the domain of g is R, and

  • the codomain of g is R. Note that this is not necessarily the range.

As a result, statements I and II are true, while statement III is false.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
