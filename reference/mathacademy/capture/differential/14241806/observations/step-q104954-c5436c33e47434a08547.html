EXPLANATION

The notation g:X→Y means that the function g maps the set X to the set Y. In this case,

  • the domain of g is X,

  • the codomain of g is Y.

The range of g is the subset of the codomain that contains all values y∈Y that are mapped to by at least one element x∈X.

With that in mind, let's inspect our diagram:

  • the domain of g is X={−1,0,1,2},

  • the codomain of g is Y={0,1,2},

  • the range of g is {0,1,2}, the subset of elements of Y that have at least one incoming arrow.

As a result, statements I and II are false, while statement III is true.

Therefore, the correct answer "III only."
