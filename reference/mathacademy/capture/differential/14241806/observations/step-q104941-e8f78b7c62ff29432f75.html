Which of the following is equivalent to h(x)=2x3−3x?

a
h:2x3↦−3x
b
h:x↦2x3−3x
c
h:x↦x3
d
h:x↦−3x
e
h:3x↦2x3
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The notation h(x)=2x3−3x means that the function h maps the element x in the domain to 2x3−3x in the codomain. This can also be written as

h:x↦2x3−3x.
