One way to bet in roulette is to place $2 on a single number. If the ball lands on that number, the player wins $70. If the ball lands on any of the other 37 slots, the player doesn't win. What is the expected profit, rounded to the nearest dollar, for a player who plays one hundred games on number 8 in a row?
The expected profit in dollars is E(p)=
−16
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First of all, profit is revenue minus cost, or in our case, winnings minus cost. So, once we have found the expected winnings after one hundred games, we need to subtract 100⋅2=200 dollars to account for the price of playing.

Now, let's find the expected winnings and summarize them in a table. If W is the winnings (in dollars) on a single game, and f(w) is the probability mass function for W, then we have the following:

f(w) 138 3738
w 70 0

The expected value of W is given by

E[W]=70⋅f(70)+0⋅f(0)=70(138)+0(3738)=7038≈1.84.

This represents the expected winnings for a single game. So, the expected winnings for 100 games on number 8 is

100⋅E[W]=100⋅(1.84)=184.

Now, subtracting the 200 dollars paid to play, we get that the expected profit (in dollars) is

E[p]=184−200=−16.
