First of all, profit is revenue minus cost, or in our case, winnings minus cost. So, once we have found the expected winnings after one hundred games, we need to subtract 100⋅1=100 dollars to account for the price of playing.

Now, let's find the expected winnings and summarize them in a table. If W is the winnings (in dollars) on a single game, and f(w) is the probability mass function for W, then we have the following:

f(w) 138 3738
w 35 0

The expected value of W is given by

E[W]=35⋅f(35)+0⋅f(0)=35(138)+0(3738)=3538≈0.92.

This represents the expected winnings for a single game. So, the expected winnings for 100 games on number 12 is

100⋅E[W]=100⋅(0.92)=92.

Now, subtracting the 100 dollars paid to play, we get that the expected profit (in dollars) is

E[p]=92−100=−8.
