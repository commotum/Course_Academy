If P(x) and Q(x) are polynomials of degree 4 and 8, respectively, which of the following statements are true?

  1. P+Q is a polynomial of degree 8
  2. P⋅Q is a polynomial of degree 32
  3. QP is a polynomial of degree 4
a
 II only
b
 I and III only
c
 III only
d
 I and II only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's look at each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The highest-degree terms in P and Q must contain x4 and x8, respectively. Since there is no x8 term in P that could possibly cancel the x8 term in Q, the leading term in P+Q, still contains x8. Therefore, the degree of P+Q must always be 8.

  • Statement II is false. Since the highest-degree terms in P and Q must contain x4 and x8, respectively, the highest-degree term in P⋅Q must contain x4⋅x8=x4+8=x12, meaning that P⋅Q has degree 12.

  • Statement III is false. For example, suppose P=x4 and Q=x8+1. Then, QP=x8+1x4=x4+1x4, which is not a polynomial.

In conclusion, only statement I is true.
