If P(x) and Q(x) are polynomials of degree 12, the highest possible degree of P+Q is
12
.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The highest possible degree of P+Q is 12.

For example, suppose P=x12 and Q=x12. Then P+Q=2x12.

Since neither P nor Q contain terms of order higher than x12, P+Q can't contain terms of order higher than x12.
