A salesperson visits one of three regions: North, South, or East. The probabilities that they are sent North, South, and East are 0.25, 0.5, and 0.25 respectively.

If the expected number of sales made daily is 9 in the North, 14 in the South, and 11 in the East, what is the expected number of sales made in a day?

The expected number of sales made is
13
.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We are given the probability of being sent to one of three regions and the expected number of sales made. This is a direct application of the law of total expectation, which tells us how to compute the overall expected value by conditioning on which region the salesperson visits.

Let X be the number of sales made in a day, and let Y be the region the salesperson visited.

  • Y=North with probability 0.25, and E[X∣Y=North]=9.

  • Y=South with probability 0.5, and E[X∣Y=South]=14.

  • Y=East with probability 0.25, and E[X∣Y=East]=11.

So, we calculate our expected value as follows:

E[X]=E[X∣Y=North]⋅P(Y=North)+E[X∣Y=South]⋅P(Y=South)+E[X∣Y=East]⋅P(Y=East)=9⋅0.25+14⋅0.5+11⋅0.25=2.25+7+2.75=12
