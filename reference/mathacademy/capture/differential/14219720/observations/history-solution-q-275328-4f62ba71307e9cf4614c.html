We are asked to compute the expected value of X, given conditional expectations of X based on the value of another random variable Y. This is a direct application of the law of total expectation, which states

E[X]=∑yE[X∣Y=y]⋅P(Y=y).

In this problem, Y takes two values:

  • Y=1 with probability 0.3, and E[X∣Y=1]=5

  • Y=2 with probability 0.7, and E[X∣Y=2]=8

So, we calculate our expected value as follows:

E[X]=E[X∣Y=1]⋅P(Y=1)+E[X∣Y=2]⋅P(Y=2)=5⋅0.3+8⋅0.7=1.5+5.6=7.1
