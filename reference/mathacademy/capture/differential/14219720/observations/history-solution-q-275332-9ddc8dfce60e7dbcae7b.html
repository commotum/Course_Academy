We are given an expression for E[X∣Y] in terms of Y, and we are asked to compute E[X]. This is a direct application of the tower property, which states that

E[X]=E[E[X∣Y]].

So, we substitute the given expression for the inner expectation:

E[X]=E[2Y+1]

Now, we apply the linearity of expectation:

E[X]=E[2Y+1]=2⋅E[Y]+E[1]=2⋅3+1=7
