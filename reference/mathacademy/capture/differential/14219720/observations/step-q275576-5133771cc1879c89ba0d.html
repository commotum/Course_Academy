In a puzzle game, a room's difficulty level n is selected randomly from 1 to 120, with each value equally likely. Inside each room are n puzzle pieces numbered from 1 to n, and one of them is selected uniformly at random. What is the expected value of the number on the selected puzzle piece?

a
29.00
b
30.50
c
31.50
d
31.25
e
30.75
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

This is a two-step random process: first, a room of difficulty level n is chosen randomly and uniformly from 1 to 120, and then a puzzle piece with a number is chosen uniformly from 1 to n. We are asked to find the expected value of the puzzle piece selected in the second step.

  • Let Y be the room difficulty level, an integer selected uniformly from 1 to 120.

  • Let X be the puzzle piece number, an integer selected uniformly from 1 to Y.

We are asked to compute E[X]. Since the distribution of X depends on Y, we apply the tower property:

E[X]=E[E[X∣Y]]

Given Y=n, the number X is chosen uniformly from 1 to n. So, we have that

E[X∣Y=n]=n+12.

Therefore, we have that

E[X]=E[Y+12].

Now, we apply the linearity of expectation:

E[X]=12⋅E[Y+1]=12⋅(E[Y]+1)

Since Y is uniformly distributed on {1,2,…,120}, we use the formula for the mean of the first n integers:

E[Y]=120+12=60.5

Substituting, we finally get

E[X]=12⋅(E[Y]+1)=12⋅(60.5+1)=12⋅61.5=30.75.
