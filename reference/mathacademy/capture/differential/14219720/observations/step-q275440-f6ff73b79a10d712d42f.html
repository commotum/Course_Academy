A tutoring agency assigns a tutor to either High School A or High School B. With probability 0.45, the tutor is sent to High School A, where the expected number of students tutored in a day is 4.8. With probability 0.55, the tutor is sent to High School B, where the expected number of students tutored is 6.2.

What is the expected number of students tutored in a day?

a
4.92
b
4.57
c
5.43
d
5.57
e
6.13
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We are asked to compute the expected number of students tutored per day. This is a classic application of the law of total expectation, which allows us to calculate an overall expected value by conditioning on a related variable — in this case, the high school the tutor is assigned to.

Let X be the number of students tutored in a day, and let Y be the school to which the tutor is assigned.

  • Y=A with probability 0.45, and E[X∣Y=A]=4.8.

  • Y=B with probability 0.55, and E[X∣Y=B]=6.2.

So, we calculate our expected value as follows:

E[X]=E[X∣Y=A]⋅P(Y=A)+E[X∣Y=B]⋅P(Y=B)=4.8⋅0.45+6.2⋅0.55=2.16+3.41=5.57
