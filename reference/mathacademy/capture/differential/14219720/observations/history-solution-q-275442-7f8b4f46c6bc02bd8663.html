We are asked to compute the expected number of orders picked per day. This is a classic application of the law of total expectation, which allows us to calculate an overall expected value by conditioning on a related variable — in this case, the section to which the picker is assigned.

Let X be the number of orders picked, and let Y be the warehouse section to which the picker is assigned.

  • Y=1 with probability 0.65, and E[X∣Y=1]=120.

  • Y=2 with probability 0.35, and E[X∣Y=2]=95.

So, we calculate our expected value as follows:

E[X]=E[X∣Y=1]⋅P(Y=1)+E[X∣Y=2]⋅P(Y=2)=120⋅0.65+95⋅0.35=78+33.25=111.25
