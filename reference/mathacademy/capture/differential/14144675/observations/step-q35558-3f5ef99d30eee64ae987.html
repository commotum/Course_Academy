Let W be the set of all polynomials in the variable x∈R with coefficients in R of degree 5 or greater. Complete the following sentence:

W is not a vector space (with respect to polynomials addition and multiplication of a polynomial by a real number) because _________.

a
addition of polynomials is not associative
b
addition of polynomials is not commutative
c
W does not contain the zero element
d
W does not contain the polynomial −p(x) for every p(x)∈W
e
addition and scalar multiplication of polynomials do not satisfy the distributive laws
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The correct answer is as follows:

W is not a vector space (with respect to polynomials addition and multiplication of a polynomial by a real number) because W does not contain the zero element.

Indeed, the zero polynomial has degree 0, which is less than 5.

On the other hand:

  • Addition of polynomials is associative. ✓

  • Addition of polynomials is commutative. ✓

  • Addition and scalar multiplication of polynomials satisfy the distributive laws. ✓

  • For any polynomial p(x) of degree 5 (or greater), the corresponding negative polynomial −p(x) has the same degree. ✓
