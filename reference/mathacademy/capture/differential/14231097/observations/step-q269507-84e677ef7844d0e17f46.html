Consider the function f:R2→R defined by f(x)=x12+x23. What is the total derivative of f with respect to x=[x1x2]?

Correct
EXPLANATION

The total derivative of a scalar-valued function f:Rn→R with respect to a vector x∈Rn is the row vector dfdx=[∂f∂x1∂f∂x2⋯∂f∂xn]∈R1×n.

In our case, the function

  • acts on a vector x∈R2, and

  • outputs a scalar f(x)∈R defined by f(x)=x12+x23.

Therefore, we have
dfdx=[∂f∂x1∂f∂x2]=[∂∂x1(x12+x23)∂∂x2(x12+x23)]=[2x13x22]∈R1×2.
