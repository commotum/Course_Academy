The distance covered by a basketball player in an official match is uniformly distributed between 3 and 4 km, inclusive. What is the mean distance covered per match?

a
 3.4km
b
 3.2km
c
 7km
d
3.5km
e
 4.5km
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let X represent the distance covered by a randomly chosen basketball player. From the problem statement, we know that X∼U[3,4], and we wish to compute E[X].

In general, if X∼U[a,b], then X has the following expected value:

E[X]=a+b2

So, for our random variable X∼U[3,4], we have the following expected value:

E[X]=3+42=3.5

Therefore, the mean distance covered is 3.5km.
