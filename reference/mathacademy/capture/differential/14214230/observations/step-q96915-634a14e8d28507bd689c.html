The height of water in a dam is uniformly distributed between 120 and 195 meters, inclusive. What is the mean height of the water?

a
 157.5m
b
 120.5m
c
 195m
d
 155.5m
e
 315m
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let X represent the height of water in a random inspection of the dam. From the problem statement, we know that X∼U[120,195], and we wish to compute E[X].

In general, if X∼U[a,b], then X has the following expected value:

E[X]=a+b2

So, for our random variable X∼U[120,195], we have the following expected value:

E[X]=120+1952=157.5

Therefore, the mean height of water in the dam is 157.5m.
