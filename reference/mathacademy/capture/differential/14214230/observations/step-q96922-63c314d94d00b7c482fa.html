The minimum time it takes a plumber to fix a leaky pipe is 30 minutes, and the maximum time is 110 minutes. Assuming that the plumber's time to fix a leaky pipe is uniformly distributed, what is the expected time to fix a pipe?

a
37min
b
30min
c
140min
d
110min
e
 70min
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let X represent the time to fix a randomly chosen leaky pipe. From the problem statement, we know that X∼U[30,110], and we wish to compute E[X].

In general, if X∼U[a,b], then X has the following expected value:

E[X]=a+b2

So, for our random variable X∼U[30,110], we have the following expected value:

E[X]=30+1102=70

Therefore, the mean time to fix the leaky pipe is 70min.
