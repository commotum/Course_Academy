EXPLANATION

In this case, we use the double-angle formula for sine: sin⁡2θ=2sin⁡θcos⁡θ⇒12sin⁡2θ=sin⁡θcos⁡θ.

Letting θ=x6, we get 12sin⁡(x3)=sin⁡(x6)cos⁡(x6).

Substituting this into our integral, we get ∫03π/2sin⁡(x6)cos⁡(x6)dx=∫03π/212sin⁡(x3)dx=12∫03π/2sin⁡(x3)dx=12[−3cos⁡(x3)]|03π/2=−32cos⁡(x3)|03π/2=−32[cos⁡(π2)−cos⁡(0)]=−32[0−1]=32.
