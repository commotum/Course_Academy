Consider the set A, defined as

A={(−1)n+1⋅n:n∈{1,2,3,4}}.

What is the supremum of A?

sup(A)=
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The supremum of a set A is the smallest upper bound of A.

If A is a finite set, the supremum of A equals its maximum.

First, let's write down all the terms of our set by substituting n=1,2,3,4 into the expression for the elements of A: (−1)1+1⋅1=1(−1)2+1⋅2=−2(−1)3+1⋅3=3(−1)4+1⋅4=−4

So, we have A={1,−2,3,−4}.

Therefore,

sup(A)=max{1,−2,3,−4}=3.
