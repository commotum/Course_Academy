If X1,X2,…,Xn is a sample of size n from a population with population mean μ and population variance σ2, then

E[X―]=μ,Var[X―]=σ2n,

and the standard error is given by

SE[X―]=Var[X―]=σn.

We're told that the shirts in the population have sizes 8 and 10, and these are distributed in the ratio 1:3. Therefore, to compute the population's mean and variance, we can consider a population consisting of 1+3=4 shirts only with the following sizes:

8,10,10,10.

Therefore, the population mean is

μ=14∑ixi=14(8+3⋅10)=14(8+30)=384=9.5,

Thus, the population standard deviation is σ=0.75, and we have that

SE[X―]=0.75n.
