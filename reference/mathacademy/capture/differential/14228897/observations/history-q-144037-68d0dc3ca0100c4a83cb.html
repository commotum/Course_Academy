5.Finding a Conditional Probability Using the Law of Total Probability?

If 25% of all cows on ranch A and 20% of all cows on ranch B are dairy cows, and the probability that a randomly selected cow from any of the three ranches is a dairy cow is 37%, what is the probability that a randomly selected cow from ranch C is a dairy cow?
