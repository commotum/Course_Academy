EXPLANATION

Let δ>0. A neighborhood of a point x0 is the set of points whose distance from x0 is less than δ. {x:|x−x0|<δ}

In two-dimensional space, a neighborhood of x0 is the open disc |x−x0|<δ, as shown below.

Furthermore, we have the following definitions:

  • A point x0 is an interior point of a set S if S contains some neighborhood of x0. The set of all interior points of S is called the interior of S.

  • A point x0 is a boundary point of a set S if every neighborhood of x0 contains points that are in S and points that are not in S. The set of all boundary points of S is called the boundary of S.

Now, the set B consists of all points that lie inside the ellipse x24+y22=1. A sketch of this region is shown below:

Let's add the points P(2,1) and Q(0,−1) and some neighborhoods of these points to our diagram.

Let's examine the statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The coordinates of P(2,1) satisfy the equation x24+y22=1. Hence, P lies in the set ∂B, the boundary of B.

  • Statement II is false. The coordinates of P(2,1) do not satisfy the inequality x24+y22<1. Hence, P does not belongs to B.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, Q is an interior point since there exists an open neighborhood of Q that is fully contained inside the set B.

Therefore, the correct answer is 'I and III only'.
