EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of the given determinants in turn.

  • The A is a triangular matrix, we obtain |A|=2⋅1⋅1⋅4=8≠0.

  • The matrix B has identical columns: B=[−20−2313101] As a result, |B|=0.

  • Since C is a 2×2 matrix, its determinant is: det(C)=5⋅4−(−2)⋅(−10)=0

Therefore, only |B| and |C| are equal to 0.
