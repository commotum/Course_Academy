EXPLANATION

The Cartesian product A×B consists of all ordered pairs whose first coordinate belongs to A and whose second coordinate belongs to B.

A×B={(a,b):a∈A,b∈B}

In this case, the set

S=[−1,1]×{−1,1} contains every point in R2 whose x-coordinate is any real number in the interval [−1,1] and whose y-coordinate is either −1 or 1.

S={(x,y):x∈[−1,1],y∈{−1,1}}.

To visualize S, consider S1⊂S containing all points whose x-coordinate is any real number in the interval [−1,1] and whose y-coordinate equals −1

S1={(x,−1):x∈[−1,1]}
