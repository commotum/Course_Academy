Let the random variable X be equal to the number of times we get an even number.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=12 rolls of the die

  • the probability of getting an even number (i.e., a 2, 4, 6, or 8) on any roll of the die is p=48=12.

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(12,12).

For a binomial random variable X, the standard deviation is given by SD[X]=Var[X]=np(1−p), where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the standard deviation of the number of times we get an even number is SD[X]=np(1−p)=12⋅12⋅(1−12)=3=1.732 rounded to 3 decimal places.
