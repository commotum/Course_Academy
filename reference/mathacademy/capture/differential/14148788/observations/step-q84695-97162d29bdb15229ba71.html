A processor manufacturer determines that 2% of the processors it produces are defective. A quality control officer inspects a random sample of 500 processors. What is the standard deviation of the number of defective processors found in the sample? Round your answer to 2 decimal places.

a
3.33
b
3.13
c
1.13
d
1.33
e
3.31
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let the random variable X be equal to the number of defective processors.

Here, we have the following information:

  • there are n=500 processors

  • the probability of a processor being defective is p=2%=0.02

So, X follows a binomial distribution, where X∼B(500,0.02).

For a binomial random variable X, the standard deviation is given by SD[X]=Var[X]=np(1−p) where n is the number of experiments, and p is the probability of success on each experiment.

So, the standard deviation of the number of defective processors is

SD[X]=np(1−p)=(500)(0.02)(1−0.02)=9.8=3.13

rounded to 2 decimal places.
