A hyperbolic paraboloid

A hyperboloid

An elliptic cylinder

EXPLANATION

Recall that the equation of a hyperbolic cylinder, whose axis of symmetry is the z-axis, is given by

x2a2−y2b2=1,

and a hyperbolic cylinder, whose axis of symmetry is parallel to the z-axis and passes through the point (x0,y0) in the xy-plane, is given by

(x−x0)2a2−(y−y0)2b2=1.

Thus, our equation is a hyperbolic cylinder with the following parameters:

(x0,y0)=(−2,1),a=b=1.
