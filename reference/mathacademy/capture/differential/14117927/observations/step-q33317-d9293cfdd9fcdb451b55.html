Find an equation of the plane that passes through the point Q(1,−4,2) and is perpendicular to the line that goes through the points A(2,1,−3) and B(1,6,−4).

a
r⋅⟨1,−4,2⟩=⟨−1,5,−1⟩
b
r⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩=−23
c
r⋅⟨1,−4,2⟩=−23
d
r⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩=⟨1,−4,2⟩
e
r⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩=0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we note that the position vector of Q is q=⟨1,−4,2⟩.

Since the line passes through the points A(2,1,−3) and B(1,6,−4) we can find a vector parallel to the line:

AB→=⟨1,6,−4⟩−⟨2,1,−3⟩=⟨−1,5,−1⟩.

So, a normal vector to the plane is n=⟨−1,5,−1⟩.

Therefore, the equation of the plane is given by (r−q)⋅n=0r⋅n−q⋅n=0r⋅n=q⋅nr⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩=⟨1,−4,2⟩⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩r⋅⟨−1,5,−1⟩=−23.
