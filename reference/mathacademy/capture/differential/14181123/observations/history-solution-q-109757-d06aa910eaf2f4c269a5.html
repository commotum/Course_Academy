Let X denote the weight of a chicken, and let Y denote the weight of a turkey. We want to find

P(5X>Y)=P(5X−Y>0).

Since X and Y are independent random variables such that X∼N(1.5,0.12) and Y∼N(8,0.42), then

5X−Y∼N(5⋅1.5−8,52⋅0.12+0.42)∼N(−0.5,0.41).

So, the variable 5X−Y is normally distributed with mean μ=−0.5 and standard deviation σ=0.41.

To find P(5X−Y>0), we convert 5X−Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(5X−Y>0)=P(Z>0−(−0.5)0.41)≈P(Z>0.78)=1−P(Z≤0.78)=1−Φ(0.78)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(0.78)≈0.7823.

Therefore,

P(5X−Y>0)≈1−Φ(0.78)≈1−0.78230.2177.
