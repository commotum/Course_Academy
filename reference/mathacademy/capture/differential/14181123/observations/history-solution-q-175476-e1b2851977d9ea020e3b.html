Let X denote the weight of a watermelon, and let Y denote the weight of a melon. We want to find P(X>3Y)=P(X−3Y>0).

Since X and Y are independent random variables such that X∼N(10,42) and Y∼N(5,22), then X−3Y∼N(10−3⋅5,16+32⋅4)∼N(−5,52).

So, the variable X−3Y is normally distributed with mean μ=−5 and standard deviation σ=52.

In order to find P(X−3Y>0), we convert X−3Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(X−3Y>0)=P(Z>0+552)≈P(Z>0.69)=1−P(Z<0.69)=1−P(Z≤0.69)=1−Φ(0.69)

Using the z-table, we see that Φ(0.69)≈0.7549.

Therefore, P(X−3Y>0)≈1−Φ(0.69)≈1−0.7549≈0.2451.

So, we conclude that the probability that the weight of a watermelon is more than 3 times greater than the weight of a melon is 0.2451.
