EXPLANATION

Recall that if X1 and X2 are independent random variables, where X1∼N(μ1,σ12) and X2∼N(μ2,σ22), and a and b are constants, then

aX1+bX2∼N(aμ1+bμ2,a2σ12+b2σ22).

Since X1 and X2 are independent, the distribution of Y is given by Y=5X1+X2∼N(5μ1+μ2,52σ12+σ22)∼N(5⋅1+4,25⋅0.4+6)∼N(9,16).

Therefore, Y is normally distributed with mean μ=9 and standard deviation σ=16=4.

We want to find P(Y<13.4). Thus, we convert Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(Y<13.4)=P(Z<13.4−94)=P(Z<1.1)=Φ(1.1)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(1.1)≈0.8643.

Therefore,

P(Y<13.4)≈0.86.
