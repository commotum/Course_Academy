Consider the function f:R2→R defined by f(x)=xT⋅[1131]⋅x, where the dot denotes matrix multiplication. What is the total derivative of f with respect to x?

a
[4x22x1]
b
[6x1x26x1x2]
c
[2x12x2]
d
[2x1+4x24x1+2x2]
e
[4x1+2x22x1+4x2]
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The total derivative of a scalar-valued function f:Rn→R with respect to a vector x∈Rn is the row vector dfdx=[∂f∂x1∂f∂x2⋯∂f∂xn]∈R1×n.

In our case, writing out the function f(x) where x=[x1x2], we get f(x)=[x1x2]⋅[1131]⋅[x1x2]=[x1+3x2x1+x2]⋅[x1x2]=x12+3x1x2+x1x2+x22=x12+4x1x2+x22.

Therefore, we have
dfdx=[∂f∂x1∂f∂x2]=[∂∂x1(x12+4x1x2+x22)∂∂x2(x12+4x1x2+x22)]=[2x1+4x24x1+2x2]∈R1×2.
