Consider the function f, defined as f(x)=[x−1x2x3]. What is the shape of the total derivative of f with respect to x?

a
 a scalar
b
 a matrix of shape 3×3
c
 a matrix of shape 1×1
d
 a column vector of shape 3×1
e
 a row vector of shape 1×3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The total derivative of a vector-valued function f:R→Rm with respect to a scalar x is the column vector

dfdx=[∂f1∂x∂f2∂x⋮∂fm∂x]∈Rm×1.

In our case, the function

  • acts on a scalar x∈R, and

  • outputs a vector f(x)∈R3.

Therefore, the total derivative will be a column vector of shape 3×1.
