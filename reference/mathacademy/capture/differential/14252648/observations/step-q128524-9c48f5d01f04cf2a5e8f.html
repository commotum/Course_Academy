 (1+ln⁡(xyz))(x2 i+y2 j+z2 k)

 (1+ln⁡(xyz))(i+j+k)

 (1+ln⁡(xyz))((y+z) i+(x+z) j+(x+y) k)

Correct
EXPLANATION

The gradient of the product of two functions f(x,y) and h(x,y) is given by ∇[fh]=f∇h+h∇f.

Now, we calculate the partial derivatives of f(x,y,z):

∂f∂x=∂∂x(xyz)=yz∂f∂y=∂∂y(xyz)=xz∂f∂z=∂∂z(xyz)=xy

Hence, ∇f(x,y,z)=∂f∂x i+∂f∂y j+∂f∂z k=yz i+xz j+xy k.

Next, we calculate the partial derivatives of h(x,y,z):

∂h∂x=∂∂x(ln⁡(xyz))=1x∂h∂y=∂∂y(ln⁡(xyz))=1y∂h∂z=∂∂z(ln⁡(xyz))=1z

Therefore, ∇h(x,y,z)=∂h∂x i+∂h∂y j+∂h∂z k=1x i+1y j+1z k.
