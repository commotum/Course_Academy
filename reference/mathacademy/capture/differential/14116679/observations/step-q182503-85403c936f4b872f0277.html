Which of the following statements are true?

  1. A⇒B≡¬A∨B
  2. ¬(A⇒B)≡A∨¬B
  3. ¬(A⇒B)≡A∧¬B
a
I and III only
b
I only
c
III only
d
I and II only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of our statements:

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, A⇒B is logically equivalent to the disjunction ¬A∨B.

  • Statement III is true, while statement II is false. From part I, we have the following equivalence: A⇒B≡¬A∨B By negating this statement, we get ¬(A⇒B)≡¬(¬A∨B). Distributing the negation over the disjunction using De Morgan's law, we have ¬(¬A∨B)≡¬(¬A)∧¬B. Canceling out double negation, we reach A∧¬B. So, ¬(A⇒B) is equivalent to A∧¬B.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
