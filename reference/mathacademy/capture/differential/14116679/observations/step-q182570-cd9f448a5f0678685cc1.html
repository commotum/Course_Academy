Question 2
?

If you don't buy a ticket, then you cannot enter the concert.

Which statement is logically equivalent to the conditional statement above?

a
Don't buy a ticket, and you cannot enter the concert.
b
Buy a ticket, or you cannot enter the concert.
c
Buy a ticket, and you cannot enter the concert.
d
Don't buy a ticket, or you can enter the concert.
e
Buy a ticket, or you can enter the concert.
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's denote by A and B the following statements:

  • A: You don't buy a ticket.

  • B: You cannot enter the concert.

Then, our initial statement can be written as A⇒B, which is logically equivalent to

¬A∨B.

The negation of A can be written as

  • ¬A: You buy a ticket.

Therefore, the given statement is logically equivalent to the following:

You buy a ticket, or you cannot enter the concert.

We can write this in more everyday language as follows:

Buy a ticket, or you cannot enter the concert.
