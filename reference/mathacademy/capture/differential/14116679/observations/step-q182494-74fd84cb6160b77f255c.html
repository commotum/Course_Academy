Simplify the statement ¬(X⇒Y).

EXPLANATION

The conditional statement P⇒Q is logically equivalent to the disjunction ¬P∨Q:

So, in this case, we have the following equivalence:

¬(X⇒Y)≡¬(¬X∨Y)

Distributing the negation over the disjunction using De Morgan's law, we have

¬(¬X∨Y)≡¬(¬X)∧¬Y.

Canceling out double negation, we reach

X∧¬Y.

So the given statement is equivalent to X∧¬Y.
