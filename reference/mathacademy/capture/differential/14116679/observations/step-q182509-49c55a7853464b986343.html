If you follow the recipe, the dish will be delicious.

Which statement is logically equivalent to the conditional statement above?

a
Either you do not follow the recipe, or the dish will be delicious.
b
Either you follow the recipe, or the dish won't be delicious.
c
Either you follow the recipe, or the dish will be delicious.
d
Don't follow the recipe, or the dish won't be delicious.
e
Follow the recipe, and the dish won't be delicious.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's denote by A and B the following statements:

  • A: You follow the recipe.

  • B: The dish will be delicious.

Then, our initial statement can be written as A⇒B, which is logically equivalent to

¬A∨B.

The negation of A can be written as

  • ¬A: You do not follow the recipe.

Therefore, the given statement is logically equivalent to the following:

You do not follow the recipe, or the dish will be delicious.

We can write this in more everyday language as follows:

Either you do not follow the recipe, or the dish will be delicious.

Note:

Using the word "Either" helps clarify that the sentence structure presents two alternatives and is particularly useful when employing the disjunctive form (¬P∨Q) in conversational or formal English. So, the phrase effectively conveys that one of the two stated conditions will hold true.
