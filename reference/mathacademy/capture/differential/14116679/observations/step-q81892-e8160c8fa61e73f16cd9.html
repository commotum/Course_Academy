EXPLANATION

First, note that (¬B∧C)⇒(B∨¬C)≡¬(¬B∧C)∨(B∨¬C).

We can distribute the negation over the disjunction using De Morgan's law:

¬(¬B∧C)∨(B∨¬C)≡(¬(¬B)∨¬C)∨(B∨¬C)

Canceling out double negations, we reach

(¬(¬B)∨¬C)∨(B∨¬C)≡(B∨¬C)∨(B∨¬C).

Finally, we simplify the disjunction of a statement with itself:

(B∨¬C)∨(B∨¬C)≡B∨¬C

So the given statement is equivalent to B∨¬C.
