Consider the affine transformation T, defined as

[xy]=[3−1−11]⋅[uv]+[20].

What is the area scale factor of the transformation?

a
1
b
4
c
2
d
−3
e
0
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The matrix representation of the linear transformation associated with T is A=[3−1−11].

Computing the determinant of A, we get det(A)=|3−1−11|=3⋅1−(−1)⋅(−1)=2.

So, the area scale factor of the transformation is |det(A)|=|2|=2.
