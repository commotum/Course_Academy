Identify the following surface: (x+2)2=−y,−∞<z<∞

a
A plane
b
A hyperbolic paraboloid
c
An elliptic cylinder
d
An elliptic paraboloid
e
A parabolic cylinder
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the equation of a parabolic cylinder, whose axis is the z-axis, is given by

x2=4py,

and a parabolic cylinder, shifted x0 along the x-axis and y0 along the y-axis, is given by

(x−x0)2=4p(y−y0).

Thus, our equation is a parabolic cylinder with the following parameters:

(x0,y0)=(−2,0),p=−14.
