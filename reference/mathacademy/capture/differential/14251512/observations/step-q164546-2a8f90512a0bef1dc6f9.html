Identify the following surface: z2=8x,−∞<y<∞.

a
A hyperbolic cylinder whose axis is the x-axis
b
A couple of planes
c
A parabolic cylinder whose axis is the y-axis
d
A hyperbolic cylinder whose axis is the y-axis
e
A parabolic cylinder whose axis is the x-axis
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that the equation of a parabolic cylinder whose axis is the z-axis is given by

x2=4py.

Thus, a parabolic cylinder whose axis is the y-axis is given by

z2=4px.

Therefore, our equation is a parabolic cylinder whose axis is the y-axis and p=2.
