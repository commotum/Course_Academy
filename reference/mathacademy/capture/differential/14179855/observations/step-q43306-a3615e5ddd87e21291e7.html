Let A be a 2×2 matrix such that |A|=0. Which of the following statements must be true?

  1. A has two identical rows
  2. One of the rows/columns of A is proportional to another row/column of A
  3. A has two identical columns
a
 II only
b
 I only
c
 I and III only
d
 I and II only
e
 I, II, and III
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's consider our statements in turn.

  • Statements I and III are false. For example, consider the matrix A=[1428]. We have that |A|=1⋅8−4⋅2=0 but A has neither two identical rows nor two identical columns.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, we can make use of the following fact:

    |A|=0 if and only if one of the rows/columns of A is a linear combination of other rows/columns of A.

    In the case of a 2×2 matrix, this means that one of the rows/columns must be proportional to another row/column.

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only".
