In this case, the dominant term in the denominator is x2. So, we divide every term in the numerator and denominator by x2, as follows: f(x)=7x−9x23x2+8x−5=(7xx2−9x2x2)(3x2x2+8xx2−5x2)=(7x−9)(3+8x−5x2)
