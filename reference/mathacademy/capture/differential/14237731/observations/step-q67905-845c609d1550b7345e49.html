A leaking paddling pool loses 6 liters of water in 23 hours. How much water will it lose in 1 hour?

a
8 liters
b
7 liters
c
4 liters
d
9 liters
e
36 liters
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find out how much water the pool will lose in 1 hour, we can compute the unit rate of liters of water lost per hour. We do this by dividing the number of liters, 6, by the number of hours, 23, as follows:

6÷23

To divide by a fraction, we multiply by the fraction's reciprocal:

6÷23=6×32=61×32=6×31×2=182=9

Therefore, the pool will lose 9 liters of water in 1 hour.
