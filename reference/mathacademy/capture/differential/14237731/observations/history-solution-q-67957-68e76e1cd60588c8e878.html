To compute the unit rate of miles per minute, we divide the number of miles, 45, by the number of minutes, 25, as follows: 45÷25

Dividing a fraction by another fraction is equivalent to multiplying the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction:

45÷25=45×52=4×55×2=2×2×55×2=2×5×22×5=2

Therefore, the unit rate is 2 miles per minute.
