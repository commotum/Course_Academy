To compute the unit rate of laps per minute, we divide the number of laps, 45, by the number of seconds, 2, as follows:

45÷2

Dividing a fraction by a whole number is equivalent to multiplying the fraction by the reciprocal of that whole number:

45÷2=45÷21=45×12=4×15×2=2×22×5=2×22×5=25

Therefore, the unit rate in this situation is 25 laps per minute.
