Suzie takes 23 hours to read 22 pages of a certain book. How many pages can she read in 1 hour?

a
3134 pages
b
30 pages
c
3212 pages
d
38 pages
e
33 pages
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the number of pages Suzie can read in 1 hour, we compute the unit rate of pages per hour. We do this by dividing the number of pages Suzie reads, 22, by the number of hours, 23, as follows:

22÷23

Dividing a fraction by a whole number is equivalent to multiplying the fraction by the reciprocal of that whole number:

22÷23=22×32=221×32=22×31×2=662=33

Therefore, Suzie can read 33 pages in 1 hour.
