To find the number of exercises Owen can do in 1 hour, we compute the unit rate of exercises per hour. We do this by dividing the number of exercises Owen does, 24, by the number of hours, 45, as follows:

24÷45

Dividing a fraction by a whole number is equivalent to multiplying the fraction by the reciprocal of that whole number:

24÷45=24×54=241×54=24×51×4=1204=30
