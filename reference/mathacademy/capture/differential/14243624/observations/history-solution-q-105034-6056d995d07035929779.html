A is a subset of B if every element of A is an element of B.

With that in mind, let's inspect the given choices.

  • Q− is a subset of R. Every negative rational number is a real number.

  • Z+ is a subset of R. Every positive integer number is a real number.

  • N a subset of R. Every natural number is a real number.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II and III".
