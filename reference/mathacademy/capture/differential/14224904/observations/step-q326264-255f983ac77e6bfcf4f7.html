The parametric equations of an ellipse centered at the origin with a horizontal radius of length a and a vertical radius of length b are x=acos⁡θ,y=bsin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

Under this parametrization, the ellipse is traversed once in the counterclockwise direction, starting from (a,0).

In our case, a=6 and b=5. Therefore, the parametric equations of the ellipse are
x=6cos⁡θ,y=5sin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).
