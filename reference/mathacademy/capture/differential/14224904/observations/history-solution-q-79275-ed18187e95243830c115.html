The parametric equations of an ellipse centered at the origin with a horizontal radius of length a and a vertical radius of length b are x=acos⁡θ,y=bsin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

In our case, a=2 and b=4. Therefore, the parametric equations of the full ellipse are
x=2cos⁡θ,y=4sin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

To restrict the parameterization to a semi-ellipse, we associate the parameter θ with the central angle formed by a point (x,y) on the elipse and the x-axis.

We can see that if we restrict θ to [0,π], we get the semi-ellipse.

Therefore, the parametric equations of the semi-ellipse are x=2cos⁡θ,y=4sin⁡θ,θ∈[0,π].
