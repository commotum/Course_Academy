EXPLANATION

To find the Cartesian equation of the ellipse, we need to eliminate the parameter θ. We can do this using the Pythagorean theorem.

By isolating the cos⁡θ term in the x-equation and squaring, we get x=62cos⁡θx62=cos⁡θx2(62)2=cos2⁡θx272=cos2⁡θ.

By isolating the sin⁡θ term in the y-equation and squaring, we obtain y=11sin⁡θy11=sin⁡θy2112=sin2⁡θy2121=sin2⁡θ.

Adding the two equations and using cos2⁡θ+sin2⁡θ=1, we get x272+y2121=cos2⁡θ+sin2⁡θ=1.

So, the Cartesian equation of the ellipse is x272+y2121=1.
