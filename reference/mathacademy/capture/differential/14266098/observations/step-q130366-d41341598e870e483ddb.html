Given that det(A)=5, which of the following statements are true?

  1. det(AT)=15
  2. 5det(AT)=1
  3. det(AT)−3det(I)=2
a
I and III only
b
II and III only
c
III only
d
I and II only
e
I only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

From the properties of determinants, we know that
det(A)=det(AT)=5 and det(I)=1.

With that in mind, let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is false. We have, det(AT)=5≠15.

  • Statement II is false. We have 5det(AT)=5⋅5=25≠1.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, det(AT)−3det(I)=5−3⋅1=2.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "III only".
