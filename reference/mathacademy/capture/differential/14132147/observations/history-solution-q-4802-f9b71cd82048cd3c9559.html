The tangent line approximation of f(x) at the point a=2 is L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)=f(2)+f′(2)(x−2).

We're told that f(2)=−1, and since f′(x)=5x−3, then f′(2)=5(2)−3=7. So, the tangent line approximation is given by L(x)=−1+7(x−2).

Now, we can approximate f(2.4) by evaluating the tangent line approximation at x=2.4. We get f(2.4)≈L(2.4)=−1+7(2.4−2)=−1+7(0.4)=−1+2.8=1.8.
