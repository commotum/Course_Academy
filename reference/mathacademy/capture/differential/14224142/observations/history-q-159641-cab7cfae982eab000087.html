3.Applications of Confidence Intervals?

It is known that the thickness of ceramic tiles from suppliers X and Y are normally distributed with standard deviations of σx=1.6mm and σy=1.2mm, respectively. A construction contractor wants to determine whether the population mean μx of the thickness of ceramic tiles from supplier X is greater than the population mean μy of the thickness of ceramic tiles from supplier Y. He constructs a 95% confidence interval for the difference between the population means.

Given that the contractor sampled 20 tiles from supplier X and 18 tiles from supplier Y and found that their mean thicknesses are 18.8mm and 18.5mm, respectively, which of the following statements are true?

  1. The end-points of the 95% confidence interval for the difference between the population means are approximately 0.3±0.894.
  2. All values contained within the 95% confidence interval are positive.
  3. The confidence interval data suggests that μx>μy.
