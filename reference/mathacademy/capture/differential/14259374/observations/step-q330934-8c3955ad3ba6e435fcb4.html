EXPLANATION

We're given that Z∼N(0,1), and we wish to compute P(−2.20<Z<2.10). The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

By the definition of the cumulative distribution function Φ(z),

P(Z≤z)=Φ(z).

So, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(−2.20<Z<2.10)=P(Z<2.10)−P(Z≤−2.20)=P(Z≤2.10)−P(Z≤−2.20)=Φ(2.10)−Φ(−2.20)

From the table, we know that

Φ(−2.20)=0.0139,Φ(−2.10)=0.0179,

but we are not given Φ(2.10). However, we can compute this using symmetry:

Φ(2.10)=P(Z≤2.10)=1−P(Z≤−2.10)=1−Φ(−2.10)=1−0.0179=0.9821

Therefore, we have

P(−2.20<Z<2.10)=Φ(2.10)−Φ(−2.20)=0.9821−0.0139=0.9682.
