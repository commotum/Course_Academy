EXPLANATION

We're given that Z∼N(0,1), and we wish to compute P(−1.55<Z<1.55). The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

By the definition of the cumulative distribution function Φ(z),

P(Z≤z)=Φ(z).

So, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(−1.55<Z<1.55)=P(Z<1.55)−P(Z≤−1.55)=P(Z≤1.55)−P(Z≤−1.55)=Φ(1.55)−Φ(−1.55)

From the table, we know that

Φ(1.55)=0.9394,

but we are not given Φ(−1.55). However, we can compute this using symmetry:

Φ(−1.55)=P(Z≤−1.55)=P(Z≥1.55)=1−P(Z<1.55)=1−P(Z≤1.55)=1−Φ(1.55)=1−0.9394=0.0606

Therefore, we have

P(−1.55<Z<1.55)=Φ(1.55)−Φ(−1.55)=0.9394−0.0606=0.8788.
