EXPLANATION

We're given that Z∼N(0,1), and we wish to compute P(−1.54<Z<1.54). The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

By the definition of the cumulative distribution function Φ(z),

P(Z≤z)=Φ(z).

So, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(−1.54<Z<1.54)=P(Z<1.54)−P(Z≤−1.54)=P(Z≤1.54)−P(Z≤−1.54)=Φ(1.54)−Φ(−1.54)

From the table, we know that

Φ(−1.54)=0.0618,

but we are not given Φ(1.54). However, we can compute this using symmetry:

Φ(1.54)=P(Z≤1.54)=1−P(Z≤−1.54)=1−Φ(−1.54)=1−0.0618=0.9382

Therefore, we have

P(−1.54<Z<1.54)=Φ(1.54)−Φ(−1.54)=0.9382−0.0618=0.8764.
