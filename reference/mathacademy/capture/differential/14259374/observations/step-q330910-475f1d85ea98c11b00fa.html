EXPLANATION

We're given that Z∼N(0,1), and we wish to compute P(−2.25<Z<2.25). The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

By the definition of the cumulative distribution function Φ(z),

P(Z≤z)=Φ(z).

So, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(−2.25<Z<2.25)=P(Z<2.25)−P(Z≤−2.25)=P(Z≤2.25)−P(Z≤−2.25)=Φ(2.25)−Φ(−2.25)

From the table, we know that

Φ(−2.25)=0.0122,

but we are not given Φ(2.25). However, we can compute this using symmetry:

Φ(2.25)=P(Z≤2.25)=1−P(Z≤−2.25)=1−Φ(−2.25)=1−0.0122=0.9878

Therefore, we have

P(−2.25<Z<2.25)=Φ(2.25)−Φ(−2.25)=0.9878−0.0122=0.9756.
