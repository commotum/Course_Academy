Which of the following sets are open?

  1. [0,15]
  2. (−3,5]∪[6,8)
  3. (1,6)
a
II only
b
I and II only
c
III only

II and III only
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that:

  • A set is open if it does not contain any of its boundary points. In other words, an open set consists only of the interior points of the set.

  • A set is closed if it contains all of its boundary points. In other words, a closed set consists of all the interior and all the boundary points of the set.

Among the given sets, only (1,6) is open. The boundary points are 1, and 6. None of them lie in the set.

The other sets are not open because:

  • [0,15] has two boundary points 0, and 15, and both of them lie in the set.

  • (−3,5]∪[6,8) has four boundary points −3, 5, 6, and 8, and the points 5 and 6 lie in the set.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only".
