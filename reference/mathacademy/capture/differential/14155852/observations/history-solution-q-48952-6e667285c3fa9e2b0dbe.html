The quotient rule for derivatives gives

q′(x)=f′(x)⋅g(x)−f(x)⋅g′(x)(g(x))2.

As a result, we have that

q′(32)=f′(32)⋅g(32)−f(32)⋅g′(32)(g(32))2.

From the graph above, we obtain that f(32)=1 and g(32)=−1.

Additionally:

  • The slope of f(x) on x∈(1,3) equals 0. So, f′(32)=0.

  • The slope of g(x) on x∈(1,3) equals 1. So, g′(32)=1.

Finally, we have

q′(32)=f′(32)⋅g(32)−f(32)⋅g′(32)(g(32))2=0⋅(−1)−1⋅1(−1)2=−1.
