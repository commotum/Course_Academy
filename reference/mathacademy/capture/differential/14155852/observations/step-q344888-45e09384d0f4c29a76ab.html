EXPLANATION

The addition and constant multiple rules for derivatives give h′(x)=2f′(x)−g′(x).

As a result, we have that h′(1)=2f′(1)−g′(1).

Also, notice that x=1 corresponds to

  • the interval x∈(0,3) for the function f, and

  • the interval x∈(−1,2) for the function g,

as shown below.

  • The slope of y=f(x) is constant on x∈(0,3) and equals ΔfΔx=1−43−0=−1. Therefore, f′(1)=−1.

  • The slope of y=g(x) is constant on x∈(−1,2) and equals ΔgΔx=1−(−2)2−(−1)=1. Therefore, g′(1)=1.

Finally, we have h′(1)=2f′(1)−g′(1)=2(−1)−1=−3.
