EXPLANATION

The product rule for derivatives gives

h′(x)=f′(x)⋅g(x)+f(x)⋅g′(x).

As a result, we have that

h′(2)=f′(2)⋅g(2)+f(2)⋅g′(2).

From the graphs above, we obtain that f(2)=4 and g(2)=3.

Additionally,

  • The slope of f(x) on x∈(−2,4) equals 0. So, f′(2)=0.

  • The slope of g(x) on x∈(−4,4) equals 12. So, g′(2)=12.

Therefore,

h′(2)=f′(2)⋅g(2)+f(2)⋅g′(2)=0⋅3+4⋅12=2.
