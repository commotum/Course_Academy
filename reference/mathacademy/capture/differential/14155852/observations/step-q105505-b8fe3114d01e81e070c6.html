EXPLANATION

The quotient rule for derivatives gives

h′(x)=f′(x)⋅g(x)−f(x)⋅g′(x)(g(x))2.

As a result, we have that

h′(1)=f′(1)⋅g(1)−f(1)⋅g′(1)(g(1))2.

From the graph above, we obtain that f(1)=0 and g(1)=−1.

Additionally:

  • The slope of f(x) on x∈(−5,5) equals 12. So, f′(1)=12.

  • The slope of g(x) on x∈(−1,2) equals −3. So, g′(1)=−3

Finally, we have

h′(1)=f′(1)⋅g(1)−f(1)⋅g′(1)(g(1))2=12⋅(−1)−0⋅(−3)(−1)2=(−12)1=−12.
