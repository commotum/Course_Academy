Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the sample mean vector is given by

m=1n∑i=1nxi,

where x1,x2,…,xn are the individual observations (columns of X).

From the matrix, we have that

x1=[−17],x2=[010],x3=[−38],x4=[−23].

Therefore, the sample mean is

m=14(x1+x2+x3+x4)=14([−17]+[010]+[−38]+[−23])=14[−628]=[−327].
