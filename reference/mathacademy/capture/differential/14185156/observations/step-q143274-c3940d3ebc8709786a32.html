Given that X1,X2,…,X72∼N(−1,122) is a random sample of size n=72 from a normal population, what is the distribution of the sample mean X―?

a
N(−1,6)
b
N(−1,4)
c
N(1,2)
d
N(1,6)
e
N(−1,2)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

If X1,X2,…,Xn is a random sample of size n from a normal population, where Xi∼N(μ,σ2),1≤i≤n, then the sample mean X― has the distribution X―∼N(μ,σ2n).

In our case, we have n=72,μ=−1,σ=12.

Therefore, the distribution of the sample mean X― is X―∼N(−1,12272)∼N(−1,2).
