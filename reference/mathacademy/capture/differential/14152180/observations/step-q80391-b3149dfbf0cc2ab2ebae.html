D=[800−2] and P=[4232]

D=[−2008] and P=[4232]
e

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the eigenvalues of the matrix are λ1=−2 and λ2=8, we construct the diagonal matrix D by writing the eigenvalues in the main diagonal:

D=[−2008].

Now, to construct the matrix P, we simply write the respective eigenvectors as columns:

  • the 1st column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ1=−2, and

  • the 2nd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ2=8.

Therefore, we get

P=[4232].
