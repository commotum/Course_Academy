Let δ>0. A neighborhood of a point x0 is the set of points whose distance from x0 is less than δ. {x:|x−x0|<δ}

In two-dimensional space, a neighborhood of x0 is the open disc |x−x0|<δ, as shown below.

Furthermore, we have the following definitions:

  • A point x0 is an interior point of a set S if S contains some neighborhood of x0. The set of all interior points of S is called the interior of S.

  • A point x0 is a boundary point of a set S if every neighborhood of x0 contains points that are in S and points that are not in S. The set of all boundary points of S is called the boundary of S.
