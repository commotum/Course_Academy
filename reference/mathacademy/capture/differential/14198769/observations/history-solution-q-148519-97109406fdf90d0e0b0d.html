Recall that the first principal component of the data corresponds to the direction in which the data varies the most (the direction where we have the maximum variance).

The second principal component of the data corresponds to the direction perpendicular to the first principal component in which the data varies the most.

In our diagram, the direction where the variance changes the most corresponds (approximately) to the vector a or to the vector c, as shown below. So, a or c define the direction of the first principal component.
