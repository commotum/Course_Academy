EXPLANATION

Recall that the unit eigenvectors of the covariance matrix C are called the principal components of the data. If

λ1>λ2>λ3

are the eigenvalues of C written in descending order, then:

  • the 1st principal component is the unit eigenvector v1 corresponding to the 1st eigenvalue λ1,

  • the 2nd principal component is the unit eigenvector v2 corresponding to the 2nd eigenvalue λ2,

  • the 3rd principal component is the unit eigenvector v3 corresponding to the 3rd eigenvalue λ3.

Notice that the covariance matrix corresponding to X is

C=1n−1XTX=1n−1(UΣVT)T(UΣVT)=1n−1VΣT(UTU)ΣVT=V(1n−1ΣTΣ)VT,

where the final expression represents the diagonal form of the covariance matrix C. Notice that:

  • 1n−1ΣTΣ is a diagonal 3×3 matrix, where the entries on the main diagonal are the eigenvalues (written in descending order) of the covariance matrix C, and

  • V is a 3×3 matrix, whose columns are the corresponding unit eigenvectors of the covariance matrix C.

Therefore, the columns of V are the principal components of the data. So, the first principal component is [235535435].
