EXPLANATION

A definite integral can be defined as the limit of a left Riemann sum as follows: ∫abf(x)dx=limn→∞∑k=0n−1f(xk)⋅Δx where Δx=b−an and xk=a+kΔx.

Notice that we're given that the lower limit a=2.

Comparing the given summation with the general expression above, we see that Δx=4n. Therefore, 4n=b−2n⟹b=6.

Consequently, xk=2+4kn and f(xk)=5xk3.

Hence, ∫265x3dx=limn→∞∑k=0n−1[5(2+4kn)3⋅4n].

Therefore, the correct answer is f(x)=5x3andb=6.
