The definite integral can be defined in terms of the limit of a left Riemann sum as ∫abf(x)dx=limn→∞∑i=0n−1f(xi)⋅Δx,

where Δx=b−an and xi=a+iΔx.

the function is f(x)=2x+3, the lower bound is a=1, and the upper bound is b=7. So, we have Δx=7−1n=6n,
