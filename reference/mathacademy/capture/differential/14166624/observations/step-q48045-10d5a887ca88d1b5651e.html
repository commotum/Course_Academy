Given that the tangent line approximation to the function f(x) at x=3 underestimates the function in the neighborhood of x=3, which of the following must be true?

a
f(x) is increasing in the neighborhood of x=3
b
f(x) is concave up in the neighborhood of x=3
c
f(x) has a local minimum at x=3
d
f(x) is concave down in the neighborhood of x=3
e
f(x) has an inflection point at x=3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the tangent line approximation of f(x) at x=3 underestimates the values of this function in the neighborhood of x=3, we have L(x)<f(x),

for all x close to (but not equal to) x=3. In other words, the line L(x) must be below the graph of f(x) near the point x=3.

Therefore, the function must be concave up in the neighborhood of x=3.
