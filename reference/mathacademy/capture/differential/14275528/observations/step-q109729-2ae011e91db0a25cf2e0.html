EXPLANATION

Recall that if X1 and X2 are independent random variables, where X1∼N(μ1,σ12) and X2∼N(μ2,σ22), and a and b are constants, then

aX1+bX2∼N(aμ1+bμ2,a2σ12+b2σ22).

Since X1 and X2 are independent, the distribution of Y is given by Y=X1−X2∼N(μ1−μ2,σ12+σ22)∼N(20−2,200+25)∼N(18,225).

Therefore, Y is normally distributed with mean μ=18 and standard deviation σ=225=15.

We want to find P(Y>1.8). Thus, we convert Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(Y>1.8)=P(Z>1.8−1815)=P(Z>−1.08)=1−Φ(−1.08)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(−1.08)≈0.1401.

Therefore,

P(Y>1.8)=1−Φ(−1.08)=1−0.1401≈0.86.
