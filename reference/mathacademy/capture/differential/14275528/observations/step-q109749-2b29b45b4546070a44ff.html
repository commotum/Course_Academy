In the same region, the heights of adult females are normally distributed with mean 65in and standard deviation 3.5in. EXPLANATION

Let X denote the height of an adult male, and let Y denote the height of an adult female. We want to find

P(X<Y)=P(X−Y<0).

Since X and Y are independent random variables such that X∼N(70,42) and Y∼N(65,3.52), then

X−Y∼N(70−65,42+3.52)∼N(5,28.25).

So, the variable X−Y is normally distributed with mean μ=5 and standard deviation σ=28.25.

To find P(X−Y<0), we convert X−Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(X−Y<0)=P(Z<0−528.25)≈P(Z<−0.94)=P(Z≤−0.94)=Φ(−0.94)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(−0.94)≈0.1736.

Therefore,

P(X−Y<0)≈Φ(−0.94)≈0.1736.

So, we conclude that the probability that a woman is taller than a man is 0.1736.
