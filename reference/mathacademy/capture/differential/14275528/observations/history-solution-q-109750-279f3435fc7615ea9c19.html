Let X denote the weight of the soda, and let Y denote the weight of the can. We want to find

P(X+Y>360).

Since X and Y are independent random variables such that X∼N(340,4) and Y∼N(25,1), then

X+Y∼N(340+25,4+1)∼N(365,5).

So, the variable X+Y is normally distributed with mean μ=365 and standard deviation σ=5.

In order to find P(X+Y>360), we convert X+Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(X+Y>360)=P(Z>360−3655)≈P(Z>−2.24)=1−P(Z≤−2.24)=1−Φ(−2.24)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(−2.24)≈0.0125.

Therefore,

P(X+Y>360)≈1−Φ(−2.24)≈1−0.0125≈0.9875.

So, we conclude that the probability that a randomly selected can with soda weighs more than 360 grams is 0.9875.
