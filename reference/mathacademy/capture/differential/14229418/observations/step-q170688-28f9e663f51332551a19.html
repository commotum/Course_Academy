The earliest that a football fan arrives at a match is 15 minutes before the beginning of the match, and the latest that they arrive is 5 minutes late. If the arrival times are uniformly distributed, what is the mean arrival time?

a
15 minutes before the beginning of the match
b
5 minutes before the beginning of the match
c
5 minutes after the beginning of the match
d
10 minutes before the beginning of the match
e
3 minutes after the beginning of the match
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let X represent the arrival time of a randomly chosen fan relative to the beginning of the match. From the problem statement, we know that X∼U[−15,5], and we wish to compute E[X].

In general, if X∼U[a,b], then X has the following expected value:

E[X]=a+b2

So, for our random variable X∼U[−15,5], we have the following expected value:

E[X]=−15+52=−5

Therefore, the mean arrival time is 5 minutes before the beginning of the match.
