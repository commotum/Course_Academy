The minimum time it takes a pizzeria to deliver an order is 15 minutes, and the maximum time is 27 minutes. If the delivery time is uniformly distributed, what is the variance in the delivery time?

a
12min2
b
27min2
c
17min2
d
15min2
e
14min2
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let X represent the time it takes to deliver a randomly selected order. From the problem statement, we know that X∼U[15,27], and we wish to compute Var[X].

In general, if X∼U[a,b], then X has the following variance:

Var[X]=(b−a)212

So, for our random variable X∼U[15,27], we have the following variance:

Var[X]=(27−15)212=14412=12

Therefore, the variance of the delivery time is 12min2.
