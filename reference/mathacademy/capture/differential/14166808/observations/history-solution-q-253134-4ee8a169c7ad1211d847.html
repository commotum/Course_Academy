The supremum of A is the smallest upper bound of A.

To find the supremum of this set, we must find the set containing all upper bounds of A (i.e., the set of upper bounds) and take the minimum of this set.

The elements of A are the terms of the sequence an, given by an=(−1)n⋅1n+1,n∈N={1,2,3,4,…}.

We see that an is an oscillating sequence converging to 0. The maximum of A is max(A)=a2=(−1)2⋅12+1=13.

So, in our case, we have that

  • any number x≥a2 is an upper bound of A,

  • any number x<a2 cannot be an upper bound because a2, an element of A, is greater than x.

Therefore, [13,∞) is the set of upper bounds of A, and

sup(A)=min[13,∞)=13. Notice that 13 is the maximum of A. Therefore, we conclude that

sup(A)=13∈A.
