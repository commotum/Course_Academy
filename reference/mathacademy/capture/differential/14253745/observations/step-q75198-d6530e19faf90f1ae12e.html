Find a tangent vector to the curve r(t)=sec⁡ti+tan⁡tj+tk at the point where t=π4.

a
2i+2j+k
b
2i+j+k
c
2i+2j+k
d
2i+2j+k

2i−2j+k
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

At the point where t=π4, the vector r′(π4) is tangent to the curve.

Let's calculate r′(t) and evaluate it at t=π4: r′(t)=ddt(sec⁡t)i+ddt(tan⁡t)j+ddt(t)k=sec⁡ttan⁡ti+sec2⁡tj+kr′(π4)=sec⁡π4tan⁡π4i+sec2⁡π4j+k=2i+2j+k

Therefore, a tangent vector to the given curve at the point where t=π4 is 2i+2j+k.
