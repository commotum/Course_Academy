Which of the following gives the equation of the tangent to the curve r(t)=eti+3tj+cos⁡2tk at the point corresponding to t=π, valid for all real values of u?

a
eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+j+2k)
b
eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+3j+k)
c
eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+j)
d
eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+3j+2k)
e
eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+3j)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

At the point where t=π, the vector r′(π) is tangent to the curve, so the tangent line to the curve at P can be parametrized as R(u)=r(π)+ur′(π),u∈(−∞,∞)

First, we find r(π): r(π)=eπi+3(π)j+cos⁡2(π)k=eπi+3πj+k

Now, we calculate r′(t):

r′(t)=ddt(et)i+ddt(3t)j+ddt(cos⁡2t)k=eti+3j−2sin⁡2tk

Then, we evaluate r′(t) at t=π:

r′(π)=e(π)i+3j+2sin⁡2(π)k=eπi+3j

Finally, the tangent line to the given curve at the point where t=π is R(u)=eπi+3πj+k+u(eπi+3j),u∈(−∞,∞).
