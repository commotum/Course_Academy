Let the function f(x,y) be defined as f(x,y)={x+y,if0<x<2,−1<y<0,sin⁡(x+y),if0<x<2,0<y<1. Then, ∬(0,2)×(−1,1)f(x,y) dA=

a
∬(0,2)(x+y)dA+∬(−1,1)sin⁡(x+y)dA
b
∬(−1,1)(x+y)dA+∬(0,2)sin⁡(x+y)dA
c
∬(0,2)×(−1,0)(x+y)dA+∬(0,2)×(0,1)sin⁡(x+y)dA
d
∬(0,1)×(−1,1)(x+y)dA+∬(1,2)×(−1,1)sin⁡(x+y)dA

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, notice that the rectangle R can be represented as a union of two smaller rectangles, as follows:

R=(0,2)×((−1,0)∪(0,1))=((0,2)×(−1,0))∪((0,2)×(0,1)).

So, we can write

∬Rf(x,y)dA=∬(0,2)×(−1,0)f(x,y)dA+∬(0,2)×(0,1)f(x,y)dA=∬(0,2)×(−1,0)(x+y)dA+∬(0,2)×(0,1)sin⁡(x+y)dA.
