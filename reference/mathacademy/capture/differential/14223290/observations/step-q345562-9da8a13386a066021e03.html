U=[||u1⋯un||],V=[||v1⋯vm||] Consider the matrices U and V above. Let X be an n×m standardized observation matrix, where each row represents one observation and each column represents one feature. Given that X=UΣVT is a singular value decomposition of X, what is the second principal component of the data?

a
v2
b
u2
c
v2Tv2
d
v2v2T
e
u2u2T
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the unit eigenvectors of the covariance matrix C are called the principal components of the data. If

λ1>λ2>⋯

are the eigenvalues of C written in descending order, then:

  • the 1st principal component is the unit eigenvector v1 corresponding to the 1st eigenvalue λ1,

  • the 2nd principal component is the unit eigenvector v2 corresponding to the 2nd eigenvalue λ2,

  • ⋯

and so on.

Notice that the covariance matrix corresponding to X is

C=1n−1XTX=1n−1(UΣVT)T(UΣVT)=1n−1VΣT(UTU)ΣVT=V(1n−1ΣTΣ)VT,

where the final expression represents the diagonal form of the covariance matrix C. Notice that:

  • 1n−1ΣTΣ is a diagonal m×m matrix, where the entries on the main diagonal are the eigenvalues (written in descending order) of the covariance matrix C, and

  • V is a m×m matrix, whose columns are the corresponding unit eigenvectors of the covariance matrix C.

Therefore, the columns of V are the principal components of the data. So, the second principal component is v2.
