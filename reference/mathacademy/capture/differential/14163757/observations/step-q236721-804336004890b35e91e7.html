EXPLANATION

We find the ranges of the individual pieces and then calculate their union:

  • The range of the branch defined on x∈(−3,−1) is (−3,−1)

  • The range of the branch defined on x∈[−1,3) is [−2,2)

Therefore, the range of f(x) is (−3,−1)∪[−2,2)=(−3,2), which can also be written as −3<f(x)<2.

Note: From the graph, we see that the values y=−3 and y=2 are NOT valid outputs of the function y=f(x). Therefore, we use "less than" (<) instead of "less than or equal to" (≤) at these endpoints.
