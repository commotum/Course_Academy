EXPLANATION

We find the ranges of the individual pieces, and then calculate their union:

  • The range of the branch defined on x∈(−4,−2) is (−2,1)

  • The range of the branch defined on x∈[2,4) is [2,4)

Therefore, the range of f(x) is (−2,1)∪[2,4).

Note: From the graph, we see that the values y=−2, y=1 and y=4 are NOT valid outputs of the function y=f(x). Therefore, we use parentheses (…) instead of brackets […] at these endpoints.
