Recall that every quadratic form defined on R2 has an associated 2×2 symmetric matrix.

With that in mind, let's examine each matrix in turn.

  • F cannot be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R2. Although it's a 2×2 matrix, F is not symmetric since f12≠f21.

  • G could be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R2 since it is a 2×2 symmetric matrix.

  • H cannot be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R2 since it's a 3×3 matrix (not 2×2).

Therefore, the correct answer is "G only."
