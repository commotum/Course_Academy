Recall that every quadratic form defined on R3 has an associated 3×3 symmetric matrix.

With that in mind, let's examine each matrix in turn.

  • H could be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3 since it is a 3×3 symmetric matrix.

  • J could also be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3 since it is a 3×3 symmetric matrix.

  • K cannot be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3. Although it's a 3×3 matrix, K is not symmetric since k23≠k32.

Therefore, the correct answer is "H and J only."
