Recall that every quadratic form defined on R3 has an associated 3×3 symmetric matrix.

With that in mind, let's examine each matrix in turn.

  • R cannot be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3 since it's a 2×2 matrix (not 3×3).

  • S cannot be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3. Although it's a 3×3 matrix, S is not symmetric since s23≠s32.

  • T could be a matrix of a quadratic form defined on R3 since it is a 3×3 symmetric matrix.

Therefore, the correct answer is "T only."
