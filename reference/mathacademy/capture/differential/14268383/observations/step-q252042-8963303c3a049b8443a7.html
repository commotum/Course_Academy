EXPLANATION

If Zi∼N(0,1) for 1≤i≤k are independent normal random variables, then the sum Z12+Z22+⋯+Zk2 follows a χ2(k) distribution with k degrees of freedom.

So, in our case, we have

Y=Z12+Z22+⋯+Z242∼χ2(24).

First, we express the given probability in the form P(Y≥y):

P(Y≤y)=1−P(Y≥y)0.95=1−P(Y≥y)P(Y≥y)=0.05

Now, we wish to find the value y such that P(Y≥y)=0.05 This can be found from the given table:

k∖p 0.010 0.050 0.100 0.150 0.200
24 42.980 36.415 33.196 31.132 29.553
25 44.314 37.652 34.382 32.282 30.675

So, we have

P(Y≥36.415)=0.05 which means P(Y≤36.415)=0.95.

Therefore, y=36.415.
