a
 y is irrational or negative.
b
 y is negative.
c
 y is irrational or negative but not both.
d
 y is irrational and negative.

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we simplify the phrase "y is irrational and it is an irrational number" to just "y is irrational." The sentence now reads

"y is irrational, or y is irrational or it is a negative number."

Then, using the associative law, we can move the comma in the sentence:

"y is irrational or y is irrational, or it is a negative number."

Now, we simplify the phrase "y is irrational or y is irrational" to just "y is irrational." The sentence now reads

"y is irrational, or it is a negative number."

Finally, we simplify the sentence to

"y is irrational or negative."
