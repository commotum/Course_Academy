Since A is a lower triangular matrix, we have that its eigenvalues are the entries in the main diagonal, that is, λ1=3, λ2=−2 and λ3=−3.

Also, since we obtained three distinct eigenvalues, the set of eigenvectors {v1,v2,v3}, where each vector corresponds to different eigenvalue, will be linearly independent.

Now, recall that v1 and v2 correspond to the eigenvalues λ1=3 and λ2=−2. Thus, the third eigenvector v3 must correspond to λ3=−3.

To compute the eigenvectors of a matrix, we need to find non-zero solutions of the matrix equation (A−λI)x=0.
