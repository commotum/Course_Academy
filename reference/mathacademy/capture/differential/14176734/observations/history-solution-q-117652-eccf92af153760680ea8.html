The solid is a rectangular pyramid since it has a rectangular base and the other 4 triangular faces meet at a single point.

Also, notice that:

  • The solid is not a cube since not all of its faces are squares.

  • The solid is not a triangular prism because it does not have two triangular bases connected by parallel line segments.

  • The solid is not a triangular pyramid since its base is not triangular.

  • The solid is not a heptagonal pyramid since its base is not a heptagon.
