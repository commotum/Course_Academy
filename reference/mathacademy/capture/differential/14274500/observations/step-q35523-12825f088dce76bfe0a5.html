What is the trace in the xy-plane of the ellipsoid (x−1)216+(y−3)28+(z+1)22=1?

a
An ellipse with semi-axes 8 and 2, centered at (−1,3) in the xy-plane
b
An ellipse with semi-axes 22 and 2, centered at (−1,−3) in the xy-plane
c
An ellipse with semi-axes 4 and 2, centered at (1,−3) in the xy-plane
d
An ellipse with semi-axes 22 and 2, centered at (1,3) in the xy-plane
e
An ellipse with semi-axes 8 and 4, centered at (1,3) in the xy-plane
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find the trace in the xy-plane, we substitute z=0 into the equation of the ellipsoid:

(x−1)216+(y−3)28+(0+1)22=1(x−1)216+(y−3)28=12(x−1)28+(y−3)24=1(x−1)2(22)2+(y−3)222=1

This gives us the equation of the ellipse with semi-axes 22 and 2, centered at (1,3) in the xy-plane.
