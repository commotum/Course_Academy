Given the ellipsoid (x+1)24+(y−2)29+z211=1, find its intercepts with the z-axis.

a
(0,0,−11) and (0,0,11)
b
(0,0,0) and (0,0,116)
c
(0,0,−116) and (0,0,116)
d
(0,0,0) only
e
(0,0,116) only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find the z-intercepts, we plug x=0 and y=0 into the equation:

(0+1)24+(0−2)29+z211=114+49+z211=1z211=1136z2=12136z=±116

Hence, we get the solutions z=−116 and z=116.

Therefore, the intercepts with the z-axis are (0,0,−116) and (0,0,116).
