Question 6

Suppose $X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_{9}$ is an I.I.D random sample with $X_i \sim B(7,\theta)$ and unknown probability of success $\theta.$ For a particular sample $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{9},$ you're given that \[ \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{9} x_i = 13. \]

Find the likelihood function of the sample up to a constant $C.$

Hint: The probability mass function for a binomial random variable is given by \[ f(x) = \displaystyle \binom{n}{x} \theta^x (1-\theta)^{n-x}, \qquad x = 0,1,2,\ldots, n. \]

a
 $C \, \theta^{50}(1 - \theta)^{50}$
b
 $C \, \theta^{50}(1 - \theta)^{13}$
c
 $C \, \theta^{13}(1 - \theta)^{50}$
d
 $C \, \theta^{13}(1 - \theta)^{63}$
e
 $C \, \theta^{63}(1 - \theta)^{13}$
