Given the vector u=[−518−3], which of the following vectors are orthogonal to u? v=[−1−725],w=[−3−5−12],x=[1−114].

a
w only
b
None of the listed
c
v only
d
x only
e
v and w only

Correct
EXPLANATION

We compute the dot product between u and each of the listed vectors:

u⋅v=u1⋅v1+u2⋅v2+u3⋅v3+u4⋅v4=(−5)⋅(−1)+1⋅(−7)+8⋅2+(−3)⋅5=5−7+16−15=−1.×

u⋅w=u1⋅w1+u2⋅w2+u3⋅w3+u4⋅w4=(−5)⋅(−3)+1⋅(−5)+8⋅(−1)+(−3)⋅2=15−5−8−6=−4.×

u⋅x=u1⋅x1+u2⋅x2+u3⋅x3+u4⋅x4=(−5)⋅1+1⋅(−1)+8⋅1+(−3)⋅4=−5−1+8−12=−10.×

Consequently, the correct option is 'None of the listed'.
