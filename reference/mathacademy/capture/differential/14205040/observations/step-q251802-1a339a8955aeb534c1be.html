xi 12 4 6 108 15 11
yi 20 10 5 1 7 4 9

Given that ∑i=17xiyi=535, calculate Sxy.

EXPLANATION

Given a set of n paired observations x1,x2,…,xn and y1,y2,…,yn, the sum of products of deviations from the means can be calculated as follows: Sxy=∑i=1n(xi−x―)(yi−y―)=∑ixiyi−1n(∑i=1nxi)(∑i=1nyi)

First, we calculate the sum of the xi and yi: ∑i=1nxi=12+4+6+10+8+15+11=66,∑i=1nyi=20+10+5+1+7+4+9=56

We are given that ∑i=17xiyi=535.

Finally, by substituting in the above formula, we get Sxy=535−17(66)(56)=535−528=7.
