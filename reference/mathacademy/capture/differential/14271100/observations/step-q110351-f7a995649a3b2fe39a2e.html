For two independent random variables X and Y, we have Var[X]=3 and Var[Y]=6. Find Var[X+Y].

a
12
b
9
c
18
d
3
e
12
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X and Y are independent random variables, then

Var[X±Y]=Var[X]+Var[Y].

Substituting the given data into the formula above, we obtain

Var[X+Y]=3+6=9.
