Recall that if X and Y are independent random variables and a and b are constants, then

Var[aX+bY]=a2Var[X]+b2Var[Y].

Also, the variance of a random variable X is determined by the formula Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2.

First, let's find Var[X]:

Var[X]=E[X2]−E[X]2=5−22=1.

Similarly,

Var[Y]=E[Y2]−E[Y]2=23−42=7.

Therefore,

Var[5X+7Y]=52Var[X]+72Var[Y]=25(1)+49(7)=368.
