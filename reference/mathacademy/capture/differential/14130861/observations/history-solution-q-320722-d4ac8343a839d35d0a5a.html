The range of θ=arctan⁡x is given by

−π2<θ<π2.

Note that the angle θ spans the first and fourth quadrants.

Since −1 is negative, our answer is an angle that lies in the fourth quadrant, where the tangent is negative.

From the special values of the trigonometric functions, we know that

tan⁡(−π4)=−1.

Since −π4 is an angle in the fourth quadrant that lies within the range of arctan, we conclude that

arctan⁡(−1)=−π4.
