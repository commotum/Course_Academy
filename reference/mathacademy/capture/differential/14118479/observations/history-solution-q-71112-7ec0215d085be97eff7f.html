First, notice that:

  • For the plane −x−2y+z=0, a normal vector to the plane is n1=⟨−1,−2,1⟩.

  • For the plane 2x+4y−2z+1=0, a normal vector to the plane is n2=⟨2,4,−2⟩.

Now, let's consider the statements.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, n2=−2n1. So, the normal vectors are parallel.

  • Statement II is true, while statement III is false.

    For the two planes to coincide, the equation of one plane should be a scalar multiple of the other. However, if we try to manipulate the equation of the second plane to resemble the equation of the first plane, we get: 2x+4y−2z+1=0x+2y−z+12=0−x−2y+z−12=0 Clearly, this equation is not the same as the equation of the first plane. So, the planes are parallel but not coincident.

Therefore, only statements I and II are true.
