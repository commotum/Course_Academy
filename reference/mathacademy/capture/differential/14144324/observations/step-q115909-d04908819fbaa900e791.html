What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola y2=3x?

a
(−34,0)
b
(3,0)
c
(0,−3)
d
(0,34)
e
(0,0)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The standard equation of a left- or right-opening parabola that has its vertex at the origin is y2=4px,

where p is a constant.

We can rewrite the given equation as

y2=4(34)x.

It is now clear that our equation is of the standard type with p=34. Therefore, our parabola has its vertex at (0,0).
