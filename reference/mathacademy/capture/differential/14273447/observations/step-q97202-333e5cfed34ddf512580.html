EXPLANATION

If X∼Bernoulli(p), then X has the following probability mass function:

f(x)={p,x=11−p,x=0

Here, X∼Bernoulli(0.87), so the distribution of X in this case is

f(x)={0.87,x=10.13,x=0.

Therefore, P(X=0)=f(0)=0.13.
