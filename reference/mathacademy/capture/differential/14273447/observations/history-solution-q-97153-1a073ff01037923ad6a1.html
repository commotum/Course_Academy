By the addition law, we have

P(X=1∪Y=0)=P(X=1)+P(Y=0)−P(X=1∩Y=0).

Using the multiplication law for independent events, we have

P(X=1∩Y=0)=P(X=1)⋅P(Y=0).

Therefore,

P(X=1∪Y=0)=P(X=1)+P(Y=0)−P(X=1)⋅P(Y=0).

Now, since X∼Bernoulli(0.4), we have

P(X=1)=0.4,P(X=0)=0.6,

and since Y∼Bernoulli(0.2), we have

P(Y=1)=0.2,P(Y=0)=0.8.

Therefore,

P(X=1∪Y=0)=P(X=1)+P(Y=0)−P(X=1)⋅P(Y=0)=0.4+0.8−0.4⋅0.8=1.2−0.32=0.88.
