If A is a 6×11 matrix that has 8 non-pivot columns, then rank(A)=

a
11
b
3
c
6
d
7
e
8
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We know that rank(A) is the number of pivot columns in A.

Since A has 11 columns and 8 of them are non-pivots, then the number of pivot columns in A is 11−8=3.

Therefore, rank(A)=3.
