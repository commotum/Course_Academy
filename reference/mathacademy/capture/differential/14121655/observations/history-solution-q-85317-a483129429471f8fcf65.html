A Poisson distribution models the number of events that occur in an interval of time or space if events occur independently and at a constant average rate.

With that in mind, let's consider each of the given random variables.

  • Random variable I follows a Poisson distribution. The number of defective televisions can be modeled as independent events with a constant average rate, and we wish to know how many events occur in an interval of time (a month).

  • Random variable II does not follow a Poisson distribution. The grade point average of a college class is not an event that occurs at a constant average rate.

  • Random variable III does not follow a Poisson distribution. The average speed of a car is not an event that occurs at a constant average rate.
