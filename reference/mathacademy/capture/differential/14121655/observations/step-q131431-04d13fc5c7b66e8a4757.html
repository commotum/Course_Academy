EXPLANATION

A Poisson random variable X with rate λ has the following probability mass function: f(x)=λxe−λx!

Let X be the number of questions that cite the interior angle theorem in the solution. Since the average rate is 6 questions that cite the interior angle theorem in the solution per batch, and events occur independently and at a constant average rate, the number of questions that cite the interior angle theorem in the solution can be modeled as a Poisson random variable with rate λ=6, and we can write X∼Po(6).

So, X has the following probability mass function: f(x)=6xe−6x!

To compute P(X>5), we will use the complement: P(X>5)=1−P(X≤5)

Therefore, the probability of finding more than 5 questions that cite the interior angle theorem in the solution in the next batch of practice questions is P(X>5)=1−P(X≤5)≈1−0.446=0.554 rounded to 3 decimal places.
