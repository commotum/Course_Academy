A Poisson random variable X with rate λ has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=λxe−λx!

Let X be the number of airplanes that are delayed by more than one hour on a particular day. Since the average rate is 4 delayed airplanes per day, and events occur independently and at a constant average rate, X can be modeled as a Poisson random variable with rate λ=4, and we can write X∼Po(4).

So, X has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=4xe−4x!

Therefore, the probability of precisely 8 airplanes will be delayed on any particular day is

P(X=8)=f(8)=48e−48!≈0.030

rounded to 3 decimal places.
