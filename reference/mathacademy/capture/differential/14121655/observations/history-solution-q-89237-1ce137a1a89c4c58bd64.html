A Poisson random variable X with rate λ has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=λxe−λx!

Let X be the number of graduate students with the Summa Cum Laude honor. Since the average rate is 5 graduates with the Summa Cum Laude honor of each group of graduates, and events occur independently and at a constant average rate, the number of graduate students can be modeled as a Poisson random variable with rate λ=5, and we can write X∼Po(5).

So, X has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=5xe−5x!

Therefore, the probability that at most 3 graduate students will have the Summa Cum Laude honor is

P(X≤3)=P(X∈{0,1,2,3})=f(0)+f(1)+f(2)+f(3)=50e−50!+51e−51!+52e−52!+53e−53!≈0.265

rounded to 3 decimal places.
