For the functions f(x) and g(x), you are given the following data:

f(0)=3,f′(0)=2,g(0)=12,g′(0)=−1

Compute ddx(f(x)g(x)) at x=0.

ddx(f(x)g(x))|x=0=
8
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Using the quotient rule, we get ddx(f(x)g(x))=f′(x)g(x)−f(x)g′(x)(g(x))2.

At the point where x=0, this gives

ddx(f(x)g(x))|x=0=f′(0)g(0)−f(0)g′(0)(g(0))2=2⋅(12)−3⋅(−1)(12)2=1+314=16.
