Given that none of the points overlap, which of the following statements are true?

  1. Only one player has exactly 10 years of experience.
  2. There are two players aged 21.
  3. None of the players has fewer than 6 years of experience.

EXPLANATION

Let's examine our scatter plot.

  • Statement I is true. The scatter plot has only one dot whose vertical coordinate equals 10, namely, (21,10). This means only one of the players has exactly 10 years of experience.
  • Statement II is true. The scatter plot has two dots whose horizontal coordinate equals 21, namely, (21,10) and (21,12). This means that two players are 21 years old.
  • Statement III is false. From the scatter plot, one player has fewer than 6 years of experience. The dot below the horizontal line represents this player.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
