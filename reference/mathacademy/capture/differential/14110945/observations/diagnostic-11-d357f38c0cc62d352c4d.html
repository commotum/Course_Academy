EXPLANATION

We differentiate using the product rule. Let u(x)=x and v(x)=ln⁡x. Then, we have

dudx=1,dvdx=1x.

Applying the product rule gives

dydx=udvdx+vdudx=x⋅1x+(ln⁡x)⋅1=1+ln⁡x.

To find the slope of the tangent line, we now substitute x=e into the formula and get

dydx|x=e=1+ln⁡x=1+ln⁡e=2.

Therefore, the slope of the tangent line is 2.
