Let R be the event that a copy has a printing defect, S be the event that a copy is sold, and D1,D2,D3 be the event that a copy is given to distributor 1, 2, or 3, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(D1)=0.36P(D2)=0.23P(S|D2)=0.92P(D3)=0.41P(S|D3)=0.95 The disjoint union D1⊔D2⊔D3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(D1)+P(D2)+P(D3)=0.36+0.23+0.41=1.

  • Since 3% of all copies has some printing defect, we have P(R)=0.03.

  • Since 70% of defective copies are sold, we have P(S|R)=0.7.

  • Since 2.5% of sold copies are defective, we have P(R|S)=0.025.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen copy bought by distributor 1 is sold, which is represented as P(S|D1).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(R|S)=P(S|R)P(R)P(S)P(R|S)=P(S|R)P(R)P(S|D1)P(D1)+P(S|D2)P(D2)+P(S|D3)P(D3)0.025=(0.7)(0.03)P(S|D1)(0.36)+(0.92)(0.23)+(0.95)(0.41)0.025=0.0210.36P(S|D1)+0.6011

Solving this equation for P(S|D1), we have

0.025(0.36P(S|D1)+0.6011)=0.0210.36P(S|D1)+0.6011=0.0210.0250.36P(S|D1)+0.6011=21250.36P(S|D1)=2125−0.6011P(S|D1)=10.36(2125−0.6011)P(S|D1)≈0.664

rounded to three decimal places.
