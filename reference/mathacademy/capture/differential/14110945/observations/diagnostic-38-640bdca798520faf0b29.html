If X1,X2,…,Xn is a sample of size n drawn from a population with mean μ=4 and variance σ2=9, determine the smallest sample size required so that the standard error of the mean is less than 0.3.

EXPLANATION

If X1,X2,…,Xn is a sample of size n from a population with population mean μ and population variance σ2, then

E[X―]=μ,Var[X―]=σ2n,

and the standard error is given by

SE[X―]=Var[X―]=σn

Since the population standard deviation is σ=9=3, we have that

SE[X―]=3n.

We require that the standard error is less than 0.3. Therefore

3n<0.3n>30.3n>10n>102n>100.

Therefore, the smallest sample size is n=101.
