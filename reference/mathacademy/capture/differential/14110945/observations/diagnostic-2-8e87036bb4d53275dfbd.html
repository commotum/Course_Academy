The point P(53,23) lies on the unit circle, as shown. What is cot⁡θ?

a
1
b
5

Correct
EXPLANATION

Any point (x,y) on the unit circle is related to the central angle θ as follows:

x=cos⁡θ,y=sin⁡θ

We're given that x=53 and y=23 at the point P. Therefore, we have

cos⁡θ=53,sin⁡θ=23.

Knowing that cot⁡θ=cos⁡θsin⁡θ, we can calculate cot⁡θ: cot⁡θ=(53)(23)=52.
