The graph shows a portion of the curve y=cos⁡x and y=−12. Which of the following generates the x-values of all of their points of intersection? Assume that n is an integer.

a
x=5π6+2nπ,x=7π6+2nπ
b
x=π6+2nπ,x=11π6+2nπ
c
x=2π3+2nπ,x=4π3+2nπ
d
x=2π3+2nπ,x=5π3+2nπ
e
x=π3+2nπ,x=5π3+2nπ

Correct
EXPLANATION

At the intersection points, the y-values are the same for both graphs. This gives the equation cos⁡x=−12.

First, we find the principal value: x1=arccos⁡(−12)=2π3

Our first solution lies in the 2nd quadrant and has a reference angle of θR=π3. Since cos⁡x<0, the second solution must lie in the third quadrant, and it also has θR=π3.

Since the second solution x2 lies in the 3rd quadrant, we can find it using the reference angle as follows:

x2=π+θR=π+π3=4π3

Hence, the general solution for the given equation is x=2π3+2nπ,x=4π3+2nπ.
