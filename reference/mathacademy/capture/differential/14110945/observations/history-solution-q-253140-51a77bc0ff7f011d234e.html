The infimum of a function is the infimum of the range of the function.

In this case, the range of f is A=(−3,2).

To find the infimum of a set A, we must find the set containing all lower bounds of A (i.e., the set of lower bounds) and take the maximum of this set.

So, in our case, we have that

  • any number y≤−3 is a lower bound of A,

  • any number y>−3 is not a lower bound of A. In other words, we can always find an element of A that is smaller than y for any y>−3.

Therefore, (−∞,−3] is the set of lower bounds of A, and infx∈Rf(x)=inf(A)=max(−∞,−3]=−3.
