Two discrete random variables X and Y with joint probability mass function f(x,y) are independent if and only if f(x,y)=fX(x)⋅fY(y) for all possible values x and y, where fX(x) and fY(y) are the marginal probability mass functions for X and Y, respectively.

Therefore, since our discrete random variables are independent, we obtain f(2,5)=fX(2)⋅fY(5)=0.2⋅0.5=0.1.
