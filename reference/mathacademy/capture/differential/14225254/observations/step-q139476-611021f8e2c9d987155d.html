EXPLANATION

The orthogonal projection of x onto the subspace spanned by the columns of a matrix A is given by projSx=A(ATA)−1ATx.

In our case, we have A=[||a1a2||]=[−111−41−1]⟹AT=[−1111−4−1].

Therefore, we get the following projection: projSx=[−111−41−1]⋅([−1111−4−1]⋅[−111−41−1])−1⋅[−1111−4−1]⋅[220]=[−111−41−1]⋅[3−6−618]−1⋅[0−6]=[−111−41−1]⋅16[6221]⋅[0−6]=[−111−41−1]⋅[−2−1]=[12−1]

The acute angle between x and S is the acute angle between x and projSx.

Computing the cosine using the dot product, we obtain

cos⁡θ=2⋅1+2⋅2+0⋅(−1)22+22+0212+22+(−1)2=686=622=32.

Therefore,

θ=arccos⁡(32)=π6,

which is already an acute angle.
