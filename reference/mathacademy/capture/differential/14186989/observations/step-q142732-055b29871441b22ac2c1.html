EXPLANATION

The table shows probabilities in the form P(X>x). So first, we express the desired probability in this form:

P(|X|>x)=1−P(|X|≤x)=1−0.9=0.1

Due to the symmetry of the student's t-distribution, we have that P(|X|≥x)=0.1 is equivalent to

2P(X≥x)=0.1⟹P(X≥x)=0.05.

Now, since X∼T20, we focus on the row of the table that corresponds to k=20.

k∖p 0.100 0.050 0.025 0.010 0.005

From this row, we see that P(X≥1.725)=0.050.

Therefore, our answer is x=1.725.
