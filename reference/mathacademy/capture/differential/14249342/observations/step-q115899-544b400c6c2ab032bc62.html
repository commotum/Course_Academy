What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola x2=8y?

a
(2,0)
b
(8,0)
c
(0,0)
d
(0,8)
e
(0,2)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The standard equation of an upward- or downward-opening parabola that has its vertex at the origin is

x2=4py,

where p is a constant.

We can rewrite the given equation as

x2=4(2)y.

It is now clear that our equation is of the standard type with p=2. Therefore, our parabola has its vertex at (0,0).
