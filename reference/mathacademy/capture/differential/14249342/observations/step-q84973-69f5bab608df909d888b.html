What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola (y−2)2=24x?

a
(−2,6)
b
(6,2)
c
(0,2)
d
(0,−2)
e
(2,0)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The standard equation of a left- or right-opening parabola that has its vertex at (h,k) is

(y−k)2=4p(x−h),

where p is constant.

We can rewrite the given equation as

(y−2)2=4(6)(x−0).

It is now clear that our equation is of the standard type with p=6.

Comparing our equation with the standard equation, we can see that we have h=0 and k=2. Therefore, the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola are (0,2).
