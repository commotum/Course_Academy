The standard equation of an upward- or downward-opening parabola that has its vertex at (h,k) is

(x−h)2=4p(y−k),

where p is constant.

Comparing our equation with the standard equation, we can see that we have h=0 and k=2. Therefore, the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola are (0,2).
