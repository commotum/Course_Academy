Let R be a 8×12 matrix that has 8 pivot columns. What is the dimension of the null space of R?

a
6
b
4
c
8
d
5
e
3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We know that dim(Null(R)) is the number of non-pivot columns in R.

Since R has 12 columns and 8 of them are pivots, then the number of non-pivot columns in R is 12−8=4.

Therefore, dim(Null(R))=4.
