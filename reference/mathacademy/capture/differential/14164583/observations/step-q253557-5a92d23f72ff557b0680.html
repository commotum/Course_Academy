EXPLANATION

The infimum of A is the greatest lower bound of A.

To find the infimum of this set, we must find the set containing all lower bounds of A (i.e., the set of lower bounds) and take the maximum of this set.

In our case, we have the following:

  • Any number in the set x≤−5 is smaller than or equal to the elements of A. Thus, the set (−∞,−5] contains only lower bounds of A.

  • Any number in the set x>−5 is not a lower bound of A. In other words, we can always find an element of A that is smaller than x for any x>−5.

Therefore, (−∞,−5] is the set of lower bounds of A, and inf(A)=max(−∞,−5]=−5.

Notice that −5∉A.
