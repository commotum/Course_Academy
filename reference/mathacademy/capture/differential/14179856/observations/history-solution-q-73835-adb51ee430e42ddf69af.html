First, we need to find the equation of the parabola. From the diagram, we see that the parabola has a vertex at (−2,2) and passes through the point (7,5).

The general standard equation of a left- or right-opening parabola with vertex (h,k) is given by

(y−k)2=4p(x−h).

Substituting (h,k)=(−2,2) into the above gives

(y−2)2=4p(x+2).

Now, we need to find p. Since the parabola passes through the point (7,5), we substitute x=7 and y=5 into the equation and get (5−2)2=4p(7+2)9=4p(9)p=14.

Therefore, the equation of the parabola is (y−2)2=(x+2).

When the parabola intersects the x-axis, we must have y=0. So, we substitute y=0 into the equation of the parabola and solve for x:

(0−2)2=(x+2)4=x+2x=4−2x=2

Therefore, the x-intercept is 2.
