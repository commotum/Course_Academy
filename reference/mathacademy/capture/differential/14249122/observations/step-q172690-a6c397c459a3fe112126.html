EXPLANATION

Recall that the intersection of a family of indexed sets consists of those elements that belong to all the sets:

⋂i∈IAi={x:x∈Akfor allk∈I}

Let's now write down one of our indexed sets. For example, if i=1, we have X1={1}×[0,1]={(1,k):k∈[0,1]}. This represents a vertical line segment from the point (1,0) to the point (1,1) in the coordinate plane, as shown below.

In general, Xi={i}×[0,i]={(i,k):k∈[0,i]} is the vertical line segment from the point (i,0) to the point (i,i).

There are no points that lie in all sets. Therefore, the intersection of the indexed sets is ∅, the empty set.
