We will use principal component analysis to reduce the dimensionality of the dataset. The first principal component is the direction in feature space with the most variance.

The second principal component is the direction orthogonal to the first principal component.

In order to reduce the dimensionality of the dataset, we will project the data onto the first few principal components, picking the number of components based on the amount of cumulative variance preserved.

The first step is to standardize the data.
