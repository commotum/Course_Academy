Suppose A is an m×n matrix. Let

{v1,v2…,vn} be an orthonormal basis of Rn consisting of the eigenvectors of ATA with corresponding eigenvalues

λ1≥λ2≥…≥λn.

Then,

  • σ1=λ1 is the length of Av1,

  • σ2=λ2 is the length of Av2,

  • ⋯

In our example, we see that

  • ||Av1||=7=σ1, which means that λ1=72=49, and

  • ||Av2||=5=σ2, which means that λ2=52=25.

Therefore, an eigenvector corresponding to the eigenvalue λ2=25 of ATA is v2.
