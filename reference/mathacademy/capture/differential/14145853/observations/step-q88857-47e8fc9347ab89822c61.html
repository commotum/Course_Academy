EXPLANATION

Suppose X1,X2,…Xn is a sequence of random variables representing a random sample of size n drawn from a population with population mean μ and population variance σ2. Then, the sample variance S2 is given by

S2=1n−1∑i=1n(Xi−X―)2=nn−1[X2―−(X―)2].

Note that:

  • X― is the sample mean,

  • the sample variance S2 is an unbiased estimator of the population variance σ2, and

  • σ^2=s2 denotes the unbiased estimate of σ2 computed from the sample x1,x2,…,xn.

First, we will compute the quantity

x2―−(x―)2.

We proceed as follows:

  • The mean of the data is given by x―=15(4+8+10+6+7)=355=7.

  • The mean of the squared data is given by x2―=15(42+82+102+62+72)=2655=53.

Therefore,

x2―−(x―)2=53−72=4.

Finally, the sample variance is

σ^2=s2=nn−1[x2―−(x―)2]=55−1⋅4=5.

Therefore, an unbiased estimate of the population variance is 5.
