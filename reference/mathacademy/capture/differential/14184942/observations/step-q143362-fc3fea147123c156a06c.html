A math test is composed of 15 exercises. The time that a student takes to solve an exercise, in minutes, is normally distributed with a mean of 6min and a standard deviation of 2min. If one student is randomly selected, what is the probability that they will take between 80min and 100min to complete all exercises? You may assume that the times spent solving each exercise are mutually independent.

EXPLANATION

Let Xi denote the time spent solving the ith exercise. Then, we have

Xi∼N(60,22),1≤i≤15.

Let the random variable W denote the time it takes a randomly selected student to complete the test. Then, since

W=X1+X2+⋯+X15,

we have that W is normally distributed, where

E[W]=15⋅6=90,Var[W]=15⋅22=60.

Therefore,

W∼N(90,60).

In order to find P(80<W<100), we convert W to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(80<W<100)=P(80−9060<Z<100−9060)≈P(−1.29<Z<1.29)=Φ(1.29)−Φ(−1.29)=Φ(1.29)−(1−Φ(1.29))=2Φ(1.29)−1

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(1.29)≈0.9015.

Therefore,

P(80<W<100)≈2Φ(1.29)−1≈0.8030.
