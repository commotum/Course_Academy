Let v1,v2,v3,v4 be linearly independent vectors, and suppose that the vector w can be written as a linear combination of v1,v2,v3+v4. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. v1,v2,v3,v4 form a basis of Span{v1,v2,v3,v4}
  2. dim(Span{v1,v2,v3,v4})=4
  3. dim(Span{v1,v2,v3,v4,w})=5
a
I and II only
b
II and III only
c
I and III only
d
I only
e
I, II, and III
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Recall that a basis of a vector space V is a set of linearly independent vectors that span V. The dimension of V is the number of vectors in any basis of V (if such a basis exists).

With that in mind, let's consider each of the statements.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, Span{v1,v2,v3,v4} is spanned by the vectors by v1,v2,v3,v4. Moreover, v1,v2,v3,v4 are linearly independent vectors. So, v1,v2,v3,v4 is a basis of the span.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, Span{v1,v2,v3,v4} is spanned by v1,v2,v3,v4, and these are linearly independent vectors. So, v1,v2,v3,v4 is a basis of the span. Therefore, we have dim(Span{v1,v2,v3,v4})=4.

  • Statement III is false. We are told that w can be written as a linear combination of v1,v2,v3+v4. Hence,w can be written as a linear combination of v1,v2,v3,v4. this means that Span{v1,v2,v3,v4,w} is completely spanned by v1,v2,v3,v4 only. So, dim(Span{v1,v2,v3,v4,w})=4 (not 5).

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
