From the properties of determinants, we know that det(A)=det(AT)=7 and det(I)=1.

With that in mind, let's analyze each statement in turn.

  • Statement I is true. Indeed, 3det(A)=3⋅7=21.

  • Statement II is false. We have −2det(AT)=−2⋅7=−14≠14.

  • Statement III is true. We have det(A)+2det(I)=7+2⋅1=9.

In conclusion, the correct answer is "I and III only".
