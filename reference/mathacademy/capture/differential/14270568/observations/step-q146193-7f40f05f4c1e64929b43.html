Recall that the conditional expected value of Y given X=x is defined by

E[Y|X=x]=∑yyfY|X(y|x).

Therefore, we obtain

E[Y|X=4]=∑yyfY|X(y|4)=2⋅56+4⋅16=53+23=73.
