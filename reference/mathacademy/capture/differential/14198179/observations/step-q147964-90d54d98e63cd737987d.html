The joint probability mass function f(x,y) for the discrete random variables X and Y is given below.

f Y=0 Y=1
X=0 0.05 0.2
X=1 0.15 0.6

Calculate the covariance of X and Y.

Hint: It can be shown that P(X=x,Y=y)=P(X=x)⋅P(Y=y) for all possible values of x and y.

EXPLANATION

The covariance of two discrete random variables X and Y with joint support S is defined as

Cov[X,Y]=∑(x,y)∈S(x−E[X])(y−E[Y])f(x,y).

We're told that

P(X=x,Y=y)=P(X=x)⋅P(Y=y)

for all possible values of x and y. Therefore, X and Y are independent.

Since X are Y are independent, we have Cov(X,Y)=0.
