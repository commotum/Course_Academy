f Y=1 Y=2

Which of the following gives the covariance of X and Y?

 ∑(x,y)∈S(xy−0.5)f(x,y)

 ∑(x,y)∈Sx(y−1.6)f(x,y)

 ∑(x,y)∈S(x−1.6)(y−0.5)f(x,y)

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The covariance of two discrete random variables X and Y with joint support S is defined as

Cov[X,Y]=∑(x,y)∈S(x−E[X])(y−E[Y])f(x,y).

First, let's find the marginal mass functions for X and Y. These are given by the row and column totals, respectively.

f Y=1 Y=2 fX
X=0 0.15 0.35 0.5
X=1 0.25 0.25 0.5
fY 0.4 0.6 1

We now compute E[X] and E[Y], as follows:

E[X]=∑x∈SXx⋅fX(x)=0⋅0.5+1⋅0.5=0+0.5=0.5E[Y]=∑y∈SYy⋅fY(y)=1⋅0.4+2⋅0.6=0.4+1.2=1.6

Applying the formula for the covariance, we have

Cov[X,Y]=∑(x,y)∈S(x−E[X])(y−E[Y])f(x,y)=∑(x,y)∈S(x−0.5)(y−1.6)f(x,y).
