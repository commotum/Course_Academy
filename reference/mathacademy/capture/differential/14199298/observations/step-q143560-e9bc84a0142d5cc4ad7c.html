Among the people arrested in a particular city in 2010, 16% were males under 18, 30% were males between 18 and 40, and 24% were males over 40. What is the probability that a person randomly selected from all those arrested in 2010 is male?

a
0.65
b
0.7
c
0.32
d
0.29
e
0.5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let M be the event that a person selected was a male, and

  • let A1 be the event that the person was under 18,

  • let A2 be the event that the person was between 18 and 40, and

  • let A3 be the event that the person was over 40.

Then the disjoint union A1⊔A2⊔A3 covers the entire sample space.

To calculate the probability that a randomly selected person is male, we use the law of total probability:

P(M)=P(M∩A1)+P(M∩A2)+P(M∩A3)

We have

P(M∩A1)=0.16,P(M∩A2)=0.3P(M∩A3)=0.24.

Finally, substituting the above information into the law of total probability, the probability that a randomly selected person is male is

P(M)=P(M∩A1)+P(M∩A2)+P(M∩A3)=0.16+0.3+0.24=0.7.
