A total of 200 colored table tennis balls are distributed among three boxes, A, B, and C. Box A contains 20 green balls, while Box B contains 30 green balls. If 45% of all the balls are green, what is the probability that a randomly selected ball is a green ball from Box C?

20%

EXPLANATION

Let G be the event that the selected ball is green, and

  • let A be the event that the selected ball is from box A,

  • let B be the event that the selected ball is from box B,

  • let C be the event that the selected ball is from box C.

Then the disjoint union A⊔B⊔C covers the entire sample space.

Recall that the law of total probability states that

P(G)=P(G∩A)+P(G∩B)+P(G∩C).

We wish to find P(G∩C):

  • To calculate P(G∩A), we take the number of green balls in box A (20) and divide by the total number of balls (200). So, we have P(G∩A)=20200.

  • To calculate P(G∩B), we take the number of green balls in box B (30) and divide by the total number of balls (200). So, we have P(G∩B)=30200.

  • Also, we're given that P(G)=45%=90200.

Finally, substituting the above information into the law of total probability and solving for P(G∩C), we get the following:

P(G)=P(G∩A)+P(G∩B)+P(G∩C)90200=20200+30200+P(G∩C)P(G∩C)=90200−20200−30200P(G∩C)=40200P(G∩C)=20%
