EXPLANATION

For a discrete random variable X and a function r(x), the rule of the lazy statistician states that

E[r(X)]=∑x∈Sr(x)f(x).

In our case, we have

r(x)=(2x+1)2.

Therefore, we obtain

E[(2X+1)2]=∑x∈S(2x+1)2f(x)=(2+1)2⋅0.2+(4+1)2⋅0.3+(6+1)2⋅0.5=9⋅0.2+25⋅0.3+49⋅0.5=1.8+7.5+24.5=33.8.
