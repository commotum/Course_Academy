Which of the following pairs of sets are disjoint?

  1. {3,4,5} and {6,7,8}
  2. {−4,−3,−2} and {2,3,4}
  3. [−3,0] and [0,3]
a
I, II, and III
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
I only
e
I and II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Two sets A and B are disjoint if they have no element in common, i.e., their intersection is empty: A∩B=∅

With that in mind, let's examine our sets.

  • Pair I is disjoint. Indeed, the intersection of the sets is empty: {3,4,5}∩{6,7,8}=∅✓

  • Pair II is also disjoint. Indeed, the intersection of the sets is empty: {−4,−3,−2}∩{2,3,4}=∅✓

  • Pair III is not disjoint since the intersection of the sets is not empty: [−3,0]∩[0,3]={0}≠∅×

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
