Find a parametric equation of the plane that passes through the point A(7,−2,3) and is parallel to the lines r=⟨9,2,1⟩+s⟨2,−2,1⟩ and r=⟨4,−5,19⟩+t⟨3,4,7⟩.

a
r=⟨7,2,3⟩+s⟨2,−1,2⟩+t⟨4,3,7⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞)
b
r=⟨2,−7,3⟩+s⟨2,−4,1⟩+t⟨3,5,2⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞)
c
r=⟨3,−2,7⟩+s⟨3,−1,5⟩+t⟨3,6,7⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞)
d
r=⟨7,−2,3⟩+s⟨2,−2,1⟩+t⟨3,4,7⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞)
e
r=⟨7,−3,2⟩+s⟨3,−2,1⟩+t⟨7,4,3⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞)

Correct
EXPLANATION

The direction vectors of the lines are v1=⟨2,−2,1⟩ and v2=⟨3,4,7⟩, respectively. These vectors are parallel to the plane but not to each other.

We also know that the point A(7,−2,3) lies on the plane and its position vector is a=⟨7,−2,3⟩.

So, the corresponding parametric equation of the plane is given by

r=a+sv1+tv2r=⟨7,−2,3⟩+s⟨2,−2,1⟩+t⟨3,4,7⟩,s,t∈(−∞,∞).
