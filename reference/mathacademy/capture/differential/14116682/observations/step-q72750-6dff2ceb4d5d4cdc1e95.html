Suppose C is a 5×5 matrix and det(C)=−24. The matrix D is obtained from C by applying the elementary row operations R1↔R5 and R3:=−16R3. What is the value of det(D)?

a
−4
b
24
c
4
d
−24
e
−20

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's figure out what each operation does to the determinant.

  • The operation R1↔R5 involves swapping two rows, so it changes the sign of the determinant to the opposite one.

  • The operation R3:=−16R3 involves multiplying a row by −16, so the determinant is multiplied by −16 as well.

Therefore, we have to change the sign of the determinant to the opposite one and multiply by −16 as well. Consequently, we get

det(D)=−16⋅(−det(C))=16det(C)=16⋅(−24)=−4.
