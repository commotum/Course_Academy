The chain rule for a multivariable function f(x,y) with two independent variables s and t, where x=x(s,t) and y=y(s,t), can be expressed as follows: ∂f∂s=∂f∂x⋅∂x∂s+∂f∂y⋅∂y∂s

Next, we take the derivatives of x and y with respect to s, and we get ∂x∂s=t2,∂y∂s=3s2.
