John is selling juice at a market stall. The table below gives the rate r(t) of juice sold over a period of 6 hours, from 1p.m. to 7p.m.

t(hours) 1 3 5 7
r(t)(liters/hour) k 2 3 4

If the left Riemann sum approximation of the total number of liters John sold during the given period has a value of 16 liters, what is the value of k?

a
3
b
1
c
2
d
4
e
5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The area A under the curve y=r(t) can be approximated using the formula

A≈(r(t0)+r(t1)+…+r(tn−1)⏞heights)⋅Δt⏟ width=(r(1)+r(3)+r(5))⋅Δt.

So, we now set up a table with the values of r(t) at each point:

t 1 3 5
r(t) k 2 3

So, we obtain the following approximation to the area: A≈(k+2+3)⋅2=2(k+5)=2k+10

In the given context, the area under the curve represents the total number of liters sold by John. Since we are given that the left Riemann sum approximation of the total number of liters sold is 16, we obtain

2k+10=162k=6k=3.
