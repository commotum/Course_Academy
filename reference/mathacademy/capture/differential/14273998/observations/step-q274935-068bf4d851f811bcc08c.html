EXPLANATION

The Hadamard product of two matrices (or vectors) is only defined if they have the same dimensions.

First, notice that A is a 2×2 matrix, B is a 2×3 matrix, and v is a 1×3 row vector.

Now, let's consider each expression in turn:

  • The dimensions of BTA are 3×2, and the dimensions of Bdiag(v) are 2×3. Hence, the Hadamard product in expression I is not well-defined.

  • The dimensions of A are 2×2, and the dimensions of BvT are 2×1. Hence, the Hadamard product in expression II is not well-defined.

  • The dimensions of AB are 2×3, and the dimensions of BvTv are 2×3. Hence, the Hadamard product in expression III is well-defined.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only".
