EXPLANATION

First, notice that, by multiplying by 5 on both sides, the given equation is equivalent to 3x=15⋅4x

Now, we take log3 on both sides of the equation:

log3⁡(3x)=log3⁡(15⋅4x)

Then, we use the fact that logb⁡(bc)=c to simplify the left-hand side:

x=log3⁡(15⋅4x)

Next, we use the product law and the power law of logarithms to simplify the right-hand side:

x=log3⁡(15⋅4x)=log3⁡(15)+log3⁡(4x)=log3⁡(15)+xlog3⁡4

Finally, we solve for x, as follows:

x=log3⁡(15)+xlog3⁡4x−xlog3⁡4=log3⁡(15)x(1−log3⁡4)=log3⁡(15)x=log3⁡(15)1−log3⁡4

Using the change of base formula, we have log3⁡(15)=ln⁡15ln⁡3≈2.464974,log3⁡4=ln⁡4ln⁡3≈1.261860.

Finally, we can approximate x as x≈2.4649741−1.261860≈−9.413, rounded to 3 decimal places.
