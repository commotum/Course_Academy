The notation (fg)(x) means the product of the functions f(x) and g(x).

Method 1

To evaluate (fg)(4), we find f(4) and g(4), and then multiply the results.

First, we find f(4) and g(4):

f(4)=4(4)−6=10g(4)=5(4)−10=10

Now, we multiply the results:

(fg)(4)=f(4)g(4)=10⋅10=100
