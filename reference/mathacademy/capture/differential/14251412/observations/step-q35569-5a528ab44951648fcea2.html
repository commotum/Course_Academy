a
The interior and boundary of an ellipse centered at (1,−2) with semi-axes of lengths 4 and 22.
b
An ellipse centered at (1,−2) with semi-axes of lengths 4 and 22.
c
The interior and boundary of an ellipse centered at (1,2) with semi-axes of lengths 4 and 2.
d
An ellipse centered at (−1,2) with semi-axes of lengths 2 and 22.

An ellipse centered at (1,−2) with semi-axes of lengths 2 and 4.

Correct
EXPLANATION

The full expression describing all points on our elliptic cylinder is

(x−1)216+(y+2)28=1,z=k where k∈R can vary arbitrarily.

The domain of any cylinder in the xy-plane is its base curve. Hence, in this case, the domain is

{(x,y)∈R2: (x−1)216+(y+2)28=1}

which can be written as

{(x,y)∈R2: (x−1)242+(y+2)2(22)2=1}.

This is an ellipse centered at (1,−2) with semi-axes of lengths 4 and 22.
