The full expression describing all points on our parabolic cylinder is (y−3)2=2(x+3),z=k where k∈R can vary arbitrarily.

The domain of any cylinder in the xy-plane is its base curve. Hence, in this case, the domain is {(x,y)∈R2:(y−3)2=2(x+3)}.

This is a rightward-opening parabola with a vertex at (−3,3).
