Correct
EXPLANATION

The maximum value of the parabola is the y-coordinate of the vertex of the parabola.

Remember that the vertex form of a parabola is

y=a(x−h)2+k

where (h,k) are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola.

The given parabola

y=−(x−2)2+5

is written in vertex form with h=2 and k=5. Therefore, the vertex of the parabola is at (2,5), and its maximum value is 5.
