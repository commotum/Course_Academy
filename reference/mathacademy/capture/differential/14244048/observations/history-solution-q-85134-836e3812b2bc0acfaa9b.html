The minimum value of the parabola is the y-coordinate of the vertex of the parabola.

Remember that the vertex form of a parabola is

y=a(x−h)2+k

where (h,k) are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola.

The given parabola

y=3(x+5)2−6

is written in vertex form with h=−5 and k=−6. Therefore, the vertex of the parabola is at (−5,−6), and its minimum value is −6.
