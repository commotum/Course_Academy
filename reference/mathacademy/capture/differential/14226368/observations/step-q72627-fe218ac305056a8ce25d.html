Let R=[0,4]×[0,2] be a region in the xy-plane, and let f(x,y) be a function defined over R. The lower and upper Riemann sums corresponding to an arbitrary partition P of R are given by

L(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1n(4xi−1+2yj−1)ΔxiΔyj,U(f,P)=∑i=1m∑j=1n(4xi+2yj)ΔxiΔyj.

Using the definition of the double integral in terms of L(f,P) and U(f,P), compute ∬Rf(x,y)dA.

Hint: You may wish to make use of the following result: ∑i=1m∑j=1n(xi−1+xi)ΔxiΔyj=(xm2−x02)(yn−y0)

EXPLANATION

For a function f(x,y), the double integral of f over a rectangular region R is the unique number I (if it exists) such that L(f,P)≤I≤U(f,P) over all partitions P of R.

First, notice that x¯i=12(xi−1+xi) will always lie between xi−1 and xi, and similarly y¯j=12(yj−1+yj) will always lie between yj−1 and yj. Therefore, 4⋅xi−1+2⋅yj−1≤4⋅12(xi−1+xi)+2⋅12(yj−1+yj)≤4⋅xi+2⋅yj which simplifies to 4xi−1+2yj−1≤2(xi−1+xi)+(yj−1+yj)≤4xi+2yj.

Multiplying this by ΔxiΔyj and applying the double summation gives: L(f,P)≤∑i=1m∑j=1n[2(xi−1+xi)+(yj−1+yj)]ΔxiΔyj≤U(f,P)

Separating the sums using the addition and constant multiple rules, we get L(f,P)≤2∑i=1m∑j=1n(xi−1+xi)ΔxiΔyj+∑i=1m∑j=1n(yj−1+yj)ΔxiΔyj≤U(f,P).

Using the hint, we get the following: 2∑i=1m∑j=1n(xi−1+xi)ΔxiΔyj=2(42−02)(2−0)=64∑i=1m∑j=1n(yj−1+yj)ΔxiΔyj=(22−02)(4−0)=16

Finally then: L(f,P)≤64+16≤U(f,P)⟹L(f,P)≤80≤U(f,P)

Therefore, we conclude that ∬Rf(x,y)dA=80.
