Notice that the elements of our set …,3,6,12,24,48,… form a two-way infinite geometric sequence with a common ratio r=2:

r=2=63=126=2412=⋯

Therefore, setting a1=6, the nth term an of our sequence for n∈Z is given by

an=a1⋅rn−1=6⋅2n−1=6⋅2n⋅2−1=62⋅2n=3⋅2n

Therefore, we can define our set using a constructive definition as follows:

S={3⋅2n:n∈Z}
