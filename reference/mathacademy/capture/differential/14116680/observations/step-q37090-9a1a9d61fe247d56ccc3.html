Which of the following statements are true?

  1. If v is an eigenvector of a matrix A corresponding to the eigenvalue 5 then 10v is also an eigenvector corresponding to 5.
  2. v=[23] is an eigenvector of A=[3234] that corresponds to the eigenvalue 6.
  3. v=[11] is an eigenvector of A=[3131] that corresponds to the eigenvalue 4.
a
None
b
 II only
c
III only
d
I and II only
e
I, II, and III
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Remember that the eigenvectors of A are the non-zero vectors v such that Av=λv. With that in mind, let's examine the statements one by one.

  • Statement I is true. We have A(10v)=10Av=10(5v)=5(10v). Since, A(10v)=5(10v), and 10v≠0, this implies that 10v is an eigenvector of A and the corresponding eigenvalue is λ=5.

Note: In general, any non-zero multiple of an eigenvector is again an eigenvector, and the corresponding eigenvalue is the same.

  • Statement II is true. We have Av=[3234][23]=[1218]=6[23]=6v. Since Av=6v, we conclude that v is an eigenvector of A and the corresponding eigenvalue is λ=6.

  • Statement III is true. We have Av=[3131][11]=[44]=4[11]=4v. Since Av=4v, we conclude that v is an eigenvector of A and the corresponding eigenvalue is λ=4.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I, II, and III".
