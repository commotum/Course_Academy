A sample X1,X2,…,X20 is drawn from a population with population mean μ and population standard deviation σ. If μ and σ are both unknown, which of the following are statistics?

  1. ∑i=120(nXi+σ)2
  2. X1+X10+X20σ
  3. median(X1,X2,…,X20)n
a
III only
b
II only
c
II and III only
d
None of those listed
e
I and II only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Suppose X1,X2,…,Xn is a sequence of random variables representing a random sample of size n drawn from a population. A statistic is a random variable expressed as a function of Xi.

Statistics cannot involve unknown population parameters.

With that in mind, let's examine each expression.

  • Expressions I and II are not statistics. They are functions that contain the unknown population standard deviation σ.

  • Expression III is a statistic. It is a function of the sample X1,X2,…,X20 only.

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
