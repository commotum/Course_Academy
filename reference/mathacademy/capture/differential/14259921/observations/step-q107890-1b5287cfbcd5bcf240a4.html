Which of the following integrals are equivalent to Γ(z) for Re(z)>0?

  1. ∫01tz−1e−tdt
  2. ∫1∞tz−1e−tdt
  3. ∫0∞tz−1e−tdt
a
III only
b
I, II, and III
c
I and II only
d
II and III only
e
II only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let's recall the definition of the gamma function:

Γ(z)=∫0∞tz−1e−tdt,Re(z)>0.

With this definition in mind, let's consider each of the given integrals.

  • Integral I does not match the definition. The upper bound of integration does not match that given in the definition.

  • Integral II does not match the definition. The lower bound of integration does not match that given in the definition.

  • Integral III matches the definition: ∫0∞tz−1e−tdt=Γ(z)

Therefore, the correct answer is "III only."
