If X1,X2,…,Xn is a sample of size n from a population with population mean μ and population variance σ2, then

E[X―]=μ,Var[X―]=σ2n,

and the standard error is given by

SE[X―]=Var[X―]=σn.

Since the population variance is σ2=25 and the sample size is n=1000, we have that

Var[X―]=251000=0.025.

Therefore,

SE[X―]=0.025≈0.16.
