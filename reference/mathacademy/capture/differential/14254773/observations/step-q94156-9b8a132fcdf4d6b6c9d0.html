EXPLANATION

We're given that Z∼N(0,1), and we wish to compute P(Z≥−1.42). The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

By the definition of the cumulative distribution function Φ(z),

P(Z≤z)=Φ(z).

Since the total area under the graph equals 1, we can express the required probability in terms of Φ(z) as follows:

P(Z≥−1.42)=1−P(Z<−1.42)=1−Φ(−1.42)

From the table, we know that

Φ(−1.42)=0.0778.

Therefore, we have

P(Z≥−1.42)=1−Φ(−1.42)=1−0.0778=0.9222.
