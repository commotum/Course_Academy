A writer is editing the first draft of a book. On average, there have been 4 typos per page. What is the probability of finding 2 typos on the next page? Round your answer to 3 decimal places. You may assume that typos occur independently and at a constant average rate.

a
 0.173
b
 0.115
c
 0.147
d
 0.500
e
 0.238
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

A Poisson random variable X with rate λ has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=λxe−λx!

Let X be the number of typos on a page of the book. Since the average rate is 4 typos per page, and events occur independently and at a constant average rate, the number of typos can be modeled as a Poisson random variable with rate λ=4, and we can write X∼Po(4).

So, X has the following probability mass function:

f(x)=4xe−4x!

Therefore, the probability of finding 2 typos on the next page is

P(X=2)=f(2)=42e−42!≈0.147

rounded to 3 decimal places.
