Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The point P(1,−1) lies on the boundary of A
  2. The point Q(0,0) belongs to A
  3. The point Q(0,0) is an interior point of A

EXPLANATION

Let δ>0. A neighborhood of a point x0 is the set of points whose distance from x0 is less than δ. {x:|x−x0|<δ}

In two-dimensional space, a neighborhood of x0 is the open disc |x−x0|<δ, as shown below.

Furthermore, we have the following definitions:

  • A point x0 is an interior point of a set S if S contains some neighborhood of x0. The set of all interior points of S is called the interior of S.

  • A point x0 is a boundary point of a set S if every neighborhood of x0 contains points that are in S and points that are not in S. The set of all boundary points of S is called the boundary of S.

Now, the set A consists of all points that lie above the line y=2x−3. A sketch of this region is shown below:

Let's add the points P(1,−1) and Q(0,0) and some neighborhoods of these points to our diagram.

Let's examine the statements in turn.

  • Statement I is true. The coordinates of P(1,−1) satisfy y=2x−3. Hence, P lies in the set ∂A, the boundary of A.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, the coordinates of Q(0,0) satisfy the inequality y>2x−3. Hence, Q lies in the set A.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, there exists an open neighborhood of Q that is entirely contained inside the set A.

Therefore, the correct answer is 'I, II, and III.'
