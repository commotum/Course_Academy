Let Z be the set of all 2×2 matrices with real entries and determinant 0. Complete the following sentence:

Z is not a vector space (with respect to addition of matrices and multiplication of a matrix by a real number) because ____.

a
multiplication by a scalar is not closed
b
addition of matrices in Z is not closed
c
there exists a matrix A∈Z such that A+B≠O for any B∈Z
d
Z does not contain the zero matrix O
e
multiplication by a scalar is not associative
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The correct answer is

Z is not a vector space (with respect to addition of matrices and multiplication of a matrix by a real number) because addition of matrices in Z is not closed.

For example, consider the matrices A=[1000] and B=[0001] in Z. Then, det(A+B)=det([1000]+[0001])=|1001|=1≠0. Hence, A+B∉Z.

On the other hand,

  • The zero matrix belongs to Z. ✓

  • Given any A∈Z, the additive inverse matrix −A=(−1)⋅A is also in Z since det(−A)=(−1)2⋅det(A)=1⋅0=0, and A+(−A)=O. ✓

  • Given any A∈Z, multiplication by a scalar α∈R is closed since det(αA)=α2⋅det(A)=α2⋅0=0. Hence, αA∈Z. ✓

  • Multiplication of matrices with real entries by a scalar is associative, in general. ✓
