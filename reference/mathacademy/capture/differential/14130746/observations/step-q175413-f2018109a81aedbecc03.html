Question 1
?

x is smaller than or equal to 3.

The predicate P(x) is given above. Which of following represents ¬P(x)?

a
x is not greater than 3
b
x is smaller than 3
c
x is not equal to 3
d
x is greater than 3
e
x is greater than or equal to 3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The symbol "¬" represents negation. So, our task is to negate the given sentence.

To negate our plain English sentence, we place the words "It is not true that" in front of it. This gives the following:

It is not true that x is smaller than or equal to 3.

In other words, x is greater than 3. Therefore, ¬P(x) means the following:

x is greater than 3.
