Question 2
?

x is smaller than y.

The predicate P(x,y) is given above. Which of following represents ¬P(x,y)?

a
x is greater than y
b
x is not greater than y
c
x is equal to y
d
x is smaller than y
e
x is greater than or equal to y
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

The symbol "¬" represents negation. So, our task is to negate the given sentence.

To negate our plain English sentence, we place the words "It is not true that" in front of it. This gives the following:

It is not true that x is smaller than y.

In other words, x is greater than or equal to y. Therefore, ¬P(x,y) means the following:

x is greater than or equal to y.
