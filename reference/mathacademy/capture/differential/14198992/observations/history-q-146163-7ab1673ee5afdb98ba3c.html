1.Finding an Approximate Probability Using the CLT for Proportions?

Suppose we take a random sample of size n=100 from a population where 60% of the population has a particular characteristic. Find a normal approximation for the probability that between 55% and 65% of the sample have the characteristic. You may assume that the population size is significantly larger than the sample size.

Hint: Φ(z) is the cumulative distribution function for the standard normal distribution.
