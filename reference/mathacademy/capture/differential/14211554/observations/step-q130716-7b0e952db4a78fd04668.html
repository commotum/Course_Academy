Given that X is a random variable such that X∼U[0,2], find SD[X]. Round your answer to three decimal places.

a
0.289
b
0.714
c
1.128
d
0.577
e
1.443
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

If the random variable X is distributed uniformly over the interval [a,b], then

Var[X]=(b−a)212.

In our case, we have a=0 and b=2. Therefore,

Var[X]=(2−0)212=13.

Finally,

SD[X]=13=33≈0.577.
