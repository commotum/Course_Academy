The level surfaces of the function f(x,y,z) are given by the following equation:

f(x,y,z)=ce−x2−y2−z2=c−x2−y2−z2=ln⁡cx2+y2+z2=−ln⁡c

Notice that 0<c≤1 since −ln⁡c must be non-negative. If we let −ln⁡c=r2, we get

x2+y2+z2=r2.

Therefore, the level surfaces form a family of spheres centered at the origin.
