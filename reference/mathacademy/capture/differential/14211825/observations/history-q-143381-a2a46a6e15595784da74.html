5.Finding Confidence Intervals for Population Proportions?

Consider a sample of size n=160 from a large population where some individuals have a particular characteristic. Given that 5% of the sample has the characteristic, find a 90% confidence interval for the population proportion of individuals having this characteristic.

Hint: Use the fact that P(Z>1.645)=0.05, where Z∼N(0,1).
