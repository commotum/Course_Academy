EXPLANATION

First, let's interpret the data:

  • the population consists of all college students in the US,

  • some of the students surveyed love soccer, and some don't,

  • the size of the random sample is n=125, and

  • the proportion of students from the sample who love soccer can be found as follows: p^=40125=0.32

Now, we have that

  • np^=125⋅0.32=40>5, and

  • n(1−p^)=125⋅(1−0.32)=85>5.

As a result, we may use the following normal approximation:

p^−pp^(1−p^)/n|∼N(0,1),

where

  • p^ is the sample proportion,

  • p is the population proportion, and

  • n is the sample size.

Given a value α between 0 and 1, the corresponding [100(1−α)]% confidence interval for the population proportion p of the original distribution can be written as

p^±zα/2⋅p^(1−p^)n,

where P(Z>zα/2)=α2 and Z∼N(0,1).

We are interested in finding a 99% confidence interval. So, we have

α=1−0.99=0.01⟹α2=0.005.

We also need to find the z-score value z0.005 such that P(Z>z0.005)=0.005, where Z∼N(0,1).

From the percentage points table of the normal distribution, we obtain that z0.005=2.576:

p 0.100 0.050 0.025 0.010 0.005
zp 1.282 1.645 1.960 2.326 2.576

Therefore, a 99% confidence interval for the population proportion p is the following:

0.32±2.576⋅0.32(1−0.32)1250.32±0.107
