The random variable X has the following probability distribution.

x −4 −2 0 2
fX(x) 0.15 0.25 0.3 0.3

Let Y=2X2+2. The distribution of Y is given below.

y 2 a 34
fY(y) c d 0.15

What is the value of a+d?

a
8.55
b
10.3
c
8.25
d
10.55
e
10.25
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's add a Y=2X2+2 row to our table.

x −4 −2 0 2
y 34 10 2 10
fX(x) 0.15 0.25 0.3 0.3

From the table, we see that Y∈{2,10,34}. Notice that we now have two columns for Y=10.

We calculate the probabilities associated with each value of Y as follows:

P(Y=2)=0.3P(Y=10)=0.25+0.3P(Y=34)=0.15

This gives the following table.

y 2 10 34
fY(y) 0.3 0.55 0.15

Therefore, a=10,d=0.55, and a+d=10.55.
