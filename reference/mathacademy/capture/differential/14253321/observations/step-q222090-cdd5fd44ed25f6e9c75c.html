EXPLANATION

The parabola's vertex lies on the symmetry axis, so we will start by computing the axis of symmetry.

The equation of the axis of symmetry of the parabola y=ax2+bx+c is given by x=−b2a.

Substituting a=−4 and b=−20 into the formula for the axis of symmetry, we get x=−(−20)2(−4)=−(−20)(−8)=−52.

Therefore, the equation of the axis of symmetry is x=−52. This also represents the x-coordinate of the vertex. To find the y-coordinate of the vertex, we substitute x=−52 into the original equation: y=−4x2−20x−15=−4(−52)2−20(−52)−15=−25+50−15=10

Therefore, the vertex is at (−52,10).
