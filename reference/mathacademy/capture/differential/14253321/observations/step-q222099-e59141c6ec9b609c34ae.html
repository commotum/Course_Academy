EXPLANATION

First, we will calculate the axis of symmetry in terms of k. Then, we will set our result equal to 32.

The equation of the axis of symmetry of the parabola y=ax2+bx+c is given by x=−b2a.

Substituting a=2 and b=k into the formula for the axis of symmetry, we get x=−k2(2)=−k4.

We already know that the axis of symmetry is x=32, so we must have −k4=32−2k=12k=−6.
