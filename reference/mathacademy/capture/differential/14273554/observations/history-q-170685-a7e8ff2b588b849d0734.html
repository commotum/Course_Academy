5.Solving for an Unknown Conditional Probability?

A farm sells its apples to three grocery stores; 1, 2, and 3.

  • Store 1 buys 14% of the apples.
  • Store 2 buys 26% of the apples, and 50% of those apples are sold in one day.
  • Store 3 buys 60% of the apples, and 25% of those apples are sold in one day.

If 63% of all apples are red, 25% of red apples are sold in one day, and 45% of apples sold in one day are red, what is the probability that a randomly chosen apple bought by store 1 is sold in one day? Round your answer to 1 decimal place.
