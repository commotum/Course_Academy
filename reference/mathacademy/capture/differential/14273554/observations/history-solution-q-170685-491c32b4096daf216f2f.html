Let R be the event that an apple is red, S be the event that the apple is sold in one day, and S1,S2,S3 be the event that an apple is sold by store 1, 2, or 3, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(S1)=0.14P(S2)=0.26P(S|S2)=0.5P(S3)=0.60P(S|S3)=0.25 The disjoint union S1⊔S2⊔S3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(S1)+P(S2)+P(S3)=0.14+0.26+0.60=1.

  • Since 63% of all apples are red, we have P(R)=0.63.

  • Since 25% of red apples are sold in one day, we have P(S|R)=0.25.

  • Since 45% of apples sold in one day are red, we have P(R|S)=0.45.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen apple bought by store 1 is sold in one day, which is represented as P(S|S3).
