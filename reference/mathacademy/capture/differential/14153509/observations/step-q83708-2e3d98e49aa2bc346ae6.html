Consider the matrix A for which λ1=2, λ2=−3, λ3=1 are eigenvalues, and v1=[101],v2=[220],v3=[111] are the respective eigenvectors. Find the matrices P and D such that PDP−1=A, where D is a diagonal matrix.

a
D=[0010−30200] and P=[121120101]
b
D=[2000−30001] and P=[12−1120−101]
c
D=[2000−30001] and P=[121001100]
d
D=[200030001] and P=[121011101]
e
D=[2000−30001] and P=[121021101]
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the eigenvalues of the matrix are λ1=2, λ2=−3, λ3=1, we construct the diagonal matrix D by writing the eigenvalues in the main diagonal:

D=[2000−30001].

Now, to construct the matrix P, we simply write the respective eigenvectors as columns:

  • the 1st column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ1=2,

  • the 2nd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ2=−3, and

  • the 3rd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ3=1.

Therefore, we get

P=[121021101].
