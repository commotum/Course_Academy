EXPLANATION

Since the eigenvalues of the matrix are λ1=3, λ2=−1, λ3=1, we construct the diagonal matrix D by writing the eigenvalues in the main diagonal:

D=[3000−10001].

Now, to construct the matrix P, we simply write the respective eigenvectors as columns:

  • the 1st column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ1=3,

  • the 2nd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ2=−1, and

  • the 3rd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ3=−1.

Therefore, we get

P=[1−1−1−110−10−1].

Finally, we obtain

A=[1−1−1−110−10−1][3000−10001][1−1−1−110−10−1]−1.

In conclusion, we get a=−1, b=−1, and c=3.
