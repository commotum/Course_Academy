D=[2000−10001] and P=[106002011]
b
D=[2000−10001] and P=[610002011]
c
A is not diagonalizable

D=[2000−10001] and P=[102006011]

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

We apply the diagonalization algorithm.

Step 1. Notice that we are already given the eigenvalues of the matrix:

λ1=2, λ2=−1, λ3=1.

Step 2. Now, we need to find an eigenvector basis of A. Applying our usual method, we find the following.

  • For λ1=2, we get A−2I=[0−300−1001−3]. Seeking a non-zero solution of (A−2I)x=0 gives us the eigenverctor v1=[100].

  • For λ2=−1, we get A−(−1)I=[3−30020010]. Seeking a non-zero solution of (A−(−1)I)x=0 gives us the eigenverctor v2=[001].

  • For λ3=1, we get A−I=[1−3000001−2]. Seeking a non-zero solution of (A−I)x=0 gives us the eigenverctor v3=[621].

Step 3. We construct the diagonal matrix D and the invertible matrix P in the usual way, and this gives us the following: D=[2000−10001],P=[106002011]
