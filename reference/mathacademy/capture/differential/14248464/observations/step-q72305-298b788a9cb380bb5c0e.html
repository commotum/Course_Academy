Given the hyperbolic paraboloid (x+3)2−2(y−3)2=z−2, find its intercepts with the x-axis.

a
(−7,0,0) and (−1,0,0)
b
(−1,0,0) and (7,0,0)
c
(−7,0,0) only
d
(−7,0,0) and (1,0,0)
e
(1,0,0)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

To find the x-intercepts of the paraboloid, we substitute y=0 and z=0 in the equation of the paraboloid:

(x+3)2−2(0−3)2=0−2(x+3)2−18=−2(x+3)2=16x+3=±4x=−3±4

This gives the solutions x=−7 and x=1.

Therefore, the intercepts with the x-axis are (−7,0,0) and (1,0,0).
