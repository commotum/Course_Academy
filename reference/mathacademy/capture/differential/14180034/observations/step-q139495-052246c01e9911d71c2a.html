Which of the following statements are true?

  1. Any 4×2 matrix A has at most 2 distinct singular values.
  2. Any matrix of the form A=[−a00a] has exactly one negative and one positive singular value.
  3. Any 2×4 matrix A has at most 4 distinct singular values.
a
I and III only
b
I and II only
c
I, II, and III
d
III only
e
II and III only

Correct
EXPLANATION

The singular values of the matrix A are the square roots of the eigenvalues of the matrix ATA.

With that in mind, let's examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. Since A is a 4×2 matrix and AT is a 2×4 matrix, ATA must be a 2×2 matrix: AT2×4×A4×2=ATA2×2 Hence, A has at most 2 distinct singular values.

  • Statement II is false. All singular values of any matrix are non-negative. So, A can't have any negative singular value.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, since A is a 2×4 matrix and AT is a 4×2 matrix, ATA must be a 4×4 matrix: AT4×2×A2×4=ATA4×4 Hence, A has at most 4 distinct singular values.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."
