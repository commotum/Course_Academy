The area under the curve y=sin⁡(x+3) between x=−3 and x=0 and the x-axis is approximated by a left Riemann sum using n subintervals of equal length. What is the expression for the Riemann sum in sigma notation?

a
∑j=1n−13nsin⁡(jn+3)
b
∑j=0n−1sin⁡(3j+3n)
c
∑j=0n3nsin⁡(3jn)
d
∑j=0n−13nsin⁡(3jn)
e
∑j=1n3nsin⁡(jn)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The left Riemann sum for y=f(x) with a regular step size in sigma notation is

∑j=0n−1f(xj)Δx,

where n is the number of subintervals, xj=a+jΔx, and Δx=b−an.

The endpoints indicate that a=−3 and b=0, so the step size is

Δx=0−(−3)n=3n.

Therefore, using sigma notation, the left Riemann sum can be expressed as

∑j=0n−1f(xk)Δx=∑j=0n−1f(a+jΔx)Δx=∑j=0n−1f(−3+j⋅3n)⋅3n=∑j=0n−1f(−3+3jn)⋅3n=∑j=0n−13n⋅sin⁡(−3+3jn+3)=∑j=0n−13nsin⁡(3jn).
