Given the orthogonal basis B of R2 and the vector x below, find the value of 30x1, where x1 is the first component of [x]B. B={[12],[−84]},x=[−32]

a
5
b
10
c
6

15

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's denote the vectors of B as q1,q2.

Since B is an orthogonal basis, we have that [x]B=[x1x2], where xi=x⋅qiqi⋅qi. Computing x1, we obtain

x1=x⋅q1q1⋅q1=−3⋅1+2⋅212+22=−3+45=15.

Therefore, 30x1=30⋅15=6.
