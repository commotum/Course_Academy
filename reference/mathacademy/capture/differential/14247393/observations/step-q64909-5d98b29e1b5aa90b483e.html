EXPLANATION

The level curves for the function f(x,y) are given by the equations f(x,y)=c.

Notice that the range of f(x,y) is (0,∞). So, we will consider only level curves for c>0.

Therefore, we have:

f(x,y)=cexy=cxy=ln⁡cy=ln⁡cx

We can split this into three cases, depending on the sign of ln⁡c.

  • Case 1: ln⁡c>0. In this case, we have the family of hyperbolas y=ln⁡cx.

  • Case 2: ln⁡c=0. In this case, we have the lines x=0 and y=0.

  • Case 3: ln⁡c<0. In this case, we have the family of hyperbolas y=−|ln⁡c|x.
