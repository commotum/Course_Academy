EXPLANATION

We write down D and the identity matrix I side-by-side (as shown below) and apply the standard Gauss-Jordan method to transform the matrix D into the identity matrix. We perform the same row operations on both matrices:

[D|I]=[−62101001]R1:=12R1∼[−311201001]R1↔R2∼[1001−31120]R2:=R2+3R1∼[100101123]
