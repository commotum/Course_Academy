Identify the sentence that is equivalent to the following: "The juice is strawberry or it is orange."

a
The juice is orange
b
The juice is orange and strawberry
c
The juice is orange or strawberry
d
The juice is strawberry
e
The juice is orange but not strawberry
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, let's write the sentence more concisely:

"The juice is strawberry or orange."

Using the commutative law, we can swap the order of the words "strawberry" and "orange." The sentence now reads

"The juice is orange or strawberry."
