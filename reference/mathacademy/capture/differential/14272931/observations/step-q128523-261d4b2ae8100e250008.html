Identify the sentence that is equivalent to the following: "6 is divisible by 2 and it is divisible by 3."

a
6 is divisible by 3 or 2
b
6 is divisible by 3
c
6 is divisible by 2 but not by 3
d
6 is divisible by 3 but not by 2
e
6 is divisible by 3 and 2
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

First, let's write the sentence more concisely:

"6 is divisible by 2 and 3."

Using the commutative law, we can swap the order of the numbers "2" and " 3." The sentence now reads

"6 is divisible by 3 and 2."
