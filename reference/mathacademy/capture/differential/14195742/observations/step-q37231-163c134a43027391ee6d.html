EXPLANATION

Let's set f(x,y,z)=ln⁡(x2+y2+z2). Then, our surface can be viewed as the level surface f(x,y,z)=1.

A normal vector to the level surface that passes through the point (0,0,e) can be found by computing ∇f(0,0,e).

So, we find the gradient and evaluate it at (0,0,e):

∇f(x,y,z)=∂∂x(ln⁡(x2+y2+z2))i+∂∂y(ln⁡(x2+y2+z2))j+∂∂z(ln⁡(x2+y2+z2))k=2xx2+y2+z2 i+2yx2+y2+z2 j+2zx2+y2+z2 k∇f(0,0,e)=2(0)(0)2+(0)2+(e)2 i+2(0)(0)2+(0)2+(e)2 j+2e(0)2+(0)2+(e)2 k=2eek

The parametric equations for the normal line to f(x,y,z) at a point (x0,y0,z0) are given by x=x0+fx(x0,y0,z0)t,y=y0+fy(x0,y0,z0)t,z=z0+fz(x0,y0,z0)t. Therefore, the parametic equations for the normal line to our surface that passes through the point (0,0,e) can be written as x=0,y=0,z=e+2tee
