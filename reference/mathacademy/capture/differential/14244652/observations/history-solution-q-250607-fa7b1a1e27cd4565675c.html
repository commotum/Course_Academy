The parabola is written in vertex form,

y=a(x−h)2+k,

where a is the leading coefficient and (h,k) is the vertex.

  • Since h=−5 and k=7, the vertex is (−5,7).

  • Since a=−3<0, the parabola opens downwards.

Because the parabola opens downwards, there is no minimum output, and the vertex corresponds to the maximum output.

Therefore, the range is (−∞,7], i.e. −∞<f(x)≤7.
