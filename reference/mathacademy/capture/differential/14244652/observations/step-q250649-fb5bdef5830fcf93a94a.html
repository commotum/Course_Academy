EXPLANATION

The given parabola is written in factored form,

y=a(x−x1)(x−x2),

with a=−4<0.

Because the leading coefficient is negative, the parabola opens downward. Consequently, there is no minimum output, and the vertex corresponds to the maximum output.

The two roots of the parabola are x1=1 and x2=4, and we can find the x-coordinate of the vertex by taking the average of the roots: x=x1+x22=1+42=52

Then, we substitute x=52 back into the function to find the y-value of the vertex: y=−4(x−1)(x−4)=−4(52−1)(52−4)=−4⋅32⋅(−32)=9

Therefore, the vertex is (52,9), and the range of the function is (−∞,9], i.e. −∞<f(x)≤9.
