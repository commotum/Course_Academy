Let X=[X1,X2]T be a bivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ given by μ=[−52],Σ=[3−2−27]. Find the distribution of Y=X1−X2.

a
Y∼N(−7,14)
b
Y∼N(−1,7)
c
Y∼N(3,7)
d
Y∼N(−7,7)
e
Y∼N(3,14)
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X=[X1,X2]T is a bivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ, given by μ=[μ1μ2],Σ=[σ12σ12σ21σ22], then the random variable Y, defined as

Y=aX1+bX2

is a normally distributed random variable for all values of a and b, where

E[Y]=aμ1+bμ2,Var[Y]=a2σ12+b2σ22+2abσ12.

In our case, we have μ1=−5 and μ2=2. Therefore, E[Y]=E[X1−X2]=E[X1]−E[X2]=μ1−μ2=−5−2=−7.

Also, we have σ12=3,σ22=7, and σ12=σ21=−2. Therefore, Var[Y]=Var[X1−X2]=12⋅3+(−1)2⋅7+2⋅1⋅(−1)⋅(−2)=3+7+4=14.

Therefore, we conclude that Y∼N(−7,14).
