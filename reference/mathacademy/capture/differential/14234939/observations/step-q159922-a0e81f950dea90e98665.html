EXPLANATION

Recall that if X=[X1,X2,…,Xk]T is a multivariate normal random vector with mean vector μ and covariance matrix Σ, then the random variable Y, defined as

Y=cTX=∑i=1kciXi

is a normally distributed random variable, where c is any k×1 column vector, and

E[Y]=cTμ,Var[Y]=cTΣc.

In our case, since we wish to know the distribution of Y=2X1+X2+2X3, we have

c=[212].

Therefore, for the mean of Y, we have

E[Y]=cTμ=[212][−101]=−2+0+2=0,

and for the variance of Y, we have

Var[Y]=cTΣc=[212][3−11−142123][212]=[212][7610]=14+6+20=40.

Therefore,

Y∼N(0,40).

Now, we need to compute P(−6<Y≤12). First, transform Y into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring:

P(−6<Y≤12)=P(−6−040<Z≤12−040)=P(−0.95<Z≤1.90)

Now, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(−0.95<Z≤1.90)=P(Z≤1.90)−P(Z≤−0.95)=Φ(1.90)−Φ(−0.95).

From the table, we know that

Φ(0.95)=0.8289Φ(1.90)=0.9713

By the symmetry of the normal distribution, we have

Φ(−0.95)=1−Φ(0.95)=1−0.8289=0.1711.

As a result,

P(−0.95<Z≤1.90)=0.9713−0.1711=0.8002.

Therefore, we conclude that

P(−6<Y≤12)=0.8002.
