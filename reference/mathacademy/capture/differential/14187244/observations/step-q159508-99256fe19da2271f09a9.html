Previous studies show that the scores of girls and boys on a particular test for dyslexia have standard deviations of 2 points and 4 points, respectively. Scientists want to determine whether the population mean μx for girls is the same as the population mean μy for boys. They construct a 99% confidence interval for the difference between the population means.

Scientists sampled 125 girls and 118 boys and found that their mean scores on the test were 42 points and 41 points. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. The end-points of the 99% confidence interval for the difference between the population means are approximately 1±1.055.
  2. All values contained within the 99% confidence interval are positive.
  3. The confidence interval data suggests that μx>μy.
Hint: Given a random variable Z∼N(0,1), the table below shows the z-scores zp such that P(Z>zp)=p for some particular values of p.

EXPLANATION

Notice that

  • the samples are independent,

  • both sample sizes are "sufficiently large," i.e., ≥30, and

  • both population variances are known.

As a result, given a value α between 0 and 1, the corresponding [100(1−α)]% confidence interval for the difference μ1−μ2 between the corresponding population means can be written as

(x―1−x―2)±E

where

  • E=zα/2σ12n1+σ22n2 is the margin of error,

  • x―1 and x―2 are the sample means, μ1 and μ2 are the population means, σ12 and σ22 are the population variances, n1 and n2 are the sample sizes,

  • P(Z>zα/2)=α2 and Z∼N(0,1).

Now, to construct the confidence interval, we proceed as follows:

  • Step 1. Find the point estimate: x―1−x―2=42−41=1

  • Step 2. Find the margin of error. Since we are interested in finding a 99% confidence interval, we have α=1−0.99=0.01⟹α2=0.005. We also need to find the z-score value z0.005 such that P(Z>z0.005)=0.005, where Z∼N(0,1). From the percentage points table of the normal distribution, we obtain that z0.05=2.576:

    p 0.100 0.050 0.025 0.010 0.005
    zp 1.282 1.645 1.960 2.326 2.576

Now, we can compute the margin of error: E=2.576⋅22125+42118≈1.055

  • Step 3. Construct the confidence interval: 1±1.055

Let's now examine our statements.

  • Statement I is true. The end-points of our confidence interval are 1±1.055.

  • Statement II is false. The confidence interval contains 0: (1−1.055,1+1.055)=(−0.055,2.055)

  • Statement III is false. Since the confidence interval contains zero, it remains possible that μx=μy.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I only."
