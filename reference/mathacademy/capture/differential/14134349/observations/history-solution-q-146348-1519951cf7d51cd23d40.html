We have a population of size N=75, and p=0.8 represents the proportion of the population with the characteristic.

Therefore,

  • the number of population members with the characteristic equals Np=75(0.8)=60,

  • the number of population members without the characteristic equals N(1−p)=75(1−0.8)=15.

We select two members of the population at random. We need to find P(X2=0|X1=0), the probability that the second member does not have the characteristic given that the first member does not have the characteristic either.

If the first member does not have the characteristic, we have

N−1=75−1=74 members remaining, and

N(1−p)−1=14 members remaining who do not have the characteristic.

Therefore, P(X2=0|X1=0)=N(1−p)−1N−1=1474=737.
