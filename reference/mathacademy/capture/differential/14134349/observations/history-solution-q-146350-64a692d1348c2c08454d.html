We have a population of size N=180, and p=0.45 represents the proportion of the population with the characteristic.

Therefore,

  • the number of population members with the characteristic equals Np=180(0.45)=81,

  • the number of population members without the characteristic equals N(1−p)=180(1−0.45)=99.

We select two members of the population at random. We need to find P(X2=1|X1=1), the probability that the second member has the characteristic given that the first member also has the characteristic.

If the first member has the characteristic, we have

N−1=180−1=179 members remaining, and

Np−1=81−1=80 members remaining that have the characteristic.

Therefore, P(X2=1|X1=1)=Np−1N−1=80179.
