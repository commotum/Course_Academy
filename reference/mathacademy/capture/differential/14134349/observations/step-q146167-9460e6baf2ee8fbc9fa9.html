EXPLANATION

We have a population of size N=20, and p=0.55 represents the proportion of the population with the characteristic.

Therefore,

  • the number of population members with the characteristic equals Np=20(0.55)=11,

  • the number of population members without the characteristic equals N(1−p)=20(1−0.55)=9.

We select two members of the population at random. We need to find P(X2=1|X1=1), the probability that the second member has the characteristic given that the first member also has the characteristic.

If the first member has the characteristic, we have

N−1=20−1=19 members remaining, and

Np−1=11−1=10 members remaining that have the characteristic.

Therefore,

P(X2=1|X1=1)=Np−1N−1=1019.
