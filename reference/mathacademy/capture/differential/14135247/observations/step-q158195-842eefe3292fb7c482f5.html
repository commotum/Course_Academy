EXPLANATION

The function is well-defined at all points (x,y) for which the following condition is satisfied:

  • The expression under the square root is non-negative.

As a result, the points in the domain must satisfy the following inequalities:

x2+y2−1≥0⟹x2+y2≥1

Therefore, the domain is

D={(x,y)∈R2:x2+y2≥1}.
