EXPLANATION

Let's find the singular value decomposition of A using the usual algorithm.

  • Step 1. Compute ATA: ATA=[0110]T[0110]=[0110][0110]=[1001]

  • Step 2. Find the singular values: Notice that since ATA is diagonal, the singular values of A are

    σ1=σ2=1.

  • Step 3. Find the right singular vectors of A (i.e., the unit eigenvectors of ATA): Seeking non-zero solutions of (ATA−1I)v=0, we obtain the unit eigenvectors

    v1=[10] and v2=[01].

  • Step 4. Find the left singular vectors of A: Since we know the singular values and right singular vectors, we can compute the columns of U as follows:

    • First, we compute u1: u1=1σ1Av1=11[0110][10]=1[01]=[01]

    • Then, we compute u2: u2=1σ2Av2=11[0110][01]=1[10]=[10]

Finally, we construct the matrices U, Σ, and V:

U=[0110],Σ=[1001],V=[1001]
