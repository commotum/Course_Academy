Let T be the event that a customer is a teenager, S be the event that a customer is satisfied, and P1,P2,P3 be the event that a customer bought the product on platform 1, 2, or 3, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(P1)=0.56P(S|P1)=0.96P(P2)=0.14P(S|P2)=0.8P(P3)=0.3P(S|P3)=0.9 The disjoint union P1⊔P2⊔P3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(P1)+P(P2)+P(P3)=0.56+0.14+0.3=1.

  • Since 45% of all customers are teenagers, we have P(T)=0.45.

  • Since 92% of teenage customers are satisfied, we have P(S|T)=0.92.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen satisfied customer is a teenager, which is represented as P(T|S).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(T|S)=P(S|T)P(T)P(S)=P(S|T)P(T)P(S|P1)P(P1)+P(S|P2)P(P2)+P(S|P3)P(P3)=(0.92)(0.45)(0.96)(0.56)+(0.8)(0.14)+(0.9)(0.3)=0.4140.9196≈0.450,

rounded to 3 decimal places.
