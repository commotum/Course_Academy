A company is selling a new product on three e-commerce platforms, platform 1, platform 2, and platform 3.

  • 44% of customers bought the product on platform 1, and 70% of those customers were satisfied with the product.
  • 25% of customers bought the product on platform 2, and 85% of those customers were satisfied with the product.
  • 31% of customers bought the product on platform 3.

If 25% of all customers are teenagers, 60% of teenage customers are satisfied with the product, and 20% of satisfied customers are teenagers, what is the probability that a randomly chosen customer from platform 3 is satisfied? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

EXPLANATION

Let T be the event that a customer is a teenager, S be the event that a customer is satisfied, and P1,P2,P3 be the event that a customer bought the product on platform 1, 2, or 3, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(P1)=0.44P(S|P1)=0.7P(P2)=0.25P(S|P2)=0.85P(P3)=0.31 The disjoint union P1⊔P2⊔P3 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(P1)+P(P2)+P(P3)=0.44+0.25+0.31=1.

  • Since 25% of all customers are teenagers, we have P(T)=0.25.

  • Since 60% of teenage customers are satisfied, we have P(S|T)=0.6.

  • Since 20% of satisfied customers are teenagers, we have P(T|S)=0.2.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen customer from platform 3 is satisfied with the product, which is represented as P(S|P3).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(T|S)=P(S|T)P(T)P(S)0.2=P(S|T)P(T)P(S|P1)P(P1)+P(S|P2)P(P2)+P(S|P3)P(P3)0.2=(0.60)(0.25)(0.7)(0.44)+(0.85)(0.25)+P(S|P3)(0.31)0.2=0.150.5205+0.31P(S|P3)

Solving this equation for P(S|P3), we have

0.2(0.5205+0.31P(S|P3))=0.150.5205+0.31P(S|P3)=0.150.20.5205+0.31P(S|P3)=340.31P(S|P3)=34−0.5205P(S|P3)=10.31(34−0.5205)P(S|P3)≈0.740
