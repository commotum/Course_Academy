A volleyball school for children splits the players into three teams, team 1, team 2, and team 3.

  • 44% of children are in team 1, and 23% of children in this team have been playing for over two years.
  • 32% of children are in team 2, and 26% of children in this team have been playing for over two years.
  • 24% of children are in team 3.

If 7% of all children can play in the blocker position, 40% of children who can play as blockers have been playing for over two years, and 10% of children who have been playing for over two years can play as blockers, what is the probability that a randomly chosen child in team 3 has been playing for over two years? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
