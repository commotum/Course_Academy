Arthur and Sara are finding the best recipe for chocolate muffins, and they test three different oven cooking modes, mode 1, mode 2, and mode 3. They also try two types of yeast, A and B.

  • They use mode 1 to cook the 30% of their muffins, and 90% of those muffins turn out well.
  • They use mode 2 to cook the 40% of their muffins, and 85% of those muffins turn out well.
  • They use mode 3 to cook the 30% of their muffins, and 88% of those muffins turn out well.

If 20% of all muffins was prepared with yeast A, and 87% of yeast A muffins turn out well, what is the probability that a randomly chosen good muffin is done with yeast A? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
