EXPLANATION

Notice that the leading coefficient is 2. So, we take out a factor of 2 from the entire expression:

2(x2−6x+8)

Let's now complete the square for the expression inside the parentheses:

x2−6x+8

First, we rewrite the coefficient of the linear term as a product with 2 as a factor:

x2−2⋅3x+8

Then, we add and then subtract 32, writing the constant term 8 last: x2−2⋅3x+32⏟−32+8

The first three terms form a perfect square, which we factor as follows:

(x−3)2−32+8

Evaluating the last two terms, we get

(x−3)2−1
