f(1,1,e)=ln⁡(1⋅1⋅e)−1⋅1=0

Recall that the level surfaces for the function f(x,y,z) are given by the equation f(x,y,z)=c.

In our case, we're interested in c=0. So, we have:

f(x,y,z)=cln⁡(xyz)−xy=0ln⁡(xyz)=xyxyz=exyz=exyxy

Therefore, the level surface that passes through the point P(1,1,e) is the surface z=exyxy.
