EXPLANATION

The cumulative distribution function of a discrete random variable X is a function F(x) such that

F(x)=P(X≤x).

So, we have

F(8)=P(X<9)=P(X∈{0,1,…,8})=P(X∈{0,1,2,3,4,5})+P(X∈{6,7,8})=P(X≤5)+P(5<X<9)=F(5)+P(5<X<9).

Therefore,

P(5<X<9)=F(8)−F(5)=0.5925−0.1912=0.4013.
