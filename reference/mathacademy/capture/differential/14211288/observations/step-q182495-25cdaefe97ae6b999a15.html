EXPLANATION

The conditional statement P⇒Q is logically equivalent to the disjunction ¬P∨Q:

So, in this case, we have the following equivalence:

¬(¬R⇒S)≡¬(¬(¬R)∨S)

Canceling out double negation, we have

¬(¬(¬R)∨S)≡¬(R∨S).

Distributing the negation over the disjunction using De Morgan's law, we reach

¬(R∨S)≡¬R∧¬S.

So the given statement is equivalent to ¬R∧¬S.
