EXPLANATION

We need to find a subset of linearly independent vectors. So, we start by creating a matrix whose columns are made up of our vectors, and then we reduce the matrix to row echelon form:

M=[73150210000]

In this case, our matrix is already in a row echelon form.

The pivot columns correspond to linearly independent vectors, while the non-pivot columns correspond to linearly dependent vectors that can be removed. Here, the 1st and 2nd columns are pivot columns, while the 3rd column is a non-pivot column.

The 1st and 2nd columns from the original matrix are a1 and a2. So, a basis of S is {a1,a2}, that is to say,

B={[700],[320]}.
