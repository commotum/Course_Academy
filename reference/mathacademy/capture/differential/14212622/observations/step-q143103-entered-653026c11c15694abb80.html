EXPLANATION

Recall that if X1,X2, and X3 are mutually independent random variables, where X1∼N(μ1,σ12),X2∼N(μ2,σ22),X3∼N(μ3,σ32),

and a1,a2,a3 are real constants, then Y=a1X1+a2X2+a3X3∼N(a1μ1+a2μ2+a3μ3,a12σ12+a22σ22+a32σ32).

In our case, we have X1∼N(1,7),X2∼N(−3,1),X3∼N(−1,1), and Y=−X1−X2+X3.

X1∼N(33,10),X2∼N(33,10),X3∼N(20,5).
