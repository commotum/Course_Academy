A particular shop sells 2 muffins in a box. The weight of a single muffin is normally distributed with a mean of 33g and a variance of 10g2, while the weight of a box is normally distributed with a mean of 20g and a variance of 5g2.

EXPLANATION

Let X1,X2, and X3 denote the weight of the two muffins, and the box, respectively. Then, we have

X1∼N(33,10),X2∼N(33,10),X3∼N(20,5).

Let the random variable Y denote the weight of the box with muffins. Then, we have

Y=X1+X2+X3.

The random variable Y is normally distributed, where

E[Y]=2⋅33+20=86,Var[Y]=2⋅10+5=25.

Therefore, Y∼N(86,25).

In order to find P(Y>90), we convert Y to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring: P(Y>90)=P(Z>90−8625)=P(Z>0.8)=1−P(Z<0.8)=1−Φ(0.80)

Using the z-table, we see that

Φ(0.80)≈0.7881.

Therefore,

P(Y>90)=1−Φ(0.8)≈1−0.7881=0.2119.
