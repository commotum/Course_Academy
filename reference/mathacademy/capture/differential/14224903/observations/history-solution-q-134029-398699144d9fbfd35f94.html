First, let's draw an approximate trend line for our scatter plot:

It seems that the trend line passes through (x1,y1)≈(2,4) and (x2,y2)≈(6,6).

We will write the equation of the line in the slope-intercept form

y=mx+b.

First, we estimate the slope m of the line as follows:

m≈y2−y1x2−x1=6−46−2=24=0.5

Substituting m=0.5 into the slope-intercept form above, we get

y=0.5x+b.

We now need to estimate the y-intercept b. We can do this by substituting the coordinates of a point on the line. Any point will do, so let's substitute (2,4):

y=0.5x+b4=0.5(2)+b4=1+b3=bb=3

Therefore, the equation of the line is

y=0.5x+3.
