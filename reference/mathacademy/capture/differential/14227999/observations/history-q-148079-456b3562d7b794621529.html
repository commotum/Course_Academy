2.Applying Bayes’ Theorem to a Situation in Context?

An English teacher believes that students who prefer cursive handwriting tend to perform better in essay writing. To prove his belief, he puts some students into groups of three according to their average writing grades and asks them if they usually write in cursive.

  • 20% of students have low grades, and 12% of those students usually write in cursive.
  • 65% of students have average grades, and 15% of those students usually write in cursive.
  • 15% of students have high grades, and 17% of those students usually write in cursive.

If 56% of students are female, and 16% of female students write in cursive, what is the probability that a randomly chosen student who writes in cursive is a female? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
