3.Applying Bayes’ Theorem to a Situation in Context?

A medical center is investigating the correlation between scoliosis and sports practice in a sample of children.

  • 25% of children in the sample don't practice sports, and 3% of those children have scoliosis.
  • 30% of children in the sample practice one sport, and 2.5% of those children have scoliosis.
  • 45% of children in the sample practice two sports, and 3.3% of those children have scoliosis.

If 52% of children in the entire sample are female, and 2% of female children have scoliosis, what is the probability that a randomly chosen child with scoliosis is female? Round your answer to 3 decimal places.
