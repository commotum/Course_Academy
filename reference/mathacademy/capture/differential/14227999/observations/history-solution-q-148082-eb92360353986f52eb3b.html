Let F be the event that a child is female, C be the event that a child has scoliosis, and S0,S1,S2 be the event that a child practice 0, 1, or 2 sports, respectively.

Let's break down the information given in the problem statement:

  • From the bullet points, we have the following information: P(S0)=0.25P(C|S0)=0.03P(S1)=0.30P(C|S1)=0.025P(S2)=0.45P(C|S2)=0.033 The disjoint union S0⊔S1⊔S2 covers the entire sample space, and we have P(S0)+P(S1)+P(S2)=0.25+0.30+0.45=1.

  • Since 52% of children in the entire sample are female, we have P(F)=0.52.

  • Since 2% of females have scoliosis, we have P(C|F)=0.02.

We want to find the probability that a randomly chosen child with scoliosis is female, which is represented as P(F|C).

Using Bayes' theorem combined with the law of total probability, we have

P(F|C)=P(C|F)P(F)P(C)=P(C|F)P(F)P(C|S0)P(S0)+P(C|S1)P(S1)+P(C|S2)P(S2)=(0.02)(0.52)(0.03)(0.25)+(0.025)(0.3)+(0.033)(0.45)=0.01040.0299≈0.348,

rounded to 3 decimal places.
