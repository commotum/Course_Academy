First, notice that the range of f(x,y) is [−1,1]. So, we will consider only c-level curves for c∈[−1,1].

The level curves for the function f(x,y) are given by the equations f(x,y)=c.

Therefore, we have f(x,y)=c−sin⁡y=cy=−arcsin⁡c+2πn, where n can be any integer.

Notice that the equations y=−arcsin⁡c+2πn do not depend on x. So, for a given c∈[−1,1] and integer n, the right-hand side of the equation is constant. As a result, we obtain a family of horizontal lines, as shown below.
