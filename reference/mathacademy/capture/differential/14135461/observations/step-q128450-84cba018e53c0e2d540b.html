a
4x2+9y2=36
b
9x2+4y2=36

9x2+4y2=16

4x2+9y2=25

Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we find the value of the function at P: f(−2,−1)=4(−2)2+9(−1)2=4(4)+9(1)=16+9=25

Hence, the level curve that passes through P(−2,−1) is the 25-level curve.

Recall that the level curves for the function f(x,y) are given by the equation f(x,y)=c.

In our case, we're interested in c=25. So, we have f(x,y)=c4x2+9y2=25

Therefore, the level curve that passes through P(−2,−1) is the ellipse 4x2+9y2=25.
