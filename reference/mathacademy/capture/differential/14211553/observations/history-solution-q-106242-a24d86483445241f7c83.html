Let E be the event that a randomly selected person eats after 6 p.m., and let M be the event that they are male. Then, we require P(M|E), which we can compute using Bayes' theorem.

P(M|E)=P(E|M)P(M)P(E)=P(E|M)P(M)P(E|M)P(M)+P(E|M′)P(M′)

From the problem statement, we have the following:

  • Since 32% of all females and 5% of all males don't eat after 6 p.m, we have P(E|M)=1−0.05=0.95,P(E|M′)=1−0.32=0.68.

  • Since 51% of the population are female, we have P(M)=1−0.51=0.49,P(M′)=0.51.

Therefore, using Bayes' theorem, we have P(M|E)=P(E|M)P(M)P(E|M)P(M)+P(E|M′)P(M′)=(0.95)(0.49)(0.95)(0.49)+(0.68)(0.51)≈57% rounded to the nearest percent.
