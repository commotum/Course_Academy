Let M be the event that the randomly selected person is a man, and let F be the event that it is over 50. Then, we require P(M|F), which we can compute using Bayes' theorem.

P(M|F)=P(F|M)P(M)P(F)

From the problem statement, we have the following:

  • P(F|M)=0.2, since 20% of the men at the game are over 50.

  • P(M)=0.6, since 60% of all the spectators are men.

  • P(F)=0.3, since 30% of all spectators are over 50.

Therefore, using Bayes' theorem, we have

P(M|F)=P(F|M)P(M)P(F)=(0.2)(0.6)0.3=0.4.
