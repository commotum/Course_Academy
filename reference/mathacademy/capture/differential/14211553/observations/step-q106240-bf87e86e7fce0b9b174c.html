A survey of adult citizens of a particular city showed that 32% of all females and 5% of all males don't eat after 6 p.m. 51% of the population are female. If a person who doesn't eat after 6 p.m. is selected at random, what is the probability of them being male? Express your answer as a percentage to the nearest percent.

a
13%
b
11%
c
10%
d
9%
e
12%
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let N be the event that a randomly selected person doesn't eat after 6 p.m., and let M be the event that they are male. Then, we require P(M|N), which we can compute using Bayes' theorem.

P(M|N)=P(N|M)P(M)P(N)=P(N|M)P(M)P(N|M)P(M)+P(N|M′)P(M′)

From the problem statement, we have the following:

  • Since 32% of all females and 5% of all males don't eat after 6 p.m, we have P(N|M)=0.05,P(N|M′)=0.32.

  • Since 51% of the population are female, we have P(M)=1−0.51=0.49,P(M′)=0.51.

Therefore, using Bayes' theorem, we have P(M|N)=P(N|M)P(M)P(N|M)P(M)+P(N|M′)P(M′)=(0.05)(0.49)(0.05)(0.49)+(0.32)(0.51)≈13% rounded to the nearest percent.
