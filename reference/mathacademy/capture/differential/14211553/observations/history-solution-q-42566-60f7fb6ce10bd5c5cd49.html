Let H be the event that a randomly selected student attends a history course, and let L be the event that they attend a language course. Then, we require P(L|H), which we can compute using Bayes' theorem.

P(L|H)=P(H|L)P(L)P(H).

From the problem statement, we have the following:

  • P(H|L)=0.45 since 45% of language students also attend a history course.

  • P(L)=0.55, since 55% of students attend a language course.

  • P(H)=0.65, since 65% of students attend a history course.

Therefore, using Bayes' theorem, we have

P(L|H)=P(H|L)P(L)P(H)=(0.45)(0.55)0.65≈0.38

rounded to 2 decimal places.
