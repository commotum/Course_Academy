The definite integral can be defined in terms of the limit of a left Riemann sum as ∫abf(x)dx=limn→∞∑i=0n−1f(xi)⋅Δx,

where Δx=b−an and xi=a+iΔx.

In the definite integral ∫24(x+1)2dx, the function is f(x)=(x+1)2, the lower bound is a=2, and the upper bound is b=4. So, we have Δx=4−2n=2n,

and therefore ∫24(x+1)2dx=limn→∞∑i=0n−1f(xi)⋅Δx=limn→∞∑i=0n−1[f(2+i⋅2n)⋅2n]=limn→∞∑i=0n−1[f(2+2in)⋅2n]=limn→∞∑i=0n−1[(2+2in+1)2⋅2n]=limn→∞∑i=0n−1[(3+2in)2⋅2n].
