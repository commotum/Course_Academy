EXPLANATION

We can find the volume V of the rectangular prism using the formula

V=lwh,

where l is the length, w is the width, and h is the height of the solid.

Substituting

l=7in,w=2in,h=3in

into the formula, we obtain

V=7⋅2⋅3=42in3.
