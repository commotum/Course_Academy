A bar of chocolate in the form of a rectangular prism is 9cm long, 5cm wide, and 2cm high. If the chocolate costs $0.02 per cubic centimeter, what is the value of four identical bars of chocolate?

a
$8.50
b
$1.80
c
$6.20
d
$7.02
e
$7.20
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

First, we need to find the volume of the bar of chocolate.

We can find the volume V of the rectangular prism using the formula

V=lwh,

where l is the length, w is the width, and h is the height of the solid.

Substituting

l=9,w=5,h=2

into the formula, we obtain

V=9⋅5⋅2=90cm3.

The total volume of the four bars of chocolate is

90⋅4=360cm3.

Finally, the cost of four chocolate bars is

360⋅0.02=$7.20.
