What is the volume of a rectangular solid that has length, width, and height of 7cm, 5cm, and 25cm, respectively?

a
145cm3
b
75cm3
c
70cm3
d
14cm3
e
35cm3
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

We can find the volume V of the rectangular prism using the formula

V=lwh,

where l is the length, w is the width, and h is the height of the solid.

Substituting

l=7cm,w=5cm,h=25cm

into the formula, we obtain

V=7⋅5⋅25=1425=70cm3.
