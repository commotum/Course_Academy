First, let's visualize the transformation Y=(X−4)2 over the interval x≥2.

Notice the following:

  • The transformation is two-to-one over the interval 2<x<6. On this interval, Y is nonzero on 0<y<4.

  • The transformation is one-to-one over the interval x≥6. On this interval, Y is nonzero on y≥4.

In this question, we're asked to consider only the interval 0<y<4.

Using the definition of the cumulative distribution of Y, for 0<y<4, we have FY(y)=P(Y≤y)=P((X−4)2≤y)=P(−y≤X−4≤y)=P(4−y≤X≤4+y)=FX(4+y)−FX(4−y).

To compute the PDF fY(y) for 0<y<4, we differentiate FY with respect to y using the chain rule. This gives fY(y)=FY′(y)=12y⋅FX′(4+y)+12y⋅FX′(4−y)=12y⋅fX(4+y)+12y⋅fX(4−y)=12y(fX(4+y)+fX(4−y)).
