The quarter-circle above is defined by the parametric equations

x=2cos⁡θ,y=2sin⁡θ.

What is the domain of the parameter θ?

a
[0,π]
b
[π2,π]
c
[π2,3π2]
d
[0,π2]
e
[π2,2π)
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The parametric equations of a circle of radius r centered at the origin are

x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

In our case, the radius r=2. So, the parametric equations of the full circle are x=2cos⁡θ,y=2sin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

To restrict the parameterization to a quarter-circle, we associate the parameter θ with the central angle formed by a point (x,y) on the circle and the x-axis.

We can see that if we restrict θ to [π2,π], we get the required quarter-circle.

Therefore, the parametric equations of the quarter-circle are x=2cos⁡θ,y=2sin⁡θ,θ∈[π2,π].
