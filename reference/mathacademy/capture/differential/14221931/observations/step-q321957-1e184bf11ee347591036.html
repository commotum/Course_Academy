EXPLANATION

The parametric equations of a circle of radius r centered at the origin are

x=rcos⁡θ,y=rsin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

In our case, the radius r=6. So, the parametric equations of the full circle are x=6cos⁡θ,y=6sin⁡θ,θ∈[0,2π).

To restrict the parameterization to the arc above, we associate the parameter θ with the central angle formed by a point (x,y) on the circle and the x-axis.

We can see that if we restrict θ to [π,3π2], we get the required arc of the circle.

Therefore, the parametric equations of the arc of the circle are x=6cos⁡θ,y=6sin⁡θ,θ∈[π,3π2].
