The variances of the random variables X and Y are 0.09 and 0.16, respectively. Given that Cov(X,Y)=−0.03, find the correlation coefficient of X and Y.

a
−0.04
b
0.5
c
−0.25
d
0.25
e
−0.5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that the correlation coefficient of the random variables X and Y is defined as

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[Y],

where

  • Cov[X,Y] is the covariance, and

  • SD[X] and SD[Y] are the standard deviations of X and Y.

In our case, we have

SD[X]=Var[X]=0.09=0.3,SD[X]=Var[Y]=0.16=0.4.

Substituting our data in the formula, we obtain

ρ=Cov[X,Y]SD[X]⋅SD[X]=−0.030.3⋅0.4=−0.25.
