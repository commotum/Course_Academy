EXPLANATION

Given a normal random variable X∼N(μ,σ2), we have

P(X<x)=P(Z<x−μσ)

where Z∼N(0,1) is a standard normal random variable. In our case, we have μ=−3 and σ=1.

First, we transform X into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring:

P(X<−2.63)=P(Z<−2.63−(−3)1)=P(Z<0.37)=P(Z≤0.37)=Φ(0.37)

The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

According to the table, we have

Φ(0.37)=0.6443.

Therefore, we conclude that

P(X<−2.63)=0.6443.
