EXPLANATION

Given a normal random variable Y∼N(μ,σ2), we have P(Y≥y)=P(Z≥y−μσ) where Z∼N(0,1) is a standard normal random variable. In our case, we have μ=8 and σ=3.

First, we transform Y into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring: P(Y≥1.88)=P(Z≥1.88−83)=P(Z≥−2.04).

Now, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z): P(Z≥−2.04)=1−P(Z<−2.04)=1−P(Z≤−2.04)=1−Φ(−2.04).

From the table, we know that Φ(−2.04)=0.0207.

Therefore, we have P(Z≥−2.04)=1−Φ(−2.04)=1−0.0207=0.9793.

Finally, we conclude that P(Y≥1.88)=0.9793.
