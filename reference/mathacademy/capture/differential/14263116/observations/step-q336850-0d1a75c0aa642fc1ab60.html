EXPLANATION

Given a normal random variable X∼N(μ,σ2), we have

P(a<X<b)=P(a−μσ<Z<b−μσ)

where Z∼N(0,1) is a standard normal random variable. In our case, we have μ=5 and σ=8.

First, we transform X into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring:

P(12.36<X<18.68)=P(12.36−58<Z<18.68−58)=P(0.92<Z<1.71).

Now, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(0.92<Z<1.71)=P(Z<1.71)−P(Z≤0.92)=P(Z≤1.71)−P(Z≤0.92)=Φ(1.71)−Φ(0.92).

P(0.92<Z<1.71)=Φ(1.71)−Φ(0.92)=0.9564−0.8212=0.1352.

P(12.36<X<18.68)=0.1352.
