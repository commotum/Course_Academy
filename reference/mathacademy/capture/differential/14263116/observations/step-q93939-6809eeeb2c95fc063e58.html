EXPLANATION

Given a normal random variable X∼N(μ,σ2), we have

P(a<X<b)=P(a−μσ<Z<b−μσ)

where Z∼N(0,1) is a standard normal random variable. In our case, we have μ=−1 and σ=4=2.

First, we transform X into a standard normal random variable Z by z-scoring:

P(1.64<X<4.4)=P(1.64−(−1)2<Z<4.4−(−1)2)=P(1.32<Z<2.7)

The required probability is represented by the area shown below.

Now, we express the required probability in terms of Φ(z):

P(1.32<Z<2.7)=P(Z<2.7)−P(Z≤1.32)=P(Z≤2.7)−P(Z≤1.32)=Φ(2.7)−Φ(1.32)

From the table, we have the following:

Φ(1.32)=0.9066Φ(2.7)=0.9965

Therefore, we have

P(1.32<Z<2.7)=Φ(2.7)−Φ(1.32)=0.9965−0.9066=0.0899.

Finally, we conclude that

P(1.64<X<4.4)=0.0899.
