Marcus has two spinners. The first of them has three sections labeled 1,2,3, and the second has four sections labeled 0,2,4,8. The random variable X represents the number obtained on a typical throw of the first spinner, and Y is the number obtained on a typical throw of the second spinner. The first spinner is rolled 10 times, and the second spinner is rolled 5 times. What is the expected value of the sum of all outcomes?

x 1 2 3
f(x) 0.4 0.4 0.2
y 0 2 4 8
f(y) 0.5 0.3 0.1 0.1

EXPLANATION

Let Xi be the ith score obtained from the first spinner, and let Yj be the jth score obtained from the second spinner. Then, we need to calculate

E[∑i=110Xi+∑j=15Yj].

Using the properties of expected value, we have

E[∑i=110Xi+∑j=15Yj]=∑i=110E[Xi]+∑j=15E[Yj]=10E[X]+5E[Y],

where the last equality above is true because E[Xi]=E[X] and E[Yj]=E[Y] for all 1≤i≤10,1≤j≤5.

The expected values of X and Y are calculated as follows: E[X]=1⋅0.4+2⋅0.4+3⋅0.2=1.8E[Y]=0⋅0.5+2⋅0.3+4⋅0.1+8⋅0.1=1.8

Hence, we get

E[10X+5Y]=10E[X]+5E[Y]=10⋅1.8+5⋅1.8=27.
