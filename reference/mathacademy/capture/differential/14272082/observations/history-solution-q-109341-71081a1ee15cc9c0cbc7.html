Recall that if X and Y are random variables (not necessarily independent), and a and b are constants, then

E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y].

First, let's find E[X] and E[Y]. If X is defined on a sample space Sx, then we have

E[X]=∑x∈Sxx⋅f(x)=−4⋅16−2⋅13+2⋅13+4⋅16=0.

Similarly, if Y is defined on a sample space Sy, then we have

E[Y]=∑y∈Syy⋅f(y)=1⋅14+2⋅14+3⋅12=94.

Hence, we have E[10X+8Y]=E[10X]+E[8Y]=10E[X]+8E[Y]=10⋅0+8⋅94=18.
