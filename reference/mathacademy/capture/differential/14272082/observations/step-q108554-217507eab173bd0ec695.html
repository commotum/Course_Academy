For the random variables X and Y, we have E[X]=4 and E[Y]=5. Find E[5X+4Y].

a
Impossible to determine because we don't know if X and Y are independent.
b
35
c
28
d
50
e
40

Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that if X and Y are random variables (not necessarily independent), and a and b are constants, then

E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y].

Hence, we have E[5X+4Y]=E[5X]+E[4Y]=5E[X]+4E[Y]=5⋅4+4⋅5=20+20=40.
