EXPLANATION

The table shows probabilities in the form P(X>x). So first, we express the desired probability in this form:

P(X>x)=1−P(X≤x)=1−0.05=0.95

Now, since X∼χ2(9), we focus on the row of the table that corresponds to k=9.

k∖p 0.995 0.990 0.975 0.950 0.900
