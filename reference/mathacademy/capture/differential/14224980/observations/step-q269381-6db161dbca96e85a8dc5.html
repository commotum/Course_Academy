Consider the function L:R2×2→R defined by L(A)=2(yTAx) where x=[90],y=[1−1]. What is ∇AL at A=[1010−5−5]?

a
 [900−9]
b
 [90−90]
c
 [00]
d
 [180−180]
e
 [18−18]
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The gradient of L with respect to A is a matrix of the same shape as A, with (i,j)th entry given by ∂L∂aij, the partial derivative of L with respect to the (i,j)th entry of A.

Writing out L in component-wise notation as L=∑i=12∑j=12aijyixj, we have ∂L∂aij=yixj.

Thus, ∇AL=2(y⋅xT).

Evaluating at the given values x=[90] and y=[1−1], we get

∇AL=2([1−1]⋅[90])=2([90−90])=[180−180].
