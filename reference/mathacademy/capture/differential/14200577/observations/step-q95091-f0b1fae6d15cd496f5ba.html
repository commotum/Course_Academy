We know that P(X>26)=0.15. First, we convert this to a cumulative probability:

P(X≤26)=1−P(X>26)=1−0.15=0.85

Next, we convert X to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring. We get

P(X≤26)=0.85P(Z≤26−21σ)=0.85Φ(5σ)=0.855σ=Φ−1(0.85)
