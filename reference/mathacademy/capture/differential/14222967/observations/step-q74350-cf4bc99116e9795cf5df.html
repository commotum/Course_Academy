The following table shows the values of a continuous function f(x) over the interval [2,6].

x 2 3.5 4 6
f(x) 1 2 3.5 8

Use the right Riemann sum, with the three subintervals indicated by the data, to approximate the area under the curve y=f(x) over the interval [2,6].

a
21
b
22.75
c
20.75
d
19.25
e
21.5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In this case, we have n=3 rectangles. Using the right Riemann sum, the area is approximated as

A≈f(3.5)Δx1+f(4)Δx2+f(6)Δx3,

where each Δxi is calculated by considering the step between a particular x-value and the previous one, i.e.,

Δxi=xi−xi−1.

We set up a value table that includes the Δx's.

x 2 3.5 4 6
Δx − 1.5 0.5 2
f(x) − 2 3.5 8

Finally, we plug in the numbers to approximate the area under the curve and get

A≈2⋅1.5+3.5⋅0.5+8⋅2=3+1.75+16=20.75.
