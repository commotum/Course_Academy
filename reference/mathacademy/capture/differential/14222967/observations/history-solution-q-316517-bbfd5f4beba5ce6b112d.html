First, let's compute the step size Δt, which is just the width of each rectangle. In this case, the endpoints of our interval are a=0 and b=40, and there are n=4 rectangles, so the step size is

Δt=b−an=40−04=10.

The area A under the graph can be approximated using the formula

A≈(R(10)+R(20)+R(30)+R(40)⏞heights)⋅Δt⏟width.

So, we now set up a table with the values of R(t) at each point:

t 10 20 30 40
R(t) 15 17 16 14

Now, we obtain the following approximation for the area: A≈(15+17+16+14)⋅10=62⋅10=620

Finally, in the given context, the area under the curve represents the total volume of water pumped over a period of 40 minutes. So, the total volume is 620 liters.
