EXPLANATION

The area A under the curve (rate vs. time) can be approximated using the formula

A≈(R(11)+R(13)+R(15)+R(17)+R(19)⏞heights)⋅Δt⏟width.

So, we now set up a table with the values of the rate at each point:

t 11 13 15 17 19
R(t) 4 3 2 4 k

Now, we obtain the following approximation for the area: A≈(4+3+2+4+k)⋅2=2(k+13)=2k+26

Finally, in the given context, the area under the curve represents the total energy removed from 9a.m to 7p.m. Since we are given that the right Riemann sum approximation of the total energy removed is 40 kilojoules, we obtain

2k+26=402k=40−26k=142k=7.
