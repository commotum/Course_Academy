Overestimates and Underestimates
?

Whether the right Riemann sum underestimates or overestimates the actual area under a function depends on whether the function is increasing or decreasing.

  • If the function is increasing, then the rectangles reach above the function, which means the right Riemann sum gives an overestimate of the actual area under the curve

  • If the function is decreasing, then the rectangles fall below the function, which means the right Riemann sum gives an underestimate of the actual area under the curve
