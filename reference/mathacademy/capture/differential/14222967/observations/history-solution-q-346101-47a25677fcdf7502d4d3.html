First, let's compute the step size Δt, which is just the width of each rectangle. In this case, the endpoints of our interval are a=0 and b=8, and there are n=4 rectangles, so the step size is

Δt=b−an=8−04=2.

The area A under the graph can be approximated using the formula

A≈(F(2)+F(4)+F(6)+F(8)⏞heights)⋅Δt⏟width.

So, we now set up a table with the values of F(t) at each point:

t 2 4 6 8
F(t) 3 5 2 6

Now, we obtain the following approximation for the area: A≈(3+5+2+6)⋅2=16⋅2=32

Finally, in the given context, the area under the curve represents the total amount of fuel used over a period of 8 hours. So, the total amount of fuel is 32 liters.
