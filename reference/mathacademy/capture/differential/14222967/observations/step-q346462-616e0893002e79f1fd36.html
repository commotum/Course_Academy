The table below gives the medication infusion rate r(t), in milligrams per minute, for a patient.

t(minutes) 0 4 7 10 13 18
r(t)(mg/min) 6 5 3 4 2 1

Use the right Riemann sum over [0,18] with five subintervals to approximate the total medication delivered (in milligrams).

The total medication delivered is
52
milligrams.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let A denote the area under the curve y=r(t) over [0,18]. In this case, we have n=5 rectangles. Using the right Riemann sum, the area is computed as A≈r(4)Δt1+r(7)Δt2+r(10)Δt3+r(13)Δt4+r(18)Δt5, where each Δti is calculated by considering the step between a particular t-value and the previous one, i.e., Δti=ti−ti−1.

We set up a value table that includes the Δt's.

t 0 4 7 10 13 18
Δt − 4 3 3 3 5
r(t) − 5 3 4 2 1

We plug in the numbers to approximate the area under the curve and get A≈5⋅4+3⋅3+4⋅3+2⋅3+1⋅5=20+9+12+6+5=52.

Finally, in the given context, the area under the curve represents the total accumulation of medication. So, the total medication delivered is 52 milligrams.
