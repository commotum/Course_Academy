a=5, b=1, and c=−1

a=5, b=1, and c=1

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the eigenvalues of the matrix are λ1=5 and λ2=−3, we construct the diagonal matrix D by writing the eigenvalues in the main diagonal:

D=[500−3].

Now, to construct the matrix P, we simply write the respective eigenvectors as columns:

  • the 1st column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ1=5, and

  • the 2nd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ2=−3.

Therefore, we get

P=[1−3−14].

Finally, we obtain

B=[1−3−14][500−3][1−3−14]−1.

In conclusion, we get a=5, b=1, and c=−1.
