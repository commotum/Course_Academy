The standard equation of an upward- or downward-opening parabola that has its vertex at (h,k) is

(x−h)2=4p(y−k), where p is constant.

We can rewrite the given equation as (x+5)2=4(−34)(y−6).

It is now clear that our equation is of the standard type with p=−34.

Comparing our equation with the standard equation, we can see that we have h=−5 and k=6. Therefore, the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola are (−5,6).
