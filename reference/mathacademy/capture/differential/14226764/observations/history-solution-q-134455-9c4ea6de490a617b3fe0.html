The linear correlation coefficient ρ of the data sets x={x1,x2,…,xn} and y={y1,y2,…,yn} is given by

ρ(x,y)=Cov(x,y)σx⋅σy=SxySxx⋅Syy.

First, we compute the sum in the numerator:

Sxy=∑i=1n(xi−x―)(yi−y―)=(13−9)(0−2)+(4−9)(−3−2)+(10−9)(9−2)=(4)(−2)+(−5)(−5)+(1)(7)=−8+25+7=24

Second, we compute the sums in the denominator:

Sxx=∑i=1n(xi−x―)2=(13−9)2+(4−9)2+(10−9)2=16+25+1=42Syy=∑i=1n(yi−y―)2=(0−2)2+(−3−2)2+(9−2)2=4+25+49=78

Finally, we use the formula:

ρ(x,y)=2442⋅78≈246.480741⋅8.831761≈0.42

rounded to 2 decimal places.
