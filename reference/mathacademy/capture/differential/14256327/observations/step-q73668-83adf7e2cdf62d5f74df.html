Calculate the integral ∫8⋅24x+6dx.

a
24x+5ln⁡2+C
b
24x+5+C
c
24x+7ln⁡2+C
d
24x+63ln⁡2+C

24x+6+C

Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's introduce the new variable u=4x+6. Differentiating gives dudx=4⟹du=4dx.

and therefore

∫8⋅24x+6dx=∫2⋅24x+6⋅4dx=2∫2udu=2⋅2uln⁡2+C=2⋅24x+6ln⁡2+C=24x+7ln⁡2+C
