a
D=[−3001] and P=[2−201]
b
D=[100−3] and P=[2−201]
c
D=[−3001] and P=[20−21]

D=[−3001] and P=[−2120]
e
D=[−3001] and P=[−2210]

Correct
EXPLANATION

Since the eigenvalues of the matrix are λ1=−3 and λ2=1, we construct the diagonal matrix D by writing the eigenvalues in the main diagonal:

D=[−3001].

Now, to construct the matrix P, we simply write the respective eigenvectors as columns:

  • the 1st column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ1=−3, and

  • the 2nd column is the eigenvector corresponding to λ2=1.

Therefore, we get

P=[2−201].
