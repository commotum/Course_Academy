EXPLANATION

The equation of the axis of symmetry of the parabola y=ax2+bx+c is given by x=−b2a.

We substitute a=4 and b=3 into the formula for the axis of symmetry. This gives x=−32(4)=−38.

Therefore, the equation of the axis of symmetry is x=−38.
