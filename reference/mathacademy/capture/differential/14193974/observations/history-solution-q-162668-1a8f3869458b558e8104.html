  • Statement I is true. Indeed, for any 2×2 matrix A=(aij)∈Y, the entries are just real numbers. Thus, we have (−1)⋅aij=−aij, and hence, (−1)⋅A=−A.

  • Statement II is true. Indeed, for any 2×2 matrix A=(aij)∈Y, the entries are just real numbers. Thus, for any α,β∈R, we obtain that (α+β)aij=αaij+βaij, and hence, (α+β)A=αA+βA.
