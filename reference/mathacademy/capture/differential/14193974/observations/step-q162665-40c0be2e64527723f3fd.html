Let S be the set of all polynomials with real coefficients and whose degree is at most 2. Which of the following statements are true?

  1. 1x2∈S
  2. For any p(x)∈S, we have (−1)⋅p(x)∈S
  3. For any p(x),q(x)∈S and α∈R, we have α(p(x)−q(x))=αp(x)−αq(x)
a
II and III only
b
I and II only
c
III only
d
II only
e
I only
Submit
Incorrect
EXPLANATION

Let's examine each of our statements in turn.

  • Statement I is false. The set S contains only polynomials whose degree is at most 2. However, 1x2 is not a polynomial. So, 1x2∉S.

  • Statement II is true. Let deg(q) the degree of a polynomial q. Then, for any p(x)∈S we have that (−1)⋅p(x) is also a polynomial, and that deg((−1)⋅p)=deg(−1)+deg(p)=0+deg(p)≤2. Hence, (−1)p(x)∈S.

  • Statement III is true. Indeed, for any p(x),q(x)∈S and for any fixed x∈R, the values p(x) and q(x) are just real numbers. Thus, for any α∈R, we obtain that α(p(x)−q(x))=αp(x)−αq(x).

Therefore, the correct answer is "II and III only."
