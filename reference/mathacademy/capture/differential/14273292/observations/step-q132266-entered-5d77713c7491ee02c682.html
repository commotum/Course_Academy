Which of the following probabilities could be approximated using the normal approximation of the binomial distribution?

  1. The probability that a fair coin lands on tails 45 times out of 80 coin flips.
  2. The probability of getting 4 even numbers on 6 rolls of a 6-sided die.
  3. The probability that a fair spinner with four regions, red, green, yellow, and blue, lands on green at least 27 times out of 60 spins.
a
III only
b
I and III only
c
I only
d
II and III only
e
II only
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Given a binomial random variable X∼B(n,p), if

  • np>5, and

  • n(1−p)>5,

we can approximate X as a normal random variable Y with mean μ=np and variance σ2=np(1−p), that is,

Y∼N(np,np(1−p)).

Let's now examine each situation.

  • In situation I, we have n=80 and p=12. Therefore, np=80⋅0.5=40>5✓n(1−p)=80⋅(1−0.5)=40>5.✓ So, we can use the normal approximation of the binomial distribution.

  • In situation II, we have n=6 and p=12. Therefore, np=6⋅0.5=3≯5.× So, we cannot use the normal approximation of the binomial distribution.

  • In situation III, we have n=60 and p=14. Therefore, np=60⋅0.25=15>5✓n(1−p)=60⋅(1−0.25)=45>5.✓ So, we can use the normal approximation of the binomial distribution.

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and III only."

EXPLANATION

Given a binomial random variable X∼B(n,p), if

  • np>5, and

  • n(1−p)>5,

we can approximate X as a normal random variable Y with mean μ=np and variance σ2=np(1−p), that is,

Y∼N(np,np(1−p)).

Here, we have n=90 and p=0.47. Therefore, np=90⋅0.47=42.3>5✓n(1−p)=90⋅(1−0.47)=47.7>5.✓ So, we can approximate our binomial random variable X with a normal random variable Y with mean

μ=np=(90)(0.47)=42.3

and variance

σ2=np(1−p)=(90)(0.47)(1−0.47)=22.419.

That is, we can approximate X by Y∼N(42.3,22.419).

Now, we can estimate the desired probability P(40≤X<50). Using a continuity correction, we have

P(40≤X<50)=P(40≤X≤49)≈P(39.5≤Y≤49.5).

To compute this probability, we transform to a standard normal random variable by z-scoring:

P(39.5≤Y≤49.5)=P(39.5−42.322.419≤Z≤49.5−42.322.419)≈P(−0.59≤Z≤1.52)=P(Z≤1.52)−P(Z<−0.59)=P(Z≤1.52)−P(Z≤−0.59)=Φ(1.52)−Φ(−0.59)=0.9357−0.2776=0.6581

Therefore,

P(40≤X<50)≈0.658. Note: Using a software package for the approximation gives a probability of 0.6587.
