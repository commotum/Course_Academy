EXPLANATION

For a discrete random variable X and a function r(x), the rule of the lazy statistician states that

E[r(X)]=∑x∈Sr(x)f(x).

In our case, we have

r(x)=x2+1.

Therefore, we obtain

E[X2+1]=∑x∈Sx2+1f(x)=(4+1)⋅0.5+(1+1)⋅0.25+(1+1)⋅0.25=5⋅0.5+2⋅0.25+2⋅0.25=2.5+0.5+0.5=3.5.
