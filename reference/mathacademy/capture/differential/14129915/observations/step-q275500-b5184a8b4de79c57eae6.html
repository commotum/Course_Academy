EXPLANATION

For a continuous random variable X and a function r(x), the rule of the lazy statistician states that

E[r(X)]=∫−∞∞r(x)f(x)dx,

where f(x) is the probability density function of X.

In this problem, X is chosen uniformly from [0,1], so the probability density function is

f(x)=1,0≤x≤1.

Let A denote the area of the triangle formed by the three points (0,0), (1,0), and (1,X). The triangle has a fixed base of length 1 along the x-axis, and a vertical height of X from (1,0) to (1,X). Using the standard formula for the area of a triangle, we have

A=12⋅base⋅height=12⋅1⋅X=X2.

r(x)=x2.
