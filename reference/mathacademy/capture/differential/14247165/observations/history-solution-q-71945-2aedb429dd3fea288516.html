Remember that the equation of a sphere centered at (x0,y0,z0) with radius r>0 is given by (x−x0)2+(y−y0)2+(z−z0)2=r2.

By completing the square for the variable x, we have:

x2−6x+y2+z2=17(x2−6x+9)+y2+z2=17+9(x−3)2+y2+z2=26

Now, we can write our equation as (x−3)2+y2+z2=(26)2, which gives us a radius of 26.
