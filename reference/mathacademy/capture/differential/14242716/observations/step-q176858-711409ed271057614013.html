Consider the following statement.

−1 is greater than −2.

What is the negation of this statement?

a
−2 is greater than −1.
b
−1 is not greater than −2.
c
−1 is greater than −2.
d
−1 is equal to −2.
e
−2 is not greater than −1.
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

Let's denote the given statement as P.

The symbol "¬" represents negation. So, our task is to negate the given sentence.

To negate our plain English sentence, we place the words "It is not true that" in front of it. This gives the following:

It is not true that −1 is greater than −2.

In other words, −1 is not greater than −2. Therefore, ¬P means the following:

−1 is not greater than −2.

Note that P is true, while ¬P is false.
