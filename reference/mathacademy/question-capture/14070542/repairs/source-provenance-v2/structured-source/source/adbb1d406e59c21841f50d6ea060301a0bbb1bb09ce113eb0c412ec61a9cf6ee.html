The diagram above shows the region of integration. It is a type I region, and its type I representation is

D={(x,y):2≤x≤4,0≤y≤x−2}.

Notice that the left boundary of the region, written in the form y=y(x), is

y=x−2⟹x=y+2

and the right boundary of the region is

x=4.

To change the order of integration, we write D as a type II region. The type II representation of the region is

D={(x,y):0≤y≤2,y−2≤x≤4},

as shown in the diagram below.

Therefore, swapping the order of integration, we obtain

∫24∫0x−2f(x,y)dydx=∫02∫y+24f(x,y)dxdy.
