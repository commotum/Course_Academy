Let T(t) represent the temperature of an object at time t, in minutes, since the moment when it was put in a room. The temperature decreases at a rate that is equal to 0.03 times the difference between the object's temperature and the room's temperature, which is 25∘C.
The following questions will make use of the above information.
Question 1 ?
Which differential equation describes the instantaneous rate of change in the object's temperature? *Hint: Write the right-hand side of the equation as an expression in terms of T.*
dTdt=
−0.03(T−25)
Submit
Correct
Explanation

The rate of change in the temperature T of the object is given by dTdt.

We are told that the temperature decreases at a rate that is equal to 0.03 times (T−25). Therefore, the corresponding differential equation is

dTdt=−0.03(T−25).
