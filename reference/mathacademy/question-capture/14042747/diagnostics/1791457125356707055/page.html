Which expression is missing in the following equation? (53)=[(42)]+(43)

(42)
b
(32)

Incorrect
EXPLANATION

To find (53), we need to find the number that is located in row 5 and position 3 of Pascal's triangle.

Important: When counting the rows and positions in Pascal's triangle, we always start counting from zero! Remember:

  • The top row of Pascal's triangle is row 0.

  • The first position of each row (the one of the left) is position number 0.

The location of (53) in Pascal's triangle is highlighted below.

(00)(10)(11)(20)(21)(22)(30)(31)(32)(33)(40)(41)(42)(43)(44)(50)(51)(52)(53)(54)(55)⋮⋮⋮⋮⋮⋮⋮

Now, (53) is the sum of the two closest numbers located in the previous row. So, (53)=(42)+(43)

Therefore, the missing expression is (42).
