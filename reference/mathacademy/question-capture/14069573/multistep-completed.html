0.137

Explanation

In each drawn game, both players deposit $2 each, or $4 in total, into the saving box with probability 13.

Hence, the saving box will contain $20 dollars if Maria and John draw 5 games.

Then, if Y is the number of times a game is drawn out of 10 possible games, then

Y∼B(10,13).

So, Y has the probability mass function

f(y)=(10y)(13)y(23)10−y.

Therefore, the desired probability is P(Y=5)=f(5)=(105)(13)5(23)10−5=252(13)5(23)5=0.137, rounded to 3 decimal places.
