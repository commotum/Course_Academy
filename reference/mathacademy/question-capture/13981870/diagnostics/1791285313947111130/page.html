Correct
EXPLANATION

Recall that cot⁡x=1tan⁡x. Therefore, the given equation is equivalent to tan⁡x=13=33.

First, we find the principal value:

x=arctan⁡(33)=π6

Since π6 lies inside the given domain, our first solution is x1=π6.
