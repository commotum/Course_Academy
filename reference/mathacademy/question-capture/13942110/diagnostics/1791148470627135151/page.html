Question 1 ?
Find the velocity of the particle at t=7π6.

Explanation

Let's remind ourselves of the special trigonometric ratios, as given by the unit circle.

From the unit circle, we see that the angle 7π6 corresponds to the point (−32,−12).

Since the sine of the angle corresponds to the y-coordinate, we have sin⁡(7π6)=−12.

Therefore, using the fact that csc⁡θ=1sin⁡θ, we have

csc⁡(7π6)=1sin⁡(7π6)=1(−12)=−2.

Finally, evaluating v(t) at t=7π6 we have 2csc2⁡(7π6)=2(−2)2=8.
