The following table shows the values of a continuous function f(x) over the interval [4,9].

x 4 5.5 8 9
f(x) 1 4 6 9.5

Use the right Riemann sum, with the three subintervals indicated by the data, to approximate the area under the curve y=f(x) over the interval [4,9].

a
30.5
b
29.25
c
31.25
d
32.55
e
34.5
Submit
Correct
EXPLANATION

In this case, we have n=3 rectangles. Using the right Riemann sum, the area is approximated as

A≈f(5.5)Δx1+f(8)Δx2+f(9)Δx3,

where each Δxi is calculated by considering the step between a particular x-value and the previous one, i.e.,

Δxi=xi−xi−1.

We set up a value table that includes the Δx's.

x 4 5.5 8 9
Δx − 1.5 2.5 1
f(x) − 4 6 9.5

Finally, we plug in the numbers to approximate the area under the curve and get

A≈4⋅1.5+6⋅2.5+9.5⋅1=6+15+9.5=30.5.

A≈(v(5)+v(10)+v(15)+v(20)⏞heights)⋅Δt⏟width.

So, we now set up a table with the values of v(t) at each point:
