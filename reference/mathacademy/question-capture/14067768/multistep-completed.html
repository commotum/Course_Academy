The equation of a circle with radius r centered at the point (a,b) in rectangular coordinates in standard form is

(x−a)2+(y−b)2=r2.

In our case, the circle is centered at (3,0) and the radius of the circle is r=3, so

(x−a)2+(y−b)2=r2(x−3)2+(y−0)2=32(x−3)2+y2=9.

Finally, the equation of the circle in rectangular coordinates is
