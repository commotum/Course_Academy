Explanation

Recall that for any linear transformation T, we have the following connection between the area of R (the old housing complex) and the area of the image T(R) (the new housing complex) under the action of T:

Area(T(R))=|det(T)|⋅Area(R)

We're given that Area(R)=16hectares.

Computing the determinant of the matrix T (found in part 1), we get det(T)=|−30−2−1|=(−3)⋅(−1)−(2)⋅0=3.

Therefore,

Area(T(R))=|det(T)|⋅Area(R)=3⋅16=48.

Therefore, the area of the new housing complex is 48 hectares.
