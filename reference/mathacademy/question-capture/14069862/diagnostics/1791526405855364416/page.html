Incorrect
EXPLANATION

The parameter C represents a horizontal shift of the function.

First, we rewrite the function by factoring the horizontal stretch factor, as follows:

y=tan⁡(3x5+C)=tan⁡(35(x+5C3))

The factor x+5C3 indicates that the graph of y=tan⁡(35(x+5C3)) is a horizontal translation of y=tan⁡(3x5). Comparing the two graphs below allows us to determine the amount of this shift.

From the diagram, we see that our curve has been obtained by shifting y=tan⁡(3x5) by π3−0=π3 units to the right. Therefore:

5C3=−π3⟹C=(−π3)(35)⟹C=−π5

Since −π2<C<π2 and −π5 is in this interval, the value of C is −π5.

T=π3.

The formula for the period T of the function y=Acot⁡(Bx+C)+D is given by

T=πB.

Applying the formula of the period, we get

π3=πB⟹B=π(π3)=3.
