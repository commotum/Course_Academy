Let's list some properties of the graph:

  • There are two pieces in the graph. These two pieces are called branches of the hyperbola.

  • The branches are symmetric over the -axis and over the -axis.

  • There are two asymptotes (dashed lines in the diagram above).

  • There are exactly two -intercepts, but there is no -intercept. The graph does not intersect the -axis at all.

  • The midpoint between the intercepts is the origin, In general, the midpoint between the intercepts is called the center of the hyperbola. In this case, we say that the hyperbola is centered at the origin.
