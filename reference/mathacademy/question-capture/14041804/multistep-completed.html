Samy can choose from a total of
120
possible names.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

The number of names from which Samy chooses is a permutation.

The number of permutations of n objects taken r at a time is given by the formula

nPr=n!(n−r)!.

There are 6 letters, so n=6. We want to select 3 of those letters to make up a 3-letter word, so we have r=3.

Therefore, the number of different made-up words is

6P3=6!(6−3)!=6!3!=6⋅5⋅4⋅3!3!=6⋅5⋅4=120.

Question 7 ?
Amy rolls a fair eight-sided die. The probability that she gets a number greater than or equal to 4 or an even number is
34​
.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

Let us call G the event "We get a number greater than or equal to 4", and E the event "We get an even number". Then the probability of getting a number greater than or equal to 4 or an even number is P(G∪E), and we can apply the addition law:

P(G∪E)=P(G)+P(E)−P(G∩E)

First, let's find P(G), P(E), and P(G∩E).

  • There are 5 numbers greater than or equal to 4 among the 8 faces of the die (4,5,6,7,8), so we have P(G)=58.

  • There are 4 even numbers among the 8 faces of the die (2,4,6,8), so we have P(E)=48.

  • There are 3 numbers, 4,6 and 8, that are both greater than or equal to 4 and even. So, we have P(G∩E)=38.

Therefore, applying the addition law, we have

P(G∪E)=P(G)+P(E)−P(G∩E)=58+48−38=68=34.

Question 8 ?
Amy then rolls two fair eight-sided dice, multiplying the numbers obtained. The probability that the product is 12 or 18 is
332​
.
Submit
Correct
Explanation

Let A be the event that the product of the obtained numbers is 12, and let B be the event that the product of the obtained numbers is 18. Then the probability that the product is 12 or 18 is given by P(A∪B).

Since the product has a single value, A and B are mutually exclusive events, i.e., P(A∩B)=0.

We can find P(A) and P(B) by computing the necessary ratios. The product could be anything between 1 and 64, and there are 8⋅8=64 possible outcomes.

A product of 12 could be formed in the following 4 ways:

{(2,6),(6,2),(3,4),(4,3)}.

A product of 18 could be formed in the following 2 ways:

{(3,6),(6,3)}.

Therefore

P(A)=464,P(B)=264.

Finally, by the addition law, we have

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)=464+264−0=664=332.
