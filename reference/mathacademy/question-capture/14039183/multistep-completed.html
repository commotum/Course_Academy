Explanation

The tangent line approximation to y(x) at the point a=1 is given by

L(x)=y(a)+y′(a)(x−a)=y(1)+y′(1)(x−1).

From the initial condition, we know that y(1)=2. We also know that y′(x)=xy2, so

y′(1)=1⋅22=4.

Substituting our values for y(1) and y′(1) into our expression for L(x) gives

L(x)=2+4(x−1)=2+4x−4=4x−2.
