### q-73002

$2!\,3!=$

The given expression is a product of two factorials.

- Computing $2!$ using the definition, we get

$$
2!=2\cdot 1=2.
$$

- Computing $3!$ using the definition, we get

$$
3!=3\cdot 2\cdot 1=6.
$$

Finally, we can evaluate the given expression:

$$
\begin{aligned}2!\,3! & =2!\cdot 3! \\ & =2\cdot 6 \\ & =12\end{aligned}
$$

### q-5371

Evaluate $\frac{6!}{3!}.$

First, notice that $6!=6\cdot 5\cdot 4\cdot 3!\,.$ Then, we can cancel the $3!$ that occurs in both the numerator and the denominator:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{6!}{3!} & =\frac{6\cdot 5\cdot 4\cdot 3!}{3!} \\ & =\frac{6\cdot 5\cdot 4\cdot \cancel{3!}}{\cancel{3!}} \\ & =\frac{6\cdot 5\cdot 4}{1} \\ & =120\end{aligned}
$$
