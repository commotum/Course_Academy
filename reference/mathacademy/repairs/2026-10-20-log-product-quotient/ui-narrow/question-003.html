Assume p>0.

ln⁡(e⋅p3)=

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

1+ln⁡(p)−ln⁡(3)

Using the quotient rule for logarithms, separate the numerator and denominator:

ln⁡(e⋅p3)=ln⁡(e⋅p)−ln⁡(3).

Now use the product rule to expand the logarithm of the numerator:

ln⁡(e⋅p)−ln⁡(3)=ln⁡(e)+ln⁡(p)−ln⁡(3).

Since ln⁡(e)=1, we obtain

ln⁡(e)+ln⁡(p)−ln⁡(3)=1+ln⁡(p)−ln⁡(3).
