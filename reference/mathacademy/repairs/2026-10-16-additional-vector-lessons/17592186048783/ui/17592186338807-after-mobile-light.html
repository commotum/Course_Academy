Suppose R is a 4×5 matrix and S is a 2×4 matrix. What are the dimensions of SR?

Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2×5

There are 4 columns in S and 4 rows in R. Since the number of columns in S equals the number of rows in R, the matrices S and R are conformable for multiplication, and SR is a 2×5 matrix:

S2×4×✓R4×5=SR2×5
