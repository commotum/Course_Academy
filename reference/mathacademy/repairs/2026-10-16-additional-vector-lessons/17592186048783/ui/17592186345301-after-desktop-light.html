Let

Y=[2−10]
U=[−403]

What are the dimensions of YU?

Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

1×1

We notice that Y is a 1×3 matrix and U is a 3×1 matrix.

There are 3 columns in Y and 3 rows in U. Since the number of columns in Y equals the number of rows in U, the matrices Y and U are conformable for multiplication.

The product YU has dimensions 1×1:

Y1×3×✓U3×1=YU1×1
