Index Notation of Matrix Multiplication

It's possible to express matrix multiplication using index notation.

To see how, first let C=AB, where A and B are two matrices that are conformable for multiplication.

Then cij represents the entry at the intersection of the ith row and jth column in C.

To compute cij, we take the dot product of the ith row of A and the jth column of B, as follows:

cij=[ai1ai2⋯ain][b1jb2j⋮bnj]=ai1b1j+ai2b2j+…+ainbnj
