Review topic

Let K=MN. What is the value of k12, if

M=[5−4−32]
N=[0−12−3−2−5]?
Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

3

The full matrix product is

K=[5−4−32][0−12−3−2−5]

Multiplying the 1st row in M by the 2nd column in N, we get

k12=[5−4][−1−2]=5⋅(−1)+(−4)⋅(−2)=−5+8=3.
