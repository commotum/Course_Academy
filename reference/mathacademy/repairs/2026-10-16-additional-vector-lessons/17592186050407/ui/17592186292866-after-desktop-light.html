Let L=MN. What is the value of l22, if

M=[2−342]
N=[120−133]?

l22=

Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

14

The full matrix product is

L=[2−342][120−133]

Multiplying the 2nd row in M by the 2nd column in N, we get

l22=[42][23]=4⋅2+2⋅3=8+6=14.
