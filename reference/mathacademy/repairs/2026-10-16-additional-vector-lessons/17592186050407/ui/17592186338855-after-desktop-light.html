Let T=UV. What is the value of t41, if

U=[−14−1−2210−31−1−5310]
V=[−2−3−2−110−1−120−1]?
Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

−3

The full matrix product is

T=[−14−1−2210−31−1−5310][−2−3−2−110−1−120−1]

Multiplying the 4th row of U by the 1st column in V, we get

t41=[−5310][−2−1−1]=(−5)⋅(−2)+3⋅(−1)+10⋅(−1)=10−3−10=−3.
