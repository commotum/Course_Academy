(−25)2=

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

425

First, notice that −25=(−2)5.

To raise the fraction to the power of 2, we must raise both the numerator and the denominator to the power of 2:

(−25)2=((−2)5)2=(−2)252

Now, using (−2)2=4 and 52=25, we get

(−2)252=425.
