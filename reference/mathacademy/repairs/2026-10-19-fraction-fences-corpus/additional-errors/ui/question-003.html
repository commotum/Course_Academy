Review topic

A health technician selects random samples of newborn babies from two different hospitals. In kilograms, the babies' weights from the first and second samples are denoted as xi and yi, respectively, where the sizes of each sample are n1=9 and n2=7. The results are summarized below. ∑i=19xi=29.90,∑i=19xi2=100.99,∑i=17yi=24.7,∑i=17yi2=89.59

Given that the samples from each hospital are independent and the population variance σ2 in each hospital may be assumed to be the same, find a pooled estimate for σ2, rounded to one decimal place.

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Given two independent random samples from populations with the same variance σ2, the pooled sample variance Sp2 is an unbiased estimator of σ2 and is calculated as follows:

Sp2=(n1−1)S12+(n2−1)S22n1+n2−2

where

  • n1 and n2 are the sample sizes,
  • S12 and S22 are the sample variances.

We must first compute the estimates s12 and s22:

s12=n1n1−1(x2―−x―2)=99−1(100.999−(29.909)2)≈0.2069s22=n2n2−1(y2―−y―2)=77−1(89.597−(24.77)2)≈0.4057

Therefore, our estimate for the pooled variance is

sp2=((9−1)⋅0.2069+(7−1)⋅0.4057)/(9+7−2)=4.089414≈0.3kg2

rounded to one decimal place.
