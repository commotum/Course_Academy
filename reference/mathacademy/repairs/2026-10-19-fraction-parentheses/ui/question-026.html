Review topic

The power of quotient rule states that if we want to raise the quotient xy to some power n, we must raise the numerator and denominator to the power n as follows:

(xy)n=xnyn

For example,

(xy)4=x4y4.

To see why the power of quotient rule is true, let's take the above example and work it out again the long way. We get the same result:

(xy)4=xy⋅xy⋅xy⋅xy=x⋅x⋅x⋅xy⋅y⋅y⋅y=x4y4

The power of quotient rule is just a shortcut for the process above.
