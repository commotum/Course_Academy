Cameron spent \[math]607 in five days. If she spent an equal amount each day, how much did Cameron spend per day?

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

To determine the amount Cameron spent each day, we need to divide 607 by 5.

We start by going through the long division procedure as usual:

1215607−500―100−100―07−5―2

Notice that the remainder 2 is less than the divisor 5. But we can proceed further by adding a 0 after the decimal point in the dividend. We also add a decimal point in the quotient, as shown below:

121.5607.0−500.0―↓100.0↓−100.0―↓07.0↓−5.0―↓20

Now we complete the division, as usual, stopping once we obtain a remainder of 0:

121.45607.0−500.0―100.0−100.0―07.0−5.0―20−20―0

Therefore,

607÷5=121.4

We conclude that Cameron spent $121.40 each day.
