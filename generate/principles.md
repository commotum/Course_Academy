# Question generation principles

Build independent practice in which the learner must perform the target operation before feedback. State what the learner must choose, recall, inspect, infer, calculate or construct, then check that the displayed question still requires it. Mathematical correctness, faithful replay and unique records are necessary evidence, but none establishes this by itself.

## Authority and product requirements

This is the canonical pedagogical contract for the current pipeline. Role prompts specify phase responsibilities and evidence; numbered guides describe implementation. Topic examples apply only to their stated task. Historical run instructions and earlier agent reports are evidence, not a second specification.

Preserve the explicit product requirements: build and verify easy, moderate and hard together; retain source provenance and eligibility; leave imported questions and learner history unchanged; use the actual grader and player; and publish only through the runner's durable transport. Follow the complete-statement and empty-subset checkbox contract in [the implementation guide](docs/6-template-generator.md#response-integrity). A coverage goal does not authorize overriding these requirements.

Agents own relevant generator and shared-tool repairs. They may correct a source interpretation, earlier finding, sampling rule or implementation default with concrete mathematical or instructional evidence. Preserve the counterexample and test the intended behavior. Do not silently waive an explicit product requirement or disguise an unresolved defect as a pass. A demonstrated inherent conflict remains an explicit limitation for a product decision; incomplete source evidence alone is not such a conflict.

## Identify the task before its parameters

Use eligible target teaching, standalone questions, canonical examples and prerequisite context to establish the learner operation. Distinguish work required before answering from explanation shown afterward. A reference KP can inform design only where its learner operation, response endpoint and activity stage transfer; its labels, formulas and stopping point are not authority for the target. A compatible reference is optional. State uncertainty about the target rather than substituting a different complete task.

Retired records and structurally contained multistep parts are not standalone templates. Keep the source-eligibility ledger and trace generated descendants explicitly; a copied fragment loses its original containment. Do not restore retired content or call a reconstructed fragment a complete import. New practice needs support from eligible target evidence or a documented instructional inference. Missing imported keys, solutions or labels remain partial evidence, not inventory-repair obligations.

## Separate source replay from fresh practice

Source replay records what every usable, eligible complete source case contained at its original parameters: prompt, fields, complete bank and worked solution. Preserve the original evidence and document errors or unavailable components. Compare mathematical content separately from normalized presentation; malformed captured TeX or wrong mathematics must not be served to satisfy fidelity.

Fresh practice preserves the learner operation, valid mathematical cases and appropriate response demands. A source's particular label, target position, truth vector, answer rank, distractor offset or surviving term is not automatically a constraint. Reuse source bank recipes only while they remain valid and useful. If a recipe collides or exposes the key, repair or author the bank rather than exclude a valid mathematical family merely to protect the recipe. Keep authored replacements distinguishable in evidence; do not claim they are exact replay.

Define the method, prerequisites, response contract and comparable workload, with variable slots and dependent prompt, key, solution and diagram components. Justify each fixed instructional feature or answer-bearing assignment whose variation could change the task. Cosmetic defaults need explanation when they create a cue, restrict cases or affect presentation. Preserve a genuine invariant, not which source objects happen to satisfy it. Do not invent new operations or response demands merely to increase variety.

## Independent practice and difficulty

Preserve the target's required decisions, method selection and prerequisite recall in every band. Make easy variants manageable through simpler mathematical instances and arithmetic, not supplied decisions, substituted setups or intermediate results. Keep required procedures and formulas in the worked solution. Source support applies to its own objective and stage; a tutorial, worked solution or different KP does not automatically authorize the same support in independent practice.

A task-defining given or unfamiliar non-target fact may be supplied only when it leaves both assessed decisions and required prerequisite recall intact. Explain this boundary when target evidence is incomplete. If a fact introduces a new unrelated skill or supplies required recall, prefer a simpler instance. Genuinely guided teaching activities remain distinct from ordinary practice; missing easy anchors do not turn practice into guidance. Correct local exemplars that violate this rule rather than treating their completeness as instructional authority.

Difficulty may vary through a controlled mathematical or numerical demand while the same reasoning remains throughout. An extra condition, identity, response conversion or reasoning stage is not required to create hard. Do not compound awkward arithmetic to manufacture difficulty. Account for changed response demands, such as recognition versus exact entry. Preserve observed labels separately from authored progression and state when bands lack empirical calibration. All three bands still require verification; absent imported labels do not make a band unsupported.

Within a band, each question stands alone at comparable scope and workload. Compare actual pre-feedback obligations across every materially different learner-operation, support and response family, with final examples from all three bands. A long solution or one good representative cannot conceal a weakened subfamily.

## Meaningful variation and shortcut evidence

Count distinct learner decisions or calculations, not seeds, UUIDs, option order, cosmetic labels or unused parameters. Choose eligible answer-bearing roles and rule cases before incidental values where the mathematics permits it. Derive every dependent part from the same instance. Verify both relevant changes that should alter the response and irrelevant changes that should preserve it, where meaningful.

Probe labels, positions, option shapes, counts, layout and recognizable template signatures using only information the strategy is allowed to observe. State the learner work bypassed, subgroup selection and construction or selection mechanism. A bank-only probe cannot read the key or a catalog of correct outputs. Confirm a discovered rule on fresh independent cases or derive its invariant from construction. A directly usable cue in a genuinely finite delivered pool can be a defect without a population-wide claim; bound that conclusion to the pool. A post-hoc 2/2 or 5/5 score alone does not establish a recurring defect.

Preserve valid simplifications, cancellations, fixed outcomes and finite families when they express the intended skill. A taught efficient method is not an incidental shortcut. Do not force answer-rank, sign, endpoint or family balance quotas, invent implausible distractors, or remove legitimate cases to defeat an infeasible probe. Explain the limits of a narrow family instead of claiming broader mastery. No finite audit proves the absence of every shortcut.

Check the ordinary generation path and what survives rejection, deduplication, difficulty filtering and applicable learner selection. A replay-only branch or fixed-bank counterfactual demonstrates a different claim from normal practice coverage. Compare representative source anchors with ordinary delivered work without demanding that every imported number or bank be randomly reachable. Preserve before/after counterexamples and name inaccessible stages as limitations.

## Correctness and presentation

Use one mathematical instance and an ordered learner derivation for prompts, answers, solutions and plots. A CAS verifies mathematics separately; its normal form does not authorize an unshown or untaught conversion. Check the exact fragment requested by a blank, domains, branches and the path from displayed final solution to key. Apply a conversion negative control when a representation-changing construction makes it relevant, not as unrelated paperwork.

Follow [the shared UI math convention](../docs/math-content.md), [presentation checks](docs/presentation-checks.md), and the actual [validation tools](pipeline/validation-tools.md). Check accepted equivalent responses and plausible wrong responses through the native grader, respecting legitimate form/precision contracts. Inspect both unanswered questions and completed solutions in the actual UI. Report source fidelity, mathematics, grading, scope, progression and delivered variation as separate claims.
