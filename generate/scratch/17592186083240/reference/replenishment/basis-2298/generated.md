For the parametric curve (shown above)

$$
x=6\left(2t{e}^{t}\right),\,y=4\left({t}^{2}+2t\right),\,0\le t<\infty ,
$$

 find the derivative $\frac{dy}{dx}$ at the point $P$ on the curve where $t=1.$

We will use the formula

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{{y}^{′}(t)}{{x}^{′}(t)}.
$$

Differentiating $x$ and $y,$ we get

$$
{x}^{′}(t)=6\left(2{e}^{t}(t+1)\right),\,{y}^{′}(t)=4\left(2t+2\right).
$$

 

Therefore,

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{4\left(2t+2\right)}{6\left(2{e}^{t}(t+1)\right)}=\frac{4\left(1\right)}{6\left({e}^{t}\right)}.
$$

At the point where $t=1$ we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{dy}{dx} & =\frac{4\left(1\right)}{6\left({e}^{1}\right)} \\  & =\frac{4\left(1\right)}{6\left(e\right)}\,.\end{aligned}
$$

For the parametric curve

$$
x=3t e^{3t},\quad y=5(t^2+2)e^{-3t},\quad -\infty<t<\infty,
$$

calculate $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as a function of $t$ for $t\ne - \frac{1}{3}.$

$\frac{dy}{dx}=$
{{field-1}}

We will use the formula

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)}.
$$

Differentiating $x$ and $y,$ we get

$$
x'(t)=3\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t\right)\right)e^{3t}+3t\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(e^{3t}\right)\right),\quad y'(t)=5\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t^2+2\right)\right)e^{-3t}+5(t^2+2)\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(e^{-3t}\right)\right)
$$

$$
x'(t)=3e^{3t}+ 3t(3e^{3t}),\quad y'(t)=5(2t)e^{-3t}+5(t^2+2)(-3e^{-3t})
$$

$$
x'(t)=3(1+3t)e^{3t},\quad y'(t)=5(2t-3(t^2+2))e^{-3t}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{5(2t-3(t^2+2))e^{-3t}}{3(1+3t)e^{3t}}=\frac{5(2t-3(t^2+2))}{3(1+3t)}e^{-6t}.
$$

This derivative is defined for $t\ne - \frac{1}{3}.$

For the parametric curve

$$
x=\frac{1t}{t+1},\quad y=\frac{1t^2}{t+4},\quad t>0,
$$

calculate $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as a function of $t.$

$\frac{dy}{dx}=$
{{field-1}}

We will use the formula

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)}.
$$

Differentiating $x$ and $y,$ we get

$$
x'(t)=\frac{1\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t\right)\right)(t+1)-1t\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t+1\right)\right)}{(t+1)^2},\quad y'(t)=\frac{1\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t^2\right)\right)(t+4)-1t^2\left(\frac{d}{dt}\left(t+4\right)\right)}{(t+4)^2}
$$

$$
x'(t)=\frac{1(t+1)-1t(1)}{(t+1)^2},\quad y'(t)=\frac{2t(t+4)-1t^2(1)}{(t+4)^2}
$$

$$
x'(t)=\frac{1t+1-1t}{(t+1)^2},\quad y'(t)=\frac{2t^2+8t-1t^2}{(t+4)^2}
$$

$$
x'(t)=\frac{1}{(t+1)^2},\quad y'(t)=\frac{1t^2+8t}{(t+4)^2}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{\frac{1t^2+8t}{(t+4)^2}}{\frac{1}{(t+1)^2}}=\frac{(1t^2+8t)(t+1)^2}{1(t+4)^2}.
$$
