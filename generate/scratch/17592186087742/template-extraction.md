# Extracted templates and complete banks

Each source's exact prompt, solution, field layout, values and parameterized complete choice recipes are retained in components.json. source-replay.json and review.md compare original-parameter replay and changed-radius variants. Profile recipes in the script are ordered 5-tuples (x amplitude, y amplitude, swap sine/cosine, x shift, y shift), expressions in the positive radius r. These describe complete mathematical functions rather than vague error names. Source bank order is not a new mathematical profile.

The recognition template supplies r>0 and theta in [0,2pi). Its solution states the general centered-circle equations, substitutes the given radius and repeats the resulting equations and domain. Oriented radio adds the counterclockwise starting-point statement between the general equations and substitution. The two-blank template retains that statement and the original “Substituting r=… gives” wording, expecting r cos(theta) and r sin(theta). Source wording and ordered stages are rendered with radius slots; whitespace differences have no mathematical significance.

The sole mathematical variable slot is the radius. Observed easy values are 15, 1/2, 5, 7, 3, 1/3; observed moderate values are 5, 9, 12, 16, 4, 10. Conservative easy sampling uses exact positive rationals <=30 with denominator <=5; moderate uses positive integers <=30. Default sampled integers start at 2; explicit boundary checks cover the accepted lower easy bound 1/5 and moderate radius 2. All guards also apply to explicit parameters. Reject a whole bank if options collide or multiple options solve the question. Reciprocal/square profiles cannot sample radius 1. The same restriction follows mathematically from complete-bank uniqueness rather than silently dropping a distractor.

Center, full domain, start axis point, orientation and unit angular speed are fixed for instructional reasons: changing them would require additional solution stages. A(r,0) depends on r everywhere, including the diagram. Swapping blank/radio layouts or banks at the same radius does not produce a new task. Task fingerprints use radius plus recognition/oriented role, exclude seed, identity, formatting and bank order, and collapse moderate radio and blank aliases. A bank can have a separate stable content identity while still being excluded from replenishment as an already existing task.

Each profile must reproduce every complete source option at the original radius, ignoring order, with the original correct designation where present. q-6896's decimal comma is explicitly corrected for generator replay only; the original malformed source is preserved and excluded. Its r/2 recipe is supported by the matching other coordinate and the complete q-15802 bank. The two incomplete source profiles are evidence-only and never sampled by ordinary generation. Their missing grading and solutions are not invented.

Correctness independently reads the displayed bank, verifies circle identity, start and determinant-of-position/tangent sign, and checks the final displayed solution. Grading fixtures exercise every radio option, canonical blank fragments, equivalent grouped fragments and incorrect translated fragments through the current application grader. Radius slot coverage and a controlled 11-to-13 radius comparison check prompt, solution, field values, identities and diagrams together.
