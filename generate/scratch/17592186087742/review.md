The parametric equations of a circle  of radius $r$  centered at  the origin are

$$
x=r\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=r\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta ,\,\theta \in [0,2\pi ).
$$

In our case, the radius $r=\frac{3}{4}.$ Therefore, the parametric equations of the circle are 

$$
x=\frac{3}{4}\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=\frac{3}{4}\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta ,\,\theta \in [0,2\pi ).
$$
