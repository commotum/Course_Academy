## Original q-110040

A website receives $v(h)$ visits every $h$ hours. What are the units of $\frac{\text{d}v}{\text{d}h}?$

We have that the time $h$ is measured in hours and the number of visits is measured in visitors. 

$$
\begin{aligned}h: & \text{hours} \\ v(h): & \text{visitors}\end{aligned}
$$

The derivative $\frac{\text{d}v}{\text{d}h}$ measures a change in $v$ over a change in $h.$ So, it represents a ratio of the units:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}v}{\text{d}h}: & \frac{\text{visitors}}{\text{hours}}\end{aligned}
$$

In other words, $\frac{\text{d}v}{\text{d}h}$ is measured in "visitors per hour ".
