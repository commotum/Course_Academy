A production process uses $Q(x)$ squared meters of material to make $x$ packages. What are the units of $\frac{\text{d}Q}{\text{d}x}?$

- squared meters per package
- packages
- squared meters
- meters per package
- packages per squared meter

We have that the quantity $Q(x)$ is measured in squared meters and the input $x$ is measured in packages. 

$$
\begin{aligned}Q(x): & \text{squared meters} \\ x: & \text{packages}\end{aligned}
$$

The derivative $\frac{\text{d}Q}{\text{d}x}$ measures a change in $Q(x)$ over a change in $x.$ So, it represents a ratio of the units:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}Q}{\text{d}x}: & \frac{\text{squared meters}}{\text{packages}}\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $\frac{\text{d}Q}{\text{d}x}$ is measured in "squared meters per package".
