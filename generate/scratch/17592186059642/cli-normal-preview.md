**E — normal/authored**

Simplify the trigonometric expression $\frac{3\cdot\left(\cos^{2} x\right)+3\cdot\left(\sin^{2} x\right)}{\cos x}.$

1. $2(3\cdot\left(\sec x\right))$

2. $3\cdot\left(\cos x\right)$

3. $-(3\cdot\left(\sec x\right))$

4. $(3\cdot\left(\sec x\right))\cos x$

5. $3\cdot\left(\sec x\right)$ **✓ Correct**

Worked solution

First, we factor the numerator:

$$
\frac{3\cdot\left(\cos^{2} x\right)+3\cdot\left(\sin^{2} x\right)}{\cos x}=\frac{3\cdot(\cos^{2} x+\sin^{2} x)}{\cos x}
$$

Next, we use the Pythagorean identity to rewrite the expression in the parentheses, and then we simplify the resulting expression as much as possible.

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{3\cdot(\cos^{2} x+\sin^{2} x)}{\cos x} & =\frac{3\cdot 1}{\cos x} \\ & =\frac{3}{\cos x} \\ & =3\cdot\left(\sec x\right)\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, we conclude that

$$
\frac{3\cdot\left(\cos^{2} x\right)+3\cdot\left(\sin^{2} x\right)}{\cos x}=3\cdot\left(\sec x\right).
$$

What is $6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos^{3} t + \cos t\sin^{2} t}\right)$?

1. $(6\cos t)\cos t$

2. $\frac{6\cos t}{2}$

3. $6\sec t$

4. $6\cos t$ **✓ Correct (inferred)**

5. $-(6\cos t)$

Worked solution

First, we factor the denominator:

$$
6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos^{3} t + \cos t\sin^{2} t}\right)=6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos t(\cos^{2} t+\sin^{2} t)}\right)
$$

Next, we use the Pythagorean identity to rewrite the expression in the parentheses, and then we simplify the resulting expression as much as possible.

$$
\begin{aligned}6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos t(\cos^{2} t+\sin^{2} t)}\right) & =6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos t\cdot 1}\right) \\ & =6\cdot\left(\frac{\cancel{\cos t}\cos t}{\cancel{\cos t}}\right) \\ & =6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos t}{1}\right) \\ & =6\cos t\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, we conclude that

$$
6\cdot\left(\frac{\cos^{2} t}{\cos^{3} t + \cos t\sin^{2} t}\right)=6\cos t.
$$

$$
3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x{cos}^{3}⁡x+\operatorname{cos}⁡x{sin}^{3}⁡x+1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right)=3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x({cos}^{2}⁡x+{sin}^{2}⁡x)+1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right)

$$
\begin{aligned}3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x({cos}^{2}⁡x+{sin}^{2}⁡x)+1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x\cdot 1+1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x+1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}+\frac{1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\operatorname{cos}⁡x}{\operatorname{cos}⁡x\cdot \operatorname{cos}⁡x}+\frac{1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x\cancel{\operatorname{cos}⁡x}}{\cancel{\operatorname{cos}⁡x}\cdot \operatorname{cos}⁡x}+\frac{1}{{cos}^{2}⁡x}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\frac{\operatorname{sin}⁡x}{\operatorname{cos}⁡x}+{(\frac{1}{\operatorname{cos}⁡x})}^{2}\right) \\  & =3\cdot\left(\operatorname{tan}⁡x+{sec}^{2}⁡x\right)\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}6\cdot\left(\frac{{sin}^{2}⁡t ({sin}^{2}⁡t +{cos}^{2}⁡t )+{sin}^{2}⁡t }{{sin}^{2}⁡t \operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) & =6\cdot\left(\frac{{sin}^{2}⁡t \cdot 1+{sin}^{2}⁡t }{{sin}^{2}⁡t \operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) \\  & =6\cdot\left(\frac{2{sin}^{2}⁡t }{{sin}^{2}⁡t \operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) \\  & =6\cdot\left(\frac{2\cdot {sin}^{2}⁡t }{{sin}^{2}⁡t \cdot \operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) \\  & =6\cdot\left(\frac{2\cdot \cancel{{sin}^{2}⁡t }}{\cancel{{sin}^{2}⁡t }\cdot \operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) \\  & =6\cdot\left(\frac{2}{\operatorname{cos}⁡t }\right) \\  & =12\operatorname{sec}⁡t \end{aligned}
