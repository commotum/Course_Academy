Given that a curve is defined parametrically as
$x = 3\cos θ - 3, y = 6\sin θ + 6, 0 \le θ < 2π$,
what are the ranges of $x$ and $y$?

**selection**
- $0\le x\le 6,\,6\le y\le 12$
- $-6\le x\le 0,\,-6\le y\le 12$
- $-6\le x\le 0,\,0\le y\le 12$ (correct)
- $0\le x\le 6,\,0\le y\le 12$
- $-6\le x\le 0,\,-12\le y\le 0$

**Worked solution**

Let's consider each variable in turn.

- To find the range of $x,$ notice that for $0\le \theta <2\pi,$ we have $-1\le \operatorname{cos}\theta\le 1.$ Consequently,

$$
\begin{aligned}-1 & \le \operatorname{cos}\theta \le 1 \\ -3 & \le 3\operatorname{cos}\theta \le 3 \\ -3-3 & \le 3\operatorname{cos}\theta -3\le 3-3 \\ -6 & \le x\le 0.\end{aligned}
$$

So, we can conclude that $-6\le x\le 0.$

- To find the range of $y,$ notice that for $0\le \theta <2\pi,$ we have $-1\le \operatorname{sin}\theta\le 1.$ Consequently,

$$
\begin{aligned}-1 & \le \operatorname{sin}\theta \le 1 \\ -6 & \le 6\operatorname{sin}\theta \le 6 \\ -6+6 & \le 6\operatorname{sin}\theta +6\le 6+6 \\ 0 & \le y\le 12.\end{aligned}
$$

So, we can conclude that $0\le y\le 12.$
