The area of $ABCD$ is $34{\text{ mm}}^{2}.$ The area of the enclosing $△ABD$ is $61{\text{ mm}}^{2}.$ What is the area of $△BCD$ rounded to the nearest integer?

- $14{\text{ mm}}^{2}$
- $95{\text{ mm}}^{2}$
- $27{\text{ mm}}^{2}$ (correct)
- $61{\text{ mm}}^{2}$
- $34{\text{ mm}}^{2}$

The difference of the areas of $△ABD$ and the quadrilateral $ABCD$ gives the area of $△BCD.$ Therefore,

$$
\begin{aligned}{A}_{△BCD} & ={A}_{△ABD}-{A}_{ABCD} \\ & =61-34 \\ & =27{\text{ mm}}^{2}.\end{aligned}
$$
