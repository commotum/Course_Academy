![](images/98/98b3193783f0ac19bcb78035b133ad8dbf956528dfb7474606cc586d3bfecc34.png)

The area of $ABCD$ is $90{\text{ in}}^{2}.$   What is the area of $△BCD$ rounded to the nearest integer?

- $49\,{\text{in}}^{2}$ (correct)
- $42\,{\text{in}}^{2}$
- $34\,{\text{in}}^{2}$
- $38\,{\text{in}}^{2}$
- $45\,{\text{in}}^{2}$

Let's start by finding the area of $△ABD.$ We get 

$$
\begin{aligned}{A}_{△ABD} & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot AB\cdot AD\cdot \operatorname{sin}⁡A \\  & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot 16\cdot 21\cdot \operatorname{sin}⁡{56}^{∘} \\  & =168\cdot \operatorname{sin}⁡{56}^{∘} \\  & =139.278… \\  & ≈139{\text{ in}}^{2},\end{aligned}
$$

rounded to the nearest integer.

The difference of the areas of  $△ABD$ and the quadrilateral $ABCD$ gives the area of $△BCD.$ Therefore, 
 

$$
\begin{aligned}{A}_{△BCD} & ={A}_{△ABD}-{A}_{ABCD} \\  & =139-90 \\  & =49{\text{ in}}^{2}.\end{aligned}
$$
