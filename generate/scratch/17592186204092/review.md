Consider the equation in the variable $y,$ given by

$$
3py=5y+8
$$

where $p$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $p?$

Suppose we have an equation of the form

$$
ax=b
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $b\ne 0.$ If $a=0,$ then the equation has no solution.

First, we rewrite the equation in the form $ay=b.$ 

We start by subtracting $5y$ from both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}3py & =5y+8 \\ 3py-5y & =5y+8-5y \\ 3py-5y & =8\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we factor $y$ on the left-hand side. This gives

$$
(3p-5)y=8.
$$

Notice that this equation is now in the form $ay=b:$

$$
{{(3p-5)}_{⏟}}_{a}y={{8}_{⏟}}_{b}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
3p-5=0.
$$

Solving this for $p,$ we get

$$
\begin{aligned}3p-5 & =0 \\ 3p-5+5 & =0+5 \\ 3p & =5 \\ \frac{3p}{3} & =\frac{5}{3} \\ p & =\frac{5}{3}.\end{aligned}
$$
