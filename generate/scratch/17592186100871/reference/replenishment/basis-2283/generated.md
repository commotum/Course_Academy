Let
$A=\begin{bmatrix}-4 & -3 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & -4 & -3 & -2\end{bmatrix}$
and
$B=\begin{bmatrix}-4 & -3 & -2 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \\ -4 & -3 & -2\end{bmatrix}.$
What are the dimensions of $BA?$

selection: The product is undefined, 4\times 3, 3\times 4, 3\times 3, 4\times 4 (correct)

We notice that $A$ is a $3\times 4$ matrix and $B$ is a $4\times 3$ matrix. 

There are $3$ columns in $B$ and $3$ rows in $A.$ Since the number of columns in $B$ equals the number of rows in $A,$ the matrices $B$ and $A$ are conformable for multiplication. 

The product $BA$ has dimensions $4\times 4:$

$$
{B}_{4\times \boxed{\text{3}}}{\times }_{✓}{A}_{\boxed{\text{3}}\times 4}={BA}_{4\times 4}
$$
