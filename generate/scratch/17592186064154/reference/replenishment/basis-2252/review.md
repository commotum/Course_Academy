After charging the battery of a PC, the percentage of charge decreases at a rate of $c(t)=100\cdot {5}^{-0.4t}$ percent per hour, where $t$ is the time, in hours, since the battery had completed its charge. What will be the rate of decrease after $10$ hours?

- $\frac{8}{25}$ percent per hour
- $\frac{2}{25}$ percent per hour
- $1000$ percent per hour
- $\frac{4}{25}$ percent per hour
- $100$ percent per hour

Worked solution:

Since we want to determine the rate of decrease in the amount of charge after $10$ hours, we have $t=10.$

Thus, the rate of decrease of the amount of charge after $10$ hours will be

$$
\begin{aligned}c(10) & =100\cdot {5}^{-0.4(10)} \\  & =100\cdot {5}^{-4} \\  & =\frac{100}{625} \\  & =\frac{4}{25}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rate of decrease will be $\frac{4}{25}$ percent per hour.

The supplied rate function is $h'(x)=\frac{1}{5\sqrt{x}}$ thousand dollars per week, for $x>0$.

What was the exact rate of change of the stock's value at week $144?$

{{field-1}}$\,$ thousand dollars per week.

Worked solution:

The function ${h}^{'}(x)$ models the rate of change of the value of the stock, applicable in the domain of $h(x).$

Thus, the rate of change of the value of the stock at $x=144$ weeks is

$$
\begin{aligned}{h}^{'}(144) & =\frac{1}{5\sqrt{144}} \\  & =\frac{1}{5\cdot 12} \\  & =\frac{1}{60}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rate of change in the stock price was $\frac{1}{60}$ thousand dollars per week.

The birth rate of crocodiles in a river is modeled by $B(t) = 4 \cdot e^{0.25t}$ crocodiles per year, while the rate of death is modeled by $D(t) = 1 \cdot e^{0.25t}$ crocodiles per year, where $t$ represents the number of years since the year $2000$.

What was the rate of change of the crocodile population in $2004$? Round your answer to the nearest integer.

> A scientific calculator is required to answer this question.

- $8$ crocodiles per year
- $-8$ crocodiles per year
- $-3$ crocodiles per year
- $14$ crocodiles per year
- $11$ crocodiles per year

Worked solution:

The rate of change of the crocodile population is the birth rate minus the death rate.

$$
B(t)-D(t).
$$

Since $2004$ is $4$ years after $2000$, substitute $t=4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}B(4)-D(4) & =4\cdot e^{0.25(4)}-1\cdot e^{0.25(4)} \\  & =3\cdot e^{1} \\  & \approx 8.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rate of change is $8$ crocodiles per year.

The rate $F$ at which a swimming pool is being filled can be modeled as $F(t) = 4t^{2} - 3t$ liters per minute, where $t$ is the time, in minutes, since the pool began to be filled. The rate at which water is leaking from the pool can be modeled as $L(t) = \frac{1}{4}t^{2}$ liters per minute, where $t$ is also the time, in minutes, since the pool began to be filled.

What is the rate of change of the volume of water in the pool $8$ minutes after the pool began to be filled?

> A scientific calculator is required to answer this question.

- $232$ liters per minute
- $216$ liters per minute
- $-216$ liters per minute
- $248$ liters per minute
- $-16$ liters per minute

Worked solution:

The rate of change of the volume of water is the filling rate minus the leaking rate.

$$
F(t)-L(t).
$$

Substituting $t=8$,

$$
\begin{aligned}F(8)-L(8) & =4(8)^{2}-3(8)-\frac{1}{4}(8)^{2} \\  & =232-16 \\  & =216.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rate of change is $216$ liters per minute.

A factory begins work at $8\text{:}00$ am. Its order rate is $O(t)=50t e^{-0.1t}$ cars per hour and its completion rate is $C(t)=80+20\sin(2\pi t/2)$ cars per hour, where $t$ is hours after work begins. What is the rate of change in the number of cars under construction at the 24-hour time $8\text{:}30$? Orders enter construction immediately. Round your answer to the nearest integer.

- $124$ cars per hour
- $76$ cars per hour
- $-100$ cars per hour
- $-76$ cars per hour
- $24$ cars per hour

Worked solution:

The rate of change is the order rate minus the completion rate. The elapsed time is $t=(510-480)/60=0.5$ hours.

$$
\begin{aligned}O(0.5)-C(0.5) & =50(0.5){e}^{-0.1(0.5)}-(80+20\sin(\frac{2\pi }{2}(0.5))) \\  & =50(0.5){e}^{-0.05}-80-20\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}) \\  & =50(0.5){e}^{-0.05}-100 \\  & \approx -76.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the rate of change is $-76$ cars per hour.
