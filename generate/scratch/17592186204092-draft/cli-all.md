Consider the equation in the variable $x,$ given by

$$
4px=19x+2
$$

where $p$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $p?$

Worked solution

Suppose we have an equation of the form

$$
ax=b
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $b\ne 0.$ If $a=0,$ then the equation has no solution.

First, we rewrite the equation in the form $ax=b.$ 

We start by subtracting $19x$ from both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}4px & =19x+2 \\ 4px-19x & =19x+2-19x \\ 4px-19x & =2\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we factor $x$ on the left-hand side. This gives

$$
(4p-19)x=2.
$$

Notice that this equation is now in the form $ax=b:$

$$
\underbrace{(4p-19)}_{a}x=\underbrace{2}_{b}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
4p-19=0.
$$

Solving this for $p,$ we get

$$
\begin{aligned}4p-19 & =0 \\ 4p-19+19 & =0+19 \\ 4p & =19 \\ \frac{4p}{4} & =\frac{19}{4} \\ p & =\frac{19}{4}.\end{aligned}
$$
