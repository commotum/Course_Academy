Consider the equation in the variable $x,$ given by

$$
4px-3=11x+1
$$

where $p$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $p?$

$p=$
{{field-1}}

Field `field-1` (:answer-field.type/blank)

* $\frac{11}{4}$

Worked solution

Suppose we have an equation of the form

$$
ax=b
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $b\ne 0.$ If $a=0,$ then the equation has no solution.

First, we rewrite the equation in the form $ax=b.$ 

We start by subtracting $11x$ from both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}4px-3 & =11x+1 \\ 4px-11x-3 & =11x+1-11x \\ 4px-11x-3 & =1\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we add $3$ to both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}4px-11x-3 & =1 \\ 4px-11x-3+3 & =1+3 \\ 4px-11x & =4\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we factor $x$ on the left-hand side. This gives

$$
(4p-11)x=4.
$$

Notice that this equation is now in the form $ax=b:$

$$
\underbrace{(4p-11)}_{a}x=\underbrace{4}_{b}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
4p-11=0.
$$

Solving this for $p,$ we get

$$
\begin{aligned}4p-11 & =0 \\ 4p-11+11 & =0+11 \\ 4p & =11 \\ \frac{4p}{4} & =\frac{11}{4} \\ p & =\frac{11}{4}.\end{aligned}
$$

Consider the equation in the variable $x,$ given by

$$
2px+5=-11x-6
$$

where $p$ is a constant. Given that this equation has no solution, what is the value of $p?$

$p=$

* $- \frac{11}{2}$

Worked solution

Suppose we have an equation of the form

$$
ax=b
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $b\ne 0.$ If $a=0,$ then the equation has no solution.

First, we rewrite the equation in the form $ax=b.$ 

We start by adding $11x$ to both sides of the equation:

$$
\begin{aligned}2px+5 & =-11x-6 \\ 2px+11x+5 & =-11x-6+11x \\ 2px+11x+5 & =-6\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we subtract $5$ from both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}2px+11x+5 & =-6 \\ 2px+11x+5-5 & =-6-5 \\ 2px+11x & =-11\end{aligned}
$$

Next, we factor $x$ on the left-hand side. This gives

$$
(2p+11)x=-11.
$$

Notice that this equation is now in the form $ax=b:$

$$
\underbrace{(2p+11)}_{a}x=\underbrace{-11}_{b}
$$

Therefore, the equation has no solution if

$$
2p+11=0.
$$

Solving this for $p,$ we get

$$
\begin{aligned}2p+11 & =0 \\ 2p+11-11 & =0-11 \\ 2p & =-11 \\ \frac{2p}{2} & =\frac{-11}{2} \\ p & =- \frac{11}{2}.\end{aligned}
$$
