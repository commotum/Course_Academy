## q-15707

Original

$\int \frac{2}{x+1}\,\text{d}x=$

First, let $u=x+1.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=1\,⟹\,\text{d}u=\text{d}x.
$$

We can now rewrite the integral in terms of $u,$ solve it, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int \frac{2}{x+1}\,\text{d}x & =2\int \frac{1}{x+1}\,\text{d}x \\  & =2\int \frac{1}{u}\,\text{d}u \\  & =2\operatorname{ln}⁡|u|+C \\  & =2\operatorname{ln}⁡|x+1|+C\end{aligned}
$$
