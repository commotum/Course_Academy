Computing the divergence of $F,$ we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}\text{div}\,F & =\frac{∂}{∂x}({e}^{z})+\frac{∂}{∂y}({x}^{2}{y}^{2})+\frac{∂}{∂z}(4{x}^{2}yz) \\  & =0+2 x^{2} y+4 x^{2} y \\  & =6{x}^{2}y.\end{aligned}
$$

Let $R$ be the solid region enclosed by $S.$ Then, using the divergence theorem, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}{∬}_{S}F\cdot dS & ={∭}_{R}6{x}^{2}y\,\text{d}V \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}{\int }_{2}^{3}{\int }_{-1}^{1}6{x}^{2}y\,\text{d}z\,\text{d}y\,\text{d}x \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}3{x}^{2}\,\text{d}x\cdot {\int }_{2}^{3}2y\,\text{d}y\cdot {\int }_{-1}^{1}\text{d}z \\  & =[{x}^{3}]_{x=2}^{x=3}\cdot [{y}^{2}]_{y=2}^{y=3}\cdot (1-(-1)) \\  & =(27-8)\cdot (9-4)\cdot 2 \\  & =190.\end{aligned}
$$
