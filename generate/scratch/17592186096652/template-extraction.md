Standard source rows and reorder rows are kept separately instead of letting CAS simplification erase stages.

Easy bounds: positive integer factors 1–4; two positive independent length multiples. Observed moderate supports magnitude <=4 with denominator 1,2,4 and fixed source signs. Each hard item therefore requires two independent ratio readings and correct assignment after reordered path addition. No within-level progression or optional new branch.
