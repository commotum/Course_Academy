Write the following first-order linear ODE in standard form.

$$
\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 16 y^{2} \cos^{2}{(5 x )}=(- 4 y \cos{(5 x )} + 3)^{2}\,.
$$

- [x] $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 24 y \cos{(5 x )}=9$
- [ ] $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 4 y \cos{(5 x )}=- 12 \cos{(5 x )} + 9$
- [ ] $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 16 y \cos^{2}{(5 x )}=(- 4 \cos{(5 x )} + 3)^{2}$
- [ ] $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}=9$
- [ ] $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 48 y \cos{(5 x )}=9$

A first-order linear ODE is in standard form if it is written as

$$
\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}+P(x)y=Q(x).
$$

Converting the given equation to standard form, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 16 y^{2} \cos^{2}{(5 x )}&=(- 4 y \cos{(5 x )} + 3)^{2} \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} + 16 y^{2} \cos^{2}{(5 x )}&=16 y^{2} \cos^{2}{(5 x )} - 24 y \cos{(5 x )} + 9 \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}&=- 24 y \cos{(5 x )} + 9 \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}+\underbrace{(24 \cos{(5 x )})}_{P(x)}y&=\underbrace{9}_{Q(x)}\end{aligned}
$$

Now, the equation is written in standard form with $P(x)=24 \cos{(5 x )}$ and $Q(x)=9.$
