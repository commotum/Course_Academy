### q-120311

Parameters: {"radius": "4", "angle": "225", "unit": "degrees"}. Observed rating: :question.difficulty/moderate.

Original prompt:

The point $P$ has polar coordinates $(4,{225}^{∘}).$ Find the Cartesian coordinates of $P.$

Field collection order: selection radio.

Original solution: To convert from polar coordinates to Cartesian coordinates, we use

$$
x=r\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=r\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta .
$$

First, we calculate the $x$-coordinate of $P:$

$$
\begin{aligned}x & =r\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta  \\  & =4\operatorname{cos}⁡({225}^{∘}) \\  & =4(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) \\  & =-2\sqrt{2}\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we calculate the $y$-coordinate of $P:$

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =r\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta  \\  & =4\operatorname{sin}⁡({225}^{∘}) \\  & =4(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) \\  & =-2\sqrt{2}\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the Cartesian coordinates of $P$ are $(-2\sqrt{2},-2\sqrt{2}).$
