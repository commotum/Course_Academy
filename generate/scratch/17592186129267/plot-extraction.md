# Projection plots: four extraction stages

1. **Specific plots.** All source plots are 360×304 PNGs with white/transparent backgrounds, thin black Cartesian axes and arrowheads, italic x/y labels, pale blue fill and medium blue boundary. The circle is centered at the origin. Full disks: radius 3 (q-39481/q-161604), 1 (q-161605), sqrt(2) (q-161607). Left half disk radius 3: q-161611. Upper half disk radius sqrt(5): q-161612. Upper-left quarter disk radius 1: q-161613. Canonical e-11470 is an upper half disk radius 2. All seven distinct rasters were opened at original resolution in the white-composited `source-plots.png`; axis directions, actual marked signed intercepts, shaded side and circle equation were inspected. q-161520 has no captured solution plot.
2. **Shared pattern.** Projection establishes constant radial range and angular sector; it is a worked-solution aid, not a separate graph-reading demand. Geometry is circular with equal axis scale.
3. **Components.** Origin, arrowed positive axes, sector arc and closing radii, blue fill, boundary x²+y²=S, signed sqrt(S) intercepts only on included axis portions. The source circle equation shifts left for left-hand sectors. Radius and equation are mathematical components, not decoration.
4. **Reusable spec.** `projection(template,parameters)` takes exact model angular bounds and S, uses Matplotlib equal aspect, draws the sector polygon and axis arrows, computes all labels from S, derives plotting range from radius, saves under content hash with unique temporary filename and atomic rename. Cache requires PNG and versioned sidecar. Raw source replay retains original image paths; normal records use regenerated local images. No SVG or image models; no cache pruning. No parameter-independent reuse when radius/sector changes. Sidecars record actual Matplotlib version and geometry.
