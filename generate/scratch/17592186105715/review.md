## q-49097 original

Calculate $\int \frac{\text{d}x}{9x}.$

First, we apply the constant factor rule and take the coefficient $\frac{1}{9}$ out of the integral. Then we compute the integral of $\frac{1}{x},$ and get

$$
\begin{aligned}\int \frac{\text{d}x}{9x} & =\frac{1}{9}\int \frac{\text{d}x}{x} \\  & =\frac{1}{9}\int \frac{1}{x}\,\text{d}x \\  & =\frac{1}{9}ln⁡|x|+C.\end{aligned}
$$
