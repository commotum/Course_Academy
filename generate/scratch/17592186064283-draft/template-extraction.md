It is evidence_only and ineligible for learning.

C is the scalar magnitude, N the power, M=N−1, and K=|C N|. Original coefficient signs stay fixed by each template. All source prose and intermediate chain stages are retained across normal parameters; original templates do not use an original-anchor bypass. Square source families keep N=2 and their shorter u stages. Higher-power families keep N in 3–8 and their complete intermediate stages. The trigonometric function and its argument remain fixed by the source template because they determine the inner derivative and original complete bank pattern. The cosine source radio profiles remain separately selectable by template; they never mix error pools.
