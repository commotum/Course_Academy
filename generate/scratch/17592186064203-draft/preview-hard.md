$\int 11(48-7x)^{8}\,\text{d}x=$

- $\frac{11}{63}(48-7x)^{9}+C$
- $-\frac{11}{7}(48-7x)^{9}+C$
- $11(48-7x)^{9}+C$
- $\frac{11}{7}(48-7x)^{9}+C$
- $-\frac{11}{63}(48-7x)^{9}+C$

Let $u=48-7x.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=-7\,⟹\,\text{d}x=-\frac{1}{7}\text{d}u.
$$

We now write our integral in terms of $u,$ calculate the integral as normal, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ at the very end:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int 11(48-7x)^{8}\,\text{d}x & =\int 11{u}^{8}\cdot (-\frac{1}{7})\text{d}u \\  & =11\cdot (-\frac{1}{7})\int {u}^{8}\cdot \,\text{d}u \\  & =-\frac{11}{7}\int {u}^{8}\,\text{d}u \\  & =-\frac{11}{7}\cdot \frac{{u}^{9}}{9}+C \\  & =-\frac{11}{63}{u}^{9}+C \\  & =-\frac{11}{63}(48-7x)^{9}+C\end{aligned}
$$

Let's double-check that our result is correct. If we differentiate the result, then it should come out to the original integrand:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[-\frac{11}{63}(48-7x)^{9}+C] & =-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[\frac{11}{63}(48-7x)^{9}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =-\frac{11}{7}(48-7x)^{8}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(48-7x)+0 \\  & =-\frac{11}{7}(48-7x)^{8}\cdot (-7) \\  & =11(48-7x)^{8}\,✓\end{aligned}
$$
