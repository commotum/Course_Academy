Which of the following equations has no solutions?

I. $y-9=y-9$

II. $-5y-2=-5y+7$

III. $6y-1=-7y-8$

Let's attempt to solve each equation in turn:

- For the first equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}y-9 & =y-9 \\ y-9-y & =y-9-y \\ -9 & =-9.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is true for all values of $y.$

- For the second equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-5y-2 & =-5y+7 \\ -5y-2+5y & =-5y+7+5y \\ -2 & =7.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is false for all values of $y.$ Therefore, the equation $-5y-2=-5y+7$ has no solutions.

- For the third equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}6y-1 & =-7y-8 \\ 6y-1+7y+1 & =-7y-8+7y+1 \\ 13y & =-7 \\ y & =- \frac{7}{13}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation $6y-1=-7y-8$ has the unique solution $y=- \frac{7}{13}.$

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."

Which of the following equations has no solutions?

I. $-4x-8=-4x-8$

II. $-4x-8=-4x-3$

III. $2x+7=-x-9$

Let's attempt to solve each equation in turn:

- For the first equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-4x-8 & =-4x-8 \\ -4x-8+4x & =-4x-8+4x \\ -8 & =-8.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is true for all values of $x.$

- For the second equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-4x-8 & =-4x-3 \\ -4x-8+4x & =-4x-3+4x \\ -8 & =-3.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is false for all values of $x.$ Therefore, the equation $-4x-8=-4x-3$ has no solutions.

- For the third equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}2x+7 & =-x-9 \\ 2x+7+x-7 & =-x-9+x-7 \\ 3x & =-16 \\ x & =- \frac{16}{3}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation $2x+7=-x-9$ has the unique solution $x=- \frac{16}{3}.$

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."

Which of the following equations has no solutions?

I. $7y+5=7y+5$

II. $-y+6=-y+5$

III. $-y+1=-3y+4$

Let's attempt to solve each equation in turn:

- For the first equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}7y+5 & =7y+5 \\ 7y+5-7y & =7y+5-7y \\ 5 & =5.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is true for all values of $y.$

- For the second equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-y+6 & =-y+5 \\ -y+6+y & =-y+5+y \\ 6 & =5.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is false for all values of $y.$ Therefore, the equation $-y+6=-y+5$ has no solutions.

- For the third equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-y+1 & =-3y+4 \\ -y+1+3y-1 & =-3y+4+3y-1 \\ 2y & =3 \\ y & =\frac{3}{2}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation $-y+1=-3y+4$ has the unique solution $y=\frac{3}{2}.$

Therefore, the correct answer is "II only."
