{{field-1}}

Answer: $-6$

For two lines to be parallel, they must have the same slope.

- The line $y = 2x + 5$ is in slope-intercept form, and its slope is $2$.
- To find the slope of the line $kx + 3y = 9$, we convert to slope-intercept form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
kx + 3y &= 9 \\
3y &= -kx + 9 \\
y &= -\frac{k}{3}x + 3
\end{aligned}
$$

In slope-intercept form, the slope is the $x$-coefficient. So, the slope is $-\dfrac{k}{3}$.

Since the slopes of the two lines must be equal, we must have

$$
-\dfrac{k}{3}=2
$$

Multiplying both sides by $-3$ gives $k=-6$.

Consequently, $k=-6$.
