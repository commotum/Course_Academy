Calculate the area ${A}_{△}$ of the triangle spanned by $p=⟨-3,6,0⟩$ and $q=⟨4,3,-6⟩.$

${A}_{△}=${{field-1}}

\frac{3\sqrt{301}}{2}

Using the formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}p\times q & =|\begin{aligned}i & j & k \\ -3 & 6 & 0 \\ 4 & 3 & -6\end{aligned}| \\  & =|\begin{aligned}6 & 0 \\ 3 & -6\end{aligned}|i-|\begin{aligned}-3 & 0 \\ 4 & -6\end{aligned}|j+|\begin{aligned}-3 & 6 \\ 4 & 3\end{aligned}|k \\  & =(6\cdot (-6)-0\cdot 3)i-((-3)\cdot (-6)-0\cdot 4)j+((-3)\cdot 3-6\cdot 4)k \\  & =-36i-18j-33k \\  & =⟨-36,-18,-33⟩.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the area of the corresponding parallelogram is

$$
\begin{aligned}A & =|\,p\times q\,| \\  & =\sqrt{(-36)^{2}+(-18)^{2}+(-33)^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{1296+324+1089} \\  & =\sqrt{2709} \\  & =3\sqrt{301}.\end{aligned}
$$

The area of the triangle is half of the area of the respective parallelogram. So, the area of the triangle is

$$
{A}_{△}=\frac{1}{2}A=\frac{3\sqrt{301}}{2}.
$$
