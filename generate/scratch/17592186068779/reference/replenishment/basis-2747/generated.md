Which of the following is the graph of $y =-\frac{2}{3x} - 2$?

1. ![](images/57/576375b288bf3875e96ff106ddc728f7f348310ba2dfe17e6e21359bbb608323.png)

2. ![](images/fe/fe999997b5d5d3a5dabe9c5f984f29bc6bf803896c45077991c31d36788bc264.png)

3. ![](images/4b/4b1faed595985fc4d15a404939f1c5c6fb6f605ae703b0d3fe436c9ff162add7.png)

4. ![](images/ad/ad1b77de19b2a3c4b11cc8f04f8bbc36c93e508c90685a6d3a42e0f21d7ab7d2.png)

5. ![](images/ef/ef104c57bb740de878b007110c3d8e3ecb4205eb5422f066553fe4f79885e15f.png)

Worked solution:

To plot $y=-\frac{2}{3x}-2,$ we apply the following steps:

- Take the curve $y=-\frac{1}{x}.$

![](images/f9/f991da5a7c524fd08efcdb83713f1f6e8cee69165a5f80bb15446649bee7dc92.png)

- Then, stretch the result by a scale factor of $\frac{2}{3}$ parallel to the $y$-axis to get the graph of $y=-\frac{2}{3x}.$

![](images/dd/dd8bf4c9f560a9fdb9de43529cd42b19832b4d9060809a6fce90d388364724e5.png)

- Translate it by $2$ units down to get the graph of $y=-\frac{2}{3x}-2.$

![](images/57/576375b288bf3875e96ff106ddc728f7f348310ba2dfe17e6e21359bbb608323.png)

- Then, stretch the result by a scale factor of $\frac{4}{3}$ parallel to the $y$-axis to get the graph of $y=-\frac{4}{3x}.$
