# Template extraction

`templates.json` retains every source prompt, solution, field, owned choice and exact value with its profile. There is one complete profile per layout and checkbox field. Mathematical components are independently parsed. `render` in the generator implements the numeric slots as simultaneous replacements and records conditions per layout. Sparse source evidence limits inference: numeric distractor extensions are expression patterns, not asserted diagnoses of errors.

Source q-6294 is captured but excluded; its duplicate fields prevent supported full replay. All 13 supported banks must replay without missing or extra options. q-101734 alone receives the documented finite-bound correction, justified by the definition of truncating an improper integral. No plot extraction/rendering is required.

Easy variants change odd root degree (3–21), positive slope, radicand shift, or a logarithmic lower cutoff. Moderate variants change one endpoint with all finite evaluations updated. Exponents and other integrals stay fixed in moderate. Every distinct task is normalized by integrands and endpoints; variable names and field/answer order do not count.
