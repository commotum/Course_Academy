### Easy — cos_reciprocal (authored coefficient extension; authored; eligible authored practice; bank cos_reciprocal_nonunit)

Simplify the trigonometric expression $\cos x(\cos x+\frac{5}{\cos x})+\sin^{2}x.$

- $6$ **correct**

- $-6$

- $5$

- $0$

- $1$



First, we expand the parentheses:

$$
\cos x(\cos x+\frac{5}{\cos x})+\sin^{2}x = \cos^{2}x+\cos x\cdot\frac{5}{\cos x}+\sin^{2}x
$$

Next, we group the $\sin^2 x$ and $\cos^2 x$ terms.

$$
\cos^{2}x+\cos x\cdot\frac{5}{\cos x}+\sin^{2}x = 5+(\cos^{2}x+\sin^{2}x)
$$

Then, we use $\sin^2{x}+\cos^2{x}=1$ to simplify the expression in the parentheses:

$$
5+(\cos^{2}x+\sin^{2}x) = 6
$$

Therefore, we conclude that

$$
\cos x(\cos x+\frac{5}{\cos x})+\sin^{2}x = 6.
$$
