# Plot extraction and generation

All six practice rasters and canonical raster were inspected at full native resolution, plus introduction and both derivation diagrams. plot-extraction.json records paths, native dimensions and SHA256. Original numerical labels were read directly: q-18021 A128/AB3/AC7, q-18023 B20/AB5/BC13, q-18027 A11/AB26/AC22, q-279752 B40/AB4/BC8, q-279753 A120/AB18ft/AC12ft, q-280260 B60/AB5/BC5; canonical C31/CB58/CA40. Original diagrams are schematic and often not to scale (notably the5,13,20° source). We do not infer numerical lengths from those pixels. The numeric side/angle labels determine the actual geometric recipe. Black triangle edges, vertex labels, side lengths, and colored included-angle sectors are meaningful; original rotation, incidental color shade and triangle orientation are not assessed.

General pattern: three labeled vertices, two supplied adjacent side lengths, included-angle sector, integer degree label; feet appears on both supplied sides only in the ft template. All changing assessment geometry is freshly reconstructed from the same question parameters. No answer-choice or solution images exist. Rating moderate: recurring assessment-bearing diagrams.

Renderer: Matplotlib3.11.2 Agg, equal axes scale,6×4in,150DPI (900×600px), white background, black edges, green included-angle sector. Included vertex is at origin; first side has15° orientation; second side is rotated by the exact included angle. Length labels lie on outward normals to their actual edges; vertex labels lie outward from the centroid. Narrow sectors (angle below35° or adjacent-side ratio above2.5) use an exterior angle-number callout with a dotted leader, keeping the number clear of incident sides; other angles use a bisector label. Bounds include geometry and all label anchors with length-relative padding. Text extents are measured after the actual renderer draw; any clip or text overlap rejects the whole instance. Side lengths/cross-product area of constructed vertices are independently checked against the displayed solution components. This geometric cross-product check is QA only, never a learner method or assessment branch.

Hard hides the included-angle number and labels the two base angles instead. Equal sides and equal base angles are enforced, so both the drawn triangle and supplied numerical givens are consistent. The included-angle sector identifies the angle to deduce without adding another question.

A content-addressed PNG plus JSON sidecar includes exact parameters, geometry bounds, data vertices, actual renderer label/axes extents, DPI, dependency/runtime versions and PNG SHA256. A cache requires both files. Fresh concurrent same/different renders use process-local unique temporary files, atomic writes and an independent private validation directory. Each final filename depends on the exact instance and renderer settings; no shared render-input file exists. No SVG, image model, installation or shared environment modification occurs. Generated easy/moderate/hard PNGs were visually inspected at full900×600 resolution: labels, units, supplied angles, sectors and unclipped geometry were legible.
