## raw source q-101719

Given that $y=7-4{log}_{3}x$, find the instantaneous rate of change at $x=\frac{1}{\operatorname{ln}3}.$


**Worked solution**

To differentiate a logarithm with base $a,$ we use the formula

$$
\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({log}_{a}x)=\frac{1}{x\operatorname{ln}a}.
$$

To find the instantaneous rate of change at a given point, we have to find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ at that point.

Taking the derivative we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(7-4{log}_{3}x) \\  & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(7)-4\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({log}_{3}x) \\  & =0-4\cdot \frac{1}{x\operatorname{ln}3} \\  & =-\frac{4}{x\operatorname{ln}3}\,.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the instantaneous rate of change at $x=1/\operatorname{ln}3$ is 

$$
\begin{aligned}{\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}|}_{x=1/\operatorname{ln}3} & ={(-\frac{4}{x\operatorname{ln}3})\,|}_{x=1/\operatorname{ln}3} \\  & =-\frac{4}{1} \\  & =-4.\end{aligned}
$$
