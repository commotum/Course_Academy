## easy — authored-easy

Which of the following lines is perpendicular to $y=10x+8?$

- $y=-10x+7$
- $y=10x-2$
- $y=-\frac{1}{10}x-9$ **Correct**
- $y=11$
- $y=\frac{1}{10}x-5$

**Worked solution**

For the two lines to be perpendicular, their slopes must multiply to $-1.$

The line $y=10x+8$ is in slope-intercept form, and its slope is $10.$

Looking at the answer choices, the line $y=-\frac{1}{10}x-9$ has a slope of $-\frac{1}{10}.$

Multiplying the slopes together, we have

$$
10\cdot -\frac{1}{10}=-1.
$$

Since the slopes multiply to $-1,$ we conclude that the line $y=-\frac{1}{10}x-9$ is perpendicular to the given line.

$$
-\frac{9}{8}\cdot \frac{8}{9}=-1.
