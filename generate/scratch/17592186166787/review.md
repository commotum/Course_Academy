Evaluate the integral
$\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\ln 2}\frac{\cosh x}{3 + \sinh x}dx$.

\ln (\frac{2}{3})
\ln 4
3\ln (\frac{4}{5})
\ln (\frac{5}{4})
\ln (\frac{3}{3})

Let $u=3+\sinh x$. Differentiating gives $\mathrm{d}u=\cosh x\,\mathrm{d}x$.

When $x=0$, $u=3$. When $x=\ln 2$,

$$u=3+\frac{e^{\ln 2}-e^{-\ln 2}}{2}
=3+\frac{2-\frac{1}{2}}{2}=\frac{15}{4}.$$

Therefore,

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^{\ln 2}\frac{\cosh x}{3+\sinh x}\,\mathrm{d}x
&=\int_3^{\frac{15}{4}}\frac{1}{u}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&=\left[\ln u\right]_3^{\frac{15}{4}} \\
&=\ln(\frac{15}{4})-\ln 3 \\
&=\ln\left(\frac{5}{4}\right).
\end{aligned}$$

Here $u>0$ throughout the interval, so the logarithm and substitution are defined.

\int_0^{\ln 12}\frac{\cosh x}{3+\sinh x}\,\mathrm{d}x
&=\int_3^{\frac{215}{24}}\frac{1}{u}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&=\left[\ln u\right]_3^{\frac{215}{24}} \\
&=\ln(\frac{215}{24})-\ln 3 \\
&=\ln\left(\frac{215}{72}\right).
\end{aligned}$$

Here $u>0$ throughout the interval, so the logarithm and substitution are defined.
