# Four-stage extraction

## 1. Specific original descriptions

`classification.md` lists every original integrand, captured level, prompt shape and final chain length. `source.json` preserves every original prompt, prose sentence, math stage, bank value, UUID and correct-or-missing designation. `templates.json` contains the complete prompt/solution masks and the five original values for each source; `bank-recipes.json` maps every option one-to-one to its source field, original value and answer UUID, expression mask, and exact coefficient/exponent expression in the inner quantity u. Nothing is omitted from the original banks. The coefficient/exponent recipes are observed expression patterns; an original learner's intended error cannot be uniquely recovered from a numerical primitive.

The coefficient k outside, leading inner coefficient a, inner degree m, outer exponent n, inner term order, differential notation, initial prose operands, prompt's Calculate/equation layout, and per-family solution row count all matter. q-72386 explicitly leaves 2 attached to the power while isolating x⁷ dx = du/8; its solution is not rewritten into the otherwise common 2x⁷ dx = du/4 presentation. q-113715 and q-72467 retain their square-root-of-cube representations after the fractional exponent rows. Source answers are complete antiderivatives including arbitrary constant C, so distinctness must be assessed modulo additive constants, not literal expression equality.

q-12494 lacks a worked solution, difficulty and correct link; its raw prompt contains the actual malformed command `\intx`. The authoritative local lesson 443 JSON widget was inspected: it confirms a five-choice multiple-choice response and the intended integral ∫x(x²+5)^10 dx, with no captured correct designation or worked solution. Its exact widget is privately retained in `reference/authoritative-q12494.json` with original file path/hash. Its raw bank still has five distinct primitives and exactly one derivative matching the intended x(x²+5)^10: (x²+5)^11/22+C. That identification is mathematical authorship, never a captured designation. Its public raw replay therefore retains all three omissions and the original malformed prompt, with `evidence_only:true` and `eligible_for_learning:false`.

q-113715 drops +C from one intermediate primitive row, then restores it in later rows. Exact raw evidence retains it. Normal output adds +C to that row, making the arbitrary-constant convention consistent. The family operation sequence is unchanged.

## 2. Generalized pattern

Given an indefinite integral k x^(m−1)(a x^m+b)^n dx, recognize that the outside factor is a constant multiple of the derivative of u=a x^m+b. Isolate the differential factor k/(a m), rewrite the integral in u, apply the power rule, simplify the coefficient, and substitute back. The square-root families additionally write √u=u^(1/2) and finish with u^(3/2)=√(u³) on u>0. The learner selects one complete primitive from the five-choice radio bank. This is the sole assessed method.

## 3. Component and bank mapping

For each row of `bank-recipes.json`, `all_displayed_choices` records every original choice, its mask and exact parameterized mathematical expression. The source banks are complete profiles selected before parameters. For these conservative replay profiles, only the inner constant is variable; numeric bank coefficients and exponents are source constants and do not depend on b. This reproduces the exact observed expressions without inventing an arbitrary polynomial fit.

q-12496's 1/44 coefficient has no uniquely defensible error operation. It remains in its full public `source:q-12496` bank as ineligible evidence. Its normal profile uses a separately authored five-option method bank: correct r u^(n+1)/(n+1), omit r, invert r, omit division by n+1, and retain the old exponent using r u^n/n. The same complete authored operation bank is used for hard. These profiles never mix a first-valid subset of candidates. Fixed factor ratios in their bounded domains guarantee five derivative-distinct options; validation checks the actual displayed values for the entire domain.

The other original banks have concrete degree/power/scaling operations mapped in `bank-recipes.json`, with error-intent uncertainty explicitly retained. Their original coefficients/exponents are frozen, so generalization does not pretend to discover how those coefficients should change under unobserved degree or power changes. The normal completed q-12494 retains its complete original bank and supplies the mathematically derived designation.

## 4. Reusable masks and slots

The fixed backbone is source prose plus ordered substitution, formal derivative/differential, three integration rows and the original family-specific primitive chain. `@@BASE@@` is the single complete polynomial slot. The allowed nonzero integer b belongs to [−40,40]; a, k, m and n stay fixed per family. All prose mentions, substitutions, prompts, repeated integrals, returned primitives and choices use the same whole-polynomial slot. No short numeral or function-label replacement is performed across source text. The separately authored q-12494 solution uses the q-2388 integer-power stage sequence with complete exponent and coefficient slots. Hard has explicit unevaluated masks for its negative factors and fractional arithmetic; CAS simplification does not construct those displayed stages.

At source parameters, `source:<MA-ID>` replays every original field attribute presence, full bank, exact prompt and exact solution or omission. Passing a different b through the same public API exercises the genuinely parameterized source recipe; it is not an anchor-only literal return. `source-cases.json` declares the original replay parameters and authored routing for the unrated legacy record. Normal profiles keep the original prose and stages with the documented completion, dropped-constant repair and opaque-bank substitution. Review includes each raw original, changed source mask, normal anchor and changed normal variant, plus two sampled examples at each difficulty.

Mathematical fingerprints use the expanded actual assessed integrand and exclude bank, layout, profile, option order and integration-constant aliases. Content IDs include version, actual prompt, actual fixed solution, difficulty or its source omission, complete sorted displayed bank, and correct designation or its omission. Seed and option order cannot change content identity. Fields and choices have owned UUID identities; correct refs point to an owned choice. Strict public guards reject bool/noninteger seeds, counts and b values, undeclared profiles, mismatched levels, wrong field type, zero shifts, out-of-bounds parameters and empty radical domains rather than silently changing them.
