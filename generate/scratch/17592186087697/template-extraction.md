# Templates and slots

The exact original prompts, solutions, labels, matrix entries, witness cells, and role sequences for all five questions are retained in templates.json. source-components.json maps all thirty source choices to their exact field-owned values and correct designations. Each template's sole bank profile has false and true on each of three checkbox fields; there are no inferred distractor recipes, pooled profiles, or added feedback. The learner selects the truth value of membership in the requested class.

The backbone is direct inspection of three supplied matrices for a diagonal or identity property. Preserve the requested criterion, matrix role sequence, three named objects, three checkbox response contracts, exact source explanatory stages, and conclusion. Identity means square with all main diagonal entries one and all other entries zero. Diagonal means square with all offdiagonal entries zero. A rectangular matrix fails both regardless of its entries. A square offdiagonal counterexample fails diagonal recognition and has a concrete witness; its offdiagonal nonzero count is retained. A selected diagonal matrix remains nonidentity, keeping the source's explanatory notice true. No arithmetic or additional classification operation is introduced.

Numerical slots: exact small integer entries in [-3,7], including all observed signed entries; offdiagonal zero structure must obey the selected role. Source replay retains the original numbers and whitespace, including the harmless text-space command in q-126222. Normal sampling uses zeros, units and small nonunits. Nonunit magnitude changes alone are ignored by task deduplication. Zero/unit/nonunit categories retain distinctions used by the identity criterion and the source's identity notices.

Structural slots: square order 2,3,4; rectangular dimensions from 2 through 4 with unequal row and column counts. Total entry inspection is bounded to 36, the largest complete source workload. These are conservative same-method generalizations, separate from observed source parameters. Each template preserves its square/rectangular, diagonal/identity/offdiagonal roles. A nonzero witness may occupy any eligible offdiagonal cell, preserving the same counterexample stage; its rendered matrix, numerical value, subscript and solution reference change together. Labels retain their original values and never establish distinctness. Dense rectangular sources remain dense; sparse rectangular sources remain sparse. Their entries are immaterial to the square-shape failure, but supplied notation stays complete.

Prompt construction replaces each matrix body in the exact source Markdown. Solutions reuse all source prose and paragraph stages, replacing only the witness cell and value. Original matrices reuse their original exact body formatting. The correct values are computed first from exact SymPy matrices. UUIDs derive from complete authored content; fields and answers belong to their question/field. Mathematical fingerprints separately ignore labels, order, seed, and irrelevant magnitudes, preventing cosmetic duplication.
