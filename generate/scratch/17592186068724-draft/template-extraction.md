# Template extraction

`templates.json` is the operational full prompt/solution/bank extraction, with named mathematical operand slots. Shared prose, placeholders, differential notation, source ordering and every mathematical row are fixed. `source.json` and `reference/sources.json` retain the complete unmodified Pull at basis 2275. `source-cases.json` identifies every MA source replay.

The supplied role is a rational integrand and the requested role is a primitive (or the primitive fragment before supplied `+K`). The instructional backbone chooses the displayed denominator as u, differentiates it, rewrites the integral in u, integrates 1/u and substitutes back. The original signed easy profile retains its unreduced and reduced coefficient stages. Moderate profiles retain the original one-step differential relation and all four integration rows.

Easy slots vary only the positive constant c in `a*z^n+c`, with a, n, numerator and variable fixed by the profile. The blank uses c=1..19, other easy profiles c=1..20. q-12501's distractor coefficient 1/c and q-49572's c*ln(u) change with that slot. Colliding full banks are rejected (e.g. c=1,2,3 for q-12501). Moderate q-113719 varies c=1..20; the other moderate profiles vary the signed linear coefficient with magnitude 1..12. Derivative, denominator, every displayed instance and every altered distractor operand update together. No variation changes degree or number of terms. Hard uses `a*x^4+b*x+c`, a in {-2,-3}, b in {2,3,4}, c in 2..8, with gcd(|a|,b,c)=1 to reject common-factor aliases, exact derivative numerator, and q-113719's complete bank and four-row solution.

For q-72566 the bank's formal operands are v, `a*x^n+b+c`, u, `n*a*x^(n-1)+b*x+c`, and reciprocal of `a*x^n+b+c`. The other moderate banks contain ln(u)/v, ln(v/u), ln(u), ln(u/v), ln(v), preserving their source order. Exact complete bank values, including compound errors, are visible per source in `source-replay.json` and `review.json`; recipe interpretation is subordinate to these operational formal operands.

All primitives are compared by derivatives on real connected intervals where relevant denominators and logarithm operands are nonzero. This establishes equivalence modulo integration constants and rules out scalar log-argument aliases as distractors. Every served source or variant must have exactly one correct choice and pairwise distinct derivatives. The positive quadratic blank is defined on all real numbers and omits the learner's integration constant as the source prompt supplies it.
