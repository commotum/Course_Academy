## original q-120254

![](images/68/6882323d2c8017e95100a0ab9d1091fdf149e884ef27d2d2bf59ab7609b1a960.png)

Consider the picture above. Given that $c=\lambda a+2b,$ find $\lambda .$


Options: 1.5, -3, 2, -2, -1.5

To find $\lambda ,$ we want to match the expression

$$
c=\lambda a+2b.
$$

We are given that one of the components is $2b.$ So, we first draw the vector $2b$ from the origin. Then we complete the path to the point $C.$

![](images/a8/a8309dc45ac43c6642010e744de5a062d6575d48ba85bd642df39dd261f47422.png)

Let $P$ be the endpoint of the vector $2b.$ Then

$$
c=\overset{\to }{OP}+\overset{\to }{PC}.
$$

In $△OPC,$ we have:

- $PC=2|a|$

- $\overset{\to }{PC}$ and $a$ have opposite directions

Hence, $\overset{\to }{PC}=-2a.$

Additionally,

$$
\begin{aligned}c & =\overset{\to }{OP}+\overset{\to }{PC} \\  & =2b-2a.\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently, $c=\lambda a+2b=-2a+2b.$

Since $a∦b,$ the linear combination can only be written in one way. Equating the coefficients of $a,$ we get:

$$
\lambda =-2
$$
