##2. Generalize

Trace OP=kb and PC=−ra. Their sum is c=kb−ra. Since a,b are nonparallel, compare this with c=λa+kb to obtain λ=−r. The grid length ratio and arrow direction are the assessed operation. Scalar arithmetic parameterizes it without another mathematical branch.

##3. Components

Fixed: vector names, source shared prose, solution stage ordering, grid style, colors and arrows. Variable: a,b grid displacements; known k; residual magnitude r; integer/fraction/decimal notation; choice bank and field layout. Conditional: kb sign; omission of unit1 before a/b; final punctuation; prompt radio versus blank masks. Source assets stay in exact replay instances and are stored in `source.json`; changed-parameter instances get newly rendered mathematical geometry. Original wrong values map one-to-one through the expressions above, and blank answers through−r.

##4. Reusable masks and limits
