Given that $y=\frac{3{x}^{2}}{(3x+5)^{2}}$, find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}.$

We use the quotient rule with the numerator $u(x)=3{x}^{2}$ and the denominator $v(x)=(3x+5)^{2}.$ 

Differentiating $u(x)$ and $v(x),$ we have

$$
{u}^{′}(x)=6x,\,{v}^{′}(x)=6(3x+5).
$$

Note that we used the chain rule to differentiate $v.$

Now, applying the quotient rule and simplifying, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =\frac{{u}^{′}(x)\cdot v(x)-u(x)\cdot {v}^{′}(x)}{[v(x)]^{2}} \\  & =\frac{(6x){(3x+5)}^{2}-(3{x}^{2})\cdot 6(3x+5)}{{((3x+5)^{2})}^{2}} \\  & =\frac{6x(3x+5)(3x+5-3x)}{{(3x+5)}^{4}} \\  & =\frac{6x(3x+5)(5)}{{(3x+5)}^{4}} \\  & =\frac{30x}{{(3x+5)}^{3}}.\end{aligned}
$$
