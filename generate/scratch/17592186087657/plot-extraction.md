# Four plot extraction stages

1. Specific originals: all nine practice assets and canonical e-3230 were inspected in original-plots.png. White background, black x/y arrow axes without ticks, red circular outline and red endpoint markers, thin blue dashed diameter, black X/Y labels including signed coordinates. Endpoint X/Y roles agree with every textual coordinate pair. Vertical, horizontal and signed diagonal orientations are present. No center marker or radius arrow appears. Axes lie at the coordinate origin even when the center is translated.
2. Shared purpose: visualize the given diameter and the circle through its endpoints. Coordinates supplied in prose are sufficient; image labels repeat them. These are parameter-dependent moderate plots.
3. Components: midpoint (h,k), radius half the endpoint distance, equal-aspect circle; segment X→Y with both endpoints; axes at x=0 and y=0; endpoint labels outside the circumference; arrow tips and x/y labels. Bounds include the circle, origin, label padding and arrow heads.
4. Specification: Matplotlib only; output 550×550 white PNG, red outline/markers, thin blue dashed segment, black axes and labels. Data come from the exact generated endpoints. Bounds use center±radius and the origin with 1.5-unit padding; label offsets are screen-space and point outward. Actual cached CPython3.10/Matplotlib and dependencies are recorded in each sidecar, not claimed as primary-venv dependencies. Unique render inputs, atomic PNG/sidecar writes, cache version matching and fresh same/different concurrent calls are checked. The sidecar includes the actual axes pixel transform. Independent raster QA fits all red pixels to a circle, checks its measured center/radius against the axes transform and checks endpoint red pixels. Stroke/antialias tolerance is 2 pixels for fit and 5 for red extent; endpoint markers are within 4 pixels. Larger radius-10 horizontal/vertical boundaries are included in review and inspected for labels/axes.

Original image references and source strings remain immutable in source.json. No image model, direct SVG, database or shared-environment write is used.
