# Four-stage plot extraction and renderer specification

1. Eight unique practice PNGs were inspected at their original full resolution. Full disks have blue circumference and pale blue fill, Cartesian axes with arrows, ±radius intercept labels and D. The upper half disk includes its y-intercept and ±R x-intercepts. The first/third quadrants use the corresponding radial axis boundaries. q-83968 is the right semicircle centered(0,-1/2), radius1/2, with boundary x=sqrt(-y²-y), marked y=-1/2,-1. q-161055 is the right semicircle centered(0,1), radius1, with boundary x=sqrt(2y-y²), marked y=1,2. Source paths are preserved in the full raw record.
2. Region plots depict the same actual bounded domain used to determine radial and angular integration limits; no plotted answer choices occur.
3. Fixed components: white background, arrowed axes, D label, blue boundary, pale blue fill, equal aspect. Variable components: radius/diameter, curve points, angular sector, intercept/center values and shifted Cartesian boundary formula. Hard plots are part of the prompt; other plots appear in the worked solution and are recreated for every changed domain.
4. `render_plots()` builds its exact geometry from `mathdata()` and sends numeric coordinates only to the private Matplotlib renderer. Origin sectors follow r=R, theta in the exact source interval. Translated half disks follow r=+/-R sin(theta) with theta in0..pi/2 or-pi/2..0. The diametric line is x=0. Bounds derive from the sampled diameter. Labels and scales depend on the same instance; they are not independently sampled.

The primary Python3.13 venv lacks Matplotlib. `render.py` uses the already cached read-only Python3.10 wheels, no installation/network. Output PNGs are180dpi, with a sidecar recording exact render parameters and runtime/version. Input, PNG and sidecar temporary files are unique; final files use atomic replacement. Cache hits require PNG and sidecar. Concurrent identical/different parameter calls are tested in a private fresh render directory without pruning the active review cache. Every produced asset is retained. No image model, direct SVG, or database write is used.
