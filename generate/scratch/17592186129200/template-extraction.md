# Four extraction stages

1. **Specific originals.** `extraction.json` retains all original prompts, complete fields, all 45 original answer fragments and full worked solutions. `source-cases.json` maps every practice ID to the exact observed coefficient/radius parameters and level. All answers are exact scalars. The two hard plots show right half disks with diameter on the y-axis, not disks centered at the origin.
2. **Shared mathematical pattern.** Substitute x=r cos(theta), y=r sin(theta), include the Jacobian r, derive the actual region bounds, evaluate the radial primitive and its endpoints, then integrate in theta with the source identity/primitive and endpoint/collection stages. Each family retains its source's separate stage count and optional identity rows. Zero answers still perform the complete conversion/evaluation method.
3. **Components and complete banks.** Every original option is mapped in `extraction.json`. `bank()` contains parameterized expressions that reproduce all five values together. Scalar values do not establish original error intent; observed patterns are explicitly unresolved and are available through `profile='observed'` (repaired teaching) or `profile='observed_raw'` (historical mathematical errors retained). No sampled perturbation or polynomial fit is promoted to a normal error recipe. Default profiles are separately authored complete operation banks, chosen before collision checking. Recipes remain outside learner prose.
4. **Reusable masks.** `worked()` owns complete mathematical components and ordered rows: initial Cartesian integral; substituted components/Jacobian; trig reduction if present; repeated-integral setup; factor separation if present; radial primitive; individually evaluated radial endpoints; angular identity if present; angular primitive; individually evaluated angular endpoints; final collection. SymPy simplifies computed values, not the structural masks. `generate()` shares parameter guards, exact computations, formatting, owned UUIDs, shuffling and plots across documented easy/moderate/hard entrypoints.

## Parameter and bank bounds

Origin-centered R is an integer in 1..5; exponential R is2..5 because the unsquared radial endpoint distractor collides at R=1. Translated families use diameter parameter R in {1/2,1,2,3,4,5}, radius R/2 and center (0,+/-R/2). Coefficients are positive integers1..8; odd-disk coefficients1..30 include the source15. Explicit inputs obey these guards. Complete bank collisions reject the whole instance; sampled inputs resample. Moderate linear a,b both remain nonzero, and equal coefficients may be rejected by whole-bank eligibility. These are parallel same-level variants, not a progression.

The odd-disk ZERO bank computes {0,v,2v,4v,-v}, v=cR^5/15, from correct full-disk evaluation, first quadrant, upper half disk, absolute sine over the full angular interval, and third quadrant. The difference ZERO bank computes {0,v,2v,-v,-2v}, v=cR^3/3, from correct quarter disk, omitted y term, changing minus to plus, omitted x term, and second quadrant. All are nonzero and mutually unequal for the positive sampled parameters. Thus neither zero family is dropped, negated zero, or assigned zero-scaled fake mistakes.

Other default banks evaluate actual integrand/bound/primitive mistakes, listed in `generation.recipes`: omitted Jacobian, altered meaningful radial/angle bound, reversed angular limits, missing lower endpoint or divisor, and omitted/change-signed actual terms. The complete selected bank must contain precisely one correct scalar and four mutually inequivalent scalars.
