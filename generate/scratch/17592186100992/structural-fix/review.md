Find $(A+B)-C-(B-C)$, if

<div class="matrix-operands">
<div>$A=\begin{bmatrix}6 & 7 \\ 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}$</div>
<div>$B=\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$</div>
<div>$C=\begin{bmatrix}5 & 1 \\ 6 & 8\end{bmatrix}$</div>
</div>

- $\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}5 & 7 \\ 7 & 5\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}5 & 1 \\ 6 & 8\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}6 & 7 \\ 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}$ (correct)

First, we simplify the expression by distributing the subtraction over the matrices in the parentheses:

$$
\begin{aligned}(A+B)-C-(B-C) & =A+B-C-B+C \\  & =A+\cancel{B}-\cancel{C}-\cancel{B}+\cancel{C} \\  & =A\end{aligned}
$$

We are left with a single matrix. So, the result is 

$$
\begin{aligned}A & =\begin{bmatrix}6 & 7 \\ 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}\end{aligned}
$$

Find $(A-C)-(A-B)+C$, if

<div class="matrix-operands">
<div>$A=\begin{bmatrix}6 & 7 \\ 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}$</div>
<div>$B=\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$</div>
<div>$C=\begin{bmatrix}5 & 1 \\ 6 & 8\end{bmatrix}$</div>
</div>

- $\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$ (correct)
- $\begin{bmatrix}0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}2 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}5 & 1 \\ 6 & 8\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}6 & 7 \\ 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}$

First, we simplify the expression by distributing the subtraction over the matrices in the parentheses:

$$
\begin{aligned}(A-C)-(A-B)+C & =A-C-A+B+C \\  & =\cancel{A}-\cancel{C}-\cancel{A}+B+\cancel{C} \\  & =B\end{aligned}
$$

We are left with a single matrix. So, the result is 

$$
\begin{aligned}B & =\begin{bmatrix}3 & 2 \\ 2 & 3\end{bmatrix}\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}C & =\begin{bmatrix}5 & 1 \\ 6 & 8\end{bmatrix}\end{aligned}
