The area of the triangle $△ABC$ is $\frac{21}{2}\,\text{mm}^{2}.$ Use $\sin30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}.$ Find $AC.$

- $\frac{6}{7}\,\text{mm}$
- $3\,\text{mm}$
- $294\,\text{mm}$
- $7\,\text{mm}$
- $6\,\text{mm}$

The formula for the area of the triangle is 

$$
A=\frac{1}{2}bc\operatorname{sin}⁡A,
$$

where $∠A$ is between the sides of lengths $b$ and $c.$ 

Here

$$
\begin{aligned}c & =AB=7\text{ mm} \\ b & =AC \\ m∠A & ={30}^{∘}.\end{aligned}
$$

Since 

$$
\operatorname{sin}⁡{30}^{∘}=\frac{1}{2},
$$

we can calculate $b=AC,$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}A & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot b\cdot c\cdot \operatorname{sin}⁡A \\ \frac{21}{2} & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot b\cdot 7\cdot \operatorname{sin}⁡{30}^{∘} \\ \frac{21}{2} & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot b\cdot 7\cdot (\frac{1}{2}) \\ \frac{21}{2} & =\frac{7}{4}b \\ b & =\frac{\frac{21}{2}}{\frac{7}{4}} \\ b & =6\text{ mm}.\end{aligned}
$$

We conclude that $AC=6\,\text{mm}.$
