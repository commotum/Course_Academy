Calculate the integral $\int \frac{3}{(-3-5x)^{4}}\,\text{d}x.$

- $\frac{5}{(-3-5x)^{3}}+C$
- $\frac{1}{5(-3-5x)^{3}}+C$
- $\frac{3}{25(-3-5x)^{5}}+C$
- $-\frac{1}{5(-3-5x)^{3}}+C$
- $-\frac{1}{(-3-5x)^{3}}+C$

First, we rewrite the integral as

$$
\int 3(-3-5x)^{-4}\,\text{d}x.
$$

Let $u=-3-5x.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=-5\,⟹\,\text{d}x=- \frac{1}{5}\text{d}u.
$$

We now write our integral in terms of $u,$ calculate the integral as normal, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ at the very end:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int 3(-3-5x)^{-4}\,\text{d}x & =(3)\int {u}^{-4}\cdot (- \frac{1}{5})\,\text{d}u \\  & =(3)\cdot (- \frac{1}{5})\int {u}^{-4}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(- \frac{3}{5})\int {u}^{-4}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(- \frac{3}{5})\cdot \frac{{u}^{-3}}{(-3)}+C \\  & =(\frac{1}{5})(-3-5x)^{-3}+C \\  & =\frac{1}{5(-3-5x)^{3}}+C\end{aligned}
$$

Let's double-check that our result is correct. If we differentiate the result, then it should come out to the original integrand:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[\frac{1}{5(-3-5x)^{3}}+C] & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[\frac{1}{5(-3-5x)^{3}}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(\frac{1}{5})\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[(-3-5x)^{-3}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(\frac{1}{5})\cdot (-3)\cdot (-3-5x)^{-4}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(-3-5x)+0 \\  & =(\frac{1}{5})\cdot (-3)\cdot (-3-5x)^{-4}\cdot (-5) \\  & =\frac{3}{(-3-5x)^{4}}\,✓\end{aligned}
$$

\int (6-2x)^{-4}\,\text{d}x.
