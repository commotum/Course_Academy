Calculate the integral $\int \frac{2}{(2x+12)^{4}}\,\text{d}x.$

- $-\frac{4}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C$
- $-\frac{1}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C$
- $-\frac{2}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C$
- $\frac{1}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C$
- $-\frac{1}{5(2x+12)^{5}}+C$

First, we rewrite the integral as

$$
\int 2(2x+12)^{-4}\,\text{d}x.
$$

Let $u=2x+12.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=2\,⟹\,\text{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\text{d}u.
$$

We now write our integral in terms of $u,$ calculate the integral as normal, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ at the very end:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int 2(2x+12)^{-4}\,\text{d}x & =(2)\int {u}^{-4}\cdot (\frac{1}{2})\,\text{d}u \\  & =(2)\cdot (\frac{1}{2})\int {u}^{-4}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(1)\int {u}^{-4}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(1)\cdot \frac{{u}^{-3}}{(-3)}+C \\  & =(- \frac{1}{3})(2x+12)^{-3}+C \\  & =-\frac{1}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C\end{aligned}
$$

Let's double-check that our result is correct. If we differentiate the result, then it should come out to the original integrand:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[-\frac{1}{3(2x+12)^{3}}+C] & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[-\frac{1}{3(2x+12)^{3}}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(- \frac{1}{3})\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[(2x+12)^{-3}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(- \frac{1}{3})\cdot (-3)\cdot (2x+12)^{-4}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(2x+12)+0 \\  & =(- \frac{1}{3})\cdot (-3)\cdot (2x+12)^{-4}\cdot (2) \\  & =\frac{2}{(2x+12)^{4}}\,✓\end{aligned}
$$
