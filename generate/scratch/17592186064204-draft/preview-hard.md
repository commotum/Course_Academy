Calculate the integral $\int \frac{-4}{(-8-5x)^{3}}\,\text{d}x.$

- $\frac{2}{(-8-5x)^{2}}+C$
- $-\frac{10}{(-8-5x)^{2}}+C$
- $\frac{2}{5(-8-5x)^{2}}+C$
- $-\frac{1}{5(-8-5x)^{4}}+C$
- $-\frac{2}{5(-8-5x)^{2}}+C$

First, we rewrite the integral as

$$
\int -4(-8-5x)^{-3}\,\text{d}x.
$$

Let $u=-8-5x.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=-5\,⟹\,\text{d}x=- \frac{1}{5}\text{d}u.
$$

We now write our integral in terms of $u,$ calculate the integral as normal, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ at the very end:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int -4(-8-5x)^{-3}\,\text{d}x & =(-4)\int {u}^{-3}\cdot (- \frac{1}{5})\,\text{d}u \\  & =(-4)\cdot (- \frac{1}{5})\int {u}^{-3}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(\frac{4}{5})\int {u}^{-3}\,\text{d}u \\  & =(\frac{4}{5})\cdot \frac{{u}^{-2}}{(-2)}+C \\  & =(- \frac{2}{5})(-8-5x)^{-2}+C \\  & =-\frac{2}{5(-8-5x)^{2}}+C\end{aligned}
$$

Let's double-check that our result is correct. If we differentiate the result, then it should come out to the original integrand:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[-\frac{2}{5(-8-5x)^{2}}+C] & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[-\frac{2}{5(-8-5x)^{2}}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(- \frac{2}{5})\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[(-8-5x)^{-2}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =(- \frac{2}{5})\cdot (-2)\cdot (-8-5x)^{-3}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(-8-5x)+0 \\  & =(- \frac{2}{5})\cdot (-2)\cdot (-8-5x)^{-3}\cdot (-5) \\  & =\frac{-4}{(-8-5x)^{3}}\,✓\end{aligned}
$$
