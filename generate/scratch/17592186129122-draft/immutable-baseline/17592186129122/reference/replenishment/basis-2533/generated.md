Calculate the period of the function $y = \frac{1}{5}\cos (\frac{1}{4}x)$.

- $4\pi$
- $16\pi$
- $\frac{\pi }{2}$
- $2\pi$
- $8\pi$ (correct)

Worked solution:
Given a function in the form $y=A\cos(Bx+C)+D,$ the period is $\frac{2\pi }{|B|}.$

In our case, we have that $B=\frac{1}{4}.$ Therefore, the period is $\frac{2\pi }{|\frac{1}{4}|}=\frac{2\pi }{\frac{1}{4}}=8\pi .$
