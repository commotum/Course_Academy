Find the gradient vector field of the function $f(x,y)=\ln{\left(4 x + 5 y \right)}.$

The gradient vector field of a function $f$ is given by $\nabla f.$

First, we find the partial derivatives:

$$
\begin{aligned}f_{x}(x,y)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\ln{\left(4 x + 5 y \right)}\right) \\ &=\frac{4}{4 x + 5 y} \\ f_{y}(x,y)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\ln{\left(4 x + 5 y \right)}\right) \\ &=\frac{5}{4 x + 5 y}\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the gradient vector field of $f(x,y)$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}\nabla f(x,y)&=f_{x}(x,y)\,i+f_{y}(x,y)\,j \\ &= \frac{4}{4 x + 5 y}\,i+\frac{5}{4 x + 5 y}\,j.\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}f_{x}(x,y,z)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\sin{\left(x z + 4 y \right)}\right) \\ &=z \cos{\left(x z + 4 y \right)} \\ f_{y}(x,y,z)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\sin{\left(x z + 4 y \right)}\right) \\ &=4 \cos{\left(x z + 4 y \right)} \\ f_{z}(x,y,z)&=\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(\sin{\left(x z + 4 y \right)}\right) \\ &=x \cos{\left(x z + 4 y \right)}\end{aligned}
