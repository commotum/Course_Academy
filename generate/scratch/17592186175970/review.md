The vector-valued function $f(t,z)$ above parametrizes the elliptic cylinder defined in Cartesian coordinates as $x^{2}+y^{2}=4.$ What is the second component of $f?$

- $\cosh{\left(t \right)}$
- $z^{2}$
- $2 \cos{\left(t \right)}$
- $2 \sinh{\left(t \right)}$
- $2 \sin{\left(t \right)}$ (correct)

Recall that the ellipse $x^{2}+y^{2}=4$, or equivalently $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$, in the $xy$-plane has the parametrization

$$
⟨x,y⟩=⟨2 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,2 \sin{\left(t \right)}⟩,\quad t\in[0,2\pi),
$$

since

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{4}&=\frac{(2 \cos{\left(t \right)})^2}{4}+\frac{(2 \sin{\left(t \right)})^2}{4}\\&=\cos^2 t+\sin^2 t\\&=1.\end{aligned}
$$

Now, for any point $(x,y,z)$ on our surface, we obtain the following:

$$
f(t,z)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨2 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,2 \sin{\left(t \right)},\,z⟩
$$

where $t\in[0,2\pi)$ and $z\in(-\infty,\infty)$.

Therefore, the second component of $f$ is $2 \sin{\left(t \right)}$.

f(t,y)=⟨\text{_____},\,y,\,7 \sin{\left(t \right)}⟩
$$

The vector-valued function $f(t,y)$ above parametrizes the elliptic cylinder defined in Cartesian coordinates as $49x^{2}+16z^{2}=784.$ What is the first component of $f?$

- $\sin{\left(t \right)}$
- $4 y^{2}$
- $\cosh{\left(t \right)}$
- $4 \cos{\left(t \right)}$ (correct)
- $4 y$

Recall that the ellipse $49x^{2}+16z^{2}=784$, or equivalently $\frac{x^{2}}{16}+\frac{z^{2}}{49}=1$, in the $xz$-plane has the parametrization

$$
⟨x,z⟩=⟨4 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,7 \sin{\left(t \right)}⟩,\quad t\in[0,2\pi),
$$

since

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{z^2}{49}&=\frac{(4 \cos{\left(t \right)})^2}{16}+\frac{(7 \sin{\left(t \right)})^2}{49}\\&=\cos^2 t+\sin^2 t\\&=1.\end{aligned}
$$

Now, for any point $(x,y,z)$ on our surface, we obtain the following:

$$
f(t,y)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨4 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,y,\,7 \sin{\left(t \right)}⟩
$$

where $t\in[0,2\pi)$ and $y\in(-\infty,\infty)$.

Therefore, the first component of $f$ is $4 \cos{\left(t \right)}$.
