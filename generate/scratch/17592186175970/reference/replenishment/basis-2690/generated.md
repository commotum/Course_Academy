The vector-valued function $f(t,z)$ above parametrizes the elliptic cylinder defined in Cartesian coordinates as $\frac{x^{2}}{36}+\frac{y^{2}}{144}=1.$ What is the second component of $f?$

- $144 z^{2}$
- $12 \sin{\left(t \right)}$ (correct)
- $12 \cos{\left(t \right)}$
- $144 \sinh{\left(t \right)}$
- $\cosh{\left(t \right)}$

Recall that the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{36}+\frac{y^{2}}{144}=1$ in the $xy$-plane has the parametrization

$$
⟨x,y⟩=⟨6 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,12 \sin{\left(t \right)}⟩,\quad t\in[0,2\pi),
$$

since

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{x^2}{36}+\frac{y^2}{144}&=\frac{(6 \cos{\left(t \right)})^2}{36}+\frac{(12 \sin{\left(t \right)})^2}{144}\\&=\cos^2 t+\sin^2 t\\&=1.\end{aligned}
$$

Now, for any point $(x,y,z)$ on our surface, we obtain the following:

$$
f(t,z)=⟨x,y,z⟩=⟨6 \cos{\left(t \right)},\,12 \sin{\left(t \right)},\,z⟩
$$

where $t\in[0,2\pi)$ and $z\in(-\infty,\infty)$.

Therefore, the second component of $f$ is $12 \sin{\left(t \right)}$.
