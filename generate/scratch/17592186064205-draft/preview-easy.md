$\int \sqrt{2x+58}\,\text{d}x=$

- $\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2x+58}+C$
- $\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}+C$
- $\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{2x+58}+C$
- $\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}+C$
- $\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}+C$

First, we rewrite the integral as

$$
\int (2x+58)^{1/2}\,\text{d}x.
$$

Now, we let $u=2x+58.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=2\,⟹\,\text{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\text{d}u.
$$

We now write our integral in terms of $u,$ calculate the integral as normal, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ at the very end:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int (2x+58)^{1/2}\,\text{d}x & =\int {u}^{1/2}\cdot (\frac{1}{2})\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{1}{2}\int {u}^{1/2}\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{2}{3}{u}^{3/2}+C \\  & =\frac{1}{3}{u}^{3/2}+C \\  & =\frac{1}{3}(2x+58)^{3/2}+C \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}+C\end{aligned}
$$

Let's double-check that our result is correct. If we differentiate the result, then it should come out to the original integrand:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}+C] & =\frac{1}{3}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[\sqrt{(2x+58)^{3}}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}[(2x+58)^{3/2}]+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(C) \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\cdot \frac{3}{2}(2x+58)^{1/2}\cdot \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(2x+58)+0 \\  & =\frac{1}{2}(2x+58)^{1/2}\cdot 2 \\  & =(2x+58)^{1/2} \\  & =\sqrt{2x+58}\,✓\end{aligned}
$$
