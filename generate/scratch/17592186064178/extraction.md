# Source components and reusable templates

The exact source components, parameter observations, field layouts, fixed instructional wording and complete choice recipes are retained in source.json, templates.json, classification.json, and source-cases.json. Source-cases independently matches each of the 25 supported options to exactly one complete mathematical value, with its source answer identity, field, correct designation and parameterized recipe. No banks are pooled.

q-113907 integrates -6 sin(theta). Its five choices are -cos(theta)/6, 6 cos(theta) (correct), -6 sin(theta), 6 sin(theta), -6 cos(theta), each with C. Generalize the magnitude 6 to a positive integer a>=2; preserve theta, outer parentheses, spacing and nested aligned solution. Canonical primitive a cos(theta) follows from the negative-sine rule. Recipes preserve reciprocal/sign/function changes exactly; the psychological interpretations are not inferred.

q-113908 integrates 5 cos(y). Its bank is -sin(y)/5, sin(y)/5, 5 cos(y), 5 sin(y) (correct), -5 cos(y), each with C. Replace magnitude 5 by a>=2, preserving y and the source solution. At a=1 the reciprocal option equals the correct answer, so the entire bank is ineligible. All source magnitudes are included in 2..9; this is a deliberately bounded sampling policy.

q-113909 integrates 2 cos(x)+3 sin(x). Write the source coefficients as a,b. Its complete bank is a sin(x)-b cos(x) (correct), a sin(x)+b cos(x), a cos(x)-b sin(x), b cos(x)-a sin(x), b sin(x)-a cos(x), all with C. Use integers a,b in 1..9 with a!=b; equality duplicates options. Preserve positive coefficients, term order and distribution/primitive/simplification steps. The signs in this family are observed; extending to other signs would need a separate validated profile.

q-17654 integrates -cos(theta). Its exact bank is -sin²(theta), sin(theta), cos²(theta), -sin(theta) (correct), -cos(theta), with C. q-2161 integrates cos(x)-sin(x); its exact bank is -sin(x)-cos(x), -sin(x)+cos(x), 2 sin(x), sin(x)+cos(x) (correct), sin(x)-cos(x), with C. The first is excluded: -sin²(theta)+C and cos²(theta)+C differ only by a constant and are the same arbitrary-C family. The second is a supported singleton that cannot replenish a pool already containing the same task.

Shared purpose: recover primitives of sine and cosine, pulling out constants and using linearity. The response is one selected antiderivative; all real variable values are valid and C is arbitrary. The field key is selection and values contain no math delimiters. Original worked-solution wording stays fixed. For every rendered equality an independent primitive table checks the mathematics modulo an additive constant, while differentiation verifies the unique correct choice and rejects every distractor. Distinctness uses expanded integrands with variable names normalized, excluding choice order, profile, seed and identity.

Incomplete/rated/unrated and undefined-h records retain all their observed values and source IDs in classification.json. They are excluded as evidence gaps, never silently corrected. The canonical example provides only supporting mathematics and is excluded from practice. No plots or standalone image rendering are needed.
