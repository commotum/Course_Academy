Identify the points that lie on the line $y=4x+2.$

- $A$ and $C$ only
- $A$ only
- $B$ and $C$ only
- $B$ only
- $C$ only

The plotted points correspond to the $xy$-pairs $A(-1,-2)$, $B(0,1)$ and $C(1,6).$

To check whether a point lies on the line, we substitute the coordinates of the point into the equation and determine whether the resulting statement is true.

- Substituting the point $A(-1,-2)$ results in a true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =4x+2 \\ (-2) & =4(-1)+2 \\ -2 & =-2\,✓\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $A$ does lie on the line.

- Substituting the point $B(0,1)$ results in a false statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =4x+2 \\ (1) & =4(0)+2 \\ 1 & =2\,\times \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $B$ does not lie on the line.

- Substituting the point $C(1,6)$ results in true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =4x+2 \\ (6) & =4(1)+2 \\ 6 & =6\,✓\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $C$ does lie on the line.

Finally, only points $A$ and $C$ lie on the line.
