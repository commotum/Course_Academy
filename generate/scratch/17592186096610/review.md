$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(- \frac{1}{4}{e}^{x}-8) \\  & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(- \frac{1}{4}{e}^{x})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(-8) \\  & =- \frac{1}{4}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({e}^{x})+0 \\  & =- \frac{1}{4}{e}^{x}.\end{aligned}
$$

$$
