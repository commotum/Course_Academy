The equation of the midline is given by

$$
y=\frac{{y}_{\text{min}}+{y}_{\text{max}}}{2},
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{{y}_{\text{min}}+{y}_{\text{max}}}{2} & =\frac{-5+9}{2} \\  & =\frac{4}{2} \\  & =2.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the midline is $y=2.$
