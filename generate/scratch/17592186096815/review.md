## Authored easy variant 5

The vector $a$ is directed horizontally to the right and has magnitude $21$, while the vector $b$ is directed vertically down and has magnitude $28$. Which of the following is a unit vector that is parallel to $a + b$?

> A scientific calculator is required to answer this question.

1. $\frac{1}{1225}(a + b)$
2. $\frac{1}{28}(a + b)$
3. $\frac{1}{49}(a + b)$
4. $\frac{1}{35}(a + b)$
5. $\frac{1}{21}(a + b)$

First, we have to find $a+b$. The given directions are perpendicular, so the triangle law of addition gives a right triangle.

To find the unit vector corresponding to $a+b$, we have to divide by the magnitude:

$$
u=\frac{a+b}{|a+b|}
$$

To find the magnitude, we can use the Pythagorean theorem:

$$
\begin{aligned}|a+b|^2&=|a|^2+|b|^2\\
&=21^2+28^2\\
&=441+784\\
&=1225\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the magnitude is

$$
|a+b|=\sqrt{1225}=35
$$

and the corresponding unit vector is

$$
\begin{aligned}u&=\frac{a+b}{|a+b|}\\
&=\frac{1}{35}(a+b)\end{aligned}
$$
