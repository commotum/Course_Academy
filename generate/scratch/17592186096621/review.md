In this case, the expression for $y$ is a quotient $\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}$ with $u(x)=sin⁡x$ and $v(x)=2x.$ 

Therefore, we apply the quotient rule as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{{u}^{′}(x)\cdot v(x)-u(x)\cdot {v}^{′}(x)}{[v(x)]^{2}} \\  & =\frac{(sin⁡x)^{′}\cdot (2x)-sin⁡x\cdot (2x)^{′}}{(2x)^{2}} \\  & =\frac{cos⁡x\cdot (2x)-sin⁡x\cdot 2}{{(2x)}^{2}} \\  & =\frac{2(xcos⁡x-sin⁡x)}{4{x}^{2}} \\  & =\frac{xcos⁡x-sin⁡x}{2{x}^{2}}\end{aligned}
$$

To find the slope of the tangent line, we now substitute $x=\pi $ into the formula and get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(\pi ) & =\frac{\pi cos⁡(\pi )-sin⁡(\pi )}{2(\pi )^{2}} \\  & =\frac{\pi (-1)-(0)}{2{\pi }^{2}} \\  & =-\frac{\pi }{2{\pi }^{2}} \\  & =-\frac{1}{2\pi }.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent line is $-\frac{1}{2\pi }.$
