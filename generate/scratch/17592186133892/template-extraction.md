# Extracted backbone and bounded generalization

`template-components.json` gives each original question's exact prompt, solution, endpoints/parametrization, integrand, bounds, field, original choice, complete mathematical value recipe, and bank profile. It includes every choice from all ten sources. `source-replay.json` distinguishes exact bank fidelity from mathematical validity; the two excluded profiles are replayed as evidence only.

The shared assessed move is ∫C f(x,y)dy = ∫ f(r(t)) y'(t)dt. The response is a complete definite integral, not its evaluated number or a partial integrand. Five choices and the `selection` radio contract are fixed. Each source's particular integrand operations and directed-interval role stay in its own conservative template. The ordered worked stages remain: state formula; construct/show parametrization and bounds (line sources retain endpoint interpolation); differentiate y; substitute f (including the Pythagorean identity for 9-y² and the source regrouping for 3x²+y²); assemble the integral. No evaluation, new operation or harder reasoning stage is introduced.

The declared variable slot is a positive integer geometry scale k in {1,2,3,4}, with original k=1 retained. For xy, 3x+2y, x²+y², x-y and x²y, both coordinates scale by k. For 9-y², the supplied constant scales to 9k² as both coordinates scale by k. For x²-y, coordinates scale by (k,k²); this weighted homogeneity keeps both summands of f at degree two. Endpoints, equations, radius, parametrization, derivative, substituted values, answer banks and plots all derive from those same coordinates. The full correct integral consequently scales by k^d, with d=3,2,3,2,4,3,4 respectively as recorded per profile in the script.

This is a deliberately narrow, explicitly documented sampling policy based on sparse bank evidence. It is not a claim that source endpoints, arc starts or orientations are mathematically essential. Broader independent endpoint, coefficient and arc variation is deferred because the source distractor patterns do not establish reliable recipes under those changes. Keeping the directed interval fixed also preserves each observed definite-integral bank's distinctness. There is no declared target/cell/position slot; the required differential stays dy. Within the declared domain all four scale values are exercised for all eight supported profiles.

Each bank is selected whole before rendering. Its observed integral pattern B_i(t) is multiplied by k^d, with original bounds and five-option count preserved. These are explicit parameterized expression recipes; the psychological interpretation of the arbitrary polynomial distractors remains unresolved. The package makes no claim that they encode a particular misconception. Positive nonzero scaling preserves complete integral inequivalence. An independent printed-value parser nevertheless checks every variant, including evaluating whole definite integrals; integrand inequality alone is insufficient.

Stable question UUIDs depend on the actual parametrized geometry, integrand and directed interval, not seed or option order. Answer UUIDs belong to their field. Generation metadata stays outside question entities. The finite domain contains 32 supported tasks, eight of which are source replays; only unseen distinct content absent from the whole KP pool is eligible for insertion. Seeds merely select those tasks. Requests above 32 or for unsupported difficulties fail explicitly.
