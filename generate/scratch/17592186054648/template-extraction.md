Fixed shape and four requested entries preserve the observed introductory workload and answer contract. Variable slots are the eight operand entries, exact integers sampled in [-12,12]. Original values remain replayable. This conservative extension includes positive, negative and zero values, independently in every cell, without changing operations or solution stages. Each cell affects its prompt operands, expanded calculation, sum, choice recipes and key together. Sampler first selects a complete source profile and rejects collisions or an ambiguous bank. Distinctness hashes the unordered pair of complete operand matrices. Exchanging operands, changing profile/identity/seed/order/letters, or requesting the same operands again adds no inventory.
