Find the exact value of the $x$-coordinate of the point on the curve $y=2{x}^{2}+4x-3$ whose tangent has a slope of $1.$

Let $f(x)=2{x}^{2}+4x-3.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to ${f}^{′}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(2{x}^{2}+4x-3) \\  & =2\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+4\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x) \\  & =2(2x)+4(1) \\  & =4x+4.\end{aligned}
$$

We now set ${f}^{′}(x)=1$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}4x+4 & =1 \\ 4x & =-3 \\ x & =-\frac{3}{4}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent is equal to $1$ at the point where $x=-\frac{3}{4}.$
