Find the positive $x$-coordinate of the point on the curve $y=8{x}^{3}-3{x}^{2}-12x-4$ whose tangent has a slope of $6.$

Bank: x=12, x=2, x=1, x=3, x=4

Let $f(x)=8{x}^{3}-3{x}^{2}-12x-4.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to ${f}^{′}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(8{x}^{3}-3{x}^{2}-12x-4) \\  & =8\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{3})-3\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})-12\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x)-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(4) \\  & =8\cdot 3{x}^{2}-3\cdot 2x-12\cdot 1-0 \\  & =24{x}^{2}-6x-12.\end{aligned}
$$

We now set ${f}^{′}(x)=6$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}24{x}^{2}-6x-12 & =6 \\ 24{x}^{2}-6x-18 & =0 \\ 6(4{x}^{2}-x-3) & =0 \\ 6(x-1)(4x+3) & =0.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent is equal to $6$ at the point where $x=1$ and $x=-\frac{3}{4}.$ Since $x>0$, our answer is $x=1.$
