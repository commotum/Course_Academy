Find the $x$-coordinates of the points on the curve $y=-2{x}^{3}+6{x}^{2}+20x-1$ whose tangent has a slope of $2.$

Bank: x=3, x=-3, x=1, x=-3,\,1., x=-1,\,3

Let $f(x)=-2{x}^{3}+6{x}^{2}+20x-1.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to ${f}^{′}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(-2{x}^{3}+6{x}^{2}+20x-1) \\  & =-2\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{3})+6\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+20\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x)-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(1) \\  & =-2\cdot 3{x}^{2}+6\cdot 2x+20\cdot 1-0 \\  & =-6{x}^{2}+12x+20.\end{aligned}
$$

We now set ${f}^{′}(x)=2$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}-6{x}^{2}+12x+20 & =2 \\ -6{x}^{2}+12x+18 & =0 \\ -6({x}^{2}-2x-3) & =0 \\ -6(x+1)(x-3) & =0.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent is equal to $2$ at the points where $x=-1,3.$
