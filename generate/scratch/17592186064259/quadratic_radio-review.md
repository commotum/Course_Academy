Find the $x$-coordinate of the point on the curve $y={x}^{2}-3x+10$ whose tangent has a slope of $-1.$

Bank: x=-1, x=-2, x=0, x=2, x=1

Let $f(x)={x}^{2}-3x+10.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to ${f}^{′}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}-3x+10) \\  & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})-3\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x)+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(10) \\  & =2x-3\cdot 1+0 \\  & =2x-3.\end{aligned}
$$

We now set ${f}^{′}(x)=-1$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}2x-3 & =-1 \\ 2x & =-1+3 \\ 2x & =2 \\ x & =1\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent is equal to $-1$ at the point where $x=1.$
