Let $f(x)=x^{3} + 3 x^{2} + 6 x - 3.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to $f^{\prime}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
f^{\prime}(x) &= \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left(x^{3} + 3 x^{2} + 6 x - 3\right) \\
&= (1)\cdot 3\cdot x^{2} + (3)\cdot 2\cdot x + (6)\cdot 1\cdot 1 \\
&= 3 x^{2} + 6 x + 6
\end{aligned}
$$

We now set $f^{\prime}(x)=3$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3 x^{2} + 6 x + 6 &= 3 \\
3 x^{2} + 6 x + 3 &= 0 \\
3 \left(x + 1\right)^{2} &= 0 \\
x &= -1
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent is equal to $3$ at the point where $x=-1.$
