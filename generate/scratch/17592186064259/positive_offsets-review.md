Let $f(x)=x^{3} - 2 x^{2} - 5 x.$ The slope of the tangent at the point $x$ is equal to $f^{\prime}(x).$ Computing the derivative, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
f^{\prime}(x) &= \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left(x^{3} - 2 x^{2} - 5 x\right) \\
&= (1)\cdot 3\cdot x^{2} + (-2)\cdot 2\cdot x + (-5)\cdot 1\cdot 1 \\
&= 3 x^{2} - 4 x - 5
\end{aligned}
$$

We now set $f^{\prime}(x)=27$ and solve for $x:$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3 x^{2} - 4 x - 5 &= 27 \\
3 x^{2} - 4 x - 32 &= 0 \\
\left(x - 4\right) \left(3 x + 8\right) &= 0 \\
x &= - \frac{8}{3},\,4
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $x>0$, the required coordinate is $x=4.$
