## Canonical normal semicircle presentation normalization

![](/home/jake/Developer/Course_Academy/generate/scratch/17592186162081/assets/47f95b4c161be1fc52202518dfdcdae02cc7f9e2f9845a79a5d2610dd636612f.png)

Calculate $\displaystyle \int_{C}ds$, where $C$ is the arc of a semicircle of radius $5$ traversed once in the counterclockwise direction, as shown above.

- $\frac{5 \pi}{2}$
- $10 \pi$
- $5 \pi$
- $25 \pi$
- $10$

The line integral of unity with respect to arc length over $C$ is equal to the arc length $L$ of $C,$ i.e.,

$$
\int_C \,\textrm d s = L.
$$

In this case, $C$ is a semicircle of radius $r = 5.$ Its arc length is given by

$$
\begin{aligned}L &=\pi r=5 \pi .\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\int_C \,\textrm d s = 5 \pi.
$$
