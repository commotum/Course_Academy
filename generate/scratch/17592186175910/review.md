The equation of the line $l_{1}$ is $y =-x + 3$ and the line $l_{2}$ is parallel to $l_{1}$. What is the equation of $l_{2}$?

Since parallel lines have equal slopes, the slope of the new line should be equal to the slope of the line

$$
y = -x + 3
$$

The slope of the line

$$
y = -x + 3
$$

is

$$
-1
$$

So, we want a line with the slope $-1$ that passes through the point $(0,-1)$. Using point-slope form and simplifying, we obtain the equation of the line:

$$
\begin{aligned}
y - y_{1} &= m(x - x_{1}) \\
y - (-1) &= -1(x - (0)) \\
y &= -x + (-1) \\
y &= -x - 1
\end{aligned}
$$
