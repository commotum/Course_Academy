$P=\begin{bmatrix}-8 & -2 & 2 \\ 8 & 4 & 4 \\ -2 & -7 & -5\end{bmatrix}$
and
$x=\begin{bmatrix}5 \\ -8 \\ -6\end{bmatrix}.$

Field selection :answer-field.type/radio

- $\begin{bmatrix}36 \\ 16 \\ 76\end{bmatrix}$

- $\begin{bmatrix}-36 \\ -16 \\ -76\end{bmatrix}$

- $\begin{bmatrix}-36 \\ 16 \\ 76\end{bmatrix}$

- $\begin{bmatrix}-36 \\ -16 \\ 76\end{bmatrix}$ (correct)

- $\begin{bmatrix}-36 \\ 16 \\ -76\end{bmatrix}$

We multiply each row in the matrix by the column vector using the dot product:

$$
\begin{aligned}Px & =\begin{bmatrix}-8 & -2 & 2 \\ 8 & 4 & 4 \\ -2 & -7 & -5\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}5 \\ -8 \\ -6\end{bmatrix} \\  & =\begin{bmatrix}(-8)\cdot 5+(-2)\cdot (-8)+2\cdot (-6) \\ (8)\cdot 5+4\cdot (-8)+(4)\cdot (-6) \\ (-2)\cdot 5+(-7)\cdot (-8)+(-5)\cdot (-6)\end{bmatrix} \\  & =\begin{bmatrix}-40+16-12 \\ 40-32-24 \\ -10+56+30\end{bmatrix} \\  & =\begin{bmatrix}-36 \\ -16 \\ 76\end{bmatrix}\end{aligned}
$$
