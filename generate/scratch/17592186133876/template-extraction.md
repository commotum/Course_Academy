# Four-stage extraction

## 1. Specific observed objects

Every decoded prompt, complete bank, and full ordered solution is retained in source.json. source-components.json independently reads those printed objects, records P, Q, point, field layout, all 58 exact options with original answer UUIDs/designations, and every ordered aligned RHS. This is a per-source extraction, not generation metadata. Easy objects include cross monomials (2xy²,4x²y), a zero held-coordinate contribution (y³−1,3xy²+1), y sin x and x² cos² y, crossed rational numerators over x²+y², ln(xy) and xy, cancellation (3xy²,−y³), and opposing square roots. The five radio banks mix scalar/vector form, wrong held coordinates, signs, omission of factors, and sometimes unexplained scalar patterns. Blanks have no authored distractors.

Moderate objects are point evaluations: the fluid components x²y and 2xy at (1,1); exp/trig components at (0,π/2); two logarithms at (0,−1); exponential product components at (2,0); radial product components at the 3-4-5 point; and y² sin(2x), x ln y at (π,1). The unrated exp/cos product source is evaluated at (0,1); its original worked solution and correct ref are absent. Product rules, chain factors and held-coordinate zero contributions are explicitly distinguished. The captured absence is preserved instead of inferred into raw source output.

## 2. Shared pattern and assessed response

For F=P i+Q j, compute P_x while holding y constant and Q_y while holding x constant; add to produce a scalar. Easy sources ask for the scalar field. Moderate sources also evaluate that field at an exact, familiar point. Source component rows remain ∂/∂x(P)+∂/∂y(Q), in that order; the generic formal P_x+Q_y row precedes them. Source reductions and evaluation rows are replayed without adding or omitting steps.

The raw extraction has one covariance parameter c. The whole field changes to cF, derivative input rows become ∂(cP)/∂x+∂(cQ)/∂y, every derived row becomes c times its original complete scalar, and all complete bank objects scale by c. This is justified by linearity of coordinate differentiation. It is an explicit evidence recipe, not an inferred diagnosis of numerical source errors. A c=1 coefficient is naturally omitted by the renderer; there is no branch that returns an entire literal question or correct-answer lookup. Source-case recipes enter through the same public generate API.

## 3. Component and complete-bank mapping

source-components.json exhaustively maps each field and every option. All 14 banks remain separate raw profiles, selected before scale sampling. For example q-41817 contains the correct 4x²+2y², a vector of crossed derivatives 4xy i+8xy j, their scalar sum 8xy, exchanged polynomial coordinates 2x²+4y², and the uncontracted vector 2y² i+4x² j. q-105554 similarly mixes scalar/vector coordinate differentiation and incomplete trig factors. The numerical point-evaluation banks often underdetermine the originating mistake: e.g. q-131174's 12,10,3,15,1 do not establish a unique parameterized wrong differentiation operation. Covariant c(12), c(10), c(3), c(15), c(1) reproduces the complete bank honestly as evidence and is never eligible for normal learner sampling.

The authored default radio bank is a complete five-option operation bank: P_x+Q_y (correct), P_x−Q_y, −P_x−Q_y, P_x, and 2P_x+Q_y. Every point-evaluation option is then evaluated at the same printed point. The four incorrect operations subtract the second contribution, reverse both signs, omit the second contribution, or duplicate the first contribution. These labels stay in author metadata, not learner content. The same bank is chosen before any parameter sampling; any equivalence, nonreal value, or invalid member rejects the whole instance. No first-valid option pool exists. Blank defaults retain a single scalar answer and the matching field placeholder.

## 4. Reusable slots, bounds and calculations

Whole field RHS slots, complete derivative argument slots, complete aligned RHS expression slots and complete scalar/vector option slots are used for raw covariance. No single-letter blind substitution crosses prose, basis vectors, function arguments, or coordinate tuples. c is a signed nonzero integer with |c|≤4, enforced for explicit calls too. Raw profile omissions of difficulty, worked solution and correct refs match each original individually.

Normal easy parameters a,b are signed nonzero integers of magnitude at most 4. The polynomial family excludes a=b; cancellation has a=±3,b=∓1. Constants −1 and +1 are incidental derivative-zero context, so they do not become free sampler parameters. Normal moderate coefficients are positive integers 1..4. Coordinates come from the finite POINTS catalog: small positive integers for the fluid field, y=0 for exponential product fields, y=π/2 or 3π/2 for exp/trig, positive-domain integer points for the logarithms, signed 3-4-5 or 5-12-13 coordinates for radial products, and familiar multiples of π with y=1 for trig/log. exp_cos uses familiar x angles π/2 or π at y=0; its raw source (0,1) is preserved separately. No decimal approximations or arbitrary transcendental evaluation are added.

The hard level is an explicitly authored design claim, not an observed difficulty label. affine_log_exp follows canonical e-8740: ln(ax+by), d x² exp(ky), with nonunit a,d,k∈2..4 and b∈1..4, at (1,0), (2,0), or (3,0). Both coordinate derivatives require retained chain factors; the log and exponential contributions stay nonzero. affine_squares adapts the immediately following sibling's two-dimensional affine-square structure: (ax+by)² and (dx+ky)², with the same coefficient bounds and points (1,±1),(2,±1). Both evaluated affine arguments must be nonzero. Both affine pairs are primitive (gcd(a,b)=1, and gcd(d,k)=1 for affine_squares), preventing an incidental common factor from removing the advertised nonunit affine workload. Each hard instance performs two complete first partial derivatives and one exact substitution, at a fixed workload within that family. It introduces no new operator, branch or progression. These are alternatives at one authored difficulty; their narrower nonunit/affine arithmetic is enforced after actual simplification as well as by coefficient/point guards. The original labels offer no empirical hard calibration.

The normal mathematical fingerprint uses the ordered independently differentiated components P_x,Q_y, family and meaningful evaluation point. It excludes answer banks/profiles and derivative-zero additive constants. Presentation identity includes version, actual prompt, fixed worked solution and complete bank with correct designation; shuffled option order and seed do not change it. Coefficient aliases and unused inputs are rejected. All values use exact SymPy arithmetic, while validate.py uses an independent printed-TeX parser rather than generation metadata.
