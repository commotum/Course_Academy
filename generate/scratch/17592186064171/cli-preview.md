$\int -12{e}^{x}\,\text{d}x=$

- $-12{e}^{x}+C$

- $\frac{{e}^{x}}{12}+C$

- $-\frac{{e}^{x}}{12}+C$

- $\frac{12}{{e}^{x}}+C$

- $12{e}^{x}+C$

Factoring out the constant $-12$ and calculating the resulting integral, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}\int -12{e}^{x}\,\text{d}x & =-12\int {e}^{x}\,\text{d}x \\  & =-12{e}^{x}+C.\end{aligned}
$$
