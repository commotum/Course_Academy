Given that $y < 480$ and $t \ge 0$, which of the following is an inhibited growth equation?

- $\frac{dy}{dt} = 2(480 - y)$
- $\frac{dy}{dt} = 480(y - 2)$
- $\frac{dy}{dt} =-2(y + 480)$
- $\frac{dy}{dt} = 2(y - 480)$
- $\frac{dy}{dt} = 480(y + 2)$

Worked solution:
An inhibited growth equation has the form

$$
\frac{dy}{dt}=k(a-y),
$$

where $k>0$ is a constant of proportionality, and $a>y$ for all $t\ge 0.$

From the given options, the only inhibited growth equation is

$$
\frac{dy}{dt} = 2(480 - y).
$$

In this case, the constant of proportionality is $k=2,$ and $a=480.$ Note that for inhibited growth, we must have $y<480$ for all $t\ge 0.$

Which of the following initial value problems is an inhibited growth problem?

- $\frac{dy}{dt}=4y(y-250),\,y(0)=50$
- $\frac{dy}{dt}=4(y-250),\,y(0)=200$
- $\frac{dy}{dt}=4(250-y),\,y(0)=150$
- $\frac{dy}{dt}=-4(y+250),\,y(0)=100$
- $\frac{dy}{dt}=4(250-y),\,y(0)=400$

Worked solution:
An inhibited growth initial value problem has the form

$$
\frac{dy}{dt}=k(a-y),\,y(0)={y}_{0}
$$

where $k>0$ is a constant of proportionality, and $a>y$ for all $t\ge 0.$

From the given options, the only inhibited growth problem is

$$
\frac{dy}{dt}=4(250-y),\,y(0)=150.
$$

In this case, $k=4,a=250,$ and ${y}_{0}=150.$ Notice that $a>{y}_{0},$ which is required for inhibited growth. 

The other options do not represent inhibited growth. In particular, the initial value problem

$$
\frac{dy}{dt}=4(250-y),\,y(0)=400,
$$

does *not* represent inhibited growth model because ${y}_{0}=400>a=250.$
