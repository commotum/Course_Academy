### q-103998 — equal_tan

$$
f(t,z)=⟨3\operatorname{sec}⁡t,\,\text{_____},\,z⟩,\,t\in (-\frac{\pi }{2},\frac{\pi }{2})
$$

The vector-valued function $f(t,z)$ above parametrizes one sheet of the hyperbolic cylinder defined in Cartesian coordinates as ${x}^{2}-{y}^{2}=9$ for $x\ge 3.$ What is the second component of $f?$

Sec axis x, tan axis y, requested y; a=3, b²=9. Function argument order is copied from the printed prompt. All free-coordinate and parameter domains remain explicit.

Recorded solution:

Recall that the hyperbola ${x}^{2}-{y}^{2}=9$ for $x\ge 3$ in $xy$-plane has the parametrization

$$
⟨x,y⟩=⟨3\operatorname{sec}⁡t,\,3\operatorname{tan}⁡t⟩,\,t\in (-\frac{\pi }{2},\frac{\pi }{2})
$$

since 

$$
\begin{aligned}{x}^{2}-{y}^{2} & =(3\operatorname{sec}⁡t)^{2}-(3\operatorname{tan}⁡t)^{2} \\  & =9({sec}^{2}⁡t-{tan}^{2}⁡t) \\  & =9.\end{aligned}
$$

Now, for any point $(x,y,z)$ on our surface, we obtain the following:

$$
\begin{aligned}f(t,z) & =⟨x,y,z⟩ \\  & =⟨3\operatorname{sec}⁡t,\,3\operatorname{tan}⁡t,\,z⟩\end{aligned}
$$

where $t\in (-\frac{\pi }{2},\frac{\pi }{2})$ and $z\in (-\infty ,\infty ).$

Therefore, the second component of $f$ is $3\operatorname{tan}⁡t.$
