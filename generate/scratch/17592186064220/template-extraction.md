The total integral scale is k for inverse sine and k/h for inverse tangent. No operation or reasoning stage is added for visual variety. Full-value blanks and radio fields vary the complete value by the integral scale.

Fixed transformed bounds are an explicit conservative sampling policy, not a claim that the source's numerical bounds are pedagogically necessary.
