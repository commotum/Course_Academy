Find the general solution to the differential equation $f'(x)=8 x.$

- $f(x)=- 4 x^{2}+C$
- $f(x)=16 x^{2}+C$
- $f(x)=8 x+C$
- $f(x)=4 x^{2}+C$
- $f(x)=\frac{x^{2}}{2}+C$

**Correct:** $f(x)=4 x^{2}+C$

First, notice that the derivative term is already isolated on the left:

$$
\begin{aligned}f'(x) &=8 x\end{aligned}
$$

Then, we integrate both sides of the equation with respect to $x,$ and we get

$$
\begin{aligned}\int f\prime(x)\,\text{d}x &=\int (8 x)\,\text{d}x \\ f(x) &=\int (8 x)\,\text{d}x \\ &=1\cdot \left(4 x^{2}\right)+C \\ &=4 x^{2}+C.\end{aligned}
$$
