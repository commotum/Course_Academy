## Original q-119805

![](/media/jake/SSD/EDB/math/images/95/95c30eab094a24beb6976262167401efa973dbac12781c69d074eb14e12e2743.png)

Consider the picture above. Which of the following is a unit vector that is parallel to $m+n?$

[]

First, we have to find $m+n.$ According to the triangle law of addition, the resultant $m+n$ goes from the tail of $m$ to the head of $n:$ 

![](images/87/87647211f77776c5f5f740c205fafb150eef1b9d3ebefb82df73a9fdcd953bdb.png)

In $△AHB$, we have $∠AHB={90}^{∘}$, $AH=0.5=\frac{1}{2}$ and $BH=2.$ So, the Pythagorean theorem gives

$$
\begin{aligned}|m+n| & =AB \\  & =\sqrt{A{H}^{2}+B{H}^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{{(\frac{1}{2})}^{2}+{2}^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{\frac{1}{4}+4} \\  & =\frac{\sqrt{17}}{2}.\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, the magnitude of $m+n$ is $\frac{\sqrt{17}}{2}.$ Consequently, the unit vector is

$$
\begin{aligned}u & =\frac{1}{|m+n|}\cdot (m+n) \\  & =\frac{2}{\sqrt{17}}(m+n).\end{aligned}
$$
