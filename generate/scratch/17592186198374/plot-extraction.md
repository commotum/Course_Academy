# Plot extraction and specification

All eight original raster assets were inspected at their original full resolution after white compositing (private .jpg inspection copies; no geometry inference from filenames).

1. Specific plots: canonical two tiny formula diagrams (186×49 T column-vector map, 200×50 inverse column-vector map) and one 362×300 rectangle `[3,5]×[0,3]`; practice five 362×300 rectangles: rational `[1,2]×[1,2]`; sine `[0,1]×[0,1]`; exponential `[1,2]×[-2,-1]`; exponential-square `[2,4]×[1,2]`; cosine-square `[1,3]×[0,π/2]`. Each rectangle has four black vertex markers with exact coordinate labels, pale blue interior Δ, top blue `v=upper` line/label, lower red `v=lower` line/label, u/v axes with positive-direction arrowheads, dashed horizontal/vertical guides and labelled coordinate values. The negative exponential rectangle is entirely below the u-axis. These are solution plots, not prompt/answer choices. No choice geometry exists.
2. General pattern: show the rectangle obtained by mapping the quadrilateral's vertices through the inferred affine inverse transformation. This supports bound identification; the prompt itself supplies all geometry as exact coordinates.
3. Components: exact bounds determine corner positions, labels, color-boundary lines, axis range and Δ location; coordinates are the same instance data as the solution/domain. Both symbolic transformation diagrams are fixed formulas. Raw evidence uses original asset references; originals are retained, hashed in plot-source-manifest.json. Canonical formula pictures can be represented by balanced TeX in normal questions.
4. Reusable spec: `rectangle_plot(parameters)` uses Matplotlib, white background, pale blue fill, red lower/blue upper edge, four markers, all four exact coordinate labels, dashed guides to visible arrow axes, and a centered Δ. Bounds derive from the instance, with margins for labels; axis zero is included even when outside the rectangle. Symbolic bounds are converted to numerical values for positions only. Files and sidecars are keyed by exact bounds, atomic and deterministic. A private process lock serializes Matplotlib mathtext rendering for simultaneous threads; independent processes use distinct temporary files and atomic final writes. Fresh simultaneous identical/different-bound calls are exercised. Sidecars record actual Matplotlib version, bounds and PNG hash. No direct SVG or image model. Plot rating moderate because a rectangle accompanies every completed solution, although the prompt does not require graph reading.

The raw original assets are faithful raster evidence; normal plots recreate their meaningful geometry and conventions, rather than preserving original pixels/font choices. No unclear fractional endpoints were inferred: all corners are explicitly labelled. Dashed guides and red/blue boundaries have no traversal meaning. The transformation is affine globally injective whenever its constant determinant is nonzero.
