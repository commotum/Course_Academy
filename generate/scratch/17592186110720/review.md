## original and parameterized replay — q-287970

Original prompt:
Consider the equation

$$
2px=3x
$$

where $p$ is a constant. Given that this equation has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of $p?$

Original answers: $\frac{3}{2}$, $\frac{2}{3}$, $-\frac{2}{3}$, $1$, $0$

Original solution:
Suppose we have an equation of the form

$$
ax=bx
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants. If $a=b,$ then the equation has infinitely many solutions.

Notice that this equation is in the form $ax=bx.$ In this case, 

$$
a=2p,\,b=3.
$$

Therefore, the equation has infinitely many solutions if

$$
2p=3.
$$

Solving this for $p,$ we get

$$
p=\frac{3}{2}.
$$
