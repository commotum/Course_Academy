Determine the domain of the vector field $F$ on ${R}^{2},$ given by

$$
F(x,y)=\frac{y}{{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}}\,i+\frac{x}{{x}^{2}-{y}^{2}}\,j\,.
$$

1. ${(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}\ge 1}$
2. ${(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,y\ne \pm x}$
3. ${(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}<1}$
4. ${(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,(x,y)\ne 0}$
5. ${(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,y\ne x}$

Worked solution:

The function $F$ can be expressed in terms of its components $P(x,y)$ and $Q(x,y)$ as

$$
F(x,y)=P(x,y)\,i+Q(x,y)\,j,
$$

where

$$
P(x,y)=\frac{y}{{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}},\,Q(x,y)=\frac{y}{{x}^{2}-{y}^{2}}.
$$

We first find the domain of each component function. Then, we compute their intersection to find the domain of $F:$

- The function $P(x,y)$ is defined everywhere other than when the denominator vanishes. To find where the denominator vanishes, we set it equal to zero and solve:

$$
{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}=0\,⟹\,x=y=0.
$$

Therefore, the domain of $P$ is given by

$$
{D}_{1}={(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,(x,y)\ne (0,0)}.
$$

- The function $Q(x,y)$ is defined everywhere other than when the denominator vanishes. To find where the denominator vanishes, we set it equal to zero and solve:

$$
{x}^{2}-{y}^{2}=0\,⟹\,y=\pm x
$$

Therefore, the domain of $Q$ is given by

$$
{D}_{2}={(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,y\ne \pm x}.
$$

Finally, the domain $D$ of $F$ is given by

$$
D={D}_{1}\cap {D}_{2}={(x,y)\in {R}^{2}\,|\,y\ne \pm x}\,.
$$
