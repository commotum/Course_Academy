Which of the following augmented matrices have coefficient matrices that are in row echelon form? Treat the final column of each augmented matrix as its constants column.

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-4 & 5 & -9 & 6 \\ 0 & -6 & -6 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -3 & -5\end{array}\right],\,B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-9 & -8 & -1 & 9 \\ 0 & 0 & -9 & 7 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -9\end{array}\right],\,C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}0 & 0 & 4 & 9 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & -4 \\ 9 & 9 & -1 & 2\end{array}\right].
$$

Select all that apply.

A coefficient matrix, obtained by omitting the final column of its augmented matrix, is in row echelon form if it satisfies the following conditions:

- All the zero rows (rows with only zeros), if any, are placed at the bottom of the matrix.
- Each leading entry of a row is in a column to the right of the leading entry of the row above it.

With that in mind, let's look at each matrix in turn.

- The coefficient matrix of $A$ is in row echelon form (both conditions are satisfied).

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-4 & 5 & -9 & 6 \\ 0 & -6 & -6 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -3 & -5\end{array}\right]
$$

- The coefficient matrix of $B$ is in row echelon form (both conditions are satisfied).

$$
B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-9 & -8 & -1 & 9 \\ 0 & 0 & -9 & 7 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -9\end{array}\right]
$$

- The coefficient matrix of $C$ is not in row echelon form since ${c}_{22}\ne 0$ (condition II is violated).

$$
C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}0 & 0 & 4 & 9 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & -4 \\ 9 & 9 & -1 & 2\end{array}\right]
$$

Therefore, only $A$ and $B$ have coefficient matrices that are in row echelon form.

Which of the following augmented matrices have coefficient matrices that are **not** in row echelon form? Treat the final column of each augmented matrix as its constants column.

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-7 & 1 & -8 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 & 8 \\ 0 & 0 & -4 & 7\end{array}\right],\,B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}8 & 8 & 5 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & -8 & -2 & -9 \\ 0 & 0 & -9 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 5\end{array}\right],\,C=\left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}3 & 0 & 3 & -3 & -4 \\ 0 & 0 & 8 & -7 & -5 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & -9 & -8 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -2 & -2\end{array}\right].
$$

Select all that apply.

A coefficient matrix, obtained by omitting the final column of its augmented matrix, is in row echelon form if it satisfies the following conditions:

I. All the zero rows (rows with only zeros), if any, are placed at the *bottom* of the matrix.

II. Each leading entry of a row is in a column *to the right* of the leading entry of the row above it.

With that in mind, let's look at each matrix in turn.

- The coefficient matrix of $A$ is not in row echelon form since ${a}_{33}\ne 0$ (condition II is violated).

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}-7 & 1 & -8 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 & 8 \\ 0 & 0 & -4 & 7\end{array}\right]
$$

- The coefficient matrix of $B$ is not in row echelon form since the second row contains only zeros and it is not at the bottom of the matrix (condition I is violated).

$$
B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}8 & 8 & 5 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & -8 & -2 & -9 \\ 0 & 0 & -9 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 5\end{array}\right]
$$

- The coefficient matrix of $C$ is not in row echelon form since ${c}_{33}\ne 0$ (condition II is violated).

$$
C=\left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}3 & 0 & 3 & -3 & -4 \\ 0 & 0 & 8 & -7 & -5 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & -9 & -8 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -2 & -2\end{array}\right]
$$

Therefore, $A,$ $B,$ and $C$ have coefficient matrices that are **not** in row echelon form.
