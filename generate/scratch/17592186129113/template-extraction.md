# Source extraction and generator contract

See template-extraction.json for every original prompt, solution, field, choice, integer matrix, dimensions, sign properties, constant column, pivot sequence, zero-row witness and allowed structural slot values. Source-choice-mapping.json maps all 66 original options to exact literal Boolean recipes and their 33 correct/33 incorrect designations. There are no unresolved distractors or mixed banks. The incomplete radio is retained as excluded evidence.

The backbone is to inspect the coefficient matrices of three labelled augmented arrays, test bottom zero rows and strictly rightward successive leading entries, then select a subset. Positive/negative query polarity and explicit source phrasing are kept as separate templates. Source solutions preserve their introductory conditions, A/B/C examination order, concrete row/entry or paired-column witnesses, displayed arrays and conclusion. Normal variants clarify that the final column supplies constants. Exact source replay returns the original wording; fidelity and mathematical validation are separately reported.

Slots: each matrix keeps its observed row/column counts and one final constants column. Leading coefficient columns vary across the full combinations preserving the observed weak order of nonzero pivots. This preserves the valid/invalid mathematical role and comparable workload. Zero coefficient rows may move to any positions retaining the source's misplaced-zero role; valid profiles keep all zero rows at bottom. Structural invariants follow the method, not the repeated position in sparse examples. All declared domains are listed and exhausted independently with the other matrices fixed. Nonzero coefficients sample signed integers -9..-1 or 1..9; trailing coefficients and independent constants sample -9..9. These are conservative same-method generalizations; all observed values (including 11) remain replayable. No new operation, matrix count, response shape, difficulty or solution stage is introduced.

Constants are not part of the echelon test. Their changes do not create distinct inventory tasks. Likewise scaling, sign, trailing coefficient changes, source profile name, seeds, UUIDs and matrix order cannot create fresh tasks: semantic deduplication uses sorted (dimensions, coefficient pivot/zero-row sequences) and query polarity. It is intentionally conservative. Source-example banks cannot be replenished merely by numerical decoration.

Generated UUIDs are derived from authored prompt/solution, field identities from question plus A/B/C, and answer identities from their own field plus Boolean value. Semantic deduplication is separate from identity. Generated records omit Math Academy IDs. Actual field grading uses the current application learning::grade_field on every Boolean choice.

Bounded sampler count failures remain explicit. Its finite structural policy is documented in capabilities.json and domain-catalog.json; it does not claim unlimited inventory or moderate/hard support.
