## Source q-42006

Which of the following augmented matrices are in row echelon form?

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}2 & 3 & -7 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 & 3 & -4 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 & 5\end{array}\right],\,B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}3 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{array}\right],\,C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}0 & 0 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & -4 & 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 2 & 3 & 2\end{array}\right].
$$

Select all that apply.

Remember that an augmented matrix is in row echelon form if its coefficient matrix satisfies the following conditions:

- All the zero rows (rows with only zeros), if any, are placed at the bottom of the matrix.
- Each leading entry of a row is in a column to the right of the leading entry of the row above it.

With that in mind, let's look at each matrix in turn.

- The matrix $A$ is in row echelon form (both conditions are satisfied).

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}2 & 3 & -7 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 & 3 & -4 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 & 5\end{array}\right]
$$

- The matrix $B$ is in row echelon form (both conditions are satisfied).

$$
B=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}3 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0\end{array}\right]
$$

- The matrix $C$ is not in row echelon form since ${c}_{22}\ne 0$ (condition II is violated).

$$
C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}0 & 0 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & -4 & 0 & 2 \\ -1 & 2 & 3 & 2\end{array}\right]
$$

Therefore, only $A$ and $B$ are in row echelon form.

A=\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & -2\end{array}\right],\,B=\left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}1 & 0 & -2 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 5\end{array}\right],\,C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}2 & 0 & 5 & 8 \\ 1 & -4 & 0 & 6 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 4\end{array}\right].
$$

Select all that apply.

Remember that an augmented matrix is in row echelon form if its coefficient matrix satisfies the following conditions:

- All the zero rows (rows with only zeros), if any, are placed at the bottom of the matrix.
- Each leading entry of a row is in a column to the right of the leading entry of the row above it.

With that in mind, let's look at each matrix in turn.

- The matrix $A$ is not in row echelon form since the second row contains only zeros and it is not at the bottom of the matrix (condition I is violated).

$$
A=\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 5 \\ 0 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & -2\end{array}\right]
$$

- The matrix $B$ is in row echelon form (both conditions are satisfied).

$$
B=\left[\begin{array}{cccc|c}1 & 0 & -2 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 5\end{array}\right]
$$

- The matrix $C$ is not in row echelon form since ${c}_{21}\ne 0$ (condition II is violated).

$$
C=\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}2 & 0 & 5 & 8 \\ 1 & -4 & 0 & 6 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 4\end{array}\right]
$$

Therefore, only $B$ is in row echelon form.
