The fixed backbone is two signed linear equations, exactly one integer row multiplication, distribution to every term and constant, cancellation of the FIRST variable, grouped collection of remaining-variable terms and constants, solve the second coordinate, substitute that coordinate into a specified original equation, evaluate its grouped operand, isolate and solve the first coordinate, and conclude with the ordered pair. Radio and the moderate supplied-pair template use first-row recovery, as their available source solutions do. The other blank template uses canonical small-row recovery, explicitly authored where the original solution is missing.

Variable slots are integer row coefficients, constants derived from a single chosen nonzero integer solution, scaled-row index, multiplier sign/magnitude, and eligible label pairs xy/ab/uv/st. The assessed target is always the first variable, regardless of label aliases. Permitted row order/sign/scale variation changes the printed operation but cannot create new mathematical tasks by itself. All signs, row names, coefficients, choices, keys, recovery operands, and prose references derive from the same instance. Singular systems, zero coefficients, invalid entire banks, and out-of-band printed or primitive-row workloads are rejected. Full systems are canonicalized by primitive signed equation triples and row order; equivalent solutions alone do not merge different systems.

Normal radio banks retain one complete authored profile: correct tuple; omit scaling the changed row's constant; omit scaling its second-variable coefficient; reverse the scaled-constant addition sign; reverse subtraction during coordinate recovery. Each incorrect second coordinate is back-substituted into the same small original row, and the final error reverses that row's subtraction. The entire five-value profile must be defined, distinct, contain exactly one solution, and include another all-integer tuple to avoid a number-form shortcut. No pooled distractors or offset filling. Blanks have no distractors.

The finite capacity evidence counts unique systems only, excluding label, equation-scale, seed, option-order, layout, and bank aliases. CLI all/count1 always produces a three-record list. Built-in integer seeds/counts are required; bool, float, string, null, nonpositive and overcapacity counts are rejected. Batch collection uses a bounded search and deduplicates mathematical fingerprints.
