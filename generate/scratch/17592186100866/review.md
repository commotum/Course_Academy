## named-radio / seed 3

Source q-126511:

Find $Zx$ if 
$Z=\begin{bmatrix}1 & -4 \\ 2 & -7\end{bmatrix}$
and
$x=\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}.$

Variant:

Find $Ax$ if 
$A=\begin{bmatrix}-4 & 7 \\ 6 & -7\end{bmatrix}$
and
$x=\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ 7\end{bmatrix}.$

We multiply each row in the matrix by the column vector using the dot product. Multiplying the first row, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{bmatrix}-4 & 7 \\ 6 & -7\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} & =\begin{bmatrix}(-4)\cdot 3+7\cdot 7 \\ ∗\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}37 \\ ∗\end{bmatrix}.\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying the second row, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}\begin{bmatrix}-4 & 7 \\ 6 & -7\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}3 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} & =\begin{bmatrix}37 \\ 6\cdot 3+(-7)\cdot 7\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}37 \\ -31\end{bmatrix}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, 
$Ax=\begin{bmatrix}37 \\ -31\end{bmatrix}.$
