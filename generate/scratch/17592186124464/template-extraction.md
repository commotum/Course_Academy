# Four extraction stages

## 1. Specific source descriptions

`extracted-components.json` is the exhaustive per-question extraction. It records each exact surface, point, chosen level function, all three exact gradient components, every ordered row of every aligned block, each field in captured order with attribute presence and correct designation, and every full option in the original complete bank. Each option is decoded as a whole point/direction pair, including symmetric relations with reversed numerators, omitted `r=` and parameter suffixes. The q-37515 legacy bank is retained separately from its authoritative current bank.

All original parameters have small integers, a half, or a familiar `pi/2` angle. Logarithms and radicals are evaluated in their real differentiable domains. Each source point lies on its printed surface and each gradient is nonzero. Formal operators use held-coordinate partial derivatives. The symbolic, substituted, partially evaluated (where present), collected basis-vector, full vector, line-construction, sign/scalar normalization and conclusion rows are individually preserved. There is no recovery of a separate equation-fragment blank merely because some vector choices omit `r=`.

## 2. Generalized family pattern

Write the surface as a level set; compute the three partial derivatives of the explicitly chosen function; evaluate the gradient at the point; use that gradient as the line's direction through the specified point; express the full line in the requested vector or symmetric form. Preserve the source family's surface-to-level convention and shared prose. A complete bank is selected before sampling parameters.

## 3. Components and choice recipes

Every captured option is mapped in `extracted-components.json`, with its original field, UUID, exact string, complete point and complete direction. Captured banks use a parameterized coordinate isometry: if `u_j = s_j (x_j-h_j)`, the new surface is the original surface in `u`, its point is `h+s*P`, and its gradient is `s*G`. Every full wrong line is transformed by the same isometry. This is a defensible geometric preservation recipe, not an inferred explanation of the historical wrong operation. Those banks remain public **opaque evidence-only** profiles, even where their likely sign/permutation mistakes look familiar. They are never normal defaults.

Normal authored banks operate on the actual assessed method. For vector questions with three active partials, the entire bank consists of the true gradient, each of the three coordinate-partial omissions, and the correct direction through a wrong basepoint. With two active partials it uses both nonzero-partial omissions, a spurious contribution in the missing coordinate, and a wrong basepoint in that coordinate. The axis case uses both misplaced-axis derivatives and both spurious second-coordinate contributions. For symmetric questions, omitting a component would introduce a zero denominator; the corresponding complete bank instead repeats one coordinate-partial contribution, separately for x, y and z, plus a wrong basepoint. These are specified method operations, not random numerical perturbations. All operations are author metadata; source learner prose contains no new distractor diagnosis.

Whole-bank checks compare full geometrical lines, allowing nonzero direction scaling, parameter rescaling and point shifts along the line when allowed. A `t=0` constraint requires the actual point at parameter zero. Banks with multiple eligible correct responses, duplicate eligible responses or singular directions are rejected as a whole. Symmetric choices additionally require all three direction components nonzero. Explicit inputs obey the same bounds as random sampling.

## 4. Reusable masks, domains and scope

The immutable source strings are format masks, not answer lookup tables. Complete function slots, formal operator arguments, symbolic component masks, numeric component masks, point tuples, whole vectors and each full line relation are transformed separately. Commands and basis labels are protected while mathematical arguments remain transformable. Original-parameter replay passes through these same operations. Original UUIDs and DB evidence are retained at the anchor; transformed evidence receives deterministic content identities and retains original refs in metadata.

At changed parameters, normal solutions add one explicit substitution row using the actual printed function and actual displayed point before the preserved source numeric reduction rows. Source partially evaluated rows remain separate and unevaluated; reflected signs group whole coefficients. All source shared prose and ordered source stages remain, including the final vector/symmetric line independent of the chosen wrong response. Reflection factors implement the chain rule. No tangent plane, unit-vector normalization or other KP is introduced.

Easy translations are integer triples in `[-2,2]^3`; moderate/hard use `[-3,3]^3`. Every level supports all eight axis reflections. These isometries preserve the original arithmetic/number forms and the underlying differentiation method; they change actual surfaces, points and full line geometry. They do not claim new recovered structures. Easy has a finite domain of at most 1,000 coordinate-isometry inputs; each observed family has at most 2,744. Some reflections are mathematical aliases when a coordinate is zero and the surface even; fingerprints deduplicate those. The broad source family coverage supports repeated practice without inventing new branches, but this version does not vary the underlying coefficients or unshifted evaluation point. That is an honest limitation.

Task fingerprints canonicalize polynomial surface relations up to nonzero constant scaling and include the actual point and response criterion; they exclude banks, profile aliases, seed and option order. Content identities include version, actual difficulty, prompt, fixed solution, field layout and complete displayed bank. All metadata stays outside learner content.
