Hard uses f=ax²yʳz+bxyzᵗ and g=1/(xyz), with nonzero signed coefficient magnitudes 1–4, r∈{1,2,3}, t∈{2,3,4}; both monomials have degree 4–6, comparable to observed trivariate degree six. Both hard monomials survive every partial derivative, so no sampled coefficient removes the two-term workload. x,y,z are nonzero. The harder workload is six derivatives with two f terms per component and six reciprocal-product scalar component operations (twelve monomial contributions), with no new topic or within-level progression.

Normal sampling serves all twelve eligible family/bank pairs at their captured levels: seven moderate families (eleven banks) and the observed hard Gaussian (one bank); the hard binomial and easy linear families are authored additions. These include eight complete method banks and four eligible observed banks, selecting each before parameters. It uses actual method quantities, not random perturbations. Instances with any collision are rejected as a whole. Explicit use of a profile may be rejected where its full bank collides; no first-valid replacement is performed.

## Fidelity, stages, identity and limits

Raw attribute presence is checked per question, including missing difficulty/solution/correct ref. Exact source strings are compared alongside independently parsed displayed scalar/vector mathematics. The native grader checks actual owned radio submissions; a UUID-selection pass is not counted as a mathematical or fidelity pass. A separate stage regression replaces the distributed row by the algebraically equal final vector: algebraic checks pass but operative-stage checks must fail. Partial-derivative operator arguments, derivative values, supplied component vectors, every distributed/reduced solution row and final vectors are read from learner-visible content.

Canonical task fingerprints use the reduced complete product, fixed variable domain and supplied/unsupplied information, excluding seed, options, profiles and unused cancellations such as the reciprocal3 x-origin. Swapped factors, coefficient aliases and banks cannot create new mathematical tasks. Presentation identities use version, actual difficulty, prompt, fixed solution, full bank and designation. Seeds only affect ordering and random selection.

The finite easy coefficient domain contains 3776 valid whole-bank parameter instances and 902 distinct reduced products after factor-order/scale aliases are deduplicated. Moderate has 1050 canonical same-method candidates (169 in each of six translated families and 36 cancellation coefficient pairs); reciprocal3 x-origin is excluded. Observed hard Gaussian has 169 translated tasks; authored hard binomial has 576 distinct reduced products. These are mathematical candidate/analytic enumeration claims, not full-domain native grading claims. No TeX escape repair is claimed: decoded ord=92 counts are recorded in decoded-strings.json; adjacent characters are the expected aligned row breaks. The captured reciprocal3 introductory prose names two variables; exact raw replay retains it, and normal generation minimally substitutes the three-variable names. No mathematical source error was found in the seven designated answers; no missing designation is counted as matched.
