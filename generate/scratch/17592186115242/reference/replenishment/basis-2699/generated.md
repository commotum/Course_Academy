Determine the value of $k$ for which the following system of linear equations is inconsistent:

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}x+2y &=-5 \\ -2x+ky &=0\end{aligned}\right.
$$

- $k=4$
- $k=-4$
- $k=0$
- $k=2$
- $k=-2$

The augmented matrix for this system is

$$
M=\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1 & 2 & -5 \\ -2 & k & 0\end{array}\right].
$$

Using the method of Gaussian elimination, we can reduce this matrix to row echelon form, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}M & =\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1 & 2 & -5 \\ -2 & k & 0\end{array}\right] & {R}_{2} & :={R}_{2}+2{R}_{1} \\  & ∼\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1 & 2 & -5 \\ 0 & k+4 & -10\end{array}\right]\end{aligned}
$$

The corresponding system is now 

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}x+2y &=-5 \\ (k+4)y &=-10.\end{aligned}\right.
$$

The system is inconsistent if it has no solutions. For the system to have no solutions, one of the equations above must be false.

If $k=-4,$ then the second equation is $0=-10,$ which is false, and the system will have no solution.

Therefore, our system is inconsistent when $k=-4.$
