Let $P=QR.$ What is the value of ${p}_{22}$, if

$$
Q=\begin{bmatrix}-1 & -5 & 2 \\ 5 & -5 & 3 \\ -6 & 3 & -3\end{bmatrix},\,R=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 \\ 6 & -1\end{bmatrix}?
$$

${p}_{22}=$
{{field-1}}

Field field-1 (:answer-field.type/blank)

* [correct] 17

Worked solution:
Multiplying the $2$nd row in $Q$ by the $2$nd column in $R$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{p}_{22} & =\begin{bmatrix}-1 & -5 & 2 \\ 5 & -5 & 3 \\ -6 & 3 & -3\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 \\ 6 & -1\end{bmatrix} \\  & =\begin{bmatrix}5 & -5 & 3\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}4 \\ 0 \\ -1\end{bmatrix} \\  & =5(4)+(-5)(0)+3(-1) \\  & =20+0-3 \\  & =17.\end{aligned}
$$
