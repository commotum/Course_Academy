$$
{∮}_{{C}_{1}}F\cdot \text{d}r=- \frac{17}{2},\,{∮}_{{C}_{2}}F\cdot \text{d}r=- \frac{19}{3},\,{∮}_{{C}_{3}}F\cdot \text{d}r=\frac{23}{3},
$$

where ${C}_{1},$ ${C}_{2},$ and ${C}_{3}$ are oriented clockwise, calculate the value of ${∮}_{∂D}F\cdot \text{d}r.$

- $\frac{4}{3}$
- $\frac{97}{6}$
- $\frac{59}{6}$
- $\frac{13}{6}$
- $- \frac{59}{6}$

Worked solution
To calculate the total circulation of a vector field over the boundary of a region containing holes, we add together the circulations over each *positively oriented* boundary.

Remember that:

- the positive direction is *counterclockwise* for the *outer* curve ${C}_{1}$ (because the region $D$ is always on the left as we traverse ${C}_{1}$ counterclockwise), and

- the positive direction is *clockwise* for the *inner* curves ${C}_{2}$ and ${C}_{3}$ (because the region $D$ is always on the left as we traverse ${C}_{2}$ and ${C}_{3}$ clockwise).

The diagram below shows the positive orientations of all three curves.

![](images/fa/fa1022d2a1718627898289abdd4d3035a6e06230eea16d0e46832675b2e37def.png)

Notice that the positive orientation of the outer curve ${C}_{1}$ is opposite that of ${C}_{1}$ as stated in the question.

Therefore, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}{∮}_{∂D}F\cdot \text{d}r & =-{∮}_{{C}_{1}}F\cdot \text{d}r+{∮}_{{C}_{2}}F\cdot \text{d}r+{∮}_{{C}_{3}}F\cdot \text{d}r \\  & =-(- \frac{17}{2})+(- \frac{19}{3})+\frac{23}{3} \\  & =\frac{-(-51)+(-38)+46}{6} \\  & =\frac{59}{6}.\end{aligned}
$$
