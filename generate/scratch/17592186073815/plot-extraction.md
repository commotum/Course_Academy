# Plot specification

Inspected all eight practice diagrams and canonical e-12612 side by side (source asset references and byte hashes in extraction.json). Every prompt has a white background, no coordinate axes or tick labels, a solid blue line with rotated equation label, red supplied point with its coordinate label, blue perpendicular foot labeled `(?,?)`, thin black dashed segment joining the point to the foot, and a filled gold right-angle marker. Positive-slope examples rise rightward; negative-slope examples fall rightward. Sources are schematic and are not consistent metric plots; numerical equations and orthogonality establish the intended geometry. There are no choice or solution plots.

For each changed instance, Matplotlib draws the exact line, target, perpendicular foot, equal-aspect geometry, segment and right-angle square from the same rational parameters used for the answer. Label placement is offset and plot bounds include all objects with padding. The foot label never reveals its coordinates. Plot rating moderate: every variant needs a prompt diagram carrying the perpendicular projection cue. At t=0 the original asset is reused unchanged. Variants keep representation, colors, line styles, labels and geometric relationships; font and precise visual angles may differ from schematic sources. No SVG renderer or AI image generation is used.

Renderer: Matplotlib Agg, 5.4 by 4.2 inches, 120 dpi, white PNG, fixed margins, no randomness. Assets have deterministic template/parameter filenames locally. Preparation hashes bytes, publishes through ImageLibrary, rewrites database-relative references, and checks stored bytes. Mathematical plotting data and exact rational values are independently validated; boundary diagrams for all profiles receive visual review.
