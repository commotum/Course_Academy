The quarter-ellipse above is defined by the parametric equations 

$$
x=3\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=2\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta .
$$

What is the domain of the parameter $\theta ?$

The parametric equations of an ellipse centered at the origin with a horizontal radius of length $a$ and a vertical radius of length $b$ are

$$
x=a\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=b\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta ,\,\theta \in [0,2\pi ).
$$

In our case, $a=3$ and $b=2.$ Therefore, the parametric equations of the *full* ellipse are

$$
x=3\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=2\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta ,\,\theta \in [0,2\pi ).
$$

To restrict the parameterization to a quarter-ellipse, we associate the parameter $\theta $ with the central angle formed by a point $(x,y)$ on the elipse and the $x$-axis. 

![](/media/jake/SSD/EDB/math/images/0b/0b38ee461b8895898a73f2b2120e1dac9823b1bf19b46924786c5c27c9beae74.png)

We can see that if we restrict $\theta $ to $[0,\frac{\pi }{2}]$, we get the quarter-ellipse.

Therefore, the parametric equations of the quarter-ellipse are 

$$
x=3\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta ,\,y=2\operatorname{sin}⁡\theta ,\,\theta \in [0,\frac{\pi }{2}].
$$
