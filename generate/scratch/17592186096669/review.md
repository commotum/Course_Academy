# Source and homothetic variants

Solve the following system of equations:
    

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}x-y-z &=4 \\ y+2z &=2 \\ z &=\frac{1}{2}\end{aligned}\right.
$$

- $⟨\frac{11}{2},1,\frac{1}{2}⟩$ (correct)
- $⟨\frac{9}{2},1,2⟩$
- $⟨\frac{9}{2},2,\frac{1}{2}⟩$
- $⟨1,\frac{9}{2},- \frac{1}{2}⟩$
- $⟨\frac{1}{2},-1,2⟩$

The third equation states that $z=\frac{1}{2}.$ Substituting this into the second equation, we get

$$
y+2(\frac{1}{2})=2\,⟹\,y=2-(1)=1.
$$

Now, we substitute $y=1$ and $z=\frac{1}{2}$ into the first equation to get

$$
x-(1)-(\frac{1}{2})=4\,⟹\,x=4-(-1)-(- \frac{1}{2})=\frac{11}{2}.
$$

Therefore, the solution is $x=\frac{11}{2}$, $y=1$, and $z=\frac{1}{2}.$ In vector form, the solution can be written as $⟨\frac{11}{2},1,\frac{1}{2}⟩.$

Solve the following system of equations:
    

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}x-y-z &=8 \\ y+2z &=4 \\ z &=1\end{aligned}\right.
$$

- $⟨11,2,1⟩$ (correct)
- $⟨9,2,4⟩$
- $⟨9,4,1⟩$
- $⟨2,9,-1⟩$
- $⟨1,-2,4⟩$

The third equation states that $z=1.$ Substituting this into the second equation, we get

$$
y+2(1)=4\,⟹\,y=4-(2)=2.
$$

Now, we substitute $y=2$ and $z=1$ into the first equation to get

$$
x-(2)-(1)=8\,⟹\,x=8-(-2)-(-1)=11.
$$

Therefore, the solution is $x=11$, $y=2$, and $z=1.$ In vector form, the solution can be written as $⟨11,2,1⟩.$

Solve the following system of equations:
    

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}{x}_{1}+{x}_{2}-{x}_{3} &=2 \\ {x}_{2}-6{x}_{3} &=3 \\ {x}_{3} &=- \frac{1}{3}\end{aligned}\right.
$$

- $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{2}{3} \\ 1 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}$ (correct)
- $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{8}{3} \\ -1 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{8}{3} \\ 1 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{2}{3} \\ 0 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{5}{3} \\ 0 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}$

The third equation states that ${x}_{3}=- \frac{1}{3}.$ Substituting this into the second equation, we get

$$
{x}_{2}-6(- \frac{1}{3})=3\,⟹\,{x}_{2}=3-(2)=1.
$$

Now, we substitute ${x}_{2}=1$ and ${x}_{3}=- \frac{1}{3}$ into the first equation to get

$$
{x}_{1}+(1)-(- \frac{1}{3})=2\,⟹\,{x}_{1}=2-(1)-(\frac{1}{3})=\frac{2}{3}.
$$

Therefore, the solution is ${x}_{1}=\frac{2}{3}$, ${x}_{2}=1$, and ${x}_{3}=- \frac{1}{3}.$ In vector form, the solution can be written as $\begin{bmatrix}\frac{2}{3} \\ 1 \\ - \frac{1}{3}\end{bmatrix}.$

Solve the following system of equations:
    

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}{x}_{1}+{x}_{2}-{x}_{3} &=6 \\ {x}_{2}-6{x}_{3} &=9 \\ {x}_{3} &=-1\end{aligned}\right.
$$

- $\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$ (correct)
- $\begin{bmatrix}8 \\ -3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}8 \\ 3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 0 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$
- $\begin{bmatrix}5 \\ 0 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}$

The third equation states that ${x}_{3}=-1.$ Substituting this into the second equation, we get

$$
{x}_{2}-6(-1)=9\,⟹\,{x}_{2}=9-(6)=3.
$$

Now, we substitute ${x}_{2}=3$ and ${x}_{3}=-1$ into the first equation to get

$$
{x}_{1}+(3)-(-1)=6\,⟹\,{x}_{1}=6-(3)-(1)=2.
$$

Therefore, the solution is ${x}_{1}=2$, ${x}_{2}=3$, and ${x}_{3}=-1.$ In vector form, the solution can be written as $\begin{bmatrix}2 \\ 3 \\ -1\end{bmatrix}.$
