# Backbone and explicit slots

The method is a single substitution u=ln(a x^n), derivative n/x, integration of one power of u (or 1/u), and back-substitution. Every response is the full antiderivative family including C. The source's shared prose is retained: “Let's set … Then, we have” and “Using the above, we can write the integral in terms of u and evaluate”. Source equations are replayed at originals, including unsimplified derivative operands, intermediate coefficient collection, power-rule and back-substitution rows. Dangling multiplication markers in two captured rows are removed as explicitly recorded source repairs.

Fixed components: indefinite integral, natural log, differential dx, one substitution, one field, five choices, field key, C response role, ordered worked stages. The x/u names serve distinct original/substitution roles; changing them provides no new mathematical task. Endpoints and endpoint contributions are inapplicable because this KP is indefinite. Other logarithm bases and definite integrals belong to later KPs and are excluded.

Variable slots are builtin integers. k is coefficient parameter1..12, with integer c=k, reciprocal c=1/k or radical c=sqrt(k) fixed by the source family. Radical k is restricted to 2,3,5,6,7,10,11. a is an inner positive scale2..6 for scaled families and1 for plain/power families. n is the inner exponent2..4 for power families and1 for linear families. Source a/k coincidences are not constraints: q-13822's inner scale and denominator scale vary independently. The outer exponent p is represented exactly by p_num/p_den. Easy p ranges1..3, a same-power-rule extension from observed1/2; each moderate/hard family's outer exponent remains its observed role (1, -2, -1/2, 1/2 or -1). a and n change only where their original role is present. No additional operation, reasoning branch or response type is introduced.

Dependencies: U=ln(a x^n), U'=n/x, F=c U^p/x; primitive c U^(p+1)/[n(p+1)]+C or c/n ln|U|+C. All prompt integrands, derivative operands, reciprocal differentials, transformed integral operands, intermediate primitive coefficients, answers and distractors derive from these slots. Source bank profiles are selected before sampling, and the complete instance is rejected if a choice collision occurs. There is no pooled first-valid bank.

Every source ID has a separately recorded full bank in choice-ledger.json. `bank()` contains the parameterized operation sequence for every option in source order. The ledger maps original option ID/value one-to-one to the expression at original parameters, including C omission and compound changes of argument, power and scale. Recipes with ambiguous pedagogical interpretation preserve the algebraic pattern; no unsupported misconception label is substituted. Numeric literals in recipes are structural exponents/scales from their profile, not unchanged captured expressions.

Real domains: require a x^n>0. Negative log powers and lnAbs primitives require ln(a x^n)≠0; roots require ln(a x^n)>0 on open integration intervals. For even n both negative and positive x branches are permitted when these inequalities hold. No identity ln(x^n)=n ln(x) is forced. Differentiation of ln|g| uses g'/g on each real connected interval where g≠0. Answer-family completeness requires C; the source's no-C distractor is intentionally incomplete even though its derivative matches.
