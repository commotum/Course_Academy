# Source-derived quartic templates

Read classification.md and the complete source ledger source-components.json together. The latter retains every prompt, field, original answer UUID/value, solution, exact original parameters, option recipe, full-bank profile and mathematical equivalence check. No unmapped option is admitted in a supported profile.

The backbone is F(x)=(x-r)(x-s)P(x), with an expanded quartic supplied, exactly two given monic linear factors, and one field requesting P(x). P(x)=(cx-a)(x-b). Fixed variable x, notation P(x), field layout, five choices for radio, and the ordered instructional stages are necessary to preserve the response contract and method. Two distinct given roots are required so the second factor continues to divide the first quotient. The blank expects the expression after the supplied P(x)=, not an equation. It has no distractors.

Slots r,s select nonzero signed integer roots; a,b select nonzero signed factor constants; c=1 for easy and c=3 for moderate. Every prompt, quotient, table, answer and solution derives from these slots. Normal bounded sampling uses r in {-4,-2,1,2,3,4,5}, s in {-5,-3,-1,1,2,3}, a and b within one of their observed values; it rejects r=s and ambiguous/equivalent options. These bounds are conservative same-method extensions, not observed source coverage. They retain integer synthetic-division arithmetic and source leading-coefficient workload. Original parameters remain replayable. No new operation or extra stage is introduced.

q-18862 divides by s first and r second; other profiles divide by r then s. This original sequence is retained. Two tables include every coefficient, including zeros; each division is followed by a quotient identity and corresponding product identity. Radio ends with factoring the quadratic and explicitly naming P(x). Blank ends with P(x) as expanded and factored forms. Wording follows the original operative clauses. Formatting differences are separately recorded: conventional powers and spacing from SymPy, normalized Markdown table headers, and a standard underbrace instead of the captured Unicode annotation.

Distractor formulas are observed expression recipes, not claims about psychological mistakes. Complete banks remain separate. Their mathematical values replay all originals; no pooled mixture is used. In q-112626 the (2-x) factor becomes (a+1-x), and (5-x)/(x-5) become (r-x)/(x-r). This is an explicitly inferred literal-pattern parameterization with unresolved error interpretation, retained in the ledger. Similarly, coefficient offsets in moderate profiles and constant offsets are observed pattern generalizations. No explanatory distractor feedback is added to the learner presentation.

Distinctness is the expanded quartic together with the unordered supplied roots, independent of seed, UUID, bank profile, option order or division order. A supplied-root order change exercises stale-reference detection but is deliberately not counted as a new mathematical task. Existing source and canonical fingerprints are excluded during preparation. Stable UUIDs include full authored bank content, preventing identity collisions across banks, while inventory deduplication still uses mathematical task fingerprints.
