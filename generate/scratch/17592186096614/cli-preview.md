## q-1920 — moderate
Calculate $\frac{dy}{dx}$ for $y =-2e^{x} + 3\ln x$.

- $-2 e^{x} + 3 \ln{(x )}$
- $2 e^{x} + \frac{3}{x}$
- $-2 e^{x} + \frac{1}{x}$
- $\frac{3}{x}$
- $-2 e^{x} + \frac{3}{x}$ (correct)

Taking the derivative, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{dy}{dx} &=\frac{d}{dx}(-2e^{x} + 3\ln x) \\ &= -2(e^{x})+3(\frac{1}{x})\\ &= -2e^{x} + \frac{3}{x}.\end{aligned}
$$
