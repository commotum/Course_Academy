Find the Jacobian determinant of the transformation $T:(u,v,w)\to(x(u,v,w),y(u,v,w),z(u,v,w))$, given that

$$
u=- 5 x\qquad v=y\qquad w=z.
$$

- $\frac{1}{25}$
- $- \frac{1}{5}$
- $\frac{1}{5}$
- $-5$
- $1$

For a transformation

$$
T:(u,v,w)\to(x(u,v,w),y(u,v,w),z(u,v,w)),
$$

the Jacobian determinant of $T$ is $\frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,w)}$.

In this case, we're only given ${T}^{-1}.$ So, we compute the Jacobian determinant of $T$ as follows:

- First, we compute $\frac{\partial(u,v,w)}{\partial(x,y,z)}$, the Jacobian determinant of ${T}^{-1}$.

- Then, we will compute the Jacobian determinant of $T$ using the relation

$$
\frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,w)}=\left(\frac{\partial(u,v,w)}{\partial(x,y,z)}\right)^{-1}.
$$

First, we find the partial derivatives:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=-5 & \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=0 & \frac{\partial u}{\partial z}=0 \\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}=0 & \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}=1 & \frac{\partial v}{\partial z}=0 \\ \frac{\partial w}{\partial x}=0 & \frac{\partial w}{\partial y}=0 & \frac{\partial w}{\partial z}=1\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the corresponding Jacobian determinant of ${T}^{-1}$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial(u,v,w)}{\partial(x,y,z)}&=\begin{vmatrix}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial u}{\partial z} \\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial v}{\partial z} \\ \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial w}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial w}{\partial z}\end{vmatrix} \\ &=\begin{vmatrix}-5 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{vmatrix} \\ &=-5\begin{vmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{vmatrix} \\ &=-5\left(1\cdot 1-0\right) \\ &=-5\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we take the reciprocal:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,w)}&=\left(\frac{\partial(u,v,w)}{\partial(x,y,z)}\right)^{-1} \\ &=- \frac{1}{5}\end{aligned}
$$
