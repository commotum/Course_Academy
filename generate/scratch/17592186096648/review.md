## Generated authored easy
Template: lambda_first; profile: method_ratio

Given that $λ(a - 2b)$ and $7a - 14b$ are equal (non-zero) vectors, find the scale factor $λ$.

- $-\frac{1}{7}$
- No such $λ$
- $-7$
- $7$
- $\frac{1}{7}$

Correct: $7$

Worked solution:
Notice that

$$
λ(a - 2b)=λ(1)a+λ(-2)b.
$$

The same scale factor must multiply the whole non-zero vector. Comparing the two coefficients gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
λ &= \frac{7}{1} = 7 \\
λ &= \frac{-14}{-2} = 7
\end{aligned}
$$

Notice that

$$
7a - 14b=7(a - 2b).
$$

Therefore,

$$
λ=7
$$

The two non-zero vectors are parallel. Their directions are the same because the factor is positive.
