# Backbone and slots

Every direct template supplies an explicit real integrand and asks for its indefinite integral. The assessed move is linearity followed by the already-known primitive rules e^x, a^x/ln(a), polynomial powers, a constant, or 1/x. The source's coefficient signs, term order, answer shape, fixed prose and ordered formal-integral stages are preserved in source-derived normal variants. The raw capture always preserves every original byte-level field value, omission and solution operand.

`templates.json` contains the six complete keyed radio source patterns. The only source-derived numerical slot is `base`, an integer 2 through 12. The exponent of the integrand remains x, and every matching base/logarithm occurrence changes together. In q-31254 and q-31260 the observed erroneous e^{base*x} option also follows the base. Other distractor literals stay fixed and are not silently reinterpreted. The exact parameterized expression and original value for every source option, field, correct designation and profile appear in `choice-recipes.json`. Wrong-choice interpretations are unresolved observed patterns, not asserted learner diagnoses. Each complete profile is chosen first; all five expressions are then constructed and jointly checked for correctness and collisions.

q-31311 has base 2 originally and q-51102 base 4; their complete observed bank expressions use the same bounded base slot at the actual exponential-base and logarithm positions. Authored keys/solutions are separate from the exact raw omissions. q-31261 has original base 8 and uses the same base slot; its field is an authored completion. The three function-blank sources have fixed original coefficients/functions and preserve their substitution and uncollected primitive stages. Their definitions are explicitly added to make the normal problem well posed. They do not contribute meaningful variety merely because they have different source IDs or whitespace.

## Same-method authored extensions

The source-derived base-only space is too narrow for repeated practice. Authored E/M/H templates have fixed per-level workloads and do not form a progression within a level. Numerical slots are shared across prompt, each formal operand, primitive arithmetic, complete bank and solution:

- Easy: exactly two terms, a·e^x+b·base^x. `base` is 2..12; a is one of ±2,±3,±4,±5; b is one of ±2,±3,±4. The signs and independent nonzero coefficients vary. Coefficients ±1 are omitted to keep comparable constant-multiple workload.
- Moderate: the same two exponential terms plus exactly one prior-rule tail c·x^n, c, or c/x. c is ±2,±3,±4; n is 1..3. The tail selector is an explicitly authored structural slot supported by the observed polynomial/constant/reciprocal families. For constant/reciprocal tails the unused power field is metadata only and is not counted as variety. Each variant needs three termwise primitives.
- Hard: exactly four terms: a·e^x+b·base^x+d·second_base^x plus the same bounded prior-rule tail. `second_base` is 2..12 and differs from `base`; d is ±2 or ±3. The added work is another application of the same general-base primitive, with signed arithmetic. No substitution, product/quotient rule, definite integral, initial value or new function is introduced. This difficulty is authored, with no claim of observed MA hard evidence.

For all authored levels, the whole five-option radio profile is selected before values are constructed. `authored-omit-log`: correct primitive; replace the b·base^x/ln(base) primitive by b·base^x; reverse the e^x sign; double its coefficient; omit the general-base primitive. `authored-multiply-log`: correct primitive; multiply the base exponential by ln(base) in place of dividing; reverse that term's sign; reverse the e^x sign; omit the e^x term. All options include C. These are authored complete banks, not mixed source banks. Coefficients are nonzero and bases are positive nonunit, so these recipes cannot coincide modulo an integration constant.

No target-selection slot is claimed: the learner always integrates the whole supplied sum. Consequently there is no changed-target test. The mutable roles are coefficients, bases and the bounded tail, and validation reads these from the actual rendered expressions. Each formal integral's outside coefficient and operand must recover an individual term in a separated stage; a full-integrand formal integral must recover the whole prompt integrand. Every row and primitive is independently checked on both x>0 and x<0. Reciprocal problems use x≠0, with separate arbitrary constants on connected intervals; all other integrands use real x.

Meaningful task identity ignores seed, bank and option order; content identity includes normalized prompt, solution, field shape, bank, correct designation and profile. Additive constants are equivalent indefinite integrals and never used as incorrect probes. The blank with outside C stores only the primitive fragment; q-31261's authored blank stores the whole primitive with C.
