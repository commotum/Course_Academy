$$
\begin{aligned}q\cdot r & ={q}_{x}\cdot {r}_{x}+{q}_{y}\cdot {r}_{y}+{q}_{z}\cdot {r}_{z}\end{aligned}

\begin{aligned}q\cdot r & =12\cdot (-4)+1\cdot 4+(-11)\cdot 3 \\  & =-48+4-33 \\  & =-77.\end{aligned}
$$
