- $\frac{4}{3}\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}+C$
- $\frac{3}{\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}}+C$
- $\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}+C$
- $\frac{3}{2\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}}+C$

Let $u={x}^{2}+12.$ Then

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=2x\,⟹\,\frac{1}{2}\text{d}u=x\,\text{d}x,
$$

and we can solve the resulting integral as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int \frac{3x}{\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}}\,\text{d}x & =3\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}}\cdot x\,\text{d}x \\  & =3\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{u}}\cdot \frac{1}{2}\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{3}{2}\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{u}}\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{3}{2}(2\sqrt{u})+C \\  & =3\sqrt{u}+C \\  & =3\sqrt{{x}^{2}+12}+C\end{aligned}
$$
