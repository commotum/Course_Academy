# Template extraction

## 1. Specific source descriptions

The classification table lists every source separately. Full decoded prompts, exact fields, original choices, correct answer IDs and worked solutions are in source.json. All bounds are rectangular. Moderate affine/polynomial families supply a constant norm; hard families require formal held-coordinate derivatives, the determinant, three signed minors, the resulting cross product, component squares and norm. q-167649 supplies previously obtained revolution parametrization and nonconstant norm in its solution; its isolated original prompt is context-dependent. ## 2. Shared pattern and assessed response

Evaluate the scalar surface integral by coordinate substitution, multiplying the pullback by the nonnegative area factor and integrating over the parameter rectangle. The response is an exact scalar, never a vector. Each family's cancellation, constant extraction, integration order and primitive conventions belong to its template. q-161242 specifically retains 2uv·10 = 5·4uv and the 2u/2v factor split. q-161185 retains both the initial iterated integral and its separation. The revolution family retains cos²θ+sin²θ, the radical cancellation and x⁸ primitive.

## 3. Components and banks

`DATA` maps every source ID to its actual coordinates, scalar function, rectangle, extracted constant, reduced integrand and integration mask kind. `source-cases.json` maps all thirteen originals to those parameters and public `observed:<source-id>` profiles. Every observed choice is owned by its original field in source.json; the replay profile constructs each scalar as its exact observed symbolic ratio to the independently computed anchor integral, multiplied by the newly computed integral. This is a parameterized evidence recipe, **not a recovered error interpretation**. All historical scalar distractor intentions remain unresolved. No observed profile is eligible for normal learning.

Normal method banks are separately authored complete five-option banks, selected before parameters and rejected in their entirety on any collision. They use actual omission of area factor; substitution of squared area factor in the integral; omission of a pullback coefficient; omission of each actual term; changing a difference of squares to a sum; missing the power-rule denominators; omitted common factor; or wrong sign after substitution. The monomial product family uses both omitted power-rule divisors (actual integrand ×4), not a generic doubling error. The symmetric linear-difference family uses omitted metric, omitted pullback factor 2, omitted surviving v-term, and reversed surviving sign. Every wrong option follows the displayed operation. No option-specific explanation is invented in learner content.

## 4. Reusable masks, constraints and calculations

The generator reads the frozen shared prose and fixed formal blocks and parameterizes complete bound, primitive and evaluated-row components in the final aligned family mask. It does not globally replace short numbers or symbols. All first five source integral stages remain operative. Changed endpoints recompute the inner definite integral, outer primitive and component evaluations. Normal solutions add both-endpoint component rows; these supplement rather than replace the source stages. An explicit input uses the same guards as random sampling. Noncharge endpoints are rational half-unit values, magnitude and interval width at most 3; balanced-product families retain zero lower limits, and source symmetric-inner families retain symmetry. No extra algebraic branch or integration method is introduced.

Normal easy parameters are integers 1..3: r=⟨au+bv,au−bv,cv⟩; bounds start at zero. The supplied metric is computed from the actual coordinates. Thus every easy task requires the same cancellation and one linear primitive. Moderate/hard geometry and integrands are source-fixed; varied evaluated endpoints are substantive changes to the assessment. Charge endpoints are 1/2, 1, 3/2 or 2; density multipliers are integers 1..8; a density multiplier 1..8 yields 32 supported charge tasks, a stated narrow family limitation rather than a claim of unlimited variety.

Raw evidence preserves it; normal output groups both negative components. Several sources use unescaped literal set braces in D; normal output supplies visible set-brace glyphs. Raw strings remain untouched in source.json. Raw replay retains original prompt, bank and full worked-solution byte strings at original parameters. Complete operative component slots preserve source J rows, rational primitive placement, endpoint arithmetic conventions and π-command spacing at changed inputs. Full source string equality is 13/13, independently checked. Normal endpoint detail is an explicit addition. Raw source mistakes are not counted as mathematically correct intermediate lines.

Historical banks are evidence only. Grading success for their designated scalar is not a claim that opaque distractor operations are recovered.

## Independent primitive-bound repair validation

Version v2 repairs wrapped primitive evaluation bounds such as `{[u²/2]}_0^{5/2}` and preserves labelled inner bounds such as `u=-3/2`. The validator reads every bounded primitive directly from the displayed solution with balanced TeX-group parsing, including fraction-valued endpoints and optional closing wrappers, and compares both endpoints and any coordinate labels with the independently parsed prompt/domain. This applies to nested inner/outer primitives, separated products, the shell blank and authored easy tasks. Source-context shell evidence uses its displayed preceding domain in the solution.

Each separated primitive is differentiated and compared with its own actual printed integral factor and differential. Equality of the derivative product is insufficient. New regressions reject a stale q-161185 upper primitive bound (2 changed to 1) and reciprocal scaling of its two primitives, even though the derivative product and final integral can stay equal. Explicit changed-bound cases cover ub=5/2 and labelled symmetric inner endpoints ±3/2. All thirteen original source solution strings must remain byte-exact.
