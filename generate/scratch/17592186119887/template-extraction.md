## 2. Generalized mathematical backbone

For the six vector families, multiply the original complete coordinate functions by positive integer coefficients `(a,b,c)`. Derive both three-coordinate partial vectors with the other independent variable held fixed, then compute `r_u × r_v`, in that order. Preserve the source's formal coordinate partial row, substituted complete-coordinate row, evaluated partial row, 3×3 determinant, signed 2×2 cofactor vector, and the source-specific final vector. q-105607 is absent-source authoring based on the canonical example; it also displays unsimplified determinant products and the outside negative middle-cofactor sign before collection.

For the existing norm family the surface scales are `(a,b,b)`, preserving circular radial coefficients. Its supplied product is `<8b²x³,-2abx² cos θ,-2abx² sin θ>`. Compute its scalar Euclidean norm. The norm stages preserve source squaring, expanded squares, trigonometric common factor, identity reduction, and collected square-root result `2b x² sqrt(16b² x²+a²)`. Positive b and real x make the outside `x²` nonnegative even for negative x. Source raw replay retains all prior full-solution stages; normal output retains only the norm stages because the product was already found in the previous step.

## 3. Prompt, choices, solutions and bank mappings

Each source bank is a complete profile, selected before parameters. A source vector option `<P,Q,R>` maps to `<bcP,acQ,abR>` under diagonal coordinate scaling. This is an explicit covariance/presentation recipe, reproducing every original mathematical object and spelling at `(1,1,1)`; many historic wrong expressions underdetermine the original error intent, so these profiles remain `evidence_only=true, eligible_for_learning=false`. This is not a claim that tangent-like options were intended to transform as cross products. Complete individual expressions and absence maps are in `source-components.json`. No arbitrary fit or source-only literal payload branch occurs: every option is a complete scalar-slot template under the public generate API, and changed coefficients are tested. All original banks, including unkeyed/duplicate banks, are retained.

Normal radio questions choose the separately authored complete `method:ordered-cross` bank before parameters. Its five recipes are: `r_u×r_v`, `r_v×r_u`, omit the outside negative sign of the middle cofactor only, `r_u`, and `r_v`. The last two represent stopping after finding one tangent instead of the product. No first-valid pool or per-member replacement is used. Every pair of complete vectors, including incorrect–incorrect pairs, must differ; failure rejects the whole instance. Learner output contains no inferred error diagnoses absent from the sources.

The scalar blank has no distractors. It preserves the expression-only fragment and field-1 placeholder. Validation compares scalar norm expressions under a real domain, rather than independently comparing vector components. Native equivalent-norm checks cover uncollected and reordered square-root forms plus wrong scalar zero.
