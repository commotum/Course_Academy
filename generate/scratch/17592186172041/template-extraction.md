Ordinary powers are 1 and hard uses exactly one power 2. A square is strictly positive away from its zero line; the unsquared factor determines the sign in hard. Response is one of five text subset options. This uses two-variable sign inequalities and the interpretation of derivative sign as slope sign, within the KP.

## 4. Reusable templates and constraints

Fixed learner prose is the original standard prompt/solution sentences. Variable full factors encode h,k,o_x,o_y,p_x,p_y; statement slots contain three distinct open regions and claimed signs. Optional conclusion is preserved per observed profile. Numbered q-96475 preserves its raw spaces, number-form layout and fraction notation. All normal APIs accept fixed parameters without silent repairs or resampling; random sampling rejects a complete ineligible source bank and samples a fresh whole instance.

Default authored whole bank `authored_truth_flips` is the computed truth vector T, flip only T₁, flip only T₂, flip only T₃, and complement all three. Each operation produces a full subset answer. These 5 vectors are provably different in 3-bit space. The three single-claim mistakes and all-claim reversal are defensible operations on the assessed evaluation, not scalar offsets or invented source author intent. No learner-facing distractor diagnosis is added. One worked solution is independent of the selected answer.

Limits enforce nonzero integer roots of magnitude ≤3, exactly two factors, no autonomous reduction, no extra roots, powers or nonlinear zero curves. Easy ordinary unit-shift factors only. Moderate two linear factors, at least one shift magnitude≥2. Hard exactly one named squared factor, its orientation normalized positive, ordinary other factor may be reversed. Invalid explicit parameters fail rather than being narrowed silently.

Meaningful task diversity is finite, not cosmetic unbounded coefficient sampling. At each fixed pair of roots there are 4 choices of 3 distinct regions and 8 claim-sign patterns = 32 unordered substantive claim sets. Easy has 1 sign-grid pattern, moderate 2 product sign-grid patterns, hard 4 (square x or y and sign of the unsquared factor). With sampled root locations: easy 4×32=128; moderate 32×2×32=2048; hard 36×4×32=4608 canonical tasks. Option and statement permutations, factor-orientation spellings that yield the same grid, source profiles and scalar magnitudes do not inflate these counts. Threshold location variation is stated separately from sign-grid/claim combinatorics. No coefficient magnitudes are sampled. Canonical task fingerprints sort claims and use roots plus full sign grid; content identities use version, full prompt, fixed solution and whole displayed bank. This preserves seed/order invariance while separating changed displayed banks.

Fidelity and correctness are separate: all 7 exact prompts, all 7 complete five-choice banks / 35 values, all 6 available correct designations, all 6 complete available worked solutions. One derived designation and authored solution are neither exact nor matched source fields. Native grading validates every presented option ID, while independent SymPy checks read the actual printed equation, zero lines, region inequalities, all three worked verdicts/sign equations/sign sentences and rendered signs.
