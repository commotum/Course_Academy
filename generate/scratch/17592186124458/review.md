where $u$ is a unit vector in the direction of $v.$

First, we find the unit vector $u$ in the direction of $v:$

$$
\begin{aligned}‖v‖ & =\sqrt{(3)^{2}+(3)^{2}}=3 \sqrt{2} \\ u & =\frac{1}{3 \sqrt{2}}(3\,i+3\,j)\end{aligned}
$$

Now, we compute the gradient $∇f(x,y)$ and evaluate it at the point $(0,3):$

$$
\begin{aligned}∇f(x,y) & =⟨\frac{∂}{∂x}(2 x^{2} + y),\frac{∂}{∂y}(2 x^{2} + y)⟩ \\  & =⟨4 x,1⟩ \\ ∇f(0,3) & =⟨4 (0),1⟩ \\  & =⟨0,1⟩\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the directional derivative is

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}_{u}^{′}(0,3) & =∇f(0,3)\cdot u \\  & =⟨0,1⟩\cdot ⟨\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}⟩ \\  & =\frac{(0)\cdot (3)+(1)\cdot (3)}{3 \sqrt{2}} \\  & =\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.\end{aligned}
$$
