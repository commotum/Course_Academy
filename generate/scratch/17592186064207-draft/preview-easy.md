Calculate $\int \frac{1}{3x+7}\,\text{d}x.$ Assume that $K$ is an arbitrary positive constant.

- $\operatorname{ln}(K|3x+7|)$
- $\frac{1}{7}\operatorname{ln}(K|3x+7|)$
- $-\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}(\frac{|3x+7|}{K})$
- $\frac{3}{7}\operatorname{ln}(K|3x+7|)$
- $\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}(K|3x+7|)$

First, let $u=3x+7.$ Then differentiating with respect to $x$ gives

$$
\frac{\text{d}u}{\text{d}x}=3\,⟹\,\text{d}x=\frac{1}{3}\text{d}u.
$$

We can now rewrite the integral in terms of $u,$ solve it, and write the final answer in terms of $x$ as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int \frac{1}{3x+7}\,\text{d}x & =\int \frac{1}{u}\cdot \frac{1}{3}\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\int \frac{1}{u}\,\text{d}u \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡|u|+C \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡|3x+7|+C\end{aligned}
$$

We can further simplify the above by writing $C=\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡K$ for another arbitrary constant $K,$ and then we can combine the two terms using the laws of logarithms, as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡|3x+7|+C & =\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡|3x+7|+\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡K \\  & =\frac{1}{3}(\operatorname{ln}⁡|3x+7|+\operatorname{ln}⁡K) \\  & =\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡(K|3x+7|)\end{aligned}
$$

So, we finally conclude that

$$
\int \frac{1}{3x+7}\,\text{d}x=\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{ln}⁡(K|3x+7|).
$$
