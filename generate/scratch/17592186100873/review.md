P=\begin{bmatrix}1 & -2 \\ 3 & -4 \\ 4 & -6 \\ 1 & -8 \\ 3 & -9\end{bmatrix},\,Q=\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & 2 & -1 & 10 \\ 2 & 0 & 5 & -2 & 7\end{bmatrix}?
$$

Multiplying the $2$nd row of $P$ by the $5$th column of $Q$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{r}_{25} & =\begin{bmatrix}1 & -2 \\ 3 & -4 \\ 4 & -6 \\ 1 & -8 \\ 3 & -9\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & 2 & -1 & 10 \\ 2 & 0 & 5 & -2 & 7\end{bmatrix} \\  & =\begin{bmatrix}3 & -4\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}10 \\ 7\end{bmatrix} \\  & =3\cdot 10+(-4)\cdot 7 \\  & =30-28 \\  & =2.\end{aligned}
$$
