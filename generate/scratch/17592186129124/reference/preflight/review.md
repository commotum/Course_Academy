## range_cos_linear — :question.difficulty/easy

What is the range of the function $y=4\cos(2x-3)-6$?

- $y\in [2,10]$
- $y\in [-10,-2]$ **correct**
- $y\in [-14,2]$
- $y\in [-7,-5]$
- $y\in [-4,4]$

The range of $\cos x$ is

$$
-1\le \cos x\le 1.
$$

Horizontal shifts and stretches have no effect on the range. Therefore,

$$
-1\le \cos(2x-3)\le 1.
$$

Multiplying the above inequality by $4$ and then subtracting $6$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned} & -1\le \cos(2x-3)\le 1 \\  & -4\le 4\cos(2x-3)\le 4 \\  & -10\le 4\cos(2x-3)-6\le -2 \\  & -10\le y\le -2.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the range is $y\in [-10,-2].$
