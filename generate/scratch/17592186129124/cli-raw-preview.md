## source_q-25512 — :question.difficulty/moderate

Find the range of the function $f(x)=-2\operatorname{sin}(3x)+1.$

*Original fields absent; evidence only.*

The range of $\operatorname{sin}x$ is

$$
-1\le \operatorname{sin}x\le 1.
$$

Horizontal shifts and stretches have no effect on the range. Therefore,

$$
-1\le \operatorname{sin}(3x)\le 1.
$$

Multiplying the above inequality by $-2$ and then adding $1$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned} & -1\le \operatorname{sin}(3x)\le 1 \\  & 2\ge -2\operatorname{sin}(2x)\ge -2 \\  & 3\ge -2\operatorname{sin}(2x)+1\ge -1 \\  & -1\le f(x)\le 3.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the range is $f(x)\in [-1,3].$
