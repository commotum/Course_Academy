### easy / directed / normalization
The vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$ has magnitude $30$. Which of the following is a unit vector that is parallel to $\overrightarrow{AB}$?

A. $\overrightarrow{AB}$
B. $\frac{1}{30}\overrightarrow{AB}$
C. $\frac{1}{30}$
D. $30\overrightarrow{AB}$
E. $\frac{1}{900}\overrightarrow{AB}$

Correct: B

To obtain the unit vector that is parallel to $\overrightarrow{AB}$, we divide $\overrightarrow{AB}$ by its length:

$$
\mathbf{u}=\dfrac{\overrightarrow{AB}}{\mid \,\overrightarrow{AB}\, \mid}=\dfrac{1}{30}\overrightarrow{AB}
$$

A. $- 5 \sqrt{19}\overrightarrow{AB}$
B. $5 \sqrt{19}\overrightarrow{AB}$
C. $-\overrightarrow{AB}$
D. $\overrightarrow{AB}$
E. $\frac{\sqrt{19}}{95}\overrightarrow{AB}$

Correct: E

To obtain the unit vector that is parallel to $\overrightarrow{AB}$, we divide $\overrightarrow{AB}$ by its length:

$$
\mathbf{u}=\dfrac{\overrightarrow{AB}}{\mid \,\overrightarrow{AB}\, \mid}=\dfrac{1}{5 \sqrt{19}}\overrightarrow{AB}=\frac{\sqrt{19}}{95}\overrightarrow{AB}
$$
