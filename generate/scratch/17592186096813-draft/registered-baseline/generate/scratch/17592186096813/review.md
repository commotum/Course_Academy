## Authored easy / named / normalization / {'m': 2}

### easy / named / normalization
The vector $\mathbf{a}$ has magnitude $2$. Find a unit vector that is parallel to $\mathbf{a}$.

A. $\mathbf{a}$
B. $\frac{1}{4}\mathbf{a}$
C. $\frac{1}{2}$
D. $2\mathbf{a}$
E. $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}$

Correct: E

To obtain the unit vector that is parallel to $\mathbf{a}$, we divide $\mathbf{a}$ by its length:

$$
\mathbf{u}=\dfrac{\mathbf{a}}{\mid \,\mathbf{a}\, \mid}=\dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}
$$
