# Raster review

All 12 practice source rasters and the canonical example were opened individually at original resolution; source-contact.png preserves the overview. Positive and negative intercept labels match the extracted radii for every source. The source plot-scale audit fits each image separately from its blue extremum bounds and its four numeric ticks; no assumption of common scaling across images was made. q-69890's nearly circular appearance corresponds to unequal axis-unit scales and is preserved in exact replay.

The generated easy, moderate and hard contact sheets were inspected for every review record. Ellipses remain centered and wide (a>b), with equal horizontal/vertical unit scaling, the expected extrema and explicit signed decimal/integer tick labels. Near-circular wide ellipses such as 4.5 by 4 and 4.25 by 3.75 are legitimate labeled wide cases. Axis arrowheads and x/y labels remain inside the canvas and away from vertex labels. No point labels are clipped, no radii are inferred from unlabelled fractions, and there are no plotted answer options requiring separate geometry. The fresh concurrent same/different render rasters were also independently tested by pixel extent ratio against their rational radii. This review is backed by full-raster geometry checks in validate.py rather than sidecar assertions alone.
