- $52$

$$
\begin{aligned}a\cdot b & ={a}_{x}\cdot {b}_{x}+{a}_{y}\cdot {b}_{y}+{a}_{z}\cdot {b}_{z} \\  & =8\cdot 3+4\cdot 7+10\cdot (-8) \\  & =24+28-80 \\  & =-28\end{aligned}
