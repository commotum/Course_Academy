\frac{1}{13}(\sech 13 - \sech 2)
13(\sech 2 - \sech 13)
\frac{1}{13}(\tanh 2 - \tanh 13)
\frac{1}{13}(\tanh^{2} 26)
\frac{1}{13}(\sech 13 - \sech 26)

Let $u=13x$. Then $\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{13}\,\mathrm{d}u$. When $x=1$, $u=13$; when $x=2$, $u=26$.

Using $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}u}\sech u=-\sech u\tanh u$,

$$\begin{aligned}
\int_1^2\sech(13x)\tanh(13x)\,\mathrm{d}x
&=\frac{1}{13}\int_{13}^{26}\sech u\tanh u\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&=\frac{1}{13}[-\sech u]_{13}^{26} \\
&=\frac{1}{13}(\sech 13-\sech 26).
\end{aligned}$$

The integrand is continuous on $[1,2]$, and the result is positive because $\sech u$ decreases for $u>0$.
