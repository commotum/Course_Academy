Let $P(-6, 6)$ and $Q(9, 9)$. Find $|\overrightarrow{PQ}|$.

We have

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{OP} &= p = - 6 i + 6 j, \\
\overrightarrow{OQ} &= q = 9 i + 9 j.
\end{aligned}
$$

Expressing $\overrightarrow{PQ}$ as the difference of position vectors, we obtain:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{PQ} &= \overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP} \\
&= q - p \\
&= (9 i + 9 j) - (- 6 i + 6 j) \\
&= 15 i + 3 j
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, using the formula for the magnitude of a vector, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
|\overrightarrow{PQ}| &= \sqrt{(15)^{2} + (3)^{2}} \\
&= \sqrt{225 + 9} \\
&= \sqrt{234} \\
&= 3 \sqrt{26}
\end{aligned}
$$

Choices: $9 \sqrt{3}$, $3 \sqrt{23}$, $24$, $15$, $3 \sqrt{26}$
