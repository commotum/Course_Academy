What is $\displaystyle \int\sinh (4x - 3)dx$?

- $\frac{1}{3}\cosh (4x + 3) + C$
- $\frac{1}{2}\cosh (4x - 3) + C$
- $-\frac{1}{4}\cosh (4x - 3) + C$
- $\frac{1}{4}\cosh (4x - 3) + C$
- $\cosh (4x - 3) + C$

Let $u=4x - 3$. Then $du=4\,dx$, so $dx=\frac{1}{4}\,du$.

$$
\int\sinh (4x - 3)dx = \frac{1}{4}\int\sinh u\,du = \frac{1}{4}\cosh u + C = \frac{1}{4}\cosh (4x - 3) + C
$$

## Source replay q-95533

Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle \int_{1/2}^{1}\cosh (\frac{2x - 1}{3})dx$.

- $\frac{2}{3}\sinh (e^{2} + 1)$
- $\frac{3}{2}\sinh (\frac{1}{3})$
- $\frac{2}{3}\sinh (\frac{3}{2})$
- $-\frac{2}{3}\sinh (e^{2} - 1)$
- $\frac{1}{3}\sinh (\frac{1}{3})$

Let $u=\frac{2x-1}{3}$. Then $du=\frac{2}{3}\,dx$, so $dx=\frac{3}{2}\,du$. The bounds $x=\frac{1}{2}$ and $x=1$ become $u=0$ and $u=\frac{1}{3}$.

$$
\frac{3}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{3}}\cosh u\,du = \frac{3}{2}[\sinh u]_{0}^{\frac{1}{3}} = \frac{3}{2}(\sinh (\frac{1}{3})-\sinh 0) = \frac{3}{2}\sinh (\frac{1}{3})
$$

- $\frac{9}{10}\sinh (e^{9} + 1)$

Evaluate the integral $\displaystyle \int_{1/2}^{1}\cosh (\frac{2x - 1}{8})dx$.

- $\frac{7}{8}\sinh (e^{7} + 1)$
- $\frac{8}{2}\sinh (\frac{1}{8})$
- $\frac{7}{8}\sinh (\frac{8}{7})$
- $-\frac{7}{8}\sinh (e^{7} - 1)$
- $\frac{1}{8}\sinh (\frac{1}{8})$

Let $u=\frac{2x-1}{8}$. Then $du=\frac{2}{8}\,dx$, so $dx=\frac{8}{2}\,du$. The bounds $x=\frac{1}{2}$ and $x=1$ become $u=0$ and $u=\frac{1}{8}$.

$$
\frac{8}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{8}}\cosh u\,du = \frac{8}{2}[\sinh u]_{0}^{\frac{1}{8}} = \frac{8}{2}(\sinh (\frac{1}{8})-\sinh 0) = \frac{8}{2}\sinh (\frac{1}{8})
$$
