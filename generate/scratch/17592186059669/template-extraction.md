# Parameter and bank extraction

Every individual source's exact prompt, point, transformations, field layout, choices and ordered solution is retained in source.json. review.md pairs all seven complete sources with original-parameter replays and changed-slot cases. choice-mapping.json maps every option of every supported source to its field, original answer UUID/value and full coordinate recipe; all 35 choices must replay exactly, including the seven keys. Every source profile remains separate. Opaque value recipes preserve mathematical patterns without claiming recovered misconception intent. No recipe is presented as learner feedback. The two incomplete unrated banks are retained literally in source.json and explicitly excluded: unsupported banks are not replaced by plausible distractors.

The supplied point (x,y), supplied curve, and requested curve are fixed in role. Every supported template asks for a guaranteed corresponding point, never another operation. The five-choice radio response is a coordinate pair. Method 1 always undoes the source transformation to f, reflects across the x axis, then shifts vertically; Method 2 infers f(u)=w and substitutes into the requested curve. The final point agrees in both methods. Shared operative sentences and the order of mathematical stages are preserved; headings, punctuation and connective wording are normalized.

Vertical shift: supplied y=f(x)+h; given point (x,y) gives u=x, w=y-h; requested y=-f(x)+k gives (u,k-w). h and k are nonzero signed shifts. Horizontal shift: supplied y=f(x+h); u=x+h, w=y; requested curve and final formula same. Vertical scale: supplied y=a f(x), a>0 and a!=1; u=x, w=y/a; requested curve and final formula same. Positive a preserves the three-stage workload. Nonzero h/k keeps the operative shifts; signs vary and direction/unit grammar follows them.

Normal finite slots: x,y in -8..8; signed h,k in {-5,-4,-3,-2,-1,1,2,3,4,5}; hard a in {1/2,1/3,1/4,2,3,4,5}. All observed parameters are replayable, including y=8, negative h, reciprocal scale, zero intermediate f value and zero final answer. Extending the observed integer point/shift values within these bounds is a conservative same-method policy. Rational arithmetic is exact. Banks that coincide, duplicate the correct value or contain duplicate choices are rejected and resampled. The domain is finite; the generator never pads a shortage with changed seeds or choice order.

The script's CASES section records source parameters and each bank's full expression pattern. At original parameters: q-119308 uses x=2,y=3,h=-1,k=4 and five offsets around c=0; q-121407 uses x=-3,y=4,h=1,k=2 with u=-2,c=-2 and an opaque mixed-coordinate bank; q-26408 uses x=-1,y=3,h=1,k=2; q-26596 uses x=3,y=2,h=-2,k=2; q-26726 uses x=4,y=2,h=2,k=4; q-121414 uses x=4,y=8,a=4,k=3; q-121418 uses x=2,y=3,a=1/3,k=5. The latter's -2c,2c,c/2 bank is an observed value-pattern extension with unresolved error intent. Profiles are selected before recipes and collisions reject the whole bank.

No errors were found in supported source correct points or stage mathematics. Source difficulty labels are retained even where difficulty order is not monotonic (fieldless hard q-158542); coefficient size does not justify changing them. Unclassified incomplete sources are never silently repaired.

UUIDv5 questions derive from the KP UUID, frozen version, full normalized supplied point/curve/target fingerprint and bank profile. Field UUIDs are owned by question; answers are owned by field and mathematical value. The inventory deduplication fingerprint ignores seed, choice order, UUID and bank profile. It compares full exact mathematical tasks across all source and generated questions, including incomplete sources and the canonical example.
