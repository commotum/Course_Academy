Let $C$ be a positively oriented, piecewise-smooth, simple closed curve in the plane, and let $D$ be the region bounded by $C.$ If $F=P\,i+Q\,j,$ and $P$ and $Q$ have continuous first derivatives on an open region containing $D,$ then the flux form of Green's theorem states that

$$
{∮}_{C}F\cdot n\,\text{d}s={∬}_{D}\text{div}\,F\,\text{d}A,
$$

which is equivalent to

$$
{∮}_{C}P\,dy-Q\,dx={∬}_{D}(\frac{∂P}{∂x}+\frac{∂Q}{∂y})\,\text{d}A.
$$

The region $D$ and the curve $C$ for this case are shown below.

![](images/f6/f6437eddec7d44a10c7fb2b3461a09844ca06e5131b69503f51432eb87fedd31.png)

We have that $P(x,y)=4 x^{2} y + 2 e^{y}$ and $Q(x,y)=4 x y^{2} + 2 e^{x}.$ So,

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}=8 x y,\qquad\frac{\partial Q}{\partial y}=8 x y.
$$

We can express the rectangular region $D$ as follows:

$$
D=\{(x,y):-1\le x\le 2,\,0\le y\le 1\}.
$$

Therefore, applying Green's theorem in flux form, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}\oint_C F\cdot n\,\mathrm{d}s &=\oint_C P\,\mathrm{d}y-Q\,\mathrm{d}x \\ &=\iint_D\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial Q}{\partial y}\right)\,\mathrm{d}A \\ &=\int_{0}^{1}\int_{-1}^{2}\left(16 x y\right)\,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y \\ &=\int_{0}^{1}\left[8 x^{2} y\right]_{x=-1}^{x=2}\,\mathrm{d}y \\ &=\int_{0}^{1}\left(24 y\right)\,\mathrm{d}y \\ &=\left[12 y^{2}\right]_{0}^{1} \\ &=12.\end{aligned}
$$
