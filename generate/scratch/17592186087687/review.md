To find the slope of the line $6x - 4y = 9$, we convert to slope-intercept form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
6x - 4y &= 9 \\
-4y &= -6x + 9 \\
y &= \frac{3}{2}x - \frac{9}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

In slope-intercept form, the slope is the $x$-coefficient. So, the slope is $\frac{3}{2}$.

For two lines to be parallel, they must have the same slope.

To find the slope of the line $-11x + 3y = 3$, we convert to slope-intercept form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
-11x + 3y &= 3 \\
3y &= 11x + 3 \\
y &= \frac{11}{3}x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

In slope-intercept form, the slope is the $x$-coefficient. So, the slope is $\frac{11}{3}$.

y = \frac{11}{3}x + 2
