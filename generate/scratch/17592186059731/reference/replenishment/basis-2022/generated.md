$$
\begin{aligned}c\cdot d & ={c}_{x}\cdot {d}_{x}+{c}_{y}\cdot {d}_{y}+{c}_{z}\cdot {d}_{z} \\  & =k\cdot (-3)+3k\cdot 2+(-4)\cdot 2 \\  & =-3k+6k-8 \\  & =3k-8.\end{aligned}
$$

Setting the dot product equal to $0,$ we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}c\cdot d & =0 \\ 3k-8 & =0 \\ k & =\frac{8}{3}.\end{aligned}
$$
