$$
\begin{aligned}\overset{\to }{PQ} & =\overset{\to }{OQ}-\overset{\to }{OP} \\  & =q-p \\  & =(-2i+j)-(i+2j) \\  & =-3i-j\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}|\overset{\to }{PQ}| & =\sqrt{(-3)^{2}+(-1)^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{9+1} \\  & =\sqrt{10}.\end{aligned}
