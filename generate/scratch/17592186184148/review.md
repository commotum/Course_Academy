The graph above shows the function $y=\operatorname{cot}⁡(Bx)+D,$ where $B$ and $D$ are constants. What is $B-2D?$

The period $T$ of the function is

$$
T=\frac{\pi }{5}.
$$

The formula for the period $T$ of the function $y=A\operatorname{cot}⁡(Bx+C)+D$ is given by 

$$
T=\frac{\pi }{B}.
$$

Applying the formula of the period, we get

$$
\frac{\pi }{5}=\frac{\pi }{B}\,⟹\,B=\frac{\pi }{(\frac{\pi }{5})}=5.
$$

The parameter $D$ represents the vertical shift of the cotangent function. We see that the function has been translated by $\frac{3}{2}$ units up. Hence, $D=\frac{3}{2}.$

Therefore, $B-2D=5-2\cdot (\frac{3}{2})=5-3=2.$

Finally, the equation of the curve is

$$
y=\operatorname{cot}⁡(5x)+\frac{3}{2}.
$$
