Calculate $det(A)$ given that
$A=[\begin{aligned}\frac{5}{8} & \frac{9}{5} \\ -\frac{2}{9} & -\frac{9}{7}\end{aligned}].$

$det(A)=$
{{field-1}}

- $-\frac{113}{280}$

For a $2\times 2$ matrix, the determinant is given by the formula

$$
|\begin{aligned}a & b \\ c & d\end{aligned}|=ad-bc.
$$

Using the formula, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\text{det}(A) & =|\begin{aligned}\frac{5}{8} & \frac{9}{5} \\ -\frac{2}{9} & -\frac{9}{7}\end{aligned}| \\  & =\frac{5}{8}\cdot (-\frac{9}{7})-\frac{9}{5}\cdot (-\frac{2}{9}) \\  & =-\frac{45}{56}-(-\frac{2}{5}) \\  & =-\frac{113}{280}.\end{aligned}
$$
