Find the slope ${m}^{'}$ of the normal line to the curve $y={x}^{2}+3x$ at the point $(-1,-2).$

${m}^{'}=$
{{field-1}}

$$
{f}^{'}(x)=\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+3x)=2x+3
$$

$$

$$
