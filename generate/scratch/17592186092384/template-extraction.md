`source.json` preserves each original prompt, field key and type, exact choices, correct designation, notation and fixed solution. `templates.json` describes every original specifically, records curve, exponents, signs, coefficient exactness, point, derivative, both slopes and every field-owned choice UUID, original value, exact mathematical value and recipe. Each complete rated source remains a separate template/profile. `review.md` places original-parameter replays and scales 2 and 12 side by side.

The shared move is to differentiate the curve, evaluate the tangent slope at the given point, then take its negative reciprocal. Generated blanks preserve field-1 and the scalar slope answer fragment; radio fields preserve selection and five options. Generated questions have one fixed solution, no added distractor feedback and no new plot assets. Literal instructional sentences and three derivative/evaluation/negative-reciprocal display blocks follow each source. At scale 1 the complete prompt and solution are byte-for-byte source replays. Extended variants recompute the display mathematics; TeX expression normalization is recorded separately from exact source replay fidelity.

The sole bounded sampling extension multiplies every coefficient of a source curve by an integer s in 1..12, preserving its source abscissa and recomputing the point ordinate. Original signs and fractional or negative exponents remain. Positive abscissas are required for fractional powers; nonzero abscissas for negative integer powers; all selected slopes are finite and nonzero. Zero tangents would give a vertical normal with no finite scalar slope and are excluded. No new field layout, arbitrary difficulty or independent coefficient range is inferred.

Recipes use m=f_s'(p), source abscissa p and scale s. Full banks are selected before rendering. The unusual q-103531 (-5/2)s pattern and its reciprocals are retained as observed algebraic patterns, with pedagogical interpretation unresolved. q-71524 m/(5s) and -2s are likewise observed parameterized patterns, not claimed learner misconceptions. No literals are copied as variable recipes without recording them: fixed ±1 options are explicit. Every eligible scale reproduces a complete, unique bank with exactly one mathematically correct choice. The complete 30/30 source options and 10/10 banks replay at s=1; there are no unsupported partial banks included in those totals.

The bounded catalog is at most 120 tasks before existing-source/canonical exclusions. Distinctness uses the exact curve and abscissa within the specified real domains, ignoring UUID, seed, profile or choice order. Fractional powers normalize only under the explicit positive-x restriction. Choice banks are not an excuse to duplicate the same mathematical question.

No Plot Extraction or Plot Generator is needed: all source mathematics is standalone TeX.
