## q-102054

Original

Which of the following graphs shows the line $y=-2x?$

We need to find two distinct points that lie on our line. Any two points will do!

- For the first point, we will let the $x$-coordinate be $x=0.$ Then the $y$-coordinate is 

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =-2x \\  & =-2(0) \\  & =0.\end{aligned}
$$

So, the first point is $(0,0).$

- For the second point, we will let the $x$-coordinate be $x=1.$ Then the $y$-coordinate is

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =-2x \\  & =-2(1) \\  & =-2.\end{aligned}
$$

So, the second point is $(1,-2).$

Finally, we plot those two points on the $xy$-plane and draw a line through them. This is the graph of $y=-2x.$
