# Extracted templates and bounds

The backbone is visual interpretation of work, followed by sign reversal. The learner sees a nonconstant planar force field and a blue path with endpoints A and B, judges three propositions about work on C or -C, and responds through the source field layout. No integration or numeric calculation is added to the learner task.

The source-specific description, exact prompts and full ordered solution text are retained in `source.json`; `source-mapping.json` enumerates every source field and option. Replays preserve these verbatim. `reference/review.md` places source instances and generated examples in one review artifact.

Easy tangent ellipse/circle: observe whether arrows work with or against the path and reverse that conclusion for -C. Examples q-125381, q-125507, q-125499, q-125497. Normal sampling allows half or three-quarter arcs; the latter is a conservative generalization of the observed three-quarter circle to an ellipse. Two positive axis lengths are geometric slots, not new operations. Moderate normal ellipse/circle: the force is perpendicular everywhere, hence zero work on both orientations. Examples q-125500, q-125501, q-125504, q-125502. Normal sampling allows quarter or half arcs. Quarter ellipses generalize observed quarter circles without changing the perpendicularity argument.

General curve opposing motion (q-125688, q-125687), general curve assisting motion (q-150013), and perpendicular straight segment (q-125380, q-150018) have exact source replay only. Their sparse plots do not determine unique analytic fields; this implementation makes no sampler claim for them.

For generated ellipses, let r=(a cos theta,b sin theta), theta=pi(start+direction*t)/2, 0<=t<=sweep. Axes (a,b) are in {(1,1),(2,1),(1,2),(3,2),(2,3)}, start in {0,1,2,3}, direction in {-1,1}, sweep in {2,3} easy or {1,2} moderate, force_sign in {-1,1}. The generated tangent field is force_sign*(-a²*y,b²*x); the generated normal field is force_sign*(b²*x,a²*y). The fields are smooth and nonconstant on R², the path is regular and nondegenerate, and neither field vanishes on it. These are exact analytic realizations of the observed relationships, not inferred original formulas.

The tangent dot product has sign direction*force_sign everywhere; the normal dot product vanishes identically. Endpoint labels, path, field, forward/reverse arrows, worked-solution sign and all correct values derive from this one parameter object. The actual solution retains the source stages: meaning of work; helping/opposing rules; judge I and II with forward diagram; judge III with reverse diagram; state true subset. Normal generated wording is concrete and independent of learner selection.

Profiles specify the three complete sign assertions. No pooled distractor recipes are used. Checkbox fields each own exactly text false and true, keyed by their statement. Radio choices use all five observed finite subsets and require exactly one correct subset. The source-specific profile mapping reproduces all 75 values, 42 distractors and 13 entire banks; all original source values remain unchanged. Nothing in the source suggests extra option-specific feedback.

Distinctness is deliberately coarser than parameter equality: circle versus noncircle ellipse, arc sweep and work alignment (easy only) define a task. Rotations, positive scale, exact noncircular aspect ratio, uniform force reversal of zero-work diagrams, seed, field layout, profiles, and option identities cannot inflate inventory. This bounded analytic sampler therefore has only eight easy and four moderate task classes, with overlap against the existing sources excluded. Replay-only source classes are separately identifiable. These are capability bounds of this implementation, not a theorem that no other questions exist.
