## q-49521 — original

What is $\displaystyle \int(\frac{1}{3 + x} + \frac{1}{3 - x})dx$?

- $-\frac{1}{3}\ln (K \mid (3 + x)(3 - x) \mid)$
- $3\ln (K \mid (3 + x)(3 - x) \mid)$
- $-3\ln (K \mid \frac{3 + x}{3 - x} \mid)$
- $\frac{1}{3}\ln (K \mid \frac{3 + x}{3 - x} \mid)$
- $\ln (K \mid \frac{3 + x}{3 - x} \mid)$

Let's solve this using the general formula:

$$
\begin{aligned}\int (\frac{1}{3 + x}+\frac{1}{3 - x})\,\text{d}x & =\int \frac{1}{3 + x}\,\text{d}x+\int \frac{1}{3 - x}\,\text{d}x \\  & =\operatorname{ln}⁡|3 + x|-\operatorname{ln}⁡|3 - x|+C \\  & =\operatorname{ln}⁡|\frac{3 + x}{3 - x}|+\operatorname{ln}⁡K \\  & =\operatorname{ln}⁡(K|\frac{3 + x}{3 - x}|)\end{aligned}
$$

Notice that we used the laws of logarithms to write the result as a single logarithm.
