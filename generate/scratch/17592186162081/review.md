![](images/b7/b7e72ce1ab38408f04f3b8d5a228ec0a3632c60086ef0a7abeb7e18af3002e6b.png)

Calculate ${\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s,$ where $C$ is the line segment shown above.

- $3\sqrt{5}$
- $3$
- $5$
- $4\sqrt{5}$
- $4$

The line integral of unity with respect to arc length over $C$ is equal to the arc length $L$ of $C,$ i.e.,

$$
{\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s=L.
$$

In this case, the path $C$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs have lengths $3$ and $4.$ Therefore, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}L & =\sqrt{{3}^{2}+{4}^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{9+16} \\  & =\sqrt{25} \\  & =5.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
{\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s=5.
$$

Calculate ${\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s,$ where $C$ is the line segment shown above.

- $\frac{14}{3}$
- $\frac{\sqrt{11785}}{12}$
- $\frac{31}{4}$
- $\frac{149}{12}$
- $\frac{11785}{144}$

The line integral of unity with respect to arc length over $C$ is equal to the arc length $L$ of $C,$ i.e.,

$$
{\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s=L.
$$

In this case, the path $C$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs have lengths $\frac{31}{4}$ and $\frac{14}{3}.$ Therefore, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}L & =\sqrt{{\frac{31}{4}}^{2}+{\frac{14}{3}}^{2}} \\  & =\sqrt{\frac{961}{16}+\frac{196}{9}} \\  & =\sqrt{\frac{11785}{144}} \\  & =\frac{\sqrt{11785}}{12}.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore,

$$
{\int }_{C}\,\text{d}s=\frac{\sqrt{11785}}{12}.
$$
