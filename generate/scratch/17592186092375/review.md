$x={t}^{3}+{t}^{2},\,y={t}^{3}-{t}^{2}$
from $t=2$ to $t=3$ ?

- $2{\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{9{t}^{2}+2t+4}\,\text{d}t$
- ${\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{9{t}^{2}+4}\,\text{d}t$
- ${\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{18{t}^{2}+8}\,\text{d}t$
- $2{\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{3{t}^{2}+2}\,\text{d}t$
- ${\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{18{t}^{2}+24t+8}\,\text{d}t$

To compute the arc length $L$ of a parametrically defined curve, we use the formula

$$
L={\int }_{{t}_{1}}^{{t}_{2}}\sqrt{{(\frac{\text{d}x}{\text{d}t})}^{2}+{(\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}t})}^{2}}\,\text{d}t.
$$

Computing the derivatives of $x$ and $y,$ we have

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}x}{\text{d}t} & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}t}({t}^{3}+{t}^{2})=3{t}^{2}+2t\end{aligned}
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}t} & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}t}({t}^{3}-{t}^{2})=3{t}^{2}-2t\,.\end{aligned}
$$

We substitute these derivatives in the formula for arc length, and we get

$$
\begin{aligned}L & ={\int }_{{t}_{1}}^{{t}_{2}}\sqrt{{(\frac{\text{d}x}{\text{d}t})}^{2}+{(\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}t})}^{2}}\,\text{d}t \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}\sqrt{{(3{t}^{2}+2t)}^{2}+{(3{t}^{2}-2t)}^{2}}\,\text{d}t \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}\sqrt{9{t}^{4}+12{t}^{3}+4{t}^{2}+9{t}^{4}-12{t}^{3}+4{t}^{2}}\,\text{d}t \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}\sqrt{18{t}^{4}+8{t}^{2}}\,\text{d}t \\  & ={\int }_{2}^{3}t\sqrt{18{t}^{2}+8}\,\text{d}t\,.\end{aligned}
$$
