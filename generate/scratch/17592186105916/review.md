Suppose $|\,a\,|=17$ and the angle between vectors $a$ and $b$ is $\theta ={135}^{∘}.$ Find the scalar projection of $a$ onto $b.$

${\text{comp}}_{b}\,a=$
{{field-1}}

- $-\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{2}$

Using the formula for the scalar projection, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}{\text{comp}}_{b}\,a & =|\,a\,|\operatorname{cos}⁡\theta  \\  & =17\operatorname{cos}⁡({135}^{∘}) \\  & =17\cdot (-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) \\  & =-\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{2}.\end{aligned}
$$
