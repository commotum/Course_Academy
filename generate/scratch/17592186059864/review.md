## q-10291

Original

Find ${f}^{′}(x)$ for  $f(x)={x}^{3}+\frac{1}{{x}^{2}}-x.$

3{x}^{3}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1
6{x}^{2}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1
3{x}^{2}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}+1
3{x}^{2}+\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1
3{x}^{2}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1

The function $f(x)$ is the sum of three power functions. So, we apply the sum rule, and we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{3}+\frac{1}{{x}^{2}}-x) \\  & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{3})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{-2})-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x) \\  & =3{x}^{(3-1)}+(-2){x}^{(-2-1)}-1 \\  & =3{x}^{2}-2{x}^{-3}-1 \\  & =3{x}^{2}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1.\end{aligned}
$$

2{x}^{2}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1

2{x}^{1}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}+1
2{x}^{1}+\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1
2{x}^{1}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1

The function $f(x)$ is the sum of three power functions. So, we apply the sum rule, and we get

$$
\begin{aligned}{f}^{′}(x) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+\frac{1}{{x}^{2}}-x) \\  & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{-2})-\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(x) \\  & =2{x}^{(2-1)}+(-2){x}^{(-2-1)}-1 \\  & =2{x}^{1}-2{x}^{-3}-1 \\  & =2{x}^{1}-\frac{2}{{x}^{3}}-1.\end{aligned}
$$

## q-339462

Original

Find ${f}^{′}(x)$ for $f(x)={x}^{5}+\sqrt[3]{{x}^{4}}.$

${f}^{′}(x)=$
{{field-1}}

5{x}^{4}+\frac{4}{3}{x}^{1/3}

$$
