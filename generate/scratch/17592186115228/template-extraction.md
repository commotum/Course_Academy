# Finite template extraction

Each source's exact prompt, ordered statements, difficulty, field layout, field-owned values and worked solution is retained in source.json. review.md places every original/replay beside a dimension preview; source-replay.json accounts for every original option and correct designation. mathematics.json independently parses every rendered statement.

Shared task: given arbitrary square matrices of one common order (and a same-order identity where stated), select exactly the universally true statements. The ordered solution inspects statements I, II and III in turn, names the operative law or failure of commutativity, and concludes with the correct subset. Checkbox families have three keys I/II/III with canonical text true/false. The radio family has key selection, five observed textual subsets, and correct I and II only. The radio bank is not converted to checkboxes. No feedback or distractors are invented.

The source-specific mathematical expressions and solution stages are fixed. Different laws, an added arithmetic operation, different factor repetition, mixing claims from other source families, or a different field contract would be new unvalidated backbones. The dimension n is a preview slot, integer 2..8, uniformly applied to matrix sizes and I_n in prompt and solution; its observed values are 2,3,4,5,6,7,8. Restricting n>=2 matters: at n=1 multiplication becomes commutative and source keys fail. Scalar 2 in q-130463 and q-130478 is fixed in their observed explanation, and varying it alone would still test the identical universal law. Matrix names denote arbitrary matrices, so alpha-renaming is cosmetic. There is no concrete queried cell/edge/target slot whose mathematical role could be varied to create fresh tasks.

Normal seeded selection replays eligible templates only. Dimension variants are preview-only and are always rejected for fresh inventory. Every seed and dimension shares the mathematical task identity. The fresh-only CLI exhausts the finite extracted source bank, excludes all source tasks unconditionally, and returns [] with an explicit finite-limit diagnostic. It never pads a requested count.

Source choice mappings: all twelve checkbox source banks have one true and one false option per field; the recipe is the truth value of that exact rendered statement. Their opposite Boolean value is the only incorrect choice in that field. The radio profile is the exact five constant subsets observed together (I and II only; II only; I and III only; III only; II and III only); all five are replayed with their correct designation. source-replay.json reports 77/77 choices and 40/40 distractors, with 13/13 complete banks and no missing/extra options. Choice order is retained for fidelity but excluded from identity.

Mathematics uses SymPy exact expansion of noncommuting symbols, with I_n as the multiplicative unit. A zero polynomial proves the identity at every order. Every nonzero source difference is accompanied by an exact 2x2 matrix witness; direct-sum embedding extends it to every allowed n. Domain boundaries n=1,9, nonintegers and Boolean dimensions are rejected. The actual application grader checks every authored choice of every source replay and every n=2..8 preview. No blank fields occur. No assets or plot generation is needed.
