## easy | authored method bank | method

![](images/3c/3c68460c25eed3e9dfa3e52d62b8b5a460a615bf00f50954ab0a920e4892bcb0.png)

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $-\frac{x}{2y}$

B. $-\frac{y}{x}$

C. $\frac{x}{y}$

D. $-\frac{x}{y}$ **correct**

E. $-\frac{2x}{y}$

Since our circle is centered at the origin and has a radius of $1,$ its equation is given by

$$
{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}={1}^{2}\,⟹\,{x}^{2}+{y}^{2}=1.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(1) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({x}^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({y}^{2}) & =0 \\ 2x+2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2x \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x}{y}\end{aligned}
$$

## moderate | authored method bank | method

![](images/38/38bd557f03e547ee2634d361fe1768cf8b2089a51230c2795e17036676b50bcd.png)

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $-\frac{x-2}{2(y-1)}$

B. $-\frac{y-1}{x-2}$

C. $\frac{x-2}{y-1}$

D. $-\frac{x-2}{y-1}$ **correct**

E. $-\frac{2(x-2)}{y-1}$

Since our circle is centered at $(2,1)$ and has a radius of $2,$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-2)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}={2}^{2}\,⟹\,(x-2)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=4.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x-2)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(4) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x-2)^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y-1)^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x-2)+2(y-1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y-1)\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x-2) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x-2}{y-1}\end{aligned}
$$

## hard | authored method bank | method

![](images/a3/a335ec0ac7f4502b973f0ef444f8ff96f4bd630e4c2e957ef86e52f29776dfbc.png)

Find $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x}$ for the circle shown above.

A. $-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{2(y+\frac{7}{2})}$

B. $-\frac{y+\frac{7}{2}}{x+\frac{7}{2}}$

C. $\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{7}{2}}$

D. $-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{7}{2}}$ **correct**

E. $-\frac{2(x+\frac{7}{2})}{y+\frac{7}{2}}$

Since our circle is centered at $(- \frac{7}{2},- \frac{7}{2})$ and has a radius of $\frac{3}{2},$ its equation is given by

$$
(x-(- \frac{7}{2}))^{2}+(y-(- \frac{7}{2}))^{2}={\frac{3}{2}}^{2}\,⟹\,(x+\frac{7}{2})^{2}+(y+\frac{7}{2})^{2}=\frac{9}{4}.
$$

Now, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{7}{2})^{2}+(y+\frac{7}{2})^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(\frac{9}{4}) \\ \frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((x+\frac{7}{2})^{2})+\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}((y+\frac{7}{2})^{2}) & =0 \\ 2(x+\frac{7}{2})+2(y+\frac{7}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =0 \\ 2(y+\frac{7}{2})\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-2(x+\frac{7}{2}) \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =-\frac{x+\frac{7}{2}}{y+\frac{7}{2}}\end{aligned}
$$
