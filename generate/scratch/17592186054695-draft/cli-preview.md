Which of the following graphs shows the line $y=x?$

![](images/7e/7e2a37f5ffc0bd1555aff489e253c309adf5d9eb6a643a2319abb72018355925.png)

![](images/8d/8d42fa2f35af1fc48689d64989138393d16141dfa2434dc142b74ea060e48e70.png)

![](images/74/7474b3711ad21733b0f43227495ca82657a7a66035db9bc6aa7a986d57c90343.png)

![](images/0a/0aa5d321a9fc1574785428f36d9ab05ac736623f9cedfc596bb52dcb6e19143f.png)

![](images/f3/f386caa0ff7e1472223bf9bf5161346a9129213d1e4131f35275a445695ffddb.png)

We need to find two distinct points that lie on our line. Any two points will do!

- For the first point, we will let the $x$-coordinate be $x=0.$ Then the $y$-coordinate is 

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =x \\  & =(0) \\  & =0.\end{aligned}
$$

So, the first point is $(0,0).$

- For the second point, we will let the $x$-coordinate be $x=-1.$ Then the $y$-coordinate is

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =x \\  & =(-1) \\  & =-1.\end{aligned}
$$

So, the second point is $(-1,-1).$

Finally, we plot those two points on the $xy$-plane and draw a line through them. This is the graph of $y=x.$
