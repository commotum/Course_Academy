## Stage 3 — Complete components and banks

`source-cases.json` maps every practice question to an explicit matrix/name/layout, family, level and public raw profile. `choice-ledger.json` maps every field and every choice occurrence to its original UUID/value/type, fixed category recipe, source profile and correct-presence status. There are **55/55** matched choice values across **11/11** complete original banks; **9/9** originally recorded correct designations match. Two source fields lack refs, so their correctness designation cannot be called matched. The entire original wrappers and nested field attributes remain in `source.json`.

All choices are exact categorical constants, assembled as one whole five-label bank. They are not calculations perturbed from the correct value. Their correctness is evaluated under the actual requested *best description*, rather than whether the broader category happens to be true. At a diagonal matrix, upper/lower are mathematically true descriptions but are not the most specific offered response. At an identity matrix, Identity would be most specific; identity matrices are excluded from normal family bounds.

Public `raw:q-N` profiles retain the source field-count/key-presence/recorded-ref-presence and full banks, and omit missing attributes. Public `source:q-N` profiles give completed normal records: a single complete bank, explicit response criterion, and q-13769's documented authored completion. Question/field/answer UUIDs are content-derived rather than copied capture IDs; capture numeric `:db/id` values and all remaining source attributes are retained in raw evidence. This representation conversion is separately normalized in fidelity assertions, never counted as literal UUID fidelity.

## Stage 4 — Reusable masks and bounds

Fixed prompt: “How can we best describe the following matrix?” followed by the complete matrix. Aligned form preserves the source trailing space, display delimiters, brackets and row separators. Inline bmatrix source form preserves its original spacing. Normal prompts append the explicit most-specific criterion; raw prompts preserve the original exactly.

Matrix slots are the complete integer array, its A/C name, size and aligned/bmatrix presentation. Solution slots are repeated full matrix; the region `above`, `below`, or `above and below`; category/article; opposite category; and first row-major nonzero witness in the active off-diagonal triangle. Witness index, name, value and above/below prose are all computed from the changed actual array. Changing the mask to move the witness from a21 to a31 or a12 to a23 is exercised through **every applicable public source recipe**. The bmatrix prompt-only source gains a solution only in the explicitly completed normal profile.

All numerical entries are integers -9..9; negatives, zeros and repeated diagonal values remain permitted. Explicit inputs enforce shape, integer type (excluding bool), magnitude, family, level, and source-layout bounds; invalid calls raise rather than silently resample. Easy: 2×2/3×3 strict upper/lower. Moderate: 3×3 nonidentity diagonal, including the square zero matrix. Hard: 4×4 strict upper/lower or nonidentity diagonal. Defaults use the observed family sentence masks and complete bank, choosing an eligible structural pattern before incidental values.

Meaningful task fingerprints are conditional. In a strict triangular case all diagonal entries are incidental, as are nonzero signs/magnitudes; dimension and off-diagonal support determine the assessed scan/witness structure. In a diagonal case the identity check makes **unit versus nonunit positions** substantive; zero and other nonunit diagonal entries are equivalent for this recognition task. Names, layout, profiles, seeds and option order never create new mathematical tasks. Full content identity separately includes version, actual prompt, fixed worked solution, difficulty presence/value, and complete bank/ref-presence; shuffling choices preserves it.

The enumerated finite catalog contains **16 easy** patterns (two orientations × [one 2×2 + seven 3×3 support masks]), **7 moderate** patterns (2³ unit/nonunit masks excluding all-one), and **141 hard** patterns (2 × 63 nonempty 4×4 strict support masks + 15 nonidentity diagonal masks). Numeric changes give presentation variety without inflating these totals. The validator independently reads every printed full-catalog matrix, checks every native bank, and tests default coverage of all eligible families. This narrow moderate recognition task genuinely has seven meaningful patterns under the requested fingerprint definition; adding unrelated mathematics to enlarge it would leave the KP.
