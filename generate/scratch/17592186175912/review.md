$=$
{{field-2}}

field-1: 4 x + y
field-2: -7

Since parallel lines have equal slopes, both lines should have the same slope.

First, write the given line in slope-intercept form:

$$
\begin{aligned}8 x + 2 y &= 1 \\ 2 y &= 1 -8 x \\ y &= \frac{1}{2} -4 x\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we wish to find a line that has a slope of $-4$ and passes through the point $(-2,1)$. To find the equation of this line, we use point-slope form:

$$
\begin{aligned}y-y_1 &= m(x-x_1) \\ y-(1) &= -4(x-(-2)) \\ y-(1) &= -4 x -8 \\ y &= -4 x -7\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, write the equation in standard form with coprime integer coefficients and a positive $x$-coefficient.

$$
\begin{aligned}y &= -4 x -7 \\ 4 x + y &= -7\end{aligned}
$$

The first blank is $4 x + y$ and the second blank is $-7$.

\begin{aligned}m &= \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \\ &= \frac{5-(-1)}{6-(3)} \\ &= 2\end{aligned}
