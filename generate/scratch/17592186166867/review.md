Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ for the right-opening parabola shown above.

- [correct] $\frac{2}{y}$
- $-\frac{2}{y}$
- $-\frac{y}{2}$
- $4y$
- $-\frac{4}{y}$

The diagram shows that the parabola has its vertex at $O,$ and passes through the point $(1,2).$

The standard equation of a left- or right-opening parabola whose vertex is at $(0,0)$ is given by

$$
{y}^{2}=4px.
$$

Substituting $x=1$ and $y=2$ into the equation above, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}(2)^{2} & =4p(1) \\ 4 & =4p \\ p & =1.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation of our parabola is 

$$
{y}^{2}=4x.
$$

Finally, to calculate $\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x},$ we use implicit differentiation:

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}({y}^{2}) & =\frac{\text{d}}{\text{d}x}(4x) \\ 2y\frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =4 \\ \frac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} & =\frac{2}{y}\end{aligned}
$$
