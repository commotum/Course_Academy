- $B$ only

The plotted points correspond to the $xy$-pairs $A(-2,-4)$, $B(-1,0)$ and $C(2,9).$

To check whether a point lies on the line, we substitute the coordinates of the point into the equation and determine whether the resulting statement is true.

- Substituting the point $A(-2,-4)$ results in a false statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =3x+3 \\ (-4) & =3(-2)+3 \\ -4 & =-3\,\times \end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $A$ does not lie on the line.

- Substituting the point $B(-1,0)$ results in a true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =3x+3 \\ (0) & =3(-1)+3 \\ 0 & =0\,✓\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $B$ does lie on the line.

- Substituting the point $C(2,9)$ results in a true statement:

$$
\begin{aligned}y & =3x+3 \\ (9) & =3(2)+3 \\ 9 & =9\,✓\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the point $C$ does lie on the line.

Finally, only points $B$ and $C$ lie on the line.
