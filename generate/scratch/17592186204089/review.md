## Replay q-288066

Which of the following equations has no solutions?

Select all that apply.

- **I.** $-3u+4=-3u-1$
- **II.** $2u+5=2u+8$
- **III.** $6u-2=3u+1$

Let's attempt to solve each equation in turn:

- For the first equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}-3u+4 & =-3u-1 \\ -3u+4+3u & =-3u-1+3u \\ 4 & =-1.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is false for all values of $u.$ Therefore, the equation $-3u+4=-3u-1$ has no solutions.

- For the second equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}2u+5 & =2u+8 \\ 2u+5-2u & =2u+8-2u \\ 5 & =8.\end{aligned}
$$

This equation is false for all values of $u.$ Therefore, the equation $2u+5=2u+8$ has no solutions.

- For the third equation, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}6u-2 & =3u+1 \\ 6u-2-3u+2 & =3u+1-3u+2 \\ 3u & =3 \\ u & =1.\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the equation $6u-2=3u+1$ has the unique solution $u=1.$

Therefore, the correct answer is "I and II only."
