## easy — scalar (scalar_method)

If $\overset{\to }{A}=⟨-4,- \frac{7}{2}⟩$, then $-2\overset{\to }{A}=$

- $⟨8,7⟩$
- $⟨-8,-7⟩$
- $⟨8,- \frac{7}{2}⟩$
- $⟨-4,7⟩$
- $⟨-6,- \frac{11}{2}⟩$

Correct: $⟨8,7⟩$

Worked solution

To compute $-2\overset{\to }{A},$ we just need to perform the required operations with each component separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}-2\overset{\to }{A} & =-2\cdot ⟨-4,- \frac{7}{2}⟩ \\  & =⟨(-2)\cdot (-4),(-2)\cdot (- \frac{7}{2})⟩ \\  & =⟨8,7⟩\end{aligned}
$$

$$

&= ⟨-9 - 22, -4 - 26⟩ \\
