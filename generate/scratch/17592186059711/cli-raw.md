## easy — sum (raw_q1301)

What is the sum of $\overset{\to }{A}=⟨10,5⟩$ and $\overset{\to }{B}=⟨8,-2⟩?$

Original answer fields absent.

Worked solution

To compute $\overset{\to }{A}+\overset{\to }{B},$ we just need to perform the required operations with each component separately:

$$
\begin{aligned}\overset{\to }{A}+\overset{\to }{B} & =⟨10,5⟩+⟨8,-2⟩ \\  & =⟨18,3⟩\end{aligned}
$$
