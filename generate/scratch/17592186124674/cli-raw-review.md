A factory's management noted that the growth of the unit production is directly proportional to the difference between the maximum number of units a factory worker can produce and the current production quantity. Let $P(t)$ be the production quantity, measured in units produced, and let $t \ge 0$ be the time in days. If the maximum number of units that a worker can produce is $280$ $units/day$, which differential equation could be used to model the factory's production? Assume that $k > 0$ is a constant of proportionality.

- $\frac{dP}{dt} = kP^{2}(280 - P)$
- $\frac{dP}{dt} = kP(280 - P)$
- $\frac{dP}{dt} =-k(280 - P)$
- $\frac{dP}{dt} =-k(280 + P)$
- $\frac{dP}{dt} = k(280 - P)$
