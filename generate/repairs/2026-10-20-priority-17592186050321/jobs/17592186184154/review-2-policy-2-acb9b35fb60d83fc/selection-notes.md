Read engine/rust/question_selection.rs::select, weights, seen_before_task; learning.rs::validate_practice_question and fresh-candidate filter. BTreeSet sorts unique candidate IDs. With configured weights, selection first draws a nonempty positively weighted difficulty band using SHA256 bytes0..8, then uniformly selects inside that band using bytes8..16 (53 bits). All generated items are moderate; conditional on that band, neither template nor bank profile has an additional weight. Current/previous seen items are excluded before selection. Consequently a positive draw probability is preserved for every fresh generated case, and arbitrary history may remove any particular case.

The independent script variation.py reproduces that actual same-band kernel with captured eligible old records and explicitly synthetic consecutive new IDs, 12000 fresh-history seeds. Captured original records with missing fields/keys are excluded. Captured inventory does not include all activity-ownership/classification facts or active policy/history, so the old-record eligibility pool and future ID ordering are conditional; no live eligibility is certified. Every delivered generated record is structurally valid in native grading and has no authored multipart dependency. All 240 new items appeared at least once. selection-sampled-records.json retains each selected item, synthetic ID and count, while selection-draws.json retains all seeds and selected IDs.

The unconfigured distribution=None branch selects the first sorted eligible ID, and an explicitly zero moderate weight excludes generated records; neither is evidence of a generator-specific cue restriction. Unknown difficulty is only fallback when no eligible positive rated band remains; no generated item is unknown. These branches were inspected in actual Rust and are not represented as a live policy simulation. Captured inventory is basis5277 and may later change. Conditional draw results are evaluated for the earlier shortcut in selection-shortcut-results.json. Other tested delivery counterexamples survive because all new items are selected, rather than relying on aggregate balance alone.
