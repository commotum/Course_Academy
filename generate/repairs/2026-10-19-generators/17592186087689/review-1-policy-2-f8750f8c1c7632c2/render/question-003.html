Find the exact solution of the equation e4+5y=11.

y=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

ln⁡(11)−45

First, we find the natural logarithm (ln) of both sides of the equation:

e4+5y=11ln⁡(e4+5y)=ln⁡(11)

Now, we use the fact that ln⁡(eu)=u to simplify the left-hand side. This gives

4+5y=ln⁡(11).

Finally, we solve for y, as follows:

4+5y=ln⁡(11)5y=ln⁡(11)−4y=15(ln⁡(11)−4)y=−45+15ln⁡(11)

Therefore, the solution is y=−45+15ln⁡(11).
