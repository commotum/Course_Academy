If log3⁡(x)=k and log3⁡(y)=−2, then log3⁡(x2y)=

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2k−2

The laws of logarithms are:

  • logn⁡(a)+logn⁡(b)=logn⁡(ab) - the product rule

  • logn⁡(a)−logn⁡(b)=logn⁡(ab) - the quotient rule

  • logn⁡(cm)=mlogn⁡(c) - the power rule

Note that log3⁡(x2y) can be rewritten using the product and power rules as follows:

log3⁡(x2y)=log3⁡(x2)+log3⁡(y)=2log3⁡(x)+log3⁡(y)

Now, since log3⁡(x)=k and log3⁡(y)=−2, we conclude that

2log3⁡(x)+log3⁡(y)=2k+(−2)=2k−2.

Therefore,

log3⁡(x2y)=2k−2.
