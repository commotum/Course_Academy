Differentiate x2tan⁡x.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

x2sec2⁡(x)+2xtan⁡(x)

Here, we have the product of the two functions u(x)=x2 and v(x)=tan⁡(x). So, we apply the product rule:

(u(x)v(x))′=u′(x)⋅v(x)+u(x)⋅v′(x)=(x2)′⋅(tan⁡(x))+(x2)⋅(tan⁡(x))′=(2x)(tan⁡(x))+(x2)(sec2⁡(x))=2xtan⁡(x)+(x2sec2⁡(x))
