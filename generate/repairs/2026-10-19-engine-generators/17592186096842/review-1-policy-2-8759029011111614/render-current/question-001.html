Find dydx for y=12x−6.

dydx=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−1(2x−6)3

We use the chain rule in the form

dydx=dydu⋅dudx.

Let u=2x−6. Then y=1u=u−1/2. Differentiating, we have

dydu=−12u−3/2=−12u3,

and

dudx=2.

Multiplying the two together gives

dydx=−12u3⋅2=−1u3=−1(2x−6)3.
