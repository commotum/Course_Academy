Given that y=4⋅22x−3x3, find dydx.

dydx=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

4⋅2−3x3+2x(2−9x2)ln⁡(2)

First, we express y as the composite function

y=4⋅2u,u=2x−3x3.

Differentiating gives

dydu=4⋅2uln⁡(2),dudx=2−9x2.

We now apply the chain rule and get

dydx=dydu⋅dudx=4⋅2uln⁡(2)⋅(2−9x2)=(2−9x2)⋅4⋅2uln⁡(2)=(8−36x2)⋅22x−3x3ln⁡(2).
