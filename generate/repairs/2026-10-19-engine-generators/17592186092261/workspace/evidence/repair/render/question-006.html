Review topic

If f(x)=4−5x3+1, find f′(x).

f′(x)= ⋅4−5x3+1

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−15x2ln⁡(4)

First, we express f as the composite function

f=4u,u=−5x3+1.

Differentiating gives

dfdu=4uln⁡(4),dudx=−15x2.

We now apply the chain rule and get

dfdx=dfdu⋅dudx=4uln⁡(4)⋅(−15x2)=(−15x2)⋅4uln⁡(4)=−15x2⋅4−5x3+1ln⁡(4).
