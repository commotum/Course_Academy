Given that f(x)=4xtan⁡(x), find f′(x).

f′(x)= +4xsec2⁡(x)

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

4xln⁡(4)tan⁡(x)

Here, we have the product of two functions u(x)=4x and v(x)=tan⁡(x). So, we apply the product rule:

f′(x)=(4x)′tan⁡(x)+4x(tan⁡(x))′=(4xln⁡(4))tan⁡(x)+4x(sec2⁡(x))=4xln⁡(4)tan⁡(x)+4xsec2⁡(x)
