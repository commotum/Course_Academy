# Instructional account

The learner applies the sine sum/difference identity, recalls signed unit-circle values, and combines the two radical products. Imported q-1708/q-65430 provide a decomposition in easy; q-332158/q-332159/q-274557/q-65427 provide it in moderate. These supplied decompositions are retained: they leave identity selection/recall and exact substitution to the learner. Imported hard q-119007 explicitly asks for sin(75 degrees) without giving a decomposition, so hard continues to require choosing a usable split, then one forward identity. No reversed identity is added. Easy and moderate are overlapping supported families; easy favors manageable acute splits and the observed 210-45 split, while moderate includes broader signed quadrants and exact entry. Units are representational variety, not proof of difficulty. Hard keeps degrees to match the imported hard anchor. Finite target coverage is disclosed, without treating different solution decompositions for the same unhinted target as new problems.
