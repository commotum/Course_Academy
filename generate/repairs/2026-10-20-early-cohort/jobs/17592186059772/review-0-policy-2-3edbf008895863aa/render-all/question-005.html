Review topic

Consider the augmented matrix U, given by

U=[1−2−9−60−66−6−4882].

What matrix results from carrying out the row operation R2:=12R2 on U?

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

[1−2−9−60−33−3−4882]

The operation R2:=12R2 means that we need to multiply the second row of the matrix by 12.

Multiplying the 2nd row by 12, we obtain the following:

U=[1−2−9−60−66−6−4882]R2:=12R2∼[1−2−9−612⋅012⋅(−6)12⋅612⋅(−6)−4882]∼[1−2−9−60−33−3−4882]
