Find the general solution to dpdt=−2e−p.

p=ln⁡( +C), where C is an arbitrary real constant.

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−2t

To find the general solution, we separate variables and integrate both sides with respect to t:

epdpdt=−2∫epdp=∫−2dtep=−2t+C

This is a general solution in implicit form. Solving for p gives

p=ln⁡(−2t+C)
