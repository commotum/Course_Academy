Given that y=−7ln⁡x, find dydx.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

−7x

The derivative of the natural logarithm is the reciprocal function:

ddx(ln⁡x)=1x

We apply our formula and get

dydx=ddx(−7ln⁡x)=−7ddx(ln⁡x)=−7⋅1x=−7x.
