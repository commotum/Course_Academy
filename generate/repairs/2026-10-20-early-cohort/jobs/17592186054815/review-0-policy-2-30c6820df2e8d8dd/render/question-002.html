Remember that R0+ is the set of all positive real numbers and zero.

  • 0: Zero is included by the subscript 0. Therefore, it belongs to R0+.

  • 2: It is positive, and it is an irrational real number. Therefore, it belongs to R0+.

  • 12: It is positive, and it is a rational number but not an integer. Therefore, it belongs to R0+.

  • −3: It is negative, and it is an integer. Therefore, it does not belong to R0+.

In conclusion, the correct answer is: 0; 2; 12.
