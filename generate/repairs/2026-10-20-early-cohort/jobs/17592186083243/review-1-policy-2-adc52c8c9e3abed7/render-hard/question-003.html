For the parametric curve

x=3cos⁡t,y=4sin⁡2t,0≤t<2π,

shown above, calculate the slope m of the tangent to the curve at the point where t=π3. Do not round the answer.

m=

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

839

We will use the formula

dydx=y′(t)x′(t).

Differentiating x and y, we get

x′(t)=−3sin⁡t,y′(t)=8cos⁡2t.

So, we have

dydx=(8cos⁡2t)(−3sin⁡t)=−8cos⁡2t3sin⁡t.

Therefore, when t=π3, the slope m of the tangent to the curve is

m=dydx|t=π3=−8cos⁡2(π3)3sin⁡(π3)=−8(−12)3(32)=839.
