Answer · correct value:

⟨52,−1,−12⟩

The third equation states that x3=−12. Substituting this into the second equation, we get

x2−(−12)=−12⟹x2=−12−(12)=−1.

Now, we substitute x2=−1 and x3=−12 into the first equation to get

x1+(−1)−(−12)=2⟹x1=2−(−1)−(12)=52.

Therefore, the solution is x1=52, x2=−1, and x3=−12. In vector form, the solution can be written as ⟨52,−1,−12⟩.
