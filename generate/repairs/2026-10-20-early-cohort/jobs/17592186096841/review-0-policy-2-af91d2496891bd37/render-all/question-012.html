Given that f(x)=(1+2x)4, the equation of the tangent at the point (1,81) is

y= .

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

216x−135

To find the slope at a given point, we have to find dfdx at that point.

We use the chain rule to find dfdx.

First, we express f(x) as a composite function:

f=g4,g=1+2x

The chain rule states that

dfdx=dfdg⋅dgdx.

Differentiating, we have

dfdg=4g3,

and

dgdx=2.

Multiplying the two together and substituting for g gives

dfdx=4g3⋅(2)=8(1+2x)3.

Therefore, the slope of the tangent line at the point where x=1 is

dfdx|x=1=[8(1+2x)3]|x=1=8(1+2(1))3=216.

Hence, the slope of the tangent line at the point (1,81) is m=216. Now, we find the equation of the tangent at that point:

y−y1=m(x−x1)y−(81)=216(x−(1))y=216x−216+81y=216x−135
