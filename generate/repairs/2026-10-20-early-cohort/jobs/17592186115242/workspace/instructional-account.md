and one affine solve sufficient. Moving k to a constant or adding variables
would change the extracted method. Hard swapped templates retain their row

constraints, unlike a nonzero answer or a nonzero parameter shift, which are
not required by the method. Every prompt, matrix, equation and conclusion

scaled/shifted k. Hard preserves swap, fractional elimination (including bare
k), and negative-parameter families. Original source labels remain unchanged.
