# Template and choice-bank extraction

The complete source-specific descriptions, original parameters, prompts, solution stages and every original choice mapping are in template-extraction.json. Each profile preserves the supplied equation placement, variable order, sign topology, variable count, equation count and omission of variable terms. The response is one five-choice radio field asking for the augmented matrix, never a solved system.

Slots are positive integer magnitudes greater than one. The conservative sampling policy couples all occurrences of an observed equal magnitude across prompt, complete bank and solution. Units and zeros are fixed because they encode implicit coefficients and omitted variables. Signs and positions stay fixed to preserve the rearrangement method, missing-variable roles and source error banks. Distinct magnitudes are sampled without replacement from 2..24; replay retains the exact source values, including repeated appearances. This narrower same-method numerical generalization deliberately does not vary equation targets or introduce operations. A slot recipe is literal signed occurrence substitution; it reproduces every complete matrix entry, including compound mistakes, without inventing a psychological interpretation for an opaque distractor.

Correct matrices are independently derived from the displayed equations by moving variables left and constants right. All choices retain their source recipe and profile; profiles are selected before sampling. Validators reject collisions and matrices equivalent by row rescaling/reordering. Each instance has one concrete solution preserving the source organization paragraph, coefficient matrix and augmentation paragraph. No feedback is added.

Source layout correction: naked capture braces between nested aligned environments are removed in generated presentation. Source coefficient-matrix vertical dividers are retained as observed notation, although these matrices have no constants column. Their numerical contents and instructional roles are independently verified. No source is edited.

Replay checks mathematical values, correct designation, all 35 options and 28 distractors separately from formatting and engine grading. All 23 allowed values of every declared magnitude slot are tested. Cases holding the other slots at source parameters test stale prompt, solution and key values; collisions are explicitly rejected and retained in slot-coverage.json. Additional distinct-magnitude cases exercise any value excluded by a fixed-source collision. Source/variant pairs for all seven profiles are in review.json and review.md.
