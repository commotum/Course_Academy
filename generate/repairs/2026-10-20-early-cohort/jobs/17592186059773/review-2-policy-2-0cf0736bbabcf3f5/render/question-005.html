Which of the following elementary row operations transforms the matrix A into the matrix B, where

A=[−802107−552−3]

B=[27−3107−552−3]?

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

R1:=R1+R2

First, notice that the 2nd and 3rd rows are the same in both matrices. The only row that has changed is the 1st row.

By considering the given row operations that modify the 1st row, we notice that

A=[−802107−552−3]R1:=R1+R2∼[27−3107−552−3]=B.

Therefore, the required row operation is

R1:=R1+R2.
