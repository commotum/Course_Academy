Which of the following elementary row operations transforms the matrix X into the matrix W, where

X=[26101−1−3005]

W=[26101−122005]?

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

R2:=R2+5R3

First, notice that the 1st and 3rd rows are the same in both matrices. The only row that has changed is the 2nd row.

By considering the given row operations that modify the 2nd row, we notice that

X=[26101−1−3005]R2:=R2+5R3∼[26101−122005]=W.

Therefore, the required row operation is

R2:=R2+5R3.
