# Rank by inspection: repair specification

The learner first compares the requested rank with each matrix's row and column
counts, excludes impossible candidates, and then inspects the remaining independent
pivots before choosing a matrix or subset. Lesson tutorial 4151 states that rank is
the number of pivot columns, equivalently nonzero rows **in row echelon form**.
Canonical e-5417 explicitly excludes B by its three rows before comparing A's
three pivots with C's four. The neighboring row-reduction knowledge point e-5418
is a different assessed method; arbitrary dense matrices are outside this package.

q-79560, q-79561); three prompt matrices and five literal subset radio options
