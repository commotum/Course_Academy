## q-140766

$$
A\text{ is invertible}\,⇔\,\{\,\begin{aligned} & \text{Col}(A)={R}^{n} \\  & \text{dim(Col}(A))=n=\text{rank}(A) \\  & \text{Null}(A)=\bbox[3pt,white,border: 2px solid Gainsboro]{\phantom{W}} \\  & \text{dim(Null}(A))=0=\text{nullity}(A)\end{aligned}
$$

Consider the equivalence above, where $A$ is an $n\,\times \,n$ matrix. Which of the following should be placed into the blank space to make a true statement?

Recall that the *invertible matrix theorem* states the following: 

*Let $A$ be an $n\,\times \,n$ matrix. Then we have the following equivalence:*

$$
A\text{ is invertible}\,⇔\,\{\,\begin{aligned} & \text{Col}(A)={R}^{n} \\  & \text{dim(Col}(A))=n=\text{rank}(A) \\  & \text{Null}(A)=\{0\} \\  & \text{dim(Null}(A))=0=\text{nullity}(A)\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the missing expression is $\{0\}.$
