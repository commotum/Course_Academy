This equation is in the standard form

dydx+P(x)y=Q(x),

with P(x)=6 and Q(x)=36.

We compute an integrating factor using the definition:

I(x)=e∫P(x)dx=e∫6dx=e6x

Multiplying both sides of the equation in standard form by I(x)=e6x yields

e6x⋅(dydx+6y)=e6x⋅36e6xdydx+6e6xy=36e6xddx(ye6x)=36e6x.

Now, we integrate with respect to x:

∫ddx(ye6x)dx=∫36e6xdxye6x=36(16e6x)+Cye6x=6e6x+Cy=(C+6e6x)e−6x

This is the general solution. To find the value of C corresponding to the specific solution, we apply the initial condition y(0)=−6 as follows:

y(0)=(6e6(0))+C(e6(0))−6=6+C1C=−12

Therefore, the solution to the initial value problem is

y=6−12e−6x.
