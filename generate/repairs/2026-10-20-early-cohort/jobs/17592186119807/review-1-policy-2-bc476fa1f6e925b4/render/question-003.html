Consider the vectors

a1=[30−2],a2=[−8−14],b=[−1−2−2].

If x1a1+x2a2=b, then what is x1+x2?

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

7

If x1a1+x2a2=b, then we have

x1[30−2]+x2[−8−14]=[−1−2−2][3x10−2x1]+[−8x2−x24x2]=[−1−2−2][3x1−8x2−x2−2x1+4x2]=[−1−2−2]

Equating the corresponding components, we get the following system:

{3x1−8x2=−1−x2=−2−2x1+4x2=−2

From the second equation we get x2=2.

Substituting x2=2 into the first equation, we get

3x1−8x2=−13x1−8(2)=−13x1=15x1=5

Finally, we substitute x1=5 and x2=2 into the third equation to get

−2x1+4x2=−2−2(5)+4(2)=−2−2=−2

which gives a true statement.

So the solution of the system is x1=5 and x2=2, which means

5a1+2a2=b.

Consequently,

x1+x2=(5)+(2)=7.
