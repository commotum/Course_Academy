## coefficient original

What is the coefficient matrix for the following system of linear equations?

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}2{x}_{2}+4{x}_{1} &=3{x}_{3} \\ -11{x}_{3} &=12\end{aligned}\right.
$$

First, we organize the system so that the variables are ordered ${x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}$ on the left-hand side, the constants are on the right-hand side, and every variable has a coefficient: 

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}4{x}_{1}+2{x}_{2}-3{x}_{3} &=0 \\ 0{x}_{1}+0{x}_{2}-11{x}_{3} &=12\end{aligned}\right.
$$

The coefficient matrix consists of the coefficients of the variables:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}4 & 2 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & -11\end{bmatrix}
$$

## augmented original

What is the augmented matrix for the following system of linear equations? 

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}a-c &=4b \\ 3c-11a &=-1\end{aligned}\right.
$$

First, we organize the system so that the variables are ordered $a,b,c$ on the left-hand side, the constants are on the right-hand side, and every variable has a coefficient:

$$
\left\{\begin{aligned}a-4b-c &=0 \\ -11a+0b+3c &=-1\end{aligned}\right.
$$

The coefficient matrix consists of the coefficients of the variables:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}1 & -4 & -1 \\ -11 & 0 & 3\end{bmatrix}
$$

The augmented matrix is the coefficient matrix augmented with the column that represents the right-hand sides of the equations:

$$
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}1 & -4 & -1 & 0 \\ -11 & 0 & 3 & -1\end{array}\right]
$$
