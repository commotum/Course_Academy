Count templates give a square matrix order and pivot or non-pivot count. Null-space templates supply independent spanning vectors or a linear free-parameter description. Each asks for the truth of three fixed source claims. Preserve the supplied-object role, claim order, field layout, complete radio subset bank or per-statement Boolean bank, and the solution stages: square-matrix invertibility equivalences; rank-nullity; statement I, II and III in turn; final truth subset.

Two conservative generalizations are supported. For count templates, multiply all positive dimensional and count literals by the same integer k in {1,2,3}, retaining zero and all theorem relations. This extends source dimensions to at most 21; no large matrix computation is added. For vector templates apply one invertible diagonal coordinate map with integer row factors in {1,2,3} to every supplied and worked-solution vector. This preserves support, independence, free-parameter count, rank-nullity and source truth patterns while permitting genuinely different subspaces. General coordinate permutations, matrix dimensions for vector sources, arbitrary statement changes, new distractor subsets, and new methods are outside this version.

These are deliberately narrow policies, not claims that broader variations are impossible. At original parameters prompt and solution must replay byte for byte and every full bank must match.
