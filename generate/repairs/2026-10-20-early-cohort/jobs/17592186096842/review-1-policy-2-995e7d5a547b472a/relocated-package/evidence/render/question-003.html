Review topic

Find the slope of the tangent to the curve y=−5x+49+4x3 at the point where x=0.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

−514

To find the slope at a given point, we have to find dydx at that point.

Let f(x)=−5x+49. We use the chain rule in the form

dfdx=dfdu⋅dudx.

Let u=−5x+49. Then f=u=u1/2. Differentiating, we have

dfdu=12u−1/2=12u

and

dudx=−5.

Multiplying the two together gives

dfdx=12u⋅(−5)=−52u=−52−5x+49.

Applying the sum rule, we get

dydx=−52−5x+49+12x2.

Finally, substituting x=0, we get

dydx|x=0=−52−5(0)+49+12(0)2=−514+0=−514.
