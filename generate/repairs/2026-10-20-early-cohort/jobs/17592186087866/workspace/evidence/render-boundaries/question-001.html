Write the following first-order linear ODE in standard form.

5dydx−5cos⁡(x)=8ysin⁡(x)+5.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

dydx−8ysin⁡(x)5=cos⁡(x)+1

A first-order linear ODE is in standard form if it is written as

dydx+P(x)y=Q(x).

Converting the given equation to standard form, we have

5dydx−5cos⁡(x)=8ysin⁡(x)+55dydx−8ysin⁡(x)=5cos⁡(x)+5dydx+(−8sin⁡(x)5)⏟P(x)y=cos⁡(x)+1⏟Q(x)

Now, the equation is written in standard form with P(x)=−8sin⁡(x)5 and Q(x)=cos⁡(x)+1.
