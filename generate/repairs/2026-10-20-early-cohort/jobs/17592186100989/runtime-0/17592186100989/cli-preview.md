Find the entry ${r}_{23}$ of the matrix $R$, where 

$$
R=[\begin{aligned}10 & 1 & 4 \\ -10 & -1 & -11 \\ 4 & -4 & 13\end{aligned}].
$$

Choices:
- $4$
- $-11$
- $-4$
- No such entry
- $-1$

Correct: -11

The entry ${r}_{23}$ lies at the intersection of the $2$nd row and $3$rd column. Therefore, ${r}_{23}=-11.$

$$
R=[\begin{aligned}10 & 1 & 4 \\ -10 & -1 & -11 \\ 4 & -4 & 13\end{aligned}]
$$
