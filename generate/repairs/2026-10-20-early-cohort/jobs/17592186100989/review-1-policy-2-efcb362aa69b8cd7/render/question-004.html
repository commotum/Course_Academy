Find the entry b24 of the matrix B, where

B=[−62−211−42308].

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

No such entry

The entry b24 should lie at the intersection of the 2nd row and 4th column. Since B is a 3×3 matrix, it does not have the 4th column.

Therefore, B does not have the entry b24.
