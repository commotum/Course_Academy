Calculate ∫23x+5dx.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

23ln|3x+5|+C

First, let u=3x+5. Then differentiating with respect to x gives

dudx=3⟹dx=13du.

We can now rewrite the integral in terms of u, solve it, and write the final answer in terms of x as follows:

∫23x+5dx=2∫1u⋅(13du)=23∫1udu=23ln|u|+C=23ln|3x+5|+C
