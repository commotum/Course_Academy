Consider a 7×12 matrix A. If rank(A)=6 then nullity(A)=

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

6

According to the rank-nullity theorem, if A is a matrix with n columns, then

rank(A)+nullity(A)=n.

For the given matrix, we have n=12. Therefore,

rank(A)+nullity(A)=n6+nullity(A)=12nullity(A)=12−6=6.
