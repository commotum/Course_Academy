Consider the augmented matrix M given by

M=[39302715].

Use the standard method of Gaussian elimination to reduce M to row echelon form, and then solve the linear system represented by the matrix.

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

M∼[131001−5], x=25 and y=−5

Reducing M to row echelon form, we have

M∼[39302715]R1:=13R1∼[13102715]R2:=R2−2⋅R1∼[131001−5].

The system is now

{x+3y=10y=−5

and we can apply back substitution. The final equation y=−5 tells us the value of y. Substituting this value into the first equation, we get

x+3(−5)=10⇒x=10+15=25.

Therefore, the solution is x=25 and y=−5.
