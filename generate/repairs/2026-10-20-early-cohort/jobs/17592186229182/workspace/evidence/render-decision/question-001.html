Review topic

An aquarium initially holds 100L of water containing 3kg of dissolved minerals. A solution containing 0.04kg/L of dissolved minerals is pumped into the aquarium at a rate of 8L/min, while the completely mixed water is drained out at a rate of 12L/min. If the amount of minerals Q(t), in kilograms, in the tank t minutes after pumping begins is given by Q(t)=α(1−t25)+β(1−t25)3 where α and β are constants, what is the value of α+β?

A scientific calculator is required to answer this question.

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

3

At t=0, the supplied model gives Q(0)=α(1−0/25)+β(1−0/25)3=α+β. The initial amount is 3kg, so α+β=3.
