Find j, if a1−ja2=b and

a1=[0−11],a2=[−210],b=[4−42].

Choose an answer
CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

2

Using the properties of addition/subtraction and scalar multiplication of vectors to isolate the j term, we get

a1−ja2=b[0−11]−j[−210]=[4−42]j[−210]=[0−11]−[4−42]j[−210]=[−420].

From the final equation above, we can tell that j=2.
