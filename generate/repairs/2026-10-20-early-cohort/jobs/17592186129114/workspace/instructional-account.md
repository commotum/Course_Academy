quota: all seven nonempty subsets and all violation cases remain eligible.

varying every label; requiring this in a two-item group would force complementary
answers and exclude ABC. Singletons and pairs therefore establish no conditional
coverage claim. Pool order, accepted seeds, duplicate and pool rejections will be
recorded. No existing individual generator parameters or source replay change.
