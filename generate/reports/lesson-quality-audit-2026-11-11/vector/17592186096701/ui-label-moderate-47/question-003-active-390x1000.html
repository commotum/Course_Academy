The arrows show OA→=a, OB→=b, and OC→=c. Grid squares have side length one unit.

Given that c=λa+3b, find λ.

λ= .

Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

3

Construct the known component 3b from O, and call its endpoint P. Count signed horizontal and vertical grid displacements:

OP→=3(−1,−1)=(−3,−3).PC→=(−6,0).

Compare the remaining displacement with a, whose grid displacement is (−2,0):

Horizontal scale=−6−2=3

The scale is positive, so the remaining vector has the same direction as a. Thus PC→=3a. By the triangle law,

c=OP→+PC→=3b+3a=3a+3b.

The vectors a and b are not parallel, so their coefficients are unique. Matching the coefficient of a gives λ=3.
