In the diagram, L lies on the segment XW. The given vectors are OX→=c and OW→=d. The lengths satisfy XL:LW=7:10.

Express OL→ in terms of c and d.

Choose an answer
Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OL→=1017c+717d

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OX→=cOW→=d.

Trace the path from X through O to W:

XW→=XO→+OW→=−c+d.

The ratio XL:LW=7:10 gives 17 parts in the whole segment. Thus

XL→=717XW→=717(−c+d).

Add along the path O→X→L and collect coefficients:

OL→=OX→+XL→=c+717(−c+d)=1017c+717d.
