In the diagram, D lies on the segment KM. The given vectors are KO→=k and MO→=v. The lengths satisfy KD:DM=1:2.

Express OD→ in terms of k and v. Fill in both coefficients, including their signs.

OD→= k+ v

CorrectPreview only
Solution:

Answer 1 · correct value:

−23

Answer 2 · correct value:

−13

First express both endpoint vectors in the directions away from O:

OK→=−kOM→=−v.

Trace the path from K through O to M:

KM→=KO→+OM→=k−v.

The ratio KD:DM=1:2 gives 3 parts in the whole segment. Thus

KD→=13KM→=13(k−v).

Add along the path O→K→D and collect coefficients:

OD→=OK→+KD→=−k+13(k−v)=−23k−13v.
