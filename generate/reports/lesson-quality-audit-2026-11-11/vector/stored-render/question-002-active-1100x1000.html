  • OR=12|r|

  • OR→ has the opposite direction to r

Hence, OR→=−12r.

Similarly:

  • RT=52|s|

  • RT→ has the opposite direction to s

Hence, RT→=−52s.

Additionally,

t=OR→+RT→=−12r−52s.

Consequently, t=λr+μs=−12r−52s.

Since s∦r, the linear combination can only be written in one way. Equating the coefficients, we get:

  • λ=−12 and μ=−52
