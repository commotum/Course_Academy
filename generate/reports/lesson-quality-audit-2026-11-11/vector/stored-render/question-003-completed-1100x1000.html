In the diagram, X lies on the segment AB. The given vectors are OA→=a and OB→=b. The lengths satisfy AX:XB=1:2.

Use the path OX→=OA→+AX→ and the given relation AB→=b−a to find OX→ in terms of a and b.

Solution:

Answer · correct value:

OX→=23a+13b

The ratio AX:XB=1:2 splits AB into 1+2=3 equal parts. Since AX→ points from A toward B, AX→=13AB→=13(b−a). Following the supplied path gives OX→=OA→+AX→=a+13(b−a)=23a+13b. The larger coefficient belongs to a: X is closer to A than to B.
