**Across the reviewed failures, compliance checks could pass while the chosen interpretation changed the learner’s task.** The current audit finds contradictory requirements and evidence gaps that can preserve this failure mode. The remedy is to make instructional purpose govern template and difficulty decisions, remove contradictory requirements, and close specific gaps between investigation, verification and delivery. Adding another general instruction to “understand the intent” would largely repeat instructions already present.

The historical evidence supports a more specific conclusion than “agents never understood the point.” Some original agents correctly described substitution recognition or matrix row/column pairing, then supplied that very work in easy questions; reviewers accepted the progression. Other agents promoted an observed answer-bank arrangement into an invariant. The failure was often in carrying the objective into construction and acceptance, even after stating it correctly.

Several repairs are already incorporated into today’s pipeline. It explicitly protects independent reasoning and recall, permits the same reasoning across difficulty levels, challenges frozen answer roles, distinguishes useful mathematical simplification from incidental cues, supports finite batch limits, and gives one persistent verifier responsibility for repair. Those protections should remain. The proposals below address contradictions and missing connections around them, rather than presenting those protections as absent. **The main causal paths are these.** A local repair can become a general requirement, a complete example can become instructional authority, and a technical proxy can become the acceptance criterion for a different educational property.

| Translation | Historical failure | Remaining pipeline exposure |
|---|---|---|
| “Produce three difficulty levels” → change the assessed task | Easy supplies substitution/formulas; hard adds unrelated work | Conflicting progression instructions; mandatory donor and exemplar completion |
| “Preserve the source” → preserve its incidental arrangement | Fixed truth profiles, numerical ranks and distractor offsets | Required guide still prescribes complete source-bank recipes in normal construction |
| “Prevent the last defect” → forbid legitimate cases | Cancellation exclusions, mutual exclusivity, narrowed parameter domains | Active topic policies encode particular signs, ranges, family quotas and a zero-result ban |
