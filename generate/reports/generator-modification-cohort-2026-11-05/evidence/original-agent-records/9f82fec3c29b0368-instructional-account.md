## Difficulty and fixed features

All bands keep 3×3 integer operands and the same multiplication method: this is the target KP, not scalar summaries, determinants, inverses, or rectangular multiplication. Signed factors lie between −3 and 3. Small arithmetic controls prerequisite load. Zeros and exact cancellations remain legitimate. Negative factors are parenthesized in derivations. No CAS normal-form conversions enter learner content.

Easy: three blanks, one in every row and every column, six product entries supplied. Each left row has one or two nonzero entries; right columns have two or three, and requested dot products have at least one nonzero term. One requested dot-product setup is supplied, along with a row/column reminder. The learner evaluates it and independently pairs the other two. Integers and signed multiplication are prerequisites; locating/pairing the entries is the target. Positions, zero locations, and setup target are variable.

Moderate (default): five blanks distributed across all rows and columns, four results supplied. Left rows have two or three nonzero entries; right columns have two or three. At least one requested dot product has three nonzero terms. No pre-answer dot-product setup. Learners choose every requested pairing and handle signed sums. This preserves the complete imported blank demand.

Hard: nine blanks, no result or method reminder; every row/column pairing must be organized independently. Both operands are dense but coefficients retain the same small range. This adds completeness and independence rather than large arithmetic. Cancellation and zero answers are permitted. It is the demanding end of assembling the product in the canonical example.

Matched comparison: use the same dense operands and five-cell selection to compare moderate with hard: hard removes the four given results and requests four additional entries. Use the same sparse operands and nested three/five target sets to compare easy with moderate: moderate removes the setup and adds two independently located calculations. These are controlled diagnostic comparisons, not assertions that every shared diagnostic instance is in both production sampling ranges.
