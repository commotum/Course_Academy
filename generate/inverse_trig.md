# Inverse-trigonometric integration generators

The four KPs under topic `17592186048189` use the shared implementation in
`generate/inverse_trig.py`. Numeric entrypoints load their own immutable source
evidence and band contracts. Publication bundles the entrypoint, helper and
evidence together, so registered packages do not depend on repository imports.

The source coefficients are examples of mathematical roles, not replaceable
strings. Sample a workload case before its independent coefficients. Inverse
sine uses `N/sqrt(A-B*x²)`, inverse tangent uses `N/(A+B*x²)`, and inverse secant
distinguishes visible matching arguments from the normalization of `|x|`.

Easy, moderate and hard are operation contracts. Increasing a coefficient does
not increase the band. Sine/tangent hard and secant easy are explicitly authored
compositions of imported practice and canonical examples, with no claim of
external difficulty calibration. Definite hard requires both normalization and
two unlike, nonzero transformed endpoints. Imported difficulty labels remain
unchanged as source evidence.

Arcsine definite bounds must stay strictly inside `(-1,1)` after substitution.
The imported `q-351834` has improper endpoints and is not a generation anchor.
Its import is preserved; normal generation never clones that endpoint family.
Secant uses positive substitution factors and shows `|u|=s|x|` explicitly, with
independent constants allowed on the two components of its real domain.

All normal banks are authored complete recipe profiles, documented in the
metadata; exact source-bank generalization is not claimed. Reject profiles
whose errors coincide with the key or each other. Reciprocal-endpoint profiles
are inapplicable to the endpoint-one and positive arctangent-pair catalogs.
A mathematical task deterministically selects one eligible bank, preventing
new seeds from manufacturing different banks for the same task. Seeded option
permutations preserve question and answer identities and do not count as variety.

Batch selection balances structural cases and alternates response shapes within
each definite family. It excludes mathematical fingerprints of imported,
canonical and captured generated questions, and deduplicates across the bands
of one batch. Unsupported sizes or exhausted catalogs raise explicit errors.
Delivery and fresh learner selection still apply their normal inventory and
history filters; the generator does not read or modify learner state.

The 2026-10-24 repair evidence includes a reader independent of generator
metadata that checks the printed integrands, normalization, reciprocals,
absolute-value identities, transformed bounds, every derivation row and keys.
Keep those checks distinct from actual `grade_field` acceptance, UI rendering,
source preservation and post-publication readback.
